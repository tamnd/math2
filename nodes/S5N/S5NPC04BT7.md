## Summary

In mathematics, a **period**, or **algebraic period**, is a [[complex number|09FTAET5Z4]] that can be expressed as an integral of an algebraic function over an algebraic [[domain|0Q4CBD3HX0]]. The periods are a class of numbers which includes, alongside the algebraic numbers, many well known mathematical constants such as the [[number $\pi$|0C1VYVNV2N]]. Sums and products of periods remain periods, so that the periods form a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]], commonly denoted $\mathcal P$.
