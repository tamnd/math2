## Summary

Temperature is the quantity that tells whether two systems are in thermal equilibrium: they are exactly when their temperatures are equal.
Heat flows by itself from the body of higher temperature to the body of lower temperature.
An empirical temperature can be read from any thermometer; the [[thermodynamic temperature|AJ8M9MGZGF]] is a scale that does not depend on the material.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Temperature measures how hot or cold something is.
When you put a thermometer in a bath, the thermometer and the bath exchange heat until they reach the same temperature, and then the thermometer shows it.
Two objects at the same temperature placed in contact exchange no net heat.
For example, a spoon left in a cup of tea ends up at the temperature of the tea.
