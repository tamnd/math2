## Summary

In graph theory, the **modular product** of [[graphs|00C0BR7D7H]] $G$ and $H$ is a graph formed by combining $G$ and $H$ that has applications to subgraph isomorphism. It is one of several different kinds of graph products that have been studied, generally using the same vertex set (the [[Cartesian product|09CEDJYBKP]] of the [[sets|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] of vertices of the two graphs $G$ and $H$) but with different rules for determining which edges to include.
