## Summary

In mathematics, in particular field theory, the **conjugate elements** or **algebraic conjugates** of an [[algebraic element|0H628RSGWX]] $\alpha$, over a [[field extension|0EQWVHF3W5]] $L/K$, are the roots of the [[minimal polynomial|0VX9EP2GS8]] $p_{K,\alpha}(x)$ of $\alpha$ over $K$. Conjugate elements are commonly called **conjugates** in contexts where this is not ambiguous. Normally $\alpha$ itself is included in the set of conjugates of $\alpha$.
