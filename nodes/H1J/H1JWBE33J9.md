## Summary

This topic is a part of [[elementary number theory|7XZ94HTPQT]].
It covers primes of special forms and the theorems that test or restrict them, such as Mersenne and Fermat numbers, Sophie Germain and Cunningham primes, Wieferich and Wilson primes, and Sierpiński and Riesel numbers.
