## Summary

The Helmholtz free energy of a system is its internal energy minus the product of its temperature and entropy.
It is the [[thermodynamic potential|MKZW03Q590]] whose natural variables are temperature and volume.
At fixed temperature and volume it is minimal in equilibrium, and in an isothermal process its decrease bounds the work that the system can do.
In statistical mechanics it is $-kT$ times the logarithm of the canonical partition function.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Not all the energy of a system can be turned into work at constant temperature.
Part of it is tied up with the disorder of the system, measured by $T S$.
The Helmholtz free energy $U - TS$ is the part that is "free" to do work.
For example, a gas compressed in a cylinder kept at room temperature can do at most as much work as its free energy drops while it expands.
