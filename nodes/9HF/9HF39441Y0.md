## Summary

The Euler free nutation is the free motion of the rotation axis of a rigid axisymmetric body about its symmetry axis, with no torque acting.
In the body frame, the angular velocity vector turns around the symmetry axis at a constant rate fixed by the moments of inertia.
Euler predicted this free motion in the eighteenth century.
For a rigid body with the Earth's moments of inertia, its period is about $305$ days.
The observed free wobble of the real Earth is the slower [[Chandler wobble|9RESF83PY2]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take a flattened rigid ball and spin it about an axis that is slightly tilted from its axis of symmetry.
Seen from the ball, the spin axis does not stay put: it walks around the symmetry axis in a small cone.
For a coin-like body the walk is fast, and for a nearly round body like the Earth it is slow.
For the Earth's shape, one loop would take about $305$ days if the Earth were rigid.
