## Summary

This topic is a part of [[algebraic combinatorics|QWD733WPW3]].
It covers the objects counted by the Catalan number of a finite reflection group, such as noncrossing and nonnesting partitions, clusters, sortable elements and Cambrian lattices, and the uniform results that connect them.
