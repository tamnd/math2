## Summary

Reidemeister torsion is an invariant of a finite CW complex $X$ together with a representation of its fundamental group.
It is defined when the twisted cellular chain complex is acyclic.
It takes values in the units of a field, modulo the ambiguity coming from the choice of cell lifts.
It was the first invariant able to tell apart homotopy equivalent spaces that are not homeomorphic, namely lens spaces.
Its smooth analogue is [[analytic torsion|MZT9D34B0A]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a chain complex is exact, its homology carries no information.
Yet the way the boundary maps fit together still leaves a number, a kind of alternating product of determinants.
Reidemeister torsion is this number for the cellular chains of a space, twisted by a representation so that the complex becomes exact.
For lens spaces it detects how the cyclic group rotates the two complex coordinates, which homology cannot see.
