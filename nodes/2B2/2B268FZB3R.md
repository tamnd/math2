## Definition

Let $0 \to I \xrightarrow{\iota} A \xrightarrow{\pi} B \to 0$ be a [[short exact sequence|KJKNJMGKZZ]] of C*-algebras and $*$-homomorphisms.
Assume that $A$ is unital; then $B$ and $\pi$ are unital.
Let $u$ be a [[unitary|KK9S67F8CA]] in $M_n(B)$.
There is a unitary $v \in M_{2n}(A)$ with $\pi(v) = \operatorname{diag}(u, u^*)$.
For such a $v$, the element $v \operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0) v^* - \operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0)$ has entries in $I$, and the *index map* $\partial \colon K_1(B) \to K_0(I)$ is defined by
$$\partial [u] = \big[v \operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0) v^*\big] - \big[\operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0)\big] \in K_0(I).$$

## Remarks

The class does not depend on the choice of $n$, $u$ in its class, or the lift $v$, and $\partial$ is a group homomorphism into [[K0|BP86XQRZD2]] of $I$.
A lift $v$ exists because $\operatorname{diag}(u, u^*)$ lies in the connected component of $1$ in the unitary group, and unitaries in that component lift along surjections.
If $u$ lifts to a [[partial isometry|GGDSF85JPD]] $w \in M_n(A)$, then $\partial [u] = [1_n - w^* w] - [1_n - w w^*]$.
Example: for $0 \to \mathcal{K} \to B(H) \to B(H)/\mathcal{K} \to 0$ and the image $u$ of a Fredholm partial isometry $w$, this gives $\partial[u] = \dim \ker w - \dim \ker w^*$ under $K_0(\mathcal{K}) \cong \Z$.
Non-example of a nonzero value: if $u$ itself lifts to a unitary $w \in M_n(A)$, take $v = \operatorname{diag}(w, w^*)$; then $v \operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0) v^* = \operatorname{diag}(1_n, 0)$ and $\partial [u] = 0$.
When $A$ is not unital, one applies the definition to the unitized sequence $0 \to I \to \widetilde{A} \to \widetilde{B} \to 0$.
