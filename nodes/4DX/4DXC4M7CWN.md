## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **Balaban 10-cage** or **Balaban $(3,10)$-cage** is a 3-[[regular graph|0KA4S8R39M]] with 70 vertices and 105 edges named after Alexandru T. Balaban. Published in 1972, It was the first 10-cage discovered but it is not unique.
