## Summary

In mathematics, the **Bombieri norm**, named after Enrico Bombieri, is a [[norm|0V2WP9B1MF]] on homogeneous polynomials with coefficient in $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ (there is also a version for non homogeneous univariate polynomials). This norm has many remarkable properties, the most important being listed in this article.
