## Statement

In number theory, a **Giuga number** is a [[composite number|008BH74D7R]] $n$ such that for each of its distinct prime factors $p_i$ we have $p_i | \left({n \over p_i} - 1\right)$, or equivalently such that for each of its distinct prime factors *p*<sub>*i*</sub> we have $p_i^2 | (n - p_i)$.
