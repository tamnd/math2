## Summary

In the mathematical theory of matroids, a **paving matroid** is a matroid in which every circuit has size at least as large as the matroid's rank. In a matroid of rank $r$ every circuit has size at most $r+1$, so it is equivalent to define paving matroids as the matroids in which the size of every circuit belongs to the set $\{r,r+1\}$. It has been conjectured that [[almost all|N1XSAXCQY3]] matroids are paving matroids.
