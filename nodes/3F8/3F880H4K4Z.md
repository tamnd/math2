## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|K80DB44C78]].
It covers real semisimple and reductive Lie groups, with real forms, the Cartan, Iwasawa and Bruhat decompositions and maximal compact subgroups, and the structure of solvable and nilpotent Lie groups.
