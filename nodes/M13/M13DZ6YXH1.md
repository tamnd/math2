## Summary

A Jordan triple system is a vector space with a trilinear product $\set{x, y, z}$ that is symmetric in $x$ and $z$ and satisfies a derivation-type identity.
Every [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]] gives a Jordan triple system, and there are further examples, such as the rectangular matrices with the product $x y^T z + z y^T x$.
Through the [[Tits–Kantor–Koecher construction|FZM1A8HAHD]], Jordan triple systems correspond to Lie algebras with a grading $\mathfrak{g}_{-1} \oplus \mathfrak{g}_0 \oplus \mathfrak{g}_1$ and an involution that exchanges $\mathfrak{g}_{-1}$ and $\mathfrak{g}_1$.
They also describe bounded symmetric domains in complex analysis.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Rectangular $p \times q$ matrices cannot be multiplied with each other when $p \ne q$.
But three of them can be combined: $x y^T z$ is again a $p \times q$ matrix.
Symmetrizing in $x$ and $z$ gives the triple product $x y^T z + z y^T x$.
A Jordan triple system is any space with a triple product that obeys the same rules as this one.
The middle variable plays a different role from the outer two, like a "dual" slot.
