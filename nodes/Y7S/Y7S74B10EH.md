## Description

Let $F$ be a field.
The *Lie operad* $\mathrm{Lie}$ is the [[operad|2C5QGNNCAE]] of vector spaces over $F$ whose [[algebras|ES9YATDWA3]] are the [[Lie algebras|5JJYBJ6A8K]] over $F$.
Its component $\mathrm{Lie}(n)$ is the subspace of the [[free Lie algebra|NNAXK7BM3H]] on $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ spanned by the bracket monomials in which each $x_i$ occurs exactly once.
The [[symmetric group|0515A6D2Y1]] $S_n$ acts on $\mathrm{Lie}(n)$ by permuting the variables.
Composition substitutes bracket monomials for variables: in $\mu \circ_i \nu$ the bracket $\nu$ is put in place of $x_i$ in $\mu$, and the variables are renumbered.

## Remarks

The space $\mathrm{Lie}(n)$ has dimension $(n - 1)!$.
A basis is given by the brackets $[\ldots[\,[x_1, x_{\sigma(2)}], x_{\sigma(3)}], \ldots, x_{\sigma(n)}]$, where $\sigma$ runs over the permutations of $\set{2, \ldots, n}$.
For example, $\mathrm{Lie}(1) = F x_1$, $\mathrm{Lie}(2) = F [x_1, x_2]$ is the sign representation of $S_2$, and $\mathrm{Lie}(3)$ has basis $[\,[x_1, x_2], x_3]$ and $[\,[x_1, x_3], x_2]$.
The third bracket $[\,[x_2, x_3], x_1]$ is a combination of these by the Jacobi identity.
An algebra over $\mathrm{Lie}$ is a vector space $V$ with maps $\mathrm{Lie}(n) \otimes V^{\otimes n} \to V$, and these are determined by the image of $[x_1, x_2]$, which is a Lie bracket on $V$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An operad lists all the operations one can build from a basic operation, and how they combine.
For Lie algebras the basic operation is the bracket, and the operations in $n$ inputs are the bracket expressions that use each input once.
Antisymmetry and the Jacobi identity cut down the number of independent expressions to $(n - 1)!$.
The Lie operad is this collection, seen as one algebraic object.
