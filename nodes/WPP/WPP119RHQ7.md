## Summary

In mathematics, an **alternating algebra** is a $\mathbf{Z}$-graded algebra for which $xy = (-1)^{\deg(x)\deg(y)}yx$ for all nonzero homogeneous elements $x$ and $y$ (i.e. it is an anticommutative algebra) and has the further property that $x^{2} = 0$ ([[nilpotence|0N9R5NN6TW]]) for every homogeneous element $x$ of odd degree.
