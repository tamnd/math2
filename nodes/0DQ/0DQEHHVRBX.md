## Summary

The slope field of a first-order equation $y' = f(x, y)$ is a picture with a short segment of slope $f(x, y)$ at many points $(x, y)$ of the plane.
The graphs of the solutions are tangent to these segments. See the [[definition|0RKQQQACY7]].
