## Summary

In algebraic geometry, a **relative cycle** is a type of algebraic cycle on a scheme. In particular, let $X$ be a scheme of finite type over a Noetherian scheme $S$, so that $X \rightarrow S$. Then a relative cycle is a cycle on $X$ which lies over the generic points of $S$, such that the cycle has a well-defined specialization to any [[fiber|5CRJ2QY8MY]] of the projection $X \rightarrow S$.(Voevodsky Suslin 2000)
