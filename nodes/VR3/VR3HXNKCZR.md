## Summary

A rheonomous constraint is a constraint of a mechanical system whose equation contains the time explicitly.
It is the opposite of a [[scleronomous constraint|Y5DPEB2N70]].
Such constraints describe parts that are moved in a prescribed way, such as a pendulum whose pivot is driven.
With rheonomous constraints the energy of the system is in general not conserved, because the driving mechanism does work.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a bead on a wire that a motor turns at a fixed rate.
The allowed positions of the bead change from moment to moment, so the rule that constrains the bead depends on the clock.
This is a rheonomous constraint.
The motor can feed energy into the bead, so its energy need not stay constant.
