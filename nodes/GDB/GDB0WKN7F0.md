## Problem

The **quadratic residuosity problem** (**QRP**) in computational number theory is to decide, given [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] $a$ and $N$, whether $a$ is a [[quadratic residue|0DKCECSYCF]] modulo $N$ or not. Here $N = p_1 p_2$ for two unknown [[primes|01826KMG2H]] $p_1$ and $p_2$, and $a$ is among the numbers which are not obviously quadratic non-residues (see below).
