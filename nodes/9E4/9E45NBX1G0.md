## Statement

In mathematics, the **local Langlands conjectures**, introduced by Robert Langlands, are part of the Langlands program. They describe a correspondence between the [[complex|09FTAET5Z4]] representations of a reductive algebraic group $G$ over a local field $F$, and representations of the Langlands group of $F$ into the $L$-group of $G$. This correspondence is not a [[bijection|0GG9TVW90M]] in general. The conjectures can be thought of as a generalization of local class field theory from [[abelian|071DDFVG4J]] [[Galois groups|0XTY86BZKY]] to non-abelian Galois groups.
