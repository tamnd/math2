## Statement

In mathematics, a **quasiperfect number** is a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] $n$ for which the sum of all its [[divisors|0PG2H7WKGG]] (the sum-of-divisors function $\sigma(n)$) is equal to $2n + 1$. Equivalently, $n$ is the sum of its non-trivial divisors (that is, its divisors excluding 1 and $n$). No quasiperfect numbers have been found so far.
