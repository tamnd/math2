## Summary

Sometimes used as a synonym of $(\square)$ and $[\square]$ for avoiding nested parentheses.

Set-builder notation for a singleton set: $\{x\}$ denotes the [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] that has $x$ as a single element.
