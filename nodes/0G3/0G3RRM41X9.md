## Summary

A Dedekind cut splits the rational numbers into a lower part and an upper part.
Each real number corresponds to exactly one cut, so the cuts give a construction of the [[real numbers|08J2M1JYAG]] from the [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]].
