## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|43C9KPSDFS]].
It covers simplicial objects in homological algebra, the derived functors of nonadditive functors, crossed modules, nonabelian cohomology, and the cohomology of small categories and of monoids.
