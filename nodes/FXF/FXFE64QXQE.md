## Statement

In number theory, a **prime quadruplet** (sometimes called a **prime quadruple**) is a set of four [[prime numbers|01826KMG2H]] of the form $\{p, p + 2, p + 6, p + 8\}.$ This represents the closest possible grouping of four primes larger than 3, and is the only prime constellation of length 4.
