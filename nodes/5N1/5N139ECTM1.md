## Summary

The Steinberg relations are three identities satisfied by the elementary matrices over any ring.
They say how two elementary matrices with the same position combine, and how the commutator of two elementary matrices looks.
Taken as defining relations, they give the Steinberg group.
The relations that hold among elementary matrices but do not follow from these three form the group $K_2$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An elementary matrix adds a multiple of one row to another row.
Doing this twice with the same rows just adds the two multiples.
Two such operations commute unless one uses as its source the row that the other changes.
In that case their commutator is again an elementary matrix, at a predictable position.
These three rules are the Steinberg relations.
