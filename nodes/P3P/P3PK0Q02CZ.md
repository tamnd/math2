## Summary

The Poynting vector is the [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] that describes the flow of electromagnetic energy.
Its direction is the direction in which energy moves, and its size is the power per unit area through a surface perpendicular to it.
It is the cross product of the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] with the magnetizing field.
It enters [[Poynting's theorem|X7XV59S3Z0]], the energy balance of electromagnetism.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Sunlight carries energy, and the Poynting vector says where that energy goes and how much arrives.
At each point it is an arrow perpendicular to both the electric and the magnetic field.
For a light beam it points along the beam, and its length is the power that hits each square metre.
Sunlight at the Earth's surface carries roughly a kilowatt per square metre at noon on a clear day.
