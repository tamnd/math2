## Problem

In number theory, **Znám's problem** asks which [[sets|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] of [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] have the property that each integer in the set is a proper divisor of the product of the other integers in the set, plus 1. Znám's problem is named after the Slovak mathematician Štefan Znám, who suggested it in 1972, although other mathematicians had considered similar problems around the same time.
