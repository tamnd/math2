## Summary

Harmonic analysis on a [[homogeneous space|85TWR6E75P]] $G / H$ studies functions on $G / H$ through the action of the group $G$.
The central question is how the representation of $G$ on $L^2(G / H)$ decomposes into irreducible [[unitary representations|74BPYJNZTM]].
For $H$ trivial this is harmonic analysis on the group $G$ itself.
For $H$ compact with $(G, H)$ a [[Gelfand pair|W38ZDXDVYC]], it leads to [[spherical functions|BKZNC76RC8]] and the spherical transform.
Classical examples are Fourier series on the circle, spherical harmonics on the sphere and analysis on the hyperbolic plane.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Many spaces look the same from every point: the circle, the sphere, the hyperbolic plane, Euclidean space.
Such a space is a homogeneous space of its symmetry group.
Harmonic analysis on it breaks functions into pieces that transform simply under the symmetries.
On the sphere $S^2 = \mathrm{SO}(3) / \mathrm{SO}(2)$ the pieces are the spherical harmonics of each degree.
