## Summary

The Swendsen–Wang algorithm is a cluster [[Monte Carlo method|DR0E829VX6]] for the [[Ising model|R0KGM32QN6]] and the [[Potts model|5GY2J6ZGXJ]].
At each step it joins equal neighbouring spins into random clusters and then gives each cluster a new random spin value.
It is based on the coupling between the Potts model and the [[Fortuin–Kasteleyn random cluster model|VCTMYPDXPX]].
Near the critical temperature it decorrelates much faster than single-spin-flip methods, because it changes large regions in one step.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Near the critical point of a magnet, the spins form large regions that point the same way.
Flipping one spin at a time, a simulation needs a very long time to turn such a region around.
The Swendsen–Wang algorithm instead links equal neighbours at random into clusters and flips whole clusters at once.
The linking probability is chosen exactly so that the result still has the correct Boltzmann distribution.
At high temperature the clusters are small, and near the critical point they can span the whole system.
