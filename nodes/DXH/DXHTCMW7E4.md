## Summary

In graph theory, the **Kneser graph** $K(n, k)$ (alternatively $KG_{n,k}$) is the [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] whose vertices correspond to the [[$k$-element subsets of a set of $n$ elements|098PHYBTV9]], and where two vertices are adjacent if and only if the two corresponding sets are [[disjoint|0W4RPA8QH3]]. Kneser graphs are named after Martin Kneser, who first investigated them in 1956.
