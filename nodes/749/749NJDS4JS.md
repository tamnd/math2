## Summary

A **pronic number** is a number that is the product of two consecutive [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]], that is, a number of the form $n(n+1)$. The study of these numbers dates back to Aristotle. They are also called **oblong numbers**, **heteromecic numbers**, or **rectangular numbers**; however, the term "rectangular number" has also been applied to the [[composite numbers|008BH74D7R]].
