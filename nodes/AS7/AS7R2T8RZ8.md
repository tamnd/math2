## Statement

Let $N_i = N_{X_i} Y|_Z$.

$$(X_1 \cdots X_r \cdot V)_Z = c(N_1) \cdots c(N_r) s(C_Z V)$$

where *s*(*C*<sub>*Z*</sub> *X*) denotes the Segre class of the normal cone to *Z* in *X* and the subscript *Z* signifies the part supported on *Z*.
