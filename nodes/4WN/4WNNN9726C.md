## Summary

An unrestricted grammar is a [[formal grammar|DP1HXZGPCM]] whose rules may replace any string containing a nonterminal by any string.
It is the most general kind of generative grammar, called type 0.
The languages generated by unrestricted grammars are exactly the [[recursively enumerable languages|67R1A5Y54R]], the languages recognised by [[Turing machines|758EYCN27B]].
So grammars of this kind are as powerful as any computer.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An unrestricted grammar may rewrite any piece of the current string, as long as that piece contains a placeholder.
Rules may also shrink the string, for example $AB \to \varepsilon$.
This freedom is enough to imitate the steps of any computer program.
As a price, there is no general method to decide whether a given word can be produced.
