## Summary

This topic is a part of [[fluid mechanics|B8CZ8SX1G5]].
It covers the flow of electrically conducting fluids coupled to magnetic fields, with the MHD equations, Alfvén waves, equilibria and stability, reconnection, and the generation of planetary and stellar magnetic fields by fluid motion.
