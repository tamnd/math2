## Summary

This topic is a part of [[manifolds and differential topology|15PK587CVP]].
It covers surgery as a geometric operation and as the main tool to classify high-dimensional manifolds in a homotopy type, with normal maps, surgery obstructions, the surgery exact sequence and the rigidity conjectures.
