## Summary

The Weyl group of a root system is the group generated by the reflections in the hyperplanes orthogonal to the roots.
It is a finite group of isometries that permutes the roots, and it is a finite Coxeter group.
The Weyl group of a semisimple Lie algebra is the Weyl group of its root system.
It controls the symmetry of weights and appears in the Weyl character formula.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take a root system and put a mirror orthogonal to each root.
Reflect in one mirror, then in another, and so on: all the motions you can reach form the Weyl group.
These motions shuffle the roots among themselves, and there are only finitely many of them.
For the hexagon system $A_2$, the Weyl group has six elements and is the symmetry group of an equilateral triangle.
