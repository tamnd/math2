## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers abstract vector spaces over a field, subspaces, linear combinations and span, linear independence, bases, coordinates and dimension, in finite and in infinite dimension.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Vector space|000AYPA9AJ]]: the eight axioms over a field, checked on examples and non-examples.
2. [[Coordinate space F^n|713F6AM1X1]]: lists of $n$ scalars with entrywise operations.
3. [[Space of matrices|0RE6X3BGPN]]: $M_{m \times n}(F)$ and its basis of matrix units.
4. [[Space of polynomials|0JGZCDT5DN]]: $F[x]$ and $P_n(F)$, and polynomials versus functions.
5. [[Spaces of functions and sequences|0GZB2SN0PH]]: $F^S$, finitely supported functions and continuous functions.
6. [[Subspace|00YC1D652K]]: the subspace test and the zero vector trap.
7. [[Intersection and union of subspaces|0V33SPH7RR]]: intersections are subspaces, unions of two usually are not.
8. [[Sum of subspaces|0JMTY8MF54]]: $U + W$ as the smallest subspace that contains both.
9. [[Linear combination|0V8F3E48E6]]: finite sums of scalar multiples.
10. [[Span|0DTY8B9HMS]]: all linear combinations, and the smallest subspace that contains a set.
11. [[Spanning set|088RYKAWK9]]: sets that span, and the pivot test in $F^m$.
12. [[Linear independence|0CRMGPAH83]]: only the trivial relation, sets versus families, and the dependence lemma.
13. [[Linear dependence of the columns of a matrix|76GG9B25A1]]: the column test by row reduction.
14. [[Basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]]: independent spanning families, unique coordinates, and the empty basis of the zero space.
15. [[Standard basis|C5QA672NPH]]: $e_1, \dots, e_n$, monomials and matrix units.
16. [[Coordinate vector|04CYQHN6MC]]: $[v]_B$ and the isomorphism $V \cong F^n$.
17. [[Steinitz exchange lemma|08ZDRN6H41]]: independent lists are not longer than spanning lists.
18. [[Dimension theorem|0WK0Y8RH1M]]: all bases have the same size, in finite and infinite dimension.
19. [[Dimension|03GSZ8RTZJ]]: the size of a basis, as a number or a cardinal.
20. [[Finite-dimensional vector space|0ZHWKQMVX5]]: a finite spanning set, and equivalent conditions.
21. [[Every spanning set contains a basis|06MRNP4BNT]]: remove redundant vectors, or use Zorn's lemma.
22. [[Extension of an independent set to a basis|0M4SXE5WW3]]: add new directions, or use Zorn's lemma.
23. [[Every vector space has a basis|00MS9VMQ9Z]]: Zorn's lemma, and Blass's converse.
24. [[Hamel basis|ZCBD0GRGVA]]: a basis of $\R$ over $\Q$, of size continuum.
25. [[Dimension of a subspace|0QW084460E]]: $\dim W \le \dim V$, and equality in finite dimension.
26. [[Right-size criterion for a basis|096V7H13H9]]: $n$ independent or $n$ spanning vectors in dimension $n$.
27. [[Dimension formula for sums of subspaces|06HTJNCMPA]]: Grassmann's formula, and its failure for three subspaces.
28. [[Vector spaces over finite fields|J9VE1X14PV]]: $q^n$ vectors and the count of ordered bases.
29. [[Complexification and realification|CT2F31EDH8]]: $V_\C$, $W_\R$ and complex structures.

### Prerequisites

- [[Field|04C5ZBG1WT]]: the scalars, including $\Q$, $\R$, $\C$ and $\mathbb{F}_q$.
- [[Vectors in the plane and in space|VKVAJ94SKZ]]: vectors in coordinates.
- [[Systems of linear equations|DXSXFVDMXQ]]: row reduction and pivots, used for the column tests.
- [[Cardinal number|0EBACQFAZW]] and [[Zorn's lemma|04RXTQA5ZA]]: for the infinite-dimensional results.
