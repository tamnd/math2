## Summary

In commutative algebra, the **constructible topology** on the spectrum $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ of a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]] $A$ is a topology where each [[closed set|0G5AV9Q4RR]] is the [[image|J5DEEKRRCY]] of $\operatorname{Spec} (B)$ in $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ for some algebra *B* over *A*. An important feature of this construction is that the map $\operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A)$ is a closed map with respect to the constructible topology.
