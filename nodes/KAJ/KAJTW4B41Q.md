## Summary

The osculating orbit of a body at an instant is the Kepler orbit it would follow if the other forces were switched off then.
Only the main central attraction is kept.
It has the same position and velocity as the true motion at that time.
Its orbital elements, the osculating elements, change slowly under perturbations, and following their change is the classical way to study perturbed motion.
See the [[definition|DE8R41FXZD]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A satellite around the Earth feels small extra forces, so its path is not quite an ellipse.
At each moment one can still draw the ellipse it would follow if the extra forces vanished.
That ellipse touches the true path at the current point, much as an osculating circle touches a curve.
As time goes on the ellipse slowly turns and changes shape.
