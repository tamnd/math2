## Statement

In mathematics, the **Mertens conjecture** is the statement that the Mertens function $M(n)$ is bounded by $\pm\sqrt{n}$. Although now disproven, it had been shown to imply the Riemann hypothesis. It was conjectured by Thomas Joannes Stieltjes, in an 1885 letter to Charles Hermite (reprinted in (txt)), and again in print by (txt), and disproved by (txt). It is a striking example of a mathematical conjecture proven false despite a large amount of computational evidence in its favor.
