## Summary

A closed trapped surface is a compact two-dimensional spacelike surface in a spacetime such that light sent out from it, both outwards and inwards, converges.
Its area decreases in every future null direction normal to it.
A trapped surface is a key hypothesis of the [[Penrose–Hawking singularity theorems|WP4YAHJ6V4]].
In many settings it signals that a [[black hole|YHJ0PDNY8T]] has formed.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a sphere that flashes light at one instant, both outwards and inwards.
In flat space, the outgoing flash forms a growing sphere and the ingoing flash a shrinking one.
Near a very compact mass, gravity is so strong that even the outgoing flash forms a shrinking sphere.
A surface where both flashes shrink is called trapped.
Inside the event horizon of a Schwarzschild black hole, every round sphere centred on the hole is trapped.
