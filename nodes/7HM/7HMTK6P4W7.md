## Summary

A Pfister form is a [[quadratic form|0RBQS1JJCR]] that is a tensor product of binary forms $\langle 1, -a \rangle$.
An $n$-fold Pfister form has dimension $2^n$.
Pfister forms are multiplicative: the nonzero values they take form a group.
They generate the powers of the fundamental ideal of the [[Witt ring|KFVY0RPGF2]], and they are the forms that correspond to symbols in Milnor K-theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The sum of two squares $x^2 + y^2$ has a special property: a product of two sums of two squares is again a sum of two squares.
The same holds for sums of four squares and of eight squares.
Pfister forms are the quadratic forms that have such a product formula, in a general form found by Pfister.
They are built by doubling: from a form $\varphi$ and a nonzero $a$ one gets $\varphi \perp (-a)\varphi$, twice as large.
For instance, $x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + x_4^2$ is a $2$-fold Pfister form, the norm form of the quaternions.
