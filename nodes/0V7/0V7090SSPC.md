## Summary

A regular curve is a continuously differentiable [[parametrised curve|0G7WM8DX4P]] whose [[tangent vector|0ZCZK72B45]] is never zero.
At each point a regular curve has a well-defined tangent line, and it can be reparametrised by arc length.
