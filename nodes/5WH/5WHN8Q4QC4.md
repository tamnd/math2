## Summary

Hypersonic flow is gas flow at very high [[Mach number|GFHJ858AN1]], conventionally above about $5$.
At such speeds the [[shock waves|FRVV0NHBXT]] lie close to the body and the gas behind them becomes very hot.
Chemical and thermal effects in the air then matter.
Its theory has its own approximations, such as hypersonic similarity for slender bodies.
Another is the independence of some quantities from the Mach number when it is very large.
It is the regime of spacecraft re-entry and of very fast missiles.
See the [[definition|8ARQCQS4FT]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A body flying much faster than sound pushes the air ahead of it into a thin, very hot layer just in front of its nose.
The shock wave hugs the body instead of standing far ahead of it.
A capsule returning from orbit enters the atmosphere at more than twenty times the speed of sound.
The air around it gets hot enough to glow, which is why capsules need heat shields.
A passenger jet, at less than the speed of sound, is far from this regime.
