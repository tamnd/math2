## Summary

A Freudenthal triple system is a vector space with a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form and a symmetric trilinear product.
The two are tied together by an identity of degree four.
The main example has dimension $56$ and is built from the [[Albert algebra|1MSMVDRAVD]].
Its automorphism group is of type $E_7$, so these systems give a uniform way to construct the [[exceptional Lie algebra E7|9YFX87K2VY]] and related Lie algebras.
They sit next to [[Jordan triple systems|M13DZ6YXH1]] in the family of structures behind the [[Freudenthal magic square|ANYDPD4HXZ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Start with a [[symplectic vector space|PQ7JJY0VPH]], a space where any two vectors have an "area" $\ip{x}{y}$ with $\ip{x}{y} = -\ip{y}{x}$.
Add a way to combine three vectors into a fourth one, which does not care about the order of the three inputs.
The two pieces must fit together through one identity of degree four.
The trivial product, which sends every triple to $0$, already gives an example.
The interesting example lives in $56$ dimensions, and its symmetries form the exceptional group $E_7$.
