## Statement

In mathematics, the **Weil conjecture on Tamagawa numbers** is the statement that the Tamagawa number $\tau(G)$ of a simply connected simple algebraic group defined over a number field is 1. In this case, *simply connected* means "not having a proper *algebraic* covering" in the algebraic group theory sense, which is not always [[the topologists' meaning|0QW64ARSHB]].
