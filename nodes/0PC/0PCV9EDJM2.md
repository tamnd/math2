## Summary

A multinomial coefficient counts the ways to distribute $n$ distinct objects into labelled boxes of given sizes. It extends the binomial coefficient from two boxes to any number of boxes. The node [[multinomial coefficient|00K0RF9BFY]] gives the precise definition.
