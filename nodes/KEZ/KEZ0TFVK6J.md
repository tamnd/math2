## Statement

Let $\Delta \subset \C^n$ be a [[polydisc|DJBWP7D3M8]]. Here each factor may be a disc of finite radius or of infinite radius, so $\Delta$ may be unbounded. For example, $\C^n$ itself is such a polydisc.

Then the [[first Cousin problem|537J79VYQR]] is solvable on $\Delta$. That is, every system of Cousin I data on $\Delta$ has a global solution.

## Remarks

The case of bounded polydiscs is the classical setting of Cousin's own work. The statement here extends it to all radii, finite or infinite.
