## Summary

A phylogenetic network is a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] that describes evolutionary relationships when they are not tree-like.
It generalizes the [[phylogenetic tree|RK58WSHJSG]] by allowing vertices with more than one parent.
Such vertices model reticulate events such as hybridization, horizontal gene transfer and recombination.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a family tree of species, each species has one parent species.
Sometimes two lineages merge: two plant species hybridize, or a bacterium picks up genes from another one.
A phylogenetic network draws this as a vertex with two incoming edges, one from each parent lineage.
A network with no such merging vertex is just an ordinary rooted tree.
