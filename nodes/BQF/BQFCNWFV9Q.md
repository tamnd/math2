## Summary

The electromagnetic four-potential is a four-vector field on spacetime that combines the [[electric potential|SAEDXNM3Y1]] and the [[magnetic vector potential|8PEMR2J7EF]].
The [[electromagnetic tensor|WRZJRJS44W]] is its antisymmetrized derivative, so the field can be recovered from it.
It is not unique: adding the gradient of any function gives the same field, and this freedom is gauge symmetry.
It is the basic variable of the Lagrangian formulation of electromagnetism and of quantum electrodynamics.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Instead of working with the six components of the electric and magnetic fields, one can work with four functions from which both fields follow by differentiation.
These four functions form the four-potential: one is the electric potential, the voltage, and three form the vector potential.
Several different four-potentials give the same fields, a bit like several different altitude maps with different sea levels give the same slopes.
In quantum mechanics the four-potential, rather than the fields, is what enters the equations of a charged particle.
