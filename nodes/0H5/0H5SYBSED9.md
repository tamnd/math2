## Summary

The gamma function extends the factorial to real arguments. For $s > 0$ it is an improper integral, and it satisfies $\Gamma(n + 1) = n!$ for natural numbers $n$. The node [[gamma function|0V3FZMVB4K]] gives the precise definition.
