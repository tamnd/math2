## Summary

In mathematics, a **Jacobi form** is an automorphic form on the Jacobi group, which is the [[semidirect product|014E1DZQE8]] of the symplectic group Sp(n;R) and the Heisenberg group $H^{(n,h)}_R$. The theory was first systematically studied by (Eichler Zagier 1985).
