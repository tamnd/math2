## Summary

A couple is a pair of forces of equal size and opposite direction that act along different parallel lines.
Its total force is zero, so it does not push the body along, but it has a nonzero [[torque|9636MFFSGC]] that tends to turn the body.
This torque, the moment of the couple, is the same about every point.
Every system of forces on a rigid body is equivalent to a single force at a chosen point together with a couple.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Turning a steering wheel with both hands, one pushing up and one pulling down, is a couple.
The two pushes cancel as far as moving the wheel sideways is concerned, but together they turn it.
Two forces of $5$ newtons in opposite directions on lines $0.4$ metres apart give a turning effect of $2$ newton metres, wherever the pivot is.
