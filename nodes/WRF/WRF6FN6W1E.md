## Definition

Let $R$ be a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] with identity and let $n \ge 2$.
For $1 \le i, j \le n$ with $i \ne j$ and $r \in R$, let $e_{ij}(r) \in GL_n(R)$ be the [[elementary matrix|01REXW3WWE]] with $1$ on the diagonal, $r$ in position $(i, j)$, and $0$ elsewhere.
The *elementary subgroup* $E_n(R)$ is the [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] of the [[general linear group|0FCHE902D7]] $GL_n(R)$ generated by all $e_{ij}(r)$.
The *stable elementary subgroup* $E(R)$ is the union of the $E_n(R)$ inside the [[stable general linear group|G8R9561PXT]] $GL(R)$.

## Remarks

The generators satisfy $e_{ij}(r) e_{ij}(s) = e_{ij}(r + s)$, so $e_{ij}(r)^{-1} = e_{ij}(-r)$.
Whitehead's lemma: $E(R)$ is equal to the commutator subgroup $[GL(R), GL(R)]$.
Example: over $\Z$, the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = e_{12}(1)\, e_{21}(-1)\, e_{12}(1)$ lies in $E_2(\Z)$, as multiplying out the right side shows.
Non-example: $\mathrm{diag}(-1, 1) \in GL_2(\Z)$ is not in $E_2(\Z)$, since every $e_{ij}(r)$ has determinant $1$ and this matrix has determinant $-1$.
Only the row operations "add a multiple of one row to another" are used; scaling a row and swapping two rows are not among the generators.
