## Summary

Problem solving is the work of finding and proving answers to mathematical questions that the solver has not met before.
A problem differs from an exercise: an exercise practises a known method, and a problem asks the solver to find a method.
Competitions use such problems to find and train talented students, and many research mathematicians took part in competitions when they were young.
Hungary started a national competition for school students in 1894, and the first International Mathematical Olympiad took place in Romania in 1959.
This topic collects the problem solving and competitions pages of math2.

Reasoning and proof, mathematics education and recreational mathematics have their own topics.
