## Summary

A Lyapunov function for an equilibrium of a differential equation is a scalar function with three properties. It is zero at the equilibrium, positive away from it, and non-increasing along solutions.
Its existence proves stability without solving the equation. It is named after Aleksandr Lyapunov. See the [[definition|04P7B5XC22]].
