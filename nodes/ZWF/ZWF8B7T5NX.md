## Summary

Streamlines, streaklines and pathlines are three families of curves that picture a flow.
A streamline is tangent to the velocity at one instant.
A pathline is the track of one fluid parcel.
A streakline joins all parcels that passed through one fixed point.
In a steady flow the three families coincide; in an unsteady flow they differ.
They are basic tools of flow visualization and of the kinematics of fluids, linking the [[Lagrangian and Eulerian descriptions|5E6K4ATM9W]] of a flow.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take a photograph of a flow with a short exposure: the little streaks you see line up along the streamlines.
Drop one speck of dust and follow it with a long exposure: you see its pathline.
Release dye continuously from a fixed nozzle: the dye line you see at a given moment is a streakline.
When the wind blows the same way all the time, the smoke from a chimney traces all three at once.
