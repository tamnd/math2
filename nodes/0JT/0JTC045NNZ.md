## Summary

The Legendre symbol $\left(\frac{a}{p}\right)$ takes the value $1$, $-1$ or $0$, and it tells whether $a$ is a square modulo an odd prime $p$.
The [[definition of the Legendre symbol|01J8AMV9RS]] gives the precise form.
