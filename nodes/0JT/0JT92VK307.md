## Summary

A Bernstein polynomial of a function on $[0, 1]$ is a weighted average of its values at the points $k/n$. For a continuous function, these polynomials converge uniformly to the function. The node [[Bernstein polynomial|0C4VWBFY73]] gives the precise definition.
