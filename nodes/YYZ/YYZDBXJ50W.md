## Summary

In algebra, an **augmentation** of an associative algebra *A* over a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]] *k* is a *k*-algebra homomorphism $A \to k$, typically denoted by ε. An algebra together with an augmentation is called an **augmented algebra**. The kernel of the augmentation is a two-sided ideal called the augmentation ideal of *A*.
