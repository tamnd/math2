## Summary

This topic is a part of [[numbers and arithmetic|7V49498ENC]].
It covers the number systems that extend the complex numbers or change their rules: the two-dimensional real algebras, quaternions as vectors and rotations, octonions and the Cayley-Dickson construction, and the theorems that say that these are all.
