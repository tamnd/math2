## Definition

Let $G$ be a group and let $R$ be a ring.
Let $BG$ be the [[classifying space|WSZ40H5E4T]] of $G$ and let $R[G]$ be the [[group ring|4NMTSX0XDS]].
Write $K(-)$ for the [[K-theory spectrum|2C1M0NWPB0]] and $\Omega^\infty_0$ for the base point component of the infinite loop space.
The inclusion $G \to GL_1(\Z[G])$ gives a map $BG \to BGL(\Z[G])^+ \simeq \Omega^\infty_0 K(\Z[G])$.
Its adjoint is a map of spectra $\Sigma^\infty BG_+ \to K(\Z[G])$.
The *assembly map* is the composite map of spectra
$$BG_+ \wedge K(R) \to K(\Z[G]) \wedge K(R) \to K(\Z[G] \otimes R) = K(R[G]),$$
where the second map is the product in K-theory and $\wedge$ is the [[smash product|2ZJ2G308QV]].
On homotopy groups it gives homomorphisms $H_n(BG; K(R)) \to K_n(R[G])$.

## Remarks

Example: for $G$ trivial, $BG_+ = S^0$ and the assembly map is the identity of $K(R)$.
Example: for $R = \Z$ and $n = 1$, the image of the assembly map is generated by the classes of $\pm g$ for $g \in G$, and its cokernel is the [[Whitehead group|8SY3AC4KW3]] $Wh(G)$.
Non-example of an isomorphism: for $G = \Z/23$ and $R = \Z$, the image on $K_0$ is $\Z$, but $K_0(\Z[\Z/23])$ also contains the class group of $\Z[e^{2\pi i/23}]$, which has order $3$.
Davis and Lück defined assembly maps $H^G_n(E; \mathbf{K}_R) \to K_n(R[G])$ for every $G$-CW complex $E$, induced by the map $E \to \mathrm{pt}$.
The map above is the case $E = EG$; the Farrell–Jones conjecture uses $E$ built from virtually cyclic subgroups.
The same construction works for L-theory of group rings with involution.
