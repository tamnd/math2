## Example

We use $K = \mathrm{Cn}(p \wedge q)$ and the ranking $11 < 10 \approx 01 < 00$ of [[the revision on two atoms|CC604TAP5T]].
We compute the contraction $K \div p$ by the Harper identity, and then recover the revision $K * \neg p$ by the Levi identity of [[AGM belief revision|Y05YQN7HGQ]].

1. The models of $\neg p$ are $01$ and $00$, and the minimal one is $01$.
   So $K * \neg p = \mathrm{Cn}(\neg p \wedge q)$.
2. The Harper identity says $K \div p = K \cap (K * \neg p)$.
   The models of an intersection of belief sets form the union of their sets of models.
3. So $\mathrm{Mod}(K \div p) = \{11\} \cup \{01\} = \{11, 01\}$, and $K \div p = \mathrm{Cn}(q)$.
4. Success: $p \notin \mathrm{Cn}(q)$, since $01$ satisfies $q$ and not $p$.
5. Inclusion: $\mathrm{Cn}(q) \subseteq \mathrm{Cn}(p \wedge q) = K$.
6. Recovery: $(K \div p) + p = \mathrm{Cn}(q \wedge p) = K$, so $K \subseteq (K \div p) + p$.
7. The Levi identity gives $(K \div \neg\neg p) + \neg p = (K \div p) + \neg p = \mathrm{Cn}(q \wedge \neg p)$, where extensionality replaces $\neg\neg p$ by $p$.
8. This equals $K * \neg p$ from step 1, so the two identities fit together.

## Remarks

The contraction keeps $q$ because the most plausible world without $p$ still has $q$.
With the ranking $11 < 01 < 10 \approx 00$ the result is the same, since only the minimal $\neg p$-worlds matter.
With the ranking $11 < 10 \approx 00 < 01$ the minimal $\neg p$-world is $00$, and $K \div p = \mathrm{Cn}(p \leftrightarrow q)$: the agent keeps only the link between $p$ and $q$.
Recovery holds in both cases, since adding $p$ back restores $K$.

Watch out: $K \div p$ is not $K \cap \mathrm{Cn}(\neg p)$ in general.
That is full meet contraction, and here it has the models $\{11\} \cup \{01, 00\}$.
So it gives $\mathrm{Cn}(p \to q)$ and loses $q$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

To stop believing "Tweety is a bird", we look at the most believable situation where Tweety is not a bird.
There Tweety still flies, say because Tweety is a toy plane.
So after giving up "bird", we still believe "flies".
If we later learn "bird" again, we get back exactly our old beliefs.

### L2 human

To contract by $\varphi$, add the minimal $\neg\varphi$-models to the models of $K$.
To revise by $\varphi$, contract by $\neg\varphi$ and then add $\varphi$, which keeps only the new models that satisfy $\varphi$.
In our case the contraction by $p$ adds $01$ to $\{11\}$, and the revision by $\neg p$ then keeps $01$ alone.
The typical use of the identities is to define one operation from the other, when one of them is easier to specify.
