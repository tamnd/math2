## Statement

The Ruzsa covering lemma states the following:

Let $A$ and $S$ be finite subsets of an [[abelian group|071DDFVG4J]] with $S$ nonempty, and let $K$ be a positive real number. Then if $|A+S| \le K|S|$, there is a subset $T$ of $A$ with at most $K$ elements such that $A \subseteq T+S-S$.
