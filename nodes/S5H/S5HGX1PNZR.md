## Summary

Cournot competition is a model of an industry in which firms selling the same good choose their quantities at the same time.
The market price is the one at which buyers take the total quantity.
Its solution, the Cournot equilibrium, is a [[Nash equilibrium|745WYP9YJ0]] of this quantity-setting game.
Antoine Augustin Cournot introduced the model in 1838.
The equilibrium lies between monopoly and perfect competition and approaches the competitive outcome as the number of firms grows.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Two oil producers each decide how many barrels to pump, without knowing what the other will do.
The more they pump in total, the lower the price they both get.
Each producer picks the amount that is best given what it expects the other to pump, and an equilibrium is a pair of amounts where both expectations are right.
For example, if the price is $10$ minus the total quantity and production is free, each producer pumps $10/3$ and the price is $10/3$.
