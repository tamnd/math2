## Summary

The inverse hyperbolic functions are the inverses of the hyperbolic sine, cosine and tangent, with a restricted domain for the cosine. They have explicit formulas in terms of the logarithm and the square root. The node [[inverse hyperbolic functions|04D7CHT5V1]] gives the precise definition.
