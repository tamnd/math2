## Summary

This topic is a part of [[set theory|G9W9J6KPAR]].
It covers large cardinal axioms in general and the large cardinals that are compatible with the axiom of constructibility, such as inaccessible, Mahlo, weakly compact, indescribable and partition cardinals.
