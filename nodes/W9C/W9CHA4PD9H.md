## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers sequences and sets that formulas cannot distinguish, the Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski method that builds models from them, and the two-cardinal theorems.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Indiscernible sequence|4016QKAHFB]]: sequences whose increasing tuples of the same length have the same type.
2. [[Existence of indiscernibles|RG6D2FAS14]]: Ramsey and Erdős-Rado give indiscernible sequences in models of every theory with infinite models.
3. [[Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski models|TSGB29FJM0]]: models generated by indiscernibles, with many automorphisms and few types.
4. [[Generalized indiscernibles|RQS808ZC8T]]: indiscernibles indexed by structures other than linear orders.
5. [[Vaught's two-cardinal theorem|XT227BYP7B]]: any gap between two sizes gives the gap between countable and the first uncountable size.
6. [[Chang's two-cardinal theorem|4STP69KESQ]]: the gap moves up to $\kappa^+$ and $\kappa$ when $\kappa^{<\kappa} = \kappa$.
7. [[Hanf number|A9XBRF3A7Z]]: the size threshold beyond which a sentence has arbitrarily large models.
