## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers sequences and sets that formulas cannot distinguish, the Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski method that builds models from them, and the two-cardinal theorems.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Indiscernible sequence|~cc1977c7]]: sequences whose increasing tuples of the same length have the same type.
2. [[Existence of indiscernibles|~c0b513d4]]: Ramsey and Erdős-Rado give indiscernible sequences in models of every theory with infinite models.
3. [[Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski models|~85a7510c]]: models generated by indiscernibles, with many automorphisms and few types.
4. [[Generalized indiscernibles|~9c5e0903]]: indiscernibles indexed by structures other than linear orders.
5. [[Vaught's two-cardinal theorem|~cdfd261d]]: any gap between two sizes gives the gap between countable and the first uncountable size.
6. [[Chang's two-cardinal theorem|~ed1f54df]]: the gap moves up to $\kappa^+$ and $\kappa$ when $\kappa^{<\kappa} = \kappa$.
7. [[Hanf number|~506bb18e]]: the size threshold beyond which a sentence has arbitrarily large models.
