## Statement

Let $m$ and $n$ be [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], that is, nonnegative integers.
If their [[sum|0S13V32CPB]] $m + n$ equals $0$, then $m = 0$ and $n = 0$.

## Remarks

Thus $0$ is the only natural number that can be written as a sum of two natural numbers in this way, and both terms must be $0$.
