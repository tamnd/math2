## Summary

The Bel–Robinson tensor is a four-index tensor built quadratically from the [[Weyl tensor|SBHY31HESP]] of a four-dimensional [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]].
It is totally symmetric and trace-free, and it is divergence-free when the spacetime is a [[vacuum solution|JRV1T69FYJ]].
It gives non-negative numbers when evaluated on future-pointing causal vectors.
For these reasons it plays the role of an energy density for the gravitational field itself.
It is used in energy estimates, such as those behind the [[stability of Minkowski space|PH4Z47SCK6]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

In general relativity there is no good local energy density of gravity.
The Bel–Robinson tensor is the best substitute: it is the square of the curvature that remains in empty space.
In the same way, the energy of an electric field is made from the field squared.
It measures how strongly tidal forces act, and it never becomes negative for an observer moving slower than light.
In flat Minkowski space the Weyl tensor is zero, so the Bel–Robinson tensor is zero too.
