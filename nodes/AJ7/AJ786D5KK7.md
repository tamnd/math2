## Summary

Branch and cut is [[branch and bound|ZZ7C50WN56]] for [[integer programs|9GX3WH5EQF]] and [[mixed-integer linear programs|3PX6EV34QK]] with extra cuts.
At the nodes of the search tree, the [[linear programming relaxation|PYMHHM5Z6F]] is tightened by [[cutting planes|PR7VMJN5BH]].
The cuts raise the quality of the bounds, so more of the tree is pruned, while branching guarantees termination when cuts alone stall.
It is the method behind modern commercial and academic MILP solvers.
Large instances of the travelling salesman problem were solved exactly with it, using cuts derived from the structure of the tour polytope.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Branch and bound splits a hard problem into smaller ones, and cutting planes sharpen the easy approximations used to judge them.
Branch and cut does both.
At each step of the search it first adds cuts to get a better estimate, and it splits the problem only when the cuts no longer help much.
Better estimates mean that many parts of the search can be skipped, so far fewer pieces must be examined.
