## Definition

Let $\Sigma$ be a closed three-manifold and let $\Psi$ be a functional of [[Riemannian metrics|5HDG31YEPW]] $h_{ij}$ on $\Sigma$.
Let $R$ be the scalar curvature of $h$, $\Lambda$ the cosmological constant, and
$$G_{ijkl} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{h}} \left( h_{ik} h_{jl} + h_{il} h_{jk} - h_{ij} h_{kl} \right)$$
the DeWitt supermetric, where $h = \det h_{ij}$.
The *Wheeler–DeWitt equation* (in units $c = 1$) is
$$\left( -16 \pi G \hbar^2 \, G_{ijkl} \frac{\delta^2}{\delta h_{ij} \, \delta h_{kl}} - \frac{\sqrt{h}}{16 \pi G} (R - 2\Lambda) \right) \Psi[h] = 0$$
at every point of $\Sigma$.
It is obtained from the [[Hamiltonian constraint|D87R3GJMH6]] $16 \pi G \, G_{ijkl} \pi^{ij} \pi^{kl} - \frac{\sqrt{h}}{16\pi G}(R - 2\Lambda) = 0$ of the [[ADM formalism|CFAFAPV7HB]] by the [[canonical quantization|7NW2C10YSR]] rule $\pi^{ij} \mapsto -i\hbar \, \delta / \delta h_{ij}$.

## Remarks

The wave function must also satisfy the momentum constraint $\nabla_j \left( \delta \Psi / \delta h_{ij} \right) = 0$, which says that $\Psi$ is invariant under diffeomorphisms of $\Sigma$.
The product of two functional derivatives at the same point is ill defined, and the ordering of $G_{ijkl}$ and the derivatives is ambiguous, so the equation is formal.
Example (minisuperspace): restricting to round three-spheres of radius $a$ reduces the equation to an ordinary differential equation in $a$, which is the setting of the Hartle–Hawking state and other proposals.
Non-example: the time-dependent equation $i \hbar \, \partial_t \Psi = \hat H \Psi$ is not of this form; here $\hat H \Psi = 0$ and no external time appears.
