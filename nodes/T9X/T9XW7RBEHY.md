## Summary

The Cayley–Dickson construction doubles an [[algebra|1SENAHZW5A]] with an [[involution|GP56NBN9RY]] to an algebra of twice the dimension with a new involution.
Starting from $\R$, it produces in turn $\C$, the [[quaternions|0GE4DVQKR8]], the [[octonions|1HRMT3DSX2]] and the [[sedenions|YADT1TBZ5A]].
From $\C$ on, each step loses a property.
Commutativity is lost at the quaternions, associativity at the octonions, and the alternative law and division at the sedenions.
With a parameter in the field it also produces the split forms.
Over a field of characteristic not $2$ it produces all unital [[composition algebras|1Q9G6AST10]], by [[Hurwitz's theorem|WZ81H3S4KK]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A complex number is a pair of real numbers with a clever product.
The same trick, applied to pairs of complex numbers, gives the quaternions.
Applied to pairs of quaternions it gives the octonions, and the process can go on forever.
Each time, the new number system is twice as large but obeys fewer of the usual rules.
