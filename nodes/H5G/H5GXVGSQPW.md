## Summary

In [[combinatorial mathematics|0JKW7W7XFR]], a **Latin rectangle** is an $r \times n$ [[matrix|0VD9ZSWWJG]] (where $r \le n$), using $n$ symbols, usually the numbers $1, 2, 3, ..., n$ or $0, 1, ..., n - 1$ as its entries, with no number occurring more than once in any row or column.
