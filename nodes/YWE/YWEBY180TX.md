## Statement

In number theory, a **Sierpiński number** is an odd [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] *k* such that $k \times 2^n + 1$ is [[composite|008BH74D7R]] for all natural numbers *n*. In 1960, Wacław Sierpiński proved that there are [[infinitely many|YZR3KZMQNZ]] odd [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] *k* which have this property.
