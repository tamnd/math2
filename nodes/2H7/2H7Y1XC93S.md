## Summary

The Grothendieck group of an [[abelian category|Y9CZ3MTNF1]] is generated by the isomorphism classes of objects.
It has a relation $[B] = [A] + [C]$ for each [[short exact sequence|KJKNJMGKZZ]] $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$.
It is the universal way to turn an additive invariant of objects, such as dimension, length or Euler characteristic, into a group homomorphism.
It was introduced by Grothendieck for coherent sheaves in his work on the Riemann–Roch theorem.
For the category of finitely generated modules over a Noetherian ring it gives the group $G_0$ of [[G-theory|YZSR4QKDQQ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a category of modules, a module that is built from a submodule and a quotient should count as their sum.
The Grothendieck group makes this rule exact: every short exact sequence turns into an equation.
For finite-dimensional vector spaces this recovers the dimension, and the group is $\Z$.
For finitely generated abelian groups, the relation $0 \to \Z \to \Z \to \Z/n\Z \to 0$, where the first map is multiplication by $n$, forces $[\Z/n\Z] = 0$, and the group is $\Z$, given by the rank.
