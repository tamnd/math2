## Summary

In cosmology, recombination is the epoch when free electrons and nuclei first combined into neutral atoms, mainly hydrogen.
It happened when the universe had cooled to about $3000$ K, at a redshift of about $1100$, some $380\,000$ years after the Big Bang.
Soon after it, photons stopped scattering on free electrons, and the light released then is the cosmic microwave background.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The early universe was a hot glowing gas of nuclei, electrons and light, in which light could not travel far without hitting an electron.
As the universe expanded and cooled, electrons were captured by protons to form hydrogen atoms.
Once most electrons were bound, the gas became transparent, like fog clearing.
The light that was then set free still reaches us today, stretched into microwaves.
The word "re" is historical: the atoms had never been combined before.
