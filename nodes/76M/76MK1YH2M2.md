## Definition

Let $G$ be a connected [[semisimple Lie group|XJS07AJ50Y]] with finite centre, with Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, and let $K$ be a [[maximal compact subgroup|T2EB8R9HEV]].
Let $\mathfrak g = \mathfrak k \oplus \mathfrak p$ be the corresponding Cartan decomposition, and let $\norm{\cdot}$ be the norm on $\mathfrak p$ given by the Killing form.
By the [[Cartan decomposition|A5JB9JQKR0]] each $g \in G$ can be written $g = k \exp X$ with $k \in K$ and $X \in \mathfrak p$ unique; put $\sigma(g) = \norm{X}$.
Let $\Xi = \varphi_0$ be the spherical function of parameter $0$ given by [[Harish-Chandra's formula|A6MQCW28KZ]].
Elements $D$ of the [[universal enveloping algebra|SCZ8PRVY00]] $U(\mathfrak g_\C)$ act on [[smooth functions|0V33BNKZ9S]] on $G$ as left-invariant differential operators $f \mapsto f D$ and as right-invariant differential operators $f \mapsto D f$.
*Harish-Chandra's Schwartz space* $\mathcal{C}(G)$ is the set of smooth functions $f \colon G \to \C$ such that
$$\sup_{g \in G} \, \abs{(D f E)(g)} \, \Xi(g)^{-1} \, (1 + \sigma(g))^{r} < \infty$$
for all $D, E \in U(\mathfrak g_\C)$ and all $r \ge 0$.

## Remarks

The suprema in the definition are seminorms, and they make $\mathcal{C}(G)$ a Fréchet space.
It is stable under left and right translations and under convolution, and it is contained in $L^2(G)$.

Example: every smooth function with compact support lies in $\mathcal{C}(G)$, since $\Xi$ is continuous and positive, so every supremum is over a compact set.

Non-example: $\Xi$ itself is not in $\mathcal{C}(G)$.
With $D = E = 1$ and $r = 1$ the quantity is $\sup_g (1 + \sigma(g))$, which is infinite because $\sigma$ is unbounded on the noncompact group $G$.
