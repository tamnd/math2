## Summary

Let $f$ be an irreducible polynomial over a field $K$.
To adjoin a root of $f$ to $K$, one forms the field $K[x]/(f)$, in which the class of $x$ is a root of $f$.
The [[definition of this construction|0M893X7YY7]] gives the details.
