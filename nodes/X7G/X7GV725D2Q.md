## Definition

Model the lithosphere as a thin elastic plate of thickness $h$, Young's modulus $E$ and Poisson's ratio $\nu$.
The plate rests on a fluid mantle of density $\rho_m$ under gravity $g$.
The depression is filled by material of density $\rho_i < \rho_m$.
Let $q(x)$ be a vertical load per unit area, depending on one horizontal coordinate $x$.
The *flexure* of the lithosphere is the downward deflection $w(x)$ of the plate that satisfies
$$D \frac{d^4 w}{dx^4} + (\rho_m - \rho_i) g \, w = q(x), \qquad D = \frac{E h^3}{12 (1 - \nu^2)},$$
and tends to $0$ far from the load.
The number $D$ is the *flexural rigidity*.

## Remarks

The equation is the [[Kirchhoff–Love plate equation|9E6E0SCEYA]] in one dimension, or the [[Euler–Bernoulli beam equation|13FGKM7NQ0]] with an elastic foundation, the buoyancy of the mantle acting as a restoring force.
Let $a = \big( 4D/((\rho_m - \rho_i) g) \big)^{1/4}$ be the flexural parameter.
Example: take a line load $V_0$ per unit length at $x = 0$.
The solution is even in $x$, and for $x \geq 0$ it is
$$w(x) = w_0 \, e^{-x/a} \left( \cos \frac{x}{a} + \sin \frac{x}{a} \right), \qquad w_0 = \frac{V_0 a^3}{8D}.$$
Check: for $x > 0$ it solves the equation with $q = 0$, and $w'(0) = 0$.
The jump of $D w'''$ across $x = 0$ is $8 D w_0 / a^3 = V_0$.
The deflection changes sign at $x = 3\pi a/4$, and the bulge is highest at $x = \pi a$, with height $w_0 e^{-\pi} \approx 0.04 \, w_0$.
Non-example: with $D = 0$ the equation becomes $w = q/((\rho_m - \rho_i) g)$, which is local [[isostasy|7CJTGB6M0V]], with no moat and no bulge.
