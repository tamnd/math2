## Summary

In algebraic geometry, a **homogeneous variety** is an algebraic variety on which an algebraic group acts transitively. Homogeneous varieties over an [[algebraically closed field|0PF6TM8TWT]] are quotient varieties $G/H$ where $G$ is an algebraic group and $H$ a subgroup scheme (for instance, an algebraic subgroup).
