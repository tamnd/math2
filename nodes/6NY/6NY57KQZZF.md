## Statement

In number theory, the **Bateman–Horn conjecture** is a statement concerning the frequency of [[prime numbers|01826KMG2H]] among the values of a system of polynomials, named after mathematicians Paul T. Bateman and Roger A. Horn who proposed it in 1962. It provides a vast generalization of such conjectures as the Hardy–Littlewood conjecture on the density of twin primes or their conjecture on primes of the form $n^2+1$. It is also a strengthening of Schinzel's hypothesis H.
