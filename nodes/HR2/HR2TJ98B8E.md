## Summary

In the geometry of complex algebraic curves, a **local parameter** for a curve *C* at a smooth point *P* is a [[meromorphic function|0NSD1TPHR0]] on *C* that has a simple zero at *P.* This concept can be generalized to curves defined over fields other than $\mathbb{C}$ (or schemes), because the local ring at a smooth point *P* of an algebraic curve *C* (defined over an [[algebraically closed field|0PF6TM8TWT]]) is always a discrete valuation ring. This valuation will show a way to count the order (at the point *P*) of rational functions (which are natural generalizations for meromorphic functions in the non-complex realm) having a zero or a pole at *P*.
