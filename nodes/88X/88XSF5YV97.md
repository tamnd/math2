## Summary

The Prandtl–Meyer function $\nu(M)$ is the angle through which a sonic flow of an ideal gas must be turned to reach the [[Mach number|GFHJ858AN1]] $M > 1$.
The turning is a smooth isentropic expansion.
It turns the computation of supersonic expansions around corners into a simple rule: the change of $\nu$ equals the turning angle.
It increases from $0$ at $M = 1$ to a finite limit as $M \to \infty$, so a supersonic flow can turn only through a bounded angle.
It is a basic tool of the method of characteristics for plane [[supersonic flow|G4EN6Y0ZZ2]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a supersonic stream of air flows around an outward corner, it turns and speeds up.
The more it turns, the faster it gets.
The Prandtl–Meyer function is the table that links the two: it says how much turning is needed to reach a given Mach number, starting from the speed of sound.
For air, turning a sonic flow by about $26.4$ degrees brings it to Mach $2$.
