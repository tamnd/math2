## Summary

In mathematics, the notion of *"common limit in the range"* property denoted by **CLRg property** is a theorem that unifies, generalizes, and extends the contractive mappings in fuzzy metric spaces, where the range of the mappings does not necessarily need to be a [[closed|0G5AV9Q4RR]] subspace of a non-empty [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] $X$.
