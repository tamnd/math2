## Summary

Set-builder notation: if $P(x)$ is a predicate depending on a variable $x$, then both $\{x : P(x)\}$ and $\{x\mid P(x)\}$ denote the [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] formed by the values of $x$ for which $P(x)$ is true.
