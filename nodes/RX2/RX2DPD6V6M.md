## Summary

Calculus is the study of change and accumulation for functions of one and several real variables, through derivatives and integrals.
The derivative of a function at a point is the limit of its difference quotients, and it gives the slope of the graph and the instantaneous rate of change.
The fundamental theorem of calculus connects derivatives with integrals, and calculus is the main mathematical tool of physics, engineering, economics and the other sciences.
Newton and Leibniz developed calculus independently in the second half of the 17th century, and Cauchy and Weierstrass gave it rigorous foundations by limits in the 19th century.
This topic collects the calculus pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Real analysis, sequences, series and summability, manifolds and differential topology and ordinary differential equations have their own topics.
