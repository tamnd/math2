## Summary

The conditional obtained by taking the conjunction of the premises of the argument as antecedent and the conclusion of the argument as consequent. For instance, the conditionalization of modus ponens, $P \to Q, P \vdash Q$, is the formula $((P \to Q) \land P) \to Q$, called pseudo modus ponens.
