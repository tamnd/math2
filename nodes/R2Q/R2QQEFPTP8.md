## Summary

The Froude number $\mathrm{Fr} = U / \sqrt{g L}$ is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that compares the inertia of a flow with gravity.
It governs flows with a free surface, such as rivers, open channels and the waves made by ships.
In shallow water it is the ratio of the flow speed to the speed of long surface waves, so $\mathrm{Fr} = 1$ separates two different regimes.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Throw a stone into a slow river and the ripples spread both upstream and downstream.
In a fast mountain stream the ripples are all swept downstream, because the water is faster than the waves.
The Froude number tells which case you are in: below $1$ the waves win, above $1$ the current wins.
For water $1\ \mathrm{m}$ deep moving at $1\ \mathrm{m/s}$, $\mathrm{Fr} = 1/\sqrt{9.81} \approx 0.32$.
