## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers linear maps between vector spaces, kernel and image, the rank-nullity theorem, isomorphisms, the vector space of linear maps, and the algebra of endomorphisms.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Linear map|0NRYHJPQG2]]: additivity and homogeneity, and the maps that fail them.
2. [[Examples of linear maps|0SS770NS8B]]: derivative, integral, shifts, evaluation, trace and transpose.
3. [[Matrix transformations of Fⁿ|W6KB0J3SZZ]]: the maps $x \mapsto Ax$, and why every linear map $F^n \to F^m$ has this form.
4. [[Linear transformations of the plane|~0b7363a3]]: rotations, reflections, shears and projections, drawn on the unit square.
5. [[Composition of linear maps|00BKRZKMSS]]: composites are linear, and composition corresponds to matrix multiplication.
6. [[Kernel of a linear map|0S23E6YE83]]: the vectors sent to $0$, a subspace of the domain.
7. [[Image of a linear map|0WSFKKT6FF]]: the values of the map, a subspace spanned by the images of a spanning set.
8. [[Injectivity and the kernel|0BJBYCGMWE]]: a linear map is injective exactly when its kernel is $\{0\}$.
9. [[Linear maps are determined by a basis|0R8X9XA83A]]: values on a basis can be chosen freely and fix the map.
10. [[Rank of a linear map|0ZZXSBTR4J]]: the dimension of the image.
11. [[Nullity|0APCFW7639]]: the dimension of the kernel.
12. [[Rank-nullity theorem|0W4FSW1TVB]]: rank plus nullity equals the dimension of the domain.
13. [[Linear isomorphism|0B8T10CE7N]]: bijective linear maps, and why their inverses are linear.
14. [[Classification of vector spaces by dimension|0VJBSK15SN]]: $V \cong F^n$ for $\dim V = n$, through a choice of basis.
15. [[Injective, surjective and bijective endomorphisms|0912EEEVCT]]: the three agree in finite dimension, and the shifts show why finiteness matters.
16. [[Space of linear maps|0X9MBPP2Z3]]: $\Hom(V, W)$ as a vector space under pointwise operations.
17. [[Dimension of Hom(V, W)|0Z9N1ER0W0]]: $\dim \Hom(V, W) = \dim V \cdot \dim W$.
18. [[Endomorphism|0J7H0YBFDJ]]: linear maps from a space to itself, their powers and automorphisms.
19. [[Algebra of endomorphisms|~42300f41]]: $\End(V)$ as an associative algebra, isomorphic to $M_n(F)$, with the scalars as centre.
20. [[Semilinear and antilinear maps|~8c967182]]: maps that twist the scalars by a field automorphism.

### Prerequisites

- [[Vector spaces, span, independence, bases and dimension|MVH97H8B2J]]: subspaces, bases and dimension.
- [[Matrices and the operations on them|Y1ESNQ2V52]]: matrix products, matrix units and the matrix ring.
- [[Systems of linear equations and row reduction|DXSXFVDMXQ]]: homogeneous systems and their solution sets.
