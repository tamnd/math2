## Statement

In mathematics, a **Cullen number** is a member of the integer sequence $C_n = n \cdot 2^n + 1$ (where $n$ is a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]]). Cullen numbers were first studied by Irish mathematician James Cullen in 1905. The numbers are special cases of Proth numbers.
