## Summary

The divergence of a differentiable [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] is a scalar function. At each point, it measures the net rate of outward flow of the field per unit volume.
In Cartesian coordinates it is the sum of the [[partial derivatives|09EGC6F8PT]] $\partial F_i / \partial x_i$ of the components.
