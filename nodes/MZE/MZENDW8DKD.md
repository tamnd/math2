## Summary

Revealed preference infers a consumer's preferences from observed choices instead of assuming a [[utility function|GZZS4MTDG9]].
If a consumer buys bundle $x$ when bundle $y$ was also affordable, then $x$ is revealed preferred to $y$.
Consistency conditions on these revealed relations are called axioms of revealed preference.
They test whether the choices can come from maximizing a utility.
The theory was started by Paul Samuelson and is the basis of nonparametric tests of consumer theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

We cannot look into a shopper's head, but we can see what they buy and what they could have bought.
If they bought apples when pears cost no more, they have shown that they like apples at least as much.
If on another day they bought pears when apples were strictly cheaper, the two days contradict each other.
Revealed preference turns such observations into a test of whether the shopper behaves as a utility maximizer.
