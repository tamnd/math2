## Summary

The Abraham–Lorentz–Dirac equation is the relativistic equation of motion of a classical point charge that includes the reaction of its own radiation.
It generalizes the non-relativistic [[Abraham–Lorentz force|TC04HE7BDQ]] to all speeds below that of light and is written with four-vectors along the worldline.
Like its non-relativistic version, it is of third order and has runaway solutions, so its physical meaning needs care.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A charge that accelerates radiates light and is pushed back by its own field.
At low speed this push is the Abraham–Lorentz force.
At high speed one needs a form that respects special relativity, and this is the Abraham–Lorentz–Dirac equation.
It contains an extra term that keeps the mass of the particle constant while it radiates.
