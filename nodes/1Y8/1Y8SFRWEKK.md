## Summary

In number theory and enumerative combinatorics, the **ordered Bell numbers** or **Fubini numbers** count the weak orderings on a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] of $n$ elements. Weak orderings arrange their elements into a sequence allowing ties, such as might arise as the outcome of a horse race.
