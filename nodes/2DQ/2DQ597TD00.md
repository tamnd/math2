## Summary

In mathematics, a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] $a$ is a **unitary divisor** (or **Hall divisor**) of a number $b$ if $a$ is a [[divisor|0PG2H7WKGG]] of $b$ and if $a$ and $b / a$ are coprime, having no common factor other than 1. Equivalently, a divisor $a$ of $b$ is a unitary divisor if and only if every [[prime|01826KMG2H]] factor of $a$ has the same multiplicity in $a$ as it has in $b$.
