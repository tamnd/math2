## Summary

In mathematics, the *i*th **Bass number** of a [[module|0NZ8JA25WK]] *M* over a local ring *R* with residue field *k* is the *k*-dimension of $\operatorname{Ext}^i_R(k,M)$. More generally the Bass number $\mu_i(p,M)$ of a module *M* over a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] *R* at a [[prime ideal|00MHHZ9SNE]] *p* is the Bass number of the localization of *M* for the localization of *R* (with respect to the prime *p*). Bass numbers were introduced by (txt, p.11).
