## Summary

**Goldbach's comet** is the name given to a plot of the function $g(E)$, the so-called **Goldbach function** . The function, studied in relation to [[Goldbach's conjecture|063JNBSMJ0]], is defined for all even integers $E > 2$ to be the number of different ways in which *E* can be expressed as the sum of two primes. For example, $g(22) = 3$ since 22 can be expressed as the sum of two primes in three different ways ($22 = 11+11 = 5+17 = 3+19$).
