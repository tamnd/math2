## Summary

Quantum teleportation is a protocol that transfers an unknown state of a [[qubit|E9QTT3XBXS]] from one party to another.
The parties use one shared [[Bell state|CA57HMCRQQ]] and two bits of classical communication, and no qubit travels between them.
It shows that a shared entangled pair plus classical messages can replace a quantum channel.
It is a basic primitive of quantum networks and of measurement-based quantum computing.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Alice holds a qubit in a state she does not know, and she wants Bob to have it.
She cannot measure it to learn it, and she cannot copy it.
If Alice and Bob share an entangled pair in advance, Alice can measure her two qubits together and phone Bob two bits.
Depending on these two bits, Bob applies one of four simple corrections, and his qubit ends up in the original state.
Alice's original is destroyed in the process, so nothing is copied, and nothing travels faster than the phone call.
