## Summary

Loop quantum gravity is an approach to [[quantum gravity|M1VXRT5X56]] that quantizes general relativity directly, without a background metric.
It rewrites the theory in terms of an $SU(2)$ connection, the [[Ashtekar variables|SH0DEE8P6K]], and uses as basic observables the [[holonomies|WHDZ3MK56B]] of this connection along curves.
Its quantum states of space are [[spin networks|B173ESJ4YX]], and areas and volumes have discrete spectra.
It was developed from the late 1980s by Abhay Ashtekar, Carlo Rovelli, Lee Smolin, Thomas Thiemann and others.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In loop quantum gravity, space is not a smooth continuum at the smallest scales.
It is made of discrete quanta, described by graphs whose edges carry labels called spins.
Each edge that crosses a surface contributes a small, definite amount of area, so area comes in discrete steps of roughly the Planck length squared.
This is similar to how the energy of an atom comes in discrete levels.
The theory has a precise description of quantum space, but how its dynamics produces smooth spacetime at large scales is still being studied.
