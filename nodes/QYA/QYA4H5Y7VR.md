## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the algebraic semantics of logics: Lindenbaum-Tarski algebras, Boolean, Heyting and modal algebras with their dualities, the algebras of many-valued and substructural logics, the algebras of first-order logic and of relations, and abstract algebraic logic.
