## Proof

We prove part 1 with the product rule in one direction and the adjugate in the other, part 2 with the Leibniz formula, and part 3 as the case of the integers.
Let $K$ be a commutative ring and let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over $K$, as in the [[theorem|03Q7RE2HXR]].

*Part 1.*

1. Suppose that $A$ is [[invertible|0TWTS88TWN]].
   By the [[determinant of the inverse|0783S9D0ZH]], which holds over every commutative ring, $\det A \cdot \det(A^{-1}) = 1$.
   So $\det A$ is a [[unit|019NFGFPR5]] of $K$.
2. Conversely, suppose that $\det A$ is a unit.
   The [[adjugate formula for the inverse|0ENVM493VK]] then shows that $A$ is invertible, with $A^{-1} = (\det A)^{-1} \operatorname{adj} A$.

*Part 2.*

3. By the [[Leibniz formula|016PE4VZ8Q]],
$$\det A = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i \sigma(i)}.$$
4. A ring homomorphism preserves sums, products and the elements $1$ and $-1$.
   Applying $\varphi$ term by term gives
$$\varphi(\det A) = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n} \varphi(a_{i \sigma(i)}).$$
5. By the Leibniz formula over $L$, the right side of step 4 is $\det \varphi(A)$.

*Part 3.*

6. The only units of $\mathbb{Z}$ are $1$ and $-1$, so part 1 with $K = \mathbb{Z}$ is part 3.

## Idea

One direction is the product rule: an inverse matrix forces $\det A \cdot \det(A^{-1}) = \det I_n = 1$, so $\det A$ has an inverse in $K$.
For the other direction, the adjugate divided by $\det A$ is an explicit inverse, and the only division it needs is by $\det A$.
Naturality holds because the Leibniz formula is built from sums and products of entries, and a ring homomorphism respects both.

## Variants

Over an integral domain $R$ with field of fractions $F$, there is a second route to part 1.
If $\det A \neq 0$, the matrix $A$ has the inverse $(\det A)^{-1} \operatorname{adj} A$ over $F$.
The adjugate has entries in $R$, so this inverse has entries in $R$ as soon as $(\det A)^{-1} \in R$.
Row reduction is not available over a general ring, since it divides by pivots, and the adjugate takes its place.
