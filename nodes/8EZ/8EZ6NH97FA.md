## Definition

The *figure of the Earth* is a closed surface in $\R^3$ chosen to model the size and shape of the Earth.
The standard models are:

1. a [[sphere|P9CM4GQ2T0]] of radius $R$, with $R \approx 6371$ km;
2. a [[reference ellipsoid|R9XX7TNJCH]], an oblate ellipsoid of revolution about the rotation axis of the Earth;
3. the [[geoid|999BYZY0ZG]], the equipotential surface of the gravity field that best fits mean sea level.

## Remarks

Example: the ellipsoid with semi-major axis $a = 6378137$ m and flattening $f = 1/298.257223563$ (the WGS 84 ellipsoid) is a figure of the Earth of the second kind.
Non-example: the actual topographic surface, with its mountains and valleys, is not used as a figure of the Earth, because it has no simple mathematical description.
The sphere gives distances with errors up to about $0.5\%$, which is enough for many navigation tasks.
