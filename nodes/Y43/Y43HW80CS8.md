## Statement

In polynomial algebra and [[field theory|04C5ZBG1WT]], **Joubert's theorem** states that if $K$ and $L$ are [[fields|04C5ZBG1WT]], $L$ is a separable field extension of $K$ of [[degree|0EAJ2XXADC]] 6, and the [[characteristic|0F6ES3BRYP]] of $K$ is not equal to 2, then $L$ is generated over $K$ by some element λ in $L$, such that the [[minimal polynomial|0VX9EP2GS8]] $p$ of λ has the form $p(t)$ = $t^6 + c_4 t^4 + c_2 t^2 + c_1 t + c_0$, for some constants $c_4, c_2, c_1, c_0$ in $K$. The theorem is named in honor of Charles Joubert, a French mathematician, *lycée* professor, and Jesuit priest.
