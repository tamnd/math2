## Statement

The first result applies to sums *s*<sub>ν</sub> where $|z_n| \ge 1$ for all *n*. For any range of *ν* of length *N*, say *ν* = *M* + 1, ..., *M* + *N*, there is some *ν* with |*s*<sub>*ν*</sub>| at least *c*(*M*, *N*)|*s*<sub>0</sub>| where

$$c(M,N) = \left({ \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \binom{M+k}{k} 2^k }\right)^{-1} \ .$$

The sum here may be replaced by the weaker but simpler $\left({ \frac{N}{2e(M+N)} }\right)^{N-1}$.

We may deduce the Fabry gap theorem from this result.
