## Summary

In mathematics, the [[Frobenius endomorphism|0GHDDVAH8P]] is defined in any [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]] $R$ that has [[characteristic|0F6ES3BRYP]] $p$, where $p$ is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]]. Namely, the mapping $\phi:r\mapsto r^p$ is a ring endomorphism of $R$.
