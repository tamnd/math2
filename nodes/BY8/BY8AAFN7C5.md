## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers second-order arithmetic and its subsystems, the program to find the axioms that the theorems of ordinary mathematics need, the five main subsystems and the many combinatorial principles between them.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Second-order arithmetic|DC60FYCSPM]]: the language, the axioms, the models and the coding of mathematics.
2. [[Reverse mathematics|7SQS69T17R]]: the program that finds the axioms a theorem needs.
3. [[RCA0|~58b63877]]: the base system of computable mathematics.
4. [[WKL0|~e7c6fa0e]]: weak König's lemma and compactness.
5. [[ACA0|~08f6623a]]: arithmetical comprehension and sequential compactness.
6. [[ATR0|~ff80e550]]: transfinite iteration of the jump along well-orderings.
7. [[Π¹₁-CA0|~0d0109c9]]: the strongest of the big five systems.
8. [[β-model|J0Z2GNJZ2W]]: the models that are correct about well-orderings.
9. [[Ramsey's theorem for pairs|~848bb923]]: a theorem outside the big five systems.
10. [[Reverse mathematics zoo|~00f7bd45]]: the principles between the big five systems.
11. [[Weak weak König's lemma|~def26058]]: measure theory and randomness.
12. [[Higher-order reverse mathematics|~19a7d96a]]: functionals of higher type.
13. [[Partial realizations of Hilbert's program|~f3242591]]: what the conservation results mean for finitism.
14. [[Predicative mathematics|~531abfcc]]: the mathematics that avoids impredicative definitions.
