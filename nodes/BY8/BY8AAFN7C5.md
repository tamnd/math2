## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers second-order arithmetic and its subsystems, the program to find the axioms that the theorems of ordinary mathematics need, the five main subsystems and the many combinatorial principles between them.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Second-order arithmetic|DC60FYCSPM]]: the language, the axioms, the models and the coding of mathematics.
2. [[Reverse mathematics|7SQS69T17R]]: the program that finds the axioms a theorem needs.
3. [[RCA0|XQENB067R3]]: the base system of computable mathematics.
4. [[WKL0|7W9EG02T79]]: weak König's lemma and compactness.
5. [[ACA0|NAASTN80QN]]: arithmetical comprehension and sequential compactness.
6. [[ATR0|MKB74TX3Z8]]: transfinite iteration of the jump along well-orderings.
7. [[Π¹₁-CA0|T21WETAHN5]]: the strongest of the big five systems.
8. [[β-model|J0Z2GNJZ2W]]: the models that are correct about well-orderings.
9. [[Ramsey's theorem for pairs|ZT5KV6KJ3E]]: a theorem outside the big five systems.
10. [[Reverse mathematics zoo|E3X5B175ZR]]: the principles between the big five systems.
11. [[Weak weak König's lemma|KPHM3025GW]]: measure theory and randomness.
12. [[Higher-order reverse mathematics|NPDRXTBW5W]]: functionals of higher type.
13. [[Partial realizations of Hilbert's program|Z26S516BKH]]: what the conservation results mean for finitism.
14. [[Predicative mathematics|43BSXNWQBG]]: the mathematics that avoids impredicative definitions.
