## Summary

The Cartan matrix of a root system is the square matrix of the integers $\langle \alpha_i, \alpha_j \rangle$ for the simple roots $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_l$.
It determines the root system up to isomorphism, and so the semisimple Lie algebra over $\C$ up to isomorphism.
Its entries appear as coefficients and exponents in the Serre relations, which present the Lie algebra by generators and relations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Cartan matrix is a small table of whole numbers that encodes the angles and the length ratios between the simple roots.
Its diagonal entries are all $2$, and its other entries are $0$, $-1$, $-2$ or $-3$.
For the hexagon system $A_2$, it is $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, which records that the two simple roots have the same length and meet at $120^\circ$.
