## Summary

In mathematics, specifically the theory of [[quadratic forms|0RBQS1JJCR]], an ***ε*-quadratic form** is a generalization of quadratic forms to skew-symmetric settings and to \*-rings; *ε* = ±1, accordingly for symmetric or skew-symmetric. They are also called $(-)^n$-quadratic forms, particularly in the context of surgery theory.
