## Summary

In mathematics, the **[[tensor product|0BF2DE2SYK]] of [[quadratic forms|0RBQS1JJCR]]** is most easily understood when one views the quadratic forms as *quadratic spaces*. If *R* is a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]] where 2 is [[invertible|0XVEZDE9J8]], and if $(V_1, q_1)$ and $(V_2,q_2)$ are two quadratic spaces over *R*, then their tensor product $(V_1 \otimes V_2, q_1 \otimes q_2)$ is the quadratic space whose underlying *R*-[[module|0NZ8JA25WK]] is the tensor product $V_1 \otimes V_2$ of *R*-modules and whose quadratic form is the quadratic form associated to the tensor product of the [[bilinear forms|0SQZMXB6GP]] associated to $q_1$ and $q_2$.
