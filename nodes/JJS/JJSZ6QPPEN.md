## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **pancake graph** *P*<sub>*n*</sub> or ***n*-pancake graph** is a graph whose vertices are the permutations of *n* symbols from 1 to *n* and its edges are given between permutations transitive by prefix reversals.
