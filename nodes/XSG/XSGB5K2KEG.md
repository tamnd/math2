## Summary

In number theory, a **cyclotomic character** is a character of a [[Galois group|0XTY86BZKY]] giving the Galois action on a group of roots of unity. As a one-dimensional representation over a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] $R$, its representation space is generally denoted by $R(1)$ (that is, it is a representation $\chi : G \to \operatorname{Aut}_{R}(R(1)) \approx \operatorname{GL}(1, R)$).
