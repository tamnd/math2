## Summary

The Gini coefficient is a number between $0$ and $1$ that measures the inequality of a distribution of income or wealth.
It equals twice the area between the diagonal and the [[Lorenz curve|JEZ26SAXT1]].
Equivalently, it is half the mean absolute difference between two random incomes, divided by the mean.
A value of $0$ means perfect equality, and values near $1$ mean that a few hold almost everything.
It is the most widely reported index of income inequality.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Pick two people at random and look at the gap between their incomes.
The Gini coefficient is the average gap divided by twice the average income.
If everyone earns the same, every gap is zero and the coefficient is $0$.
If one person out of many has all the income, almost every gap involves that person, and the coefficient is close to $1$.
For incomes $1, 2, 3, 4$ the coefficient is $1/4$.
