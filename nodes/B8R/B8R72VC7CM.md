## Summary

In descriptive set theory, a **coanalytic set** is a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] (typically a subset of a Polish space) that is the [[complement|0RKF1ZT5JJ]] of an analytic set. In the projective hierarchy, coanalytic sets correspond to $\mathbf{\Pi}^1_1$ sets.
