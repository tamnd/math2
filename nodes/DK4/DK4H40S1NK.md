## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|G7N6BDTEZ2]].
It covers division rings in general: the quaternions, Wedderburn's little theorem, Frobenius's theorem on real division algebras, the Cartan-Brauer-Hua theorem and Hua's theorem, linear algebra and determinants over division rings, and constructions of infinite-dimensional division rings.
