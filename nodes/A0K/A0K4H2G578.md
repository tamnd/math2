## Summary

In the mathematical fields of set theory and extremal combinatorics, a **sunflower** or **$\Delta$-system** is a collection of [[sets|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] in which the [[intersection|0ZNMMA9TF5]] of any two distinct sets is the same. This common intersection is called the **kernel** of the sunflower.
