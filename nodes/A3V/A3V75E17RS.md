## Summary

Flow plasticity theory describes [[plasticity|K8BBMTN0KV]] by rates.
It gives the rate of plastic strain in terms of the current stress, its rate and the state of hardening.
Its ingredients are a [[yield surface|CWWCEXZ8BH]], a flow rule for the direction of plastic flow, a hardening law, and loading and unloading conditions.
Unlike deformation theories, it makes the response depend on the loading path, which matches the behaviour of real metals.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A metal that is being bent beyond its elastic limit does not have a stress fixed by its present shape alone; how it got there matters.
Flow theory follows the deformation step by step.
At each moment it asks: is the stress at the yield limit, and is the load still increasing?
If so, it adds a little plastic strain in a direction fixed by the flow rule; if not, the material responds elastically.
