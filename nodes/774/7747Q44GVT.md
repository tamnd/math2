## Statement

Let $A$ be a C*-algebra and let $\alpha$ be an automorphism of $A$.
Let $A \rtimes_\alpha \Z$ be the [[crossed product|8HG8TS46A6]] of $A$ by the action $n \mapsto \alpha^n$ of $\Z$.
Let $\iota \colon A \to A \rtimes_\alpha \Z$ be the canonical inclusion and $\alpha_*$ the map induced by $\alpha$ on [[K0|BP86XQRZD2]] and [[K1|7NTVTHC7CG]].
Then there are homomorphisms $\delta_0 \colon K_0(A \rtimes_\alpha \Z) \to K_1(A)$ and $\delta_1 \colon K_1(A \rtimes_\alpha \Z) \to K_0(A)$ such that the cyclic diagram
$$
\begin{array}{ccccc}
K_0(A) & \xrightarrow{\id - \alpha_*} & K_0(A) & \xrightarrow{\iota_*} & K_0(A \rtimes_\alpha \Z) \\
\uparrow{\scriptstyle \delta_1} & & & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle \delta_0} \\
K_1(A \rtimes_\alpha \Z) & \xleftarrow{\iota_*} & K_1(A) & \xleftarrow{\id - \alpha_*} & K_1(A)
\end{array}
$$
is [[exact|JB7ZBFBA9C]] at each of its six groups.

## Remarks

Pimsner and Voiculescu proved it with a Toeplitz-type algebra $\mathcal{T}_\alpha$ that fits into an extension $0 \to A \otimes \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{T}_\alpha \to A \rtimes_\alpha \Z \to 0$.
The main step shows that the inclusion $A \to \mathcal{T}_\alpha$ is an isomorphism on K-theory, and the six-term exact sequence of the extension then becomes the sequence above.
Example: for $A = C(\mathbb{T})$ and $\alpha$ the rotation by an angle $2 \pi \theta$, the rotation is homotopic to the identity, so $\alpha_* = \id$ and $\id - \alpha_* = 0$.
The sequence then splits into short exact sequences $0 \to K_i(C(\mathbb{T})) \to K_i(A_\theta) \to K_{1-i}(C(\mathbb{T})) \to 0$, which give $K_0(A_\theta) \cong K_1(A_\theta) \cong \Z^2$ for the noncommutative torus $A_\theta$.
For the trivial automorphism, $A \rtimes \Z \cong A \otimes C(\mathbb{T})$, and the sequence gives short exact sequences $0 \to K_i(A) \to K_i(A \otimes C(\mathbb{T})) \to K_{1-i}(A) \to 0$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A single automorphism of a C*-algebra gives a dynamical system, and the crossed product packs it into a new C*-algebra.
The theorem computes the K-theory of this new algebra from the K-theory of $A$ and the action of the automorphism on it.
Roughly, the crossed product sees the classes that the automorphism leaves fixed and the classes that it shifts.

### L2 human

Together with the Connes–Thom isomorphism for actions of $\R$, it is the basic tool for crossed products.
Classical applications are the K-theory of the irrational rotation algebras and their embedding into AF algebras.
It also gives the K-theory of the Cuntz algebras through a crossed product description.
