This topic is a part of [[operator algebras and noncommutative geometry|Q1CY5TTNK5]].
It covers modules over C*-algebras with inner products that take values in the algebra, their adjointable and compact operators, C*-correspondences, and strong Morita equivalence of C*-algebras.
