## Summary

In proof theory, a branch of mathematical logic, **elementary function arithmetic** (**EFA**), also called **elementary arithmetic** and **exponential function arithmetic**, is the system of arithmetic with the usual elementary properties of 0, 1, +, ×, $x^y$, together with [[induction|0ASJVEFA9J]] for formulas with bounded quantifiers.
