## Summary

In mathematics, a **multiset** (or **bag**, or **mset**) is a modification of the concept of a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] that, unlike a set, allows for multiple instances for each of its elements. The number of instances given for each element is called the *multiplicity* of that element in the multiset.
