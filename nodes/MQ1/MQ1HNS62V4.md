## Summary

Means "greater than or equal to". That is, whatever $A$ and $B$ are, $A \ge B$ is equivalent to $A > B\text{ or } A = B$.

Between two groups, may mean that the second one is a [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] of the first one.
