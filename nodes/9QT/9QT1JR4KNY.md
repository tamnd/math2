## Description

The complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak{e}_8$ is the [[simple Lie algebra|XDAXWHBNSZ]] over $\C$ whose [[root system|AVVV93CASN]] has type $E_8$.
The root system $E_8$ lies in $\R^8$ and consists of the $240$ vectors of the following two kinds:

1. the $112$ vectors with two entries $\pm 1$ and six entries $0$;
2. the $128$ vectors $(\pm \tfrac12, \dots, \pm \tfrac12)$ with an even number of minus signs.

Check of the count: $\binom{8}{2} \cdot 4 = 112$ and $2^8 / 2 = 128$.
All roots have squared length $2$.
The rank is $8$, and the dimension is $8 + 240 = 248$.

## Remarks

The [[Dynkin diagram|CS73JZQS66]] of $E_8$ is a tree with $8$ vertices: a branch vertex with arms of $1$, $2$ and $4$ further vertices.
The [[Weyl group|YT1ZJ2S4EE]] of $E_8$ has order $696\,729\,600$.
The smallest nontrivial representation of $\mathfrak{e}_8$ is the adjoint representation, of dimension $248$.
The roots span the [[E8 lattice|2CXH65EKY7]], and the compact Lie group of this type is the [[exceptional Lie group E8|MKT1263M0A]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

$\mathfrak{e}_8$ is the largest exceptional simple Lie algebra, of dimension $248$.
Its $240$ roots form a highly symmetric configuration in eight-dimensional space.
Unlike the other simple Lie algebras, its smallest nontrivial representation is the adjoint one, on the algebra itself.
