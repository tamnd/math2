## Summary

A real form of a complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ is a real Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_0$ whose complexification is $\mathfrak{g}$, that is, $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{g}_0 \oplus i\mathfrak{g}_0$.
One complex Lie algebra can have several non-isomorphic real forms.
A real simple Lie algebra is a real form of a complex simple Lie algebra or a complex simple Lie algebra seen as real.
So the classification of real simple Lie algebras reduces to the classification of real forms.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A complex Lie algebra can sometimes be written with real numbers only, after a good choice of basis.
The real algebra obtained this way is a real form.
For example, $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\R)$, the real $2 \times 2$ matrices of trace $0$, is a real form of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\C)$.
The algebra $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ of skew-Hermitian matrices of trace $0$ is another real form of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\C)$, and it is not isomorphic to $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\R)$.
