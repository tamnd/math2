## Summary

A Riccati equation is a first-order equation $y' = q_0(x) + q_1(x) y + q_2(x) y^2$, with a right side quadratic in $y$.
It can be changed into a second-order linear equation. See the [[definition|0E1ZZBHZ3Z]].
