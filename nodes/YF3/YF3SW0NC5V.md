This topic is a part of [[control and systems theory|5T1K3KMHA0]].
It covers the estimation of the state of a stochastic system from noisy outputs, with the Kalman-Bucy filter, nonlinear Kalman filters, smoothers, resampling methods and the Wiener filter.
