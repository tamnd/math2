## Summary

A Malcev algebra is an [[anticommutative algebra|CCZ0W5WGWX]] that satisfies a weakened form of the Jacobi identity, the Malcev identity.
Every [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] is a Malcev algebra, and the commutator algebra of every [[alternative algebra|N8K9SSVMRN]] is a Malcev algebra.
Malcev algebras are to analytic Moufang loops what Lie algebras are to Lie groups.
The purely imaginary octonions with the commutator bracket form a seven-dimensional simple Malcev algebra that is not a Lie algebra.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The commutator $xy - yx$ of square matrices satisfies the Jacobi identity, because matrix multiplication is associative.
For octonions, which are not associative but only alternative, the commutator fails the Jacobi identity.
Malcev found the weaker identity that the commutator of an alternative algebra still satisfies.
Algebras with an antisymmetric product satisfying this identity are now called Malcev algebras.
