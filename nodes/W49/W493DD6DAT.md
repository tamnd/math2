## Summary

In abstract algebra, **alternativity** is a property of a [[binary operation|0580ZJSA3Q]]. A magma $G$ is said to be **left alternative** if $(xx)y = x(xy)$ for all $x, y \in G$ and **right alternative** if $y(xx) = (yx)x$ for all $x, y \in G$. A magma that is both left and right alternative is said to be **alternative**.
