## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers the standard applications in textbooks of linear algebra, written as examples of the theory.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Applications of linear systems|~804444f5]]: traffic, circuits, chemical equations and the input-output model.
2. [[Polynomial interpolation by a linear system|~ecba65a7]]: the Vandermonde system and the Lagrange basis.
3. [[Curve fitting and linear regression by least squares|~9d6b4476]]: normal equations and $R^2$ as a projection.
4. [[Linear recurrences by linear algebra|K00NJY6C33]]: matrix powers, closed forms and Binet's formula.
5. [[Linear discrete dynamical systems|~3507d389]]: predator-prey and Leslie models, attractors and saddles.
6. [[Steady-state vectors of Markov chains|~f92b40f5]]: the eigenvalue $1$ of a stochastic matrix.
7. [[PageRank as an eigenvector problem|~dd98a0b6]]: the Google matrix and power iteration.
8. [[Linear systems of ODEs by eigenvectors|DH7CQXQYJX]]: solutions $e^{\lambda t} v$ and real solutions from complex eigenvalues.
9. [[Linear algebra in computer graphics|~14be26aa]]: homogeneous coordinates and perspective projection.
10. [[Change of basis in signal and image processing|~4183944f]]: the Fourier and Haar bases, YCbCr and JPEG.
11. [[Image compression by the SVD|~e0dc1c00]]: low-rank approximation and its error.
12. [[Lights Out puzzle|~c4bcd5dd]]: a linear system over $\mathbb F_2$.
13. [[Matrices of graphs as linear algebra examples|~9c52ce32]]: walks, cycles and Kirchhoff's laws.

- [[Diagonalization, triangularization and invariant subspaces|Z6S6PWMJPH]]: rows 4 to 8.
- [[Isometries and the orthogonal and unitary groups|AZS5BPF5H3]]: rows 9 and 10.
- [[Positive definite matrices and the singular value decomposition|SS4EVPKT3A]]: row 11.
