## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers the standard applications in textbooks of linear algebra, written as examples of the theory.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Applications of linear systems|1QXFM0DNAH]]: traffic, circuits, chemical equations and the input-output model.
2. [[Polynomial interpolation by a linear system|NR58EBS932]]: the Vandermonde system and the Lagrange basis.
3. [[Curve fitting and linear regression by least squares|C170QFW8QB]]: normal equations and $R^2$ as a projection.
4. [[Linear recurrences by linear algebra|K00NJY6C33]]: matrix powers, closed forms and Binet's formula.
5. [[Linear discrete dynamical systems|2F1B461YN7]]: predator-prey and Leslie models, attractors and saddles.
6. [[Steady-state vectors of Markov chains|Y9FRWY8YX4]]: the eigenvalue $1$ of a stochastic matrix.
7. [[PageRank as an eigenvector problem|9ZPQYZ5NDP]]: the Google matrix and power iteration.
8. [[Linear systems of ODEs by eigenvectors|DH7CQXQYJX]]: solutions $e^{\lambda t} v$ and real solutions from complex eigenvalues.
9. [[Linear algebra in computer graphics|REB4ZHN28T]]: homogeneous coordinates and perspective projection.
10. [[Change of basis in signal and image processing|6NANBCYMG2]]: the Fourier and Haar bases, YCbCr and JPEG.
11. [[Image compression by the SVD|9QB82PPEMV]]: low-rank approximation and its error.
12. [[Lights Out puzzle|E8DZ9CPEEE]]: a linear system over $\mathbb F_2$.
13. [[Matrices of graphs as linear algebra examples|JFWP2SBCF0]]: walks, cycles and Kirchhoff's laws.

- [[Isometries and the orthogonal and unitary groups|AZS5BPF5H3]]: rows 9 and 10.
- [[Positive definite matrices and the singular value decomposition|SS4EVPKT3A]]: row 11.
