## Statement

In 1951, Tikao Tatsuzawa proved an 'almost' effective version of Siegel's theorem, showing that for any fixed $0 < \varepsilon < \frac{1}{11.2}$, if $|D| > e^{1/\varepsilon}$ then

$$L(1,\chi_D) > 0.655\varepsilon|D|^{-\varepsilon},$$

*with the possible exception of at most one fundamental discriminant.* Using the 'almost effectivity' of this result, P. J. Weinberger (1973) showed that Euler's list of 65 idoneal numbers is complete except for at most two elements.
