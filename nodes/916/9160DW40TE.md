## Summary

In mathematics, specifically abstract algebra, a **square class** of a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $F$ is an element of the **square class group**, the [[quotient group|0KYX2N69J0]] $F^\times/ F^{\times 2}$ of the multiplicative group of nonzero elements in the field modulo the [[square|B5J0419GZP]] elements of the field. Each square class is a [[subset|0BJR9QR08E]] of the nonzero elements (a [[coset|564VVAQTNR]] of the multiplicative group) consisting of the elements of the form *xy*<sup>2</sup> where *x* is some particular fixed element and *y* ranges over all nonzero field elements.
