## Summary

Sample average approximation (SAA) solves a [[stochastic program|2YW60J1GT6]] approximately by replacing the expectation in the objective by an average over a random sample.
The result is a deterministic optimization problem that standard solvers can handle.
Under mild conditions, its optimal value and solutions converge to those of the true problem as the sample size grows.
It is a Monte Carlo method and is widely used when the expectation cannot be computed exactly.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The average cost over all possible futures is often impossible to compute.
Instead, simulate many possible futures, say a thousand, and optimize the average cost over those.
With enough simulated futures, the answer is close to the best decision for the real uncertain future.
For the newsvendor problem, this amounts to ordering a sample quantile of the observed demands.
