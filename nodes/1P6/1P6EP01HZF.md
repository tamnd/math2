## Summary

A string, or word, over an [[alphabet|RABRP2KG81]] $\Sigma$ is a finite sequence of symbols of $\Sigma$, such as $0110$ over $\set{0, 1}$.
The set $\Sigma^*$ of all strings, with concatenation, is the [[free monoid|CV013KHVJG]] on $\Sigma$, with the empty string as identity.
Strings are the inputs and outputs of machines such as [[Turing machines|758EYCN27B]] and finite automata.
Sets of strings are [[formal languages|5MFZ4VNY6D]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A string is a finite row of symbols written one after another.
Over the letters $a$ and $b$, examples are $ab$, $bba$ and the empty string with no symbols at all.
Two strings can be joined end to end: joining $ab$ and $ba$ gives $abba$.
The length of a string is its number of symbols, so $abba$ has length $4$.
