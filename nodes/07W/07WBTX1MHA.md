## Summary

Let $F$ be a function of a complex variable. Its inverse Laplace transform is a piecewise continuous function $f$ of exponential growth whose Laplace transform is $F$.
It can be computed by the Bromwich integral or by Post's inversion formula. See the [[definition|0JRW4X1MDC]].
