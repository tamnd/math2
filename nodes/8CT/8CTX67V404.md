## Summary

A Galilean transformation is a change of coordinates between two [[inertial frames|Q05G0NZDNB]] in Newtonian mechanics.
It combines a shift of time, a shift and a rotation of space, and a uniform motion of one frame relative to the other.
Galilean transformations form a group, the Galilean group, which is the symmetry group of Newtonian space and time.
They preserve time differences and the distances between simultaneous events.

## Explanations

### L1 human

You stand on a platform and a friend sits in a train moving at $20$ m/s.
A ball rolls forward in the train at $1$ m/s according to your friend.
For you the ball moves at $21$ m/s.
The rule that turns your friend's positions and velocities into yours is a Galilean transformation: subtract or add the relative velocity, and keep the same clock.
