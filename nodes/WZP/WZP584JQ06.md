## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers logics with more than two truth values: logical matrices, the finitely valued logics of Łukasiewicz, Kleene, Post and Belnap, infinite-valued Łukasiewicz logic, fuzzy logics based on t-norms, and probability logic.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Many-valued logic|DWYCKAKXZD]]: truth values beyond true and false, and why they are useful.
2. [[Logical matrix|P7E926JK13]]: an algebra with designated values, the common frame of all the logics below.
3. [[Łukasiewicz three-valued logic|1CE2TGM6MQ]]: the first many-valued logic, with the value $\tfrac12$ for future contingents.
4. [[Strong Kleene logic|SZHA10A9X7]]: a third value for "undefined", and a logic without tautologies.
5. [[Weak Kleene logic|BCK5QXQXA6]]: a third value that spreads to every compound formula.
6. [[First-degree entailment|KXTP4P3GHT]]: four values for true, false, neither and both.
7. [[Post's many-valued logics|YHYSGRE7JV]]: a functionally complete logic for each finite number of values.
8. [[Sheffer function|1K829SJG3K]]: one connective that defines all others in a finitely valued logic.
9. [[Łukasiewicz infinite-valued logic|NFCEM44XYH]]: truth values in $[0, 1]$, with strong and weak connectives.
10. [[Chang's completeness theorem|QM0F33QE9P]]: the algebraic completeness theorem by MV-algebras.
11. [[McNaughton's theorem|E11A9H8Z7Q]]: the functions that Łukasiewicz formulas define.
12. [[Fuzzy logic|8DH4QM6AX8]]: fuzzy logic as a logic with a consequence relation, and its other use in engineering.
13. [[Triangular norm|VR6D8JXX1W]]: the truth functions of conjunction, and their residua.
14. [[Hájek's basic logic BL|3AQ5GHTXA4]]: the logic of all continuous t-norms.
15. [[Monoidal t-norm based logic|PT5PHKJDYM]]: the logic of all left-continuous t-norms.
16. [[Product fuzzy logic|YTHRDT9PDR]]: the logic of the product t-norm.
17. [[First-order Gödel logics|H8C323CV3T]]: quantifiers over the minimum t-norm, and sets of truth values.
18. [[Pavelka logic|YG62F8YC0P]]: truth constants in the language and graded consequence.
19. [[Arithmetical complexity of first-order Łukasiewicz logic|VAHH1505Z0]]: why no complete axiomatization by a computable list exists.
20. [[Rényi-Ulam game with one lie|402GXME26B]]: a search game with lies, described by Łukasiewicz values.
21. [[Probability logic|Z2WRGJDRQT]]: probabilities on formulas, which are not truth values.
22. [[Vector logic|DJ41X602BJ]]: truth values as orthonormal vectors and connectives as matrices.

### Prerequisites

- [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: formulas, valuations, tautologies and Hilbert calculi.
- [[Algebraic logic|QYA4H5Y7VR]]: Boolean algebras, lattices and the algebraic view of a logic.
- For rows 17 and 19, also first-order structures and the [[arithmetical hierarchy|7CVJCQKY41]].

### Notation

The notation for strong conjunction varies between sources.
Wikipedia writes $\otimes$, SEP writes $\&$, and the MV-algebra literature writes $\odot$.
This topic writes $\odot$ for the strong conjunction of Łukasiewicz logic and $\oplus$ for its strong disjunction.
For general t-norm logics such as BL and MTL, it writes $\otimes$ for the strong conjunction and $*$ for a t-norm.
In [[vector logic|DJ41X602BJ]], $\otimes$ is the Kronecker product, which is a different operation.
