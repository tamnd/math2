## Statement

The **Forster–Swan theorem** is a result from commutative algebra that states an upper bound for the minimal number of [[generators|0MBV2VHMXA]] of a [[finitely generated module|05QY1VEBYD]] $M$ over a commutative [[Noetherian ring|0670T59HGE]]. The usefulness of the theorem stems from the fact, that in order to form the bound, one only needs the minimum number of generators of all localizations $M_{\mathfrak{p}}$.
