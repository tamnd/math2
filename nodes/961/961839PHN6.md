## Summary

The Péclet number is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that compares transport by the flow with transport by diffusion.
It appears when the [[advection–diffusion equation|DG49VBBT91]] is written in dimensionless form.
When it is large, the flow carries heat or solute much faster than diffusion spreads it, and thin diffusive layers form.
When it is small, diffusion dominates and the flow hardly matters.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A flow can move heat or dye in two ways: it carries it along, or the substance spreads by itself through diffusion.
The Péclet number is the ratio of the two rates.
In a river, dye is carried kilometres before it spreads much sideways, so the Péclet number is huge.
In a drop of water under a microscope, a tiny particle diffuses about as fast as slow flows move it, and the Péclet number is near $1$.
