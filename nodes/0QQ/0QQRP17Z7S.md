## Summary

A linear differential equation is an equation in which the unknown function and its derivatives occur linearly, with coefficients that are functions of the variable.
Its solutions are a particular solution plus the solutions of the associated homogeneous equation, which form a vector space. See the [[definition|0PB7K8M7FG]].
