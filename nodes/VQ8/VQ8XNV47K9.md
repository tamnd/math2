## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|WWQWPSF9Q4]].
It covers the deformation theory of Galois representations, the Taylor-Wiles method and the modularity lifting theorems, from the modularity theorem for elliptic curves to potential modularity, the Sato-Tate theorem and symmetric power functoriality.
