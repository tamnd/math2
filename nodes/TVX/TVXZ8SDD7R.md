## Definition

Let $F$ be a field.
Let $\mathbb{Z}[F]$ be the [[free abelian group|0WJX8MGG37]] on symbols $[x]$ for $x \in F \setminus \{0, 1\}$.
The *pre-Bloch group* $\mathcal{P}(F)$ is the quotient of $\mathbb{Z}[F]$ by the subgroup generated by the five-term elements
$$[x] - [y] + \Big[\frac{y}{x}\Big] - \Big[\frac{1 - x^{-1}}{1 - y^{-1}}\Big] + \Big[\frac{1 - x}{1 - y}\Big]$$
for all $x, y \in F \setminus \{0, 1\}$ with $x \ne y$.
Let $\Lambda^2_\Z F^\times$ be the second [[exterior power|0MDMZ965VE]] over $\Z$ of the [[unit group|07C6ECNJET]] $F^\times$, written additively.
The map $\delta \colon \mathcal{P}(F) \to \Lambda^2_\Z F^\times$, $[x] \mapsto x \wedge (1 - x)$, is a well-defined homomorphism.
The *Bloch group* of $F$ is its kernel:
$$\mathcal{B}(F) = \ker\big(\delta \colon \mathcal{P}(F) \to \Lambda^2_\Z F^\times\big).$$

## Remarks

For $x \ne y$ in $F \setminus \{0, 1\}$, all five arguments lie in $F \setminus \{0, 1\}$, so the five-term elements make sense.

Example: for every $x \in F \setminus \{0, 1\}$, the element $[x] + [1 - x]$ lies in $\mathcal{B}(F)$.
Indeed $\delta([x] + [1 - x]) = x \wedge (1 - x) + (1 - x) \wedge x = 0$.

Non-example: in $\mathcal{P}(\Q)$ the element $[3]$ is not in $\mathcal{B}(\Q)$.
Its image is $3 \wedge (-2) = 3 \wedge (-1) + 3 \wedge 2$, and $3 \wedge 2 \ne 0$ in $\Lambda^2_\Z \Q^\times$, because $3$ and $2$ are independent in the free part of $\Q^\times$.

For $F = \C$, the [[Bloch–Wigner function|Q8K5MWEXB9]] $D$ satisfies the five-term relation and $D(x) + D(1 - x) = 0$, so it induces a homomorphism $\mathcal{P}(\C) \to \R$, and in particular one on $\mathcal{B}(\C)$.
