## Summary

Semi-infinite programming studies optimization problems with finitely many variables and infinitely many constraints.
The constraints are usually indexed by the points of a compact set, such as an interval of time or a region of space.
Best uniform approximation of functions, robust design and filter design lead to such problems.
Methods include discretisation of the index set, exchange methods and reduction to finitely many local constraints.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A usual optimization problem has a list of conditions, such as "at most $10$ hours" and "at least $3$ units".
A semi-infinite problem has a condition for every value of a parameter, for example "the temperature stays below $100$ at every time $s$ between $0$ and $1$".
There are only a few unknowns, but infinitely many conditions.
For example, the best straight line $a + b s$ that stays within distance $t$ of $s^2$ for all $s \in [0, 1]$, with $t$ as small as possible, is a semi-infinite problem.
