## Summary

The Kalai–Smorodinsky bargaining solution is a rule for the two-person [[bargaining problem|93STGYCSTS]].
It gives each player the same fraction of her largest possible gain over the disagreement point.
Ehud Kalai and Meir Smorodinsky proposed it in 1975 as an alternative to the [[Nash bargaining solution|KJGNE96Q17]], replacing Nash's independence axiom by a monotonicity axiom.

## Explanations

### L1 human

For each player, find the best outcome she could hope for if the other player only got his fallback.
Draw the line from the fallback point to the point made of these two best hopes.
The agreement is the farthest point on this line that the players can still reach together.
So each player gets the same share of what she could ideally hope for.
