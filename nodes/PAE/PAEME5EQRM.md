## Summary

A lattice model is a model of statistical mechanics whose variables sit at the sites, or on the edges, of a lattice such as $\Z^d$.
The energy is a sum of local terms that couple only nearby sites.
The [[Ising model|R0KGM32QN6]], the [[Potts model|5GY2J6ZGXJ]] and lattice gases are lattice models.
They are simple enough to analyse rigorously, yet they show phase transitions and critical phenomena of real materials.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Replace a piece of material by a grid, and put at each grid point a small variable, such as a spin pointing up or down.
Neighbouring variables interact, and the total energy is a sum of these neighbour interactions.
The temperature decides how strongly the system prefers low energy over randomness.
For example, on a square grid with spins $\pm 1$ and energy $-1$ for each pair of agreeing neighbours, low temperature makes large regions of equal spins.
