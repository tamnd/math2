## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|43C9KPSDFS]].
It covers the localization, dévissage, resolution and homotopy invariance theorems, the variants KV and KH, rigidity, and the computation of the K-groups of finite fields and of the integers.
