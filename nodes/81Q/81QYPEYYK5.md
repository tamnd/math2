## Summary

In mathematics, a **Størmer number** or **arc-cotangent irreducible number** is a positive [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] $n$ for which the greatest prime factor of $n^2+1$ is greater than or equal to $2n$. They are named after Carl Størmer.
