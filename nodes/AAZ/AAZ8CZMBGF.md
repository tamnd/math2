## Summary

An **imaginary number** is the product of a [[real number|08J2M1JYAG]] and the [[imaginary unit|0ESCX36MMC]] $i$, which is defined by its property $i^{2} = -1$. The square of an imaginary number $bi$ is $-b^{2}$. For example, $5i$ is an imaginary number, and its square is $-25$. The number zero is considered to be both real and imaginary.
