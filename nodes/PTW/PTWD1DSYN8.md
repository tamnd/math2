## Summary

In mathematics, a univariate polynomial of degree $n$ with real or complex coefficients has $n$ complex *roots* (if counted with their multiplicities). They form a [[multiset|MYJ8Y050J0]] of $n$ points in the [[complex plane|0CTE7A31XQ]], whose geometry can be deduced from the degree and the coefficients of the polynomial.
