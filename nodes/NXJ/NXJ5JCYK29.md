## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|9A5DVGMPEB]].
It covers the zeroth, first, second and third laws of thermodynamics, the classical statements of the second law and their equivalence, entropy and absolute temperature, and the axiomatic and geometric forms of the theory.
