## Summary

A completely multiplicative function is an arithmetic function with $f(1) = 1$ and $f(mn) = f(m) f(n)$ for all positive integers $m$ and $n$.
The [[definition of a completely multiplicative function|0GS27TKF1V]] gives the precise form.
