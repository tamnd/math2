## Summary

The microcanonical ensemble describes an isolated system whose energy, volume and particle number are fixed.
It gives equal probability to every [[microstate|S1NST0AG71]] with the prescribed energy.
Its entropy is $k_B \ln W$, where $W$ counts or measures these microstates and $k_B$ is the Boltzmann constant.
The other equilibrium ensembles can be derived from it by looking at a small part of a large isolated system.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Seal a gas in a perfectly insulated box, so that no energy gets in or out.
The energy is known exactly, but the arrangement of the molecules is not.
The microcanonical ensemble says: every arrangement with that energy is equally likely.
For example, if three identical two-level atoms share exactly one unit of energy, there are three microstates, and each has probability $1/3$.
