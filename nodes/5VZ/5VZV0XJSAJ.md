## Problem

In the theory of parallel algorithms, the **1-vs-2 cycles problem** concerns a simplified case of graph connectivity. The input to the problem is a [[2-regular graph|0KA4S8R39M]], forming either a single connected $n$-vertex cycle or two disconnected $n/2$-vertex cycles. The problem is to determine whether the input has one or two cycles.
