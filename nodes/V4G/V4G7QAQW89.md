## Example

Let $S$ be the unilateral shift on $\ell^2(\N)$, $S e_k = e_{k+1}$, and let $\mathcal{T}$ be the [[Toeplitz algebra|3SKRBF4KG1]], the C*-algebra generated by $S$.
Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the [[ideal of compact operators|2WAZF1RV2Y]] on $\ell^2(\N)$.
The *Toeplitz extension* is the short exact sequence
$$0 \to \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{T} \xrightarrow{\sigma} C(\mathbb{T}) \to 0.$$
Here $\sigma$ is the symbol map, determined by $\sigma(S) = z$.
Check of the ideal: $1 - S S^*$ is the rank-one projection onto $\C e_0$, so $\mathcal{T}$ contains a nonzero compact operator.
Since $\mathcal{T}$ acts irreducibly, it contains all of $\mathcal{K}$.
The quotient $\mathcal{T}/\mathcal{K}$ is commutative, because $S^* S - S S^* = 1 - S S^*$ is compact, and it is generated by a unitary with spectrum $\mathbb{T}$, so it is $C(\mathbb{T})$.

The [[index map|XBVMWZJ5FF]] $\partial \colon K_1(C(\mathbb{T})) \to K_0(\mathcal{K})$ sends the generator $[z]$ to $-1$.
Indeed $S$ is a lift of $z$ and a partial isometry, so $\partial[z] = [1 - S^* S] - [1 - S S^*] = 0 - [e_0 \text{ projection}]$, which is $-1$ under $K_0(\mathcal{K}) \cong \Z$.
This is the [[Fredholm index|2V9Q4KHY1T]] of $S$: $\dim \ker S - \dim \ker S^* = 0 - 1$.
Since $\partial$ is an isomorphism $\Z \to \Z$, the [[six-term exact sequence|NMBY32JM60]], with $K_1(\mathcal{K}) = 0$ and $K_0(C(\mathbb{T})) \cong \Z$, gives
$$K_0(\mathcal{T}) \cong \Z, \qquad K_1(\mathcal{T}) = 0.$$
The class $[1]$ generates $K_0(\mathcal{T})$, and the inclusion $\mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{T}$ is zero on $K_0$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Toeplitz algebra is generated by the shift that moves each basis vector one step to the right.
Modulo compact operators, the shift behaves like multiplication by $z$ on the circle, a unitary.
But the shift itself is not unitary: it misses the first basis vector.
The index map records this defect as the number $-1$, and K-theory then computes the groups of the Toeplitz algebra from those of the circle and of the compact operators.
