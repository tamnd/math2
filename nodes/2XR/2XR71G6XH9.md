## Summary

The **Okamoto–Uchiyama cryptosystem** is a public key cryptosystem proposed in 1998 by Tatsuaki Okamoto and Shigenori Uchiyama. The system works in the [[multiplicative group of integers modulo n|0F8X8ZRQ53]], $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^*$, where *n* is of the form *p*<sup>2</sup>*q* and *p* and *q* are large [[primes|01826KMG2H]].
