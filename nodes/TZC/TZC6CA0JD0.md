## Statement

Let $n$ be any [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]], including $0$. Then multiplying $n$ by $1$ on either side gives back $n$. That is, $n \cdot 1 = 1 \cdot n = n$.

Here $\cdot$ is the [[multiplication of natural numbers|06J5DE801P]]. So $1$ is an [[identity element|07WYQPB8E3]] for this operation.

## Remarks

The law holds on both sides. When multiplication is commutative, one side implies the other. When multiplication is built from repeated addition, the two sides are shown separately.
