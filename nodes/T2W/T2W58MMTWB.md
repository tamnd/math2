## Summary

In graph theory, an $n$-dimensional **De Bruijn graph** of $m$ symbols is a [[directed graph|0KFYGSHHF5]] representing overlaps between sequences of symbols. It has $m^{n}$ vertices, consisting of all possible length-$n$ sequences of the given symbols; the same symbol may appear multiple times in a sequence.
