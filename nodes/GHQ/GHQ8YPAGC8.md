## Summary

The reduced group C\*-algebra $C^*_r(G)$ of a [[locally compact group|AHV05ZBCYW]] $G$ is a [[C*-algebra|M2WFY1D3SZ]] of operators on $L^2(G)$.
It is the norm closure of the operators of convolution by integrable functions.
It is built from the [[left regular representation|PR8C073VWY]] alone, while the full [[group C*-algebra|JKANN4VBYH]] uses all unitary representations.
The natural map from the full algebra onto the reduced one is an isomorphism exactly when $G$ is an [[amenable group|CEAT9BESKK]].
For abelian $G$ the reduced group C\*-algebra is the algebra of continuous functions vanishing at infinity on the [[dual group|FMW57WNPDB]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A group acts on the functions on itself by shifting them.
Mixing these shifts with integrable weights gives operators of convolution with a function.
The reduced group C\*-algebra collects these operators and all their limits in operator norm.
For the integers, convolution on $\ell^2(\Z)$ becomes multiplication by a function on the circle after the Fourier transform.
So $C^*_r(\Z)$ is the algebra of continuous functions on the circle.
