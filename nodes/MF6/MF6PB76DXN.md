## Summary

This topic is a part of [[manifolds and differential topology|15PK587CVP]].
It covers the calculus of maps in infinite dimensions that differential topology uses, with the inverse and implicit function theorems in Banach and Fréchet spaces, the Sard-Smale theorem, degree for Fredholm maps, and fixed point and Newton methods on manifolds.
