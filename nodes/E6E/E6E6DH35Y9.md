## Statement

For $c \in \C$ let $f_c(z) = z^2 + c$ be the [[quadratic family|WDZFV2ACXR]], and let $M$ be the [[Mandelbrot set|QSWSVYRCS1]].
For $c \in M$, call $f_c$ hyperbolic when it has an attracting periodic cycle in $\C$.
The conjecture states that the set of $c \in M$ for which $f_c$ is hyperbolic is [[dense|0BSJSX7ME2]] in $M$.

## Remarks

The set of hyperbolic parameters in $M$ is open, and its connected components are called hyperbolic components.
An equivalent form of the conjecture says that every connected component of the interior of $M$ is a hyperbolic component.
The main cardioid, where $f_c$ has an attracting fixed point, is one hyperbolic component.
The quadratic case is part of a broader conjecture attributed to Fatou: hyperbolic maps are dense among rational maps of each degree.
The conjecture is open.
For real parameters $c \in [-2, 1/4]$ it is proved: the real hyperbolic parameters are dense in $[-2, 1/4]$, a result of Lyubich and, independently, of Graczyk and Świątek.
Douady and Hubbard showed that the local connectivity of $M$ (the MLC conjecture) would imply this conjecture.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A hyperbolic map is one whose behaviour is stable.
Apart from a thin set of exceptional points, every starting point either escapes to infinity or is pulled towards a single repeating cycle.
Small changes of $c$ do not change the picture of such a map in any essential way.
The conjecture says that stable maps are everywhere in the Mandelbrot set: arbitrarily close to any parameter of $M$ there is one with an attracting cycle.
So the chaotic parameters, while they exist, could never fill a whole region.
