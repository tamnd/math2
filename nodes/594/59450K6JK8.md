## Summary

Maximum parsimony is a criterion for choosing a [[phylogenetic tree|RK58WSHJSG]]: prefer the tree that explains the observed characters with the fewest evolutionary changes.
For a fixed tree the least number of changes is easy to compute, but finding the best tree among all trees is NP-hard.
The method is simple and needs no model of evolution.
But it can be misled when rates of change differ a lot between lineages, as in [[long branch attraction|RRCMFK045C]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Compare the DNA of four species at one position, where two of them have the letter A and two have the letter G in that place.
The tree that groups the two A species together needs only one mutation to explain this position.
A tree that splits them needs at least two.
Maximum parsimony adds up such counts over all positions and picks the tree with the smallest total.
