## Summary

In mathematics, a **locally constant function** is a [[function|0EPAT51DXQ]] from a [[topological space|0CQHFPGJ45]] into a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] with the property that around every point of its [[domain|0Q4CBD3HX0]], there exists some neighborhood of that point on which it restricts to a constant function.
