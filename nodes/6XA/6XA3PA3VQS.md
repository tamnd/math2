## Summary

This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|S5M5GNQ7P1]].
It covers linear differential operators between vector bundles over manifolds, their symbols, Sobolev theory, and the Fredholm property of elliptic operators on closed manifolds and on manifolds with boundary.
