## Definition

Let $R$ be a [[lambda-ring|E2KA3XP47F]] with operations $\lambda^n$, and give $\Z$ the lambda-ring structure $\lambda^n(k) = \binom{k}{n}$.
Let $\varepsilon \colon R \to \Z$ be an *augmentation*, that is, a ring homomorphism with $\varepsilon(\lambda^n(x)) = \binom{\varepsilon(x)}{n}$ for all $x$ and $n$.
The *gamma operations* $\gamma^n \colon R \to R$ are defined by
$$\sum_{n \ge 0} \gamma^n(x) t^n = \lambda_{t/(1-t)}(x) = \sum_{n \ge 0} \lambda^n(x) t^n (1 - t)^{-n}.$$
The *gamma filtration* is the sequence of subgroups $R = F^0_\gamma \supseteq F^1_\gamma \supseteq F^2_\gamma \supseteq \cdots$ with $F^0_\gamma = R$, $F^1_\gamma = \ker \varepsilon$, and, for $n \ge 1$, $F^n_\gamma$ the additive subgroup generated by all products
$$\gamma^{n_1}(x_1) \gamma^{n_2}(x_2) \cdots \gamma^{n_k}(x_k), \qquad x_i \in \ker \varepsilon, \quad n_1 + \cdots + n_k \ge n.$$

## Remarks

Each $F^n_\gamma$ is an ideal and $F^m_\gamma F^n_\gamma \subseteq F^{m+n}_\gamma$, so the graded group $\bigoplus_n F^n_\gamma / F^{n+1}_\gamma$ is a ring.

Example: in $K_0(\mathbb{P}^1_k) = \Z[t]/(1 - t)^2$ with $t = [\mathcal{O}(-1)]$ and $\varepsilon$ the rank, the element $x = t - 1$ lies in $F^1_\gamma$.
For a line element $L$ one has $\gamma_t(L - 1) = 1 + (L - 1) t$, so $\gamma^1(x) = x$ and $\gamma^n(x) = 0$ for $n \ge 2$.
Here $x^2 = 0$, and $F^2_\gamma = 0$.

The filtration needs the augmentation: the ring $\Z \times \Z$ with componentwise binomial operations has two augmentations, the two projections, and they give different $F^1_\gamma$.

For a smooth quasi-projective variety $X$ over a field, with $\varepsilon$ the rank on a connected $X$, the Chern character induces isomorphisms $F^n_\gamma / F^{n+1}_\gamma \otimes \Q \cong \operatorname{CH}^n(X) \otimes \Q$.
