## Summary

Means "less than or equal to". That is, whatever $A$ and $B$ are, $A \le B$ is equivalent to $A < B\text{ or } A = B$.

Between two groups, may mean that the first one is a [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] of the second one.
