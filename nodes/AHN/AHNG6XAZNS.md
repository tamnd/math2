## Summary

In geometry, an **epicycloid** (also called **hypercycloid**) is a plane curve produced by tracing the path of a chosen point on the circumference of a [[circle|060W5CBZP6]]—called an *epicycle*—which rolls without slipping around a fixed circle. It is a particular kind of roulette.
