## Summary

The McKendrick–von Foerster equation is a first-order [[partial differential equation|GC5BDZ6QBC]] for the age density of a population in continuous time and continuous age.
It is a [[transport equation|PVD7VZJZ9B]] with a death term, closed by a boundary condition at age $0$ that counts the births.
It is the continuous analogue of the Leslie model and the basic equation of age-structured [[population models|1YEMFQ1H32]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture the population as a crowd walking along an age axis: everyone ages one year per year, so the crowd drifts to the right at speed one.
On the way, people drop out because they die.
New people enter at age zero, and how many enter depends on how many people of child-bearing age there are.
The equation says exactly this, with a death rate $\mu(a)$ and a birth rate $\beta(a)$ for each age $a$.
