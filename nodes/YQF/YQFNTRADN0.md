## Summary

This topic is a part of [[arithmetic geometry|73BKSN9KXY]].
It covers the conjecture of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer, its refined formula, and the methods that prove it in analytic rank at most one, such as Heegner points, Euler systems and Iwasawa theory.
