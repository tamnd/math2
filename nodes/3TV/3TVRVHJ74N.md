## Statement

(Ford 1999) proved that for every integer $k \ge 2$ there is a totient number $m$ of multiplicity $k$: that is, for which the equation $\varphi(n) = m$ has exactly $k$ solutions; this result had previously been conjectured by Wacław Sierpiński, and it had been obtained as a consequence of Schinzel's hypothesis H. Indeed, each multiplicity that occurs, does so infinitely often.

However, no number $m$ is known with multiplicity $k = 1$. Carmichael's totient function conjecture is the statement that there is no such $m$.
