## Summary

The Gompertz growth model is a [[population model|1YEMFQ1H32]] of limited growth in which the per capita growth rate is proportional to $\ln(K/N)$.
Its solutions are Gompertz functions: S-shaped curves that rise to the [[carrying capacity|X23CRWHRCH]] $K$ but are not symmetric about their inflection point.
It is used for the growth of tumours, of organisms, and of the sales of new products.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Like the logistic curve, the Gompertz curve starts low, rises, and levels off at a ceiling $K$.
The difference is in the shape: the Gompertz curve rises fast early and approaches the ceiling slowly.
Its fastest growth happens at about $37\%$ of the ceiling, at $K/e$, while for the logistic curve it happens at half the ceiling.
Tumour sizes often follow such a curve: rapid early growth that slows as the tumour becomes large.
