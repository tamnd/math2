## Summary

Physical cosmology is the study of the universe as a whole: its large-scale structure, its expansion, its contents and its history.
It applies general relativity, nuclear and particle physics and statistical physics to the universe on the largest scales, and tests the resulting models against observations.
Its standard model describes an expanding universe that was hot and dense in the past.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Astronomy studies stars and galaxies; physical cosmology asks about the universe they all live in.
How old is it, how big, what is it made of, and how did galaxies form?
A key observation is that distant galaxies move away from us, faster the farther they are, so the universe expands.
Running the expansion backwards gives a hot dense early universe, which explains the light elements and the faint microwave glow that fills the sky.
