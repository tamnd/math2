## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers linear systems in any number of unknowns over a field, their matrices, elementary row operations, echelon forms, elimination, and the structure of the solution set.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Linear equation|0Q3ZT8J05R]]: one equation in $n$ unknowns over a field, and its solution set.
2. [[System of linear equations|0GT4WWGWH2]]: the row picture, the column picture and the matrix form $Ax = b$.
3. [[Consistent and inconsistent systems|0X6V9B1V10]]: systems with and without solutions.
4. [[Homogeneous system|04KGXJG1A2]]: the system $Ax = 0$ and its nontrivial solutions.
5. [[Coefficient matrix|0P7A2MJD24]]: the matrix of coefficients and $Ax$ as a combination of columns.
6. [[Augmented matrix|0YCDZ7N8VD]]: the matrix $[A \mid b]$ and several right sides at once.
7. [[Elementary row operations|04XWH28452]]: the three operations, their inverses and elementary matrices.
8. [[Row equivalence|006BHFWMAP]]: an equivalence relation that keeps solutions, row space and null space.
9. [[Row echelon form|0VYCDGMJJJ]]: the staircase pattern, and the two meanings of "echelon form".
10. [[Reduced row echelon form|0WF5AQYTW6]]: leading $1$s with zeros above and below, and reading the solutions.
11. [[Uniqueness of the reduced row echelon form|BF9SMZMJZE]]: one RREF for each matrix, with proof.
12. [[Pivots, basic variables and free variables|0J0BBCMJ5W]]: pivot positions and columns, and the two meanings of "pivot".
13. [[Gaussian elimination|0JVJ2WCXZH]]: the forward phase, row exchanges, and the count $\tfrac23 n^3$.
14. [[Back substitution|0ATFVE08MW]]: triangular systems from the bottom up, and forward substitution.
15. [[Gauss-Jordan elimination|098D6FQN3N]]: reduction to the RREF, its cost, and inverses.
16. [[Linear systems as vector equations|E7SAHXQEVC]]: consistency means that $b$ is in the column space.
17. [[Existence and uniqueness of solutions|BKA191KD1S]]: the pivot test for none, one or many solutions.
18. [[Parametric form of the solution set|KQT68YPHD1]]: a point plus a span of independent directions.
19. [[Structure of the solution set|PDH3HKXWAZ]]: a particular solution plus the null space.
20. [[Rouché-Capelli theorem|0HV1SKWPMS]]: consistency and dimension by ranks.
21. [[Overdetermined and underdetermined systems|QJ0P26J9BV]]: the usual behaviour, least squares and minimum length.
22. [[Linear systems over finite fields|J6A58MEM8R]]: elimination modulo $p$, the count $q^{n-r}$, and Lights Out.

### Prerequisites

- [[Vectors in the plane and in space|VKVAJ94SKZ]]: vectors, lines and planes in coordinates.
- [[Elementary algebra and functions|DPW5ETDQ27]]: systems of two and three equations solved by substitution.
