## Summary

Reasoning and proof is the study of how mathematicians state results, prove them, write the proofs, and find them.
A proof is an argument that shows that a statement follows from the axioms, the definitions and the results that were proved before.
The area treats logic as a tool of practice: it explains how to read and write statements, which methods of proof exist, and how to check a proof.
This topic collects the pages on the nature of proof, the language of statements, definitions, notation, the proof methods, induction, counting and extremal arguments, proof writing, errors in proofs, heuristics, experimental mathematics, rigour and the teaching of proof.
Formal logic, set theory and constructive mathematics have their own topics.
