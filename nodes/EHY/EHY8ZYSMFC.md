This topic is a part of [[fluid mechanics|B8CZ8SX1G5]].
It covers finite-amplitude waves, with Stokes waves and the highest wave, the variational and Hamiltonian forms of the water wave problem, the derivation of the weakly nonlinear model equations, modulational instability, breaking, and the statistics of ocean waves.
