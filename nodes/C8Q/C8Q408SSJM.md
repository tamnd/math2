## Summary

In algebraic number theory, an **algebraic integer** is a [[complex number|09FTAET5Z4]] that is integral over the [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]]. That is, an algebraic integer is a complex root of some polynomial which is [[monic|0BDX4MBKT4]] (its leading coefficient is 1) and has coefficients that are all integers. The set of all algebraic integers $A$ is closed under addition, subtraction and multiplication and therefore is a [[commutative|0310REJJY8]] [[subring|0GNCRPYXYV]] of the complex numbers.
