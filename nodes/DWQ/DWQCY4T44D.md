## Summary

In mathematics, a **permutation polynomial** (for a given [[ring|0B1261B5ED]]) is a polynomial that acts as a [[permutation|05RWFKVTJ7]] of the elements of the ring, i.e. the map $x \mapsto g(x)$ is a [[bijection|0GG9TVW90M]]. In case the ring is a [[finite field|0ZXZ82FTX2]], the Dickson polynomials, which are closely related to the Chebyshev polynomials, provide examples.  Over a finite field, every function, so in particular every permutation of the elements of that field, can be written as a polynomial function.
