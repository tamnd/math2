## Summary

Image of a subset: if $S$ is a [[subset|0BJR9QR08E]] of the [[domain of the function|0Q4CBD3HX0]] $f$, then $f[S]$ is sometimes used for denoting the image of $S$. When no confusion is possible, notation $f(S)$ is commonly used.
