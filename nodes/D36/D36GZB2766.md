## Summary

An irreversible process is a process that is not [[reversible|F3DHZ45H6A]]: the system and its surroundings cannot both be brought back to their initial states.
All real processes are irreversible, because of friction, heat flow across temperature differences, mixing and similar effects.
In an irreversible process the total [[entropy|SPY89W0RJ7]] of the system and its surroundings increases.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Milk stirred into coffee never separates again by itself.
A hot pan left on the table cools down, and it never warms up again by taking heat from the cooler air.
These are irreversible processes: they go one way only.
They are the reason why time has a direction in everyday life.
