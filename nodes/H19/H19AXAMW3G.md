## Summary

In mathematics, and more particularly in number theory, **primorial**, denoted by "$p_{n}\#$", is a [[function|0EPAT51DXQ]] from [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]] to natural numbers similar to the [[factorial|0XWG0JGDFW]] function, but rather than successively multiplying positive integers, the function only multiplies [[prime numbers|01826KMG2H]].
