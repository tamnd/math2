## Summary

The Frobenius endomorphism of a commutative ring of prime characteristic $p$ is the map that sends each element to its $p$-th power.
It is a ring homomorphism.
The [[definition of the Frobenius endomorphism|0GHDDVAH8P]] gives the details.
