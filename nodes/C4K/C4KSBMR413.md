## Summary

The Prandtl number $\mathrm{Pr} = \nu / \alpha$ is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that compares how fast a fluid diffuses momentum with how fast it diffuses heat.
Unlike most dimensionless numbers it depends only on the fluid and its state, not on the flow.
It controls the relative thickness of the velocity and thermal boundary layers, so it enters every problem of heat transfer by convection.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Stir a fluid and the motion spreads to its neighbours through [[viscosity|NM5Q4845Z4]]; heat one spot and the warmth spreads through conduction.
The Prandtl number says which of the two spreads faster.
For air it is about $0.7$, so motion and heat spread at similar rates.
For water it is about $7$ at room temperature, and for thick oils it is in the hundreds or more.
In those fluids heat spreads much more slowly than motion.
