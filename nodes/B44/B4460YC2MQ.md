## Definition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \R^n$ be a domain and let $\alpha > 0$ be a [[thermal diffusivity|CS4ZZX092Z]].
Consider a heat conduction problem for $u(x, t)$, with $x \in \Omega$ and $t > 0$.
It consists of the [[heat equation|VNB1BFDEYK]] $u_t = \alpha \Delta u + g(x, t)$ and an initial temperature $u(x, 0) = u_0(x)$.
It also has linear boundary conditions on $\partial \Omega$ whose coefficients do not depend on $t$.
Assume that $u$, $g$ and the boundary data have [[Laplace transforms|05XT5AVJBK]] in $t$ for $s$ in some half-line $s > s_0$.
The *Laplace transform method* consists of the following steps.

1. Transform in $t$: put $U(x, s) = \int_0^\infty e^{-st} u(x, t)\, dt$, and likewise $G(x, s)$ for $g$ and transforms for the boundary data.
2. Use the [[rule for derivatives|0K090GVHVN]], $\mathcal{L}\set{u_t} = s U - u_0$, to get the boundary value problem $\alpha \Delta U - s U = -u_0 - G$ in $\Omega$ with the transformed boundary conditions.
3. Solve this problem for $U(\cdot, s)$, with $s$ as a parameter.
4. Recover $u$ by the [[inverse Laplace transform|0JRW4X1MDC]], using tables, the convolution theorem or the Bromwich integral.

## Remarks

Example: a semi-infinite solid $x > 0$ at initial temperature $0$, with [[thermal conductivity|XBQBT9E7JT]] $k$, receives a constant heat flux $q_0$ at its surface, so $-k u_x(0, t) = q_0$.
Step 2 gives $\alpha U_{xx} = s U$, and the bounded solutions are $U = A(s) e^{-x \sqrt{s/\alpha}}$.
The boundary condition $-k U_x(0, s) = q_0 / s$ gives $A(s) = q_0 \sqrt{\alpha} / (k s^{3/2})$.
Since $\mathcal{L}\set{2 \sqrt{t / \pi}} = s^{-3/2}$, the surface temperature is $u(0, t) = (2 q_0 / k) \sqrt{\alpha t / \pi}$.
Non-example: let the surface lose heat with a coefficient $h(t)$ that varies in time, so $-k u_x(0, t) = h(t)\, u(0, t)$.
The transform of $h(t)\, u(0, t)$ is not a multiple of $U(0, s)$, so step 2 does not give a boundary value problem for $U$ alone.
The behaviour of $U$ for large $s$ gives the behaviour of $u$ for small $t$, which is useful when the inversion cannot be done in closed form.
