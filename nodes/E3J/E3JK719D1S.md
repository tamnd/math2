## Statement

Let $F$ be a [[graph|0JD5C82QMB]].
Then $F$ is a [[forest|09JP6G9FTA]] if and only if, for any two vertices $u$ and $v$ of $F$, there is at most one [[path|0JDE56HWQ8]] from $u$ to $v$.

## Remarks

This extends the case of trees, where connectedness gives exactly one path between two vertices. In a forest, two vertices in different components have no path between them.
Two different paths between the same two vertices would together contain a cycle, and this is why a forest has at most one.
