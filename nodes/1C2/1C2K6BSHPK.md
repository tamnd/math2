## Summary

KR-theory is a version of topological K-theory for spaces with an [[involution|GP56NBN9RY]], introduced by Michael Atiyah.
It is built from complex vector bundles with an antilinear involution that lies over the involution of the space.
It contains both [[complex K-theory|9E4JE4902G]] and [[real K-theory|P7MN6MQV7P]] as special cases, and it explains why real Bott periodicity has period $8$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Complex conjugation turns a complex vector space into itself, but it is antilinear: it changes $i$ into $-i$.
A "Real" space in Atiyah's sense is a space with a flip, such as the complex plane with conjugation.
A Real vector bundle over it is a complex vector bundle with a conjugation that follows the flip of the base.
When the flip of the base does nothing, such a bundle is the same as a real vector bundle, and KR-theory becomes real K-theory.
When the space is two copies of $X$ exchanged by the flip, KR-theory becomes complex K-theory of $X$.
