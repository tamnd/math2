## Summary

The **Misra & Gries edge-coloring algorithm** is a polynomial-time algorithm in graph theory that finds an [[edge coloring|0B5WCC02AN]] of any simple graph. The coloring produced uses at most $\Delta+1$ colors, where $\Delta$ is the maximum [[degree|0KS0KRDV4Y]] of the graph. This is optimal for some graphs, and it uses at most one color more than optimal for all others. The existence of such a coloring is guaranteed by Vizing's theorem.
