## Summary

Mark and recapture is a method to estimate the size of an animal population that cannot be counted directly.
A first sample of animals is caught, marked and released.
Later a second sample is caught, and the share of marked animals in it is used to infer the total size.
The simplest [[estimator|0SSS6ERQ0S]] it gives is the Lincoln–Petersen estimator.

## Explanations

### L1 human

You want to know how many fish live in a lake.
You catch $100$ fish, tag them, and let them go.
A week later you catch $50$ fish and find $5$ tagged ones, so one fish in ten is tagged.
If the tagged fish are mixed well, about one fish in ten in the whole lake is tagged too, so the lake holds about $100 \times 10 = 1000$ fish.
