## Definition

Let $k \ge 1$ and $n \ge 1$.
Write the [[sphere|T62369RFH6]] $S^k$ as the union of two closed hemispheres $D_+$ and $D_-$, each homeomorphic to the disc $D^k$, with intersection the equator $S^{k-1}$.
Let $f \colon S^{k-1} \to GL_n(\C)$ be a continuous map into the [[general linear group|0FCHE902D7]].
The *clutching construction* produces the complex [[vector bundle|P4DZM975BV]]
$$E_f = \left( (D_- \times \C^n) \sqcup (D_+ \times \C^n) \right) / \sim$$
over $S^k$, where $(x, v) \in D_- \times \C^n$ is identified with $(x, f(x) v) \in D_+ \times \C^n$ for every $x \in S^{k-1}$ and $v \in \C^n$.
The map $f$ is the *clutching function* of $E_f$.

## Remarks

If $f$ and $g$ are [[homotopic|9YA6S6HD9V]], then $E_f \cong E_g$.
Every complex vector bundle of rank $n$ over $S^k$ is isomorphic to some $E_f$.
Since $GL_n(\C)$ is connected, this gives a bijection between homotopy classes of maps $S^{k-1} \to GL_n(\C)$ and isomorphism classes of rank $n$ complex vector bundles over $S^k$.
The same construction with $GL_n(\R)$ gives real vector bundles; then base points and orientations need more care, as $GL_n(\R)$ has two components.

Example: for $k = 2$ and $n = 1$, the maps $f_m(z) = z^m$ from the unit circle to $\C^\times = GL_1(\C)$ represent all homotopy classes.
So the line bundles over $S^2$ are the $E_{f_m}$, $m \in \Z$, classified by the degree $m$.
The tautological line bundle over $\C P^1 = S^2$ is $E_{f_m}$ for $m = 1$ or $m = -1$, depending on orientation conventions.

Non-example: the constant map $f = 0$ into the $n \times n$ matrices is continuous, but its values are not invertible, so it is not a map into $GL_n(\C)$.
The gluing then collapses fibres over the equator, and the quotient is not a vector bundle.
