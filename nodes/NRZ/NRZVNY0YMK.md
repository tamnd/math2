## Summary

In mathematics, the **Euler sequence** is a particular exact sequence of sheaves on *n*-dimensional projective space over a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]]. It shows that the sheaf of relative differentials is stably isomorphic to an $(n+1)$-fold sum of the dual of the Serre twisting sheaf.
