## Summary

In mathematics, the **ring of modular forms** associated to a [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] $\Gamma$ of the [[special linear group|0V9PMN0RBH]] $\operatorname{SL}(2, \mathbf{Z})$ is the graded ring generated by the modular forms of $\Gamma$. The study of rings of modular forms describes the algebraic structure of the space of modular forms.
