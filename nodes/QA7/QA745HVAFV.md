## Summary

In number theory, **Heegner numbers** are [[square-free positive integers|004JW7A2N5]] $d$ such that the imaginary quadratic field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ has class number 1. Equivalently, the ring of algebraic integers of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ has unique factorization.
