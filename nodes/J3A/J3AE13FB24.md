## Summary

An extension *R*⊆*S* of commutative rings is said to have the going down property if whenever *p*<sub>1</sub>⊆*p*<sub>2</sub> is a chain of prime ideals in *R* and *q*<sub>2</sub> is a prime ideal of *S* with *q*<sub>2</sub>∩*R*=*p*<sub>2</sub>, there is a prime ideal *q*<sub>1</sub> of *S* with *q*<sub>1</sub>⊆*q*<sub>2</sub> and *q*<sub>1</sub>∩*R*=*p*<sub>1</sub>.

The going down theorem states that an integral extension *R*⊆*S* such that *S* is a domain and *R* is integrally closed has the going down property.
