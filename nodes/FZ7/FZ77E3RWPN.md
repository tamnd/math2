## Summary

A Lie ring is an [[abelian group|082GHNPJ53]] with a biadditive bracket that is alternating and satisfies the Jacobi identity.
It is a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] without a field of scalars, or equivalently a Lie algebra over the integers $\Z$.
Every associative ring becomes a Lie ring under the commutator $xy - yx$.
Lie rings appear in group theory, for example through the lower central series of a group.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Lie algebra lets you add vectors, multiply them by scalars, and take brackets.
A Lie ring drops the scalars and keeps only addition and the bracket.
For example, the vectors of $\Z^3$ with integer coordinates, with the cross product as bracket, form a Lie ring.
So does the ring of $n \times n$ integer matrices with the bracket $AB - BA$.
