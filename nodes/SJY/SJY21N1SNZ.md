## Summary

The angular power spectrum of the cosmic microwave background gives the strength of its temperature fluctuations on each angular scale on the sky.
It is the sequence of variances $C_\ell$ of the coefficients of the temperature map in [[spherical harmonics|3F32Y21HP4]].
Its peaks, produced by sound waves before [[recombination|EH021K5438]], fix many cosmological parameters with high precision.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The microwave sky has the same temperature in all directions up to tiny ripples of about one part in a hundred thousand.
Break the map of these ripples into waves on the sphere, from large blobs to fine speckles.
The power spectrum tells how strong the ripples are at each size.
It shows a large peak at about one degree on the sky, then smaller peaks, then a fall-off at small scales.
The positions and heights of these peaks measure the geometry and contents of the universe.
