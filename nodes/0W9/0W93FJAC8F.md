## Summary

A Fermat pseudoprime to a base $a$ is a composite number $n$ that satisfies the congruence $a^{n-1} \equiv 1 \pmod n$ of Fermat's little theorem.
The [[definition of a Fermat pseudoprime|02M2K4QH9P]] gives the precise form.
