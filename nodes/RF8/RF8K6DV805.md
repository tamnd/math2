## Summary

Secret sharing splits a secret into shares given to several participants, so that enough of them together can rebuild the secret.
In a threshold scheme with threshold $t$, any $t$ shares determine the secret, while fewer than $t$ shares reveal nothing about it.
Adi Shamir and George Blakley introduced threshold schemes independently in 1979.
Secret sharing is a basic tool for protecting keys and for secure multiparty computation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A bank vault might need any two of three managers to open it, so that no single manager can open it alone.
Secret sharing does this with information: each manager gets a share of the code.
Two shares together give the code, and one share alone tells nothing.
A simple case with two people: give one a random number $r$ and the other $s - r$; their sum is the secret $s$, and each part alone looks random.
