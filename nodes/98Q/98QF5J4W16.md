## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers logics that drop or control the structural rules, such as linear logic, the Lambek calculus, relevance logics, affine and bunched logics, and the full Lambek calculus as their common base.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Substructural logic|~fef4b751]]: the family of logics that drop weakening, contraction or exchange.
2. [[Weakening|VHRXX0T5XT]]: the rule that adds a formula to a sequent.
3. [[Contraction|~54d8cf2f]]: the rule that merges two copies of a formula.
4. [[Exchange|YAFD18WYJ8]]: the rule that permutes the formulas of a sequent.
5. [[Full Lambek calculus|~31b7d92c]]: the common base FL and its extensions by structural rules.
6. [[Lambek calculus|5FAC9KX4H0]]: the non-commutative calculus of categorial grammar.
7. [[Linear logic|2P2184B6HE]]: the multiplicative, additive and exponential connectives of Girard.
8. [[Proof net|~ba2ae7a0]]: graphs for multiplicative proofs and the Danos-Regnier criterion.
9. [[Phase semantics|~db29c154]]: models of linear logic in commutative monoids.
10. [[Coherence space|~3ae331d6]]: webs, cliques and stable functions.
11. [[Decidability of linear logic|~673b5224]]: the complexity of MLL, MALL and full linear logic.
12. [[Light linear logics|~76e48ce9]]: weak exponentials and polynomial time.
13. [[Relevance logic|~366e4c45]]: variable sharing and the systems T, E and R.
14. [[Routley-Meyer semantics|H4Z8J0KMAY]]: ternary frames and the Routley star.
15. [[Urquhart's undecidability theorem|~35a13d34]]: the relevance logics R, E and T are undecidable.
16. [[Affine logic|K67C2N2QAH]]: linear logic with weakening.
17. [[Bunched logic|~b9c991fb]]: two conjunctions, two implications and resource models.
18. [[Abelian logic|W3CPQAFQJ3]]: the logic of the ordered group of real numbers.
19. [[Ludics|~dbdabe57]]: Girard's analysis of inference rules by loci and focusing.

### Prerequisites

- [[Structural proof theory|R10RMQCJQX]]: sequent calculus, structural rules and cut elimination.
- [[Intuitionistic and intermediate logics|38EQ20GR7M]]: intuitionistic logic and Kripke semantics.
- Basic notions of monoids, lattices and ordered groups.
