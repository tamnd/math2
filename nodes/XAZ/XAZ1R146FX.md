## Statement

In graph theory, the **Graham–Pollak theorem** states that the edges of an $n$-vertex [[complete graph|03WKB2H474]] cannot be partitioned into fewer than $n-1$ [[complete bipartite graphs|0V0GC7BQAN]]. It was first published by Ronald Graham and Henry O. Pollak in two papers in 1971 and 1972 (crediting Hans Witsenhausen for a key lemma), in connection with an application to telephone switching circuitry.
