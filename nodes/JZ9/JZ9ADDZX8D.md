## Summary

The light cone of an event is the set of events that a flash of light emitted at, or arriving at, that event can reach.
In [[Minkowski space|G49G4R1K04]] it is the double cone of null vectors through the event.
It splits spacetime into the future, the past and the elsewhere of the event, and so encodes which events can influence which.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a flash of light at one point at one moment.
The light spreads out as a growing sphere; drawn in spacetime, with time going up, this sphere sweeps out a cone.
Events inside the upper cone can be reached by something slower than light, so they are in the future of the flash.
With one space dimension and $c = 1$, the light cone of the origin is the pair of lines $x = t$ and $x = -t$.
