## Proof

We derive the diamond forms of $\mathrm{T}$, $\mathrm{4}$ and $\mathrm{5}$ first, and then we derive the missing axioms of each logic from the axioms it has, which proves [[the axioms of S5|ECYRK9C2ZT]].
We use the derived rules of [[normal modal logic K|JS8W7PA6JK]]: RM (from $\vdash \alpha \to \beta$ infer $\vdash \square\alpha \to \square\beta$), RE, the duality $\lozenge = \neg\square\neg$, and propositional reasoning with [[tautologies|05006AJ7DZ]].

### Part A: three diamond forms

1. With $\mathrm{T}$, the instance $\square\neg p \to \neg p$ contraposes to $p \to \neg\square\neg p$, which is $p \to \lozenge p$.
2. With $\mathrm{4}$, the instance $\square\neg p \to \square\square\neg p$ contraposes to $\neg\square\square\neg p \to \neg\square\neg p$.
3. By RE, $\neg\square\square\neg p$ is equivalent to $\neg\square\neg\neg\square\neg p$, which is $\lozenge\lozenge p$.
   The consequent $\neg\square\neg p$ is $\lozenge p$, so step 2 gives $\lozenge\lozenge p \to \lozenge p$.
4. With $\mathrm{5}$, the instance $\lozenge\neg p \to \square\lozenge\neg p$ contraposes to $\neg\square\lozenge\neg p \to \neg\lozenge\neg p$.
5. By duality, $\neg\lozenge\neg p$ is equivalent to $\square p$, and $\neg\square\lozenge\neg p$ is equivalent to $\lozenge\neg\lozenge\neg p$, hence by RE to $\lozenge\square p$.
   So step 4 gives $\lozenge\square p \to \square p$.

### Part B: KTB4 is contained in KT5

6. $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{B}$, because chaining $p \to \lozenge p$ from step 1 with the axiom $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$ gives $p \to \square\lozenge p$.
7. The instance of $\mathrm{B}$ with $\square p$ for $p$ is $\square p \to \square\lozenge\square p$.
8. RM applied to step 5 gives $\square\lozenge\square p \to \square\square p$.
9. Chaining steps 7 and 8 gives $\square p \to \square\square p$, so $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{4}$.
10. $\mathsf{KT5}$ is a normal logic that contains $\mathrm{T}$, $\mathrm{B}$ and $\mathrm{4}$, so it contains the smallest such logic, and $\mathsf{KTB4} \subseteq \mathsf{KT5}$.

### Part C: KT5 is contained in KTB4

11. The instance of $\mathrm{B}$ with $\lozenge p$ for $p$ is $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge\lozenge p$.
12. RM applied to step 3 gives $\square\lozenge\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$.
13. Chaining steps 11 and 12 gives $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$, so $\mathsf{KTB4}$ contains $\mathrm{5}$.
14. $\mathsf{KTB4}$ also contains $\mathrm{T}$, so $\mathsf{KT5} \subseteq \mathsf{KTB4}$ by minimality.

### Part D: the other two logics

15. $\mathsf{KT45}$ and $\mathsf{KTB5}$ contain $\mathrm{T}$ and $\mathrm{5}$, so each contains $\mathsf{KT5}$.
16. $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{4}$ by step 9 and $\mathrm{B}$ by step 6, so it contains $\mathsf{KT45}$ and $\mathsf{KTB5}$ by minimality.
17. Parts B, C and D give $\mathsf{KT5} = \mathsf{KTB4} = \mathsf{KT45} = \mathsf{KTB5}$.

## Idea

Each missing axiom is a composition of two axioms that the logic has.
The axiom $\mathrm{5}$ is $\mathrm{B}$ followed by the diamond form of $\mathrm{4}$, and $\mathrm{4}$ is $\mathrm{B}$ followed by the diamond form of $\mathrm{5}$.
The axiom $\mathrm{T}$ is used only once, to get $\mathrm{B}$ from $\mathrm{5}$ through $p \to \lozenge p$.
That is why the case without $\mathrm{T}$ behaves differently.

## Variants

A semantic proof goes through frames.
By [[the completeness theorem|FXTCKYR3AQ]] and its canonical model method, each of the four logics is complete for its class of frames.
The four classes are the reflexive frames that are Euclidean, that are symmetric and transitive, that are transitive and Euclidean, and that are symmetric and Euclidean.
Each class is exactly the class of [[equivalence relations|0G8VEZ3WTK]], so the four logics have the same valid formulas, and by completeness the same theorems.
This proof is shorter, but it uses the full completeness theorem, while the derivations above use only the rules of $\mathsf{K}$.
