## Summary

A thermodynamic system is the part of the physical world that one chooses to study in thermodynamics.
It is separated from the rest, its surroundings, by a boundary made of walls.
The kind of walls fixes what the system can exchange with its surroundings, and this gives the classes of isolated, closed and open systems.

## Explanations

### L1 human

To apply thermodynamics, first draw a line around the thing you want to study.
Inside the line is the system; outside is the surroundings.
A gas in a sealed steel bottle is a closed system: heat can pass through the steel, but the gas cannot.
A pot of boiling water without a lid is an open system, because steam escapes.
A drink in a perfect thermos flask would be an isolated system: nothing goes in or out.
