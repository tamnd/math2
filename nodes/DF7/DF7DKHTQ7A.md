## Summary

Potential energy is energy stored in the position of a system, such as a raised weight or a stretched spring.
Mathematically, a potential energy of a [[conservative force field|0PKG97PX9Y]] $F$ is a function $V$ with $F = -\nabla V$.
So $-V$ is a [[scalar potential|0H7C3T1GJY]] of $F$ in the convention $\nabla \phi = F$.
The work done by the force along a path is the drop of $V$ from the start to the end.
The sum of kinetic and potential energy is then constant along each motion.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Lifting a book onto a shelf takes work, and that work is stored: if the book falls, it speeds up and the energy comes back as motion.
The stored energy depends only on the height, not on the route the book took.
Near the ground, a mass of $1$ kg raised by $2$ m stores about $1 \cdot 9.8 \cdot 2 \approx 20$ joules.
Forces always push towards lower potential energy, like a ball rolling downhill.
