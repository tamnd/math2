## Summary

The Lorenz gauge condition is the requirement that the four-divergence of the [[electromagnetic four-potential|BQFCNWFV9Q]] vanish.
It is a [[gauge fixing|TXD7MTYQYS]] condition that is the same in every inertial frame.
In this gauge each component of the potential solves an inhomogeneous [[wave equation|5EHD2XBQDG]], with the charge density or a current component as source.
It is named after Ludvig Lorenz, and it is often misattributed to Hendrik Lorentz.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Many different potentials describe the same electric and magnetic fields, so physicists pick one by adding a rule.
The Lorenz rule ties the time change of the electric potential to the spread of the vector potential.
With this rule the equations for the potentials become four separate wave equations, one per component, and they look the same to every observer moving at constant speed.
This makes it the natural choice for radiation problems and for relativity.
