## Summary

A polytropic process is a quasistatic process of a fluid along which $p V^n$ is constant for a fixed real exponent $n$.
For an ideal gas this one family contains the [[isobaric|WYAY33ZVYQ]], [[isothermal|C9MM7GFRWN]], [[isentropic|ECAXF3AWWX]] and [[isochoric|YT8APTNYD5]] processes as special cases.
It is used to model real compressions and expansions that lie between these ideal cases.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When the air in a real engine is compressed, some heat escapes, but not all of it.
The process is then between an isothermal one and an adiabatic one.
A polytropic law $p V^n = C$ with an exponent $n$ chosen to fit measurements describes it well.
Different values of $n$ give a whole family of curves in the pressure-volume plane.
