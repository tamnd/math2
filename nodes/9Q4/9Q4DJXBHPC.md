## Summary

The surgery obstruction groups are the [[L-groups|P5CRN7BZV0]] $L_n(\Z[\pi], w)$ of the integral [[group ring|4NMTSX0XDS]] of a group $\pi$.
Here the involution is twisted by an orientation character $w$.
Wall showed that they contain the obstruction to turning a degree one normal map into a simple homotopy equivalence by [[surgery|MHHN8386TG]].
This holds in dimensions at least $5$.
They depend only on the fundamental group, the orientation character and the dimension modulo $4$.
They are the algebraic part of the surgery exact sequence that classifies manifolds within a homotopy type.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Surgery tries to turn a map between manifolds into a homotopy equivalence by cutting and gluing.
At the end there may remain a piece of algebra that cannot be removed: an intersection form on the middle-dimensional homology.
These pieces of algebra are elements of the surgery obstruction groups.
For simply connected manifolds the groups are $\Z$, $0$, $\Z/2$, $0$, and the obstruction is a signature difference divided by $8$ or an Arf invariant.
For manifolds with a nontrivial fundamental group, the groups are larger and harder to compute.
