## Summary

A quantale is a [[complete lattice|D7A0ZGZT5S]] with an associative multiplication that distributes over arbitrary joins on both sides.
Lattices of ideals of a ring, frames of open sets, and power sets of monoids are quantales.
Quantales generalise frames and locales to a non-commutative or non-idempotent setting.
They appear in noncommutative topology and linear logic.
The multiplication of a quantale always has left and right residuals, which links quantales with [[residuated lattices|E337A6WVH3]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take the ideals of a commutative ring.
You can join any family of ideals by taking the ideal they generate, and you can multiply two ideals.
Multiplying an ideal by the join of a family gives the join of the products.
A quantale is any complete lattice with a multiplication with this property.
