## Summary

The associated flow rule is the rule of [[flow plasticity theory|A3V75E17RS]] that the plastic strain rate points along the outward normal of the [[yield surface|CWWCEXZ8BH]].
Equivalently, the plastic potential equals the yield function.
It is also called the normality rule, and it follows from Drucker's stability postulate and from the principle of maximum plastic dissipation.
It is the standard rule for metals.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a material flows plastically, the flow rule says in which direction its shape changes.
The associated rule picks the direction perpendicular to the yield surface at the current stress.
For metals described by the von Mises criterion, this means the plastic flow follows the shear part of the stress and keeps the volume constant.
