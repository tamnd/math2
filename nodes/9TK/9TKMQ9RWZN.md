## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **windmill graph** $\operatorname{Wd}(k,n)$ is an undirected graph constructed for $k \ge 2$ and $n \ge 2$ by joining $n$ copies of the [[complete graph|03WKB2H474]] $K_{k}$ at a shared universal vertex. That is, it is a 1-clique-sum of these complete graphs.
