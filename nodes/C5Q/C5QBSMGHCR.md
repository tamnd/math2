## Summary

A Mach wave is the front along which an infinitely weak pressure disturbance spreads in a [[supersonic flow|G4EN6Y0ZZ2]].
It makes the Mach angle $\mu = \arcsin(1/M)$ with the local flow direction, where $M$ is the [[Mach number|GFHJ858AN1]].
Mathematically, Mach waves are the characteristics of the steady supersonic flow equations.
Many weak Mach waves can add up to a shock wave, and spreading Mach waves form expansion fans.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A small bump on the wall of a supersonic wind tunnel sends out a faint straight line of pressure change into the flow.
That line is a Mach wave.
It slopes downstream at an angle that is steeper for slower flow and flatter for faster flow.
For example, at Mach $2$ it makes an angle of $30$ degrees with the flow, and at Mach $1$ it stands straight across it.
