## Statement

In combinatorial number theory, the **Erdős–Graham problem** is the problem of proving that, if the set $\{2,3,4,\dots\}$ of [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] greater than one is [[partitioned|08QMKTWF9T]] into finitely many subsets, then one of the subsets can be used to form an [[Egyptian fraction|VB4CMQ82C0]] representation of unity. That is, for every $r > 0$, and every $r$-coloring of the integers greater than one, there is a finite monochromatic subset $S$ of these integers such that.
