## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|K80DB44C78]].
It covers reductive groups over fields that are not algebraically closed: forms, their classification by Galois cohomology, relative root systems and the Tits index, and the arithmetic of groups over local, global and finite fields.
