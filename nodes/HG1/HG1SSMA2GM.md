## Summary

Thermodynamics is the theory of heat, work, temperature and energy in macroscopic systems.
It describes a [[system|GYNNJBMTCP]] by a few state variables and relates them through a small number of laws.
Its results hold for any material, without a model of the atoms inside it, which makes it a basis for physics, chemistry and engineering.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A cup of coffee contains a huge number of molecules, but a few numbers describe it well: its temperature, its volume, its pressure.
Thermodynamics is the study of how such numbers change when the coffee gains or loses heat or when work is done on it.
Its laws say, for example, that energy is conserved and that heat flows by itself only from hot to cold.
An engine, a refrigerator and a chemical reaction all obey these same laws.
