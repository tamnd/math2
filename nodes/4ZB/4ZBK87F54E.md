## Definition

Consider two well-mixed boxes of sea water of equal volume $V$.
The equatorial box has temperature $T_e$ and salinity $S_e$, and the polar box has temperature $T_p$ and salinity $S_p$.
Let $\Delta T = T_e - T_p$ and $\Delta S = S_e - S_p$.
Use the linear [[equation of state of seawater|M8PJQA6BPK]] $\rho = \rho_0 \big( 1 - \alpha (T - T_0) + \beta (S - S_0) \big)$ with $\alpha, \beta > 0$.
The boxes exchange water at the rate
$$q = k (\alpha \, \Delta T - \beta \, \Delta S), \qquad k > 0.$$
This rate is proportional to the density difference $(\rho_p - \rho_e)/\rho_0$.
The exchange mixes the boxes at rate $\abs{q}$ whatever the sign of $q$.
The *Stommel box model* is the system of [[ordinary differential equations|0NT64JV96A]]
$$V \frac{d T_e}{dt} = -\abs{q} \, \Delta T + V \gamma (T_e^* - T_e), \qquad V \frac{d T_p}{dt} = \abs{q} \, \Delta T + V \gamma (T_p^* - T_p),$$
$$V \frac{d S_e}{dt} = -\abs{q} \, \Delta S + F, \qquad V \frac{d S_p}{dt} = \abs{q} \, \Delta S - F.$$
Here $T_e^* > T_p^*$ are restoring temperatures, $\gamma > 0$ is a restoring rate, and $F > 0$ is a salt flux.
The flux $F$ represents evaporation at low latitudes and precipitation at high latitudes.

## Remarks

Example: a common reduction assumes that the restoring of temperature is fast, so that $\Delta T$ is a fixed constant.
Then $V \, d\Delta S / dt = 2F - 2 \abs{q} \Delta S$.
In the variable $x = \beta \Delta S / (\alpha \Delta T)$ the [[equilibria|0N8NGKDZDZ]] solve $x \abs{1 - x} = \lambda$, with $\lambda = F \beta / (k \alpha^2 \Delta T^2)$.
For $x < 1$ (thermal mode, sinking at the pole) the equation $x(1 - x) = \lambda$ has the two roots $\tfrac12 \big( 1 \pm \sqrt{1 - 4 \lambda} \big)$ when $\lambda < 1/4$; the smaller one is stable and the larger one is unstable.
For $x > 1$ (haline mode, reversed flow) there is the single stable root $\tfrac12 \big( 1 + \sqrt{1 + 4\lambda} \big)$.
So for $0 < \lambda < 1/4$ there are two stable states, and at $\lambda = 1/4$ the thermal mode disappears in a [[saddle-node bifurcation|MAP5J6HK0T]].

Non-example: if salinity is held fixed ($F = 0$ and $\Delta S = 0$), the flow is $q = k \alpha \Delta T > 0$ in every equilibrium, so there is only one mode of circulation.

Stommel's original paper restored the salinities toward fixed values as well; the form with a fixed salt flux $F$ is a common later variant.
