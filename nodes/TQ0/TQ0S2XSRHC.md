## Summary

This topic is a part of [[dynamical systems|WCNCV98C8R]].
It covers diffeomorphisms and flows with uniform contraction and expansion on an invariant set, such as Anosov systems, Axiom A systems and horseshoes, with Markov partitions and rigidity.
