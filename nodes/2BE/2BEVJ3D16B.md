## Statement

Let $X$ and $Y$ be two finite sets, and suppose that neither is empty.
Form their [[Cartesian product|09CEDJYBKP]] $X \times Y$ of all ordered pairs $(x, y)$ with $x \in X$ and $y \in Y$.
Then the [[number of elements|05099E1NMJ]] of $X \times Y$ is the product of the numbers of elements of $X$ and of $Y$:

$$|X \times Y| = |X| \cdot |Y|.$$
