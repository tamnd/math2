## Example

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite box of $\Z^d$ and pick one coordinate axis, the axial direction.
Let $J_0, J_1 > 0$ and $J_2 > 0$ be constants and let $\beta \ge 0$ be the inverse temperature.
A configuration is $\sigma \colon \Lambda \to \set{-1, +1}$, and $e$ denotes the unit vector of the axial direction.
Its energy is
$$H(\sigma) = -J_0 \sum_{\text{in-layer}} \sigma_x \sigma_y - J_1 \sum_{x} \sigma_x \sigma_{x + e} + J_2 \sum_{x} \sigma_x \sigma_{x + 2e},$$
where the first sum runs over nearest-neighbour pairs $\set{x, y}$ with $y - x$ orthogonal to $e$.
The *ANNNI model* (axial next-nearest-neighbour Ising model) is the probability measure on $\set{-1, +1}^\Lambda$ proportional to $e^{-\beta H(\sigma)}$.

## Remarks

The nearest-neighbour terms favour alignment, while the next-nearest-neighbour axial term with $J_2 > 0$ favours opposite spins at distance two, so the interactions compete.
At zero temperature, along one axial chain the ferromagnetic pattern has energy $-J_1 + J_2$ per site and the pattern $++--$ has energy $-J_2$ per site.
So the ground state is ferromagnetic when $J_2 / J_1 < 1/2$ and of type $++--$ when $J_2 / J_1 > 1/2$.
At positive temperature the model shows many modulated phases with different periods along the axis.
For $J_2 = 0$ it is an anisotropic [[Ising model|R0KGM32QN6]] with couplings $J_0$ and $J_1$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

This is an Ising magnet with a twist in one direction.
Along that direction a spin wants to agree with its neighbour but disagree with the spin two steps away.
The two wishes cannot both be met, and the result is a rich family of striped patterns.
