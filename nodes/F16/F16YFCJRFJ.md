## Summary

The upper-convected time derivative is the rate of change of a tensor field seen by an observer who moves, turns and stretches with the fluid.
It adds to the [[material derivative|CJW50K5HZB]] terms that remove the change caused only by the local deformation of the flow.
It is used to write frame-invariant constitutive equations for viscoelastic fluids, such as the [[upper-convected Maxwell model|VN6MY7KWJ8]] and the [[Oldroyd-B model|VPACKRZSA8]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a tiny stretched rubber band carried along in a flowing fluid.
Even if nothing happens to the band itself, its direction and length change because the flow turns and stretches it.
The upper-convected derivative subtracts this passive change, so it is zero exactly when the tensor is just carried along with the deformation of the flow.
In a uniform translation it is just the material derivative, since the flow does not deform anything.
