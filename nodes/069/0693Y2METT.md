## Summary

The Hessian matrix of a real-valued function of $n$ variables at a point is the $n \times n$ matrix of its second [[partial derivatives|09ZXGX55DM]] at that point.
It describes the curvature of the function, and it is the main tool of the second derivative test for local extrema.
