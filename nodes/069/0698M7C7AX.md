## Summary

The triple integral of a function of three variables over a solid region is a limit of Riemann sums. These sums use partitions of the region into small boxes.
It extends the [[double integral|0WAR4F23B4]] to three dimensions, and it computes volumes, masses and centres of mass of solids.
