## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical finance and insurance|8K1BEXG3N8]].
It covers algorithms for pricing and hedging when closed forms do not exist, such as Monte Carlo and quasi-Monte Carlo methods, the simulation of Greeks, regression methods for American options, PDE schemes and Fourier methods.
