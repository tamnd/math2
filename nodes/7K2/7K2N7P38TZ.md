## Statement

A group $G$ is not [[amenable|CEAT9BESKK]] if and only if $G$ contains a [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] isomorphic to the [[free group|0KTW26C80J]] on two generators.

## Remarks

The direction "if" is true: a group that contains a free group of rank $2$ is not amenable, as von Neumann showed when he introduced amenability.
The direction "only if" is false.
Ol'shanskii proved in 1980 that some Tarski monster groups are not amenable, and these groups have no free subgroups; see [[Ol'shanskii's counterexample|XA7YE70A6P]].
Adian showed in 1982 that free Burnside groups of large odd exponent are further counterexamples.
Ol'shanskii and Sapir gave finitely presented counterexamples in 2003, and Monod gave counterexamples made of piecewise projective homeomorphisms of the line in 2013.
The conjecture holds for finitely generated linear groups, by the Tits alternative.
The name refers to von Neumann, but the first written statement appears to be Day's of 1957.
Whether Thompson's group $F$, which has no free subgroup of rank $2$, is amenable is still open.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Free groups are the basic example of groups that are too big to be averaged fairly.
The conjecture guessed that they are the only reason: that every group that cannot be averaged fairly contains a free group.
This turned out to be false, but finding counterexamples required very exotic infinite groups.
