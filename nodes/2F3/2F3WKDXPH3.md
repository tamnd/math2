## Summary

In [[combinatorial mathematics|0JKW7W7XFR]], a **$t$-core partition** is a [[partition|0JV30XN73Z]] which has no hooks of length $t$. Such partitions have been used in the study of Ramanujan's congruences on the [[partition function|001CX45SWQ]] and for representation theory of the [[symmetric group|0515A6D2Y1]], especially modular representation theory.
