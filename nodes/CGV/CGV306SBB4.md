## Summary

A Lie triple system is a vector space with a trilinear product $[x, y, z]$ that behaves like the double bracket $[\,[x, y], z]$ in a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]].
Every subspace of a Lie algebra that is closed under the double bracket is a Lie triple system.
Lie triple systems are the infinitesimal objects of [[symmetric spaces|PAA93R4WYD]], in the same way as Lie algebras are the infinitesimal objects of Lie groups.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Lie algebra has a product of two elements, the bracket.
Sometimes only a part of a Lie algebra is of interest, and that part is not closed under the bracket.
It can still be closed under brackets of three elements, $[\,[x, y], z]$.
For example, the commutator $XY - YX$ of two real symmetric matrices is skew-symmetric, so the symmetric matrices are not closed under the commutator.
But the commutator of a skew-symmetric matrix with a symmetric matrix is symmetric again, so the symmetric matrices are closed under $[\,[X, Y], Z]$.
A Lie triple system keeps exactly the rules that such a triple bracket obeys.
