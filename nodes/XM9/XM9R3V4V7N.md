## Statement

In number theory, **Moessner's theorem** or **Moessner's magic** is related to an arithmetical algorithm to produce an infinite [[sequence|0G9EKM0E15]] of the exponents of positive integers $1^n, 2^n, 3^n, 4^n, \cdots ~,$ with $n \geq 1 ~,$ by recursively manipulating the sequence of integers algebraically. The algorithm was first published by Alfred Moessner in 1951; the first proof of its validity was given by Oskar Perron that same year.
