## Summary

The grand potential, or Landau free energy, is the [[thermodynamic potential|MKZW03Q590]] of an open system whose natural variables are temperature, volume and chemical potential.
It equals the Helmholtz free energy minus the product of chemical potential and number of particles.
It is minimal in equilibrium for a system in contact with a reservoir of heat and particles.
In statistical mechanics it is $-kT$ times the logarithm of the grand canonical partition function.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a small region of a gas that freely exchanges heat and molecules with the rest.
Neither its energy nor its number of molecules is fixed; only the temperature and the chemical potential, set by the surroundings, are fixed.
The grand potential is the energy-like quantity that is natural in this situation.
For a uniform fluid it is simply minus the pressure times the volume.
