## Definition

Let $R$ be a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]] and let $P$ be a [[finitely generated projective|HQA9YHDSH5]] $R$-module.
For a [[prime ideal|00MHHZ9SNE]] $\mathfrak{p}$ of $R$, the [[localization|4C1AE84N5N]] $P_\mathfrak{p}$ is a finitely generated projective module over the [[local ring|MZ02BKPHJA]] $R_\mathfrak{p}$.
So it is [[free|942FK83S09]] of some finite rank $r_\mathfrak{p}$.
The *rank* of $P$ is the function $\mathrm{rk}\, P \colon \mathrm{Spec}\, R \to \N$, $\mathfrak{p} \mapsto r_\mathfrak{p}$.
The module $P$ has *constant rank* $n$ if $r_\mathfrak{p} = n$ for every prime $\mathfrak{p}$.

## Remarks

The rank is locally constant on the [[spectrum|FB45BHHA2J]] with its Zariski topology.
It is additive, $\mathrm{rk}(P \oplus Q) = \mathrm{rk}\, P + \mathrm{rk}\, Q$, and multiplicative for tensor products.
For an integral domain $R$ with field of fractions $K$, the rank is constant and equals $\dim_K (K \otimes_R P)$.

Example: $R^n$ has constant rank $n$, since $(R^n)_\mathfrak{p} = R_\mathfrak{p}^n$.

Non-example of constant rank: let $R = \Z/6\Z$ and $P = \Z/2\Z$.
The spectrum has two points, $(2)$ and $(3)$.
At $(2)$, $R_{(2)} \cong \Z/2\Z$ and $P_{(2)} \cong \Z/2\Z$ has rank $1$; at $(3)$, $P_{(3)} = 0$ has rank $0$.
