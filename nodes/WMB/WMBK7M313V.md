## Summary

Matrix theory and multilinear algebra is the study of matrices beyond the first course in linear algebra, and of tensors and the algebras built from them.
Matrix theory treats a matrix as an object of its own: it bounds and locates eigenvalues and singular values, compares matrices by norms and orders, and studies the classes of matrices that a pattern or a sign condition defines.
The results are used in numerical analysis, statistics, optimization, control theory, quantum information and combinatorics.
Grassmann introduced the exterior algebra in 1844, and Perron and Frobenius proved their theorems on positive and nonnegative matrices between 1907 and 1912.
This topic collects the matrix theory and multilinear algebra pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Linear algebra, numerical analysis, Lie theory and random structures have their own topics.
