## Summary

A social welfare function turns the preferences of the members of a society into one ranking of the alternatives for the society as a whole.
In the sense of Arrow, its inputs are the individual [[preference relations|JGP0Q56FRY]] and its output is a [[weak ordering|PW2NKAPXWA]] of the alternatives.
It is the central object of Arrow's impossibility theorem.
In welfare economics the same name is also used for a function that combines the utility levels of individuals into one number.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a committee where each member ranks some candidates from best to worst.
A social welfare function is a fixed rule that reads all these rankings and writes down one ranking for the committee.
For two candidates, majority vote is such a rule: the candidate preferred by more members is ranked first, and a tie gives indifference.
With three or more candidates, finding a good rule is surprisingly hard.
