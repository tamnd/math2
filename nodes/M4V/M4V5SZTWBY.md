## Statement

Inspired by Gandhi's proof, Golomb proved the following recurrence

$$p_n =\lim_{s\to\infty} \left(\zeta(s) \prod_{k=1}^{n-1} (1-p_k^{-s}) - 1\right)^{-\frac{1}{s}},$$

where $\zeta$ denotes the Riemann zeta function. It is based on the Euler product for $\zeta$.
