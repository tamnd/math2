## Statement

Consider the [[SIR model|J5FHBDJGJA]] with population size $N$, transmission rate $\beta$, recovery rate $\gamma$ and [[basic reproduction number|VY4H52692D]] $R_0 = \beta / \gamma > 1$.
Suppose that a fraction $p \in [0, 1]$ of the population is immune at time $0$, so that $S(0) \le (1 - p) N$, and that $I(0) > 0$.
If
$$p \ge p_c = 1 - \frac{1}{R_0},$$
then $I$ is decreasing on $[0, \infty)$, so no epidemic outbreak occurs.
If $p < p_c$ and $S(0) = (1 - p) N - I(0)$ with $I(0)$ small enough, then $I$ increases at first.
The number $p_c$ is the *herd immunity threshold*.

## Remarks

Proof idea: $I' = \gamma I \left( R_0 S / N - 1 \right)$.
If $p \ge 1 - 1/R_0$, then $S(t) \le S(0) \le (1 - p) N \le N / R_0$ for all $t$, so $I' \le 0$.
If $p < 1 - 1/R_0$, then $(1 - p) N > N / R_0$, and for $I(0)$ small, $S(0) > N / R_0$, so $I'(0) > 0$.
The quantity $R_0 (1 - p)$ is the effective reproduction number at the start, and the threshold is where it equals $1$.

Example: for $R_0 = 15$, the threshold is $p_c = 1 - 1/15 \approx 0.93$, so about $93\%$ of the population must be immune.
For $R_0 = 2$, it is $50\%$.

The hypothesis of homogeneous mixing is essential.
In populations structured by age, space or contacts, the threshold depends on who is immune and can differ from $1 - 1/R_0$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If enough people are immune, an infected person rarely meets someone who can catch the disease.
Then each case causes less than one new case on average, and outbreaks die out.
This protects also the people who are not immune.
The needed fraction is $1 - 1/R_0$: the more contagious the disease, the closer it is to $100\%$.

### L2 human

The formula is the basis of vaccination targets.
With a vaccine of effectiveness $E$ that gives full immunity to a fraction $E$ of vaccinated people, the required vaccination coverage is $p_c / E$, which exceeds $1$ when $E < p_c$.
The threshold is the level at which the [[threshold theorem of epidemiology|XQQTXNF7BT]] switches from no epidemic to epidemic.
In an epidemic without vaccination, the final immune fraction overshoots $p_c$, as the [[final size equation|8ABBB524PN]] shows.
