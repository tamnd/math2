## Summary

In mathematics, the **free factor complex** (sometimes also called the **complex of free factors**) is a [[free group|020Z5SEN27]] counterpart of the notion of the curve complex of a finite type surface. The free factor complex was originally introduced in a 1998 paper of Allen Hatcher and Karen Vogtmann. Like the curve complex, the free factor complex is known to be Gromov-hyperbolic. The free factor complex plays a significant role in the study of large-scale geometry of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$.
