## Statement

Let $N \ge 2$ be an integer and let $g_s > 0$.
Consider type IIB [[superstring theory|N993FCEJY2]] on the spacetime $AdS_5 \times S^5$, with string coupling $g_s$.
Here $AdS_5$ is five-dimensional anti-de Sitter space and $S^5$ is the five-sphere, both of radius $L$.
There are $N$ units of five-form flux through $S^5$.
Consider also $\mathcal{N} = 4$ [[supersymmetric|S847N922FY]] [[Yang–Mills theory|A0J2688KZF]] on four-dimensional Minkowski space, with gauge group $SU(N)$ and coupling $g_{YM}$, which is a [[conformal field theory|837V89WJKS]].
The conjecture states that these two theories are equivalent, with $g_{YM}^2 = 4\pi g_s$ and $L^4 = 4\pi g_s N \alpha'^2$ in a common normalization, where $\alpha'$ is the Regge slope.
Each bulk field $\phi$ of the string theory is dual to a local operator $\mathcal{O}$ of the gauge theory.
The string partition function with boundary values $\phi_0$ of $\phi$ equals the generating function $\left\langle \exp \int \phi_0\, \mathcal{O} \right\rangle$ of correlators of $\mathcal{O}$.

## Remarks

The conjecture is open; there is no proof, and the string theory side has no complete non-perturbative definition.
It has passed many checks: the symmetry groups match, since both sides have the supergroup $PSU(2,2 \mid 4)$, and many protected quantities agree.
The precise dictionary between partition functions and correlators was given in 1998 by Gubser, Klebanov and Polyakov and by Witten.
Take the limit $N \to \infty$ with the 't Hooft coupling $\lambda = g_{YM}^2 N$ fixed, and then $\lambda$ large.
Then the string side reduces to classical type IIB supergravity, so strongly coupled gauge theory questions become classical gravity computations.
The name is also used for the wider family of conjectured dualities between gravity on anti-de Sitter space and conformal field theories on its boundary.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The conjecture says that two very different-looking theories are the same theory in disguise.
One is a theory of strings and gravity in a curved five-dimensional space times a sphere.
The other is a quantum field theory of particles, without gravity, living in ordinary four-dimensional spacetime, which can be viewed as the boundary of the curved space.
It is a precise form of the holographic idea that a theory with gravity can be encoded on a boundary of one dimension less.
It lets physicists compute hard questions about strongly interacting particles with easier calculations in gravity, and the reverse.
