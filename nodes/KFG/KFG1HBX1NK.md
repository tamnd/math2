## Statement

Let $n \ge 0$ and let $\Z^n$ be the [[free abelian group|0WJX8MGG37]] of rank $n$.
Then its [[Whitehead group|8SY3AC4KW3]] is trivial:
$$\mathrm{Wh}(\Z^n) = 0.$$
Equivalently, $K_1(\Z[\Z^n]) \cong \set{\pm 1} \times \Z^n$, generated by the classes of the units $\pm t_1^{a_1} \cdots t_n^{a_n}$ of the [[group ring|4NMTSX0XDS]].

## Remarks

The group ring $\Z[\Z^n]$ is the [[Laurent polynomial ring|1R5P656GS6]] $\Z[t_1^{\pm 1}, \dots, t_n^{\pm 1}]$.
The proof goes by induction on $n$ with the [[Bass–Heller–Swan theorem|R8FZDQT1QM]]: for a regular Noetherian ring $R$, $K_1(R[t, t^{-1}]) \cong K_1(R) \oplus K_0(R)$, and $K_0$ of these Laurent polynomial rings is $\Z$.
Each step adds one factor $\Z$ to $K_1$, generated by the class of the new variable $t_n$, which is a trivial unit.
The case $n = 0$ is $\mathrm{Wh}(1) = 0$, coming from $K_1(\Z) = \set{\pm 1}$.
The statement fails for finite groups in general: $\mathrm{Wh}(\Z/5\Z)$ is infinite cyclic.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Whitehead group measures invertible matrices over a group ring that are not explained by obvious units.
For the free abelian groups, which are the fundamental groups of tori, there are no such matrices.
So every homotopy equivalence onto a torus is a simple homotopy equivalence.

### L2 human

The result is a basic input in geometric topology.
By the [[s-cobordism theorem|E1E5ABP89W]], every h-cobordism of dimension at least $6$ whose fundamental group is free abelian is a product.
It is a special case of the Farrell–Jones conjecture, which predicts that $\mathrm{Wh}(G) = 0$ for every torsion-free group $G$.
The proof is a typical use of the [[fundamental theorem of algebraic K-theory|YB37MJ90SB]], of which the Bass–Heller–Swan theorem is the case of $K_1$.
