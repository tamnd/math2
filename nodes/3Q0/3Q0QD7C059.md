## Summary

A **Riordan array** is an infinite lower triangular matrix, $D$, constructed from two [[formal power series|033WGN00BF]], $d(t)$ of order 0 and $h(t)$ of order 1, such that $d_{n,k} = [t^n]d(t) h(t)^k$.
