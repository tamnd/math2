## Summary

Poinsot's ellipsoid is an ellipsoid attached to a rigid body that moves without torque.
It is a level set of the rotational kinetic energy as a function of the angular velocity.
During the motion it rolls without slipping on a fixed plane perpendicular to the angular momentum.
This picture, due to Poinsot, describes torque-free rigid body motion without solving any equation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Throw a book in the air with a spin, and it tumbles in a complicated way.
Poinsot found a picture of this tumbling.
Imagine an egg-shaped surface glued to the book, centred at its centre of mass, whose shape comes from how the mass of the book is spread.
As the book flies, this egg rolls on an invisible fixed table, without slipping, and the motion of the egg is the motion of the book.
For a uniform ball the egg is a round sphere, and the ball just spins about a fixed axis.
