## Statement

Let $n \ge 2$. Let $U \subset \C^n$ be a [[bounded|09QSZVVM26]] domain, that is, a [[connected|0SPE8WNWFV]] open set, whose boundary $\partial U$ is connected and [[real-analytic|Z7H98E1NHP]].
Let $f \colon \partial U \to \C$ be a real-analytic [[CR function|JFKYYK2CEB]].
Then there is an open set $U' \subset \C^n$ containing $\overline{U}$ and a [[holomorphic function|507C34W0ZT]] $F \colon U' \to \C$ such that $F$ agrees with $f$ on $\partial U$.
