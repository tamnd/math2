## Description

The complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak{e}_6$ is the [[simple Lie algebra|XDAXWHBNSZ]] over $\C$ whose [[root system|AVVV93CASN]] has type $E_6$.
The root system $E_6$ has rank $6$ and $72$ roots, all of the same length.
It can be realized as the set of roots of [[E8|9QT1JR4KNY]] that are orthogonal to the roots of a subsystem of type $A_2$.
The dimension of $\mathfrak{e}_6$ is the rank plus the number of roots, $6 + 72 = 78$.

## Remarks

The [[Dynkin diagram|CS73JZQS66]] of $E_6$ is a tree with $6$ vertices: a branch vertex with arms of $1$, $2$ and $2$ further vertices.
It is the only exceptional diagram with a nontrivial automorphism, which exchanges the two arms of length $2$.
The [[Weyl group|YT1ZJ2S4EE]] of $E_6$ has order $51\,840$.
The smallest nontrivial representations of $\mathfrak{e}_6$ have dimension $27$; there are two of them, dual to each other.

## Explanations

### L1 human

$\mathfrak{e}_6$ is an exceptional simple Lie algebra of dimension $78$.
Its root system sits inside the root system of $E_8$.
Its Weyl group is the symmetry group of the configuration of the $27$ lines on a smooth cubic surface.
