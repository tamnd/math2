## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|9A5DVGMPEB]].
It covers approximations that replace the fluctuating neighbours of a site by an average field, Landau's expansion of the free energy in the order parameter, and the criteria that tell when these approximations fail.
