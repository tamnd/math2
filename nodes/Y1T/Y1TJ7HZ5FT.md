## Statement

In mathematics, **Cartan's theorems A and B** are two results [[proved|0B94TKFQJX]] by Henri Cartan around 1951, concerning a coherent sheaf $F$ on a Stein manifold $X$. They are significant both as applied to several complex variables, and in the general development of sheaf cohomology.
