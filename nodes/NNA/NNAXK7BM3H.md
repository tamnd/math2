## Summary

The free Lie algebra on a set $X$ is the [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] generated by $X$ with no relations other than those forced by the axioms.
It is characterized by a universal property: every map from $X$ to a Lie algebra extends uniquely to a homomorphism.
It is the [[free object|QBDT694BAP]] in the category of Lie algebras, analogous to the [[free group|0KTW26C80J]] for groups.
Its elements are linear combinations of iterated brackets of elements of $X$, and bases such as the Hall basis make this precise.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Start with some letters $x, y, \dots$ and form all brackets of them, such as $[x, y]$ and $[x, [x, y]]$.
Impose only the rules that every Lie algebra must obey: bilinearity, $[a, a] = 0$ and the Jacobi identity.
The result is the free Lie algebra on these letters.
Any other Lie algebra generated by the same letters is a quotient of it, obtained by adding extra relations.
