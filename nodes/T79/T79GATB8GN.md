## Definition

A process of a closed thermodynamic system is *isochoric* if the volume of the system is the same at all times of the process.

## Remarks

If only pressure-volume [[work|S8H21RWQ86]] is possible, an isochoric process has $W = 0$.
So the [[heat|CY4GJQGF06]] absorbed equals the change of [[internal energy|F6JVQ12X43]]: $Q = \Delta U$.
For an ideal gas, an isochoric process satisfies $p / T$ constant, where $T$ is the thermodynamic temperature.
Heating a gas in a cylinder with a free piston is not isochoric: the piston moves out and the volume grows.
