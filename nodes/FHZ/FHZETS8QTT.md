## Summary

The spin connection of a [[pseudo-Riemannian manifold|GH6CXWFNDJ]] is the [[Levi-Civita connection|CAZYGE1T4E]] written in a local orthonormal frame.
It is a matrix of 1-forms with values in the Lie algebra of the orthogonal group of the metric.
Because it takes values in this Lie algebra, it lifts to the spin group.
So it defines a covariant derivative of spinor fields, which is where its name comes from.
It is the basic tool to couple fermions to gravity and to write the Dirac operator on a curved [[spinor bundle|PYYQQKAZ0C]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

On a curved space one can choose, at each point, a set of perpendicular unit arrows, a frame.
Moving from one point to a nearby one, the frame turns a little compared with parallel transport.
The spin connection records this small rotation, direction by direction.
In the plane with polar coordinates, take the frame made of the radial arrow and the angular arrow.
It turns by the angle $d\varphi$ when one moves by $d\varphi$, and the spin connection is exactly $d\varphi$.
Since spinors are objects that also rotate, this rotation tells how to differentiate them.
