## Summary

A microstate of a physical system is a complete description of the state of every one of its microscopic parts.
A macrostate is described by a few bulk quantities, such as energy, volume and particle number, and many microstates are compatible with one macrostate.
Counting or weighting the microstates behind a macrostate is the basic step of [[statistical mechanics|X3QSEMFQWW]], and it leads to entropy.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of four coins on a table.
The microstate lists each coin: for example heads, tails, tails, heads.
The macrostate only says how many heads there are, here two.
There are $16$ microstates in all, and $6$ of them have exactly two heads, so the macrostate "two heads" is the most likely one.
For a gas, a microstate lists the position and the momentum of every molecule.
