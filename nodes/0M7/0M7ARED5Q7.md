## Summary

A Pythagorean triple is a triple of positive integers $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, the side lengths of a right triangle.
The [[definition of a Pythagorean triple|01G1WY1GVX]] gives the precise form.
