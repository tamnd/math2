## Summary

In mathematics, a form (i.e. a homogeneous polynomial) *h*(*x*) of [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] 2*m* in the [[real|08J2M1JYAG]] *n*-dimensional vector *x* is sum of squares of forms (SOS) if and only if there exist forms $g_1(x),\ldots,g_k(x)$ of degree *m* such that

$$h(x) = \sum_{i=1}^k g_i(x)^2 .$$
