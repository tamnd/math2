## Summary

In mathematical optimization, the **Rosenbrock function** is a non-[[convex|07Q7CNM5WF]] [[function|0EPAT51DXQ]], introduced by Howard H. Rosenbrock in 1960, which is used as a performance test problem for optimization algorithms. It is also known as **Rosenbrock's valley** or **Rosenbrock's banana function**.
