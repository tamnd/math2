## Statement

In number theory, **Meyer's theorem** on [[quadratic forms|0RBQS1JJCR]] states that an indefinite quadratic form $Q$ in five or more variables over the [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] of [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]] nontrivially represents zero. In other words, if the equation $Q(x) = 0$ has a non-zero [[real|08J2M1JYAG]] solution, then it has a non-zero rational solution. By clearing the denominators, an [[integral|0C4JS9RF5M]] solution $x$ may also be found.
