## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|KKBKTW8YB8]].
It covers finite commutative rings and the polynomials over them, and the standard constructions that give examples and counterexamples: semigroup rings, idealization, fiber products, D+M rings, rings of functions and star operations.
