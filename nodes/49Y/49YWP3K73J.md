## Statement

(txt) showed that $Z_\phi (s,\chi)$ is a rational function in $t=q^{-s}$. The proof uses Heisuke Hironaka's theorem about the resolution of singularities. Later, an entirely different proof was given by Jan Denef using p-adic cell decomposition. Little is known, however, about explicit formulas. (There are some results about Igusa zeta functions of Fermat varieties.)
