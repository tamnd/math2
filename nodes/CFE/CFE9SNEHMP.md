## Summary

Precession is the slow turning of the rotation axis of a spinning body around another fixed direction.
For a spinning top it is caused by the torque of gravity, and for a freely tumbling body it happens without any torque.
The axis of the Earth precesses under the torques of the Sun and the Moon on its equatorial bulge.
Precession is one of the three motions described by the Euler angles, together with nutation and spin.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Spin a top and tilt it: instead of falling over, its axis slowly sweeps around a cone about the vertical.
This sweeping is precession.
Gravity pulls the top down, but because the top is spinning, the pull turns the axis sideways instead of tipping it over.
The faster the spin, the slower the precession.
The Earth's axis does the same, very slowly, completing one turn in about $26\,000$ years.
