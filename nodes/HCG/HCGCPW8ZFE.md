## Summary

In mathematics, a **square number** or **perfect square** is an [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] that is the square of an integer; in other words, it is the product of some integer with itself. For example, 9 is a square number, since it equals $3^{2}$ and can be written as $3\,\times\,3$.
