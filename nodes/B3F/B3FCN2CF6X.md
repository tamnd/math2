## Summary

In computational number theory, the **index calculus algorithm** is a probabilistic algorithm for computing [[discrete logarithms|0HJHYMGN3C]]. Dedicated to the discrete logarithm in $(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})^*$ where $q$ is a prime, index calculus leads to a family of algorithms adapted to [[finite fields|0ZXZ82FTX2]] and to some families of elliptic curves. The algorithm collects relations among the discrete logarithms of small primes, computes them by a linear algebra procedure and finally expresses the desired discrete logarithm with respect to the discrete logarithms of small primes.
