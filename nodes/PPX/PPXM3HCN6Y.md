## Summary

The bulk modulus $K$ measures the resistance of a substance to uniform compression.
It is the ratio of an increase of pressure to the resulting relative decrease of volume.
It is defined for solids, liquids and gases alike, and for an isotropic linear elastic solid it equals $\lambda + \tfrac23 \mu$ in terms of the [[Lamé parameters|80737NDZ4V]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Squeeze a sponge from all sides and its volume shrinks a lot; squeeze a steel ball the same way and its volume hardly changes.
The bulk modulus measures this difference.
If raising the pressure by $1$ MPa shrinks the volume by $0.05\%$, the bulk modulus is $1 / 0.0005 = 2000$ MPa, about the value for water.
Steel has a bulk modulus of about $160$ GPa, so it is about $80$ times harder to compress than water.
