## Summary

A pure strategy is one definite choice of a player in a game.
A mixed strategy is a probability distribution over the pure strategies, so the player lets chance pick the choice.
Mixed strategies make the strategy sets convex.
Without them many games, such as matching pennies, have no Nash equilibrium.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In rock-paper-scissors the pure strategies are "rock", "paper" and "scissors".
A mixed strategy is a rule such as "roll a die; play rock on 1 or 2, paper on 3 or 4, scissors on 5 or 6".
Against such a player you cannot do better on average than to break even.
Mixing is useful whenever being predictable can be exploited.
