## Summary

The Lorenz curve describes a distribution of income or wealth.
For each fraction $p$ of the population, it gives the share of the total held by the poorest fraction $p$.
It runs from $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$, is convex, and lies on or below the diagonal.
The diagonal corresponds to perfect equality, and the further the curve sags below it, the more unequal the distribution.
Indices of inequality such as the [[Gini coefficient|22JK76F1SW]] are computed from it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Line everyone up from poorest to richest.
Walk along the line, and at each point ask what share of all income the people behind you have.
Plot that share against the fraction of people behind you, and you get the Lorenz curve.
If four people earn $1, 2, 3, 4$, the poorest quarter has $1/10$ of the income, the poorest half $3/10$, and the poorest three quarters $6/10$.
