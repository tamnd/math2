## Summary

A **mathematical constant** is a number whose value is fixed by an unambiguous definition, often referred to by a special symbol (e.g., an alphabet letter), or by mathematicians' names to facilitate using it across multiple mathematical problems. Constants arise in many areas of mathematics, with constants such as $e$ and [[$\pi$|0C1VYVNV2N]] occurring in such diverse contexts as geometry, number theory, statistics, and calculus.
