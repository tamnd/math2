## Summary

In mathematics, a **cuspidal cubic** or **semicubical parabola** is an algebraic plane curve that has an implicit equation of the form $y^2 - a^2 x^3 = 0$ (with $a \ne 0$) in some [[Cartesian coordinate system|0RWKYX8339]].
