## Summary

A graph is acyclic if it has no cycles. An undirected acyclic graph is the same thing as a [[forest|062D9KXJB1]]. An acyclic directed graph, which is a digraph without directed cycles, is often called a directed acyclic graph, especially in computer science.

An acyclic coloring of an undirected graph is a proper coloring in which every two color classes induce a forest.
