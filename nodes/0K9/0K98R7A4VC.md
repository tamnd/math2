## Summary

The lower central series of a group $G$ is the sequence $G = \gamma_1(G) \supseteq \gamma_2(G) \supseteq \cdots$ with $\gamma_{i+1}(G) = [\gamma_i(G), G]$.
The group is nilpotent if and only if this series reaches the trivial subgroup.
