## Summary

The characteristic polynomial of a linear recurrence encodes its coefficients as a polynomial. Its roots give the geometric solutions, and they lead to closed formulas for all solutions. The node [[characteristic polynomial of a linear recurrence|0BWDJGQYST]] gives the precise definition.
