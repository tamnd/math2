## Summary

In number theory, **Skewes's number** is the smallest [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] $x$ for which the [[prime-counting function|0RQSNVTFVZ]] $\pi(x)$ exceeds the logarithmic integral function $\operatorname{li}(x).$ It is named for the South African mathematician Stanley Skewes who first computed an upper bound on its value.
