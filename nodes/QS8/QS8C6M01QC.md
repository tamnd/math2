## Summary

This topic is a part of [[analytic number theory|FM3WJEEPWE]].
It covers primes among the values of polynomials in one and several variables, Landau's problems, the conjectures of Bunyakovsky, Schinzel, and Bateman and Horn, the results of Iwaniec, Friedlander and Heath-Brown, and Artin's conjecture as an analytic problem.
