## Summary

Renormalization is the set of methods that remove the infinities of [[quantum field theory|W031YNVRE3]] by expressing predictions in terms of measured parameters.
One first regularizes the theory with a cutoff.
Then the bare parameters of the Lagrangian are made to depend on the cutoff so that physical quantities have finite limits.
A theory is renormalizable when finitely many such parameters suffice at every order of perturbation theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When one computes quantum corrections in field theory, some integrals over high momenta diverge.
The fix is to notice that the mass and charge written in the equations are not the ones measured in the laboratory.
One adjusts these written parameters, in a way that depends on the cutoff, so that the measured values come out right.
In quantum electrodynamics, after this adjustment, all other predictions are finite, and some agree with experiment to many decimal places.
