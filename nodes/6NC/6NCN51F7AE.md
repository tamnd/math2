## Summary

Two vertices $u,v$ are true twins if they have the same closed neighborhood: $N_{G}[u] = N_{G}[v]$ (this implies $u$ and $v$ are neighbors), and they are false twins if they have the same open neighborhood: $N_{G}(u) = N_{G}(v))$ (this implies $u$ and $v$ are not neighbors).
