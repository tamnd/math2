## Summary

In mathematics, the **rational normal curve** is a smooth, rational curve $C$ of degree $n$ in projective n-space $\mathbf{P}^{n}$. It is a simple example of a projective variety; formally, it is the Veronese variety when the domain is the projective line. For $n = 2$ it is the [[plane conic|00HJCMC2CD]] $Z_{0}Z_{2} = Z^{2}_{1},$ and for $n = 3$ it is the twisted cubic. The term "normal" refers to projective normality, not normal schemes. The intersection of the rational normal curve with an [[affine space|09XNAPTJQ7]] is called the moment curve.
