## Definition

Let $X$ and $Z$ be the [[Pauli matrices|XAQCW3D4QQ]] $\sigma_x$ and $\sigma_z$, and let $|\Phi^\pm\rangle, |\Psi^\pm\rangle$ be the [[Bell states|CA57HMCRQQ]].
Alice and Bob share two [[qubits|E9QTT3XBXS]] $A$ and $B$ in the state $|\Phi^+\rangle_{AB}$, and Alice wants to send two bits $(a, b) \in \set{0,1}^2$.
*Superdense coding* is the following protocol.

1. Alice applies $Z^a X^b$ to her qubit $A$.
2. Alice sends the qubit $A$ to Bob.
3. Bob measures the pair $AB$ in the Bell basis and reads $(a, b)$ from the outcome.

## Remarks

The check: applying $Z^a X^b$ to the first qubit of $|\Phi^+\rangle$ gives
$$(a,b) = (0,0) \mapsto |\Phi^+\rangle, \quad (0,1) \mapsto |\Psi^+\rangle, \quad (1,0) \mapsto |\Phi^-\rangle, \quad (1,1) \mapsto |\Psi^-\rangle.$$
For example, $(X \otimes I)(|00\rangle + |11\rangle)/\sqrt2 = (|10\rangle + |01\rangle)/\sqrt2 = |\Psi^+\rangle$.
The four results are orthogonal, so the Bell measurement identifies $(a, b)$ with certainty.

Non-example: if Alice and Bob start from the product state $|00\rangle$ instead, the four states $(Z^a X^b \otimes I)|00\rangle$ are $\pm|00\rangle$ and $\pm|10\rangle$.
Only two of them are distinguishable, so Bob recovers just one bit.

The shared pair is consumed, and two classical bits per transmitted qubit is the maximum.
