## Summary

The Catalan numbers $C_n$ form a sequence that counts many recursive structures, such as Dyck words, binary trees and bracketings. They satisfy a convolution recurrence and the formula $C_n = \binom{2n}{n}/(n+1)$. The node [[Catalan numbers|092DRBFKMG]] gives the precise definition.
