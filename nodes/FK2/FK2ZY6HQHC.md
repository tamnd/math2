## Statement

In number theory, a **Wieferich prime** is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] *p* such that *p*<sup>2</sup> divides $2^{p - 1} - 1$, therefore connecting these primes with [[Fermat's little theorem|0M25PRN3WQ]], which states that every odd prime *p* divides $2^{p - 1} - 1$. Wieferich primes were first described by Arthur Wieferich in 1909 in works pertaining to [[Fermat's Last Theorem|0Y5X3SDBCZ]], at which time both of Fermat's theorems were already well known to mathematicians.
