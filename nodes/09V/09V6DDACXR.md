## Summary

The scalar multiple $cA$ of a [[matrix|0VD9ZSWWJG]] $A$ by a scalar $c$ is the matrix with entries $c\, a_{ij}$.
The [[formal definition|0K28YXMS7Q]] states the rule.
The page on [[matrix addition|0Y7H3SAVA1]] treats scalar multiplication together with addition, with examples, non-examples and properties.
