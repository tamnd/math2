## Summary

In number theory, the $n$-th **cabtaxi number**, typically denoted $\operatorname{Cabtaxi}(n)$, is defined as the smallest positive [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] that can be written as the sum of two *positive or negative or 0* cubes in $n$ ways. Such numbers exist for all $n$, which follows from the analogous result for taxicab numbers.
