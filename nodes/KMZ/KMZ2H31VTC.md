## Statement

Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]]. Then addition and multiplication of rationals obey three laws.

Both operations are commutative: $x+y=y+x$ and $xy=yx$.

Both operations are associative: $x+(y+z)=(x+y)+z$ and $x(yz)=(xy)z$.

Multiplication distributes over addition: $x(y+z)=xy+xz$.
