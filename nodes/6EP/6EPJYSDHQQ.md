## Statement

In number theory, **Dickson's conjecture** is the statement that for a finite set of linear forms $a_1+b_1n, a_2+b_2n, \dots, a_k+b_kn$ with each $b_i\geq 1$, there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ for which they are all [[prime|01826KMG2H]], unless there is a congruence condition preventing this. The conjecture is named after Leonard Dickson, who first proposed it in 1904.
