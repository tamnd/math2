## Summary

In algebra, a **multilinear polynomial** is a multivariate polynomial that is linear (meaning [[affine|0JF0TVYGWT]]) in each of its variables [[separately|0443XNARDV]], but not necessarily [[simultaneously|0NRYHJPQG2]]. It is a polynomial in which no variable occurs to a power of $2$ or higher; that is, each monomial is a constant times a product of distinct variables. For example $f(x,y,z) = 3xy + 2.5 y - 7z$ is a multilinear polynomial of [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] $2$ (because of the monomial $3xy$) whereas $f(x,y,z) = x^2 +4y$ is not. The [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] of a multilinear polynomial is the maximum number of distinct variables occurring in any monomial.
