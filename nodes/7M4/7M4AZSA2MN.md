## Summary

Angular acceleration is the rate of change of [[angular velocity|2FTZ7D8Z0F]] with respect to time.
It plays for rotation the part that [[acceleration|Q8D2JWY8G6]] plays for straight motion.
For a rigid body turning about a fixed axis, torque equals moment of inertia times angular acceleration.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A wheel that starts from rest and turns faster and faster has angular acceleration.
If it goes from $0$ to $10$ turns per second in $5$ seconds at a steady rate, its angular acceleration is $2$ turns per second each second.
A wheel spinning at a constant rate about a fixed axis has no angular acceleration, even though every point on its rim accelerates toward the centre.
