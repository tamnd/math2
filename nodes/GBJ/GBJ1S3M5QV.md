## Summary

The reduced group $\tilde{K}_0(R)$ is the quotient of [[$K_0(R)$|Q14JS2MA1C]] by the subgroup generated by the class of the free module $R$.
It removes the part of $K_0(R)$ that only counts free modules.
It vanishes exactly when every finitely generated projective module is [[stably free|YMXJ4JTQPF]].
For a [[Dedekind domain|7NPY2CN5KB]] it is the [[ideal class group|WQ46BD3ARY]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The group $K_0(R)$ always contains the classes of the free modules $R^n$, and these carry no surprise.
Dividing them out leaves only the information about projective modules that are not stably free.
So $\tilde{K}_0(R) = 0$ is a clean way to say that projective modules over $R$ are free up to adding free modules.
For a field, a local ring or the integers, the reduced group is $0$.
