## Summary

In [[combinatorics|0JKW7W7XFR]], the **Narayana numbers** $\operatorname{N}(n, k), n \in \mathbb{N}^+, 1 \le k \le n$ form a triangular array of [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], called the **Narayana triangle,** that occur in various counting problems. They are named after Canadian mathematician T. V. Narayana (1930–1987).
