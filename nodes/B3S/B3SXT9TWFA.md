## Summary

Vorticity is the [[curl|0NF6GQNXMY]] of the velocity field of a flow, $\boldsymbol{\omega} = \nabla \times \mathbf{u}$.
It measures the local rate of rotation of the fluid: a small parcel spins with angular velocity $\boldsymbol{\omega}/2$.
Flows with zero vorticity are called irrotational, and vorticity is the key quantity in the study of vortices and turbulence.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put a tiny paddle wheel into a moving fluid at a point.
If the wheel starts to turn, the fluid has vorticity there; the axis and speed of the turning give its direction and size.
A fluid turning as a rigid body with angular speed $\Omega$ has vorticity $2\Omega$ everywhere.
Even a flow along straight parallel lines can have vorticity.
In the shear flow $(y, 0)$, the top of the paddle wheel is pushed harder than the bottom, so the wheel turns.
