## Summary

A **Schnorr group**, proposed by Claus P. Schnorr, is a large prime-order [[subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]] of $\mathbb{Z}_p^\times$, the [[multiplicative group of integers modulo $p$|0F8X8ZRQ53]] for some [[prime|01826KMG2H]] $p$.
