## Summary

Breadth-first search (BFS) explores a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] from a source vertex in layers: first the source, then its neighbours, then their unvisited neighbours, and so on.
It uses a queue and runs in time $O(\abs{V} + \abs{E})$.
It finds every vertex reachable from the source and, for each, a path with the fewest edges, so it computes [[distances|0QV9VNNB4Z]] in unweighted graphs.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Drop a stone in a pond: the ripple reaches nearby points first and far points later.
BFS spreads through a graph the same way, one ring at a time.
In a social network, it finds your friends first, then friends of friends, and so on.
The ring in which a vertex is first reached is its distance from the source.
