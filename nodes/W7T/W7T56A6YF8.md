## Summary

The group $K_2(R)$ of a ring $R$ is Milnor's second algebraic K-group.
It is the kernel of the map from the Steinberg group to the group of elementary matrices.
So it collects the relations among elementary matrices that are not consequences of the obvious ones.
For a field it is generated by symbols $\set{a, b}$, and it is linked to reciprocity laws, to the Brauer group and to Galois cohomology.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Invertible matrices can be reduced by row operations.
The group $K_1$ records what row operations cannot do.
The group $K_2$ records something subtler: the non-obvious ways in which a sequence of row operations can bring a matrix back to where it started.
For the integers this group has exactly two elements.
For the rational numbers it contains the information of the quadratic reciprocity law.
