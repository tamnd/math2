## Summary

In commutative algebra, a **Zariski ring** is a commutative [[Noetherian|0670T59HGE]] topological ring *A* whose topology is defined by an [[ideal|096Z9MP2ME]] $\mathfrak a$ contained in the Jacobson radical, the intersection of all maximal ideals. They were introduced by (txt) under the name "semi-local ring" which now means something different, and named "Zariski rings" by (txt). Examples of Zariski rings are noetherian local rings with the topology induced by the maximal ideal, and $\mathfrak a$-adic completions of Noetherian rings.
