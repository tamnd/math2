## Summary

In mathematics, **Turing's method** is used to verify that for any given Gram point $g_{m}$ there lie *m* + 1 zeros of $\zeta(s)$, in the region $0 < \operatorname{Im}(s) < \operatorname{Im}(g_{m})$, where $\zeta(s)$ is the Riemann zeta function. It was discovered by Alan Turing and published in 1953, although that proof contained errors and a correction was published in 1970 by R. Sherman Lehman.
