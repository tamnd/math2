## Example

Let $\theta \in \R$ and let $A_\theta$ be the [[noncommutative torus|PZ97S3QQF1]], generated by unitaries $U, V$ with $V U = e^{2 \pi i \theta} U V$.
Its K-groups are
$$K_0(A_\theta) \cong \Z^2, \qquad K_1(A_\theta) \cong \Z^2.$$

Check by the [[Pimsner–Voiculescu exact sequence|7747Q44GVT]]: $A_\theta$ is the [[crossed product|8HG8TS46A6]] $C(\mathbb{T}) \rtimes_\alpha \Z$ with $\alpha(f)(z) = f(e^{2 \pi i \theta} z)$.
Here $U$ is the function $z$, and $V$ is the unitary that implements $\alpha$.
The rotations by $e^{2 \pi i t \theta}$, $t \in [0, 1]$, join $\alpha$ to the identity, so $\alpha_* = \id$ on $K_*(C(\mathbb{T}))$ and the maps $\id - \alpha_*$ vanish.
The sequence then gives short exact sequences
$$0 \to K_i(C(\mathbb{T})) \to K_i(A_\theta) \to K_{1-i}(C(\mathbb{T})) \to 0, \qquad i = 0, 1.$$
Since $K_0(C(\mathbb{T})) \cong K_1(C(\mathbb{T})) \cong \Z$, each $K_i(A_\theta)$ is an extension of $\Z$ by $\Z$, hence $\Z^2$.

For irrational $\theta$, $A_\theta$ has a unique tracial state $\tau$, and $\tau_*(K_0(A_\theta)) = \Z + \theta \Z \subseteq \R$.
The class $[1]$ has trace $1$, and For irrational $\theta$ with $0 < \theta < 1$, Rieffel constructed a projection in $A_\theta$ of trace $\theta$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The noncommutative torus is a "quantum" version of the ordinary torus, built from two unitaries that commute only up to a phase.
Its K-groups are the same as those of the ordinary torus: $\Z^2$ in both degrees.
The difference shows in the sizes of projections: their traces fill the dense subgroup $\Z + \theta \Z$ of the real line, while for the ordinary torus they are only integers.
For irrational $\theta$, this trace data tells apart the algebras $A_\theta$ up to $\theta \mapsto 1 - \theta$ and integer shifts.
