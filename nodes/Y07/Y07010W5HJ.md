## Summary

Tidal harmonic constituents are the sinusoidal terms into which the tide at a place is decomposed.
Each has a frequency fixed by astronomy, from the rates of the Earth's rotation and of the orbits of the Moon and the Sun.
Its amplitude and phase are fixed by the local response of the sea.
Harmonic analysis of a tide gauge record estimates these amplitudes and phases, and summing the terms predicts future tides.
The main ones are the lunar semidiurnal $M_2$, the solar semidiurnal $S_2$ and the diurnal $K_1$ and $O_1$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The tide looks complicated because several regular rhythms are added together.
One rhythm follows the Moon, with high water about every $12$ hours $25$ minutes.
Another follows the Sun, every $12$ hours exactly.
When the two are in step, at new and full Moon, tides are large; when they are out of step, tides are small.
Each rhythm is a constituent, and a few dozen of them predict the tide at a port very well.
