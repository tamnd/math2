## Statement

In number theory, **Euler's conjecture** is a disproved conjecture related to [[Fermat's Last Theorem|0Y5X3SDBCZ]]. It was presented by Leonhard Euler in 1778 to the Academy of Sciences of St. Petersburg. It states that for all integers $n$ and $k$ greater than 1, if the sum of $n$ many $k$th powers of positive integers is itself a $k$th power, then $n$ is greater than or equal to $k$:
