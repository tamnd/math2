## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $\Z[G]$ be its integral [[group ring|4NMTSX0XDS]].
Let $T \subseteq K_1(\Z[G])$ be the subgroup of classes of the $1 \times 1$ matrices $(\pm g)$ with $g \in G$, inside [[K1 of a ring|SXVNJY4XFY]].
The *Whitehead group* of $G$ is
$$\mathrm{Wh}(G) = K_1(\Z[G]) / T.$$

## Remarks

Example: for the trivial group, $K_1(\Z) = \set{\pm 1}$ is generated by the class of $(-1)$, so $\mathrm{Wh}(1) = 0$.
Non-example: for $G = \Z/5\Z$, the group $\mathrm{Wh}(G)$ is infinite cyclic, so $T$ is not all of $K_1(\Z[G])$; there are units of $\Z[G]$ not of the form $\pm g$.
$\mathrm{Wh}(G)$ is a functor of $G$, and it is abelian since $K_1$ is.
One also writes $\mathrm{Wh}(G) = \tilde K_1(\Z[G]) / \set{\pm g}$ in terms of the reduced group $\tilde K_1(\Z[G]) = K_1(\Z[G])/\set{\pm 1}$.
