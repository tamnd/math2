## Summary

The Schrödinger picture is the way of describing quantum dynamics in which state vectors change in time and [[observables|DF5RN297GE]] stay fixed.
A state vector evolves by $\psi(t) = e^{-itH/\hbar}\psi(0)$, where $H$ is the [[Hamiltonian|VXDEQB46GV]], so it solves the [[Schrödinger equation|EPZR6H2WJN]].
It gives the same expectation values as the [[Heisenberg picture|TAFA5VK0ST]], where the observables move instead.
It is the natural picture for wave functions and for solving the Schrödinger equation as a partial differential equation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

There are two ways to film a moving system: let the system move in front of a fixed camera, or keep the system fixed and move the camera.
In the Schrödinger picture the state moves and the measuring devices, the observables, stay put.
A wave packet of a free particle, for example, moves and spreads out in time, while the position operator is always multiplication by $x$.
