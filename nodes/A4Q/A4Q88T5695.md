## Summary

The Bloch sphere is the unit [[sphere|P9CM4GQ2T0]] in $\R^3$, viewed as the space of pure states of a [[qubit|E9QTT3XBXS]].
Mixed qubit states fill the solid unit ball, called the Bloch ball, with the maximally mixed state at the center.
Orthogonal pure states sit at antipodal points, and unitary gates act on the sphere by rotations.
It gives a faithful picture of all states of one qubit.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Every pure state of a qubit corresponds to a point on the surface of a ball.
The north pole is $|0\rangle$, the south pole is $|1\rangle$, and points on the equator are even superpositions with different phases.
The latitude tells how likely a measurement gives $0$ or $1$, and the longitude records the relative phase.
Noisy, mixed states lie inside the ball, and the center is the completely random state.
Quantum gates on one qubit turn the ball like a rotation of a globe.
