## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers linear forms, the dual space and dual bases, the double dual, annihilators, the dual map and the transpose, in finite and in infinite dimension.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Linear form|07YQ1C78G0]]: a linear map from a vector space to its field of scalars.
2. [[Hyperplanes and linear forms|~577d8228]]: a nonzero form has a hyperplane as kernel, and the kernel fixes the form up to a scalar.
3. [[Dual space|07JBSZE0V4]]: the vector space $V^*$ of all linear forms on $V$.
4. [[Dual basis|0ZD2CF6ZS0]]: the coordinate forms of a basis, and why $V \cong V^*$ through them depends on the basis.
5. [[Dimension of the dual space|00889YD15K]]: the dual basis is a basis of $V^*$, so $\dim V^* = \dim V$ in finite dimension.
6. [[Erdős-Kaplansky theorem|~ecdd288b]]: the exact dimension $\abs{F}^{\dim V}$ of $V^*$ for infinite-dimensional $V$.
7. [[Canonical isomorphism with the double dual|0ZJM9D7NPS]]: the natural map $V \to V^{**}$, an isomorphism exactly in finite dimension.
8. [[Annihilator|0ASHNYF7AG]]: the forms that vanish on a subspace, and the reversal of inclusions.
9. [[Dimension of the annihilator|06Q1EFVH0Q]]: $\dim U + \dim U^0 = \dim V$, and the duals of quotients and subspaces.
10. [[Dual map|0AZ5ARP287]]: the pullback $f^*(\varphi) = \varphi \circ f$, which goes in the opposite direction.
11. [[Matrix of the dual map is the transpose|0ZPB3180AY]]: in dual bases, the matrix of $f^*$ is the transpose of the matrix of $f$.
12. [[Kernel and image of the dual map|~0b4dab92]]: $\ker f^* = (\im f)^0$, $\im f^* = (\ker f)^0$, and the equality of row rank and column rank.
