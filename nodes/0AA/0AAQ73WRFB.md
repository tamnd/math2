## Summary

A limit cycle is a closed trajectory of a planar dynamical system with one property. At least one other trajectory spirals into it as time tends to $\infty$ or to $-\infty$.
Limit cycles model oscillations that sustain themselves. See the [[definition|0EERDDPSVT]].

## Remarks

Henri Poincaré started the study of limit cycles.
