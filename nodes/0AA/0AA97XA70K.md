## Summary

Let a point that is not an equilibrium return to itself after a fixed positive time. Its orbit is a periodic orbit of the dynamical system.
For an autonomous differential equation, it is the closed curve traced by a non-constant periodic solution. See the [[definition|01DSNP0B4R]].
