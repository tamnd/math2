## Definition

Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a complex [[semisimple Lie algebra|EY9DN7QAX1]] with [[Cartan subalgebra|BB098JPWGA]] $\mathfrak{h}$ and a choice of positive roots $\Phi^+$.
Let $\mathfrak{n}^+ = \bigoplus_{\alpha \in \Phi^+} \mathfrak{g}_\alpha$ and let $\mathfrak{b} = \mathfrak{h} \oplus \mathfrak{n}^+$ be the [[Borel subalgebra|AP4MMD8FPR]].
For $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^*$, let $\C_\lambda$ be the one-dimensional $\mathfrak{b}$-module on which $h \in \mathfrak{h}$ acts by $\lambda(h)$ and $\mathfrak{n}^+$ acts by $0$.
Let $U$ denote the [[universal enveloping algebra|SCZ8PRVY00]].
The *Verma module* of highest weight $\lambda$ is the $\mathfrak{g}$-module
$$M(\lambda) = U(\mathfrak{g}) \otimes_{U(\mathfrak{b})} \C_\lambda.$$
Here $\mathfrak{g}$ acts by left multiplication on the first factor.

## Remarks

The vector $v_\lambda = 1 \otimes 1$ has weight $\lambda$, is killed by $\mathfrak{n}^+$, and generates $M(\lambda)$.
With $\mathfrak{n}^- = \bigoplus_{\alpha \in \Phi^+} \mathfrak{g}_{-\alpha}$, the map $u \mapsto u v_\lambda$ is a linear isomorphism $U(\mathfrak{n}^-) \to M(\lambda)$, by the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem.
Every module generated by a vector of weight $\lambda$ killed by $\mathfrak{n}^+$ is a quotient of $M(\lambda)$.
$M(\lambda)$ has a unique maximal submodule, and the quotient is the irreducible module $L(\lambda)$.
Example: for $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\C)$ with basis $e, f, h$, put $v_k = f^k v_\lambda$ and write $\lambda$ for $\lambda(h)$.
Then $h v_k = (\lambda - 2k) v_k$, $f v_k = v_{k+1}$ and $e v_k = k(\lambda - k + 1) v_{k-1}$.
If $\lambda = n \in \Z_{\ge 0}$, then $e v_{n+1} = 0$, the vectors $v_k$ with $k > n$ span a submodule isomorphic to $M(-n-2)$, and the quotient is $L(n)$ of dimension $n + 1$.
Non-example: $L(n)$ itself is generated by a highest weight vector $v$, but $f^{n+1} v = 0$, so $u \mapsto uv$ is not injective on $U(\mathfrak{n}^-)$ and $L(n)$ is not a Verma module.
