## Summary

Real K-theory $KO$ is the analogue of [[complex K-theory|9E4JE4902G]] built from real [[vector bundles|P4DZM975BV]].
It is a generalized cohomology theory whose groups repeat with period $8$, by the real form of [[Bott periodicity|WN2DCBSX1C]].
Its coefficient groups, the groups of a point, are $\Z, \Z/2, \Z/2, 0, \Z, 0, 0, 0$ and then repeat.
They detect subtle phenomena in the topology of manifolds and in homotopy theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Real K-theory does for real vector bundles what complex K-theory does for complex ones: it allows formal differences of bundles.
Real bundles have more ways to twist than complex ones.
For example, on the circle the Möbius band is a real line bundle that is not trivial, while every complex bundle on the circle is trivial.
This extra twisting shows up as the groups $\Z/2$ in real K-theory, and makes the period $8$ instead of $2$.
