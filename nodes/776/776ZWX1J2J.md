## Summary

In mathematics, a **sparse polynomial** (also **lacunary polynomial** or **fewnomial**) is a polynomial that has far fewer terms than its [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] and number of variables would suggest. For example, $x^{10} + 3x^3 + 1$ is a sparse polynomial, as it is a trinomial with a [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] of $10$.
