## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homotopy theory|PGHYV9X502]].
It covers the Whitehead and Hurewicz theorems, homotopy excision and the Freudenthal suspension theorem, which relate homotopy groups to homology and control homotopy groups in a range of dimensions.
