## Summary

This area studies algebras of bounded operators on Hilbert space, their abstract versions, and the geometry and topology that are built on them.
A C*-algebra is a Banach algebra with an involution in which the norm of the product of x* and x is the square of the norm of x, for each element x.
By the Gelfand-Naimark theorem each commutative C*-algebra is an algebra of continuous functions on a locally compact space, so a general C*-algebra is studied as a noncommutative space.
Murray and von Neumann started the theory of von Neumann algebras in the 1930s, and Gelfand and Naimark gave the abstract theory of C*-algebras in 1943.
This topic collects the operator algebras and noncommutative geometry pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Functional analysis, operator theory and quantum theory have their own topics.
