## Summary

In number theory, a **Fortunate number** is the smallest integer $m > 1$ such that, for a given positive [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] $n$, $p_n\#+m$ is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]], where the primorial $p_n\#$ is the product of the first $n$ prime numbers. They are named after Reo Fortune.
