## Summary

The harmonic coordinate condition asks that each coordinate function of a spacetime solve the wave equation of the metric.
It is also called the de Donder gauge or the wave gauge.
In such coordinates the [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]] become a system of quasilinear [[wave equations|5EHD2XBQDG]] for the components of the metric.
This is the key step in the proof that the Einstein equations have a well-posed initial value problem.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Einstein equations do not fix the coordinates, so they are not a well-behaved evolution equation as they stand.
Choosing coordinates that themselves obey a wave equation removes this freedom.
After this choice, each component of the metric obeys a wave equation with extra lower-order terms, a type of equation whose solutions are well understood.
In flat Minkowski space the usual coordinates $t, x, y, z$ are harmonic, because they are linear functions.
