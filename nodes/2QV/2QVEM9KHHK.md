## Summary

In the mathematical area of graph theory, an undirected graph $G$ is **strongly chordal** if it is a chordal graph and every [[cycle|0Z05VCBQ7Y]] of even length (≥ 6) in $G$ has an *odd chord*, i.e., an edge that connects two vertices that are an odd [[distance|0QV9VNNB4Z]] (>1) apart from each other in the cycle.
