## Statement

In 1978 Patterson conjectured that θ<sub>*p*</sub> was equidistributed with error term asymptotically of order $X^{\frac{5}{6}}$ instead of quadratic as with Gauss sums which could explain the initial bias observed by Kummer. Next year his subsequent work with Heath-Brown disproving Kummer's conjecture showed that in fact it was equidistributed, but whether the order of the asymptotic was correct remained unknown. More than 20 years later, Heath-Brown closed on the problem, giving a new sieve method, and conjectured that it could be improved to obtain the predicted order. In 2021 the problem was demonstrated conditionally on the generalized Riemann hypothesis by Alexander Dunn and Maksym Radziwill, who also showed that the sieve of Heath Brown could not be improved as expected.
