## Summary

A sequence has a limit $L$ when its terms come and stay arbitrarily close to $L$ for large indices. Limits of sequences are the base of the notions of convergence, continuity and series in analysis. The node [[limit of a sequence|0XKWSSY8P2]] gives the precise definition.
