## Summary

The retarded potentials are the [[electric potential|SAEDXNM3Y1]] and the [[magnetic vector potential|8PEMR2J7EF]] produced by given charge and current densities.
Each source point contributes with the delay that light needs to travel to the field point.
They are the solution of the [[inhomogeneous electromagnetic wave equations|1AE7YGNX2R]] in the Lorenz gauge that is built from the past of the sources only.
They express causality in classical electrodynamics: a change in the sources is felt at distance $d$ only after the time $d / c$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a charge moves, the news of its motion spreads out at the speed of light.
So the potential at a point here and now depends on where the charges were a little earlier, not where they are now.
The retarded potential adds up the contributions of all charges, each one taken at its own earlier time.
For charges at rest the delay changes nothing, and one gets back the Coulomb potential $q / (4 \pi \varepsilon_0 r)$ of electrostatics.
