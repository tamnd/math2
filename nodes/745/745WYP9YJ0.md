## Summary

A Nash equilibrium is a strategy profile in which no player can gain by changing only her own strategy.
Each player's strategy is a best response to the strategies of the others.
It is the central solution concept of noncooperative game theory.
John Nash proved that every finite game has one in mixed strategies.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine everyone has made a choice, and then each person is told what the others chose.
If nobody regrets their own choice, the situation is a Nash equilibrium.
For two drivers on a road, "both keep right" is an equilibrium, and so is "both keep left".
"One keeps right and the other keeps left" is not, since each driver would want to switch.
