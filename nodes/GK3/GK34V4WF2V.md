## Summary

The epicyclic frequency $\kappa$ is the frequency of the radial oscillation of a star on a nearly circular orbit in a disc.
It is fixed by the [[rotation curve|V4FYBDSGMT]] of the disc.
It enters the theory of spiral arms, of resonances, and of the stability of discs.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Push a star on a circular orbit slightly outward.
It does not fly away: it swings in and out around a guiding circle while still going round the centre.
Seen from a frame that turns with the guiding circle, the star traces a small ellipse, the epicycle.
The frequency of this swing is $\kappa$.
Around a point mass $\kappa$ equals the orbital angular velocity, so the orbit closes into a Kepler ellipse.
