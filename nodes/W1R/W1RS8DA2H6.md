## Summary

An adiabatic process is a process in which no [[heat|CY4GJQGF06]] passes between the system and its surroundings.
Energy then changes only through work, so the change of internal energy equals the work done on the system.
Fast compressions and expansions, such as in an engine cylinder or in rising air, are close to adiabatic.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Pump up a bicycle tyre quickly and the pump gets warm.
The air is compressed too fast for heat to escape, so the work you do goes into heating the air.
That is an adiabatic compression.
In the same way, air that rises in the atmosphere expands and cools, which is why mountain tops are cold.
