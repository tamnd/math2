## Summary

The Newman–Penrose formalism is a way to write the geometry of a four-dimensional [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]] in a frame of four null vectors, a null tetrad.
Every tensor is replaced by its complex components in this frame.
The connection becomes twelve complex spin coefficients, and the [[Weyl tensor|SBHY31HESP]] becomes five complex scalars $\Psi_0, \dots, \Psi_4$.
It is the natural language for the [[Petrov classification|VAD96VS5HT]], for gravitational radiation and for algebraically special solutions such as the Kerr metric.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Light rays play a special role in relativity, so it is natural to measure everything along light-like directions.
The formalism chooses two real light-like directions, one going out and one coming in, and one complex direction that describes the plane across them.
All the curvature is then written as a handful of complex numbers.
For the Schwarzschild black hole, with a well chosen tetrad, only one of the five Weyl scalars is nonzero: $\Psi_2 = -M/r^3$.
This shows at a glance that the field is of the simplest non-radiating kind.
