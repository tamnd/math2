## Summary

The Cauchy principal value gives a value to some improper integrals that do not converge in the usual sense, see the [[definition|0V3RDQV3ZX]].
It removes a symmetric neighborhood of the singular point, or integrates over a symmetric interval $[-R, R]$, and then takes the limit.
