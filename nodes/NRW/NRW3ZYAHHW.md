## Summary

Let $f$ be a function and let $x$ be a point of its domain.
The derivative function of $f$ is the new function $f'$ whose value at $x$ is the [[limit of a function|0TXTSEMHR5]]
$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}.$$
The function $f'$ is defined exactly at those points $x$ where this limit exists.
At each such point, $f'(x)$ is the [[derivative|0F1WR9C83K]] of $f$ at $x$, so the pointwise derivative becomes a function of the input.
