## Summary

This topic is a part of [[enumerative combinatorics|FRSTEPZPCY]].
It covers paths on the integer lattice and the numbers that count them, such as the ballot, Catalan, Motzkin, Schröder, Narayana and Delannoy numbers, and walks in cones.
