## Summary

In mathematics, the **reflexive closure** of a [[binary relation|05AVARPXA2]] $R$ on a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] $X$ is the smallest [[reflexive relation|0X8NMST0J2]] on $X$ that contains $R$, i.e. the set $R \cup \{(x,x) \mid x \in X \}$.
