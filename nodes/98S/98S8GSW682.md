## Summary

In mathematics, a **weak Maass form** is a smooth function $f$ on the upper half plane, transforming like a modular form under the action of the modular group, being an eigenfunction of the corresponding hyperbolic [[Laplace operator|0E0B6RA8K0]], and having at most linear exponential growth at the cusps. If the eigenvalue of $f$ under the Laplacian is zero, then $f$ is called a **harmonic weak Maass form**, or briefly a **harmonic Maass form**.
