## Statement

Let $m$, $n$ and $p$ be [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], including zero.
If $m + p = n + p$ under [[addition of natural numbers|0S13V32CPB]], then $m = n$.

## Remarks

The same common summand $p$ is removed from both sides.
The law says that adding a fixed number is an injective map on the natural numbers.
