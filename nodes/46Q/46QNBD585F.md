## Statement

Use geometric units $G = c = 1$.
Let $(M, g)$ be a stationary, asymptotically flat spacetime containing a black hole.
Assume its event horizon $H$ is a [[Killing horizon|9ZXYW3NX4A]] of the [[Killing vector field|5AKNNW3W4Z]] $\chi = \xi + \Omega_H \eta$.
Here $\xi$ is the stationary Killing field, $\eta$ the axial one, and $\Omega_H$ the angular velocity of the horizon.
The surface gravity $\kappa$ is the function on $H$ defined by $\nabla_\chi \chi = \kappa \chi$.
Write $M$, $J$, $Q$ for the mass, angular momentum and charge of the black hole.
Write $A$ for the area of a cross-section of $H$, and $\Phi_H$ for the electric potential of the horizon.

1. (Zeroth law) If the matter satisfies the dominant [[energy condition|ENWD1YR20P]], then $\kappa$ is constant on $H$.
2. (First law) For a perturbation to a nearby stationary electrovacuum black hole,
$$\delta M = \frac{\kappa}{8\pi}\, \delta A + \Omega_H\, \delta J + \Phi_H\, \delta Q.$$
3. (Second law) If the null energy condition holds, the area of the event horizon does not decrease in time.

## Remarks

The laws were formulated and proved in this form by Bardeen, Carter and Hawking in 1973.
The second law is the [[Hawking area theorem|42EQ4G6978]].
They are formally identical to the laws of thermodynamics with $\kappa/2\pi$ in the role of temperature and $A/4$ in the role of entropy.
Hawking's discovery of [[Hawking radiation|Z1Y6E4YCTZ]] showed that this is more than an analogy: the temperature is $\hbar\kappa/2\pi$ in suitable units.

A third law was also proposed: $\kappa$ cannot be reduced to $0$ in finitely many steps.
It is not a theorem; in 2022 Kehle and Unger constructed gravitational collapse of charged matter that forms an extremal black hole, with $\kappa = 0$, in finite time.

Example: for the Kerr metric, $A = 8\pi m r_+$ with $r_+ = m + \sqrt{m^2 - a^2}$, and $\kappa = (r_+ - m)/(2 m r_+)$.
For Schwarzschild ($a = 0$) this gives $\kappa = 1/(4m)$ and $A = 16\pi m^2$, and indeed $\frac{\kappa}{8\pi}\, dA = \frac{1}{32\pi m}\, 32\pi m\, dm = dm$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Black holes obey rules that look exactly like the laws of heat.
Their surface gravity is the same all over the horizon, as temperature is the same throughout a body in equilibrium.
Changes in mass relate to changes in area as energy relates to entropy.
And the area never decreases, as entropy never decreases.

### L2 human

The first law follows from an identity relating the Komar integrals at infinity and on the horizon, or from Hamiltonian methods in the covariant phase space.
The zeroth law uses the Einstein equations and the energy condition to show that the derivative of $\kappa$ along the horizon vanishes.
With the [[Bekenstein–Hawking entropy|ANNJ7JR36T]] $S = A/4$ and the Hawking temperature $T = \kappa/2\pi$ (with $\hbar = 1$), the laws become genuine laws of thermodynamics.
