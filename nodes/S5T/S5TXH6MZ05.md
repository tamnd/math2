## Summary

A double-couple source is a point source made of two force couples of equal and opposite moment.
So its net force and net torque vanish.
It is the point-source equivalent of shear slip on a small fault, and so the standard model of an ordinary tectonic earthquake.
Its [[moment tensor|ZNN5WAT301]] has eigenvalues $M_0$, $0$, $-M_0$, where $M_0$ is the [[seismic moment|88YRH7XQDX]].
The eigenvectors give the tension, null and pressure axes of the earthquake.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A single pair of opposite forces offset from each other would make a body spin.
Add a second such pair, turning the other way, and the spinning cancels.
These two pairs together produce exactly the same seismic waves as a small patch of fault slipping.
This is why seismologists model most earthquakes as a double couple.
