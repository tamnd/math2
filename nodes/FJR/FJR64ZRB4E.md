## Summary

A Lindblad resonance is a radius in a rotating disc where stars meet a rotating pattern at the rate of their radial oscillation.
The pattern may be a bar or a spiral.
There the pattern pushes the orbit in step with its [[epicyclic|GK34V4WF2V]] motion, and its response is strong.
Lindblad resonances shape spiral arms, bars and rings in galaxies, and gaps and waves in planetary rings.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A child on a swing goes high if pushed once per swing.
A star in a galaxy also swings in and out at its epicyclic frequency.
A rotating bar or spiral pattern pulls on the star each time the star passes it.
If the passes happen once per swing, the effect adds up: that is a Lindblad resonance.
There is usually an inner one, where stars overtake the pattern, and an outer one, where the pattern overtakes the stars.
