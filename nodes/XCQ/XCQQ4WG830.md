## Summary

In arithmetic and algebra, the **cube** of a number $n$ is its third power, that is, the result of multiplying three instances of $n$ together. The cube of a number $n$ is denoted $n^{3}$, using a superscript 3, for example 2<sup>3</sup> = 8. The cube operation can also be defined for any other mathematical expression, for example $(x + 1)^{3}$.
