## Summary

In astrophysics, the **Eddington number**, $N_{\operatorname{Edd}}$, is the number of protons in the observable universe. Eddington originally calculated it as about \1.57 × 10<sup>79</sup>; current estimates make it approximately × 10<sup>80</sup>.
