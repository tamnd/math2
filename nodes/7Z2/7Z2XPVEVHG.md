## Summary

Superdense coding is a protocol that sends two classical bits by transmitting a single [[qubit|E9QTT3XBXS]], with the help of a previously shared [[Bell state|CA57HMCRQQ]].
The sender encodes the two bits by a Pauli operation on her half of the pair.
The receiver decodes them by a measurement in the Bell basis.
It is the counterpart of teleportation: there, one shared pair and two classical bits carry one qubit.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Without help, one qubit can carry only one bit of usable information.
If Alice and Bob already share an entangled pair, Alice can do one of four things to her half and then mail it to Bob.
The four choices turn the pair into four perfectly distinguishable states.
Bob measures both qubits together and learns which of the four choices Alice made, that is, two bits.
