## Summary

The deflection of the vertical at a point is the angle between the plumb line there and the normal to the [[reference ellipsoid|R9XX7TNJCH]].
It is usually split into a north–south and an east–west component.
It measures the slope of the [[geoid|999BYZY0ZG]] relative to the ellipsoid, and it links astronomical and geodetic coordinates.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A plumb bob hangs along the local direction of gravity.
Near a large mountain the mountain pulls the bob slightly sideways, so the bob does not line up exactly with the line perpendicular to the Earth's reference ellipsoid.
The small angle between the plumb line and the ellipsoid normal is the deflection of the vertical.
Typical values are a few seconds of arc, and in high mountains they can reach about a minute of arc.
