## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers inverse matrices, elementary matrices and the factorizations that come from elimination.
The topics are the invertible matrix theorem, the general and special linear groups as groups of matrices, and LU and block elimination.
