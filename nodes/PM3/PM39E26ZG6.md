## Summary

Let $x$ be an element of a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] $\mathfrak g$.
Its Engel subalgebra is the set of $y \in \mathfrak g$ that are killed by some power of $\operatorname{ad} x$.
It is the generalized eigenspace of $\operatorname{ad} x$ for the eigenvalue $0$, and it is a subalgebra containing $x$.
Engel subalgebras are the main tool for proving that [[Cartan subalgebras|BB098JPWGA]] exist.
For a suitably generic $x$, the Engel subalgebra of $x$ is a Cartan subalgebra.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Fix an element $x$ and look at the map "bracket with $x$".
Some elements are sent to $0$ after applying this map enough times.
They form the Engel subalgebra of $x$.
Take $2 \times 2$ matrices of trace zero and $x = \operatorname{diag}(1, -1)$.
Bracket with $x$ multiplies the two off-diagonal basis matrices by $2$ and $-2$ and kills $x$.
So the Engel subalgebra of $x$ is the line spanned by $x$.
