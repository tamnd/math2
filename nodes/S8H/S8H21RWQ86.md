## Summary

Thermodynamic work is energy transferred between a system and its surroundings by macroscopic forces, such as a piston pushing on a gas.
Like heat, it is a [[process function|10J7Y8HKGP]] and depends on the path of the process.
For a fluid changed slowly, the work done on it is $-\int p \, dV$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Push a piston into a cylinder of air and the air is squeezed; you have done work on it.
The air gets a bit warmer, because the energy you put in stays inside it.
If the air pushes the piston out, the air does work on the surroundings and loses energy.
The amount of work is the pressure times the change of volume when the pressure stays constant.
