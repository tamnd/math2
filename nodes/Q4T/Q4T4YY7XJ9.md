## Summary

In sequent calculus, the **completeness of atomic initial sequents** states that initial sequents $A \vdash A$ (where $A$ is an arbitrary formula) can be derived from only atomic initial sequents $p \vdash p$ (where $p$ is an atomic formula). This theorem plays a role analogous to eta expansion in lambda calculus, and dual to cut elimination and beta reduction. Typically it can be established by induction on the structure of $A$, much more easily than cut elimination.
