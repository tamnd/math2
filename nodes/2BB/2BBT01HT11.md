## Summary

Quantum theory is the physical theory of matter and radiation at the scale of atoms and below, together with its mathematical formulation.
In quantum mechanics a state of a system is a unit vector in a Hilbert space up to a phase factor, an observable is a self-adjoint operator, and the Born rule gives the probabilities of the results of a measurement.
The theory explains atomic spectra, chemical bonds, the properties of solids and the elementary particles, and it is the basis of quantum information and quantum computing.
Planck introduced the quantum of action in 1900, and Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Born and Dirac gave quantum mechanics its modern form in 1925 and 1926.
This topic collects the quantum theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
The spectral theory of Schrödinger operators, operator algebras, quantum computation, quantum error correction and quantum cryptography have their own topics.
