## Summary

Game theory is the mathematical study of situations in which several decision makers interact.
The outcome for each of them depends on the choices of all of them.
It provides models such as [[normal-form games|5MZTZRC3CZ]] and [[extensive-form games|4Y8AF9H9HA]], and solution concepts that predict or recommend choices.
It is used in economics, political science, biology and computer science.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of two shops that set prices at the same time.
Each shop's profit depends on its own price and on the price of the other shop.
Game theory studies such situations, called games, and asks what each player should do when it knows that the others are also thinking.
A small example is rock-paper-scissors: no single move is best, and the sensible plan is to pick each move at random with probability $1/3$.
