## Summary

A function satisfies a Lipschitz condition when the distance between two values is at most a constant times the distance between the two points.
This is a strong form of continuity. In the theory of differential equations, it gives uniqueness of solutions. See the [[definition|055VVSN00V]].

## Remarks

It is named after Rudolf Lipschitz.
