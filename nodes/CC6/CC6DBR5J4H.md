## Summary

This topic is a part of [[elementary number theory|7XZ94HTPQT]].
It covers integer sequences that linear and nonlinear recurrences or simple iterations define, such as Lucas sequences and divisibility sequences, their periodicity modulo m, the Skolem-Mahler-Lech theorem, Somos sequences and the Collatz problem.
