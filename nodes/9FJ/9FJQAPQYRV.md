## Statement

In mathematics, the **conductor–discriminant formula** or **Führerdiskriminantenproduktformel**, introduced by (txt) for abelian extensions and by (txt) for [[Galois extensions|0DS2F7GK05]], is a formula calculating the relative discriminant of a finite Galois extension $L/K$ of local or global fields from the Artin conductors of the irreducible characters $\mathrm{Irr}(G)$ of the [[Galois group|0XTY86BZKY]] $G = G(L/K)$.
