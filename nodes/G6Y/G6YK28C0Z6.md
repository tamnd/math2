## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[locally compact group|AHV05ZBCYW]].
Let $M(G)$ be the space of [[complex measures|NY6TYHRGMS]] $\mu$ on the Borel sets of $G$ whose [[total variation|EPBND51CR5]] $\abs{\mu}$ is a [[Radon measure|HZNEQQBCW4]].
The *measure algebra* of $G$ is $M(G)$ with the following structure.

1. The norm is the total variation $\norm{\mu} = \abs{\mu}(G)$.
2. The product is the *convolution* $\mu * \nu \in M(G)$, determined by
$$\int_G f \, d(\mu * \nu) = \int_G \int_G f(xy) \, d\mu(x) \, d\nu(y)$$
for every $f$ in the space $C_0(G)$ of [[continuous functions vanishing at infinity|N59BW1QFD1]].
3. The involution is $\mu^*(A) = \overline{\mu(A^{-1})}$ for Borel sets $A$, where $A^{-1} = \set{x^{-1} : x \in A}$.

## Remarks

By the Riesz representation theorem, $M(G)$ is the dual of the Banach space $C_0(G)$, and the norm in clause 1 is the dual norm.
The convolution is well defined since the right side of clause 2 is a bounded linear functional of $f$, of norm at most $\norm{\mu} \norm{\nu}$.
With these operations $M(G)$ is a [[Banach algebra|9KYWN94QDN]] with isometric involution, with unit the point mass $\delta_e$ at the identity $e$.
It is commutative exactly when $G$ is abelian.
For a left Haar measure $m$, the map $f \mapsto f \, dm$ embeds the [[group algebra|6GD25S53ND]] $L^1(G)$ isometrically into $M(G)$ as a closed two-sided ideal.

Example: point masses.
For $x, y \in G$, clause 2 gives $\int f \, d(\delta_x * \delta_y) = f(xy)$, so $\delta_x * \delta_y = \delta_{xy}$.
Thus $x \mapsto \delta_x$ is an injective homomorphism of $G$ into the group of invertible elements of $M(G)$.

Non-example: Lebesgue measure $\lambda$ on $\R$ is a Radon measure but not an element of $M(\R)$, since $\lambda(\R) = \infty$ and so its total variation norm is infinite.
