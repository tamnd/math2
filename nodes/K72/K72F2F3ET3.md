## Statement

Let $X$ be a smooth variety over a field.
For $p \ge 0$ let $\mathcal{K}_p$ be the sheaf on $X$ for the [[Zariski topology|8SQK7RTB63]] associated with the presheaf $U \mapsto K_p(\mathcal{O}(U))$, where $K_p$ is Quillen's [[algebraic K-group|5GVFZWMWC8]].
Then for every $p \ge 0$ there is a natural isomorphism
$$H^p(X, \mathcal{K}_p) \cong \operatorname{CH}^p(X)$$
between [[sheaf cohomology|PQ6Q143ARJ]] and the [[Chow group|EBEF6PEK4N]] of codimension $p$ cycles.

## Remarks

The proof uses the [[Gersten complex|W4BRV4PT2F]].
For $X$ smooth over a field, Quillen proved that the sheafified Gersten complex is a flasque resolution of $\mathcal{K}_p$.
So $H^p(X, \mathcal{K}_p)$ is the cokernel of the last map $\bigoplus_{x \in X^{(p-1)}} k(x)^\times \to \bigoplus_{x \in X^{(p)}} \Z$, which takes a rational function on a subvariety of codimension $p - 1$ to its divisor.
This cokernel is the group of codimension $p$ cycles modulo rational equivalence.

Example: for $p = 1$, $\mathcal{K}_1 = \mathcal{O}_X^\times$, and the formula is the classical isomorphism $H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X^\times) = \operatorname{Pic}(X) \cong \operatorname{CH}^1(X)$.
Bloch proved the case $p = 2$ for surfaces, and Quillen proved the general case.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Chow groups count subvarieties of a fixed codimension up to deformation in families.
K-theory is defined quite differently, from vector bundles and matrices.
Bloch's formula says that the Chow groups can be recovered from K-theory by taking sheaf cohomology.
It is a bridge that lets tools of one theory be used in the other.

### L2 human

The case $p = 1$ is the familiar identification of line bundles with divisor classes.
For $p = 2$ the formula expresses $\operatorname{CH}^2(X)$ through $K_2$ of local rings, which brought Milnor's $K_2$ and symbols into the study of zero-cycles on surfaces.
The formula is a key reason to expect a motivic cohomology theory linking K-theory and algebraic cycles, as in the motivic spectral sequence.
