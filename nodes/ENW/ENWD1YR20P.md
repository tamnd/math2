## Summary

Energy conditions are inequalities on the [[stress–energy tensor|REDZ92MKXX]] of matter in general relativity that express that energy density is not negative in a suitable sense.
The main ones are the null, weak, strong and dominant energy conditions.
The Einstein equations alone allow any metric if the matter is arbitrary, so such conditions are needed to prove general theorems.
They are hypotheses of the singularity theorems, the positive energy theorem and the area theorem for black holes.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In general relativity, any curved spacetime solves Einstein's equations if one allows whatever matter is needed to produce it.
Some of that matter would be very strange, for example with negative energy.
Energy conditions rule out such matter by asking that every observer measures a non-negative energy density, or a similar rule.
Ordinary matter like dust, gas and light satisfies them.
For a fluid with density $\rho$ and pressure $p$, the weak energy condition says simply $\rho \ge 0$ and $\rho + p \ge 0$.
