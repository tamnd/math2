## Summary

Stochastic analysis is the calculus of random processes, with stochastic integrals, stochastic differential equations and the analysis of functionals of Brownian motion.
The Itô integral of an adapted process against Brownian motion is the limit in mean square of sums in which the integrand takes its value at the left end of each interval.
Stochastic differential equations model systems under random forcing in physics, biology, engineering and finance, and their solutions give probabilistic formulas for the solutions of partial differential equations.
Itô defined the stochastic integral and stochastic differential equations in the 1940s.
This topic collects the stochastic analysis pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Stochastic processes, random structures and probabilistic models, and partial differential equations have their own topics.
