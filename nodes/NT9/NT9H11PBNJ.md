## Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an [[abelian category|Y9CZ3MTNF1]] whose isomorphism classes of objects form a set.
Let $F$ be the [[free abelian group|0WJX8MGG37]] on the isomorphism classes $[A]$ of objects $A$ of $\mathcal{A}$.
Let $N \subseteq F$ be the subgroup generated by the elements $[B] - [A] - [C]$, one for each [[short exact sequence|KJKNJMGKZZ]] $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ in $\mathcal{A}$.
The *Grothendieck group* of $\mathcal{A}$ is the [[quotient group|0KYX2N69J0]] $K_0(\mathcal{A}) = F / N$.

## Remarks

The map $A \mapsto [A]$ is universal among additive functions.
For every abelian group $G$ and every function $\chi$ from objects to $G$ with $\chi(B) = \chi(A) + \chi(C)$ for each short exact sequence, there is exactly one homomorphism $K_0(\mathcal{A}) \to G$ sending $[A]$ to $\chi(A)$.
The sequence $0 \to A \to A \oplus C \to C \to 0$ gives $[A \oplus C] = [A] + [C]$, and $0 \to 0 \to 0 \to 0 \to 0$ gives $[0] = 0$.

Example: let $\mathcal{A}$ be the category of finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field $F$.
Every short exact sequence satisfies $\dim B = \dim A + \dim C$, so the dimension induces a homomorphism $K_0(\mathcal{A}) \to \Z$.
It is an isomorphism, since $V \cong F^{\dim V}$ gives $[V] = \dim V \cdot [F]$.

Non-example: for finitely generated abelian groups, this group is not the group completion of the monoid of isomorphism classes under direct sum.
In $K_0$, the sequence $0 \to \Z \to \Z \to \Z/2\Z \to 0$, with first map multiplication by $2$, forces $[\Z/2\Z] = 0$, while in the group completion $[\Z/2\Z] \ne 0$, since finitely generated abelian groups can be cancelled from direct sums.
