## Summary

In mathematics, [[Bertrand's postulate|0FJTGZA4GS]] (now a theorem) states that, for each $n \ge 2$, there is a [[prime|01826KMG2H]] $p$ such that $n<p<2n$. First conjectured in 1845 by Joseph Bertrand, it was first [[proven|0B94TKFQJX]] by Chebyshev, and a shorter but also advanced proof was given by Ramanujan.
