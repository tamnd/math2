## Summary

In mathematics, a **Frey curve** or **Frey–Hellegouarch** curve is the elliptic curve

$$y^2 = x(x - \alpha)(x + \beta)$$

associated with an ABC triple $\alpha+\beta=\gamma$. This relates properties of solutions of equations to elliptic curves. This curve was popularized in its application to Fermat’s Last Theorem where one investigates a (hypothetical) solution of [[Fermat's equation|0Y5X3SDBCZ]]
