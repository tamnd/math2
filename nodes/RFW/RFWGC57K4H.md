## Summary

Steady-state heat conduction is [[conduction|SW8X60GYZT]] in which the temperature at every point no longer changes in time.
Then the [[heat equation|VP2HN86VAG]] reduces to [[Laplace's equation|S8RG6N1PRT]], or to [[Poisson's equation|DWK5N5SKTK]] when heat is produced inside the body.
It describes walls, pipes and machine parts long after the conditions around them have become constant.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Switch on a heater in a room and wait.
At first the walls warm up, but after a while the temperature at each point of a wall stops changing.
Heat still flows from inside to outside, but as much heat enters each piece of the wall as leaves it.
In a flat wall the temperature then falls in a straight line from the warm face to the cold face.
