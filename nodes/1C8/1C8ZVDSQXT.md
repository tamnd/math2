## Statement

In mathematics, **Tarski's theorem**, proved by (txt), states that in [[ZF|0HE5WDQ9FJ]] the statement "For every infinite set $A$, there is a bijective map between the sets $A$ and $A\times A$" implies the [[axiom of choice|0HTGB13FE2]]. The opposite direction was already known (provable via an explicit [[pairing function for any aleph number|0NP5N5PYW5]]), thus the statement and axiom of choice are equivalent.
