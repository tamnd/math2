## Summary

Convective heat transfer is the transport of heat by a moving fluid, through the combined action of bulk flow and [[conduction|SW8X60GYZT]].
It governs heat exchange between a solid surface and a gas or liquid flowing past it.
It is forced when a pump, fan or wind drives the flow, and natural when buoyancy from temperature differences drives it.
Engineers summarize it by a [[heat transfer coefficient|J821AD6YTE]] and the [[Nusselt number|THH3V9AHDN]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Blowing on hot soup cools it faster than leaving it alone.
The moving air carries the warmed air away from the surface and brings fresh cool air in its place.
Heat still has to cross a thin layer of air near the surface by conduction, but the flow keeps this layer thin.
Warm air rising above a radiator is the natural kind of convection; a fan heater is the forced kind.
