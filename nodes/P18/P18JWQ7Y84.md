## Statement

Let $G$ be a [[locally compact group|AHV05ZBCYW]] with a left [[Haar measure|VY0HXD3GC4]], and let $\lambda$ be its [[left regular representation|PR8C073VWY]] on $L^2(G)$.
For $f \in L^1(G)$, let $\lambda(f)$ be the bounded operator $\xi \mapsto f * \xi$ on $L^2(G)$.
The following are equivalent.

1. $G$ is [[amenable|CEAT9BESKK]].
2. $\lambda$ has almost invariant vectors: for every compact $K \subseteq G$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a unit vector $\xi \in L^2(G)$ with $\norm{\lambda(x)\xi - \xi}_2 < \varepsilon$ for all $x \in K$.
3. $\norm{\lambda(f)} = \int_G f \, dm$ for every $f \in L^1(G)$ with $f \ge 0$, where $m$ is the Haar measure.

## Remarks

Condition 2 says that the trivial representation is weakly contained in $\lambda$.
From [[Reiter's condition|CJ3JX5D3QJ]] one passes to condition 2 by taking $\xi = f^{1/2}$, using $\norm{a^{1/2} - b^{1/2}}_2^2 \le \norm{a - b}_1$ for nonnegative $a, b$.
Condition 2 gives condition 3, because $\ip{\lambda(f)\xi}{\xi}$ is close to $\int f$ for almost invariant $\xi$, while $\norm{\lambda(f)} \le \norm{f}_1$ always.

Example: for $G = \Z$, take $\xi = n^{-1/2} \mathbf{1}_{\set{0, \ldots, n-1}}$; then $\norm{\lambda(k)\xi - \xi}_2^2 = 2\abs{k}/n$ for $\abs{k} \le n$.
For the free group $F_2$ with generators $a, b$ and $f = \frac14(\delta_a + \delta_{a^{-1}} + \delta_b + \delta_{b^{-1}})$, one has $\norm{\lambda(f)} = \sqrt{3}/2 < 1$, so $F_2$ is not amenable.

A consequence is that, for amenable $G$, the full and the reduced group C\*-algebras coincide.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Amenability can be read off from how the group acts on square-integrable functions by translation.
The group is amenable exactly when there are functions that translations barely move.
Equivalently, averaging operators built from probability measures on the group keep their full norm $1$.

### L2 human

The theorem puts amenability into the language of unitary representations: the trivial representation lies in the reduced dual, which is the support of $\lambda$ for the [[Fell topology|SV5YRK473H]].
For a discrete group, condition 3 applied to a symmetric probability measure gives [[Kesten's criterion|FYJE0R4PGT]].
Non-amenability is a weak relative of [[Kazhdan's property (T)|GTFZ277047]].
A non-amenable group has no almost invariant vectors in $L^2(G)$.
Property (T) forbids them in every representation without invariant vectors.
