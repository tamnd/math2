## Summary

In mathematics and abstract algebra, a **Boolean domain** is a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] consisting of exactly two elements whose interpretations include *false* and *true*. In logic, mathematics and theoretical computer science, a Boolean domain is usually written as {0, 1}, or $\mathbb{B}.$
