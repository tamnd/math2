## Summary

Minkowski space is the four-dimensional real vector space $\R^4$ with the indefinite quadratic form $-(x^0)^2 + (x^1)^2 + (x^2)^2 + (x^3)^2$.
It is the arena of special relativity: its points are events, and its symmetries are the Lorentz and Poincaré transformations.
It is the flat model of a [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]], as Euclidean space is the flat model of a Riemannian manifold.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Minkowski space joins time and the three directions of space into one four-dimensional space.
Distances are measured with a twist: the time part enters with a minus sign.
So a vector can have positive, zero or negative squared length.
For example, let $x^0 = ct$.
The vector $(1, 1, 0, 0)$ has squared length $-1 + 1 = 0$, which is the path of a light ray.
The vector $(1, 0, 0, 0)$ has squared length $-1$, which is a clock at rest.
