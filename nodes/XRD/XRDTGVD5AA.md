## Summary

Probability theory is the mathematical study of chance, of random variables and their distributions, and of the limit laws for sums of many random terms.
A probability space is a measure space with total mass one, and a random variable is a measurable function on such a space.
Probability is the base of statistics, of statistical physics and of the analysis of randomized algorithms, and it gives tools to many other parts of mathematics.
Pascal and Fermat started the theory in their letters of 1654, and Kolmogorov gave its axioms in the language of measure theory in 1933.
This topic collects the probability theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Stochastic processes, stochastic analysis, random structures and probabilistic models, and statistical inference have their own topics.
