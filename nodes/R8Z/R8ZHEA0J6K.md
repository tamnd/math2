## Summary

In graph theory, a **wheel graph** is a graph formed by connecting a single universal vertex to all vertices of a [[cycle|0QQ0F12962]]. A wheel graph with $n$ vertices can also be defined as the 1-skeleton of an ($n - 1$)-gonal pyramid.
