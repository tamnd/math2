## Statement

In algebraic geometry, the **main theorem of elimination theory** states that every projective scheme is proper. A version of this theorem predates the existence of scheme theory. It can be stated, proved, and applied in the following more classical setting. Let $k$ be a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]], denote by $\mathbb{P}_k^n$ the $n$-dimensional projective space over $k$. The main theorem of elimination theory is the statement that for any $n$ and any algebraic variety $V$ defined over $k$, the projection map $V \times \mathbb{P}_k^n \to V$ sends Zariski-closed subsets to Zariski-closed subsets.
