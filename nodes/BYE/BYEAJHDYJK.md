## Summary

A **strong positional game** (also called **Maker-Maker game**) is a kind of positional game. Like most positional games, it is described by its set of *positions* ($X$) and its family of *winning-sets* ($\mathcal{F}$- a [[family of subsets|05Q6G4EH2G]] of $X$). It is played by two players, called First and Second, who alternately take previously untaken positions.
