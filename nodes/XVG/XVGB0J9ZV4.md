## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical economics and social sciences|D7CC1S998N]].
It covers the division of a heterogeneous divisible good, or of a set of tasks and rents, among agents with different valuations, with fairness notions, existence theorems and cut-and-choose protocols.
