## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the mathematical treatment of truth and of the paradoxes of self-reference.
The main parts are Tarski's theory, the liar paradox and its relatives, and the fixed-point and revision theories.
The last part is the axiomatic theories of truth over arithmetic.
The topic also treats two paradoxes that are close to the liar: the knower paradox and the sorites paradox.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Liar paradox|~005ac188]]: the naive truth schema is inconsistent over Robinson arithmetic.
2. [[Semantic conception of truth|8FBF0EFFC9]]: Tarski's conditions of formal correctness and material adequacy.
3. [[Tarskian hierarchy|Z6MCGB0ZFX]]: a separate truth predicate at each level of a hierarchy of languages.
4. [[Truth-teller sentence|~66f66b7a]]: a sentence that is consistent with either truth value.
5. [[Berry paradox|~6cd3e1b8]]: definability in few words, and its precise form for arithmetic.
6. [[Grelling-Nelson paradox|~1ca05504]]: heterological adjectives and the naive satisfaction schema.
7. [[Yablo paradox|~c38274a5]]: an infinite sequence of sentences gives an omega-inconsistent theory.
8. [[Knower paradox|~ba539499]]: a knowledge predicate with factivity and necessitation is inconsistent.
9. [[Kripke's theory of truth|~1dd87a57]]: fixed points of the strong Kleene jump, and grounded sentences.
10. [[Revision theory of truth|~721e1cff]]: truth as a circular definition, with revision sequences.
11. [[Truth-value gap|4CBCWZQZ12]]: sentences that are neither true nor false.
12. [[Truth-value glut|WH94DG6D3Q]]: sentences that are both true and false, in the logic of paradox.
13. [[Axiomatic theories of truth|~67eedfc4]]: truth as a primitive predicate with axioms over Peano arithmetic.
14. [[Compositional truth over PA proves the consistency of PA|~5af95d4a]]: the strength that induction adds to compositional truth.
15. [[Kripke-Feferman theory|~73df3209]] and its [[definition|~a625d303]]: the axioms that describe Kripke's fixed points in classical logic.
16. [[McGee's theorem|~e7fe60d3]]: natural type-free truth axioms give an omega-inconsistent theory.
17. [[Sorites paradox|~13b952b1]]: tolerance for a vague predicate contradicts two clear cases.

### Prerequisites

- [[Structures, satisfaction and truth|NJDME6F845]]: truth of a sentence in a structure, used on almost every page.
- [[Formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]]: Robinson arithmetic, Peano arithmetic and the standard model.
- [[Gödel's incompleteness theorems and related limits|HXHHWY3328]]: Gödel numbering, the diagonal lemma and the undefinability of truth.
- [[Many-valued and fuzzy logics|WZP584JQ06]]: strong Kleene logic, the logic of paradox and Łukasiewicz logic, for rows 9, 11, 12 and 17.
