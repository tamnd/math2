## Summary

This area studies equations in which the unknown is a function and the equation relates the values of the function at different points, its differences, or its integrals against a kernel.
An integral equation contains the unknown function under an integral sign, and in a Fredholm equation of the second kind the function equals a given function plus a multiple of the integral of a kernel times the function.
These equations come from boundary value problems, population models, renewal theory, scattering and the characterization of the elementary functions, and the Fredholm theory of integral equations was a main source of functional analysis.
Cauchy studied the additive functional equation in 1821, and Fredholm published his theory of integral equations in 1903.
This topic collects the functional, difference and integral equations pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Operator theory, ordinary differential equations and real analysis have their own topics.
