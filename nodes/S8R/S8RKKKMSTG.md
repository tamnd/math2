## Summary

A four-vector is a vector of [[Minkowski space|G49G4R1K04]].
In each inertial frame it has four components, which change by a [[Lorentz transformation|WEVGN4C7PQ]] when the frame changes.
Physical quantities such as displacement in spacetime, four-velocity, four-momentum and the wave four-vector are four-vectors.
Writing laws with four-vectors makes their agreement with the principle of relativity automatic.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In ordinary physics a vector has three components, and they change in a fixed way when you rotate your axes.
In relativity time joins space, so vectors get a fourth, time-like component, and they change in a fixed way when you change your speed.
The squared length $-(A^0)^2 + (A^1)^2 + (A^2)^2 + (A^3)^2$ of a four-vector is the same for every observer.
For example, energy and momentum combine into the four-vector $(E/c, p_x, p_y, p_z)$, and its squared length $-m^2 c^2$ depends only on the mass.
