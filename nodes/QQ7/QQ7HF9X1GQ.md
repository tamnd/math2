## Summary

A magnetic monopole is a hypothetical particle that carries a net magnetic charge.
It is a source of the [[magnetic field|XRED3C547P]] in the way an electric charge is a source of the electric field.
Ordinary [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]] exclude it through [[Gauss's law for magnetism|2K5D5HH89T]], and no monopole has been observed.
Dirac showed that the existence of a single monopole would force all electric charges to be integer multiples of a basic charge.
In geometry the field of a monopole is described by a connection on a nontrivial circle bundle over the sphere.
Monopoles of gauge theories are a major topic in mathematics.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole, and cutting a magnet in two gives two smaller magnets, each with both poles.
A magnetic monopole would be an isolated north pole, or an isolated south pole.
Its field would point straight out from it in all directions, like the electric field of a point charge.
Physicists have searched for such particles, but none has been found.
