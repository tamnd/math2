## Summary

In mathematics, given an additive subgroup $\Gamma \subset \mathbb{R}$, the **Novikov ring** $\operatorname{Nov}(\Gamma)$ of $\Gamma$ is the subring of $\mathbb{Z}[\![\Gamma]\!]$ consisting of formal sums $\sum n_{\gamma_i} t^{\gamma_i}$ such that $\gamma_1 > \gamma_2 > \cdots$ and $\gamma_i \to -\infty$. The notion was introduced by Sergei Novikov in the papers that initiated the generalization of Morse theory using a closed one-form instead of a function. The notion is used in quantum cohomology, among the others.
