## Summary

The Prandtl–Reuss equations are the constitutive equations of an isotropic elastic-plastic solid at small strains.
They use the [[von Mises yield criterion|V82XMG8WPE]] and the [[associated flow rule|80NPDZSKBT]].
They give the strain rate as the sum of an elastic part from Hooke's law and a plastic part parallel to the deviatoric stress.
They are the standard model of metal plasticity at small strains; without the elastic part they become the Lévy–Mises equations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

These equations say how a metal deforms once it starts to yield.
Part of the deformation is elastic and follows Hooke's law, as before yielding.
The other part is plastic: it changes the shape in the direction of the shear stress and does not change the volume.
