## Summary

A coherent state of a quantum harmonic oscillator is an eigenvector of the [[annihilation operator|7N0J4DF8N5]].
Its position and momentum have the smallest product of uncertainties allowed by the Heisenberg relation.
Under the oscillator's evolution it stays coherent, and its centre follows a classical orbit.
Coherent states describe the light of a laser, and they give a continuous overcomplete family of vectors that resolves the identity.
The idea extends to coherent states attached to other Lie groups.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A coherent state is the quantum state that looks most like a classical oscillating particle.
Its wave function is a Gaussian bump that swings back and forth without changing its shape.
It is labelled by one complex number $\alpha$, whose real and imaginary parts give the average position and momentum.
The number of quanta in it is random with a Poisson distribution of mean $\abs{\alpha}^2$.
For $\alpha = 0$ it is the ground state of the oscillator.
