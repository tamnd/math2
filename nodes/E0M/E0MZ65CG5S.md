## Statement

In mathematics, **Lehmer's totient problem** asks whether there is any [[composite number|008BH74D7R]] $n$ such that [[Euler's totient function|0DAMHCT17R]] $\varphi(n)$ divides $n - 1$. This is an unsolved problem.
