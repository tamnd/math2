## Summary

In graph theory, a **Moore graph** is a [[regular graph|0KA4S8R39M]] whose girth (the shortest [[cycle|0Z05VCBQ7Y]] length) is more than twice its diameter (the distance between the farthest two vertices). If the [[degree|0KS0KRDV4Y]] of such a graph is $d$ and its diameter is $k$, its girth must equal $2k + 1$.
