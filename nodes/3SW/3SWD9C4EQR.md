## Summary

Having to do with coloring; see color. Chromatic graph theory is the theory of graph coloring. The chromatic number $\chi(G)$ is the minimum number of colors needed in a proper coloring of $G$. $\chi\,'(G)$ is the chromatic index of $G$, the minimum number of colors needed in a proper [[edge coloring|0B5WCC02AN]] of $G$.
