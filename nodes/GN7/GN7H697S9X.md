## Definition

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $a_0, \ldots, a_{n-1}$, $b_0, \ldots, b_{n-1}$ and $d$ be real numbers.
A single-input single-output system $x' = Ax + Bu$, $y = Cx + Du$ with state $x \in \R^n$ is in *controllable canonical form* if

1. $A$ is the [[companion matrix|0ZCWPNZTQ3]] with ones on the superdiagonal, last row $\begin{pmatrix} -a_0 & -a_1 & \cdots & -a_{n-1} \end{pmatrix}$ and zeros elsewhere;
2. $B$ is the last standard basis vector $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}^T$;
3. $C = \begin{pmatrix} b_0 & b_1 & \cdots & b_{n-1} \end{pmatrix}$ and $D = d$.

## Remarks

The equations read $x_k' = x_{k+1}$ for $k < n$ and $x_n' = -a_0 x_1 - \cdots - a_{n-1} x_n + u$.
With zero initial state, $X_{k+1} = s^{k} X_1$ and $(s^n + a_{n-1} s^{n-1} + \cdots + a_0) X_1 = U$, so the [[transfer function|86JKV3TRFD]] is
$$G(s) = \frac{b_{n-1} s^{n-1} + \cdots + b_1 s + b_0}{s^n + a_{n-1} s^{n-1} + \cdots + a_0} + d.$$
The denominator is the [[characteristic polynomial|0VSSPAFM2C]] of $A$.
The pair $(A, B)$ is always [[controllable|A9WFM77Z4P]]: the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} B & AB & \cdots & A^{n-1} B \end{pmatrix}$ has ones on the antidiagonal and zeros above it, so it has rank $n$ by the [[Kalman rank condition|9NSCZ2KBEB]].
Conversely, every controllable pair $(A, B)$ with a single input is [[similar|0E89HQATM8]] to this form, with $a_0, \ldots, a_{n-1}$ the coefficients of its characteristic polynomial.
Example: $G(s) = 1/(s^2 + 3s + 2)$ gives $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -2 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $C = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, $D = 0$.
Non-example: the same $A$ with $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$ fails clause 2, so it is not in controllable canonical form, although this pair is still controllable.
Some books order the state variables the other way, which puts the coefficients in the first row of $A$.
