## Summary

In graph theory, a **dominating set** for a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] $G$ is a subset $D$ of its vertices, such that any vertex of $G$ is in $D$, or has a neighbor in $D$. The **domination number** $\gamma(G)$ is the number of vertices in a smallest dominating set for $G$.
