## Summary

A designated vertex in a graph, particularly in directed trees and rooted graphs.

The inverse operation to a graph power: a $k$th root of a graph $G$ is another graph on the same vertex set such that two vertices are adjacent in $G$ if and only if they have distance at most $k$ in the root.
