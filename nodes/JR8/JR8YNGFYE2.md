## Summary

A **refactorable number** or **tau number** is an integer *n* that is divisible by the count of its [[divisors|0PG2H7WKGG]], or to put it algebraically, *n* is such that $\tau(n)\mid n$ with $\tau(n)=\sigma_0(n)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(e_i+1)$ for $n=\prod_{i=1}^np_i^{e_i}$.
