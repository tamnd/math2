## Summary

Landau theory describes a continuous phase transition by a free energy that is a polynomial in the [[order parameter|XSA203109Z]].
The coefficients depend on the temperature, and the terms allowed are those invariant under the symmetry of the system.
The equilibrium value of the order parameter is the point where this free energy is minimal.
The theory predicts the mean-field [[critical exponents|SQ41AZGZ2P]], and with a gradient term it becomes the Ginzburg–Landau theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Draw the free energy as a curve over the possible values of the order parameter.
Above the critical temperature the curve is a bowl with its bottom at $0$, so there is no order.
Below the critical temperature the bottom bulges up and the curve becomes a double well, with two minima at $\pm m_0$.
The system falls into one of the two wells, and this choice is the spontaneous breaking of the symmetry.
For $f(m) = a m^2 + m^4$, the minima are at $m = 0$ when $a \ge 0$ and at $m = \pm \sqrt{-a/2}$ when $a < 0$.
