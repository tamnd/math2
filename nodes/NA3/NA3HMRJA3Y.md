## Summary

Quintessence is a model of dark energy.
In it, a scalar field that changes slowly in time drives the accelerated expansion of the universe.
Unlike the [[cosmological constant|WVKKDY9V61]], it has an energy density that can vary with time.
Its [[equation of state|6A915X05CK]] parameter $w$ can vary too.
The field has a potential energy $V(\phi)$.
When the potential energy is much larger than the kinetic energy, the pressure is negative and close to minus the energy density.
Observations of $w$ and of its time dependence are used to tell quintessence apart from a cosmological constant.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The expansion of the universe is speeding up, and something called dark energy must cause it.
Quintessence proposes that dark energy is a field that fills all of space, like a ball rolling very slowly down a gentle hill.
While the ball rolls slowly, almost all its energy is stored energy of height, and such energy pushes space apart.
For example, with a very flat potential the field barely moves, and it then behaves almost like a cosmological constant with $w \approx -1$.
If the ball speeds up, the push weakens, so quintessence predicts a dark energy that can change over cosmic time.
