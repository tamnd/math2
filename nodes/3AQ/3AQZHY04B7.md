## Summary

**Graph cut optimization** is a combinatorial optimization method applicable to a family of [[functions|0EPAT51DXQ]] of discrete variables, named after the concept of cut in the theory of flow networks. Thanks to the max-flow min-cut theorem, determining the minimum cut over a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] representing a flow network is equivalent to computing the maximum flow over the network.
