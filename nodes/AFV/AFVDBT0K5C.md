## Summary

Dynamo theory studies how the motion of an electrically conducting fluid can create and maintain a magnetic field against its decay by electrical resistance.
It explains the magnetic fields of the Earth, the Sun, other planets and stars, and galaxies.
Its basic mathematical problem is the kinematic dynamo problem: for which velocity fields does the induction equation of [[magnetohydrodynamics|T8JWCBK9M6]] have solutions that do not decay?
Antidynamo theorems, such as [[Cowling's theorem|X9PZ8RJH90]], show that flows or fields with too much symmetry cannot work.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Without a source, the Earth's magnetic field would fade away in some tens of thousands of years because of the resistance of the core.
Yet rocks show that the field has existed for billions of years.
The answer is that the liquid iron of the outer core moves, and moving conductors in a magnetic field generate electric currents.
These currents can rebuild the field that produced them, like a self-exciting electric generator.
Dynamo theory asks which motions can do this.
