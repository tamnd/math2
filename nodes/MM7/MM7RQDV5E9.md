## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|S60Z63NM98]].
It covers representations of the quantized enveloping algebras of Drinfeld and Jimbo at generic q and at roots of unity, the canonical bases of Lusztig and the crystal bases of Kashiwara, and representations of quantum affine algebras and Yangians.
