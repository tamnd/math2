## Summary

The Tits–Kantor–Koecher construction builds a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] from a [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]], or more generally from a [[Jordan pair|CDN2B4S8YW]].
The Lie algebra is graded in degrees $-1$, $0$ and $1$, with two copies of the Jordan structure in degrees $\pm 1$.
Conversely, every Lie algebra with such a grading gives a Jordan pair, so the construction translates Jordan theory into Lie theory.
It is named after Jacques Tits, Issai Kantor and Max Koecher.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Lie algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ has a basis $e$, $h$, $f$ in which $h$ has degree $0$, $e$ has degree $1$ and $f$ has degree $-1$.
The construction generalizes this picture: put a Jordan algebra in degree $1$, a second copy in degree $-1$, and a space of operators in degree $0$.
Brackets between degrees $1$ and $-1$ are given by the Jordan triple product.
Starting from the one-dimensional Jordan algebra one gets $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ back, and starting from the Albert algebra one gets the exceptional Lie algebra $E_7$.
