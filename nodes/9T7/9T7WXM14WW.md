## Statement

The **Lambek–Moser theorem** is a mathematical description of the partitions of [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]] into two [[complementary sets|0RKF1ZT5JJ]]. For instance, it applies to the partition of numbers into even and odd, or into [[prime|01826KMG2H]] and non-prime (one and the [[composite numbers|008BH74D7R]]). There are two parts to the Lambek–Moser theorem. One part states any two [[non-decreasing|0A7KV2WB7A]] integer functions that are inverse, in a certain sense, can be used to split the natural numbers into two complementary subsets, and the other part states every complementary partition can be constructed in this way. When a formula is known for the $n$th natural number in a set, the Lambek–Moser theorem can be used to obtain a formula for the $n$th number not in the set.
