## Summary

A σ-algebra on a set is a family of subsets that contains the whole set and is closed under complements and countable unions.
In probability theory, the σ-algebra is the family of events, the sets to which we give a probability. The node [[sigma-algebra|04WSW8T3SY]] gives the precise definition.
