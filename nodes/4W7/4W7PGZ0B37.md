## Summary

The figure of the Earth is the size and shape of the Earth, described by a mathematical model.
The usual models form a hierarchy: a [[sphere|P9CM4GQ2T0]], an oblate [[reference ellipsoid|R9XX7TNJCH]], and the [[geoid|999BYZY0ZG]].
Each model is more accurate and more complicated than the previous one.
The choice of model fixes how coordinates, distances and heights are computed.

## Explanations

### L1 human

At first sight the Earth is a ball with radius about $6371$ km.
A closer look shows that it bulges at the equator, because it spins, so a slightly squashed ball fits better.
Its flattening is small: the polar radius is shorter than the equatorial radius by about one part in $298$.
For heights one uses a bumpy surface, the geoid, that follows mean sea level.
Which model is "the shape of the Earth" depends on the precision one needs.
