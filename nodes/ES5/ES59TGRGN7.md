## Summary

Jerk is the rate of change of [[acceleration|Q8D2JWY8G6]] with respect to time, the third [[derivative|04TKTF8KDK]] of position.
It does not enter Newton's laws directly, but it measures how abruptly forces change.
Engineers limit jerk in lifts, trains, roads and robot arms to make motion smooth and to reduce wear and vibration.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a lift starts, the push on your feet grows from nothing to its full value.
If it grows suddenly, you feel a jolt; if it grows gently, the ride feels smooth.
The rate at which the push, and so the acceleration, changes is the jerk.
A good driver brakes so that the car does not jerk forward at the moment it stops.
