## Summary

The parameterized post-Newtonian (PPN) formalism is a framework for a broad class of metric theories of gravity.
It writes the first [[post-Newtonian|7HC8RM1EH9]] metric of each of them in one common form with ten numerical parameters.
Each theory corresponds to particular values of the parameters; general relativity has $\gamma = \beta = 1$ and all others zero.
Solar-system experiments measure these parameters, so one experiment constrains many theories at once.
It is the standard language for weak-field tests of general relativity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Many theories of gravity have been proposed, and they all agree with Newton when gravity is weak and motion is slow.
They differ in the small corrections beyond Newton.
The PPN formalism writes these corrections in a single template with ten adjustable numbers.
For example, the number $\gamma$ says how much space is curved by a unit of mass, and it controls how much light bends near the Sun.
Measurements with radio signals from the Cassini spacecraft found $\gamma$ equal to $1$ within a few parts in $10^5$, as general relativity predicts.
