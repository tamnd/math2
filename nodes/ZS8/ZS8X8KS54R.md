## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers ages, amalgamation and Fraïssé limits, the countable ultrahomogeneous structures that they give, their classification, and their automorphism groups as topological groups.
Every class of finite structures with the hereditary, joint embedding and amalgamation properties has a unique countable ultrahomogeneous limit, by [[Fraïssé's theorem|V5CKNFP3HC]].
Examples include the rationals, the [[Rado graph|RMXR0FEF54]] and the [[Henson graphs|TZT25G2YB3]].
Variations of the method lead to the [[Hrushovski construction|933BFNKPXF]] and to the link between Ramsey theory and topological dynamics.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Age of a structure|5FKW715RGJ]]: the class of finitely generated structures that embed into a given one.
2. [[Amalgamation property|XSCSX39GEZ]]: two extensions of a common piece fit into a third.
3. [[Joint embedding property|59FQ5Q2B8R]]: any two pieces fit into a third.
4. [[Ultrahomogeneous structure|H8VM37SSAX]]: every finite partial isomorphism extends to an automorphism.
5. [[Fraïssé's theorem|V5CKNFP3HC]]: ages of countable ultrahomogeneous structures are exactly the classes with these properties.
6. [[Fraïssé limit|HJVRBDQSGG]]: the unique countable ultrahomogeneous structure with a given age.
7. [[Rado graph|RMXR0FEF54]]: the limit of all finite graphs.
8. [[Henson graphs|TZT25G2YB3]]: the limits of the finite graphs omitting a complete subgraph.
9. [[Lachlan-Woodrow theorem|F7PA1RVBCG]]: the classification of countable homogeneous graphs.
10. [[Homogeneous structures beyond graphs|CWKWP7G6HB]]: posets, tournaments, digraphs and permutations.
11. [[Hrushovski's extension property|TA9KGWV9NM]]: partial isomorphisms of a finite graph extend inside a larger finite graph.
12. [[Small index property|RZAF6NVEXC]]: subgroups of countable index in the automorphism group are open.
13. [[Kechris-Pestov-Todorcevic correspondence|96Q4P7PZVY]]: extreme amenability matches the Ramsey property.
14. [[Ramsey class|8XS8FCTF8T]]: classes in which colorings of copies have monochromatic copies.
15. [[Generic structures and Hrushovski amalgamation|933BFNKPXF]]: amalgamation over self-sufficient sets with a predimension.

- [[Back-and-forth methods and Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé games|N64R1MGDBN]]: the back-and-forth method used for uniqueness of limits.
- [[Countable models and categoricity|6XEXRME4C1]]: $\aleph_0$-categoricity and the Ryll-Nardzewski theorem.
- [[Basic notions of model theory|AY09KYSW81]]: structures, substructures, embeddings and automorphisms.
