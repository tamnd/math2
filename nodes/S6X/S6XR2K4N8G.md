## Summary

Thermal radiation is the [[electromagnetic radiation|AC6R5A3M1C]] that matter emits because of its temperature.
It is the only mode of heat transfer that needs no medium, which is how the Sun heats the Earth across empty space.
Its upper limit at each wavelength is the radiation of a [[black body|A2RMYX8A65]] at the same temperature, given by [[Planck's law|QKQ0V5S04R]].
The emitted power grows like the fourth power of the absolute temperature, so radiation dominates heat transfer at high temperatures.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Hold your hand near a hot stove plate without touching it, and you feel warmth: that is thermal radiation.
Every object emits it, even an ice cube, but cool objects emit mostly invisible infrared light.
As an object gets hotter it emits more, and the colour shifts from infrared to red, then orange, then white.
A night-vision camera sees people because a body at about $37$ °C glows brightly in the infrared.
