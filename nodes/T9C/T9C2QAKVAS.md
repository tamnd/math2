## Summary

Differential geometry is the study of curves, surfaces and manifolds with the methods of calculus.
Its central object is a Riemannian manifold, a smooth manifold with an inner product on each tangent space that varies smoothly from point to point.
Curvature is its main invariant, and the theory gives the mathematical language of general relativity and of gauge theory in physics.
Gauss proved in 1827 that the curvature of a surface depends only on its intrinsic metric, and Riemann extended these ideas to spaces of any dimension in his lecture of 1854.
This topic collects the differential geometry pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Symplectic and Poisson geometry, complex geometry, manifolds and differential topology and global analysis have their own topics.
