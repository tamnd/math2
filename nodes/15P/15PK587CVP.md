## Summary

Manifolds and differential topology is the study of manifolds as spaces and of the smooth maps between them.
A manifold of dimension n is a Hausdorff space with a countable base in which each point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to n-dimensional Euclidean space.
Manifolds are the spaces of geometry and physics, and differential topology gives the tools to classify them, such as transversality, cobordism and surgery.
Whitney proved in 1936 that every smooth manifold embeds in a Euclidean space, and Milnor found exotic smooth structures on the 7-sphere in 1956.
This topic collects the manifolds and differential topology pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Differential geometry, algebraic topology, low-dimensional topology and knot theory and global analysis have their own topics.
