## Summary

The electric displacement field $D$ is the [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] $D = \varepsilon_0 E + P$, built from the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] $E$ and the [[polarization density|ASQAH710WN]] $P$.
Its divergence is the density of free charge only, with the bound charge of the material removed.
This makes Gauss's law in matter depend only on the charges one controls.
In a linear medium $D$ is proportional to $E$, and the factor is the permittivity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a charged object sits inside an insulating material, the material reacts and partly hides the charge.
The electric field $E$ sees both the real charge and this reaction.
The field $D$ is a bookkeeping device that sees only the free charge that was placed there.
Its flux through a closed surface counts just the free charge inside, whatever the material does.
