## Summary

In the theory of lattices, the **dual lattice** is a construction analogous to that of a [[dual vector space|07JBSZE0V4]]. In certain respects, the geometry of the dual lattice of a lattice $L$ is the reciprocal of the geometry of $L$, a perspective which underlies many of its uses.
