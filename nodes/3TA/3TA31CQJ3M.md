## Summary

In [[combinatorial|0JKW7W7XFR]] mathematics, a **De Bruijn torus**, named after Dutch mathematician Nicolaas Govert de Bruijn, is an [[array|0VD9ZSWWJG]] of symbols from an alphabet (often just 0 and 1) that contains every possible [[matrix|0VD9ZSWWJG]] of given dimensions $m \times n$ exactly once. It is a [[torus|03NM7DZXXC]] because the edges are considered wraparound for the purpose of finding matrices. Its name comes from the De Bruijn sequence, which can be considered a special case where $n = 1$ (one dimension).
