## Summary

A walk in a graph is a sequence of vertices in which consecutive vertices are adjacent. Walks are the basic notion of movement in a graph, and trails, paths, circuits and cycles are special walks. The node [[walk|0VJZM0F9YM]] gives the precise definition.
