## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|G7N6BDTEZ2]].
It covers algebras whose product need not be associative: the definitions, the associator and the nucleus, identities and varieties, power-associative, flexible and anticommutative algebras, and nonassociative division algebras.
