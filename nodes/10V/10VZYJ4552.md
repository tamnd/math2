## Summary

A quantum channel is a completely positive, trace-preserving linear map between spaces of operators.
It describes any physical transformation of quantum states that happens with certainty.
Examples are transmission through a noisy fibre and the evolution of an [[open quantum system|6D9YCNZ7X9]] over a fixed time.
Channels are the [[quantum operations|F1K9J81GCT]] that preserve the trace, so they map density operators to density operators.
Quantum information theory studies their capacities for sending classical and quantum information.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A quantum channel is a box that takes a quantum state in and gives a quantum state out.
Inside, the state may interact with an environment that is then thrown away, so the channel can add noise.
For example, the depolarizing channel on a qubit keeps the state with probability $1 - p$ and replaces it by the completely mixed state $\tfrac12 I$ with probability $p$.
Every channel can be built from a unitary interaction with an extra system followed by forgetting that system.
