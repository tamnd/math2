## Summary

An abelian Lie algebra is a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] in which every bracket is zero.
Any vector space becomes an abelian Lie algebra with the zero bracket.
Two abelian Lie algebras are isomorphic exactly when they have the same dimension.
Abelian Lie algebras are the Lie algebras of abelian connected Lie groups, such as tori and $\R^n$.
They are the simplest solvable and nilpotent Lie algebras.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In an abelian Lie algebra nothing fails to commute, so all brackets vanish.
The structure is then just that of a vector space.
For example, the diagonal $n \times n$ matrices commute with each other, so with $[A, B] = AB - BA$ they form an abelian Lie algebra.
The name comes from abelian groups, whose operation is commutative.
