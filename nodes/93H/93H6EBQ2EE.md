## Summary

Given a blow-up $\pi: \widetilde{X} \to X$ along a closed subscheme *Z* and a morphism $f: Y \to X$, the strict transform of *Y* (also called proper transform) is the blow-up $\widetilde{Y} \to Y$ of *Y* along the closed subscheme $f^{-1} Z$. If *f* is a closed immersion, then the induced map $\widetilde{Y} \hookrightarrow \widetilde{X}$ is also a closed immersion.
