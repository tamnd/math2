## Summary

An exact equation is a first-order equation $M\, dx + N\, dy = 0$ whose left side is the total differential of a function $\psi(x, y)$.
Its solutions lie on the level curves $\psi(x, y) = C$. See the [[definition|0EC7AGJ0PX]].
