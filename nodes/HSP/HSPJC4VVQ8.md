## Summary

The Silver–Müller radiation condition is a condition at infinity on time-harmonic electromagnetic fields in the exterior of a bounded set.
It says that, far away, the fields look like outgoing spherical waves.
So energy flows out to infinity and does not come in from it.
It is the electromagnetic analog of the Sommerfeld radiation condition for the [[Helmholtz equation|MNXKTXJ35G]].
It makes exterior radiation and scattering problems for Maxwell's equations uniquely solvable.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When an antenna or an obstacle sends out waves, the waves far away should move away from it, not toward it.
The equations alone do not know this: incoming waves solve them as well.
The radiation condition is the extra rule that keeps only the outgoing waves.
For light this means that far away the electric field, the magnetic field and the outward direction form a right-handed triple, as in a wave that travels outward.
