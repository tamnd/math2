## Summary

An **Erdős–Diophantine graph** is an object in the mathematical subject of Diophantine equations consisting of a set of [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] points at integer distances in the plane that cannot be extended by any additional points. Equivalently, in geometric graph theory, it can be described as a [[complete graph|03WKB2H474]] with vertices located on the integer square grid $\mathbb{Z}^2$ such that all mutual [[distances|0QV9VNNB4Z]] between the vertices are integers, while all other grid points have a non-integer distance to at least one vertex.
