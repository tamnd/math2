## Summary

This topic is a part of [[enumerative combinatorics|FRSTEPZPCY]].
It covers the methods that find and prove identities for sums of products of binomial coefficients, such as bijections, involutions, generating functions, the algorithms of Gosper, Zeilberger and Petkovšek, WZ pairs and constant term identities.
