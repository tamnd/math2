## Summary

Reptation is the snake-like motion of a long polymer chain in a dense entangled melt or gel.
The surrounding chains confine each chain to a tube along its own contour, and the chain can only move by sliding along this tube.
The model is due to de Gennes.
It predicts that the time a chain needs to leave its tube grows like the cube of its length.
It also predicts that the diffusion coefficient of the chain falls like the inverse square of its length.
It is the basis of the tube theory of the flow of polymer melts.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a bowl of very long cooked spaghetti.
One strand cannot move sideways, because the other strands are in the way.
It can only slither along its own length, like a snake in a narrow tunnel.
To escape its old tunnel, the strand has to crawl its whole length, which takes a long time for a long strand.
Doubling the length of the strands makes this escape about eight times slower.
