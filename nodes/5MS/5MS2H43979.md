## Summary

This topic is a part of [[graph theory|BCQTYD70W4]].
It covers graphs in which the chromatic number equals the clique number on every induced subgraph, the perfect graph theorems, the intersection graph classes that are perfect or close to perfect, and the Lovász theta function.
