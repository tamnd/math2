## Summary

In algebraic number theory, the **genus field** $\Gamma(K)$ of an algebraic number field $K$ is the maximal abelian extension of $K$ which is obtained by composing an absolutely abelian field with $K$ and which is unramified at all finite primes of $K$. The **genus number** of $K$ is the degree $[\Gamma(K):K]$ and the **genus group** is the [[Galois group|0XTY86BZKY]] of $\Gamma(K)$ over $K$.
