## Summary

A spin glass is a magnetic system whose interactions are random in sign and strength.
No arrangement of the spins satisfies all of these interactions at once.
In a mathematical model, the couplings between spins are random variables fixed once and for all.
Each sample of the couplings gives its own Gibbs measure.
Spin glasses show frustration, many competing low-energy states and a slow dynamics.
Their theory, built on the [[replica trick|4Y5V47G9NH]] and the [[Parisi formula|32S4Y36QNK]], has spread to optimization, coding and neural networks.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a set of small magnets where some pairs want to point the same way and other pairs want to point opposite ways, chosen at random.
Take three magnets in a triangle where every pair wants to point opposite ways.
With only two directions, up and down, two of the three must agree, so one pair is always unhappy.
This conflict is called frustration.
In a spin glass it happens everywhere, and the system freezes into one of many messy states instead of a neat ordered one.
