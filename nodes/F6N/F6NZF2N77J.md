## Summary

A photon sphere is a sphere around a compact mass on which light can travel in circular orbits.
For the [[Schwarzschild metric|6Y50T68WKY]] of mass $M$ it is the sphere $r = 3GM/c^2$, one and a half times the [[Schwarzschild radius|G48AGJAZNG]].
The circular light orbits there are unstable, and they shape the dark shadow that a [[black hole|YHJ0PDNY8T]] casts on a bright background.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Light passing a black hole is bent, and the closer it passes, the more it bends.
At one special distance the bending is just enough to make the light go round in a circle.
For a non-rotating black hole this happens at three times its mass in geometric units, that is at $r = 3M$.
The circle is like a ball balanced on a hilltop: a tiny push sends the light either into the hole or out to infinity.
