## Example

The *Furuta pendulum*, or rotational inverted pendulum, has two rigid links.
The first link, the arm, turns in a horizontal plane about a vertical axis through one of its ends.
A motor at this axis applies a torque $\tau(t)$ to the arm.
The second link, the pendulum, is hinged at the free end of the arm and swings freely in the vertical plane perpendicular to the arm.
The [[generalized coordinates|BCF1EX9W2M]] are the arm angle $\varphi$ and the pendulum angle $\theta$, measured from the upward vertical.
So the [[configuration space|MGYKGP7PW3]] is the torus $S^1 \times S^1$, and there are two degrees of freedom.

Check that it is underactuated: the input $\tau$ is a single number, and it acts only on the equation for $\varphi$.
The equations of motion have the form $M(q) \ddot q + c(q, \dot q) = B \tau$ with $q = (\varphi, \theta)$, an invertible inertia matrix $M(q)$ and $B = (1, 0)^T$.
So the input enters the accelerations through $M(q)^{-1} B$, which has rank $1 < 2$.
Hence the Furuta pendulum is an [[underactuated system|RJSM2ZNV61]].

## Remarks

The configurations with $\theta = 0$ and $\dot\varphi = \dot\theta = 0$, rod upright, are equilibria for $\tau = 0$, and they are unstable.
The configurations with $\theta = \pi$ and $\dot\varphi = \dot\theta = 0$, rod hanging down, are also equilibria for $\tau = 0$, and after a small push the rod only swings near the hanging position.
Two standard control tasks are swing-up, which brings the rod from the hanging position to near the top, and balancing near the top.
It plays the same role as the [[inverted pendulum|D0J7A9CG77]] on a cart, but the arm turns instead of moving on a track, so it needs no long rail.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a motor that turns a horizontal arm, like the hand of a clock lying flat.
A free rod hangs from the tip of the arm and can swing up and down.
By turning the arm back and forth, the motor can swing the rod up and then keep it balanced pointing up.
The motor only acts on the arm, never directly on the rod, which is what makes the task hard and interesting.
It is a compact laboratory device for testing nonlinear control methods.
