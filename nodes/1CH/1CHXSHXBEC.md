## Summary

In the study of the arithmetic of elliptic curves, the ***j*-line** over a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] *R* is the coarse moduli scheme attached to the moduli problem sending a ring $R$ to the set of isomorphism classes of elliptic curves over $R$. Since elliptic curves over the complex numbers are isomorphic (over an algebraic closure) if and only if their $j$-invariants agree, the affine space $\mathbb{A}^1_j$ parameterizing j-invariants of elliptic curves yields a coarse moduli space. However, this fails to be a fine moduli space due to the presence of elliptic curves with automorphisms, necessitating the construction of the Moduli stack of elliptic curves.
