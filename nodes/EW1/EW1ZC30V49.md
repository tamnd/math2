## Definition

Let $R$ be a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]], let $GL(R) = \bigcup_k GL_k(R)$, and let $n \ge 1$.
Write $K_n(R) = \pi_n(BGL(R)^+)$, using the [[plus construction|XFGZFM8PC6]].
The *Dennis trace map* $K_n(R) \to HH_n(R)$ is the composite of four maps:

1. the Hurewicz map $\pi_n(BGL(R)^+) \to H_n(BGL(R)^+; \Z) = H_n(GL(R); \Z)$;
2. the map $H_n(G; \Z) \to HH_n(\Z[G])$ for $G = GL(R)$, induced on chains by $(g_1, \ldots, g_n) \mapsto (g_1 \cdots g_n)^{-1} \otimes g_1 \otimes \cdots \otimes g_n$;
3. the map $HH_n(\Z[GL(R)]) \to HH_n(M(R))$ induced by the ring map $\Z[GL(R)] \to M(R) = \bigcup_k M_k(R)$;
4. the trace isomorphism $HH_n(M(R)) \cong HH_n(R)$ of Morita invariance.

Here $H_n(G; \Z)$ is [[group homology|CFAG3VRXEA]] and $HH_n$ is [[Hochschild homology|S5H153HFWC]] over $\Z$.

## Remarks

Example: in degree $1$, a unit $u$ of $R$ gives $u \in GL_1(R)$, and its image in $HH_1(R)$ is the class of $u^{-1} \otimes u$.
For $R$ commutative, $HH_1(R) \cong \Omega^1_{R/\Z}$ by $a \otimes b \mapsto a \, db$, so the Dennis trace sends $u$ to $u^{-1} du$, a logarithmic [[Kähler differential|7HCFARHG7D]].
Non-example of injectivity: for $R = \Q$, one has $\Omega^1_{\Q/\Z} = 0$, so the Dennis trace on $K_1(\Q) = \Q^\times$ is zero although $K_1(\Q)$ is not.
The Dennis trace factors through negative cyclic homology $HC^-_n(R)$, and its topological refinement is the map $K(R) \to THH(R)$.
