## Summary

The Polyakov action is the [[action|T0WAGV4KP4]] of a relativistic string in [[string theory|GM58M1HFY0]].
It is a functional of two fields on the two-dimensional worldsheet of the string.
They are the embedding of the worldsheet into spacetime and an independent metric on the worldsheet.
It is classically equivalent to the [[Nambu–Goto action|W4GZMZ796R]].
But it is quadratic in the embedding fields and has no square root of the induced area element.
This makes it the usual starting point for the quantization of strings, where it becomes a two-dimensional field theory with local Weyl symmetry.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A string moving through spacetime sweeps out a surface, its worldsheet, just as a point particle sweeps out a curve.
The Nambu–Goto action measures the area of that surface, which involves an awkward square root.
Polyakov's form adds an auxiliary metric on the surface and writes an energy-like integral of the squared derivatives of the embedding instead.
When one chooses the auxiliary metric to be flat, the equations of motion for the embedding become free wave equations in two variables.
So each spacetime coordinate of the string vibrates like an ordinary violin string.
