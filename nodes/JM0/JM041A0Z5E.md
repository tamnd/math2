## Definition

Let a [[lattice model|PAEME5EQRM]] on the square lattice have spins in a finite set $S$ and nearest-neighbour Boltzmann weights.
Let $\Lambda$ be the square of side $2m + 1$ centred at the origin, with fixed spins on its outer boundary.
The horizontal and vertical lines through the origin cut $\Lambda$ into four quadrants.
Take the lower right quadrant.
Let $\sigma = (\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_m)$ be the spins on the half-row from the origin to the right edge.
Let $\sigma' = (\sigma'_0, \dots, \sigma'_m)$ be the spins on the half-column from the origin to the bottom edge.
The *corner transfer matrix* $A$ is the matrix indexed by $S^{m+1} \times S^{m+1}$ defined as follows.
Its entry $A_{\sigma, \sigma'}$ is $0$ if $\sigma_0 \ne \sigma'_0$.
Otherwise it is the sum, over all spins strictly inside the quadrant, of the product of the Boltzmann weights of the quadrant.
The corner transfer matrices $B$, $C$, $D$ of the other three quadrants are defined in the same way, going around the origin.

## Remarks

The partition function of $\Lambda$ is $Z = \tr(ABCD)$, with the weights on the two cutting lines shared so that each is counted once.
For a function $g$ of the centre spin, the expectation is $\langle g(\sigma_0) \rangle = \tr(G \, ABCD) / \tr(ABCD)$, where $G$ is the diagonal matrix with entries $g(\sigma_0)$.
For an isotropic model with a reflection symmetry, all four matrices can be taken equal, and $Z = \tr(A^4)$.
For the Ising model, $S = \set{-1, +1}$ and each corner transfer matrix has size $2^{m+1} \times 2^{m+1}$, with nonnegative entries.
Baxter studied models whose weights satisfy the star–triangle relation.
For them the corner transfer matrices become simple as $m \to \infty$.
Suitably normalized, their eigenvalues are integer powers of one number that depends on the spectral parameter.
Non-example: the row [[transfer matrix|EXX6S3K5Z5]] of the same model is not a corner transfer matrix.
It is indexed by the spins of whole rows and builds the region row by row, not quadrant by quadrant.
