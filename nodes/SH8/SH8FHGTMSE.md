## Summary

An isentropic flow is a flow of a fluid in which the entropy of each fluid particle stays constant.
This holds when there is no heat exchange and no dissipation.
It is the standard model of [[compressible flow|FJC2GMVARF]] away from shock waves and boundary layers.
For an ideal gas it gives algebraic relations between pressure, density, temperature and [[Mach number|GFHJ858AN1]].
These reduce many problems, such as flow in nozzles, to one-dimensional calculations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When air flows quickly through a nozzle, there is no time for heat to leak in or out, and friction matters only in a thin layer near the walls.
In the rest of the flow the air is squeezed and released in a reversible way, like an ideal spring.
Then pressure, density and temperature move together by fixed rules.
For example, air that speeds up from rest to the speed of sound cools from $300$ to $250$ kelvin.
