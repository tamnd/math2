## Statement

**Frucht's theorem** is a result in algebraic graph theory, conjectured by Dénes Kőnig in 1936 and proved by Robert Frucht in 1939. It states that every finite group is the [[group of symmetries|0CWXCWP0Y0]] of a finite undirected graph. More strongly, for any finite group $G$, there exist infinitely many [[non-isomorphic|00CT0PTH74]] simple connected graphs such that the [[automorphism group|0CWXCWP0Y0]] of each of them is isomorphic to $G$.
