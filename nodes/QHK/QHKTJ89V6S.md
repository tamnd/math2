## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers internal and external direct sums, products of spaces, complements, projections, quotient spaces, and the isomorphism theorems for vector spaces.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Internal direct sum of subspaces|0XYYT5KNTX]]: unique decomposition $v = u_1 + \cdots + u_k$, and the criteria $U \cap W = \{0\}$ and $\dim U + \dim W = \dim V$.
2. [[External direct sum|0S7W6EG32V]]: the direct sum of spaces that are not inside a common space, and its universal property.
3. [[Direct product of vector spaces|0EX2HPXPJ6]]: the product of a family, its universal property, and how it differs from the direct sum for infinite families.
4. [[Complementary subspace|0M83V17KHX]]: subspaces $W$ with $V = U \oplus W$, and why every complement is isomorphic to $V / U$.
5. [[Existence of a complementary subspace|0P9T8WHNMH]]: every subspace has a complement, by extending a basis or by Zorn's lemma.
6. [[Projection|033DA1WE8Y]]: idempotent linear maps, and their correspondence with direct sum decompositions.
7. [[Quotient space|0BR3FY5Q8M]]: cosets $v + U$, the quotient map, and why $V / U$ is a vector space.
8. [[Dimension of a quotient space|0SXDQ6TN00]]: $\dim V / U = \dim V - \dim U$.
9. [[Universal property of the quotient space|0ZERJX6PH6]]: a linear map that vanishes on $U$ factors uniquely through $V / U$.
10. [[First isomorphism theorem for vector spaces|0MTBGVZ4XJ]]: $V / \ker T \cong \operatorname{im} T$.
11. [[Second isomorphism theorem for vector spaces|0APN65D6Y6]]: $(U + W) / W \cong U / (U \cap W)$, and the Grassmann formula.
12. [[Third isomorphism theorem for vector spaces|07RB3EMBWW]]: $(V / U) / (W / U) \cong V / W$, and the correspondence of subspaces.
13. [[Codimension of a subspace|JJCGJ1EGCQ]]: $\dim V / U$, which makes sense in infinite dimension, and its behaviour under intersection.
14. [[Exact sequence of vector spaces|946YAZNYKX]]: short exact sequences, their splitting, and the alternating sum of dimensions.

### Prerequisites

- [[Vector spaces, span, independence, bases and dimension|MVH97H8B2J]]: subspaces, sums and intersections of subspaces, bases and dimension.
- [[Linear maps|9080WQTZ3M]]: kernel, image, isomorphisms and the rank-nullity theorem.
