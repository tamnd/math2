## Summary

An exponential generating function encodes a sequence $(a_n)$ as the series $\sum a_n x^n / n!$. It is the natural tool to count labelled structures, because products give binomial convolutions. The node [[exponential generating function|0KE6NBTVY8]] gives the precise definition.
