## Summary

In number theory, a **noncototient** is a positive integer $n$ that cannot be expressed as the difference between a positive integer $m$ and the number of coprime integers below it. That is, $m - \varphi(m) = n$, where $\varphi$ stands for [[Euler's totient function|0DAMHCT17R]], has no solution for $m$. The *cototient* of $n$ is defined as $n - \varphi(n)$, so a **noncototient** is a number that is never a cototient.
