## Summary

A **composite field** or **compositum of fields** is an object of study in field theory. Let *K* be a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]], and let $E_1$, $E_2$ be subfields of *K*. Then the (internal) composite of $E_1$ and $E_2$ is the field defined as the intersection of all subfields of *K* containing both $E_1$ and $E_2$. The composite is commonly denoted $E_1E_2$.
