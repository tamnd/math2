## Summary

The emissivity of a surface compares the [[thermal radiation|S6XR2K4N8G]] it emits with that of a [[black body|A2RMYX8A65]].
It is the ratio of the two, both taken at the same temperature.
It is a number between $0$ and $1$, and it can depend on wavelength, direction and temperature.
It is the main surface property in radiative heat transfer calculations.
By [[Kirchhoff's law|KRF21D5QVT]], at each wavelength and direction it equals the absorptivity of the surface.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A black body is the best possible emitter of heat radiation, and emissivity says how close a real surface comes to it.
Matte black paint has emissivity near $0.95$, while polished aluminium has about $0.05$.
So at the same temperature, the painted surface radiates about twenty times as much heat as the polished one.
This is why radiators are painted and why emergency blankets are shiny.
