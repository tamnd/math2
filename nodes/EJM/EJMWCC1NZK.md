## Statement

In number theory, **Glaisher's theorem** is an identity useful to the study of [[integer partitions|0JV30XN73Z]]. [[Proved|0B94TKFQJX]] in 1883 by James Whitbread Lee Glaisher, it states that the number of partitions of an [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] $n$ into parts not divisible by $d$ is equal to the number of partitions in which no part is repeated $d$ or more times. This generalizes a result established in 1748 by Leonhard Euler for the case $d=2$.
