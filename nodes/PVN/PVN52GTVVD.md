## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|S60Z63NM98]].
It covers the deformations and centralizer algebras that control the representations of finite and p-adic reductive groups and of tensor spaces: Iwahori-Hecke algebras, their affine and double affine versions, and diagram algebras with a cellular structure.
