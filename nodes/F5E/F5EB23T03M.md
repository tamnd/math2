## Summary

LOCC, short for local operations and classical communication, is the class of [[quantum channels|10VZYJ4552]] that distant parties can carry out on a shared system.
Each party acts only on its own subsystem, and the parties may exchange the outcomes of their measurements over a classical channel.
LOCC cannot create [[entanglement|CHNXT8CCK7]] from [[separable states|7F386TSFRW]].
For this reason it is the natural class of free operations in the resource theory of entanglement.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture two labs far apart, each with part of a quantum system and a telephone.
Each lab may do anything to its own part, including measurements, and may phone the other lab with the results.
What they cannot do is send quantum systems to each other.
The operations they can perform together are called LOCC.
For example, Alice can measure her qubit, call Bob with the result, and Bob can then flip his qubit if the result was $1$.
