## Summary

The halo mass function gives the number density of dark matter halos per unit mass, as a function of mass and redshift.
It is a central prediction of a cosmological model, because it depends on the [[matter power spectrum|FWRHZ27J6Q]] and on the growth of structure.
Counts of galaxy clusters compare with its high-mass tail and so constrain the amount of matter and the amplitude of fluctuations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine sorting all the dark matter clumps of a large box of universe by weight and drawing a histogram.
The halo mass function is that histogram, divided by the volume of the box.
Small halos are very common and huge halos, the hosts of galaxy clusters, are rare.
Above some mass the number drops very fast, and that mass grows as the universe ages.
