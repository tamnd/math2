## Summary

EXPTIME is the [[complexity class|ESPYW94XGR]] of decision problems that a deterministic [[Turing machine|758EYCN27B]] solves in time $2^{p(n)}$ for some polynomial $p$.
Here $n$ is the length of the input.
It contains [[P|GVT2NKN37K]], [[NP|QQRA9GBW51]] and [[PSPACE|T9NTRY93ZT]].
By the [[time hierarchy theorem|A6D1D67GHA]], P is strictly smaller than EXPTIME, so EXPTIME-complete problems provably have no polynomial-time algorithm.

## Explanations

### L1 human

EXPTIME collects the problems that can be solved by brute force over exponentially many cases.
Trying all $2^n$ assignments of $n$ Boolean variables is a typical EXPTIME computation.
Its known relations to other classes form the chain $\mathrm{P} \subseteq \mathrm{NP} \subseteq \mathrm{PSPACE} \subseteq \mathrm{EXPTIME}$.
At least one inclusion in this chain is strict, because P differs from EXPTIME, but no one knows which.
Deciding which player wins in a generalised chess position on an $n \times n$ board is a well-known EXPTIME-complete problem.
