## Summary

In number theory, **Berlekamp's root finding algorithm**, also called the **Berlekamp–Rabin algorithm**, is the probabilistic method of finding roots of polynomials over the [[field|0ZXZ82FTX2]] $\mathbb F_p$ with $p$ elements. The method was discovered by Elwyn Berlekamp in 1970 as an auxiliary to the algorithm for polynomial factorization over finite fields. The algorithm was later modified by Rabin for arbitrary finite fields in 1979. The method was also independently discovered before Berlekamp by other researchers.
