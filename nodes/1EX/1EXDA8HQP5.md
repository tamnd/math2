## Summary

Take two functions $g$ and $G$. The function $G$ is called an antiderivative of $g$ when the [[derivative function|0QKJ6NT0R9]] of $G$ is $g$, that is, $G' = g$.

The definition names no interval and no special class of functions. The relation $G' = g$ already requires $G$ to be differentiable where it is considered.
