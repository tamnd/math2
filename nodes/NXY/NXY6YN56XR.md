## Summary

A JB-algebra is a real [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]] with a complete norm that satisfies the same norm identities as the self-adjoint part of a C*-algebra.
It is the abstract counterpart of a [[Jordan operator algebra|QYES5210FJ]].
In the same way, a C*-algebra is the abstract counterpart of an algebra of operators.
Every Jordan operator algebra is a JB-algebra, but the [[Albert algebra|1MSMVDRAVD]] is a JB-algebra that is not of this form.
JB-algebras were introduced by Alfsen, Shultz and Størmer.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The self-adjoint operators on a Hilbert space have a norm with special rules, such as $\norm{a^2} = \norm{a}^2$.
A JB-algebra keeps only the Jordan product and these norm rules, and forgets the Hilbert space.
The rules capture positivity: adding a square can only make a square larger in norm.
By a theorem of Alfsen, Shultz and Størmer, every JB-algebra embeds in a sum of two parts.
One part is a Jordan operator algebra, and the other consists of continuous functions with values in the Albert algebra.
So the Albert algebra is the only obstruction to a realization by operators.
