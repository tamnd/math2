## Summary

A [[system|GYNNJBMTCP]] is in thermodynamic equilibrium when its macroscopic state does not change in time.
In equilibrium there are no net flows of matter or energy inside the system or across its boundary.
It combines thermal, mechanical and chemical equilibrium.
Classical thermodynamics describes systems in equilibrium, where a few state variables are enough to fix the [[state|TD4C13V3J6]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Pour hot water into a cold glass and wait.
At first heat flows from the water to the glass, and the temperatures change.
After a while nothing changes any more: water and glass have the same temperature, and no flow goes on.
That final calm condition is thermodynamic equilibrium.
