## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Diophantine approximation and geometry of numbers|YB905F2AQG]].
It covers properties of approximation that hold for almost all numbers or for sets of given Hausdorff dimension, such as Khinchin's theorem, the Duffin-Schaeffer theorem, the Jarník-Besicovitch theorem, approximation on manifolds and Schmidt's game.
