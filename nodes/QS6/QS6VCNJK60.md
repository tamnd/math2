## Summary

A best response of a player is a strategy that gives the highest payoff against fixed strategies of the other players.
The map that sends the others' strategies to the set of best responses is the best-response correspondence.
Nash equilibria are exactly the fixed points of this correspondence.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If you knew exactly what everyone else will do, your best response is simply the choice that serves you best.
In rock-paper-scissors, the best response to an opponent who always plays rock is paper.
Against an opponent who mixes the three moves equally, every move is a best response, because each one wins on average nothing.
