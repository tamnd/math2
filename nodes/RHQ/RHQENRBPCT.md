## Statement

Let $n \ge 2$. Let $U \subset \C^n$ be a [[bounded|09QSZVVM26]] domain with smooth boundary, and assume that $U$ is strongly convex.
Let $f \colon \partial U \to \C$ be a smooth [[CR function|JFKYYK2CEB]].
Then there is a continuous function $F \colon \overline{U} \to \C$ that is [[holomorphic|507C34W0ZT]] in $U$ and agrees with $f$ on $\partial U$.

## Remarks

This is the special case of the Hartogs–Bochner extension theorem for strongly convex domains.
For such a domain the complement of the closure is connected, so the general theorem applies.
