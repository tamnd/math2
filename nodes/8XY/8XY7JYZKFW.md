## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers translations and functional interpretations between theories, conservation theorems and interpretability, and their use to extract information from proofs.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Double-negation translation|HGJVM49PA4]]: the Kolmogorov, Gödel-Gentzen and Kuroda translations, and why PA is consistent if HA is.
2. [[Friedman's A-translation|~10a427f4]]: replace $\bot$ by a formula, and get the $\Pi^0_2$ conservativity of PA over HA.
3. [[Gödel's system T|~62fef358]]: primitive recursive functionals of finite type, the recursor and strong normalization.
4. [[Dialectica interpretation|HZ17ES9PDY]]: Gödel's interpretation of HA in system T, its soundness proof and its extension to PA.
5. [[No-counterexample interpretation|~42908449]]: Kreisel's interpretation of PA by functionals that refute counterexample functions.
6. [[Bar recursion|~a7df5e71]]: Spector's extension of system T that interprets classical analysis.
7. [[Proof mining|~7cf2af55]]: bounds and rates of metastability extracted from classical proofs in analysis.
8. [[Conservation theorems|~fc5d3da7]]: when a stronger theory proves no new theorems of a given form, and three ways to prove it.
9. [[Parsons's theorem|~066e4d82]]: $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ is $\Pi^0_2$-conservative over primitive recursive arithmetic.
10. [[Interpretability between theories|~95e26594]]: relative interpretations, their link to consistency and to $\Pi_1$-conservativity.
11. [[Herbrand analysis|~22b3510a]]: primitive recursive witnesses read off from proofs of $\Sigma_1$ formulas in $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$.

### Prerequisites

- The topic [[structural proof theory|R10RMQCJQX]], for sequent calculus, natural deduction and cut elimination.
- The topic [[formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]], for PA, $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ and primitive recursive arithmetic.
- The area [[type theory and constructive mathematics|K6P3NSQEZ3]], for Heyting arithmetic and realizability.
