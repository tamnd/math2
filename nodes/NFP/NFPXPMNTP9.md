## Example

The *Einstein–de Sitter universe* is the spatially flat [[Friedmann–Lemaître–Robertson–Walker metric|ECJMS4VDTF]] filled with pressureless [[dust|H4FNPG6ZY1]] and with no cosmological constant.
In units with $c = 1$, its scale factor, Hubble parameter and density are
$$a(t) = \left( \frac{t}{t_0} \right)^{2/3}, \qquad H(t) = \frac{2}{3t}, \qquad \rho(t) = \frac{1}{6\pi G t^2}, \qquad t > 0.$$

Check with the [[Friedmann equations|PPHFS2P4G4]] for $k = 0$, $p = 0$, $\Lambda = 0$.
The first reads $H^2 = \tfrac{8\pi G}{3}\rho$: the left side is $\tfrac{4}{9t^2}$ and the right side is $\tfrac{8\pi G}{3} \cdot \tfrac{1}{6\pi G t^2} = \tfrac{4}{9t^2}$.
The second reads $\ddot a / a = -\tfrac{4\pi G}{3}\rho$: the left side is $-\tfrac{2}{9t^2}$ and the right side is $-\tfrac{4\pi G}{3} \cdot \tfrac{1}{6\pi G t^2} = -\tfrac{2}{9t^2}$.

## Remarks

The density equals the critical density $3H^2/(8\pi G)$ at all times, so the density parameter is $\Omega = 1$.
The age of the universe at the time when the Hubble parameter is $H_0$ is $t_0 = 2/(3H_0)$.
The deceleration parameter is $q = 1/2$, and the particle horizon has proper distance $3t$.
Einstein and de Sitter proposed the model together in 1932.
It is a good approximation to the matter-dominated era of the Lambda-CDM model.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Einstein–de Sitter universe is the simplest expanding universe filled with ordinary matter.
Space is flat, and the expansion is just fast enough to go on forever while slowing down all the time.
All distances grow like the time to the power $2/3$.
It was long the default model of cosmology, before the discovery that the expansion is now speeding up.
