## Summary

A K-orientation of a real vector bundle is a Thom class for the bundle in [[complex K-theory|9E4JE4902G]].
It is the K-theory analogue of an orientation, which is a Thom class in ordinary integral cohomology.
Complex vector bundles have canonical K-orientations, and so do real bundles with a spin$^c$ structure, by the [[Atiyah–Bott–Shapiro construction|N963WB4X84]].
A K-orientation gives a Thom isomorphism in K-theory and push-forward maps, which are the basis of index theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An orientation of a vector space is a choice of "positive direction", and an orientation of a bundle is a consistent choice on all fibres.
In ordinary cohomology, an orientation is the same thing as a class on the Thom space that looks like the standard class on each fibre.
A K-orientation asks for such a class in K-theory instead.
It is a stronger condition: an orientable bundle need not be K-orientable, and the obstruction is a further characteristic class.
