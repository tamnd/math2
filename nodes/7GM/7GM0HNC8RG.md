## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **butterfly graph** (also called the **bowtie graph** and the **hourglass graph**) is a [[planar|029JQEK15B]], undirected graph with 5 vertices and 6 edges. It can be constructed by joining 2 copies of the [[cycle graph|0QQ0F12962]] $C_{3}$ with a common vertex and is therefore [[isomorphic|00CT0PTH74]] to the friendship graph $F_{2}$.
