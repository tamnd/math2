## Summary

Heat transfer is the flow of thermal energy between bodies, or between parts of a body, caused by differences of temperature.
It takes place by three modes: conduction through matter at rest, convection by a moving fluid, and thermal radiation by electromagnetic waves.
Its mathematical models are partial differential equations, such as the heat equation, with boundary conditions describing contact with the surroundings.
It is basic to engineering design, from engines and buildings to electronics cooling.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Heat always flows from hot to cold until the temperatures even out.
A spoon in hot soup warms up by conduction.
The air above a radiator rises and carries heat by convection.
The sun warms the earth by radiation across empty space.
The rate of the flow grows with the temperature difference: for a window, it is about proportional to the difference between inside and outside temperatures.
Heat transfer studies how fast these flows are and how temperatures change in time and space.
