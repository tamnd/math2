## Definition

Let $k$ be a field and let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] over $k$.
Let $k[t, t^{-1}]$ be the [[ring of Laurent polynomials|QMY5JTZZB8]] in one variable $t$.
The *loop algebra* of $\mathfrak{g}$ is the vector space $L\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{g} \otimes_k k[t, t^{-1}]$, a [[tensor product|0BF2DE2SYK]], with the bracket
$$[x \otimes f, y \otimes g] = [x, y] \otimes fg$$
for $x, y \in \mathfrak{g}$ and $f, g \in k[t, t^{-1}]$, extended bilinearly.

## Remarks

On the basis elements $x \otimes t^m$ the bracket reads $[x \otimes t^m, y \otimes t^n] = [x, y] \otimes t^{m+n}$.
It is antisymmetric and satisfies the Jacobi identity because the bracket of $\mathfrak{g}$ does and $k[t, t^{-1}]$ is commutative and associative.

Example: for $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{sl}_2$ with basis $e, f, h$, the loop algebra has basis $e \otimes t^m$, $f \otimes t^m$, $h \otimes t^m$ for $m \in \Z$.
For instance $[e \otimes t, f \otimes t^{-1}] = h \otimes 1$.

Non-example: in the affine Lie algebra $\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2$ one has $[e \otimes t, f \otimes t^{-1}] = h \otimes 1 + (e \mid f)\, c$ with a nonzero central element $c$ and a nonzero invariant form value $(e \mid f)$.
This bracket is not of the form $[x, y] \otimes fg$, so the affine algebra is a central extension of the loop algebra and not a loop algebra itself.

The subalgebra $\mathfrak{g} \otimes k[t]$ is often called the current algebra $\mathfrak{g}[t]$.
Over $\C$ one often writes $L\mathfrak{g}$ for this algebra of polynomial loops and uses other names for completions, such as smooth loops $S^1 \to \mathfrak{g}$.
