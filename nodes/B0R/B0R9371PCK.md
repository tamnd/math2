## Summary

In commutative algebra, a **J-0 ring** is a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] $R$ such that the set of regular points, that is, points $p$ of the spectrum at which the localization $R_p$ is a regular local ring, contains a non-empty open subset, a **J-1 ring** is a ring such that the set of regular points is an [[open subset|01ZQQDCERP]], and a **J-2 ring** is a ring such that any finitely generated algebra over the ring is a J-1 ring.
