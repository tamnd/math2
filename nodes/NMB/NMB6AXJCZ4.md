## Summary

In algebra, the **bicommutant** of a [[subset|0BJR9QR08E]] *S* of a [[semigroup|0XMEM406JM]] (such as an algebra or a group) is the commutant of the commutant of that subset. It is also known as the double commutant or second commutant and is written $S^{\prime \prime}$.
