## Statement

**Legendre's conjecture**, proposed by Adrien-Marie Legendre, states that there is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$ for every positive integer $n$. The conjecture is one of Landau's problems (1912) on prime numbers and is one of many open problems on the spacing of prime numbers.
