## Summary

Shear and moment diagrams are the graphs of the shear force and of the [[bending moment|Q6XAPXMGH0]] along a beam.
They are drawn from the loads and the support reactions by the equilibrium relations $V' = -q$ and $M' = V$.
They show at a glance where a beam is most loaded, which is where it is designed or checked.
The moment diagram is also the input of deflection methods such as [[Macaulay's method|5S63FW31DK]] and the [[conjugate beam method|FCGFMFM68F]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Walk along a loaded beam and, at each point, ask how hard the two sides try to slide past each other and to turn against each other.
The first answer, plotted along the beam, is the shear diagram; the second is the moment diagram.
For a shelf supported at both ends and loaded evenly, the shear goes down in a straight line from one end to the other.
The moment is an arch-shaped parabola with its top in the middle.
The top of that parabola is where the shelf is most likely to break.
