## Summary

A unique factorisation domain is an integral domain in which every nonzero nonunit is a product of irreducible elements, in an essentially unique way.
The fundamental theorem of arithmetic says that $\mathbb{Z}$ is one.
The [[definition of a unique factorisation domain|06YMXJMXYS]] gives the conditions.
