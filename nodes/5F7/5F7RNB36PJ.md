## Statement

Let $m$ and $n$ be [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]] with $m \neq 0$ and $n \neq 0$.
Then the [[product|06J5DE801P]] $mn$ is not $0$.

## Remarks

Equivalently, if $mn = 0$ for natural numbers $m$ and $n$, then $m = 0$ or $n = 0$. The natural numbers have no zero divisors.
