## Summary

In field theory, a branch of mathematics, the **Stufe** (; German: "level") *s*(*F*) of a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] *F* is the least number of squares that sum to −1. If −1 cannot be written as a sum of squares, *s*(*F*) = $\infty$. In this case, *F* is a formally real field. Albrecht Pfister proved that the Stufe, if finite, is always a power of 2, and that conversely every power of 2 occurs.
