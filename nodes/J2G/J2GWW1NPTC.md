## Summary

The shear modulus, also called the modulus of rigidity, measures the resistance of a material to shearing.
It is the ratio of shear stress to the resulting shear angle in a state of simple shear.
For an isotropic linear elastic material it equals the second [[Lamé parameter|80737NDZ4V]] $\mu$.
It governs the speed of shear waves and the stiffness of shafts in torsion.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put a thick book on a table, press your hand on top and push sideways.
The pages slide a little over each other and the book leans, without changing its volume much.
The shear modulus tells how hard you must push to get a given small lean angle.
Steel has a shear modulus of about $80$ GPa; fluids at rest have shear modulus $0$, which is why they flow.
