## Statement

Let $A$ be a real [[symmetric matrix|0VR671PHE5]]. The signs of its [[eigenvalues|0BQAA2BK3Q]] sort $A$ into classes.

The matrix $A$ is [[positive definite|0KX66CZMBB]] when every eigenvalue of $A$ is strictly positive.

The matrix $A$ is [[positive semidefinite|0880F9EQCP]] when every eigenvalue of $A$ is at least zero.

The matrix $A$ is [[indefinite|01RBSSGMY5]] when at least one eigenvalue is positive and at least one is negative.

## Remarks

Here the classes are described by eigenvalues. This agrees with the description by the quadratic form $x^T A x$, because a symmetric matrix has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors.

Every positive definite matrix is positive semidefinite. An indefinite matrix is neither.
