## Summary

In the mathematical discipline of graph theory, a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] $C$ is a **covering graph** of another graph $G$ if there is a **covering map** from the vertex set of $C$ to the vertex set of $G$. A covering map $f$ is a surjection and a local isomorphism: the neighbourhood of a vertex $v$ in $C$ is mapped [[bijectively|0GG9TVW90M]] onto the neighbourhood of $f(v)$ in $G$.
