## Summary

A universal bundle for a [[topological group|92700QAB5Y]] $G$ is a [[principal $G$-bundle|8E37E74XVP]] $EG \to BG$ with a classifying property.
Every principal $G$-bundle over a CW complex is its pullback along a map, unique up to homotopy.
Its base $BG$ is the [[classifying space|WSZ40H5E4T]] of $G$.
A principal bundle with contractible total space has this property under mild conditions, which gives a practical way to recognize universal bundles.
Universal bundles turn the classification of bundles into the computation of homotopy classes of maps.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A universal bundle is a master bundle that contains every other bundle with the same group.
To get a bundle over a space $X$, one maps $X$ into the base of the master bundle and pulls the master bundle back.
Homotopic maps give the same bundle, so bundles over $X$ correspond to maps up to deformation.
For the group $\Z/2\Z$, the double cover $S^\infty \to \R P^\infty$ is a universal bundle, and it classifies the double covers of a CW complex.
