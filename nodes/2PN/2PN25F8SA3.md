## Summary

An **Avoider-Enforcer game** (also called **Avoider-Forcer game** or **Antimaker-Antibreaker game**) is a kind of positional game. Like most positional games, it is described by a set of *positions/points/elements* ($X$) and a [[family of subsets|05Q6G4EH2G]] ($\mathcal{F}$), which are called here the *losing-sets*. It is played by two players, called Avoider and Enforcer, who take turns picking elements until all elements are taken. Avoider wins if he manages to avoid taking a losing set; Enforcer wins if he manages to make Avoider take a losing set.
