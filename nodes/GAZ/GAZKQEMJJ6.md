## Summary

An electrovacuum solution is a spacetime whose only source of gravity is an electromagnetic field with no charges or currents present.
The metric and the field together solve the [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]] and the source-free Maxwell equations.
It is the next simplest kind of exact solution after a [[vacuum solution|JRV1T69FYJ]].
The [[Reissner–Nordström metric|8YYV4NRRMB]] and the [[Kerr–Newman metric|NM34PFSE8A]] are the best known examples.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In general relativity, energy curves spacetime, and an electric or magnetic field carries energy.
So a field alone, in otherwise empty space, bends spacetime a little.
An electrovacuum solution describes exactly this situation: there is no matter, only light-like field energy and gravity.
A charged black hole is the standard picture: the charge sits at the singularity, and outside it there is only the electric field and the curved geometry.
Setting the field to zero gives back an ordinary vacuum solution such as the [[Schwarzschild metric|6Y50T68WKY]].
