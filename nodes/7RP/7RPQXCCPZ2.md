## Summary

In algebra, the **dual numbers** are a quadratic algebra first introduced in the 19th century. They are expressions of the form $a + b\varepsilon$, where $a$ and $b$ are [[real numbers|08J2M1JYAG]], and $\varepsilon$ is a symbol taken to satisfy $\varepsilon^2 = 0$ with $\varepsilon\neq 0$.
