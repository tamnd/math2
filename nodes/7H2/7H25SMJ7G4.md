## Statement

Let $L > 0$. Let $f$ be a continuous, piecewise smooth function that is the sum of the [[trigonometric series|FZK2M8KGXQ]]
$$f(t) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \Big[ a_n \cos\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) + b_n \sin\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) \Big],$$
that is, a [[Fourier series|G12KXDHPPS]] with period $2L$. Assume also that the derivative $f'$ is piecewise smooth.

Then the derivative is the sum of the termwise derivatives:
$$f'(t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n\pi}{L} \Big[ -a_n \sin\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) + b_n \cos\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) \Big].$$

## Remarks

Continuity of $f$ is needed. A jump in $f$ would add a singular term to the derivative, and the termwise series would fail to give $f'$.

The general result on [[term-by-term differentiation|0TJ8QBJX5K]] needs uniform convergence of the derived series. This statement uses the smoothness of $f'$ instead.
