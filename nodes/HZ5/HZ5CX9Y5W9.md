## Summary

In mathematics, the **Johnson scheme**, named after Selmer M. Johnson, is also known as the triangular association scheme. It consists of the set of all binary vectors *X* of length *ℓ* and weight *n*, such that $v=\left|X\right|=\binom{\ell}{n}$.
