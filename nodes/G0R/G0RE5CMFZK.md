## Statement

In algebraic number theory, a **supersingular prime** for a given elliptic curve is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] with a certain relationship to that curve. If the curve $E$ is defined over the [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]], then a prime $p$ is **supersingular for *E*** if the reduction of $E$ modulo $p$ is a supersingular elliptic curve over the residue field $\mathbb{F}_p$.
