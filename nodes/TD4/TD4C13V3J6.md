## Summary

The thermodynamic state of a [[system|GYNNJBMTCP]] is its macroscopic condition at a given time, given by the values of its state variables.
For a system in equilibrium a few variables are enough, such as pressure, volume and amount of substance for a gas.
The set of states forms a state space, and a process is a change of state.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The state of a balloon of air is not the list of positions of all its molecules.
It is a short list of measurable numbers: its pressure, its volume, its temperature.
These numbers are not independent: for a fixed amount of gas, two of them determine the third.
So the equilibrium states of the gas form a two-dimensional space, which can be drawn as a plane with axes volume and pressure.
