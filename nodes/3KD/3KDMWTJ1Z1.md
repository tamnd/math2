## Summary

A **system of polynomial equations** (sometimes simply a **polynomial system**) is a set of simultaneous equations $f_{1} = 0, ..., f_{h} = 0$ where the $f_{i}$ are polynomials in several variables, say $x_{1}, ..., x_{n}$, over some [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $k$.
