## Summary

A Euclidean Jordan algebra is a finite-dimensional real [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]] with a unit and an inner product for which every multiplication operator is self-adjoint.
In finite dimension these are exactly the [[formally real Jordan algebras|WYE4E80T43]].
The interior of the set of squares of a Euclidean Jordan algebra is a [[symmetric cone|NZMXY4WTZX]].
By the [[Koecher–Vinberg theorem|PXXEWA9V2S]], every symmetric cone arises in this way.
Euclidean Jordan algebras are used in harmonic analysis on cones, in semidefinite optimization and in the study of symmetric spaces.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The model is the space of real symmetric matrices, with the averaged product $\frac{1}{2}(XY + YX)$ and the inner product $\tr(XY)$.
Moving a factor across the inner product works as for ordinary numbers: $\ip{X \circ Y}{Z} = \ip{Y}{X \circ Z}$.
This one rule, together with positivity of the inner product, already forces a rich structure, such as a spectral theorem.
Every element can be written as a combination of orthogonal idempotents, just as a symmetric matrix can be diagonalized.
