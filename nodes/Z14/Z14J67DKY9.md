## Summary

Measure and integration is the study of measures, which give a size to the sets of a family, and of the integral that a measure defines.
A measure is a function on a sigma-algebra of sets, with values in the nonnegative extended real numbers, that is zero on the empty set and countably additive.
The Lebesgue integral that a measure defines has good convergence theorems, and it is the base of probability theory, functional analysis and harmonic analysis.
Lebesgue defined his measure and integral in his thesis of 1902, and Kolmogorov built probability theory on measure theory in 1933.
This topic collects the measure and integration pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Real analysis, probability theory, ergodic theory and functional analysis have their own topics.
