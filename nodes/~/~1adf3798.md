## Summary

Operator theory is the study of linear and nonlinear operators on Banach and Hilbert spaces.
A bounded linear operator is a linear map between normed spaces that sends bounded sets to bounded sets, and this property is the same as continuity.
Operators model differential and integral equations and the observables of quantum mechanics, and their spectra generalize the eigenvalues of matrices.
Hilbert's study of integral equations and von Neumann's spectral theory of unbounded self-adjoint operators were the early bases of the subject.
This topic collects the operator theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Functional analysis, operator algebras and partial differential equations have their own topics.
