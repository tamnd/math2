## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers non-holomorphic automorphic forms on the upper half-plane, with the Laplacian, Maass forms, the real-analytic Eisenstein series, the spectral decomposition of the modular surface, and the main problems on eigenvalues and eigenfunctions.
