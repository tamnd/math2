## Description

The imaginary unit is the [[complex number|~bc6635c6]] $i = 0 + 1 i$. It satisfies $i^2 = -1$, see [[i squared is minus one|~89a7369e]].
It is a solution of the equation $x^2 + 1 = 0$. The other solution is $-i$.
In the [[complex plane|~d5458ec5]], $i$ is the point $(0, 1)$, at distance $1$ above $0$.

## Remarks

The powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$: $i^0 = 1$, $i^1 = i$, $i^2 = -1$, $i^3 = -i$ and $i^4 = 1$.
Multiplication by $i$ turns the complex plane by a quarter turn about $0$, counterclockwise.
In electrical engineering, people often write $j$ for $i$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The imaginary unit is a new number whose square is $-1$.
No real number has this property, so $i$ is not on the real number line.
It sits one unit above $0$ in the plane of complex numbers.
