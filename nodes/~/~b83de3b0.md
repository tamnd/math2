## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|~640bdf1c]].
It covers the mathematical treatment of truth and of the paradoxes of self-reference: Tarski's theory, the liar paradox and its relatives, the fixed-point and revision theories, and axiomatic theories of truth over arithmetic.
