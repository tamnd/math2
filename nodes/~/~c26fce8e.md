## Summary

The least common multiple of two nonzero integers is the smallest positive integer that is a multiple of both of them.
We write it as $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b)$.
The [[definition of the least common multiple|~eba0240b]] gives the precise form and the convention when one of the numbers is $0$.
