## Summary

Potential theory is the study of harmonic and subharmonic functions and of the potentials of measures.
A harmonic function is a twice differentiable function whose Laplacian is zero.
The theory started from the gravitational and electrostatic potentials of physics, and it has deep links with Brownian motion, with complex analysis and with elliptic partial differential equations.
Green introduced the functions that now have his name in 1828, and Gauss studied the equilibrium distribution of charges in 1840.
This topic collects the potential theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Complex analysis, partial differential equations and stochastic processes have their own topics.
