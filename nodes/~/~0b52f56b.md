This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|~d000caef]].
It covers models whose free energy or correlations have closed forms, transfer matrices, vertex models, Baxter's commuting and corner transfer matrices, the hard hexagon model, and the Bethe ansatz for lattice and continuum systems.
