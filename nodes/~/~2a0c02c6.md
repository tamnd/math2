This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers the structure of noncommutative Noetherian rings: prime ideals, Krull and Gelfand-Kirillov dimension, the growth of algebras, the Kurosh and Golod-Shafarevich problems, and noncommutative projective algebra.
