This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|~1c787828]].
It covers flows of Riemannian and Kähler metrics other than the Ricci flow, such as the Yamabe, Kähler-Ricci, Calabi and higher-order flows, and flows of geometric structures.
