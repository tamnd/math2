## Statement

Let $n$ be a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] with $n \neq 0$.
Then there is a natural number $m$ such that $n = s(m)$, where $s$ is the [[successor function|0832XVR512]].

## Remarks

In other words, each natural number other than $0$ has a predecessor.
This complements the fact that $0$ is not a successor.
