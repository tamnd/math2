## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|~3656b229]].
It covers the operator algebras built from a locally compact group as tools for its representations: the L1 algebra, the C*-algebras and the von Neumann algebra of the group, the abstract Plancherel theorem, and the rigidity properties (T) and Haagerup.
