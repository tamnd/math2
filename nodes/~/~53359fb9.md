## Summary

A branch of the logarithm on an open set $U \subseteq \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$ is a continuous function $L$ on $U$ with $e^{L(z)} = z$, see the [[definition|~14ef1d0d]].
On a domain, two branches differ by a constant integer multiple of $2\pi i$.
