## Statement

Let $M(x,y)$ and $N(x,y)$ be two functions of the plane with continuous first partial derivatives. Suppose that $\partial M/\partial y = \partial N/\partial x$ holds where both are defined.

Then every point has a neighbourhood on which there is a function $F(x,y)$ with $\partial F/\partial x = M$ and $\partial F/\partial y = N$.

## Remarks

The condition on $M$ and $N$ says that the [[one-form|0MTENFBW3Q]] $M\,dx + N\,dy$ is [[closed|0F1AVGX26N]]. The conclusion says that this form is exact near each point.

This is the planar, local case of the Poincaré lemma. It is the basis for solving exact first order equations $M\,dx + N\,dy = 0$, whose solutions are the level curves of $F$.
