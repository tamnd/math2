## Summary

This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers real submanifolds of complex manifolds and abstract CR structures, the tangential Cauchy-Riemann equations, CR extension and approximation, normal forms, the embedding of abstract CR manifolds and the Kohn-Rossi complex.
