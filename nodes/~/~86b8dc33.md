## Proof

We use the notation $V(\Gamma)$ and the basic facts 1 to 6 of the [[definition of phase spaces|~09c29208]].
Fact 4 and the fact $A^* = (A^*)^{\perp\perp}$ give the rule we use most: $V(\Gamma) (A^*)^{\perp} \subseteq \bot$ if and only if $V(\Gamma) \subseteq A^*$.

**Part 1: soundness.**
Fix a phase model.
We show that each rule of MALL takes valid premises to a valid conclusion, by cases on the rule.

1. Axiom $\vdash A^{\perp}, A$: valid if and only if $A^* \subseteq A^*$, which holds.
2. Cut on $\vdash \Gamma, A$ and $\vdash \Delta, A^{\perp}$: the premises give $V(\Gamma) \subseteq A^*$ and $V(\Delta) A^* \subseteq \bot$, since $((A^{\perp})^*)^{\perp} = A^*$.
   So $V(\Gamma) V(\Delta) \subseteq A^* V(\Delta) \subseteq \bot$.
3. $\otimes$: the premises give $V(\Gamma) \subseteq B^*$ and $V(\Delta) \subseteq C^*$.
   So $V(\Gamma) V(\Delta) \subseteq B^* C^* \subseteq (B^* C^*)^{\perp\perp} = (B \otimes C)^*$.
4. $\mathbin{\wp}$: the premise says $V(\Gamma) (B^*)^{\perp} (C^*)^{\perp} \subseteq \bot$.
   Fact 5 with $X = (B^*)^{\perp} (C^*)^{\perp}$ gives $V(\Gamma) X^{\perp\perp} \subseteq \bot$, and $X^{\perp\perp} = ((B \mathbin{\wp} C)^*)^{\perp}$.
5. $\mathbin{\&}$: the premises give $V(\Gamma) \subseteq B^*$ and $V(\Gamma) \subseteq C^*$, so $V(\Gamma) \subseteq B^* \cap C^*$.
6. $\oplus$: the premise gives $V(\Gamma) \subseteq B^*$, and $B^* \subseteq (B^* \cup C^*)^{\perp\perp}$ by fact 1.
   The second form is the same.
7. $1$: $V(1) = (\set{1}^{\perp\perp})^{\perp} = \set{1}^{\perp} = \bot$ by facts 3 and 6.
8. $\bot$: the premise says $V(\Gamma) \set{1} \subseteq \bot$.
   Fact 5 gives $V(\Gamma) \set{1}^{\perp\perp} \subseteq \bot$, and $\set{1}^{\perp\perp} = \bot^{\perp}$ by fact 6.
9. $\top$: $(\top^*)^{\perp} = M^{\perp}$, and $V(\Gamma) M^{\perp} \subseteq \bot$, since $m n \in \bot$ for each $n \in M^{\perp}$ and each $m \in M$.

By induction on the derivation, every derivable sequent is valid.

**Part 2: completeness.**
We build the *syntactic phase model*.

1. $M$ is the set of finite multisets of formulas, with multiset union as product and the empty multiset as unit.
   We write $\Delta, \Sigma$ for the product.
2. $\bot$ is the set of multisets $\Delta$ such that $\vdash \Delta$ has a cut-free derivation.
   We write $\vdash_{cf} \Delta$.
3. For a formula $A$, let $[A] = \set{A}^{\perp} = \set{\Delta \mid \vdash_{cf} \Delta, A}$.
   Put $p^* = [p]$ for each atom $p$, which is a fact by fact 3.

*Lemma.* For every formula $A$: (a) $\set{A^{\perp}} \in A^*$, and (b) $\set{A} \in (A^*)^{\perp}$.
We use induction on the size of $A$, with these cases.

1. $A = p$: (a) says $\vdash_{cf} p^{\perp}, p$, an axiom. (b) says $\set{p} \in \set{p}^{\perp\perp}$, which is fact 1.
2. $A = p^{\perp}$: $A^* = (p^*)^{\perp}$.
   Then (a) for $A$ is (b) for $p$, and (b) for $A$ is (a) for $p$, since $(p^*)^{\perp\perp} = p^*$.
3. $A = B \otimes C$, part (a): let $\Delta \in (B^* C^*)^{\perp}$.
   By (a) for $B$ and $C$, $\Delta, B^{\perp}, C^{\perp} \in \bot$, and the rule $\mathbin{\wp}$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B^{\perp} \mathbin{\wp} C^{\perp}$.
   So $\set{A^{\perp}} \in (B^* C^*)^{\perp\perp} = A^*$.
4. $A = B \otimes C$, part (b): $(A^*)^{\perp} = (B^* C^*)^{\perp}$ by fact 3.
   For $\Delta \in B^*$ and $\Sigma \in C^*$, (b) for $B$ and $C$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B$ and $\vdash_{cf} \Sigma, C$.
   The rule $\otimes$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, \Sigma, B \otimes C$.
5. $A = B \mathbin{\wp} C$: this case is dual to cases 3 and 4.
   For (a), $\Delta \in (B^*)^{\perp}$ and $\Sigma \in (C^*)^{\perp}$ give $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B^{\perp}$ and $\vdash_{cf} \Sigma, C^{\perp}$ by (a) for $B$ and $C$, and $\otimes$ applies.
   For (b), $\Delta \in ((B^*)^{\perp} (C^*)^{\perp})^{\perp}$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B, C$ by (b) for $B$ and $C$, and $\mathbin{\wp}$ applies.
6. $A = B \mathbin{\&} C$: for (a), let $\Delta \in (B^*)^{\perp}$.
   By (a) for $B$, $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B^{\perp}$, and $\oplus$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B^{\perp} \oplus C^{\perp}$.
   So $\set{A^{\perp}} \in (B^*)^{\perp\perp} = B^*$, and also $\set{A^{\perp}} \in C^*$.
   For (b), $\Delta \in B^* \cap C^*$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B$ and $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, C$, and $\mathbin{\&}$ applies.
7. $A = B \oplus C$: for (a), $\Delta \in (B^* \cup C^*)^{\perp}$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B^{\perp}$ and $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, C^{\perp}$, and $\mathbin{\&}$ applies.
   For (b), $\Delta \in B^*$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, B$, and $\oplus$ applies; the same holds for $C^*$.
8. $A = 1$: (a) for $\Delta \in \set{\emptyset}^{\perp} = \bot$, the rule $\bot$ gives $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, \bot$, so $\set{\bot} \in \set{\emptyset}^{\perp\perp} = 1^*$. (b) $(1^*)^{\perp} = \bot$ contains $\set{1}$, since $\vdash 1$ is an axiom.
9. $A = \bot$: (a) $\set{1} \in \bot = \bot^*$. (b) $\set{\bot} \in \bot^{\perp}$, by the rule $\bot$ as in case 8.
10. $A = \top$ and $A = 0$: $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, \top$ for all $\Delta$, so $\set{\top} \in M^{\perp} = 0^*$ and $\set{\top} \in M^{\perp} = (\top^*)^{\perp}$.
    Also $\set{0} \in M = \top^*$ and $\set{0} \in M^{\perp\perp} = (0^*)^{\perp}$.

*End of Part 2.*

1. Let $\vdash A_1, \ldots, A_n$ be valid in every phase model, so it is valid in the syntactic one.
2. By (b) of the lemma, $\set{A_i} \in (A_i^*)^{\perp}$ for each $i$.
3. Validity gives $\set{A_1} \cdots \set{A_n} = \set{A_1, \ldots, A_n} \in \bot$, that is, $\vdash_{cf} A_1, \ldots, A_n$.

**Part 3.**

1. A derivable sequent is valid by Part 1, and it has a cut-free derivation by Part 2.

## Idea

Soundness checks each rule against the closure facts of the definition.
For completeness, a formula $A$ is read in the syntactic model as a set of contexts.
The lemma says that this set lies between the axiom context $A^{\perp}$ and the set of all contexts $\Delta$ with $\vdash_{cf} \Delta, A$.
Since $\bot$ holds only cut-free derivable sequents, the cut rule is never used.

## Variants

Okada's cited paper extends the method to first-order and higher-order linear logic.
With $\bot$ the set of all derivable sequents, the same model proves completeness, but it gives no information on cuts.
