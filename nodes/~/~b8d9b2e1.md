## Summary

A conservative vector field is a [[vector field|~edfef10c]] that is the [[gradient|~aca5eb00]] of a scalar function, called a [[scalar potential|~ff8fe028]] of the field.
Its line integrals depend only on the end points of the path, and they vanish around each closed path.
