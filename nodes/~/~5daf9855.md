## Summary

Set theory is the study of sets, the collections from which almost all mathematical objects can be built.
The standard axiom system is Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory with the axiom of choice, called ZFC, in which every object is a set and membership is the only basic relation.
Set theory gives a common language and a foundation for mathematics, and it studies the infinite through ordinal and cardinal numbers.
Cantor created the subject in the 1870s, Zermelo gave the first axioms in 1908, and the work of Gödel and Cohen showed that the continuum hypothesis is independent of ZFC.
This topic collects the set theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Mathematical logic, model theory, computability theory, and type theory and constructive mathematics have their own topics.
