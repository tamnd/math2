## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]] or a [[multigraph|~fac563f7]].
An Eulerian trail of $G$ is a [[trail|~1080e4ae]] in $G$ that uses every edge of $G$ exactly once.
An Eulerian circuit is an Eulerian trail that starts and ends at the same vertex.

## Remarks

A graph with an Eulerian circuit is called Eulerian.
[[Euler's theorem|~36631fcb]] characterizes the connected graphs with an Eulerian circuit by the parity of the degrees.
The [[Seven Bridges of Königsberg|~a931b5ee]] form a multigraph with no Eulerian trail.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An Eulerian trail draws the whole graph in one stroke, without lifting the pen and without going over any line twice.
An Eulerian circuit also ends where it starts.
