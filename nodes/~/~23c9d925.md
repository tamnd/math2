## Statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer.
The number of invertible elements of the ring $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ of the [[integers modulo n|~6506d19f]] is $\varphi(n)$, where $\varphi$ is [[Euler's totient function|~97075527]].

## Remarks

By the [[existence of inverses modulo n|~28ced755]], the class $\overline{a}$ is invertible if and only if $\gcd(a, n) = 1$. The classes of $1, \dots, n$ that are coprime to $n$ give $\varphi(n)$ classes.
The invertible elements form a group under multiplication, the group of units $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times$. It has order $\varphi(n)$.
For example, $(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z})^\times = \{ \overline{1}, \overline{3}, \overline{5}, \overline{7} \}$ has $\varphi(8) = 4$ elements.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Modulo $n$, you can divide by exactly $\varphi(n)$ of the $n$ residues.
For $n = 9$, these are the residues of $1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8$, and $\varphi(9) = 6$.
