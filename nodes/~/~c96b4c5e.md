## Statement

Let $X$ be a [[path-connected space|~3f35983a]].
Then $X$ is [[connected|~711a13dd]].

## Remarks

Proof: suppose open sets $U$ and $V$ split $X$, with $x \in U$ and $y \in V$. Take a path $\gamma$ from $x$ to $y$. Then $\gamma([0, 1])$ is connected, by [[continuous image of a connected set is connected|~7bd6316c]] and [[connectedness of intervals|~700dbf8a]]. But $U$ and $V$ split it, a contradiction.
The converse fails, as the [[topologist's sine curve|~f763700e]] shows. It holds for [[open subsets of R^n|~121fcd83]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

If you can walk between any two points, the space cannot be split into two separate open parts.
