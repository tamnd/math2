## Summary

This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers the correspondence between stable holomorphic bundles and Hermitian-Einstein metrics on compact Kähler manifolds, and its nonabelian Hodge analogue for Higgs bundles and flat bundles.
