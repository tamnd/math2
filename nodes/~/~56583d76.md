## Statement

Let $X$ be a [[topological space|~77bfcc55]] and let $S \subseteq X$.
Then the [[closure|~8d5f2dc5]] $\overline{S}$ is a [[closed set|~dfeacff3]] of $X$.

## Remarks

Proof: $\overline{S}$ is an intersection of closed sets, and each [[intersection of closed sets is closed|~a0642a65]].
It follows that $\overline{\overline{S}} = \overline{S}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The closure is built as an intersection of closed sets, and such an intersection is again closed.
