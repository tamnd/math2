## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Diophantine approximation and geometry of numbers|~db363e14]].
It covers the minima of positive definite quadratic forms, reduced bases and the algorithms that find them, with the Hermite constant, perfect and extreme forms, the LLL algorithm and the hard lattice problems.
