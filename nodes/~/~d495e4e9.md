## Statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $F_n = 2^{2^n} + 1$ be the [[Fermat number|~04e5f905]].
Then $F_n$ is prime if and only if
$$3^{(F_n - 1)/2} \equiv -1 \pmod{F_n}.$$

## Remarks

For example, $F_1 = 5$ and $3^2 = 9 \equiv -1 \pmod 5$.
The power can be computed by $2^n - 1$ squarings with [[modular exponentiation|~757169f9]].
The "only if" part follows from [[Euler's criterion|~6247822c]] and quadratic reciprocity, since $3$ is a nonresidue modulo $F_n$.

## History

The test is named after Théophile Pépin.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Pépin's test decides whether a Fermat number is prime with one large modular power.
The Fermat numbers grow so fast that the test is practical only for small $n$.
