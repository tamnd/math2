## Summary

This topic is a part of [[enumerative combinatorics|~6569444c]].
It covers counting functions that are polynomials or quasi-polynomials, their rational generating functions, and the reciprocity laws for their values at negative integers, with P-partitions, linear Diophantine systems and magic squares.
