## Statement

Let $X$ and $Y$ be [[finite sets|070R982G4B]].
Suppose there is an [[injective function|0PVG1CRAJN]] from $X$ to $Y$ and an injective function from $Y$ to $X$.
Then there is a [[bijective function|0SNQEF7Z53]] $h \colon X \to Y$.

## Remarks

This is the case of finite sets of the general Schröder–Bernstein theorem [[Schröder–Bernstein theorem|0A9V6RKQG6]].
For finite sets it follows by counting: an injection gives $|X| \le |Y|$, the other gives $|Y| \le |X|$, so the sizes are equal.
