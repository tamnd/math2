## Summary

A real-analytic hypersurface is a real hypersurface $M \subset \R^n$ that near each of its points is the graph of a real [[analytic function|081WKEDK6C]] of $n-1$ real variables.

To be precise, around every point of $M$ one can choose the coordinates of $\R^n$, possibly after renaming them, as $(x,y) \in \R^{n-1} \times \R$. In a neighbourhood of that point, $M$ is then the set where $y = \varphi(x)$, and $\varphi$ is real-analytic.

Such a hypersurface is a special case of a [[real analytic manifold|Z7H98E1NHP]] of dimension $n-1$.
