This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers the Calabi conjecture and Yau's solution, Kähler-Einstein metrics for the three signs of the first Chern class, Calabi-Yau manifolds, and the stability conditions for Fano manifolds and for metrics of constant scalar curvature.
