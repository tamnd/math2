## Statement

The number $\sqrt[3]{2}$ is not a [[constructible number|~012d5a00]].
So the cube cannot be [[doubled|~7c15cdcb]] with straightedge and compass.

## Remarks

The proof: $x^3 - 2$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, by [[Eisenstein's criterion|~576899eb]] with $p = 2$. So $[\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[3]{2}) : \mathbb{Q}] = 3$.
Since $3$ is not a power of $2$, the number $\sqrt[3]{2}$ is not constructible, by the [[degree theorem|~f4af4673]].

## History

Pierre Wantzel proved the impossibility in 1837.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The edge of the doubled cube is the cube root of $2$.
Its minimal polynomial has degree $3$, but constructible numbers have degree a power of $2$.
