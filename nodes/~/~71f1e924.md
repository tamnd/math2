## Definition

Let $A$ and $B$ be [[sets|~bd92ceee]].
The intersection of $A$ and $B$ is the set
$$A \cap B = \{x \in A \mid x \in B\}.$$
So an object $x$ is an [[element|~6dec28e3]] of $A \cap B$ when $x \in A$ and $x \in B$.

## Remarks

Example: $\{1, 2\} \cap \{2, 3\} = \{2\}$.
Intersection is commutative and associative, and $A \cap B \subseteq A$.
When $A \cap B = \varnothing$, the sets are [[disjoint|~7e63af19]].
The intersection of a family of sets is in [[intersection of a family of sets|~c8579c2e]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The intersection keeps only what the two sets have in common.
The intersection of the even numbers and the multiples of $3$ is the set of multiples of $6$.
