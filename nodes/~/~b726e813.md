## Definition

Let $A$ be a [[set|~bd92ceee]].
The identity function of $A$ is the [[function|~d103e2ff]] $\mathrm{id}_A \colon A \to A$ with $\mathrm{id}_A(a) = a$ for each $a \in A$.

## Remarks

For each function $f \colon A \to B$, $f \circ \mathrm{id}_A = f$ and $\mathrm{id}_B \circ f = f$. See [[function composition|~f5ac250d]].
The identity function is a [[bijection|~14a2ca3d]], and it is its own inverse.
Its graph is the diagonal $\{(a, a) \mid a \in A\}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The identity function does nothing: each input comes out unchanged.
It plays the role for composition that the number $1$ plays for multiplication.
