This topic is a part of [[order, lattices and universal algebra|~96bdc890]].
It covers Boolean algebras as complemented distributive lattices and as Boolean rings: axioms, ideals and quotients, finite, free, atomless and complete algebras, and the structure theory of infinite Boolean algebras.
