## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]] with vertex set $V$.
Then $G$ is bipartite if $V$ is the union of two disjoint sets $A$ and $B$ with this property.
Each edge of $G$ has one end in $A$ and one end in $B$.
The pair $(A, B)$ is a bipartition of $G$.

## Remarks

Equivalently, $G$ is bipartite if it has a proper [[colouring|~ed132083]] with $2$ colours.
The [[complete bipartite graphs|~13c6e22b]], the even [[cycle graphs|~19143ce5]] and all [[trees|~29e53124]] are bipartite.
A graph is bipartite if and only if it has no cycle of odd length. The node [[a graph is bipartite iff it has no odd cycle|~547b729a]] states this.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A graph is bipartite when its vertices split into two groups, and every edge goes between the groups.
Students and the courses they take form a bipartite graph.
