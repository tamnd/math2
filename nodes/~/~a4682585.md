## Statement

Call a connected open set $U \subseteq \C^n$ circular if $e^{i\theta} z \in U$ for every $z \in U$ and every real $\theta$.

Let $U, V \subseteq \C^n$ be [[bounded|09QSZVVM26]] circular domains that both contain $0$.
Let $f \colon U \to V$ be a [[biholomorphism|FX92PP3HZR]] with $f(0) = 0$.
Then $f$ is a linear map.

## Remarks

The result follows from [[Cartan's uniqueness theorem|EQHPSKC5SY]].
One compares $f$ with the maps $z \mapsto e^{-i\theta} f(e^{i\theta} z)$ and $z \mapsto f(z)$, which agree at $0$ and have related derivatives there.
Averaging over $\theta$ shows that $f$ equals its own first-order Taylor term at $0$.
