## Definition

Let $X$ be a set and let $k$ be a natural number.
A $k$-combination of $X$ is a subset $S \subseteq X$ with $|S| = k$.

The set of all $k$-combinations of $X$ is written $\binom{X}{k}$.

## Remarks

A combination does not record an order of its elements. Two lists with the same elements in a different order give the same combination.
For $X = \{a, b, c\}$, the 2-combinations are $\{a, b\}$, $\{a, c\}$ and $\{b, c\}$.
The [[number of k-element subsets|~e9269e78]] of an $n$-element set is the [[binomial coefficient|~b1f658a8]] $\binom{n}{k}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A combination is a selection of $k$ objects where the order does not matter.
Choosing a team of 2 from Ann, Bob and Cy gives 3 teams, because the team $\{$Ann, Bob$\}$ is the same as $\{$Bob, Ann$\}$.
