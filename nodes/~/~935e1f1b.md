## Summary

A linear recurrence with constant coefficients makes each term a fixed linear combination of the previous terms. Its solutions have closed forms in terms of the roots of its characteristic polynomial. The node [[linear recurrence with constant coefficients|~d312aec3]] gives the precise definition.
