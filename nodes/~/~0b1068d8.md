## Summary

Let $W$ be a [[subspace|00YC1D652K]] of $\mathbb{R}^m$. Its orthogonal complement, written $W^\perp$, is the set of all vectors in $\mathbb{R}^m$ that are [[orthogonal|0TS7VZJBWD]] to every vector of $W$. Here orthogonality is taken with respect to the [[dot product|0S52TNNTH9]] of $\mathbb{R}^m$.

In symbols, $W^\perp = \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^m : v \cdot w = 0 \text{ for every } w \in W \}$.
