## Example

Let $a = (1, 2, 3)$, $b = (0, 1, 4)$ and $c = (5, 6, 0)$.
We compute the [[scalar triple product|~d7839aac]] in two ways.

1. [[Cross product|0N5N15HWZ7]]: $b \times c = (1 \cdot 0 - 4 \cdot 6, \; 4 \cdot 5 - 0 \cdot 0, \; 0 \cdot 6 - 1 \cdot 5) = (-24, 20, -5)$.
2. [[Dot product|0S52TNNTH9]]: $a \cdot (b \times c) = 1 \cdot (-24) + 2 \cdot 20 + 3 \cdot (-5) = -24 + 40 - 15 = 1$.
3. [[Laplace expansion|0C08BQ3M05]] of the determinant with the rows $a$, $b$, $c$ along row $1$:
$$1 \cdot \det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 6 & 0 \end{pmatrix} - 2 \cdot \det \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ 5 & 0 \end{pmatrix} + 3 \cdot \det \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix} = 1 \cdot (-24) - 2 \cdot (-20) + 3 \cdot (-5) = 1.$$
4. Both ways give $1$, as the [[determinant formula|059Q2XWJDC]] says.
5. By the [[volume theorem|~15dbbcb4]], the parallelepiped on $a$, $b$, $c$ has the volume $1$.
6. The value is not $0$, so $a$, $b$, $c$ are not coplanar, and they form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$.
7. The value is positive, so $(a, b, c)$ is a right-handed triple.

## Remarks

The lengths are $\|a\| = \sqrt{14}$, $\|b\| = \sqrt{17}$ and $\|c\| = \sqrt{61}$, so the box is long and thin.
Its volume $1$ is much less than $\sqrt{14 \cdot 17 \cdot 61} \approx 120$, the volume of a box with perpendicular edges of these lengths.
The tetrahedron with the vertices $0$, $a$, $b$, $c$ has the volume $\tfrac{1}{6}$.
The matrix with the rows $a$, $b$, $c$ has determinant $1$, so its inverse has integer entries.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The three arrows $(1, 2, 3)$, $(0, 1, 4)$ and $(5, 6, 0)$ make a slanted box.
The computation gives the number $1$, so the box holds exactly one unit cube of space, though its edges are long.

### L2 human

The two computations in steps 2 and 3 use the same three $2 \times 2$ minors; step 1 lists them as the components of $b \times c$.
The middle minor is $-20$, and the middle component of $b \times c$ is $+20$; the minus sign of the Laplace expansion accounts for this.
A trap: a wrong sign on the middle term gives $-24 - 40 - 15 = -79$.
