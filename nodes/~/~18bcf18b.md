This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers rings defined by conditions on their elements: von Neumann regular, unit-regular, clean and exchange rings, Dedekind-finite rings, Baer and Rickart rings, stable range, and the commutativity theorems.
