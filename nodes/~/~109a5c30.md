## Example

Full [[linear logic|H408H4WHBA]] derives *digging*, $!p \multimap !!p$, but elementary linear logic (ELL) does not.
So full linear logic is not a light logic.

Derivation in linear logic, in one-sided form $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !!p$:

1. $\vdash p^{\perp}, p$ is an axiom.
2. Dereliction gives $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, p$.
3. Promotion gives $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !p$, since the context is $?p^{\perp}$.
4. Promotion again gives $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !!p$.

Non-derivability in ELL, by a translation into classical logic with atoms $p_0, p_1, p_2, \ldots$:

1. For $k \geq 0$, put $T_k(p) = p_k$ and $T_k(p^{\perp}) = \neg p_k$.
   Let $T_k$ send $\otimes$, $\mathbin{\&}$ to $\wedge$, and $\mathbin{\wp}$, $\oplus$ to $\vee$.
   Let $1, \top$ go to true and $\bot, 0$ to false.
2. Put $T_k(!A) = T_k(?A) = T_{k+1}(A)$.
   By induction on $A$, $T_k(A^{\perp}) = \neg T_k(A)$, and $T_{k+1}(A)$ is $T_k(A)$ with each $p_j$ renamed $p_{j+1}$.
3. Call $\vdash A_1, \ldots, A_n$ *good* if $T_0(A_1) \vee \cdots \vee T_0(A_n)$ is a classical tautology.
4. The axiom, cut and the rules for $\otimes$, $\mathbin{\wp}$, $\mathbin{\&}$, $\oplus$ and the units keep goodness, by the classical meaning of the connectives.
5. Weakening $?W$ adds a disjunct, and contraction $?C$ replaces $X \vee X$ by $X$.
   Both keep goodness.
6. Functorial promotion takes $\vdash \Gamma, A$ to $\vdash ?\Gamma, !A$.
   The new disjunction is the old one with each $p_j$ renamed $p_{j+1}$, and renaming keeps tautologies.
7. So every sequent derivable in ELL is good, by induction on the derivation.
8. $T_0$ of $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !!p$ is $\neg p_1 \vee p_2$.
   It is false when $p_1$ is true and $p_2$ is false, so the sequent is not good.
9. So $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !!p$ is not derivable in ELL.

## Remarks

The same translation shows that dereliction is not derivable in ELL: $!p \multimap p$ goes to $\neg p_1 \vee p_0$, which is not a tautology.
The translation fails for full linear logic exactly at dereliction, which takes $\vdash \Gamma, A$ to $\vdash \Gamma, ?A$ and changes the level of $A$ alone.
The [[functorial promotion example|~eb708ff1]] goes to $\neg (\neg a_1 \vee b_1) \vee \neg a_1 \vee b_1$ for $A = a$ and $B = b$, which is a tautology, as it must be.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The translation gives each atom a level: the number of "!" and "?" around it.
The rules of ELL change all levels together, so a proof always matches atoms of the same level.
Digging would match $p$ at level $1$ with $p$ at level $2$, so ELL cannot prove it.

### L2 human

We check step 6 on the derivation of $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !p$ from the axiom.

1. The axiom $\vdash p^{\perp}, p$ goes to $\neg p_0 \vee p_0$, a tautology.
2. Functorial promotion gives $\vdash ?p^{\perp}, !p$, which goes to $\neg p_1 \vee p_1$.
3. This is step 1 with $p_0$ renamed $p_1$, and it is a tautology.
4. A second promotion gives $\vdash ??p^{\perp}, !!p$, which goes to $\neg p_2 \vee p_2$, again a tautology.

The typical use of this translation is to prove that a formula is not derivable in a stratified logic.
A trap: the translation is sound for ELL but not complete; many good sequents, such as $\vdash p^{\perp}, p \otimes p$, are not derivable.
