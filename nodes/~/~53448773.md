## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|~ce40ba2a]].
It covers the Chevalley-Eilenberg complex, the lemmas of Whitehead, the cohomology of semisimple Lie algebras and of compact Lie groups, continuous cohomology, and deformations controlled by differential graded Lie algebras.
