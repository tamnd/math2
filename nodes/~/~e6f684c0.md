## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers trace formulas for the group SL2(R) and its discrete subgroups, the Selberg trace formula in the compact and the cofinite case and its consequences, and the Kuznetsov formula, which links Fourier coefficients with Kloosterman sums.
