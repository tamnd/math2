## Summary

A linear first-order equation is an equation $y' + p(x) y = q(x)$ with given functions $p$ and $q$.
It is the simplest [[linear differential equation|~ebff7098]], and an [[integrating factor|~54719f20]] solves it explicitly. See the [[definition|~e63ef4b9]].
