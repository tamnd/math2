## Statement

Let $x$ and $y$ be real numbers with $x < y$.
Then there is an [[irrational number|~9900a69e]] $z$ with $x < z < y$.

## Remarks

Proof: by the [[density of the rationals|~a8cca681]], there is a rational $q$ with $x / \sqrt{2} < q < y / \sqrt{2}$ and $q \neq 0$. Then $z = q \sqrt{2}$ is irrational and $x < z < y$.
Thus both $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$ are dense in $\mathbb{R}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Between any two different real numbers, there is also a number that is not a fraction.
So fractions and non-fractions are mixed together everywhere on the number line.
