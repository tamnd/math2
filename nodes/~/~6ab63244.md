## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|~a5df4ed5]].
It covers the classification of endomorphisms up to similarity: cyclic subspaces, the rational canonical form and the Jordan normal form with their computation, both by elementary linear algebra and by modules over a polynomial ring.
