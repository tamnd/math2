## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|~3656b229]].
It covers the characters of the finite general linear groups and of other finite reductive groups by Harish-Chandra and Deligne-Lusztig induction, the classification of Lusztig, and the rational representations of reductive algebraic groups in positive characteristic.
