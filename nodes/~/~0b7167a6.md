## Summary

This topic is a part of [[classical geometries|~e5d03ec0]].
It covers the incidence geometries of circles such as Möbius, Laguerre and Minkowski planes, their generalization to chain geometries over rings, and the geometries of oriented spheres of Laguerre and Lie.
