## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|~2c3bc0e0]].
It covers integral domains and the classes of domains defined by divisibility, such as Euclidean, principal ideal, Bézout, GCD, atomic and unique factorization domains, with the standard examples and counterexamples.
