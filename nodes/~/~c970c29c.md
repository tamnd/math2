## Summary

This topic is a part of [[manifolds and differential topology|~d33a3ff0]].
It covers subbundles of the tangent bundle, the Frobenius theorem, and the geometric theory of foliations with leaves, holonomy, codimension-one foliations, classifying spaces and characteristic classes.
