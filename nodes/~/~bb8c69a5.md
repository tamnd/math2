## Statement

For every real $x > 0$ and every natural number $n$,
$$\ln(x^n) = n \ln x.$$

## Remarks

Induction on $n$ with the [[product rule for logarithms|~08ac218d]] gives the claim.
For [[real exponents|~ddd19c24]], $\ln(x^a) = a \ln x$ holds by the definition $x^a = e^{a \ln x}$.
Mathlib states it as `Real.log_pow`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The logarithm brings an exponent down as a factor.
For example, $\ln(x^3) = 3 \ln x$.
