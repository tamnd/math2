## Statement

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix of rank $r$ and let $b \in \R^m$.
Suppose $A = U_r \Sigma_r V_r^T$ is a reduced form of the [[singular value decomposition|000NPTMX6J]] of $A$.
Here $U_r$ is $m \times r$ and $V_r$ is $n \times r$, both with orthonormal columns, and $\Sigma_r$ is the invertible $r \times r$ diagonal matrix of the nonzero singular values.

Then the vector
$$\hat x = V_r \Sigma_r^{-1} U_r^T b$$
is a [[least squares|0BYTQ4X1HC]] solution of $Ax = b$. That is, it minimises $\norm{Ax - b}$ over $x \in \R^n$.

## Remarks

The matrix $V_r \Sigma_r^{-1} U_r^T$ is the [[Moore–Penrose inverse|0YY8VX28JR]] of $A$.
The formula works even when the columns of $A$ are dependent, where the normal equations have many solutions.
