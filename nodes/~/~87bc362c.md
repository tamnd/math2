## Summary

Let $R$ be a [[ring|~447242bf]] and let $I$ and $J$ be left [[ideals|~521b4541]] of $R$.
The sum of $I$ and $J$ is $I + J = \{a + b : a \in I, b \in J\}$.
It is a left ideal, and it is the smallest left ideal that contains $I$ and $J$.

## Remarks

The sum $I + J$ is the ideal [[generated by|~417bb915]] $I \cup J$.
In $\mathbb{Z}$, $m\mathbb{Z} + n\mathbb{Z} = d\mathbb{Z}$, where $d$ is the greatest common divisor of $m$ and $n$.
The ideals $I$ and $J$ are [[coprime|~8ebefd7b]] when $I + J = R$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

To add two ideals, take all the sums of one element from each.
In the integers, the sum of the multiples of $4$ and the multiples of $6$ is the set of the multiples of $2$.
