## Summary

This topic is a part of [[random structures and probabilistic models|~2d4dfb89]].
It covers the probabilistic theory of random permutations and partitions, with cycle structure and its Poisson limits, the Ewens family, the Chinese restaurant process and Poisson-Dirichlet laws, exchangeable partitions, and nonuniform models of random permutations.
