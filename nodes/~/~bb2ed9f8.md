## Summary

Elementary algebra and functions is the study of the algebra and the functions that a student meets from the first use of letters for numbers to the end of pre-calculus.
Elementary algebra is calculation with expressions that contain variables, together with the solution of equations and inequalities in these variables.
The elementary functions are the functions that come from the rational functions, the exponential, the logarithm and the trigonometric functions by arithmetic operations, roots, inverses and composition.
This material is the language of most later mathematics and of physics, engineering and economics.
This topic collects the elementary algebra and functions pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Numbers and arithmetic, calculus, real analysis, special functions, and fields and Galois theory have their own topics.
