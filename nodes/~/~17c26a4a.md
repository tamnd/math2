## Statement

Let $G$ be a finite simple [[planar graph|029JQEK15B]] with $n$ vertices, where $n \ge 3$. The graph $G$ need not be connected. Then $G$ has at most $3n - 6$ edges.

## Remarks

The bound is the same as for connected graphs. A disconnected planar graph can be extended by edges to a connected one, so the count does not grow beyond $3n-6$.
