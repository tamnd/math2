## Definition

A [[topological space|~77bfcc55]] $X$ is a Baire space when it has the property that follows.
Let $(F_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a [[countable|0ZG61TFBES]] family of [[closed subsets|~dfeacff3]] of $X$ with $\operatorname{int} F_n = \emptyset$ for each $n$.
Then also $\operatorname{int} \bigcup_n F_n = \emptyset$.

## Remarks

Here $\operatorname{int}$ is the [[interior|~51ce20fc]].
Equivalently, in a Baire space each [[countable intersection of dense open sets is dense|~80b383a3]].
In a Baire space, each [[meagre set|~6616ba2d]] has empty interior. Thus a nonempty Baire space is not meagre in itself.
Each complete metric space is a Baire space by the [[Baire category theorem|~3a5c4a06]]. The space $\mathbb{Q}$ is not a Baire space.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a Baire space, countably many thin closed sets can never fill up an open set.
This says that the space is large. It lets you prove that some objects exist: you show that the bad cases form a small set.
