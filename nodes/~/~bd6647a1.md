## Definition

An entire function is a function $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ that is [[holomorphic|~1a187203]] on all of $\mathbb{C}$.

## Remarks

Polynomials, the [[complex exponential|~aa66b863]] and the complex [[sine and cosine|~7f553682]] are entire.
Sums, products and compositions of entire functions are entire.
An entire function is equal to one power series $\sum_{n \ge 0} a_n z^n$ that converges for every $z \in \mathbb{C}$.
By [[Liouville's theorem|~bf2debd1]], each bounded entire function is constant.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An entire function is a function that is holomorphic at every complex number, with no exceptions.
For example, $z^3 - 2z + 5$ and $e^z$ are entire, but $1/z$ is not, because it is not defined at $0$.
