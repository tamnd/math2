## Summary

An Archimedean ordered field is an [[ordered field|~b2a583aa]] that has no infinitely large and no infinitely small positive elements.
In such a field, the natural numbers are not bounded above.
The fields $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ are Archimedean.
