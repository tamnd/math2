## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers modular forms for GL2 over totally real fields and over imaginary quadratic fields, with the Hilbert modular group and its forms, Hilbert modular varieties, lifts from the rational numbers, special cycles, and Bianchi modular forms on hyperbolic 3-space.
