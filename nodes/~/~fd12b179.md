## Statement

Let $F$ be a [[finite field|~3adaef07]].
Then the [[characteristic|~1e7ebd1c]] of $F$ is a prime number $p$, and $|F| = p^n$ with $n = [F : \mathbb{F}_p]$.

## Remarks

The proof: a finite field has nonzero characteristic, and [[the characteristic of a domain is $0$ or prime|~e8defa3b]]. The [[prime subfield|~9ecb3f42]] is $\mathbb{F}_p$, and $F$ is a vector space of dimension $n$ over it, so it has $p^n$ elements.
So there is no field with $6$ or $10$ elements.
The converse, that a field of order $p^n$ exists for each $n \ge 1$, is the [[existence theorem|~19874e82]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A finite field is a vector space over the field with $p$ elements.
A vector space of dimension $n$ over it has $p^n$ elements.
So the size of a finite field is always a power of a prime.
