## Summary

A quadratic form on a vector space is a map $Q$ to the scalars with $Q(av) = a^2 Q(v)$.
Also, $Q(u + v) - Q(u) - Q(v)$ must be bilinear in $u$ and $v$.
In coordinates, it is a homogeneous polynomial of degree two.
