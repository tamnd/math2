## Summary

This topic is a part of [[elementary number theory|~2dce2207]].
It covers the integers that are sums of two, three or four squares, the identities behind these results, the primes that a form x squared plus n times y squared represents, and the quadratic equations of Pythagoras, Legendre and Markov.
