## Proof

We prove (1) $\Rightarrow$ (2) $\Rightarrow$ (1), then (1) $\Rightarrow$ (3) $\Rightarrow$ (1), and finally (3) $\Leftrightarrow$ (4).

### Conditions 1 and 2

1. (1) $\Rightarrow$ (2). If $M \preceq N$, then every formula with parameters in $M$ has the same truth value in $M$ and $N$, in particular every existential formula.
2. (2) $\Rightarrow$ (1). Let $M \subseteq N$ be models of $T$. We first show: there is $M_1$ with $N \subseteq M_1$ and $M \preceq M_1$.
3. By the [[diagram lemmas|EP5E317TNW]], it suffices that $\mathrm{ElDiag}(M) \cup \mathrm{Diag}(N)$ is consistent. By [[compactness|1NR3HAWG9D]] it suffices to satisfy finitely many sentences of $\mathrm{Diag}(N)$, joined into one $\chi(\bar a, \bar b)$ with $\bar a$ in $M$ and $\bar b$ in $N \setminus M$.
4. The formula $\exists \bar y \, \chi(\bar a, \bar y)$ is existential and true in $N$. By (2) it is true in $M$, so $M$ with suitable values for $\bar b$ satisfies this finite part.
5. $M_1$ is a model of $T$, since $M \preceq M_1$. So $N \subseteq M_1$ is again an inclusion of models, and the first step gives $N_1$ with $M_1 \subseteq N_1$ and $N \preceq N_1$.
6. Repeating, we get $M = M_0 \subseteq N_0 \subseteq M_1 \subseteq N_1 \subseteq \cdots$ with $N_0 = N$, $M_i \preceq M_{i+1}$ and $N_i \preceq N_{i+1}$.
7. The two chains have the same union $U$. By the [[elementary chain theorem|NHCRZ74JHH]], $M \preceq U$ and $N \preceq U$.
8. For $\bar a$ in $M$ and any formula $\varphi$: $M \models \varphi(\bar a)$ iff $U \models \varphi(\bar a)$ iff $N \models \varphi(\bar a)$. So $M \preceq N$.

### Condition 3 from condition 1

Assume (1), and fix $\varphi(\bar x)$. Add new constants $\bar c$.

1. Let $\Gamma(\bar c)$ be the set of universal $\psi(\bar c)$ with $T \models \forall \bar x \, (\varphi \to \psi)$. We claim that $T \cup \Gamma(\bar c) \models \varphi(\bar c)$.
2. Suppose not, and let $M \models T \cup \Gamma(\bar c) \cup \set{\neg \varphi(\bar c)}$. We show that $T \cup \mathrm{Diag}(M) \cup \set{\varphi(\bar c)}$ is consistent.
3. Otherwise, by compactness, $T \models \varphi(\bar c) \to \neg \delta(\bar c, \bar d)$, with $\delta$ a conjunction of sentences of $\mathrm{Diag}(M)$ and $\bar d$ constants for other elements of $M$.
4. The constants $\bar d$ do not occur in $T$ or $\varphi$, so $T \models \varphi(\bar c) \to \forall \bar y \, \neg \delta(\bar c, \bar y)$. Then $\forall \bar y \, \neg \delta(\bar c, \bar y) \in \Gamma$.
5. So $M \models \forall \bar y \, \neg \delta(\bar c, \bar y)$, which contradicts $M \models \delta(\bar c, \bar d)$.
6. Let $N \models T \cup \mathrm{Diag}(M) \cup \set{\varphi(\bar c)}$. By the diagram lemmas, $M$ embeds into $N$, and we identify $M$ with its image.
7. By (1), $M \preceq N$, so $M \models \varphi(\bar c)$. This contradicts the choice of $M$, and the claim holds.
8. By compactness, finitely many $\psi_1, \dots, \psi_m \in \Gamma$ give $T \models \bigwedge_i \psi_i(\bar c) \to \varphi(\bar c)$. A conjunction of universal formulas is equivalent to a universal formula $\psi$.
9. As $\bar c$ is new, $T \models \forall \bar x \, (\psi \leftrightarrow \varphi)$. So $\varphi$ is equivalent to a universal formula.

### Conditions 3 and 4

1. (3) $\Rightarrow$ (1). Let $M \subseteq N$ be models and $\bar a$ in $M$. Universal formulas go down from $N$ to $M$, by [[this result|C9494XYEMN]].
2. If $N \models \varphi(\bar a)$, the universal equivalent of $\varphi$ goes down, so $M \models \varphi(\bar a)$.
3. If $N \models \neg\varphi(\bar a)$, the same argument for $\neg \varphi$ gives $M \models \neg \varphi(\bar a)$. So $M \preceq N$.
4. (3) $\Rightarrow$ (4). Let $\forall \bar y \, \chi$ be a universal equivalent of $\neg \varphi$. Then $\varphi$ is equivalent to $\exists \bar y \, \neg \chi$, which is existential.
5. (4) $\Rightarrow$ (3). In the same way, an existential equivalent $\exists \bar y \, \chi$ of $\neg \varphi$ gives the universal equivalent $\forall \bar y \, \neg \chi$ of $\varphi$.

## Idea

The direction (2) $\Rightarrow$ (1) builds two interleaved chains, one of elementary extensions of $M$ and one of $N$, with the same union.
The direction (1) $\Rightarrow$ (3) is the compactness argument of the Łoś-Tarski theorem: a formula preserved by passing to submodels is equivalent to a universal formula.
The rest is the preservation of universal formulas by substructures and the duality between $\exists$ and $\forall$ under negation.

## Variants

The [[relative form of the Łoś-Tarski theorem|9RN0KYXNT1]], for formulas modulo $T$, gives (1) $\Rightarrow$ (3) in one line.
For complete $T$ one can work inside a monster model, where (2) says that every small model is existentially closed in the monster.
