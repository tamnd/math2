## Summary

Let $U \subset \C^n$ be open and let $X$ be a subvariety of $U$, as in [[a complex analytic variety|GB7M582M5Y]].
Each [[regular point|ZW5A88PCPK]] $q$ of $X$ has a manifold dimension $\dim_q X$.

The dimension of $X$ at any point $p \in X$ is the largest integer $k$ with this property: every open neighbourhood of $p$ contains a regular point $q$ of $X$ with $\dim_q X = k$.

The dimension of the whole of $X$ is the largest value of $\dim_p X$ over the regular points $p$ of $X$.

The dimension of a germ $(X,p)$ is the dimension at $p$ of any representative of the germ.

The variety $X$ has pure dimension $k$ when $\dim_p X = k$ at every regular point $p$.
A germ $(X,p)$ has pure dimension $k$ when some representative of it has pure dimension $k$.

The codimension of $X$ is $n - \dim X$, the ambient dimension minus the dimension of $X$.
