## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|~9807873f]].
It covers the cohomology rings of finite groups, periodic cohomology, cohomological dimension and its theorems, and the cohomology theories of groups that use bounded cochains or constructions of Tate type for infinite groups.
