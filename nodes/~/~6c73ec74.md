## Statement

The [[natural logarithm|~317c1d19]] is strictly [[concave|~269ffe14]] on $(0, \infty)$.
That is, $\ln(t x + (1 - t) y) > t \ln x + (1 - t) \ln y$ for all $x \ne y$ in $(0, \infty)$ and $t \in (0, 1)$.

## Remarks

The [[derivative of $\ln x$|~275c907b]] is $1/x$, and its second derivative is $-1/x^2 < 0$.
The concavity gives the [[AM–GM inequality|~b0382275]] through Jensen's inequality.
Mathlib states it as `strictConcaveOn_log_Ioi`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The graph of $\ln x$ bends downward everywhere.
Every chord of the graph lies below the graph.
