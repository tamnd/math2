## Summary

The curl of a differentiable [[vector field|~edfef10c]] in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is a vector field that measures the rotation of the field at each point.
Its direction is the axis of the strongest rotation, by the right-hand rule, and its length measures the rate of rotation.
