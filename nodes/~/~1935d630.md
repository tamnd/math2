## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]].
A vertex cover of $G$ is a set $S$ of vertices of $G$ that contains at least one end of each edge.

## Remarks

A set $S$ is a vertex cover if and only if its complement is an [[independent set|~7e245cc8]].
For the [[complete bipartite graph|~13c6e22b]] $K_{m,n}$, the least size of a vertex cover is $\min\{m, n\}$.
Each vertex of a cover meets at most one edge of a [[matching|~4a67f6e1]]. Thus every vertex cover has at least as many vertices as any matching has edges.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A vertex cover is a set of vertices that touches every edge.
Think of guards at crossings: each street must have a guard at one of its two ends.
