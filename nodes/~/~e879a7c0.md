## Summary

The central binomial coefficients $\binom{2n}{n}$ are the middle entries of the even rows of Pascal's triangle. They count balanced lattice paths and appear in the formula for Catalan numbers. The node [[central binomial coefficient|~c9d56707]] gives the precise definition.
