## Statement

Consider the relation $\cong$ on the set of [[ordered pairs|0WFCNG0MZC]] of [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]] used in the [[construction of the integers|0NS58JP68E]]. It is given by $(a, b) \cong (c, d)$ when $a + d = b + c$.

This relation is a [[reflexive relation|0X8NMST0J2]]. That is, $(a, b) \cong (a, b)$ for every pair $(a, b)$.

## Remarks

The proof is one line. For $(c, d) = (a, b)$ the condition reads $a + b = b + a$, which holds because addition is commutative.

This is one of three parts of the check that $\cong$ is an equivalence relation. The other two parts are symmetry and transitivity.
