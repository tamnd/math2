## Statement

Let $F$ be an [[Archimedean ordered field|~cf990382]] and let $x$ be an element of $F$.
Then there is a natural number $n$ with $x < n$.

## Remarks

Proof: apply the definition with $y = 1$.
Thus the natural numbers are not [[bounded above|~98a1bdde]] in $F$.
The [[real numbers are Archimedean|~37d4f574]], thus this holds for each real number $x$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

No element of an Archimedean field is larger than all the natural numbers.
For example, for the real number $x = 10^{6} + 0.5$, the natural number $n = 10^{6} + 1$ is larger.
