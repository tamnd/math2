## Summary

Let $a$ be algebraic over a field $K$.
The minimal polynomial of $a$ over $K$ is the monic polynomial of smallest degree in $K[x]$ that has $a$ as a root.
The [[definition of the minimal polynomial|~fe14474d]] states it.
