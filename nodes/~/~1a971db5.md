## Statement

Let $n \ge 2$, let $U \subseteq \C^n$ be an [[open set|02BH3MCBQB]], and let $p$ be a point of $U$.
Then every [[holomorphic function|507C34W0ZT]] on $U \setminus \set{p}$ is the restriction of a holomorphic function on $U$.

## Remarks

The open set $U$ need not be connected. The claim reduces to the component of $U$ that contains $p$, and this case follows from [[Hartogs's extension theorem|FSGES670AZ]] with the compact set $\set{p}$.

The hypothesis $n \ge 2$ is needed: $1/z$ is holomorphic on $\C \setminus \set{0}$ and has no extension to $\C$.
