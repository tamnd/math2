## Definition

Let $\sum a_n$ be a [[series|~6612f89e]] of real numbers with [[partial sums|~50195ca2]] $s_N = \sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n$.
The series is convergent if the sequence $(s_N)$ is a [[convergent sequence|~d628ad87]].

## Remarks

The series $\sum 1/2^n$ converges, since its partial sums $2 - 1/2^N$ tend to $2$.
The limit of the partial sums is the [[sum of the series|~a213c9c0]].
A series that does not converge is a [[divergent series|~00e906fa]].
Changing finitely many terms does not change whether a series converges. It can change the sum.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A series converges when its running totals approach a fixed number.
Adding more and more terms then changes the total less and less.
