## Statement

Let $a > 0$ and $L > 0$. Consider the [[wave equation|5EHD2XBQDG]] $y_{tt} = a^2 y_{xx}$ for $0 < x < L$ and $t > 0$.
Impose the [[Dirichlet boundary condition|EETVA6QYX4]] $y(0,t) = y(L,t) = 0$ for $t > 0$.
Prescribe the initial position $y(x,0) = f(x)$ and the initial velocity $y_t(x,0) = g(x)$ for $0 < x < L$.

Suppose that both data have [[sine series|Y6EPBG6S8G]] on $(0, L)$:
$$f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty c_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right), \qquad g(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right).$$

Then the solution of the problem is given by the series
$$y(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)\left[ c_n \cos\left(\frac{n\pi a t}{L}\right) + b_n \frac{L}{n\pi a} \sin\left(\frac{n\pi a t}{L}\right) \right].$$

This is the sum of two solutions. One has initial position $f$ and initial velocity $0$. The other has initial position $0$ and initial velocity $g$.
