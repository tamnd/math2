## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical biology and chemistry|~d1bb5fbe]].
It covers models of the shape of RNA, proteins and DNA, with the combinatorics of secondary structure and folding, lattice models of proteins, the elastic and statistical geometry of DNA, and the symmetry of viruses.
