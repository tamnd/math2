## Summary

Two ideals of a ring are coprime when their sum is the whole ring.
For the integers, the ideals $(m)$ and $(n)$ are coprime exactly when $m$ and $n$ are coprime numbers.
The [[definition of coprime ideals|~8ebefd7b]] gives the condition.
