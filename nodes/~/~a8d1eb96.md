## Definition

Let $F$ be a field, let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over $F$, and let $T \colon V \to W$ be a [[linear map|~2402e937]].
The *rank* of $T$ is
$$\rank T = \dim \im T,$$
the [[dimension|~3d1088d4]] of the [[image|~d0d621a8]] of $T$.

## Remarks

For the map $x \mapsto Ax$ of a matrix $A$, the rank is the [[rank of the matrix|~802904f9]].
The rank satisfies $\rank T \le \dim V$ and $\rank T \le \dim W$.
The [[rank–nullity theorem|~dbabb598]] relates the rank and the [[nullity|~40113303]].
The rank of a composition satisfies $\rank(T \circ S) \le \min(\rank T, \rank S)$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The rank of a linear map counts the dimensions of its output.
A map from space onto a plane has rank $2$.
The zero map has rank $0$.
