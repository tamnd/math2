This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers the Jacobson radical and the arguments of Nakayama type in noncommutative rings, nil and nilpotent ideals and the Köthe problem, general radical theory, and the structure of prime, semiprime and primitive rings with the density theorem.
