## Statement

Take a [[linear system|0GT4WWGWH2]] and form its [[augmented matrix|0T6MWGZSXA]].
The system is [[inconsistent|0X6V9B1V10]] exactly when the rightmost column of that matrix holds a [[pivot|0J0BBCMJ5W]] position.
So the system has at least one solution exactly when the rightmost column has no pivot position.
