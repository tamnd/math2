## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers the classes of nonassociative algebras that identities other than the Lie, Jordan and alternative laws define: Malcev, Lie-admissible, pre-Lie, Novikov, Leibniz, Zinbiel, dendriform, Akivis and structurable algebras, and the genetic algebras of population biology.
