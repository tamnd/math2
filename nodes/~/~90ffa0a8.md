## Definition

A forest is an [[acyclic|~d867bc00]] [[graph|~3f82cef4]].

## Remarks

The connected components of a forest are [[trees|~29e53124]]. Thus a forest is a disjoint union of trees.
Every tree is a forest. The graph with no edges on $n$ vertices is a forest with $n$ components.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A forest is a graph with no cycles, and it may be in several pieces.
Each piece is a tree, as the name suggests.
