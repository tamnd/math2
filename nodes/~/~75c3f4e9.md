## Summary

A binary tree is a recursive structure: it is empty, or it is a root with an ordered pair of binary trees as left and right subtrees. Binary trees are a basic data structure, and they are counted by the Catalan numbers. The node [[binary tree|~ed6d9b86]] gives the precise definition.
