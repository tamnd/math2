This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the differentiation of modular forms, with the Serre derivative, Ramanujan's differential equations, quasimodular and nearly holomorphic forms, Rankin-Cohen brackets and Eichler integrals.
