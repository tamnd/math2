## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]] and let $e$ be an edge of $G$.
Let $G - e$ be the graph that $G$ gives when $e$ is deleted and all vertices are kept.
The edge $e$ is a bridge of $G$ if $G - e$ has more [[connected components|~b47736e3]] than $G$.

## Remarks

Let $e = uv$. Then $e$ is a bridge if and only if $u$ and $v$ are not [[reachable|~fb98409f]] from each other in $G - e$. In this case, deleting $e$ increases the number of components by exactly one.
In a [[tree|~29e53124]] every edge is a bridge. In a [[cycle graph|~19143ce5]] no edge is a bridge.
[[Bridges are the edges on no cycle|~f31c8c57]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A bridge is an edge that holds the graph together: delete it, and one piece breaks into two.
Think of the only road between two towns.
