## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers modular forms for the symplectic group on the Siegel upper half-space, with Fourier-Jacobi expansions, Eisenstein and theta series, the structure theory in genus 2, Hecke theory and spinor L-functions, lifts, and the paramodular conjecture.
