## Summary

The elementary symmetric polynomials in $n$ variables are the sums of all products of $k$ distinct variables, for $k = 0, \dots, n$.
They generate all symmetric polynomials.
The [[definition of the elementary symmetric polynomials|~71a32e63]] gives the formula.
