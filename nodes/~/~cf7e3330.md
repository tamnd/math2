## Definition

Let $G$ and $H$ be [[groups|G5S1M9]] and let $\varphi \colon G \to H$ be a [[group homomorphism|~c666c876]].
The image of $\varphi$ is the set $\operatorname{im} \varphi = \varphi(G) = \{\varphi(g) : g \in G\}$.

## Remarks

The image is a [[subgroup|~b600248b]] of $H$, because $\varphi(a) \varphi(b)^{-1} = \varphi(ab^{-1})$.
The homomorphism $\varphi$ is surjective exactly when $\operatorname{im} \varphi = H$.
The image of the map $n \mapsto 2^n$ from $\mathbb{Z}$ to $\mathbb{Q}^\times$ is the set of integer powers of $2$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The image is the part of the target group that the homomorphism actually reaches.
It is always a group of its own, inside the target.
The [[first isomorphism theorem|~6f634155]] says that the image looks like a quotient of the source group.
