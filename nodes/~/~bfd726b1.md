## Statement

The [[complex exponential|~aa66b863]] $\exp : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is an [[entire function|~bd6647a1]]: it is complex differentiable at each $z \in \mathbb{C}$.

## Remarks

Proof: $\exp$ is given by a power series with infinite radius of convergence, and [[power series are holomorphic in their disc of convergence|~e89eb384]].
Its [[derivative|~4217b303]] is $\exp$ itself.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The exponential function has a complex derivative at every point of the plane.
So it is a holomorphic function without any exceptional points.
