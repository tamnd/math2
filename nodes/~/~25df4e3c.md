## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homotopy theory|~0064e77b]].
It covers the homotopy theory of spaces and spectra with a group action, from G-CW complexes and Elmendorf's theorem to genuine G-spectra, Mackey functors and the slice spectral sequence.
