## Definition

We use the formulas of MLL without units: atoms, negated atoms, $\otimes$ and $\mathbin{\wp}$, with the one-sided rules of [[linear logic|H408H4WHBA]].
A *proof structure* is a finite set of formula occurrences with a finite set of *links* of four types.

1. An axiom link has no premise and two conclusions $A$ and $A^{\perp}$.
2. A cut link has two premises $A$ and $A^{\perp}$ and no conclusion.
3. A $\otimes$ link has premises $A$ and $B$ and conclusion $A \otimes B$.
4. A $\mathbin{\wp}$ link has premises $A$ and $B$ and conclusion $A \mathbin{\wp} B$.

Each occurrence is the conclusion of exactly one link and the premise of at most one link.
The occurrences that are the premise of no link are the *conclusions* of the structure.

Each derivation $\pi$ of $\vdash \Gamma$ in MLL without units has a *translation* $N(\pi)$, a proof structure with conclusions $\Gamma$.

1. An axiom $\vdash A^{\perp}, A$ translates to one axiom link.
2. A $\otimes$ rule on $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$ translates to the disjoint union of $N(\pi_1)$ and $N(\pi_2)$ with a new $\otimes$ link on the two active conclusions.
3. A $\mathbin{\wp}$ rule on $\pi$ translates to $N(\pi)$ with a new $\mathbin{\wp}$ link on the two active conclusions.
4. A cut on $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$ translates to the disjoint union of $N(\pi_1)$ and $N(\pi_2)$ with a new cut link on the two cut formulas.

A *proof net* is a proof structure that is the translation of some derivation.

A *switching* of a proof structure chooses, for each $\mathbin{\wp}$ link, one of its two premises.
Its *correction graph* is the undirected graph with the occurrences as vertices and these edges:

1. for each axiom link and each cut link, an edge between its two occurrences;
2. for each $\otimes$ link, an edge from its conclusion to each premise;
3. for each $\mathbin{\wp}$ link, an edge from its conclusion to the chosen premise only.

## Notation

Many texts draw links as small labeled boxes.
We write an edge between occurrences $x$ and $y$ as $x - y$.

## Remarks

A proof structure with $k$ links of type $\mathbin{\wp}$ has $2^k$ switchings.
Units are left out because, as the SEP entry reports, MLL with units has no satisfactory notion of proof net.
The [[Danos-Regnier theorem|~0f57c82a]] characterizes proof nets by their correction graphs.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A proof structure is a drawing of formulas joined by lines.
A proof net is a drawing that comes from a real proof.
A switching cuts one of the two lines above each "par", and we look at what is left.

### L2 human

We list the switchings and correction graphs of the net of $\vdash p \mathbin{\wp} p^{\perp}$.

1. The structure has an axiom link between $p$ and $p^{\perp}$ and a $\mathbin{\wp}$ link with conclusion $P = p \mathbin{\wp} p^{\perp}$.
2. Switching 1 keeps the edge $P - p$.
   The edges are $p - p^{\perp}$ and $P - p$, a path on three vertices.
3. Switching 2 keeps the edge $P - p^{\perp}$.
   The edges are $p - p^{\perp}$ and $P - p^{\perp}$, also a path.
4. Both correction graphs are trees, as the Danos-Regnier theorem requires.

The typical use of correction graphs is to test a proof structure without a search for a derivation.
A trap: if the $\mathbin{\wp}$ link is replaced by a $\otimes$ link, both edges stay and the graph has a cycle.
