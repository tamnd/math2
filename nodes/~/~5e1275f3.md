## Summary

The history of mathematics studies what people knew of mathematics in the past, who found it, and how it was written, taught and passed on.
Its sources are documents such as clay tablets, papyri, manuscripts and printed books, and historians read each source in its own language and context.
The history shows where the concepts and notations of present mathematics come from, and it shows that many cultures made mathematics.
Eudemus of Rhodes wrote a history of geometry in the fourth century BCE, and Montucla published a large history of mathematics in 1758.
This topic collects the history of mathematics pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Mathematics and culture and the philosophy of mathematics have their own topics.
