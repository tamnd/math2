## Statement

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $M \subset \C^n$ be a compact hypersurface that is [[real analytic|Z7H98E1NHP]].
Then no point $q \in M$ carries a [[germ of an analytic set|Q9B74BYZ8C]] $(X,q)$ of positive dimension that lies inside the germ $(M,q)$.

In other words, a compact real-analytic hypersurface of $\C^n$ contains no complex analytic curve or higher dimensional complex analytic set, even locally.

## Remarks

The result is due to Diederich and Fornæss.
Compactness is needed: a real hyperplane in $\C^n$ with $n \ge 2$ contains complex hyperplanes.
Real analyticity is needed as well, since smooth compact hypersurfaces can contain complex discs.
