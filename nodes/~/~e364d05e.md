## Definition

Let $G$ be a finite [[graph|~3f82cef4]] with a nonempty vertex set $V$.
The minimum degree of $G$ is
$$\delta(G) = \min_{v \in V} \deg(v),$$
the smallest [[degree|~939465ed]] of a vertex of $G$.

## Remarks

The [[maximum degree|~41541976]] $\Delta(G)$ is the largest degree. Always $\delta(G) \le \Delta(G)$.
Each [[cycle graph|~19143ce5]] has $\delta = 2$, and each tree with at least two vertices has $\delta = 1$.
A condition on $\delta(G)$ gives a Hamiltonian cycle in [[Dirac's theorem|~7231cc59]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The minimum degree is the smallest number of edges at any vertex.
If $\delta(G) = 3$, then each vertex has at least three neighbours.
