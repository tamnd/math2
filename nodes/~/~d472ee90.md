## Summary

A bounded sequence is a real sequence whose terms all lie in one fixed interval. Boundedness is a necessary condition for convergence, and it is the hypothesis of the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem. The node [[bounded sequence|~0a464768]] gives the precise definition.
