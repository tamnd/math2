## Statement

Let $G$ be a [[solvable group|~d517865e]] and let $\varphi \colon G \to K$ be a surjective [[group homomorphism|~c666c876]].
Then $K$ is solvable.
In particular, $G/N$ is solvable for each [[normal subgroup|~ebf813cb]] $N$ of $G$.

## Remarks

A proof uses the [[derived series|~fc177e14]]: $\varphi(G^{(i)}) = K^{(i)}$ for each $i$, because $\varphi$ maps commutators onto commutators.
So $G^{(n)} = \{e\}$ implies $K^{(n)} = \{e\}$.
The second part follows with the projection $G \to G/N$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A surjective homomorphism sends the commutators of $G$ onto the commutators of $K$.
So the derived series of $K$ is the image of the derived series of $G$.
If one reaches the identity, the image does too.
