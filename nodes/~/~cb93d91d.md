## Summary

Sine and cosine are the basic trigonometric functions. For an angle $x$, the point $(\cos x, \sin x)$ lies on the unit circle, and the two functions are periodic with period $2\pi$. The node [[sine and cosine|~45dd0733]] gives the precise definition by power series.
