## Definition

Let $L$ be a [[field|~92ce2090]] and let $H$ be a group of ring automorphisms of $L$.
The fixed field of $H$ is
$$L^H = \{ a \in L : \sigma(a) = a \text{ for every } \sigma \in H \}.$$

## Remarks

The set $L^H$ is a [[subfield|~00a26465]] of $L$, because each $\sigma \in H$ respects the field operations.
For $L = \mathbb{C}$ and $H = \{\mathrm{id}, \sigma\}$, with $\sigma$ complex conjugation, $L^H = \mathbb{R}$.
If $H$ is finite, then $[L : L^H] = |H|$ and $L/L^H$ is a [[Galois extension|~aaec2530]], by a theorem of Emil Artin.
More generally, a group acting on a ring has a fixed subring, the ring of invariants.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The fixed field is the part of the field that a group of symmetries does not move.
In the [[fundamental theorem of Galois theory|~c4e91ea3]], fixed fields turn subgroups into intermediate fields.
