## Definition

A tree is a [[graph|~3f82cef4]] that is [[connected|~c1305259]] and [[acyclic|~d867bc00]].

## Remarks

Let $T$ be a finite graph with $n \ge 1$ vertices. Then these conditions are equivalent: $T$ is a tree; $T$ is connected with $n - 1$ edges; $T$ is acyclic with $n - 1$ edges.
A graph is a tree if and only if any two vertices are joined by exactly one path. The node [[unique paths in trees|~43015c6d]] states this.
The [[path graphs|~c147bff4]] and the stars $K_{1,n}$ are trees.
