## Statement

Let $n \geq 3$.
The [[dihedral group|~42ac19c1]] $D_n$ has [[order|~89358ce0]] $2n$.

## Remarks

A symmetry of the regular $n$-gon is determined by the image of one vertex and by whether it keeps or reverses the orientation.
There are $n$ choices for the image of the vertex and $2$ choices for the orientation.
For example, $D_3$ has order $6$ and $D_4$ has order $8$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A symmetry can send a fixed vertex to any of the $n$ vertices.
After that, the polygon can be face up or face down.
So there are $n \cdot 2 = 2n$ symmetries.
