## Statement

Let $T$ be a [[tree|~29e53124]].
Then $T$ is [[bipartite|~729625d7]].

## Remarks

Proof: fix a vertex $r$. Put a vertex in $A$ if its [[distance|~bc6ed22e]] to $r$ is even, and in $B$ if it is odd. The ends of an edge have distances that differ by $1$.
It also follows from the theorem that [[a graph is bipartite iff it has no odd cycle|~547b729a]], because a tree has no cycle at all.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Colour the vertices of a tree by levels, from a chosen root: even levels white, odd levels black.
Each edge goes between neighbouring levels, so it joins a white vertex and a black vertex.
