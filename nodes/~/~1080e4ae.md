## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]].
A trail in $G$ is a [[walk|~8945d13d]] $(w_0, w_1, \dots, w_k)$ whose edges $w_0 w_1, w_1 w_2, \dots, w_{k-1} w_k$ are pairwise distinct.

## Remarks

A trail may visit a vertex more than once, but it uses each edge at most once.
Every [[path|~ab8676be]] is a trail. In the graph of two triangles that share a vertex $c$, the closed walk $a, b, c, d, e, c, a$ is a trail but not a path.
An [[Eulerian trail|~1455a470]] is a trail that uses every edge.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A trail is a walk that never uses the same edge twice.
It is the kind of route that you draw without lifting your pen and without retracing a line.
