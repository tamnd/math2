## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|~a5df4ed5]].
It covers bilinear and sesquilinear forms over any field: their matrices, congruence, nondegeneracy, orthogonality, symmetric and alternating forms, symplectic spaces, and Witt's theorem.
