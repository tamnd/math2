## Statement

Let $n \geq 5$.
Then the [[symmetric group|~f6704bbc]] $S_n$ is not [[solvable|~d517865e]].

## Remarks

The group $A_n$ is [[simple|~bebb971d]] and not abelian, so its commutator subgroup is $A_n$, and the derived series of $A_n$ is constant.
So $A_n$ is not solvable, and then $S_n$ is not solvable, because [[subgroups of solvable groups are solvable|~fda9137c]].
The groups $S_1, S_2, S_3$ and $S_4$ are solvable.
By Galois theory, this is the reason why the general polynomial equation of degree $n \geq 5$ is not solvable by radicals.

## Explanations

### L1 human

From five letters on, the even permutations form a simple group that is not abelian.
Such a group cannot be cut into abelian layers.
So the full symmetric group cannot be cut into abelian layers either.
