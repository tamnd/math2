## Statement

Let $W$ be a subspace of $\R^m$. Let $w_1, \ldots, w_n$ be an orthogonal basis of $W$. This means that the $w_i$ are nonzero, they are pairwise orthogonal, and they span $W$.

Let $b \in \R^m$. Let $\hat b$ be the [[orthogonal projection|0B8KZRT28M]] of $b$ onto $W$.

Then $\hat b$ is the sum over $i = 1, \ldots, n$ of the vectors $\frac{b \cdot w_i}{w_i \cdot w_i}\, w_i$, where $\cdot$ is the [[dot product|0S52TNNTH9]].

That is,
$$\hat b = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{b \cdot w_i}{w_i \cdot w_i}\, w_i.$$

## Remarks

Each term is the projection of $b$ onto the line spanned by $w_i$. The projection onto $W$ is the sum of these projections, because the $w_i$ are orthogonal.
