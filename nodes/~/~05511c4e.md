## Definition

Let $K$ and $L$ be [[fields|~92ce2090]].
The field $L$ is an extension of $K$ if $K$ is a [[subfield|~00a26465]] of $L$.
One writes $L/K$ for this field extension.

## Remarks

Often one also calls $L$ an extension of $K$ when there is an injective [[ring homomorphism|~8febae30]] $K \to L$. Then one identifies $K$ with its image.
An extension $L/K$ makes $L$ a vector space over $K$. Its dimension is the [[degree of the extension|~dede2e51]].
Examples: $\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})/\mathbb{Q}$.
The notation $L/K$ does not mean a quotient.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A field extension is a bigger field that contains a given field.
For example, the complex numbers extend the real numbers by an element $i$ with $i^2 = -1$.
Field theory studies how the bigger field is built from the smaller one.
