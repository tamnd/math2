This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers integral formulas with explicit kernels, multidimensional residues, and the function theory of the ball and of strongly pseudoconvex domains, with Hardy spaces, boundary values and function algebras.
