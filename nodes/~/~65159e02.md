## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|~9807873f]].
It covers the K-groups of rings of integers, Borel's computation of their ranks and the regulators, the Lichtenbaum, Birch-Tate and Beilinson conjectures on special values, and the Bloch group with polylogarithms.
