## Summary

This topic is a part of [[operator algebras and noncommutative geometry|~d4b9579e]].
It covers Connes' noncommutative geometry, with spectral triples as noncommutative Riemannian spin manifolds, the Dixmier trace, the local index formula and the Chern character, and the applications to physics and number theory.
