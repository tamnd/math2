## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|~d000caef]].
It covers the molecular explanation of the properties of gases, the Maxwell distribution, mean free path and transport coefficients, the paradoxes of irreversibility, the BBGKY hierarchy and Lanford's theorem, and the methods that lead from kinetic to fluid equations.
