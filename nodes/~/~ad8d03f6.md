## Summary

Mathematical software and formalization is the study of mathematics done with computers.
A proof assistant is a program that checks each step of a formal proof against the rules of a logic.
Computers now check long proofs, search large sets of finite cases, compute with symbols, and store large collections of mathematical data.
Automated theorem proving began in the 1950s, de Bruijn's Automath system of the late 1960s started the machine checking of formal proofs, and Appel and Haken proved the four colour theorem with a computer in 1976.
This topic collects the math2 pages on proof assistants and formal libraries, landmark formalizations, automated theorem proving, SAT and SMT solving, computer-assisted proof, computer algebra, experimental mathematics, mathematical databases, knowledge management, typesetting, and artificial intelligence in mathematics.
Type theory and constructive mathematics, mathematical logic, theory of computation, and the mathematics of machine learning have their own topics.
