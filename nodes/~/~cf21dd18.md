## Statement

Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be any [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]]. Then three things hold:

1. $x + y = y + x$.
2. $x + (y + z) = (x + y) + z$.
3. If $x + y = x + z$, then $y = z$.

## Remarks

The first two parts say that the order and the grouping of a sum do not change its value. The third part says that an integer can be cancelled from both sides of an equation of sums.
