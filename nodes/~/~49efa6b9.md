## Statement

Let $F$ be a field, let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $F$, and let $T \colon V \to V$ be a linear map with [[characteristic polynomial|~fab11e9c]] $p_T$.
Then $p_T(T) = 0$, where $p_T(T)$ is the [[value of $p_T$ at $T$|~818f89ba]].

## Remarks

It follows from the [[Cayley–Hamilton theorem for matrices|~c462ba29]] applied to the matrix of $T$ in a basis.
The map $p \mapsto p([T]_B)$ corresponds to $p \mapsto p(T)$ under $T \mapsto [T]_B$.
The statement also holds for endomorphisms of finitely generated modules over a commutative ring, with a suitable characteristic polynomial.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The theorem does not depend on a basis.
A linear map satisfies its characteristic polynomial, because each of its matrices does.
