## Summary

The falling factorial $(x)_k$ is the product of $k$ consecutive descending factors that start at $x$. It counts ordered selections without repetition, and it is a basis of the polynomials that is useful in finite difference calculus. The node [[falling factorial|~f1d2b52b]] gives the precise definition.
