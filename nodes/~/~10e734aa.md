This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|~9807873f]].
It covers the K-theory of schemes after Quillen and after Thomason and Trobaugh, Gersten's conjecture and Bloch's formula, lambda-rings and the gamma filtration, the motivic spectral sequence and étale K-theory.
