## Summary

This topic is a part of [[analytic number theory|~124efb1e]].
It covers twin primes and prime constellations, the Hardy-Littlewood conjectures, and the methods of Goldston, Pintz and Yıldırım, of Zhang and of Maynard and Tao for bounded gaps between primes.
