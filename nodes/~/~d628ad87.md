## Definition

A [[real sequence|~bf43de5c]] $(a_n)$ is convergent if it converges to some real number $L$.
Here convergence is in the sense of the [[limit of a sequence|~23896e76]].

## Remarks

The sequences $1/(n+1)$ and $(n+1)/(n+2)$ are convergent, with limits $0$ and $1$.
The constant sequence $a_n = c$ converges to $c$.
A sequence that is not convergent is a [[divergent sequence|~ae7cead2]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A convergent sequence is a sequence that has a limit.
Its terms settle down near one real number.
