## Statement

Let $X$ be a [[topological space|~77bfcc55]] and let $x \in X$.
The connected component of $x$ is the [[union|05GK32H72H]] $C(x)$ of all [[connected subsets|~6c57b623]] of $X$ that contain $x$.
Then $C(x)$ is connected and [[closed|~dfeacff3]] in $X$.

## Remarks

Proof: a union of connected sets with a common point is connected, so $C(x)$ is the largest connected set that contains $x$. The [[closure|~8d5f2dc5]] of $C(x)$ is also connected, so it equals $C(x)$.
The components of $X$ form a partition of $X$.
Components need not be open. In $\mathbb{Q}$, the components are the single points, which are not open.
If $X$ has only finitely many components, then each of them is also open.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The component of a point is the largest piece of the space that contains the point and is in one piece.
Taking the closure cannot make the piece larger, so each component is closed.
