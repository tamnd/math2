## Summary

This topic is a part of [[convex and discrete geometry|~ab4e904a]].
It covers packings and coverings of space of three or more dimensions by balls and convex bodies, the Kepler conjecture, the solutions in dimensions 8 and 24, and the bounds in high dimensions.
