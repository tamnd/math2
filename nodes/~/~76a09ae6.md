## Statement

Let $L > 0$. Let $f$ be a piecewise smooth function on the real line whose [[Fourier series|G12KXDHPPS]] with period $2L$ is
$$\frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \Big[ a_n \cos\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) + b_n \sin\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) \Big].$$

Then an [[antiderivative|0BYWRWFT4F]] $F$ of $f$ is found by integrating the series one term at a time. It has the form
$$F(t) = \frac{a_0 t}{2} + C + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \Big[ \frac{a_n L}{n\pi} \sin\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) - \frac{b_n L}{n\pi} \cos\Big(\frac{n\pi t}{L}\Big) \Big],$$
where $F' = f$ and $C$ is an arbitrary constant.

## Remarks

The linear term $\frac{a_0 t}{2}$ comes from the constant term of the series. The result gives a formula for the antiderivative, with the freedom of one additive constant.
