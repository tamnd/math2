## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the correspondences between p-adic or mod p representations of p-adic groups and of Galois groups, the Serre weight and Breuil-Mézard conjectures, completed cohomology, eigenvarieties and local-global compatibility.
