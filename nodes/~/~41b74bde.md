## Definition

Let $R$ and $S$ be [[rings|~447242bf]].
A ring isomorphism from $R$ to $S$ is a [[ring homomorphism|~8febae30]] $f \colon R \to S$ that is bijective.
The rings $R$ and $S$ are isomorphic, written $R \cong S$, if there is a ring isomorphism from $R$ to $S$.

## Remarks

The inverse map of a ring isomorphism is again a ring homomorphism.
A ring isomorphism from $R$ to $R$ is an automorphism of $R$.
Complex conjugation is an automorphism of $\mathbb{C}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Two isomorphic rings are the same ring with other names for the elements.
The isomorphism is the dictionary between the names.
