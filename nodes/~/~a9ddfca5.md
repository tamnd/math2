This topic is a part of [[symplectic, contact and Poisson geometry|~11552e10]].
It covers presymplectic, almost symplectic, locally conformally symplectic, cosymplectic, multisymplectic, orbifold and infinite-dimensional structures that weaken or extend the symplectic condition.
