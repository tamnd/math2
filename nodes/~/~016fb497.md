## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|~3656b229]].
It covers the representation theory of real semisimple and reductive Lie groups after Harish-Chandra: (g, K)-modules, characters, the principal and discrete series, the Langlands classification, the Plancherel theorem and the unitary dual.
