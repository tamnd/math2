## Proof

We prove the equalities of [[the axioms of S5|ECYRK9C2ZT]] by derivations in normal logics.
We use the derived rules of [[normal modal logic K|~b1c67ad7]]: RM (from $\vdash \alpha \to \beta$ infer $\vdash \square\alpha \to \square\beta$), RE, duality, and propositional reasoning with [[tautologies|05006AJ7DZ]].

Part A: three derived formulas.

1. In any normal logic with $\mathrm{T}$: the instance $\square\neg p \to \neg p$ and contraposition give $p \to \neg\square\neg p$, that is, $p \to \lozenge p$.
2. In any normal logic with $\mathrm{4}$: the instance $\square\neg p \to \square\square\neg p$ and contraposition give $\neg\square\square\neg p \to \neg\square\neg p$.
3. By duality and RE, $\neg\square\beta$ is equivalent to $\lozenge\neg\beta$. With $\beta = \square\neg p$, the antecedent is equivalent to $\lozenge\neg\square\neg p$, which is $\lozenge\lozenge p$.
4. The consequent $\neg\square\neg p$ is $\lozenge p$. So step 2 gives $\lozenge\lozenge p \to \lozenge p$.
5. In any normal logic with $\mathrm{5}$: the instance $\lozenge\neg p \to \square\lozenge\neg p$ and contraposition give $\neg\square\lozenge\neg p \to \neg\lozenge\neg p$.
6. By duality, $\neg\lozenge\neg p$ is equivalent to $\square p$, and $\neg\square\lozenge\neg p$ is equivalent to $\lozenge\neg\lozenge\neg p$, hence by RE to $\lozenge\square p$.
7. So step 5 gives $\lozenge\square p \to \square p$, the dual form of $\mathrm{5}$.

Part B: $\mathsf{KTB4} \subseteq \mathsf{KT5}$.

8. $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{T}$. It contains $\mathrm{B}$: chain $p \to \lozenge p$ from step 1 with the axiom $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$.
9. For $\mathrm{4}$, the instance of $\mathrm{B}$ with $\square p$ for $p$ gives $\square p \to \square\lozenge\square p$.
10. RM applied to step 7 gives $\square\lozenge\square p \to \square\square p$.
11. Chaining steps 9 and 10 gives $\square p \to \square\square p$, so $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{4}$.
12. Since $\mathsf{KTB4}$ is the smallest normal logic with $\mathrm{T}$, $\mathrm{B}$, $\mathrm{4}$, we get $\mathsf{KTB4} \subseteq \mathsf{KT5}$.

Part C: $\mathsf{KT5} \subseteq \mathsf{KTB4}$.

13. The instance of $\mathrm{B}$ with $\lozenge p$ for $p$ gives $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge\lozenge p$.
14. RM applied to step 4 gives $\square\lozenge\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$.
15. Chaining steps 13 and 14 gives $\lozenge p \to \square\lozenge p$, so $\mathsf{KTB4}$ contains $\mathrm{5}$.
16. It contains $\mathrm{T}$, so $\mathsf{KT5} \subseteq \mathsf{KTB4}$ by minimality.

Part D: the other two logics.

17. $\mathsf{KT5} \subseteq \mathsf{KT45}$ and $\mathsf{KT5} \subseteq \mathsf{KTB5}$, since the larger logics contain $\mathrm{T}$ and $\mathrm{5}$.
18. $\mathsf{KT5}$ contains $\mathrm{4}$ by step 11 and $\mathrm{B}$ by step 8, so $\mathsf{KT45} \subseteq \mathsf{KT5}$ and $\mathsf{KTB5} \subseteq \mathsf{KT5}$.
19. Parts B to D give $\mathsf{KT5} = \mathsf{KTB4} = \mathsf{KT45} = \mathsf{KTB5}$.

## Idea

The axiom $\mathrm{5}$ is $\mathrm{B}$ composed with $\mathrm{4}$ in dual form, and $\mathrm{4}$ is $\mathrm{B}$ composed with $\mathrm{5}$ in dual form.
The axiom $\mathrm{T}$ is used only to get $\mathrm{B}$ from $\mathrm{5}$, through $p \to \lozenge p$.

## Variants

1. A semantic proof: by [[the completeness theorem|~1d9447ce]] and its method, the four logics are complete for four classes of reflexive frames. These classes are the reflexive frames that are Euclidean, symmetric and transitive, transitive and Euclidean, and symmetric and Euclidean.
2. These four classes coincide, since each is the class of [[equivalence relations|0G8VEZ3WTK]]; by completeness the logics coincide too.
