## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|~3656b229]].
It covers infinite-dimensional highest weight modules of semisimple Lie algebras: Verma modules, the category O of Bernstein, Gelfand and Gelfand with its structure, the Kazhdan-Lusztig conjecture, and the functors that act on this category.
