## Definition

An [[algebraic extension|~2c8cc802]] $L/K$ is a Galois extension if it is [[normal|~43fb03bd]] and [[separable|~6b50578f]].

## Remarks

For a finite extension $L/K$, these conditions are equivalent.

1. $L/K$ is Galois.
2. $L$ is the splitting field of a separable polynomial over $K$.
3. $|\operatorname{Aut}(L/K)| = [L : K]$.
4. $K$ is the [[fixed field|~c1e8263c]] of $\operatorname{Aut}(L/K)$.

Examples: $\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})/\mathbb{Q}$ and every extension of finite fields $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}/\mathbb{F}_p$. The extension $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[3]{2})/\mathbb{Q}$ is not Galois.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Galois extension has as many symmetries over the base field as its degree allows.
For these extensions, the subgroups of the Galois group describe all the intermediate fields.
