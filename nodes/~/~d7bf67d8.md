## Statement

Let $X$ and $Y$ be [[topological spaces|~77bfcc55]] and give $X \times Y$ the [[product topology|~be34ec23]].
Then the projections $\pi_1 \colon X \times Y \to X$, $(x, y) \mapsto x$, and $\pi_2 \colon X \times Y \to Y$, $(x, y) \mapsto y$, are [[continuous|~18b16c9f]].

## Remarks

Proof: for $U$ open in $X$, we have $\pi_1^{-1}(U) = U \times Y$, which is a basic open set. The proof for $\pi_2$ is the same.
The projections are also [[open maps|~a4ccb3ae]], but in general they are not closed maps.
The product topology is the coarsest topology on $X \times Y$ that makes both projections continuous.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Taking one coordinate of a point is a continuous operation.
For example, the map $\mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$, $(x, y) \mapsto x$, is continuous.
