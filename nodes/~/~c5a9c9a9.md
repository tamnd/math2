## Summary

This topic is a part of [[electromagnetism and optics|~b5a2c73b]].
It covers scalar and vector diffraction theory and its standard phenomena, with the Huygens-Fresnel principle, the Kirchhoff and Rayleigh-Sommerfeld formulas, Fresnel and Fraunhofer diffraction, gratings and the resolution limit.
