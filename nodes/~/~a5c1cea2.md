## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Diophantine approximation and geometry of numbers|~db363e14]].
It covers the approximation of points of n-dimensional space by rational points with a common denominator, the dual problem of small values of linear forms, exponents of approximation, transference and the Littlewood conjecture.
