## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|~ce40ba2a]].
It covers the rigidity results for lattices in semisimple groups: local rigidity, Mostow rigidity, the superrigidity and arithmeticity theorems of Margulis, the normal subgroup theorem, and the Zimmer program.
