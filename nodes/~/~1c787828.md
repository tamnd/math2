## Summary

Global analysis and geometric analysis is the study of analysis on manifolds and on spaces of maps, and of the use of differential equations to answer questions of geometry and topology.
A typical object is an elliptic differential operator on a closed manifold, such as the Laplacian, whose kernel and spectrum carry geometric and topological information.
The subject gives the index theorem, the spectral theory of manifolds, and the geometric flows that Perelman used to prove the Poincaré conjecture.
Morse developed the calculus of variations in the large in the 1920s and 1930s, and Atiyah and Singer proved their index theorem in 1963.
This topic collects the global analysis and geometric analysis pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Differential geometry, partial differential equations, manifolds and differential topology and calculus of variations and optimal control have their own topics.
