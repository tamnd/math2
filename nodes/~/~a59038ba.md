This topic is a part of [[random structures and probabilistic models|~2d4dfb89]].
It covers the Kardar-Parisi-Zhang class of random growth, with the KPZ equation, last-passage percolation, Tracy-Widom fluctuations, the Airy processes, the KPZ fixed point and its space-time extension, and the integrable models behind them.
