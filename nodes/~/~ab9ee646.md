This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers the Kodaira embedding theorem, Chow's theorem, GAGA, meromorphic function fields and Moishezon manifolds, which decide when a compact complex manifold or analytic space is algebraic.
