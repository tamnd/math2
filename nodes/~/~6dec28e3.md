## Definition

Let $A$ be a [[set|~bd92ceee]] and let $x$ be an object.
The object $x$ is an element of $A$, written $x \in A$, when $A$ contains $x$.
Otherwise, $x$ is not an element of $A$, written $x \notin A$.
The proposition $x \notin A$ is the negation of $x \in A$.

## Remarks

Other phrases for $x \in A$ are "$x$ is a member of $A$", "$x$ belongs to $A$" and "$x$ is in $A$".
For $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$, $2 \in A$ and $4 \notin A$.
An element of a set can itself be a set: $\{1\} \in \{\{1\}, 2\}$, but $1 \notin \{\{1\}, 2\}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The symbol $\in$ asks one yes or no question: is this object in the set?
For the set of even numbers, $6 \in$ the set and $7 \notin$ the set.
