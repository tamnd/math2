## Summary

The exterior derivative extends the differential of a function to [[differential forms|~b6723bc2]] of higher degree. It maps $k$-forms to $(k + 1)$-forms.
In $\mathbb{R}^3$ it unifies the gradient, curl and divergence, and it gives a common form of the theorems of Green, Stokes and Gauss. Élie Cartan described it in its current form in 1899.
