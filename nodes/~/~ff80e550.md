## Summary

$\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is the subsystem of [[second-order arithmetic|DC60FYCSPM]] whose axioms are those of [[ACA0|~08f6623a]] together with arithmetical transfinite recursion: every arithmetical construction can be iterated along every countable [[well-ordering|01CGSJ0GNQ]], as in its [[definition|~9f6fce13]].
It is the fourth of the big five systems of reverse mathematics.
It is equivalent over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ to the comparability of countable well-orderings, and it is the system of predicative reductionism.

## Motivation

Some theorems need the Turing jump iterated transfinitely often.
The classical proof of the comparability of well-orderings builds an isomorphism by recursion along the orderings, and the Cantor-Bendixson analysis of closed sets removes isolated points along countable ordinals.
$\mathsf{ACA}_0$ only iterates the jump finitely often, and arithmetical transfinite recursion is the natural extension.

## Notation

The name abbreviates "arithmetical transfinite recursion".
$\mathrm{WO}(W)$ denotes the $\Pi^1_1$ statement that $W$ is a countable well-ordering.

## Remarks

### Examples

- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ proves that [[the ω-th jump of the empty set exists|~631d55be]], which $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ does not prove.
- The [[full structure on the natural numbers|~9a3df793]] is a model of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$.
- Over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is equivalent to the statement that any two countable well-orderings are comparable: one is isomorphic to an initial segment of the other.

### Non-examples

- The [[arithmetical sets|~46cb5771]] form a model of $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, but not of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$: the hierarchy of jumps along $\omega$ is missing.

### Basic properties

- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ contains $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ properly, and it is contained properly in $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.
- Over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is equivalent to each of these: comparability of countable well-orderings; every uncountable closed subset of $\R$ has a perfect subset; the Lusin separation theorem; Ulm's theorem for countable reduced abelian $p$-groups; the determinacy of open games on $\N^\N$.
- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ has no least $\omega$-model. The hyperarithmetical sets are the intersection of all its $\omega$-models, but they do not form a model of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$.
- Every $\beta$-model is a model of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$.
- The [[proof-theoretic ordinal|C72RBH0CDR]] of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is $\Gamma_0$, the Feferman-Schütte ordinal.
- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ proves the same $\Pi^1_1$ sentences as Feferman's predicative system $\mathsf{IR}$.

### History

Harvey Friedman introduced $\mathsf{ATR}_0$, and he proved that it is equivalent to the comparability of countable well-orderings.

### Common errors

- A model of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ can contain a linear order that it thinks is a well-ordering, but which has a descending sequence outside the model. Only in $\beta$-models do the well-orderings of the model agree with the true ones.
- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ iterates arithmetical steps only.
- $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ does not prove transfinite induction along well-orderings for every formula of $L_2$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

$\mathsf{ATR}_0$ allows one to repeat an arithmetical construction transfinitely often, along any countable well-ordering.
It is the strength needed to compare two well-orderings, and to analyse closed sets of reals by removing isolated points again and again.

### L2 human

A proof in $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ usually defines an object by transfinite recursion along a well-ordering, with an arithmetical step.
A reversal to $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ usually goes through comparability of well-orderings: from two well-orderings, one builds an instance whose solution gives a comparison map.
A trap is to use transfinite induction for an arbitrary formula with set quantifiers, because $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ does not prove it in general.

### L3 human

$\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is the upper limit of predicative reductionism in the sense of Feferman and Schütte: its strength is measured by $\Gamma_0$.
It proves that for every set $X$ the $\omega$-th jump $X^{(\omega)}$ exists, so it proves that there is a countable coded $\omega$-model of $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, and thus it proves the consistency of $\mathsf{ACA}_0$.
Pseudo-hierarchies along non-well-founded orders that look well-founded from inside the model are a main tool in its study.
