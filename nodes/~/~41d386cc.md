## Summary

This topic is a part of [[electromagnetism and optics|~b5a2c73b]].
It covers Maxwell's theory written with differential forms on spacetime and on space, with the Faraday and Maxwell forms, the metric-free and metric parts of the theory, and the topological aspects of fields and charges.
