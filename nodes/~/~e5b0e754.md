## Summary

A decimal expansion writes a nonnegative real number as an integer part and an infinite sequence of digits after the decimal point.
It represents the number as a sum of multiples of powers of $10$.
For example, $1/4 = 0.25000\dots$ and $1/3 = 0.333\dots$.
