## Example

Let $f(x) = |2x - 1|$ on $[0, 1]$.
It is continuous, and its pieces $2x - 1$ and $1 - 2x$ have integer coefficients.
So [[McNaughton's theorem|~dde0176d]] says that a formula defines it.
We check that $(p \odot p) \oplus (\neg p \odot \neg p)$ is such a formula in [[Łukasiewicz logic|~3e406741]].

1. $x \odot x = \max(0, 2x - 1)$.
2. $\neg x \odot \neg x = \max(0, 1 - 2x)$.
3. For $x \ge \tfrac12$, the first value is $2x - 1$ and the second is $0$.
4. For $x \le \tfrac12$, the first value is $0$ and the second is $1 - 2x$.
5. The sum is $|2x - 1| \le 1$, so $\oplus$ does not cut it, and the formula defines $f$.
6. At $x = 0.3$: $0.3 \odot 0.3 = 0$, $0.7 \odot 0.7 = 0.4$, and $0 \oplus 0.4 = 0.4 = |0.6 - 1|$.

Two simpler McNaughton functions are $x \oplus x = \min(1, 2x)$ and $\neg x \odot y = \max(0, y - x)$.

## Remarks

1. The function $g(x) = x / 2$ is continuous and affine, but it is not a McNaughton function, because the slope $\tfrac12$ is not an integer.
2. So no formula in one variable has value $\tfrac12$ at $x = 1$.
3. The function $f$ is $1$ at $0$ and at $1$, so its formula is a classical tautology that is not a Łukasiewicz tautology.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The graph of $|2x - 1|$ is a V shape with its tip at $x = \tfrac12$.
The formula builds the right arm with $p \odot p$ and the left arm with $\neg p \odot \neg p$.
Then it adds the two arms; only one of them is nonzero at each point.

### L2 human

1. Split the function at the point where its pieces cross.
2. Find a formula that gives each piece and is $0$ elsewhere.
3. Join them with $\oplus$ when they never overlap, or with $\max$ in general.

A trap: $\oplus$ cuts at $1$, so joining overlapping pieces with $\oplus$ can give a wrong value; $\max$ is the safe join.
