## Statement

Let $\sum a_n$ be a [[convergent|~b727755f]] real series.
Let $(b_n)$ be a [[monotone|~f256f483]] and [[bounded|~0a464768]] real sequence.
Then the series $\sum a_n b_n$ converges.

## Remarks

The sequence $(b_n)$ converges to some $b$, by the [[monotone convergence theorem|~471ab2b3]]. Then $\sum a_n b_n = \sum a_n (b_n - b) + b \sum a_n$.
The first series converges by [[Dirichlet's test|~2264f60d]], since $b_n - b$ is monotone with limit $0$.
The hypotheses on $\sum a_n$ are stronger than in Dirichlet's test, and those on $(b_n)$ are weaker.

## History

Niels Henrik Abel proved the test in 1826.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Multiplying the terms of a convergent series by a bounded monotone sequence of weights keeps convergence.
The weights settle down to a limit, so they cannot disturb the series too much.
