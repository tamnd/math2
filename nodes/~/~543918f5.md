## Statement

Let $R \subseteq \R^2$ be a [[simply connected domain|0QW64ARSHB]].
Let $f$ and $g$ be continuously differentiable functions on $R$.
Consider the [[autonomous system|07BNRFQ0PJ]] $x' = f(x, y)$, $y' = g(x, y)$.
Assume that the [[divergence|0MWV6ZTK6S]]
$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial g}{\partial y}$$
is strictly positive on $R$, or strictly negative on $R$.
The sign may fail only on a small exceptional set, such as isolated points or curves.
Then the system has no closed trajectory, that is no [[periodic orbit|0AA97XA70K]], inside $R$.

## Remarks

This is the special case of the [[Bendixson–Dulac theorem|3QWVVM56JF]] in which the weight function $\varphi$ is the constant $1$.
