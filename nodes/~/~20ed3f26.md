## Definition

Let $I$ be an open interval and let $f \colon I \to \mathbb{R}$ be [[differentiable|~0f274b29]] on $I$.
The derivative function of $f$ is the function $f' \colon I \to \mathbb{R}$ that maps each $x \in I$ to the [[derivative|~9c0b2396]] $f'(x)$ of $f$ at $x$.

## Remarks

Other notations are $\frac{df}{dx}$ and $D f$.
For example, the derivative function of $x^3$ is $3 x^2$.
The derivative function need not be continuous. See [[x^2 sin(1/x)|~603a7cc5]].
Mathlib defines `deriv f` on all of $\mathbb{R}$, with value $0$ at points where $f$ is not differentiable.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The derivative function collects the slopes of $f$ at all points into one new function.
We can then differentiate it again.
