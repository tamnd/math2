## Summary

This topic is a part of [[algebraic number theory|~e51500ce]].
It covers the growth of class groups in towers of number fields whose Galois group is the group of p-adic integers, p-adic L-functions, the main conjecture, and the p-adic aspects of modular forms at the border with automorphic forms.
