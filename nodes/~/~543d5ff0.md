## Summary

A two-sided ideal of a ring is an additive subgroup that is closed under multiplication by ring elements on the left and on the right.
The two-sided ideals are the kernels of ring homomorphisms.
The [[definition of a two-sided ideal|~833ffd8a]] gives the conditions.
