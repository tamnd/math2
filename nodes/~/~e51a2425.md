## Statement

For every real $x$,
$$\cos^2 x = \frac{1 + \cos 2x}{2}, \qquad \sin^2 x = \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}.$$

## Remarks

The [[addition formula|~b24c628a]] gives $\cos 2x = \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x$. With the [[Pythagorean identity|~3b6893cc]], $\cos 2x = 2 \cos^2 x - 1 = 1 - 2 \sin^2 x$.
These formulas give $\int_0^{\pi} \cos^2 x \, dx = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
Mathlib states the first one as `Real.cos_sq`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A square of a cosine is a cosine of the double angle, shifted and scaled.
This turns squares into first powers, which are easy to integrate.
