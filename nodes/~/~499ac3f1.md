## Summary

In a Cauchy sequence, any two terms with large enough indices are as near to each other as we wish. A metric space is complete when each Cauchy sequence in it converges, and the real numbers form a complete space. The node [[Cauchy sequence|~7d922c14]] gives the precise definition.
