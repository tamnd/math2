## Summary

The Stirling numbers of the second kind $S(n, k)$ count the partitions of an $n$-element set into $k$ nonempty blocks. They connect ordinary powers with falling factorials and they sum to the Bell numbers. The node [[Stirling numbers of the second kind|~564c92de]] gives the precise definition.
