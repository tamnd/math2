## Statement

Let $p$ be a prime number.
Then $\sqrt{p}$ is an [[irrational number|~9900a69e]].

## Remarks

This generalizes the [[irrationality of the square root of 2|~07cf1fcf]].
More generally, the square root of a natural number $n$ is irrational unless $n$ is a perfect square. See [[the roots of integers|~8dd28d50]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The numbers $\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt{7}$ are all irrational.
The proof for $\sqrt{2}$ works for each prime: if $a^2 = p b^2$, then $p$ divides $a$, and then $p$ also divides $b$.
