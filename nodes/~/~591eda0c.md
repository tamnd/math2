## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers coalgebras, bialgebras and Hopf algebras as algebraic objects: the structure theory of finite-dimensional Hopf algebras, Hopf actions, braided and quasitriangular Hopf algebras, the Yang-Baxter equation, and quantum groups as algebras.
