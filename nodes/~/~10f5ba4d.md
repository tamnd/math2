## Proof

We give the structure of the proof.
Steps 1 to 3 and steps 6 and 7 are proved here; the two lemmas in steps 4 and 8 are cited without proof.

1. Let $L$ be a superintuitionistic logic. Its only rule is modus ponens, and its theorems are closed under substitution. So $L$ has the deduction theorem: $\Gamma, \alpha \vdash_L \beta$ iff $\Gamma \vdash_L \alpha \to \beta$, where $\vdash_L$ is derivability from $L$ and the assumptions by modus ponens.
2. Say $L$ has the *deductive interpolation property* if $\alpha \vdash_L \beta$ implies $\alpha \vdash_L \gamma$ and $\gamma \vdash_L \beta$ for some $\gamma$ in the shared variables.
3. By step 1, $\alpha \vdash_L \beta$ iff $L \vdash \alpha \to \beta$, and the same for $\gamma$. So the deductive interpolation property is the Craig interpolation property of the statement.
4. Lemma A, the bridge theorem, cited from Maksimova through the surveys of Fussner and Metcalfe. $L$ has the deductive interpolation property iff the variety $\mathbf{V}(L)$ of [[Heyting algebra semantics|~32a20f35]] has the [[amalgamation property|XSCSX39GEZ]]. For varieties of Heyting algebras this is also equivalent to the superamalgamation property.
5. By step 3 and Lemma A, the theorem is equivalent to: exactly eight varieties of Heyting algebras have the amalgamation property, namely $\mathbf{V}(L)$ for the eight logics $L$ of the statement.
6. The inconsistent logic has interpolation directly. If $L$ is inconsistent and $L \vdash \varphi \to \psi$, take $\chi = \bot$. Then $L \vdash \varphi \to \bot$ and $L \vdash \bot \to \psi$, since $L$ proves every formula, and $\bot$ has no variables.
7. $\mathsf{CPC}$ has interpolation by [[Craig interpolation for propositional logic|TRS2HY57EG]], with $\top$ written as $\bot \to \bot$.
8. Lemma B, Maksimova's classification, cited without proof. The varieties $\mathbf{V}(L)$ for the remaining six logics, $\mathsf{IPC}$, $\mathsf{KC}$, $\mathsf{LC}$, $\mathsf{LP}_2$ and the two extensions of $\mathsf{LP}_2$, have the amalgamation property. No other variety of Heyting algebras has it.
9. For $\mathsf{IPC}$ there is also a proof-theoretic route: induction on a cut-free derivation in the intuitionistic [[sequent calculus|PZX9J3VEMT]] LJ, given by [[cut elimination|AYRZ98Y0NW]], builds an interpolant. We do not reproduce it.
10. By steps 5 to 8, $L$ has interpolation iff $L$ is one of the eight logics. Removing the inconsistent logic leaves seven [[intermediate logics|~57af80cc]]. $\blacksquare$

## Idea

Interpolation is first translated into amalgamation of Heyting algebras, using the deduction theorem and algebraic completeness.
Then one checks which varieties allow two algebras to be joined over a common subalgebra.
Only eight varieties pass, and they include the trivial variety, which corresponds to the inconsistent logic.

## Variants

For $\mathsf{IPC}$, Schütte's and Nagashima's proofs are proof-theoretic, and Gabbay gave a proof with Kripke models; they cover the predicate case too.
Ghilardi and Zawadowski, building on Pitts' theorem for $\mathsf{IPC}$, proved uniform interpolation for the eight logics.
The full proof of Lemma B is in the monograph of Gabbay and Maksimova on interpolation and definability.
