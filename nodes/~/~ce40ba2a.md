## Summary

Lie theory and topological groups is the study of groups that carry a topology or a smooth structure, and of the Lie algebras that belong to them.
A Lie group is a group that is also a smooth manifold, such that multiplication and inversion are smooth maps.
Lie groups describe continuous symmetry, so they occur in differential geometry, in number theory and in physics.
Sophus Lie introduced continuous groups of transformations in the 1870s to study differential equations, and Killing and Cartan later classified the simple Lie algebras over the complex numbers.
This topic collects the Lie theory and topological groups pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Representation theory, harmonic analysis, group theory and differential geometry have their own topics.
