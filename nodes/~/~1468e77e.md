## Statement

Let $n$ be a natural number.
Then
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} = 2^n.$$

## Remarks

For $n = 4$: $1 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 1 = 16$.
The identity is the [[binomial theorem|~05b522d1]] with $x = y = 1$.
Combinatorially, both sides count the subsets of an $n$-element set. The [[number of subsets of a finite set|~47c2f7b8]] is $2^n$, and $\binom{n}{k}$ of them have size $k$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The numbers in row $n$ of [[Pascal's triangle|~9e20c12e]] add up to $2^n$.
This is because each subset of an $n$-element set has some size $k$ from $0$ to $n$, and there are $2^n$ subsets in total.
