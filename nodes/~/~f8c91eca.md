## Summary

This topic is a part of [[random structures and probabilistic models|~2d4dfb89]].
It covers random geometric objects in Euclidean space, such as random closed sets, germ-grain and Boolean models, continuum percolation, random tessellations, Euclidean functionals of random points, and the analysis of Poisson functionals.
