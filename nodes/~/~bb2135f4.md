## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|~d000caef]].
It covers the statistical mechanics of superfluid helium and of superconductors, Landau's criterion and the two-fluid model, quantized vortices, BCS theory, the London and Ginzburg-Landau theories, and the Josephson effects.
