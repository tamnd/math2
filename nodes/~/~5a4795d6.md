## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers orders and lattices in finite-dimensional semisimple algebras over the fraction field of a Dedekind domain: maximal and hereditary orders, and the orders of Eichler and Hurwitz.
