## Statement

Let $G$ be a finite simple [[graph|~3f82cef4]] with $n \ge 3$ vertices.
Suppose that every vertex of $G$ has [[degree|~939465ed]] at least $n/2$.
Then $G$ is [[Hamiltonian|~2d8c7d85]].

## Remarks

The hypothesis says that the [[minimum degree|~e364d05e]] is at least $n/2$.
The theorem follows from [[Ore's theorem|~a2180540]]: if all degrees are at least $n/2$, then each sum of two degrees is at least $n$.
The bound is sharp. The complete bipartite graph $K_{k, k+1}$ has minimum degree $k < (2k+1)/2$, and it is not Hamiltonian.

## History

G. A. Dirac published the theorem in 1952.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If each vertex is joined to at least half of the other vertices, there is a round trip through all vertices.
A graph with this many edges cannot avoid a Hamiltonian cycle.
