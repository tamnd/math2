## Definition

A [[graph|~3f82cef4]] is acyclic if it has no [[cycle|~da3e8f8b]].

## Remarks

An acyclic graph is also called a [[forest|~90ffa0a8]]. A connected acyclic graph is a [[tree|~29e53124]].
The [[path graphs|~c147bff4]] and the graphs with no edges are acyclic. The [[cycle graphs|~19143ce5]] are not.
A finite acyclic graph with $n$ vertices and $c$ components has $n - c$ edges.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A graph is acyclic when you cannot go around any loop in it.
Each edge then splits its piece of the graph in two when you delete it.
