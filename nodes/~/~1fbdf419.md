## Summary

This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|~1c787828]].
It covers the wave group on a Riemannian manifold, the trace formula that relates the spectrum to closed geodesics, and the high-energy behaviour of eigenfunctions in relation to the geodesic flow.
