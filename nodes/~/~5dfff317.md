## Summary

The quantile function of a real distribution is a generalized inverse of its cumulative distribution function.
For a level $p$ between $0$ and $1$, it gives the smallest value $x$ with probability at least $p$ of not being exceeded. The node [[quantile function|~f518f7ce]] gives the precise definition.
