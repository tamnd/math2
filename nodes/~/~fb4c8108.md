## Summary

A multinomial coefficient counts the ways to distribute $n$ distinct objects into labelled boxes of given sizes. It extends the binomial coefficient from two boxes to any number of boxes. The node [[multinomial coefficient|~1e22e24a]] gives the precise definition.
