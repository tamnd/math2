## Proof

We prove the five parts of [[the truth lemma|~f856785f]] in order.
We use three facts about a maximal $\Lambda$-consistent set $\Gamma$, each from consistency and maximality.
First, $\Lambda \subseteq \Gamma$.
Second, for each $\varphi$, exactly one of $\varphi$ and $\neg\varphi$ is in $\Gamma$.
Third, $\Gamma$ is closed under modus ponens.
These facts give $\varphi \to \psi \in \Gamma$ if and only if $\varphi \notin \Gamma$ or $\psi \in \Gamma$.

1. Lindenbaum, the chain. The language is countable, so list all formulas as $\psi_0, \psi_1, \psi_2, \dots$. Let $\Gamma$ be $\Lambda$-consistent and put $\Gamma_0 = \Gamma$.
2. Put $\Gamma_{n+1} = \Gamma_n \cup \set{\psi_n}$ if this set is $\Lambda$-consistent, and $\Gamma_{n+1} = \Gamma_n \cup \set{\neg\psi_n}$ otherwise.
3. Each $\Gamma_{n+1}$ is consistent. If both $\Gamma_n \cup \set{\psi_n}$ and $\Gamma_n \cup \set{\neg\psi_n}$ were inconsistent, finite $\alpha, \beta$, conjunctions of members of $\Gamma_n$, would give $\Lambda \vdash \neg(\alpha \wedge \psi_n)$ and $\Lambda \vdash \neg(\beta \wedge \neg\psi_n)$.
4. These two theorems tautologically give $\Lambda \vdash \neg(\alpha \wedge \beta)$, so $\Gamma_n$ would be inconsistent, against the [[induction hypothesis|0ASJVEFA9J]].
5. Lindenbaum, the limit. Let $\Gamma^+ = \bigcup_n \Gamma_n$. A finite subset of $\Gamma^+$ lies in some $\Gamma_n$, so $\Gamma^+$ is consistent. For each $n$, $\psi_n$ or $\neg\psi_n$ is in $\Gamma^+$, so no proper extension of $\Gamma^+$ is consistent. This is part 1.
6. Box lemma, first direction. Let $\set{\varphi : \square\varphi \in \Gamma} \subseteq \Delta$ and $\varphi \in \Delta$. If $\lozenge\varphi \notin \Gamma$, then $\neg\lozenge\varphi \in \Gamma$, and $\Lambda \vdash \neg\lozenge\varphi \to \square\neg\varphi$ by the duality of [[K|~b1c67ad7]].
7. So $\square\neg\varphi \in \Gamma$, hence $\neg\varphi \in \Delta$, which contradicts $\varphi \in \Delta$ and the consistency of $\Delta$. So $\lozenge\varphi \in \Gamma$.
8. Box lemma, second direction. Let $\set{\lozenge\varphi : \varphi \in \Delta} \subseteq \Gamma$ and $\square\varphi \in \Gamma$. If $\varphi \notin \Delta$, then $\neg\varphi \in \Delta$, so $\lozenge\neg\varphi \in \Gamma$.
9. By duality $\lozenge\neg\varphi$ is equivalent in $\Lambda$ to $\neg\square\varphi$, so $\neg\square\varphi \in \Gamma$, which contradicts $\square\varphi \in \Gamma$. So $\varphi \in \Delta$. This is part 2.
10. Existence, the set. Let $\lozenge\varphi \in \Gamma$, and put $\Delta_0 = \set{\varphi} \cup \set{\psi : \square\psi \in \Gamma}$. We show that $\Delta_0$ is $\Lambda$-consistent.
11. If not, there are $\psi_1, \dots, \psi_n$ with $\square\psi_i \in \Gamma$ and $\Lambda \vdash (\psi_1 \wedge \dots \wedge \psi_n) \to \neg\varphi$. Here the normality of $\Lambda$ is used.
12. Rule RK of [[K|~b1c67ad7]] gives $\Lambda \vdash (\square\psi_1 \wedge \dots \wedge \square\psi_n) \to \square\neg\varphi$. As $\Gamma$ contains each $\square\psi_i$ and $\Lambda$, it contains $\square\neg\varphi$.
13. But $\square\neg\varphi$ is equivalent in $\Lambda$ to $\neg\lozenge\varphi$, and $\lozenge\varphi \in \Gamma$. This contradicts the consistency of $\Gamma$.
14. Existence, the world. By part 1, $\Delta_0$ extends to a maximal consistent $\Delta$. Then $\varphi \in \Delta$ and $\set{\psi : \square\psi \in \Gamma} \subseteq \Delta$, that is, $\Gamma R^\Lambda \Delta$. This is part 3.
15. Truth lemma, the induction. We use [[structural induction|12MQ2NJ29A]] on $\varphi$, for all $\Gamma$ at once. For a variable $p$: $\Gamma \models p$ iff $\Gamma \in V^\Lambda(p)$ iff $p \in \Gamma$, by the definition of $V^\Lambda$.
16. For $\bot$: $\bot$ is false at $\Gamma$, and $\bot \notin \Gamma$ since $\Gamma$ is consistent. Maximality is used in the next two cases.
17. For $\varphi \to \psi$: $\Gamma \models \varphi \to \psi$ iff $\Gamma \not\models \varphi$ or $\Gamma \models \psi$ iff $\varphi \notin \Gamma$ or $\psi \in \Gamma$, by the induction hypothesis, iff $\varphi \to \psi \in \Gamma$, by the facts above.
18. For $\square\varphi$, the direction from membership. Let $\square\varphi \in \Gamma$ and $\Gamma R^\Lambda \Delta$. Then $\varphi \in \Delta$ by the definition of $R^\Lambda$, so $\Delta \models \varphi$ by the induction hypothesis. So $\Gamma \models \square\varphi$.
19. For $\square\varphi$, the direction to membership. Let $\square\varphi \notin \Gamma$. Then $\neg\square\varphi \in \Gamma$, so $\lozenge\neg\varphi \in \Gamma$ by duality.
20. Part 3 gives $\Delta$ with $\Gamma R^\Lambda \Delta$ and $\neg\varphi \in \Delta$. Then $\varphi \notin \Delta$, so $\Delta \not\models \varphi$ by the induction hypothesis, and $\Gamma \not\models \square\varphi$. This is part 4.
21. Canonical model theorem. Let $\Sigma$ be $\Lambda$-consistent. By part 1 it lies in some $\Gamma \in W^\Lambda$, and by part 4 every member of $\Sigma$ is true at $\Gamma$.
22. If $\Lambda \vdash \varphi$, then $\varphi$ is in every maximal consistent set, so $\mathfrak{M}^\Lambda \models \varphi$ by part 4. If $\Lambda \nvdash \varphi$, then $\set{\neg\varphi}$ is consistent, so $\varphi$ is false at some world by step 21. This is part 5.

## Idea

Lindenbaum's lemma makes worlds; the existence lemma makes successors.
The existence lemma is the one place where the modal axioms enter: rule RK turns an inconsistency below the box into an inconsistency at $\Gamma$.
The truth lemma is then a routine induction, whose box case calls the existence lemma.

## Variants

1. For an uncountable set of variables, step 1 uses [[Zorn's lemma|04RXTQA5ZA]] on the consistent supersets of $\Gamma$ instead of an enumeration.
2. Restricting all sets to a finite set $\Sigma$ closed under subformulas and negations gives a finite model; this is the canonical form of [[filtration|~9960fa2c]].
3. In the algebraic proof, step 1 is the ultrafilter theorem for the Lindenbaum algebra. Steps 10 to 14 are the representation of the operator $\lozenge$.
