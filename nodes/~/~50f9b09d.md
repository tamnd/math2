## Definition

Let $A$ be a set.
A total order on $A$ is a [[partial order|~dd266d19]] $\le$ on $A$ such that, for all $a, b \in A$, $a \le b$ or $b \le a$.

## Remarks

Other names are linear order and simple order.
The usual orders on $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ are total orders.
The order $\subseteq$ on the power set of $\{1, 2\}$ is not total.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a total order, all elements stand on one line.
For any two of them, one comes before the other, or they are equal.
