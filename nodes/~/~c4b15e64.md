## Definition

Let $P = (x_0, \dots, x_n)$ be a [[partition|~de6fd15c]] of $[a, b]$.
The mesh of $P$ is
$$\| P \| = \max_{1 \le i \le n} (x_i - x_{i-1}),$$
the length of the longest subinterval of $P$.

## Remarks

Refining a partition does not increase its mesh.
The [[Riemann integral|~d843dc28]] is a limit of Riemann sums as the mesh tends to $0$.
For the partition of $[0, 1]$ into $n$ equal pieces, the mesh is $1/n$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The mesh measures how fine a partition is.
A small mesh means that all the pieces are short.
