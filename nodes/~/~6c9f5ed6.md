## Example

Let $\mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PRA})$ be the arithmetic sentence "no number codes a proof of $0 = 1$ in [[primitive recursive arithmetic|WCXH72T8D8]]".
It has the form $\forall p\, \neg \mathrm{Prf}(p)$ with $\mathrm{Prf}$ primitive recursive, so it is a $\Pi^0_1$ sentence.
The claim is that $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \mathrm{BW}$ proves $\mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PRA})$, so it is not $\Pi^0_2$-conservative over $\mathsf{PRA}$, in contrast with $\mathsf{WKL}_0$.

1. By the [[Bolzano-Weierstrass reversal|~cd23ea7b]], $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \mathrm{BW}$ proves every axiom of [[ACA0|~bffbe3ad]].
2. $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ proves every instance of the induction scheme for arithmetical formulas: arithmetical comprehension forms the set of numbers with the property, and the induction axiom applies to this set.
3. So $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ proves every theorem of [[Peano arithmetic|HACXBCM7SS]].
4. Lemma, cited without proof: Peano arithmetic proves $\mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PRA})$.
5. By steps 1, 3 and 4, $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \mathrm{BW} \vdash \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PRA})$.
6. $\mathsf{PRA}$ is consistent, recursively axiomatized and strong enough for the [[second incompleteness theorem|4PZM5GJTR5]]. So $\mathsf{PRA} \nvdash \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PRA})$.
7. So $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \mathrm{BW}$ proves a $\Pi^0_1$ sentence that $\mathsf{PRA}$ does not prove. The conclusion of the [[conservation theorem for WKL0|~de3d00df]] fails for it.

## Remarks

This example is the reason that the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem lies outside the part of analysis that $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ reduces to finitism.
The same argument applies to every theorem that implies $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, such as [[König's lemma|~330fcb37]].
The Heine-Borel theorem for $[0, 1]$, which is equivalent to $\mathsf{WKL}_0$, behaves differently.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem is so strong that, together with the weak base theory, it proves that primitive recursive arithmetic is consistent.
A finitistic system cannot prove its own consistency, so this theorem cannot be reduced to finitism in the way $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ is.
