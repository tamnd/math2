## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|~2c3bc0e0]].
It covers modules over a commutative ring as the area uses them: finiteness conditions, tensor products and base change, the determinant trick and Nakayama's lemma, Fitting ideals and the structure theorem over a principal ideal domain.
