## Definition

The language of MTL has propositional variables, the binary connectives $\otimes$, $\land$ and $\to$, and the constant $\bot$.
The defined connectives are $\neg A := A \to \bot$, $\top := \bot \to \bot$ and $A \lor B := ((A \to B) \to B) \land ((B \to A) \to A)$.

*MTL* is the Hilbert calculus with the single rule [[modus ponens|0Y0MQYST0M]] and these axiom schemes:

1. (MTL1) $(A \to B) \to ((B \to C) \to (A \to C))$;
2. (MTL2) $A \otimes B \to A$;
3. (MTL3) $A \otimes B \to B \otimes A$;
4. (MTL4a) $A \land B \to A$;
5. (MTL4b) $A \land B \to B \land A$;
6. (MTL4c) $A \otimes (A \to B) \to A \land B$;
7. (MTL5a) $(A \to (B \to C)) \to (A \otimes B \to C)$;
8. (MTL5b) $(A \otimes B \to C) \to (A \to (B \to C))$;
9. (MTL6) $((A \to B) \to C) \to (((B \to A) \to C) \to C)$;
10. (MTL7) $\bot \to A$.

An *MTL-algebra* is a structure $\langle L, \land, \lor, \otimes, \Rightarrow, 0, 1 \rangle$ such that:

1. $\langle L, \land, \lor, 0, 1 \rangle$ is a bounded [[lattice|WH4SFAACAS]], with least element $0$ and greatest element $1$;
2. $\langle L, \otimes, 1 \rangle$ is a commutative [[monoid|0N6R902FG1]];
3. residuation: $z \otimes x \le y \iff z \le x \Rightarrow y$, for all $x, y, z \in L$;
4. prelinearity: $(x \Rightarrow y) \lor (y \Rightarrow x) = 1$, for all $x, y \in L$.

It is an *MTL-chain* if its lattice order is total.
For a left-continuous [[t-norm|~a4e21349]] $*$, the *standard MTL-algebra* $[0, 1]_*$ has $\min$, $\max$, $*$, the residuum of $*$, $0$ and $1$.

An evaluation $e$ in $L$ sends $\otimes$, $\land$, $\to$ and $\bot$ to $\otimes$, $\land$, $\Rightarrow$ and $0$.
$\Gamma \models_{\mathrm{MTL}} A$ means: for every left-continuous t-norm $*$ and every evaluation $e$ in $[0, 1]_*$, if $e(B) = 1$ for all $B \in \Gamma$, then $e(A) = 1$.

## Notation

SEP writes $\&$ for $\otimes$ and $\overline{0}$, $\overline{1}$ for $\bot$, $\top$.
The axiom labels follow the numbering of Hájek's axioms for BL, as Wikipedia explains: MTL4a to MTL4c replace the divisibility axiom BL4.

## Remarks

1. The residuum in $[0, 1]_*$ exists because $*$ is left-continuous; see [[the left-continuity statement|~42fe79f8]].
2. Wikipedia notes that MTL2 and MTL3 are redundant (Cintula 2005; Chvalovský 2012), and that the other axioms are independent (Chvalovský 2012).
3. SEP gives a variant axiom list, with $(C \to A) \to ((C \to B) \to (C \to A \land B))$ in place of MTL4c.
4. Residuation can be expressed by identities, so MTL-algebras form a variety, as Wikipedia states.
5. The calculus is strongly complete for $\models_{\mathrm{MTL}}$: $\Gamma \vdash A$ iff $\Gamma \models_{\mathrm{MTL}} A$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

MTL is a list of ten axiom forms with the rule modus ponens.
It has two "and" connectives: a strong one from the t-norm and a weak one, the minimum.
Its theorems are the formulas that are fully true for every left-continuous t-norm.

### L2 human

To check an axiom in $[0, 1]_*$, read $\otimes$ as $*$, $\land$ as $\min$, and $\to$ as the residuum.
Use the rule $x \Rightarrow y = 1$ iff $x \le y$.
For MTL4c: $x * (x \Rightarrow y) \le x$, and $x * (x \Rightarrow y) \le y$ by residuation, so it is at most $\min(x, y)$.
A trap: MTL4c is only one direction of divisibility; the other direction is the BL axiom.

### L3 human

An MTL-algebra is a prelinear, commutative, bounded, integral residuated lattice.
Integral means that the monoid unit $1$ is the top element of the lattice.
Sub-area 27 treats [[residuated lattices|E337A6WVH3]] in general.
