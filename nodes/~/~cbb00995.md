## Proof

Let $S = (\Omega, \ell, \prec)$ be a smooth [[preferential model|~d45c3d4a]], and write $\mathrel{|\!\sim}$ for $\mathrel{|\!\sim}_S$.
We check the six rules.
The basic fact is: if $X \subseteq Y \subseteq \Omega$ and $s \in X$ is minimal in $Y$, then $s$ is minimal in $X$.

Reflexivity.

1. Every state of $\min \widehat{\alpha}$ is in $\widehat{\alpha}$, so its label satisfies $\alpha$. Hence $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \alpha$.

Left Logical Equivalence.

2. If $\vDash \alpha \leftrightarrow \beta$, then $\widehat{\alpha} = \widehat{\beta}$, so $\min \widehat{\alpha} = \min \widehat{\beta}$, and the two formulas have the same consequences.

Right Weakening.

3. Let $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \beta$ and $\vDash \beta \to \gamma$. Every $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$ has $\ell(s) \vDash \beta$, so $\ell(s) \vDash \gamma$. Hence $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$.

Cut.

4. Let $\alpha \land \beta \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$ and $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \beta$, and let $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$.
5. By $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \beta$, $\ell(s) \vDash \beta$, so $s \in \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$.
6. Since $\widehat{\alpha \land \beta} \subseteq \widehat{\alpha}$ and $s$ is minimal in $\widehat{\alpha}$, the basic fact gives $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$.
7. By $\alpha \land \beta \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$, $\ell(s) \vDash \gamma$. Hence $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$.

Or.

8. Let $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$ and $\beta \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$, and let $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha \lor \beta}$.
9. Then $\ell(s) \vDash \alpha$ or $\ell(s) \vDash \beta$. Suppose $\ell(s) \vDash \alpha$; the other case is symmetric.
10. Since $\widehat{\alpha} \subseteq \widehat{\alpha \lor \beta}$ and $s \in \widehat{\alpha}$, the basic fact gives $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$.
11. By $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$, $\ell(s) \vDash \gamma$. Hence $\alpha \lor \beta \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$.

Cautious Monotony.

12. Let $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \beta$ and $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$, and let $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$. We show $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$.
13. Suppose not. Since $s \in \widehat{\alpha}$, smoothness gives a state $t \prec s$ with $t \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$.
14. By $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \beta$, $\ell(t) \vDash \beta$, so $t \in \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$.
15. Then $t \prec s$ and $t \in \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$, which contradicts the minimality of $s$ in $\widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$.
16. So $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$, and $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$ gives $\ell(s) \vDash \gamma$. Hence $\alpha \land \beta \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$.

## Idea

Each rule compares the minimal states of two sets of states, one contained in the other.
A minimal state of the larger set that lies in the smaller set is minimal there too.
Cautious Monotony needs the converse direction, and smoothness provides it: a non-minimal $\alpha$-state is beaten by a minimal one, which satisfies $\beta$.

## Variants

The same proof shows that a ranked model satisfies Rational Monotony.
Let $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\sim} \gamma$ and $\alpha \mathrel{|\!\not\sim} \neg\beta$, so some $u \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$ satisfies $\beta$.
If $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$ is not in $\min \widehat{\alpha}$, smoothness gives $t \prec s$ in $\min\widehat{\alpha}$; modularity gives $u \prec s$ or $t \prec u$.
The second is impossible, since $u$ is minimal in $\widehat{\alpha}$; the first contradicts the minimality of $s$, since $u \in \widehat{\alpha \land \beta}$.
So $s \in \min \widehat{\alpha}$, and $\ell(s) \vDash \gamma$.
