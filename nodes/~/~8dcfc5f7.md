## Summary

Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}^n$ be an [[open set|02BH3MCBQB]].
The set of all [[holomorphic functions|0NAHXR1FRH]] $f : U \to \mathbb{C}$ is written $\mathcal{O}(U)$.
With pointwise addition and multiplication it is a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]].

The letter $\mathcal{O}$ honours Kiyoshi Oka, who made basic contributions to the theory of several complex variables.
