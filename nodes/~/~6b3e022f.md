## Definition

Let $F$ be a field and let $V$ be a vector space over $F$.
An *endomorphism* of $V$ is a [[linear map|~2402e937]] $T \colon V \to V$.
The set of all endomorphisms of $V$ is written $\End_F(V)$.

## Remarks

An endomorphism is also called a *linear operator* on $V$.
Endomorphisms can be composed with each other, so their powers $T^k$ and polynomials $p(T)$ are defined.
Under composition and addition, $\End_F(V)$ is the [[algebra of endomorphisms|~7569166a]].
Eigenvalues, the trace and the determinant are defined for endomorphisms of finite-dimensional spaces.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An endomorphism maps a space into itself.
So we can apply it again and again, and compare a vector with its image.
In a basis, an endomorphism of an $n$-dimensional space is a square $n \times n$ matrix.
