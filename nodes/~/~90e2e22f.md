## Definition

Let $k$ be a natural number.
The divisor function $\sigma_k$ is the [[arithmetic function|~0abb0ec7]] given by
$$\sigma_k(n) = \sum_{d \mid n} d^k,$$
where the sum runs over the [[positive divisors|~5b1ad39b]] $d$ of $n$.

## Remarks

The case $k = 0$ is the [[number of divisors|~77af7cfd]], and the case $k = 1$ is the [[sum of divisors|~74f0ab43]].
For example, $\sigma_2(6) = 1 + 4 + 9 + 36 = 50$.
The same formula defines $\sigma_k$ for each real or complex number $k$.
The function $\sigma_k$ is [[multiplicative|~eca9601a]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

To compute $\sigma_k(n)$, list the divisors of $n$, raise each one to the power $k$, and add the results.
