## Definition

A [[topological space|~77bfcc55]] $X$ is totally disconnected when each [[connected subset|~6c57b623]] of $X$ has at most one point.

## Remarks

Equivalently, each connected component of $X$ is a single point.
Each [[discrete space|~67d1e560]] is totally disconnected. So are $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$, as subspaces of $\mathbb{R}$.
The [[Cantor set|~903cc646]] is totally disconnected, although it is compact and has no isolated points.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A totally disconnected space falls apart into single points: no subset with two or more points is in one piece.
This does not mean that the space is discrete. The rationals are a good example.
