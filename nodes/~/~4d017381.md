## Summary

Graph theory is the study of graphs.
A graph is a set of vertices together with a set of edges, where each edge joins two vertices.
Graphs are models of networks of many kinds, such as road maps, electrical circuits, molecules and the links between web pages, and they are a basic tool of computer science.
The subject started with Euler's solution of the problem of the seven bridges of Königsberg in 1736.
This topic collects the graph theory pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Extremal and probabilistic combinatorics, algebraic combinatorics, designs and matroids, and geometric group theory have their own topics.
