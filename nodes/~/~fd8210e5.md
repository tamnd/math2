## Statement

Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers.
Then
$$\binom{n+1}{k+1} = \binom{n}{k} + \binom{n}{k+1}.$$

## Remarks

For $n = 4$ and $k = 1$ it gives $\binom{5}{2} = \binom{4}{1} + \binom{4}{2} = 4 + 6 = 10$.
Combinatorial proof: fix one element $x$ of an $(n+1)$-element set. A $(k+1)$-subset contains $x$, and then it has $k$ other elements, or it omits $x$.
The rule builds [[Pascal's triangle|~9e20c12e]] row by row.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Each number in Pascal's triangle is the sum of the two numbers above it.
This rule is the same fact: a team of $k+1$ from $n+1$ people either includes a fixed person or does not.
