## Statement

Let $K$ be a [[field|~92ce2090]] and let $f \in K[x]$ have degree $n \ge 1$.
Then $f$ has a [[splitting field|~8d6926f1]] $L$ over $K$, and $[L : K] \le n!$.

## Remarks

The proof is by induction on $n$. [[Some extension $K_1$ contains a root $a_1$ of $f$|~d7420d8c]], with $[K_1 : K] \le n$. Then $f = (x - a_1) g$ in $K_1[x]$, and a splitting field of $g$ over $K_1$ is a splitting field of $f$ over $K$.
The bound $n!$ follows from the [[tower law|~1fa33f36]].
The bound is reached: the splitting field of $x^3 - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ has degree $6 = 3!$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

You build a splitting field one root at a time.
Each step adds a root of a remaining factor, and the degree of the step is at most the degree of that factor.
