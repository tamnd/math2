## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $H$ be a [[subgroup|~b600248b]] of $G$.
The index of $H$ in $G$, written $[G : H]$ or $|G : H|$, is the cardinality of the set of [[left cosets|~9c4c9f74]] of $H$ in $G$.

## Remarks

The index is also the number of right cosets, because $gH \mapsto Hg^{-1}$ is a bijection between the two sets of cosets.
In $(\mathbb{Z}, +)$, the subgroup $n\mathbb{Z}$ has index $n$ for each positive integer $n$.
A subgroup of a finite group has index $|G| / |H|$, but an infinite group can have a subgroup of finite index, for example $2\mathbb{Z}$ in $\mathbb{Z}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The index counts how many disjoint copies of the subgroup you need to cover the whole group.
For the even integers inside the integers, you need two copies: the even and the odd integers.
Thus the index of the even integers is $2$.
