## Summary

This area studies the models and the methods of statistics as a working science, such as the description of data, regression models, the design of experiments and surveys, and the methods for data with special structure.
A regression model describes how the distribution of a response variable depends on explanatory variables, and the linear model takes the mean of the response to be a linear function of these variables.
These methods are the daily tools of data analysis in medicine, biology, economics, engineering and the social sciences.
Legendre and Gauss introduced the method of least squares in the early nineteenth century, and Fisher developed the analysis of variance and the design of experiments in the 1920s and 1930s.
This topic collects the statistical models and methods pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Statistical inference, probability theory and the mathematics of machine learning have their own topics.
