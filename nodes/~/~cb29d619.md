This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers modular curves as Riemann surfaces and as moduli spaces over the complex numbers, modular forms as differentials and sections, Hecke correspondences, the Eichler-Shimura theory and modular symbols.
