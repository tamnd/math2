## Summary

The Jordan measure, or Jordan content, extends the volume of boxes in [[R^n|~a5928a9d]] to some bounded sets. Finite unions of boxes approximate these sets well from inside and from outside.
A bounded set has Jordan measure exactly when its indicator function is Riemann integrable, and then the measure is the integral of that function.
