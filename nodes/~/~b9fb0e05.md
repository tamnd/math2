## Example

Take the [[temporal frame|8XB117FKKV]] $(\mathbb{Z}, <)$ of the [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] with the valuation
$$V(p) = \set{1, 2, 3}, \qquad V(q) = \set{-2, 4}.$$
We use the strict clauses.
The formula $\varphi \, U \, \psi$ is true at $t$ when some $u > t$ has $\psi$ and every $v$ with $t < v < u$ has $\varphi$.
The formula $\varphi \, S \, \psi$ is true at $t$ when some $u < t$ has $\psi$ and every $v$ with $u < v < t$ has $\varphi$.

1. $p \, U \, q$ at $0$: take $u = 4$, where $q$ is true. The points strictly between are $1, 2, 3$, and $p$ is true at each. So $p \, U \, q$ is true at $0$.
2. $p \, U \, q$ at $-1$: the only $q$-point after $-1$ is $4$. Between $-1$ and $4$ lies $0$, where $p$ is false. So $p \, U \, q$ is false at $-1$.
3. $\neg p \, S \, q$ at $1$: take $u = -2$. The points strictly between are $-1$ and $0$, where $p$ is false. So $\neg p \, S \, q$ is true at $1$.
4. $\neg p \, S \, q$ at $2$: the only $q$-point before $2$ is $-2$, and $1$ lies between with $p$ true. So it is false at $2$.
5. Next as until: $\bot \, U \, p$ at $t$ asks for $u > t$ with $p$ and no point strictly between. In $\mathbb{Z}$ this means $u = t + 1$. So $\bot \, U \, p$ is true at $0$, $1$ and $2$, and false at $3$.
6. Eventually as until: $\top \, U \, q$ at $t$ asks only for some $u > t$ with $q$. It is true exactly at the points $t < 4$.

## Remarks

Step 5 shows that the operator "next" $X$ is definable from $U$ on discrete time.
On $(\mathbb{Q}, <)$ the formula $\bot \, U \, p$ is false at every point under every valuation, because no point has an immediate successor.
So the same formula means "next" on one frame and "false" on another.
If we change $V(p)$ to $\set{0, 1, 2, 3}$, step 2 changes: $p \, U \, q$ becomes true at $-1$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

"It stays sunny until it rains" is true today when rain comes on some later day and every day in between is sunny.
Here rain comes on day 4, and days 1, 2 and 3 are sunny, so the sentence is true on day 0.
On day $-1$ it is false, because day 0 is in between and is not sunny.

### L2 human

1. Write the points $-2$ to $4$ in a row with their letters: $-2$ has $q$, $1$, $2$ and $3$ have $p$, and $4$ has $q$.
2. For $U$ at $t$, scan to the right for a $q$, and check every point passed.
3. For $S$ at $t$, scan to the left in the same way.

The typical use of $U$ is the specification "the request stays pending until it is granted".
A trap: with the strict clause, $p \, U \, q$ does not require $p$ at the present point, and books with the reflexive clause give different answers.
