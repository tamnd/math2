## Summary

Relativity, gravitation and cosmology study space, time and gravity, and the structure and history of the universe.
In general relativity spacetime is a four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold, and the Einstein field equations relate its curvature to the energy and momentum of matter.
The theory predicts the bending of light, gravitational waves and black holes, it is the frame of modern cosmology, and satellite navigation systems must correct for its effects.
Einstein published special relativity in 1905 and general relativity in 1915, and Friedmann and Lemaître found the expanding models of the universe in the 1920s.
This topic collects the relativity, gravitation and cosmology pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Differential geometry, the partial differential equations of general relativity, quantum field theory and classical mechanics have their own topics.
