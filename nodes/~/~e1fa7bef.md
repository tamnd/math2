## Summary

An equilibrium point of a dynamical system is Lyapunov stable if all solutions that start near it stay near it for all future time.
The theory goes back to Aleksandr Lyapunov. See the [[definition|~eb28076b]] and the stronger notion of [[asymptotic stability|~216081a5]].
