## Statement

Let $S \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ be the [[successor function|~717c529c]] of the natural numbers.
For each natural number $n$, $S(n) \neq 0$.

## Remarks

This is one of the [[Peano axioms|~85dcaa42]]. In a construction of $\mathbb{N}$, it is a theorem.
With the [[injectivity of the successor function|~ba83e295]], it shows that the numbers $0, S(0), S(S(0)), \dots$ are all different.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When you count up from any natural number, you never get back to $0$.
Thus the natural numbers do not go around in a circle, like the hours on a clock do.
