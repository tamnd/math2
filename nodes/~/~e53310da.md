## Summary

Each power series with center $c$ has a unique $R$ in $[0, \infty]$ such that it converges for $|x - c| < R$ and diverges for $|x - c| > R$. This $R$ is its radius of convergence. The node [[radius of convergence|~83292ea3]] gives the precise definition.
