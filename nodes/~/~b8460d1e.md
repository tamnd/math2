## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the Hecke algebra and its action on modular forms, the Petersson inner product, eigenforms and their multiplicative coefficients, the theory of oldforms and newforms, and the trace formulas for Hecke operators.
