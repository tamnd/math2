## Example

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]].
The map $G \times G \to G$, $(g, x) \mapsto gx$, is an [[action|~0ceb002c]] of $G$ on the set $G$.
The two axioms are $ex = x$ and $g(hx) = (gh)x$, and they follow from the group axioms.
This action is [[transitive|~1e6a929e]], because $(yx^{-1})x = y$ for all $x, y \in G$.
The [[stabiliser|~847dc0b4]] of each point is $\{e\}$, because $gx = x$ implies $g = e$.
In particular, the action is [[faithful|~1605daff]], and this gives [[Cayley's theorem|~3461de53]].
