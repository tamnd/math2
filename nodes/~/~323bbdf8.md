## Statement

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, and let $f_n \colon S \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous for each $n \ge 0$.
If the [[series|~a7daf5e1]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} f_n$ converges uniformly on $S$, then its sum is continuous on $S$.

## Remarks

The partial sums are continuous, and they converge uniformly. Apply the [[uniform limit theorem|~e994aadc]].
Uniform convergence often comes from the [[M-test|~2034293f]]. Mathlib states the result in this form as `continuous_tsum`.
For example, $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(n x)}{n^2}$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Adding infinitely many continuous functions gives a continuous function, if the sum converges uniformly.
