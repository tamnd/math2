This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the first explicit modular forms and functions and their arithmetic, with holomorphic Eisenstein series, the discriminant function and the Ramanujan tau function, the Dedekind eta function, the j-function and its relatives.
