## Summary

Let $A$ be a stochastic matrix. A steady-state vector, also called a stationary vector, of $A$ is a probability vector $q$ that $A$ leaves fixed, that is, $Aq = q$.

A probability vector has non-negative entries that sum to $1$. So a steady-state vector is an eigenvector of $A$ for the eigenvalue $1$ that is also a probability vector.

In this convention the matrix acts on column vectors. This is the transpose of the row-vector form $\pi P = \pi$ used for a [[stationary distribution|02KYGSCTCN]] of a Markov chain.
