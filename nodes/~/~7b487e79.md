## Statement

Let $W$ be a subspace of $\R^n$ and let $W^\perp$ be its [[orthogonal complement|0XJXTBCB2T]].
Each vector $b \in \R^n$ can be written as a sum $b = \hat b + b^\perp$ with $\hat b \in W$ and $b^\perp \in W^\perp$.
This sum is unique: only one pair $(\hat b, b^\perp)$ has these properties.

The summand $\hat b$ is the [[orthogonal projection|0B8KZRT28M]] of $b$ onto $W$.
The summand $b^\perp$ is the orthogonal projection of $b$ onto $W^\perp$.
