## Summary

For $\R^n$, the [[tangent bundle|WY6K3P4YCG]] $T\R^n$ is the disjoint union of the tangent spaces $T_p\R^n$ over all points $p \in \R^n$.
A pair $(p,a) \in \R^n \times \R^n$ is matched with the tangent vector $\sum_k a_k \, \partial/\partial x_k|_p$ at $p$.
This matching is a bijection $\R^n \times \R^n \to T\R^n$, and it gives $T\R^n$ its topology and smooth structure.
The projection $\pi \colon T\R^n \to \R^n$ sends a vector at $p$ to $p$, and its fibre over $p$ is $T_p\R^n$.

A smooth vector field on an open set $V \subset \R^n$ is a sum $X = \sum_k a_k \, \partial/\partial x_k$ with smooth coefficient functions $a_k$.
Equivalently, it is a smooth map $X \colon V \to T\R^n$ with $X_p \in T_p\R^n$ at each $p$.
In the language of bundles, it is a [[section|WWX7H5W1RP]] of $T\R^n$.

For a submanifold $M \subset \R^n$, the tangent bundle is $TM = \bigcup_{p \in M} T_pM$.
A vector field on $M$ is a vector field $X$ with $X_p \in T_pM$ for every $p \in M$.
