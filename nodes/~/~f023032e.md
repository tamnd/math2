This topic is a part of [[statistical models and methods|~93b7a9d5]].
It covers inference for samples from multivariate distributions, with the sampling theory of the mean vector and the covariance matrix, tests on means and covariances, multivariate regression, copulas and high-dimensional covariance estimation.
