## Statement

Let $F$ be a [[finite field|~3adaef07]] of order $q = p^n$, with $p$ prime.
Then $F$ is a [[splitting field|~8d6926f1]] of $x^q - x$ over its prime subfield $\mathbb{F}_p$.

## Remarks

The proof: [[$x^q - x = \prod_{a \in F} (x - a)$|~84cd3106]], so $x^q - x$ splits over $F$, and its roots are all the elements of $F$. So the roots generate $F$.
With the [[uniqueness of splitting fields|~597b727b]], this gives the [[uniqueness of finite fields|~c18f73cf]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A finite field consists exactly of the roots of $x^q - x$.
So it is the smallest field that contains all these roots.
