## Definition

A complex number is transcendental if it is not an [[algebraic number|~991749fb]].
That is, it is not a root of any nonzero polynomial with rational coefficients.

## Remarks

The numbers $e$ and $\pi$ are transcendental, by the [[Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem|~47506aea]].
Each [[Liouville number|~9302c464]] is transcendental, by [[Liouville's theorem|~94d76d11]].
The algebraic numbers are countable, so almost all real numbers are transcendental.
Joseph Liouville first proved that transcendental numbers exist, in 1844.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A transcendental number solves no polynomial equation with integer coefficients.
It is usually hard to prove that a given number is transcendental, even though most numbers are.
