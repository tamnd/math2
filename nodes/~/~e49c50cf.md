This topic is a part of [[philosophy of mathematics|~dce90d7f]].
It covers the philosophical case for constructive mathematics, with Brouwer's intuitionism, Dummett's argument from meaning, semantic anti-realism and the constructivisms of Kronecker, Bishop and Markov.
