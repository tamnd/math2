## Statement

Let $x_1, \dots, x_n \ge 0$, and let $w_1, \dots, w_n \ge 0$ with $w_1 + \cdots + w_n = 1$.
Then
$$x_1^{w_1} x_2^{w_2} \cdots x_n^{w_n} \le w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \cdots + w_n x_n.$$
In particular, $\sqrt[n]{x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n} \le \frac{x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n}{n}$.

## Remarks

For positive $x_i$, apply [[Jensen's inequality|~01371218]] to the [[concave logarithm|~6c73ec74]], and then the exponential function.
The second form uses equal weights $w_i = 1/n$. Equality holds there if and only if $x_1 = \cdots = x_n$.
Mathlib states the weighted form as `Real.geom_mean_le_arith_mean_weighted`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The geometric mean of nonnegative numbers is at most their arithmetic mean.
For two numbers, $\sqrt{a b} \le (a + b)/2$: a square has the least perimeter among rectangles of a given area.
