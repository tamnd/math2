## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|~ce40ba2a]].
It covers the Killing form, semisimple and reductive Lie algebras, Cartan subalgebras and the root space decomposition over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0, up to the presentation of Serre.
