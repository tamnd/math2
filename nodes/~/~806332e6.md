## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the cohomology of locally symmetric spaces as a home for automorphic forms, with compactifications, the theorems of Matsushima and Franke, Eisenstein cohomology, torsion classes and their Galois representations, and limit multiplicities.
