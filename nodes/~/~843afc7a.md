## Summary

This topic is a part of [[manifolds and differential topology|~d33a3ff0]].
It covers generalizations of smooth manifolds by a change of the sheaf of functions or of the class of test maps, such as supermanifolds, graded manifolds, diffeological spaces, differential spaces and manifolds over other fields.
