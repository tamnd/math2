## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers the standard ways to build new noncommutative rings: free algebras and presentations, monoid and group rings with the conjectures of Kaplansky, skew polynomial rings and Ore extensions, and Weyl algebras and rings of differential operators.
