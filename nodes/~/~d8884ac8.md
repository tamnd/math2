## Definition

Let $p$ be a prime number and let $R$ be a [[commutative ring|~79bc52c4]] of [[characteristic|~1e7ebd1c]] $p$.
The Frobenius endomorphism of $R$ is the map $F \colon R \to R$, $F(x) = x^p$.
It is a [[ring homomorphism|~8febae30]] from $R$ to $R$.

## Remarks

The map $F$ preserves products because $R$ is commutative. It preserves sums by the [[freshman's dream|~07da31fd]].
If $R$ is an [[integral domain|~6bd4a5ff]], then $F$ is injective.
If $R$ is a [[finite field|~3adaef07]], then $F$ is an automorphism of $R$.
On $\mathbb{F}_p$, the map $F$ is the identity, by Fermat's little theorem.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In characteristic $p$, raising to the $p$-th power respects addition and multiplication.
So it is a homomorphism from the ring to itself.
This simple map is a basic tool in the study of finite fields.
