## Statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $d_n$ be the number of [[derangements|0TH61JR04G]] of the set $\{1, \dots, n\}$.
Then
$$d_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k \binom{n}{k} (n-k)!.$$

## Remarks

The term for $k$ counts, with sign, the permutations that fix a chosen set of $k$ points. There are $\binom{n}{k}$ such sets, and each leaves $(n-k)!$ ways to place the other points. The formula follows by the [[inclusion–exclusion principle|0FF7726C7P]].
For $n = 3$: $6 - 6 + 3 - 1 = 2$.
Since $\binom{n}{k}(n-k)! = n!/k!$, this agrees with the node [[number of derangements|03WY4HHMT1]] in its form $n!\sum_k (-1)^k/k!$.
