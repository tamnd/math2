## Definition

A division ring is a [[ring|~447242bf]] $D$ with these two properties.

1. $1 \neq 0$ in $D$.
2. Every nonzero element of $D$ is a [[unit|~11c1c339]]: for each $a \neq 0$ in $D$, there is $b \in D$ with $a b = b a = 1$.

## Remarks

A commutative division ring is a [[field|~92ce2090]].
The [[quaternions|~a3bbb4e7]] are a division ring that is not commutative.
By [[Wedderburn's little theorem|~9233f9ee]], every finite division ring is a field.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a division ring, you can divide by every nonzero element, as in a field.
But $a b$ and $b a$ can be different.
The quaternions of Hamilton are the standard example.
