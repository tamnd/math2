## Definition

The hyperbolic functions are the functions $\sinh$, $\cosh$ and $\tanh$ on $\mathbb{R}$, together with their reciprocals:
$$\coth x = \frac{\cosh x}{\sinh x} \ (x \ne 0), \qquad \operatorname{sech} x = \frac{1}{\cosh x}, \qquad \operatorname{csch} x = \frac{1}{\sinh x} \ (x \ne 0).$$
Here [[$\sinh$|~d61164d5]], [[$\cosh$|~53d4ba6c]] and [[$\tanh$|~77b8adc1]] are the hyperbolic sine, cosine and tangent.

## Remarks

They satisfy identities similar to the trigonometric identities, often with a change of sign.
The point $(\cosh t, \sinh t)$ lies on the hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = 1$, as $(\cos t, \sin t)$ lies on the circle.
Vincenzo Riccati introduced the hyperbolic functions in 1757.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The hyperbolic functions are built from $e^x$ and $e^{-x}$.
They play the role for the hyperbola that sine and cosine play for the circle.
