## Summary

This topic is a part of [[approximation theory|~02d28db3]].
It covers best uniform approximation from Haar subspaces, with the alternation theorem, uniqueness, lower bounds, strong uniqueness, the Remez method and the extremal polynomials of Chebyshev and Zolotarev.
