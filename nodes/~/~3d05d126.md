## Statement

Let $L/K$ be a [[field extension|~05511c4e]], let $a \in L$ be algebraic over $K$, and let $m_a$ be its [[minimal polynomial|~fe14474d]].
For every $f \in K[x]$ with $f(a) = 0$, the polynomial $m_a$ [[divides|~693cf517]] $f$ in $K[x]$.

## Remarks

The proof: [[polynomial division|~0ad30747]] gives $f = q m_a + r$ with $\deg r < \deg m_a$. Then $r(a) = 0$, so $r = 0$ by the minimality of $\deg m_a$.
Equivalently, the kernel of the evaluation map $K[x] \to L$, $f \mapsto f(a)$, is the ideal $(m_a)$.
If $a$ is a root of $f$ and $f$ is monic and irreducible over $K$, then $f = m_a$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Every polynomial equation that the element satisfies comes from the minimal polynomial.
It is a multiple of the minimal polynomial.
