## Summary

An analytic disc in $\C^n$ is a [[holomorphic map|3MQTWKBBBT]] from the [[unit disc|8G5ABXRK85]] $\mathbb{D}$ into $\C^n$ that is not constant.

A closed analytic disc is an analytic disc whose map extends to a continuous map on the closed unit disc $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$.

Often the image $\Delta = \varphi(\mathbb{D})$ is called the disc, instead of the map $\varphi$.
For a closed analytic disc, the set $\partial \Delta = \varphi(\partial \mathbb{D})$ is called the boundary of the disc.
