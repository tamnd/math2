## Summary

A conformal map is a map that preserves angles and their orientation at each point, but not necessarily lengths, see the [[definition|~beb1327b]].
In the plane, the conformal maps on an open set are the [[holomorphic|~1a187203]] functions with a derivative that is never zero.
