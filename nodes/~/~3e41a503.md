## Example

Let $n$ be a positive integer.
The integers modulo $n$, $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} = \{0, 1, \ldots, n - 1\}$, with addition modulo $n$, form a [[cyclic group|~333626b6]] of [[order|~89358ce0]] $n$.
The residue $1$ generates it, because each residue $k$ is the sum of $k$ copies of $1$.
Each cyclic group of order $n$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, and this group is often written $C_n$.

## Remarks

A residue $k$ generates $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ exactly when $\gcd(k, n) = 1$.
The [[group of $n$th roots of unity|~9cd6d68c]] is a cyclic group of order $n$ in multiplicative notation.
The rotations of a regular $n$-gon also form a cyclic group of order $n$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a clock with $n$ hours, where you add hours and wrap around after $n$.
This clock arithmetic gives the cyclic group of order $n$.
Every cyclic group of order $n$ looks the same as this clock, up to the names of the elements.
