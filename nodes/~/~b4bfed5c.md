## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|~9807873f]].
It covers what homological algebra needs from abelian categories beyond the axioms: exact functors, diagram chases with pseudo-elements, the embedding theorems, Grothendieck categories, Serre quotients, torsion theories and exact categories.
