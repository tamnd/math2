## Summary

A Möbius transformation is a map $z \mapsto \frac{az + b}{cz + d}$ of the Riemann sphere to itself, with $ad - bc \neq 0$, see the [[definition|~972db8e1]].
These maps are bijective and holomorphic, they preserve angles, and they [[map circles and lines to circles and lines|~d408c057]].
