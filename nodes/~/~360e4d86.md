## Statement

Let $I$ be an interval, let $f \colon I \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and differentiable at each interior point of $I$, and let $K \ge 0$.
If $|f'(x)| \le K$ for each interior point $x$ of $I$, then $f$ is [[Lipschitz|~c5cffb2c]] with constant $K$.

## Remarks

The [[mean value theorem|~9bd25708]] gives $|f(y) - f(x)| = |f'(c)| \, |y - x| \le K |y - x|$.
For example, $\sin$ is Lipschitz with constant $1$ on $\mathbb{R}$.
Mathlib states a form for convex sets as `Convex.lipschitzOnWith_of_nnnorm_deriv_le`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If the slope of the graph never exceeds $K$ in size, no chord can be steeper than $K$.
