## Statement

A superintuitionistic logic $L$ has the *Craig interpolation property* if the following holds.
Whenever $L \vdash \varphi \to \psi$, there is a formula $\chi$ with $L \vdash \varphi \to \chi$ and $L \vdash \chi \to \psi$, whose variables occur in both $\varphi$ and $\psi$.
The language contains $\bot$, so $\chi$ may be a formula without variables when $\varphi$ and $\psi$ share none.

**Theorem.** A superintuitionistic logic has the Craig interpolation property iff it is one of the following eight logics.

1. [[Intuitionistic logic|Q11N8X99FK]] $\mathsf{IPC}$.
2. [[The logic KC|H9Z8BWRN69]] $= \mathsf{IPC} + \neg p \vee \neg \neg p$.
3. [[Gödel-Dummett logic|~694d0fb1]] $\mathsf{LC} = \mathsf{IPC} + (p \to q) \vee (q \to p)$.
4. [[The logic of depth at most two|~d54501b3]] $\mathsf{LP}_2 = \mathsf{IPC} + p \vee (p \to (q \vee \neg q))$, also written $\mathsf{BD}_2$.
5. $\mathsf{LP}_2 + (p \to q) \vee (q \to p) \vee (p \leftrightarrow \neg q)$.
6. $\mathsf{LP}_2 + \neg p \vee \neg \neg p$.
7. Classical logic $\mathsf{CPC} = \mathsf{IPC} + p \vee \neg p$.
8. The inconsistent logic, the set of all formulas.

So exactly seven [[intermediate logics|~57af80cc]], the consistent ones, have the Craig interpolation property.

## Remarks

### Why each hypothesis is needed

The count depends on the trap in the terminology. "Superintuitionistic" includes the inconsistent logic, which has interpolation, so the count is eight. "Intermediate" excludes it, so the count is seven.
The theorem is about propositional logics.
Intuitionistic predicate logic also has interpolation, but the theorem says nothing about predicate logics.

### Consequences

1. Among the continuum many intermediate logics of [[Jankov's theorem|~7c8927a2]], only finitely many have interpolation.
2. Among the seven consistent logics on the list, only $\mathsf{IPC}$ has the [[disjunction property|~59f11dc8]]. So [[Kreisel-Putnam logic|~496da4a3]], [[Medvedev's logic|~6aab2c7f]] and every other intermediate logic with the disjunction property except $\mathsf{IPC}$ lack interpolation.
3. The logics of bounded depth $\mathsf{BD}_n$ for $n \geq 3$ lack interpolation, since they are not on the list.

### Converse

The theorem is an equivalence.
The direction "on the list implies interpolation" needs a proof for each logic, and the other direction is the classification.

### Generalizations

1. By the bridge theorem, the same eight logics correspond to the eight varieties of [[Heyting algebras|NG943XNQWH]] with the [[amalgamation property|XSCSX39GEZ]].
2. Ghilardi and Zawadowski showed that the eight logics even have uniform interpolation.
3. Among the axiomatic extensions of $\mathsf{S4}$, Maksimova showed that between 31 and 37 have Craig interpolation. Continuum many extensions of $\mathsf{GL}$ have it.

## History

Maksimova proved the theorem in 1977, as reported in Metcalfe's survey.
Her proof first showed that interpolation is equivalent to amalgamation of the associated variety, and then classified the varieties with amalgamation.
A full account is in the monograph of Gabbay and Maksimova on interpolation and definability.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Suppose "$A$ implies $B$", where $A$ talks about some letters and $B$ about others.
An interpolant is a middle statement that uses only the letters they share.
Classical logic always has one, and so does intuitionistic logic.
Among the infinitely many logics in between, only seven have this property.

### L2 human

1. Take $\varphi = p \wedge q$ and $\psi = p \vee r$. Then $\mathsf{IPC} \vdash \varphi \to \psi$.
2. The shared variable is $p$. The interpolant is $\chi = p$.
3. Indeed $\mathsf{IPC} \vdash p \wedge q \to p$ and $\mathsf{IPC} \vdash p \to p \vee r$.
4. For $\varphi = p \wedge \neg p$ and $\psi = q$, no variable is shared. The interpolant is $\bot$.

The typical use is to test a logic: if it is not one of the eight, some implication has no interpolant.
A trap: the theorem counts the inconsistent logic among the superintuitionistic logics, so "eight" and "seven" are both correct, for different notions.

### L3 human

The proof runs through the bridge between logic and algebra: the interpolation property of $L$ is equivalent to the amalgamation property of the [[variety|AJNZJD8442]] $\mathbf{V}(L)$ of [[Heyting algebra semantics|~32a20f35]].
This bridge works for many algebraizable logics, and Metcalfe's survey treats modal and substructural logics in the same way.
On the frame side, amalgamation of algebras is dual to a gluing of frames over a common p-morphic image.
The algebra of logic treats varieties with amalgamation in general.
