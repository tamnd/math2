## Definition

A [[topological space|~77bfcc55]] $(X, \tau)$ is metrizable when there is a [[metric|~200ea42b]] $d$ on $X$ whose [[metric topology|~2852bf47]] is $\tau$.

## Remarks

The metric is not unique: equivalent metrics give the same topology.
Each metrizable space is [[Hausdorff|~a1fe0c25]] and [[normal|~07c6c39a]].
An indiscrete space with at least two points is not metrizable.
Urysohn's metrization theorem: each [[second-countable|~44c1fcec]] [[regular|~9f103e07]] Hausdorff space is metrizable.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A metrizable space is a topological space where you can measure distances in a way that gives exactly the open sets you already have.
Properties that hold in all metric spaces then hold in this space.
