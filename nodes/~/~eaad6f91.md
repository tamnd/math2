## Summary

This topic is a part of [[set theory|~5daf9855]].
It covers the arrow notation for partition relations and the theorems on colourings of infinite sets, such as Ramsey's theorem for the natural numbers, the Erdős-Rado and Erdős-Dushnik-Miller theorems, and the negative results.
