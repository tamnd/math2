## Statement

Let $X$ be a [[compact space|~68153d33]] that is [[Hausdorff|~a1fe0c25]].
Then $X$ is [[normal|~f4483346]].

## Remarks

Proof sketch: closed subsets of $X$ are compact, by [[closed subsets of a compact set are compact|~436de6d7]]. In a Hausdorff space, a point and a disjoint compact set have disjoint open neighbourhoods. A second use of compactness separates two disjoint compact sets.
With [[Urysohn's lemma|~e88e4f6a]], this shows that compact Hausdorff spaces have many continuous real functions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a compact Hausdorff space, you first separate points by open sets, and then compactness lets you combine finitely many of these choices.
The result separates any two disjoint closed sets.
