## Definition

Let $E$ be a set with a σ-algebra $\mathcal{E}$.
A probability distribution on $(E, \mathcal{E})$ is a [[probability measure|~61dcdc08]] $\mu$ on $(E, \mathcal{E})$.
A probability distribution on $\mathbb{R}$ is a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with its [[Borel σ-algebra|~e1f58f6e]] $\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R})$.

## Remarks

Each random variable $X$ with values in $E$ gives a distribution, its [[law|~e6cf98ca]] $B \mapsto P(X \in B)$. Conversely, each distribution $\mu$ is the law of some random variable, for example the identity map on $(E, \mathcal{E}, \mu)$.
A distribution on $\mathbb{R}$ is determined by its [[cumulative distribution function|~d33bcb6a]].
Important types are discrete distributions, given by a [[probability mass function|~301be016]], and absolutely continuous distributions, given by a [[probability density function|~ecb6d374]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A distribution tells you which values are possible and how likely each range of values is.
For a fair die, the distribution gives each of the values $1, \dots, 6$ the probability $1/6$.
