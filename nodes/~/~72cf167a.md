## Summary

This topic is a part of [[harmonic analysis|~b23c1f2b]].
It covers the Fourier transform on integrable and square-integrable functions, on the Schwartz space and on tempered distributions, with its algebra, inversion, the Plancherel theorem and Poisson summation.
