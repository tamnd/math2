## Summary

This topic is a part of [[algebraic number theory|~e51500ce]].
It covers cyclotomic fields as number fields, with their integers, discriminants and splitting of primes, units and class numbers, Kummer's work on Fermat's Last Theorem, and the action of the Galois group on the class group.
