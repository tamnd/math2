## Summary

This topic is a part of [[electromagnetism and optics|~b5a2c73b]].
It covers the solutions of the paraxial wave equation and their exact counterparts, such as Gaussian beams, higher-order modes, nondiffracting and self-accelerating beams, beams with orbital angular momentum, and resonator modes.
