## Summary

This topic is a part of [[functional, difference and integral equations|~aff73214]].
It covers integral equations in which the unknown enters nonlinearly, such as the Hammerstein and Urysohn equations, with existence by fixed points and monotonicity, and nonlinear Volterra equations.
