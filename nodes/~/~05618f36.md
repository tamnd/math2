## Summary

The study of inequalities collects the named classical inequalities of analysis and algebra and the methods that prove them.
An inequality states that one expression is at most another for all values of its variables in a given set, and a sharp inequality also tells when equality holds.
Inequalities give the estimates that analysis, probability and the theory of differential equations need, and they are a standard part of olympiad mathematics.
Hardy, Littlewood and Pólya made the subject a field of its own with their book Inequalities of 1934.
This topic collects the inequalities pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Real analysis, convex and discrete geometry, matrix theory and multilinear algebra and problem solving and competitions have their own topics.
