## Definition

Let $G$ be a finite [[graph|~3f82cef4]].
The clique number $\omega(G)$ is the largest number of vertices of a [[clique|~d6405616]] in $G$.

## Remarks

The vertices of a clique need different colours. Thus $\omega(G) \le \chi(G)$, where $\chi(G)$ is the [[chromatic number|~5301b5d7]].
For the [[complete graph|~b29ee1b7]], $\omega(K_n) = n$. For the [[cycle graph|~19143ce5]] $C_n$ with $n \ge 4$, $\omega(C_n) = 2$.
The inequality $\omega(G) \le \chi(G)$ can be strict: for $C_5$, $\omega = 2$ and $\chi = 3$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The clique number is the size of the largest group of vertices that are all joined to each other.
It gives a lower bound for the number of colours that a graph needs.
