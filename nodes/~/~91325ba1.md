## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|~cd4b88f0]].
It covers rings that satisfy a polynomial identity: the Amitsur-Levitzki theorem, the theorems of Kaplansky and Posner, central polynomials, generic matrices, T-ideals and the Specht problem, codimension growth, and trace and generalized identities.
