## Statement

Assume the [[axiom of choice|~9fdddcbe]].
Let $A$ and $B$ be [[sets|~bd92ceee]].
Then there is an [[injective function|~3e705960]] from $A$ to $B$ or an injective function from $B$ to $A$.
So $|A| \le |B|$ or $|B| \le |A|$.

## Remarks

With the [[Schröder–Bernstein theorem|~5f17792e]], it follows that the [[order of cardinals|~ac796cdf]] is a total order.
The statement is equivalent to the axiom of choice over ZF.
One proof uses [[Zorn's lemma|~6a9de93f]] on the set of injective partial functions from $A$ to $B$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Any two sets can be compared in size: one of them fits into the other.
Without the axiom of choice, two sets can exist such that neither fits into the other.
