## Statement

Let $\mathbf{w}_1, \dots, \mathbf{w}_n$ be an orthogonal set of nonzero vectors, so each pair of them is [[orthogonal|0Y2X7P2WB7]] under the [[dot product|0S52TNNTH9]]. The vectors must be nonzero for the quotients below to make sense.

Suppose a vector $\mathbf{b}$ is a [[linear combination|0V8F3E48E6]] of $\mathbf{w}_1, \dots, \mathbf{w}_n$. Then the weights of that combination are forced. They are given by

$$\mathbf{b} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{w}_i}{\mathbf{w}_i\cdot\mathbf{w}_i}\,\mathbf{w}_i.$$

## Remarks

To see it, write $\mathbf{b} = \sum_j c_j \mathbf{w}_j$ and dot both sides with $\mathbf{w}_i$. All terms with $j \ne i$ vanish, which leaves $\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{w}_i = c_i\,\mathbf{w}_i\cdot\mathbf{w}_i$.
