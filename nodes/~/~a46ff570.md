## Summary

This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers Stein manifolds and Stein spaces as the global analogue of domains of holomorphy, the vanishing of coherent cohomology on them, and the classical problems that this vanishing solves.
