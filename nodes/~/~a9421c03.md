## Statement

Let $I$ be an interval, and let $p$ and $q$ be functions on $I$. Consider the second-order [[homogeneous linear equation|0EQZSAB9EC]]

$$y'' + p(x)\, y' + q(x)\, y = 0$$

on $I$. Suppose that $y_1$ and $y_2$ both solve it on $I$. Then for all constants $C_1$ and $C_2$, the function $C_1 y_1 + C_2 y_2$ also solves it on $I$.

## Remarks

The proof uses the linearity of differentiation. Write $Ly = y'' + p y' + q y$. Then $L(C_1 y_1 + C_2 y_2) = C_1 L y_1 + C_2 L y_2 = 0$.
