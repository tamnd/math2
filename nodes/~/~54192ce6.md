## Summary

The argument of a nonzero complex number $z$ is the angle from the positive real axis to $z$ in the [[complex plane|~d5458ec5]].
It is determined up to a multiple of $2\pi$.
The usual choice in $(-\pi, \pi]$ is the [[principal value|~5f7ab665]].
