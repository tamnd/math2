## Example

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]].
The map $G \times G \to G$, $(g, x) \mapsto gxg^{-1}$, is an [[action|~0ceb002c]] of $G$ on the set $G$.
Indeed, $exe^{-1} = x$ and $g(hxh^{-1})g^{-1} = (gh)x(gh)^{-1}$.
The [[orbits|~401e0177]] of this action are the [[conjugacy classes|~a445ebec]] of $G$.
The [[stabiliser|~847dc0b4]] of $x$ is the [[centraliser|~524f86a3]] $C_G(x)$.
The [[fixed points|~2926e5eb]] are the elements of the [[centre|~ff52f9c7]] $Z(G)$.
For an [[abelian group|~b6df27fe]], each element is a fixed point, and each orbit has one element.
