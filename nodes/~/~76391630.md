## Statement

Let $A$ be a real $n \times n$ matrix.
Suppose $A$ has $n$ pairwise distinct real eigenvalues.
Then $\mathbb{R}^n$ has a [[basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]] made up of [[eigenvectors|0N6960W0BV]] of $A$.

## Remarks

Pick one eigenvector for each of the $n$ eigenvalues.
These vectors are [[linearly independent|0HNMFG8F8K]].
A set of $n$ independent vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$ is a basis.
