## Statement

Consider a [[homogeneous linear equation|0KP0QNXPRT]] of order $n$, that is, a [[linear differential equation of order n|0QQRP17Z7S]] with right-hand side zero.

Suppose that $y_1, \dots, y_n$ are $n$ solutions of it that are [[linearly independent|0CRMGPAH83]].

Then the general solution of the equation is $y = C_1 y_1 + C_2 y_2 + \dots + C_n y_n$, with constants $C_1, \dots, C_n$. This means that every solution has this form for some choice of the constants.

## Remarks

The set $\{y_1, \dots, y_n\}$ is a [[fundamental system of solutions|0DS2QFYJHE]]. The result follows from the fact that the solution space has dimension $n$, see [[the dimension of the solution space|0GK68PZ6KH]].

Example: $\cos x$ and $\sin x$ are independent solutions of $y'' + y = 0$, so every solution is $C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x$.
