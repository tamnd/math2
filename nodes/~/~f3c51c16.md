## Definition

A prime number is a natural number $p$ such that $p > 1$ and the only positive divisors of $p$ are $1$ and $p$.

## Remarks

The first prime numbers are $2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13$. The number $2$ is the only even prime.
The number $1$ is not prime by this definition. This choice makes the [[prime factorization|~26a03fbe]] of each integer unique.
A natural number $n > 1$ that is not prime is a [[composite number|~def9c278]].
An equivalent property for $p > 1$ is [[Euclid's lemma|~e025b2a9]]: if $p \mid a b$, then $p \mid a$ or $p \mid b$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A prime number is a number greater than $1$ that you cannot split as a product of two smaller natural numbers.
For example, $7$ is prime, but $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ is not.
The primes are the building blocks of the natural numbers under multiplication.
