## Statement

Let $(a_n)_{n\ge 0}$ and $(b_n)_{n\ge 0}$ be two sequences of numbers. Let $A(x)=\sum_{n\ge 0} a_n x^n$ and $B(x)=\sum_{n\ge 0} b_n x^n$ be their [[generating functions|0PHAC833JF]].

Then the product $A(x)B(x)$ is the generating function of the sequence $(c_n)_{n\ge 0}$ with

$$c_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k b_{n-k}=a_0b_n+a_1b_{n-1}+\dots+a_nb_0.$$

## Remarks

The sequence $(c_n)$ is the convolution of $(a_n)$ and $(b_n)$. Each term $a_k x^k$ of $A$ pairs with the term $b_{n-k}x^{n-k}$ of $B$ to give a multiple of $x^n$.
