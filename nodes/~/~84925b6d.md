## Example

The symmetric group $S_3$ has the six elements $e$, $(1\ 2)$, $(1\ 3)$, $(2\ 3)$, $(1\ 2\ 3)$ and $(1\ 3\ 2)$.
It is [[isomorphic|~4c4a647d]] to the [[dihedral group|~42ac19c1]] $D_3$ of the symmetries of an equilateral triangle, when you number the vertices $1$, $2$, $3$.
The $3$-cycles are the rotations, and the transpositions are the reflections.
It is not [[abelian|~b6df27fe]], because $(1\ 2)(2\ 3) = (1\ 2\ 3)$ but $(2\ 3)(1\ 2) = (1\ 3\ 2)$.
Thus $S_3$ is the smallest group that is not abelian.
Its [[conjugacy classes|~86de96b5]] are $\{e\}$, the three transpositions, and the two $3$-cycles.
Its subgroups are $\{e\}$, three subgroups of order $2$, the [[alternating group|~de0bd4cd]] $A_3$ of order $3$, and $S_3$.
Only $\{e\}$, $A_3$ and $S_3$ are [[normal|~ebf813cb]].
