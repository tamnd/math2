## Proof

The proof is by contraposition, and it cites one lemma without proof.

Lemma E (cited): for every countable model $(M, \mathcal{S})$ of $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ there is a family $\mathcal{S}' \supseteq \mathcal{S}$ of subsets of $M$ such that $(M, \mathcal{S}')$ is a model of $\mathsf{WKL}_0$.
The standard proof lists the infinite binary trees of the model, adds a path through one tree at a time with a forcing or low basis argument that keeps $\Sigma^0_1$ induction true, and takes the union of the resulting families.

1. Let $\sigma = \forall X\, \theta(X)$ with $\theta$ arithmetical, and assume that $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ does not prove $\sigma$.
2. By [[Gödel's completeness theorem|WQ1ZE255SF]], there is a model of $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \neg\sigma$. The language $L_2$ is countable, so by the [[Löwenheim-Skolem theorem|484M0TZ1S7]] there is a countable one, $(M, \mathcal{S})$.
3. Since $\neg\sigma$ holds, there is $A \in \mathcal{S}$ with $(M, \mathcal{S}) \models \neg\theta(A)$.
4. Lemma E gives $\mathcal{S}' \supseteq \mathcal{S}$ with $(M, \mathcal{S}') \models \mathsf{WKL}_0$. The set $A$ is still in $\mathcal{S}'$.
5. The formula $\neg\theta(A)$ has no set quantifiers. Its truth depends only on $M$ and on $A$, and both are the same in the two structures.
6. So $(M, \mathcal{S}') \models \neg\theta(A)$, and $(M, \mathcal{S}') \models \neg\sigma$.
7. By the [[soundness theorem|4A84VHDTHV]], $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ does not prove $\sigma$.
8. By contraposition, if $\mathsf{WKL}_0 \vdash \sigma$, then $\mathsf{RCA}_0 \vdash \sigma$.

## Idea

Adding sets to a model does not change the truth of a statement about numbers and one given set.
So it is enough to add paths through trees to any model of the base theory, without changing its numbers.

## Variants

For $\omega$-models, Lemma E follows from the relativized low basis theorem; the difficulty in the general case is to keep $\Sigma^0_1$ induction in a model with non-standard numbers.
Later proof-theoretic work gives an explicit transformation of $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ proofs of $\Pi^1_1$ sentences into $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ proofs; it is not given here.
