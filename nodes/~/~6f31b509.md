## Statement

Take four [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]] $x, y, z, w$.
Suppose that $x$ equals $y$ and that $z$ equals $w$.
Then the sum $x+z$ equals the sum $y+w$, and the product $xz$ equals the product $yw$.

## Remarks

This says that the sum and the product of two rationals do not depend on the choice of the fractions that name them.
So addition and multiplication are well defined operations on $\mathbb{Q}$.
