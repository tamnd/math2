This topic is a part of [[extremal and probabilistic combinatorics|~b6b1922e]].
It covers how evenly a set system can be coloured with two colours, with random and partial colourings, Spencer's theorem, the Beck-Fiala and Komlós problems, and algorithmic discrepancy.
