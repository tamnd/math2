## Definition

Let $G$ be a finite [[graph|~3f82cef4]] with a nonempty vertex set $V$.
The maximum degree of $G$ is
$$\Delta(G) = \max_{v \in V} \deg(v),$$
the largest [[degree|~939465ed]] of a vertex of $G$.

## Remarks

The complete graph $K_n$ has $\Delta = n - 1$.
A [[greedy colouring|~b2a39f97]] uses at most $\Delta(G) + 1$ colours. [[Brooks' theorem|~8d0ebd98]] improves this to $\Delta(G)$ in most cases.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The maximum degree is the largest number of edges at any vertex.
In a star with one centre and $5$ leaves, the maximum degree is $5$.
