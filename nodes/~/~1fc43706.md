## Summary

A Cauchy–Euler equation is a linear equation $\sum_k a_k x^k y^{(k)} = 0$ with constant $a_k$, in which the $k$-th derivative has the factor $x^k$.
It has solutions of the form $x^m$, and the change of variable $x = e^t$ gives constant coefficients. See the [[definition|~13cfe47c]].
