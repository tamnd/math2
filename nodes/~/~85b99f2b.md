## Summary

Let $A$ be an $m\times m$ [[symmetric matrix|0VR671PHE5]].
The quadratic form defined by $A$ is the real function $q_A$ on $\mathbb{R}^m$ that sends a vector $x$ to the [[dot product|0S52TNNTH9]] of $x$ with the [[matrix-vector product|0PDVQEP0ZC]] $Ax$.

In symbols, $q_A(x) = x \cdot (Ax) = x^T A x$.
This is a special case of a [[quadratic form|0RBQS1JJCR]], and it is a homogeneous polynomial of degree two in the entries of $x$.
