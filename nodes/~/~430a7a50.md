## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $[G, G]$ be its [[commutator subgroup|~b33e4ba0]].
The abelianisation of $G$ is the [[quotient group|~592bfb26]] $G^{\mathrm{ab}} = G/[G, G]$.

## Remarks

The group $G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is [[abelian|~b6df27fe]], by [[this criterion|~83091c4f]].
If $G$ is abelian, then $G^{\mathrm{ab}} = G$.
The abelianisation of the symmetric group $S_n$ for $n \geq 2$ is cyclic of order $2$, and the sign of a permutation gives the isomorphism.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The abelianisation forces all elements of a group to commute, and changes nothing more than it must.
It keeps as much information about the group as an abelian group can keep.
For a group that is already abelian, it does nothing.
