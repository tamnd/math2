## Definition

Let $(P, \le)$ be a [[partially ordered set|~dd266d19]] and let $S$ be a [[subset|~a9b9ba7e]] of $P$.
An element $g \in S$ is the greatest element of $S$ when $s \le g$ for each $s \in S$.

## Remarks

Another name is maximum. By antisymmetry, a set has at most one greatest element.
A greatest element is a [[maximal element|~baf61a38]] and an [[upper bound|~498abb0b]] of $S$ that lies in $S$.
The interval $[0, 1)$ in $\mathbb{R}$ has no greatest element.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The greatest element beats every other element of the set.
In $\{1, 2, 3, 6\}$ under divisibility, $6$ is the greatest element, because each element divides $6$.
