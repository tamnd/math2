## Statement

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $H$ be a [[characteristic subgroup|~09059a4f]] of $G$.
Then $H$ is a [[normal subgroup|~ebf813cb]] of $G$.

## Remarks

Each [[inner automorphism|~d62e904c]] $c_g$ is an automorphism, so $gHg^{-1} = c_g(H) = H$ for each $g \in G$.
The converse is false: in the Klein four-group $V = \{e, a, b, ab\}$, each subgroup is normal, but an automorphism can swap $\{e, a\}$ and $\{e, b\}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Normal means fixed by the inner automorphisms, and characteristic means fixed by all automorphisms.
All automorphisms include the inner ones.
So a characteristic subgroup is normal.
