## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the upper half-plane and the action of the modular group on it, the definitions of modular functions, modular forms and cusp forms of integral weight, and the structure of the spaces that they form.
