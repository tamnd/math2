## Definition

Let $(b_n)$ be a real sequence with $b_n > 0$ for all $n$.
The series
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n b_n = b_0 - b_1 + b_2 - b_3 + \cdots$$
is an alternating series.
More generally, a [[series|~6612f89e]] is alternating if its terms are nonzero and their signs alternate.

## Remarks

Some texts allow $b_n \ge 0$.
The [[alternating harmonic series|~18f79025]] $1 - 1/2 + 1/3 - \cdots$ is the standard example.
The [[alternating series test|~689a0b54]] gives a simple condition for convergence.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In an alternating series, the signs of the terms go plus, minus, plus, minus.
The partial sums then move up and down, like a pendulum.
