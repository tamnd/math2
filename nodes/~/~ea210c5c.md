## Statement

Let $A$ be a square matrix, and let $A'$ be a matrix obtained from $A$ by one elementary [[row operation|04XWH28452]]. Write $\det$ for the [[determinant|00KDZRW4C0]].

If the operation multiplies one row by a scalar $k$, then $\det(A') = k\det(A)$.

If the operation swaps two rows, then $\det(A') = -\det(A)$.

If the operation adds a multiple of one row to another row, then $\det(A') = \det(A)$.

## Remarks

The three cases give a way to compute a determinant by row reduction. One tracks the scalings and the swaps along the way.
