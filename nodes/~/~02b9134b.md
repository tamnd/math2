## Statement

Let $m \ge 2$, $V = \set{0, 1, \dots, m - 1}$, and define
$$w(x, y) = \max(x, y) + 1 \bmod m.$$

**Theorem.** $w$ is a [[Sheffer function|6KFS240R42]] on $V$: every operation $V^n \to V$ with $n \ge 1$ is a composition of copies of $w$.

The proof defines Post's connectives from $w$; see [[the proof|~7475c39a]].

## Remarks

### Why each hypothesis is needed

1. The shift "$+1$". The operation $\max(x, y)$ alone is not a Sheffer function; see [[the non-example|~dc3a6ee8]].
2. The reduction "mod $m$". Without it, $w$ does not map $V$ into $V$.
3. $m \ge 2$. On a one-element set every operation is a projection, and the notion is empty.

### Consequences

1. Every $m$-valued logic with finitely many truth-functional connectives can be rewritten with $w$ as its only connective.
2. For $m = 2$, $w(x, y) = 1 - \max(x, y)$ is NOR, so the theorem extends the two-valued fact.

### Converse

Not every Sheffer function on $V$ has the form of $w$.
For $m = 2$, NAND is a Sheffer function and is not NOR.

### Generalizations

1. Any operation from which Post's $\neg$ and $\max$ are terms is a Sheffer function, by [[Post's theorem|~a7498925]].
2. For $m \ge 3$, Słupecki's criterion and Rosenberg's classification decide the question for arbitrary operations; see [[Sheffer function|1K829SJG3K]].

## History

Webb published the result in 1935, in a note in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.
The Wikipedia article on NOR reports that his $n$-valued non-disjunction is called the Webb operation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take the larger of two values and move it one step up, with the top wrapping around to $0$.
This single operation can build every table on the $m$ values.
For two values it is the NOR gate.

### L2 human

1. Feed the same input twice: $w(x, x)$ is the shift by one.
2. Shift $m - 1$ more times to undo the "$+1$" in $w(x, y)$; this gives $\max$.
3. Shift $m - 1$ times to get Post's negation.

The typical use is the reduction to a known complete set.
A trap: the shift and $\max$ alone suffice only because $\max$ is combined with a negation that moves every value.

### L3 human

The clone generated by $w$ contains $S$ and $\max$, hence Post's connectives, hence all operations.
