## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers automorphic forms on the adele points of reductive groups, cusp forms and Eisenstein series, the spectral decomposition, the tensor product theorem, multiplicity one and the Jacquet-Langlands correspondence.
