## Statement

Let $A$ be a [[set|~bd92ceee]].
Then the [[power set|~1481eeb8]] of $A$ is equinumerous with the set $\{0, 1\}^A$ of functions from $A$ to $\{0, 1\}$, so
$$|\mathcal{P}(A)| = 2^{|A|}.$$

## Remarks

The bijection sends a subset $S$ to its [[indicator function|~2dbb70a8]] $\mathbf{1}_S$.
For a finite set with $n$ elements, the power set has $2^n$ elements.
For $A = \mathbb{N}$, $|\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{N})| = 2^{\aleph_0}$, the [[cardinality of the continuum|~19005d6c]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A subset is a choice of "in" or "out" for each element.
With $n$ elements, there are $2 \cdot 2 \cdots 2 = 2^n$ such choices.
