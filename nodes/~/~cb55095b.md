## Summary

A Laurent series is a series $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} a_n (z - c)^n$ with powers of $z - c$ of all integer degrees, see the [[definition|~b2d962ad]].
It represents holomorphic functions on annuli, also near isolated singularities where a Taylor series does not exist.
