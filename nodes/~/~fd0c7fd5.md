## Summary

Continuum mechanics is the study of bodies that deform, and solid mechanics is its part that deals with solids.
A continuum is a model of matter that fills space without gaps, so that quantities such as density, displacement and stress are fields on the body.
The theory gives the deformation and the stress in structures, machines, materials and the Earth, and engineers use it to design bridges, aircraft and buildings.
Cauchy introduced the stress tensor in the 1820s, and Navier, Cauchy and Poisson wrote the first equations of linear elasticity at about the same time.
This topic collects the continuum and solid mechanics pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Fluid mechanics, classical mechanics, partial differential equations, the calculus of variations and numerical methods for differential equations have their own topics.
