## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|~d000caef]].
It covers ensembles of quantum systems described by density matrices, Bose-Einstein and Fermi-Dirac statistics, ideal quantum gases, Bose-Einstein condensation and the rigorous theory of interacting Bose gases.
