## Proof

1. Let $\varphi \colon V \to V / U \times V / W$ be $\varphi(v) = (v + U, v + W)$. It is linear, since both [[quotient maps|0J0SK5FF4M]] are linear and the [[product|0MM085RX00]] has componentwise operations.
2. $\varphi(v) = 0$ means $v + U = U$ and $v + W = W$, that is, $v \in U$ and $v \in W$. So $\ker \varphi = U \cap W$.
3. By the [[first isomorphism theorem|0MTBGVZ4XJ]], $V / (U \cap W) \cong \operatorname{im} \varphi$.
4. By the [[dimension of a direct product|06HN47VPTH]], the space $V / U \times V / W$ is finite-dimensional of dimension $\operatorname{codim} U + \operatorname{codim} W$.
5. $\operatorname{im} \varphi$ is a subspace of it. By the [[dimension of a subspace|0QW084460E]], it is finite-dimensional with $\dim \operatorname{im} \varphi \le \operatorname{codim} U + \operatorname{codim} W$, and equality holds exactly when $\varphi$ is surjective.
6. By step 3, $\operatorname{codim}(U \cap W) = \dim\big(V / (U \cap W)\big) = \dim \operatorname{im} \varphi$. This proves finiteness and the inequality.
7. It remains to show that $\varphi$ is surjective if and only if $U + W = V$.
8. Assume $U + W = V$, and let $(a + U, b + W)$ be given. Write $a - b = u + w$ with $u \in U$ and $w \in W$, and let $v = a - u = b + w$.
9. Then $v - a = -u \in U$ and $v - b = w \in W$. So $\varphi(v) = (a + U, b + W)$, and $\varphi$ is surjective.
10. Conversely, assume $\varphi$ is surjective, and let $x \in V$. Choose $v$ with $\varphi(v) = (x + U, 0 + W)$.
11. Then $x - v \in U$ and $v \in W$. So $x = (x - v) + v \in U + W$, and $U + W = V$.
12. By steps 5, 6 and 7 to 11, equality holds if and only if $U + W = V$.

## Idea

The pair of quotient maps embeds $V / (U \cap W)$ into the product $V / U \times V / W$.
The dimension of the product bounds the codimension, and the embedding is onto exactly when every pair of conditions can be met at once.

## Variants

- In finite dimension, one can instead use [[Grassmann's formula|06HTJNCMPA]] for $U$ and $W$ and subtract from $\dim V$.
- The same argument with $k$ subspaces gives $\operatorname{codim} \bigcap_i U_i \le \sum_i \operatorname{codim} U_i$.
