## Statement

Let $L/K$ be a finite [[Galois extension|~aaec2530]].
Then
$$|\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)| = [L : K].$$

## Remarks

Conversely, a finite extension with $|\operatorname{Aut}(L/K)| = [L : K]$ is Galois. So the [[general bound|~f531c8fd]] is an equality exactly for Galois extensions.
For $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})/\mathbb{Q}$, the Galois group has order $4$: an automorphism can change the signs of $\sqrt{2}$ and of $\sqrt{3}$ independently.
For $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}/\mathbb{F}_p$, the Galois group is cyclic of order $n$, generated by the [[Frobenius automorphism|~88b222eb]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

For a Galois extension, counting symmetries is the same as computing the degree.
This links the size of a group to the size of a field extension.
