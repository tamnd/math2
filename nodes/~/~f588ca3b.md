## Summary

Let $M \subset \R^n$ be an embedded [[submanifold|VKFW27WXEZ]] with boundary of dimension $k \geq 2$.

An orientation of $M$ is a family of local parametrizations that covers $M$ and is consistent. Each parametrization is a map $\varphi \colon U \to M$ from an open set $U$ of the half space $\mathbb{H}^k$.

Consistent means this. Take two parametrizations $\varphi \colon U \to M$ and $\widetilde{\varphi} \colon \widetilde{U} \to M$ in the family whose images meet. Then the transition map $\widetilde{\varphi}^{-1} \circ \varphi$ is smooth on the set where it is defined. Its [[Jacobian determinant|0ZCSMYHTBP]] must be positive at every point.

One usually takes the largest family with this property. If $M$ has such a family, it is oriented.

For an oriented $M$, restrict the parametrizations to the boundary $\partial \mathbb{H}^k$ of the half space. These restrictions form a family of parametrizations of $\partial M$. The orientation that they give is the induced orientation on $\partial M$.
