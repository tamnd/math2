## Statement

Let $a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n$ be real numbers.
Then the function $p \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ given by $p(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + \cdots + a_n x^n$ is [[continuous|~9520bdea]].

## Remarks

Constant functions and the identity $x \mapsto x$ are continuous. Sums and [[products of continuous functions are continuous|~4ed33a3e]], so induction on $n$ gives the result.
Mathlib states it as `Polynomial.continuous`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Polynomials are built from $x$ and constants by addition and multiplication.
These operations keep continuity, so each polynomial is continuous.
