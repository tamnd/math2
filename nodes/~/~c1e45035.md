## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Lie theory and topological groups|~ce40ba2a]].
It covers solvable, unipotent and reductive linear algebraic groups over an algebraically closed field: Borel subgroups, tori, root data, the Bruhat decomposition and the classification by root data.
