## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical economics and social sciences|~c2a9947c]].
It covers the axiomatic theory of the aggregation of individual preferences into a social preference or choice, from Condorcet's paradox and Arrow's theorem to domain restrictions and spatial voting.
