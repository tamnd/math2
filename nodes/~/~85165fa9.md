## Statement

Define an order on the [[rational numbers|~e25d26db]] as follows. For integers $a, c$ and positive integers $b, d$, let $\frac{a}{b} \le \frac{c}{d}$ when $a d \le b c$ in $\mathbb{Z}$.
This relation is well defined, and with it $\mathbb{Q}$ is an [[ordered field|~5ec46103]].

## Remarks

The order on $\mathbb{Q}$ extends the [[order on the integers|~583ba74a]].
This is the only order on $\mathbb{Q}$ that makes it an ordered field.
The ordered field $\mathbb{Q}$ is Archimedean, but it is not complete: the set $\{q \in \mathbb{Q} : q^2 < 2\}$ has no least upper bound in $\mathbb{Q}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

To compare two fractions, write them with positive denominators and cross-multiply.
For example, $2/3 < 3/4$ because $2 \cdot 4 = 8 < 9 = 3 \cdot 3$.
