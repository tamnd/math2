## Summary

This area studies ordinary differential equations, which relate an unknown function of one variable to its derivatives.
A solution of an ordinary differential equation on an interval is a differentiable function that satisfies the equation at each point of the interval.
Ordinary differential equations are the main models for systems that change in time, in mechanics, electrical circuits, chemistry, biology and economics.
Newton and Leibniz solved the first differential equations in the seventeenth century, and Cauchy proved the first general existence theorem in the nineteenth century.
This topic collects the ordinary differential equations pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Dynamical systems, partial differential equations and numerical methods for differential equations have their own topics.
