## Summary

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be open. The Jacobian matrix of a map $f \colon U \to \mathbb{R}^m$ at a point $a$ is the $m \times n$ matrix of all first [[partial derivatives|~4c15835a]] $\partial f_i / \partial x_j$ at $a$.
When $f$ is differentiable at $a$, it is the matrix of the [[total derivative|~5145a974]] $Df(a)$ in the standard bases.
