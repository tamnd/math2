## Example

Let $U_1, U_2$ be nonempty sets, and give each the universal relation, so each is a frame of [[S5|VC3A2VWXND]].
In the [[product frame|~65e11534]] $U_1 \times U_2$, $(u, v) R_h (u', v')$ iff $v = v'$, and $(u, v) R_v (u', v')$ iff $u = u'$.
A valuation gives each variable $p$ a binary relation $P \subseteq U_1 \times U_2$.

1. $(u, v) \models \lozenge_1 \varphi$ iff $(u', v) \models \varphi$ for some $u' \in U_1$. So $\lozenge_1$ acts as $\exists x$ and $\square_1$ as $\forall x$.
2. In the same way, $\lozenge_2$ acts as $\exists y$ and $\square_2$ as $\forall y$.
3. So $\lozenge_1 \square_2 p$ reads $\exists x \forall y \, P(x, y)$, and $\square_2 \lozenge_1 p$ reads $\forall y \exists x \, P(x, y)$.
4. The valid formula $\lozenge_1 \square_2 p \to \square_2 \lozenge_1 p$ is the first-order law $\exists x \forall y \, P \to \forall y \exists x \, P$.
5. Now let $U_1 = U_2 = \set{0, 1}$, and let $P = \set{(0, 0), (1, 1)}$ be the diagonal.
6. For every $y$, $P(y, y)$ holds, so $\square_2 \lozenge_1 p$ is true at every point.
7. No $x$ has $P(x, 0)$ and $P(x, 1)$, so $\lozenge_1 \square_2 p$ is false at every point.
8. So the converse $\square_2 \lozenge_1 p \to \lozenge_1 \square_2 p$ fails, as $\forall y \exists x$ does not imply $\exists x \forall y$.

## Remarks

The example shows the main idea of $\mathsf{S5} \times \mathsf{S5}$: it is a modal notation for a fragment of first-order logic with two variables $x$ and $y$.
The axiom $\square_1 \square_2 p \leftrightarrow \square_2 \square_1 p$ is the law $\forall x \forall y \leftrightarrow \forall y \forall x$.
Its algebras are two-dimensional diagonal-free [[cylindric algebras|1MDT9BF81H]], with $\lozenge_1$ and $\lozenge_2$ as the cylindrifications.
The logic $\mathsf{S5} \times \mathsf{S5}$ is decidable, while $\mathsf{S5}^3$ is undecidable by the theorem of Hirsch, Hodkinson and Kurucz.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Fill a table with marks.
"Some row is marked in every column" is stronger than "every column has a mark in some row".
In the product of two $\mathsf{S5}$ frames, the two boxes say "in every row" and "in every column", and the modal formulas express exactly this difference.

### L2 human

1. Draw a $2 \times 2$ table and mark the two cells on the diagonal.
2. Each column contains a mark, so "every column has a mark" is true.
3. No row is fully marked, so "some row is marked in every column" is false.
4. This one table refutes $\square_2 \lozenge_1 p \to \lozenge_1 \square_2 p$ at every cell.

The typical use is to read $\mathsf{S5}$ products as quantifier logics and to transfer results from first-order logic.
A trap: the variables of the modal language stand for binary relations with a fixed order of arguments, so this is only a fragment of two-variable logic.
