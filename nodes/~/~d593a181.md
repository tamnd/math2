## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers modular forms on groups other than the modular group and its congruence subgroups, such as general Fuchsian groups, unitary and orthogonal groups, and Drinfeld modular forms.
