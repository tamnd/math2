## Statement

Let $n$ be any [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]], where the natural numbers include $0$.
Then adding $0$ to $n$ on either side gives back $n$:
$$n + 0 = n \quad\text{and}\quad 0 + n = n.$$
Here $+$ is the [[addition of natural numbers|0W5DSDFQ4G]].

## Remarks

So $0$ is an identity element for addition on the nonnegative integers.
