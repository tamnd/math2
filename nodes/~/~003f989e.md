## Definition

Let $A$ be a set.
A preorder on $A$ is a [[binary relation|~41fc00d0]] $\le$ on $A$ that is [[reflexive|~cda728b2]] and [[transitive|~675931ef]]. For all $a, b, c \in A$:

1. $a \le a$;
2. $a \le b$ and $b \le c$ imply $a \le c$.

## Remarks

Divisibility on $\mathbb{Z}$ is a preorder. It is not antisymmetric, because $2 \mid -2$ and $-2 \mid 2$.
An antisymmetric preorder is a [[partial order|~dd266d19]].
For a preorder, $a \le b$ and $b \le a$ together define an [[equivalence relation|~4e2853d5]]. The preorder gives a partial order on the [[quotient set|~6df55656]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Compare people by height with "is at most as tall as".
This is a preorder: two different people can have the same height.
