## Definition

Let $n$ be a positive integer.
The alternating group $A_n$ is the set of the [[even permutations|~6663e04f]] in $S_n$, with composition as the operation.

## Remarks

For $n \geq 2$, $A_n$ is the [[kernel|~695eca04]] of the [[sign homomorphism|~d9bbcc4a]], so it is a [[normal subgroup|~ebf813cb]] of $S_n$.
For $n \geq 2$, $A_n$ is also the [[commutator subgroup|~b33e4ba0]] of $S_n$.
The group $A_3$ is cyclic of order $3$, and $A_4$ has order $12$.
The group $A_n$ is abelian if and only if $n \leq 3$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The alternating group keeps only the even permutations.
The product of two even permutations is even, so these form a group.
For $n \geq 5$ it is one of the most important examples of a simple group.
