## Summary

Mathematics and culture is the study of mathematics as a part of human culture today.
It looks at the links of mathematics with music, the visual arts, architecture, literature and film, and at the way mathematicians work, publish and give credit.
Some of these links produce mathematics of their own, such as the theory of musical tuning, the symmetry groups of ornament and the rules of constrained writing.
Pythagorean tuning, which builds a scale from the frequency ratios 2 to 1 and 3 to 2, is one of the oldest uses of mathematics in an art.
This topic collects the mathematics and culture pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
The history of mathematics, the philosophy of mathematics, mathematics education and recreational mathematics have their own topics.
