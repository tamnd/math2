## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]] and let $v$ be a vertex of $G$ with a finite [[neighbourhood|~2e4eb2d2]] $N(v)$.
The degree of $v$ is the number
$$\deg(v) = |N(v)|$$
of vertices adjacent to $v$. It is also the number of edges that contain $v$.

## Remarks

A vertex of degree $0$ is isolated, and a vertex of degree $1$ is a leaf.
A graph is locally finite when each vertex has finite degree. Each finite graph is locally finite.
In a [[multigraph|~fac563f7]], the degree counts edges with multiplicity, and a loop counts twice.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The degree of a vertex is the number of edges at that vertex.
In a friendship graph it is the number of friends of a person.
