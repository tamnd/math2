This topic is a part of [[complex geometry and several complex variables|~5ce2f64b]].
It covers the harmonic theory of compact complex and Kähler manifolds, the Kähler identities, the Hodge decomposition, the Lefschetz theorems and the Hodge-Riemann relations.
