## Summary

The quotient ring of a ring by a two-sided ideal is the set of the cosets of the ideal.
Its operations come from the operations of the ring.
The integers modulo $n$ are the main example.
The [[definition of the quotient ring|~fcc6d7c6]] gives the construction.
