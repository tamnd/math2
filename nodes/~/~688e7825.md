## Summary

This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|~1c787828]].
It covers the analytic foundations of gauge theory, with the gauge group that acts on connections, the Yang-Mills functional, gauge fixing, compactness and regularity, the construction of instanton moduli spaces and gluing.
