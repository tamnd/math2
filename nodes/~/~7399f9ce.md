## Example

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $H$ be a [[subgroup|~b600248b]] of $G$.
Let $G/H$ be the set of the [[left cosets|~9c4c9f74]] of $H$ in $G$.
The map $G \times G/H \to G/H$, $(g, aH) \mapsto gaH$, is an [[action|~0ceb002c]] of $G$ on $G/H$.
The map is well defined, because $aH = bH$ implies $gaH = gbH$.
The action is [[transitive|~1e6a929e]], because $(ba^{-1}) \cdot aH = bH$.
The [[stabiliser|~847dc0b4]] of the coset $aH$ is the conjugate subgroup $aHa^{-1}$.
The kernel of the associated homomorphism $G \to \operatorname{Sym}(G/H)$ is the [[normal core|~5510a64e]] of $H$, the intersection of all conjugates of $H$.
For $H = \{e\}$, this is the [[action by left multiplication|~e06801e5]].
