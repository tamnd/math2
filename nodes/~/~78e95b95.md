## Example

Let $a$ and $r$ be real numbers with $|r| < 1$.
The geometric series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a r^n$ converges, and
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a r^n = \frac{a}{1 - r}.$$
The [[sum of a finite geometric series|~c2f57d88]] gives the partial sums $s_N = a (1 - r^{N+1})/(1 - r)$.
Since [[$r^{N+1} \to 0$|~417e3c2d]], the partial sums tend to $a/(1 - r)$.

## Remarks

For $a \ne 0$ and $|r| \ge 1$, the series diverges by the [[term test|~b65556fc]], because $|a r^n| \ge |a|$.
For $a = 1$ and $r = 1/2$, it gives $1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + \cdots = 2$.
The decimal $0.333\ldots$ is the geometric series with $a = 3/10$ and $r = 1/10$, and its sum is $1/3$.
The terms form a [[geometric sequence|~0bd6125c]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Walk half of the way to a wall, then half of the rest, and so on.
The distances $1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + \cdots$ add up to the whole distance $1$.
