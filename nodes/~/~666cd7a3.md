## Statement

Let $W$ be a subspace of $\R^m$. Let $u_1, \dots, u_n$ be an [[orthonormal basis|0S9GGTR0EX]] of $W$.
Let $Q$ be the $m \times n$ matrix whose columns are $u_1, \dots, u_n$.
Then the [[orthogonal projection|0B8KZRT28M]] of $\R^m$ onto $W$ is the matrix transformation $x \mapsto Px$, where $P = QQ^T$.

## Remarks

Here $QQ^T x = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (u_i \cdot x)\, u_i$, which is the sum of the projections of $x$ onto the lines spanned by the $u_i$.
The matrix $P$ is symmetric and satisfies $P^2 = P$.
Note that $Q^TQ$ is the $n \times n$ identity matrix, but $QQ^T$ is the identity only when $W = \R^m$.
