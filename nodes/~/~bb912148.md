## Statement

In classical logic, for each [[proposition|~c068c095]] $p$, the proposition $\neg \neg p$ is [[logically equivalent|~01a642be]] to $p$.
$$\neg \neg p \equiv p$$

## Remarks

The implication $p \to \neg \neg p$ is also valid in intuitionistic logic.
The other direction, $\neg \neg p \to p$, needs classical logic.
This direction is the basis of [[proof by contradiction|~05010c83]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

"It is not true that $5$ is not prime" says the same as "$5$ is prime".
Two negations cancel each other.
