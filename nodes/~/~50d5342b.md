## Summary

An irreducible polynomial is a nonconstant polynomial that is not a product of two polynomials of smaller degree, over a field.
More generally, it is an irreducible element of a polynomial ring.
The [[definition of an irreducible polynomial|~25830f16]] gives the conditions.
