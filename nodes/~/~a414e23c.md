## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|~3656b229]].
It covers the basic theory of smooth representations of reductive groups over non-archimedean local fields: Hecke algebras, Jacquet modules, supercuspidal representations, the Bernstein decomposition and unramified representations.
