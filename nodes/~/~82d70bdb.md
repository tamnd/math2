## Summary

Functional analysis is the study of vector spaces of infinite dimension that carry a topology, and of the continuous linear maps between them.
A Banach space is a normed vector space that is complete, so that each Cauchy sequence in it converges.
Function spaces such as the Lp spaces and the Sobolev spaces are Banach spaces, and this frame is used in the theory of differential equations, in probability and in quantum mechanics.
Hilbert, Riesz, Banach and von Neumann built the subject in the first half of the twentieth century.
This topic collects the functional analysis pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Operator theory, operator algebras and harmonic analysis have their own topics.
