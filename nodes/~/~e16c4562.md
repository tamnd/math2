## Statement

Let $z$ be a complex number, let $\bar{z}$ be its [[conjugate|~46f1a741]] and let $|z|$ be its [[modulus|~a255a1b0]].
Then
$$z \bar{z} = |z|^2.$$
In particular, $z \bar{z}$ is a nonnegative real number.

## Remarks

With $z = a + b i$: $(a + b i)(a - b i) = a^2 - (b i)^2 = a^2 + b^2$.
This identity gives the formula for the [[inverse of a complex number|~1f49e9c8]].
It also gives a quick proof that the [[modulus is multiplicative|~2172b4be]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

When you multiply a complex number by its conjugate, the imaginary parts cancel.
What remains is the square of the length of the number, for example $(3 + 4 i)(3 - 4 i) = 9 + 16 = 25$.
