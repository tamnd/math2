## Statement

Let $a < b$ and let $f \colon [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous.
Then there is $c \in [a, b]$ with
$$\int_a^b f(x) \, dx = f(c) \, (b - a).$$

## Remarks

Let $m$ and $M$ be the minimum and the maximum of $f$, from the [[extreme value theorem|~8ce35667]]. By [[monotonicity|~9809027d]], the average $\frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b f$ lies in $[m, M]$. The [[intermediate value theorem|~ba9d91ae]] gives $c$.
The number $\frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b f$ is the average value of $f$ on $[a, b]$.
Mathlib has a form for averages, `exists_eq_interval_average`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A continuous function takes its average value somewhere.
The area under the graph equals the area of a rectangle whose height is a value of $f$.
