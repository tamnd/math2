## Definition

Let $R \subseteq A \times B$ be a [[binary relation|~41fc00d0]] from $A$ to $B$.
The converse of $R$ is the relation from $B$ to $A$
$$R^{-1} = \{(b, a) \in B \times A \mid (a, b) \in R\}.$$
So $b \mathrel{R^{-1}} a$ if and only if $a \mathrel{R} b$.

## Remarks

Other names are the inverse relation and the transpose. Another notation is $R^{T}$.
The converse of $<$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is $>$.
The converse of the converse of $R$ is $R$.
For a [[bijection|~14a2ca3d]] $f$, the converse of the graph of $f$ is the graph of the [[inverse function|~6b98c003]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

To get the converse, read each pair backwards.
The converse of "is a child of" is "is a parent of".
