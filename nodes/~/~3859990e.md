## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[graph|~3f82cef4]].
A spanning tree of $G$ is a [[subgraph|~e95ddbc1]] $T$ of $G$ that contains every vertex of $G$ and is a [[tree|~29e53124]].

## Remarks

A graph has a spanning tree if and only if it is connected. The node [[every connected graph has a spanning tree|~93442e0f]] gives one direction.
A spanning tree of a finite connected graph with $n$ vertices has $n - 1$ edges.
The complete graph $K_n$ has $n^{n-2}$ spanning trees, by [[Cayley's formula|~5d710fd8]]. A [[minimum spanning tree|~d3fa04ae]] has the least total weight.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A spanning tree keeps all the vertices of a graph and just enough edges to stay connected without any loop.
For a road network, it is a smallest set of roads that still links all towns.
