This topic is a part of [[mathematical economics and social sciences|~c2a9947c]].
It covers the Arrow-Debreu model, the existence theorems by fixed points, the structure of the equilibrium set, the dynamics of price adjustment, equilibrium under uncertainty and incomplete markets, and the computation of equilibria.
