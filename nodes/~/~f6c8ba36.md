## Summary

This topic is a part of [[electromagnetism and optics|~b5a2c73b]].
It covers the response of matter to intense light, with nonlinear susceptibilities, three-wave and four-wave mixing, phase matching, the optical Kerr effect, self-focusing and optical solitons.
