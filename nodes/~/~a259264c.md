## Summary

The del operator $\nabla = \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial x_1}, \dots, \frac{\partial}{\partial x_n} \right)$ is a vector of partial derivative operators on $\mathbb{R}^n$.
It gives a short notation for the [[gradient|~aca5eb00]] $\nabla f$, the [[divergence|~c81d52f0]] $\nabla \cdot F$, the [[curl|~ac308283]] $\nabla \times F$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and the [[Laplacian|~7d34da67]] $\nabla^2 f = \nabla \cdot \nabla f$.

## Remarks

The notation treats $\nabla$ like a vector, but it is an operator. For example, $\nabla \cdot F$ and $F \cdot \nabla$ are different: the first is a function, and the second is the operator $\sum_i F_i \, \partial / \partial x_i$.
The directional derivative of $f$ along $v$ is $(v \cdot \nabla) f = v \cdot \nabla f$.
The symbol $\nabla$ is called nabla.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Del is a shorthand that makes vector calculus formulas look like vector algebra.
Multiply it by a function to get the gradient. Dot it with a field to get the divergence, and cross it with a field to get the curl.
