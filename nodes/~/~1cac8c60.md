## Example

We list the [[signatures|WDVVEYD6AT]] of the most common first-order languages, each with its arities and one typical axiom.
For each item we check that every symbol has one fixed arity, and that the axiom is a [[sentence|DKFGQTRYZE]] over that signature.

1. Pure equality: the empty signature. A typical axiom is $\exists x \, \exists y \, \neg (x = y)$, "there are at least two elements". It uses only variables and $=$.
2. Orders: $\set{<}$, one binary relation symbol. The [[total orders|0FRXBVVR03]] satisfy $\forall x \, \forall y \, (x < y \lor x = y \lor y < x)$. Each atomic part applies $<$ to two variables.
3. Graphs: $\set{E}$, one binary relation symbol. A [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] satisfies $\forall x \, \neg E(x, x)$ and $\forall x \, \forall y \, (E(x, y) \to E(y, x))$.
4. Groups: $\set{\cdot, {}^{-1}, e}$ with arities $2, 1, 0$. The inverse axiom of [[groups|G5S1M9]] is $\forall x \, (x \cdot x^{-1} = e \land x^{-1} \cdot x = e)$. Here $x^{-1}$ is the term ${}^{-1}(x)$, written in the usual way.
5. Rings: $\set{+, -, \cdot, 0, 1}$ with arities $2, 1, 2, 0, 0$. A [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] satisfies $\forall x \, \forall y \, \forall z \, (x \cdot (y + z) = x \cdot y + x \cdot z)$.
6. Fields: the ring signature again. The axiom $\forall x \, (\neg (x = 0) \to \exists y \, (x \cdot y = 1))$ of [[fields|04C5ZBG1WT]] uses no new symbol. Ordered fields add $<$.
7. Arithmetic: $\set{0, S, +, \cdot}$ with arities $0, 1, 2, 2$. The [[Peano axioms|0QFT7Z76N5]] include $\forall x \, \neg (S(x) = 0)$. Some books add $<$ as a binary relation symbol.
8. Set theory: $\set{\in}$, one binary relation symbol. The axiom of extensionality of [[ZF|0HE5WDQ9FJ]] is $\forall x \, \forall y \, (\forall z \, (z \in x \leftrightarrow z \in y) \to x = y)$.
9. Vector spaces over a fixed field $K$: $\set{+, -, 0} \cup \set{\lambda_k \mid k \in K}$, with a unary symbol $\lambda_k$ for "multiply by $k$". A [[vector space|000AYPA9AJ]] satisfies $\forall x \, \forall y \, (\lambda_k(x + y) = \lambda_k(x) + \lambda_k(y))$ for each $k$.
10. Lattices: $\set{\sqcup, \sqcap}$ with arities $2, 2$. The absorption law is $\forall x \, \forall y \, (x \sqcup (x \sqcap y) = x)$. The symbols differ from the connectives $\lor$ and $\land$.

In each item every symbol has one arity, and each axiom has no free variables, so each is a sentence over its signature.

## Remarks

The choice of signature changes the substructures: in the signature of item 4, every substructure of a group is a subgroup.
Item 9 has one symbol for each scalar, so for $K = \mathbb{R}$ the signature is uncountable; [[many-sorted logic|~4886a7ff]] treats scalars as a second sort instead.
Some languages are relational on purpose: item 3 and item 8 have no function symbols.
A set of axioms over one of these signatures is a [[first-order theory|92SWXST1ZM]], such as the theory of groups or Peano arithmetic.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Each area of mathematics has its own small list of symbols.
Group theory uses multiplication, inverse and the identity.
Ring theory uses addition, negation, multiplication, $0$ and $1$.
Set theory uses only the membership symbol $\in$.
With these lists fixed, the axioms of each area can be written as first-order sentences.

### L2 human

We check one entry in detail: the field axiom of item 6.

1. The symbols $\cdot$, $0$ and $1$ are in the ring signature, with arities $2$, $0$, $0$.
2. The strings $x = 0$ and $x \cdot y = 1$ are atomic formulas, since $x$, $0$, $x \cdot y$ and $1$ are terms.
3. The quantifier $\exists y$ binds $y$, and $\forall x$ binds $x$. No variable is free, so the axiom is a sentence.
4. The axiom is not universal, so a substructure of a field need not be a field: $\mathbb{Z}$ is a substructure of $\mathbb{Q}$ in the ring signature, and $2$ has no inverse in $\mathbb{Z}$.

The typical use of this list is as a reference when an exercise says "in the language of rings" or "in the language of arithmetic".
A trap: "the language of fields" is the ring signature, without a symbol for division, because division by $0$ is undefined and every function symbol must be total.
