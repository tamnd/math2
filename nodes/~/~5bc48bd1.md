## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $A = (a_{ij})$ be an $n \times n$ [[square matrix|~92565f6b]] over $R$.
The *trace* of $A$ is
$$\tr A = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{ii} = a_{11} + a_{22} + \cdots + a_{nn}.$$

## Remarks

The trace of $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ is $5$.
The trace is [[linear|~6ee6674a]], it is [[invariant under transpose|~e6a5bd62]], and [[$\tr(AB) = \tr(BA)$|~a43cc644]].
Similar matrices have the same trace, so the [[trace of a linear map|~62cc6894]] is well defined.
Over an algebraically closed field, the trace is the [[sum of the eigenvalues|~7c1a6c14]], counted with multiplicity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The trace adds up the numbers on the main diagonal of a square matrix.
It is a single number that does not change when we change the basis.
For the identity matrix of order $n$, the trace is $n$.
