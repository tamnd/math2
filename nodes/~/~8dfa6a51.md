This topic is a part of [[analytic number theory|~124efb1e]].
It covers asymptotic formulas for sums of arithmetic functions, the divisor and circle problems, the densities of squarefree and coprime integers, and the largest and smallest values of the divisor, sum of divisors and totient functions.
