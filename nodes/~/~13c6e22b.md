## Example

Let $m$ and $n$ be natural numbers.
The complete bipartite graph $K_{m,n}$ is the [[graph|~3f82cef4]] whose vertex set is a disjoint union $A \sqcup B$ with $|A| = m$ and $|B| = n$.
Two vertices are adjacent if and only if one is in $A$ and the other is in $B$.

## Remarks

$K_{m,n}$ has $m + n$ vertices and $mn$ edges.
It is [[bipartite|~729625d7]] with parts $A$ and $B$. For $m, n \ge 1$ its [[chromatic number|~37c0ab6a]] is $2$.
$K_{1,n}$ is a star. [[$K_{3,3}$ is not planar|~42286052]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Split the vertices into two groups and join every vertex of one group to every vertex of the other group.
There are no edges inside a group.
