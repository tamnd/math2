## Example

Let $X$ be a set and let $\tau = \{\varnothing, X\}$.
Then $\tau$ is a [[topology|~77bfcc55]] on $X$, the indiscrete topology, also called the trivial topology.

## Remarks

It is the coarsest topology on $X$.
Each function from any topological space to an indiscrete space is [[continuous|~18b16c9f]].
In an indiscrete space, each sequence converges to each point. Thus, if $X$ has at least two points, the space is not [[Hausdorff|~a1fe0c25]] and not [[metrizable|~db159499]].
Each indiscrete space is [[compact|~68153d33]] and [[connected|~711a13dd]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

In the indiscrete topology, the only open sets are the empty set and the whole space.
The topology cannot tell any two points apart.
