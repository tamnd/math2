## Statement

Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ real matrix. Let $v$ be a vector in $\mathbb R^m$ and $w$ a vector in $\mathbb R^n$.
Then the [[dot product|0S52TNNTH9]] of $v$ with the [[product|0PDVQEP0ZC]] $Aw$ equals the dot product of $A^T v$ with $w$.
Here $A^T$ is the [[transpose|09DXWYRD43]] of $A$.

In particular, suppose $A$ is a [[symmetric matrix|0VR671PHE5]], so $A$ is $n \times n$ and $A^T = A$.
Then $v \cdot (Aw) = (Av) \cdot w$ for all $v, w$ in $\mathbb R^n$.

## Remarks

The identity says that $A^T$ plays the role of an adjoint of $A$ for the dot product.
It follows from writing the dot product as the matrix product $v^T(Aw)$ and using $(v^T A) = (A^T v)^T$.
