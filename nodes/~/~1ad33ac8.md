## Summary

This topic is a part of [[operator algebras and noncommutative geometry|~d4b9579e]].
It covers the classification of simple separable nuclear C*-algebras by K-theory and traces, with AF and UHF algebras, Kirchberg algebras, the Jiang-Su algebra and Z-stability, regularity properties and the counterexamples.
