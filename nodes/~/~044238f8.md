## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|~2c3bc0e0]].
It covers rings of invariants of groups that act on commutative rings: finite generation, degree bounds, Hilbert series, the Cohen-Macaulay property, Hilbert's fourteenth problem and modular invariant theory.
