## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|~2c3bc0e0]].
It covers the Frobenius map as a tool for rings of prime characteristic: perfect and F-finite rings, Kunz's theorem, tight closure, F-singularities, Hilbert-Kunz multiplicity and F-signature, Witt vectors and reduction to characteristic p.
