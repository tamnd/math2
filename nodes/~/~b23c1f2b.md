## Summary

Harmonic analysis is the study of functions through their decomposition into basic waves.
Its central tool is the Fourier transform, which writes a function on the circle, on n-dimensional space or on a locally compact abelian group in terms of the characters of the group.
Fourier methods are used to solve the heat and wave equations, to analyse signals, and in number theory and probability.
Fourier used trigonometric series in his work on heat conduction at the start of the nineteenth century, and the question of their convergence shaped much of modern analysis.
This topic collects the harmonic analysis pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Representation theory, functional analysis and approximation theory have their own topics.
