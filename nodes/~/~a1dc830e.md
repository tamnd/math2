## Summary

This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|~d000caef]].
It covers the Ising model of ferromagnetism, the rigorous proofs of its phase transition, correlation inequalities, the Lee-Yang theorem, the exact solution in two dimensions and the modern results on its critical behaviour.
