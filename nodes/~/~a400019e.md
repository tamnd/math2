## Summary

The hyperbolic tangent is the quotient of the hyperbolic sine by the hyperbolic cosine. It is a bounded, odd, strictly increasing function with range $(-1, 1)$. The node [[hyperbolic tangent|~77b8adc1]] gives the precise definition.
