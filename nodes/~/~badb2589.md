## Summary

This glossary gives a short definition of each main term of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]], in alphabetical order.
Each entry links to the page of the term when that page exists.
The definitions are short and exact, but they leave out examples and proofs.
The glossary is a tool for quick reference: a reader who meets an unknown word in another page can find its meaning here in one line.

## Motivation

Group theory has a large vocabulary, and many terms are defined in terms of other terms.
For example, a nilpotent group is defined with the lower central series, which uses commutators, which use inverses.
A reader who studies one page often needs the meaning of a word from another sub-area.
This glossary collects these meanings in one place.

## Notation

In this glossary, $G$ is a group with the operation written as a product $ab$.
The identity element of $G$ is $e$, and the inverse of $a$ is $a^{-1}$.
We write $H \le G$ when $H$ is a subgroup of $G$, and $N \trianglelefteq G$ when $N$ is a normal subgroup of $G$.
We write $|G|$ for the order of $G$, $[G : H]$ for the index of $H$ in $G$, and $\langle S \rangle$ for the subgroup generated by a subset $S$.
Some books write $1$ in place of $e$, and they use additive notation $a + b$, $0$ and $-a$ for abelian groups.
The word "order" has two meanings: the order $|G|$ of a group and the order of an element.

## Remarks

### A to C

- [[Abelian group|082GHNPJ53]]: a group in which $ab = ba$ for all $a, b \in G$.
- [[Abelianisation|0AQZV0905N]]: the quotient $G / [G, G]$ of $G$ by its commutator subgroup. It is the largest abelian quotient of $G$.
- [[Additive group of integers|0BSCJA5G53]]: the set $\mathbb{Z}$ with addition. It is the infinite cyclic group.
- [[Alternating group|0Z5905Q73D]]: the group $A_n$ of the even permutations of $\{1, \ldots, n\}$. For $n \ge 2$ it has order $n!/2$.
- [[Associativity|0K8F7PVDMX]]: the law $(ab)c = a(bc)$ for all $a, b, c$.
- [[Automorphism|DSQ17TNW1C]]: an isomorphism from a group to itself.
- [[Automorphism group|0K1SCPBNKJ]]: the group $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ of all automorphisms of $G$, with composition as the operation.
- [[Binary operation|0M418FE7BB]]: a function $S \times S \to S$ on a set $S$.
- [[Burnside's lemma|0Z47M1B2VS]]: for a finite group acting on a finite set, the number of orbits is the average number of fixed points of the elements.
- [[Cancellation laws|0VSNX4P2Q0]]: in a group, $ab = ac$ implies $b = c$, and $ba = ca$ implies $b = c$.
- [[Cauchy's theorem|0JM7PA288B]]: when a prime $p$ divides the order of a finite group $G$, then $G$ has an element of order $p$.
- [[Cayley graph|0GYQK9NDJF]]: for a generating set $S$ of $G$, the graph with the elements of $G$ as vertices and an edge from $g$ to $gs$ for each $s \in S$.
- [[Cayley table|0YB2NRP0T5]]: the multiplication table of a finite group.
- [[Cayley's theorem|0151VYVX27]]: each group is isomorphic to a subgroup of the group of permutations of its own elements.
- [[Centraliser|02YYV36BFM]]: the subgroup $C_G(a) = \{ g \in G : ga = ag \}$ of the elements that commute with $a$.
- [[Centre of a group|0S7Y9Q990Q]]: the subgroup $Z(G) = \{ z \in G : zg = gz \text{ for all } g \in G \}$.
- Centreless group: a group whose centre is $\{e\}$. For example, the symmetric group $S_n$ is centreless for $n \ge 3$.
- [[Characteristic subgroup|0KABPMRHM2]]: a subgroup $H$ with $\varphi(H) = H$ for each automorphism $\varphi$ of $G$. A characteristic subgroup is normal.
- [[Circle group|04EAFVFTF7]]: the group of the complex numbers of absolute value $1$, with multiplication.
- [[Class equation|08BPSC6YTE]]: for a finite group, $|G| = |Z(G)| + \sum_i [G : C_G(x_i)]$. The $x_i$ represent the conjugacy classes with more than one element.
- [[Classification of finite simple groups|06J6P03MG7]]: each finite simple group is cyclic of prime order, alternating, of Lie type or one of $26$ sporadic groups.
- Commutator: the element $[a, b] = a^{-1} b^{-1} a b$. It equals $e$ exactly when $ab = ba$.
- [[Commutator subgroup|0ASGSTXSBW]]: the subgroup $[G, G]$ generated by all commutators. It is also called the derived subgroup $G'$.
- [[Commutativity|099E72KTF8]]: the law $ab = ba$.
- [[Composition series|0XV2A49X6Q]]: a chain $\{e\} = G_0 \trianglelefteq G_1 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq G_n = G$ in which each factor $G_{i+1} / G_i$ is simple.
- [[Conjugacy class|0DEBXAJ5SA]]: the set $\{ g a g^{-1} : g \in G \}$ of all conjugates of an element $a$.
- Conjugacy-closed subgroup: a subgroup $H$ such that two elements of $H$ that are conjugate in $G$ are also conjugate in $H$. The subgroup $A_3$ of $S_3$ is not conjugacy-closed.
- [[Conjugate elements|0DXD4QSZ8A]]: elements $a, b$ with $b = g a g^{-1}$ for some $g \in G$.
- [[Conjugate subgroup|0H11ZPX4ND]]: a subgroup of the form $g H g^{-1}$.
- Contranormal subgroup: a subgroup whose normal closure is the whole group. For example, $\langle (1\,2) \rangle$ is contranormal in $S_3$.
- [[Coset|564VVAQTNR]]: a set $gH = \{ gh : h \in H \}$, a left coset, or $Hg$, a right coset, for a subgroup $H$.
- [[Cycle|0Z05VCBQ7Y]]: a permutation $(a_1\, a_2\, \cdots\, a_k)$ that sends $a_1 \mapsto a_2 \mapsto \cdots \mapsto a_k \mapsto a_1$ and fixes all other points.
- [[Cyclic group|0KVGH0J5B3]]: a group generated by one element.
- [[Cyclic subgroup|08ABT85339]]: the subgroup $\langle a \rangle = \{ a^n : n \in \mathbb{Z} \}$ generated by one element $a$.

### D to H

- [[Derived series|0Q5G5DJ4P2]]: the chain $G \ge G' \ge G'' \ge \cdots$, in which each term is the commutator subgroup of the term before it.
- [[Dihedral group|0JZ27DMJ5D]]: the group of the symmetries of a regular polygon with $n$ sides. It has order $2n$, and books write it $D_n$ or $D_{2n}$.
- [[Direct product of groups|00HF4RJ5QN]]: the set $G \times H$ with the operation $(g, h)(g', h') = (g g', h h')$.
- [[Divisible group|ZXVBEPMTCN]]: an abelian group $A$ such that for each $a \in A$ and each integer $n \ge 1$ the equation $nx = a$ has a solution.
- [[Elementary abelian group|DZSZ8ZS554]]: an abelian group in which each element other than $e$ has order $p$, for one prime $p$.
- [[Elementary theory of groups|~0757d1ee]]: the set of the first-order sentences that are true in all groups.
- [[Endomorphism|0J7H0YBFDJ]]: a homomorphism from a group to itself.
- [[Exponent of a group|0ZDW0H0VX4]]: the smallest integer $n \ge 1$ with $g^n = e$ for all $g \in G$, when such an integer exists.
- [[Faithful action|0P7G9NGG2G]]: an action in which only the identity element acts as the identity map.
- [[Finitely generated group|082DWWXMRJ]]: a group that is generated by a finite subset.
- [[First isomorphism theorem|0CXXJNG5B6]]: for a homomorphism $\varphi$ from $G$, the quotient $G / \ker \varphi$ is isomorphic to the image of $\varphi$.
- [[Fitting subgroup|N219CDB623]]: the largest nilpotent normal subgroup of a finite group.
- [[Frattini subgroup|KXJG5VV340]]: the intersection of all maximal subgroups of $G$. It is $G$ itself when $G$ has no maximal subgroup.
- [[Free abelian group|0WJX8MGG37]]: an abelian group isomorphic to a direct sum of copies of $\mathbb{Z}$.
- [[Free group|0KTW26C80J]]: the group $F(S)$ of the reduced words in the letters $s$ and $s^{-1}$ for $s \in S$. Each map from $S$ to a group $H$ extends to exactly one homomorphism $F(S) \to H$.
- [[Free product|TM7QS7P7X9]]: the group $G * H$ of the reduced words whose letters come from $G$ and $H$ in turn.
- [[Frobenius group|JXS91FGFHY]]: a transitive permutation group on a finite set in which no element other than $e$ fixes two points. Some element other than $e$ must fix a point.
- [[General linear group|0FCHE902D7]]: the group $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ of the invertible $n \times n$ matrices over a field $F$, with matrix multiplication.
- [[Generators of a group|0MBV2VHMXA]]: a subset $S$ with $\langle S \rangle = G$.
- [[Group|G5S1M9]]: a set with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and an inverse for each element.
- [[Group action|0WCY42EV9V]]: a map $G \times X \to X$, $(g, x) \mapsto g \cdot x$, with $e \cdot x = x$ and $g \cdot (h \cdot x) = (gh) \cdot x$.
- [[Group extension|MF4N3VGGFV]]: a group $G$ with a normal subgroup $N$ such that $G / N$ is isomorphic to a given group $Q$.
- [[Group homomorphism|0JYTG5K9Y4]]: a map $\varphi \colon G \to H$ with $\varphi(ab) = \varphi(a) \varphi(b)$ for all $a, b \in G$.
- [[Group isomorphism|08MG7H8JW5]]: a bijective homomorphism. Isomorphic groups have the same structure.
- [[Group of nth roots of unity|0WX11EA3F3]]: the group $\mu_n$ of the complex numbers $z$ with $z^n = 1$. It is cyclic of order $n$.
- [[Hall subgroup|KPWW9X14V2]]: a subgroup $H$ of a finite group whose order $|H|$ is coprime to its index $[G : H]$.
- [[Holomorph|V13JPPAZ4J]]: the semidirect product $G \rtimes \operatorname{Aut}(G)$.
- [[Hopfian group|PHEN4E1JPT]]: a group in which each surjective endomorphism is an isomorphism.

### I to N

- [[Identity element|07WYQPB8E3]]: the element $e$ with $ea = ae = a$ for all $a$.
- [[Image of a homomorphism|0112DTS464]]: the subgroup $\varphi(G) = \{ \varphi(g) : g \in G \}$ of $H$, for a homomorphism $\varphi \colon G \to H$.
- [[Index of a subgroup|0DTXBQPA2S]]: the number $[G : H]$ of left cosets of $H$ in $G$.
- [[Inner automorphism|0RN00NTM3S]]: an automorphism $x \mapsto g x g^{-1}$ for a fixed $g \in G$. The inner automorphisms form a group isomorphic to $G / Z(G)$.
- [[Internal direct product|0VBZH90R1A]]: a group $G$ with normal subgroups $H$ and $K$ such that $HK = G$ and $H \cap K = \{e\}$. Then $G$ is isomorphic to $H \times K$.
- [[Inverse element|0ZNZH0EXFX]]: for an element $a$, the element $a^{-1}$ with $a a^{-1} = a^{-1} a = e$.
- Isotropy group: another name for the stabilizer $G_x$ of a point $x$ under a group action.
- [[Jordan-Hölder theorem|0E088ZP533]]: two composition series of a group have the same length and the same factors, up to isomorphism and order.
- [[Kernel of a homomorphism|0W88CEGQP1]]: the normal subgroup $\ker \varphi = \{ g \in G : \varphi(g) = e \}$.
- [[Klein four-group|0PMRQJVTME]]: the group $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, the smallest group that is not cyclic.
- [[Lagrange's theorem|3C53TD]]: for a subgroup $H$ of a finite group $G$, the order $|H|$ divides $|G|$.
- [[Lattice of subgroups|0B8BW4YWTF]]: the set of all subgroups of $G$, ordered by inclusion.
- [[Laws of exponents|00DRK90Z6K]]: the rules $a^m a^n = a^{m+n}$ and $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$ for all integers $m, n$.
- [[Lower central series|0K98R7A4VC]]: the chain $\gamma_1(G) = G$, $\gamma_{i+1}(G) = [\gamma_i(G), G]$.
- [[Magma|9689SFPVE9]]: a set with one binary operation, with no further law.
- Maximal subgroup: a proper subgroup $H$ of $G$ such that no subgroup lies strictly between $H$ and $G$.
- [[Monoid|0N6R902FG1]]: a set with an associative binary operation and an identity element.
- [[Monster group|C9VY20TAPT]]: the largest sporadic simple group, of order about $8 \cdot 10^{53}$.
- [[Nilpotent group|0Q7MMQHC1P]]: a group whose lower central series reaches $\{e\}$ after finitely many steps.
- [[Normal closure|6XM4CCVTKE]]: the smallest normal subgroup of $G$ that contains a given subset.
- [[Normal core|0GETX79CJN]]: the largest normal subgroup of $G$ contained in $H$. It equals $\bigcap_{g \in G} g H g^{-1}$.
- [[Normal subgroup|0CB0DX5BVV]]: a subgroup $N$ with $g N g^{-1} = N$ for all $g \in G$.
- [[Normalizer|TJ9G1SBBJP]]: the subgroup $N_G(H) = \{ g \in G : g H g^{-1} = H \}$. It is the largest subgroup of $G$ in which $H$ is normal.

### O to R

- [[Opposite group|D33V8HE0KY]]: the set of $G$ with the product $a \ast b = ba$. It is isomorphic to $G$ by the map $a \mapsto a^{-1}$.
- [[Orbit|0YQPZB5Q1G]]: for an action of $G$ on $X$, the set $G \cdot x = \{ g \cdot x : g \in G \}$.
- [[Orbit-stabilizer theorem|0S19CWFZM2]]: for an action of $G$, the size of the orbit of $x$ equals the index $[G : G_x]$ of the stabilizer.
- [[Order of a group|0B85S2V2DR]]: the number $|G|$ of elements of $G$.
- [[Order of a power|0VVPQNVM8S]]: when $a$ has order $n$, the power $a^k$ has order $n / \gcd(n, k)$.
- [[Order of an element|0WE2NHZJ7B]]: the smallest integer $n \ge 1$ with $a^n = e$, or infinity when no such $n$ exists.
- [[Outer automorphism group|0Q8Y862DTZ]]: the quotient $\operatorname{Out}(G) = \operatorname{Aut}(G) / \operatorname{Inn}(G)$.
- [[P-group|06QYRTX9KQ]]: for a prime $p$, a group in which the order of each element is a power of $p$. A finite $p$-group has order $p^n$.
- [[Perfect group|HWPXM2JSJ9]]: a group equal to its commutator subgroup. The alternating group $A_5$ is perfect.
- [[Permutation|05RWFKVTJ7]]: a bijection from a set to itself.
- [[Permutation group|ZDJQRKPWGA]]: a subgroup of the group of all permutations of a set.
- [[Polycyclic group|9RBA56NXX3]]: a group with a chain of subgroups, each normal in the next, in which all factors are cyclic.
- [[Presentation of a group|0JGEQXQ4AC]]: a description $\langle S \mid R \rangle$ of a group by generators $S$ and relations $R$.
- [[Product of two subgroups|0PT52XQZD7]]: the set $HK = \{ hk : h \in H, k \in K \}$. For finite subgroups, $|HK| = |H| |K| / |H \cap K|$.
- [[Quaternion group|0NRJGW864D]]: the group $Q_8 = \{ \pm 1, \pm i, \pm j, \pm k \}$ with $i^2 = j^2 = k^2 = ijk = -1$.
- [[Quotient group|0KYX2N69J0]]: for $N \trianglelefteq G$, the set $G / N$ of cosets with the product $(aN)(bN) = abN$.
- [[Rank of an abelian group|EMCTDD3HZ1]]: the largest number of elements of an abelian group that are linearly independent over $\mathbb{Z}$.
- [[Residually finite group|HEE922EHQ8]]: a group in which each element $g \ne e$ lies outside some normal subgroup of finite index.

### S to Z

- [[Semidirect product|0H3WZXCZNF]]: for a homomorphism $\varphi \colon H \to \operatorname{Aut}(N)$, the set $N \times H$ with $(n, h)(n', h') = (n \varphi_h(n'), h h')$.
- [[Semigroup|0XMEM406JM]]: a set with an associative binary operation.
- [[Sign of a permutation|02DKV69ZH0]]: the number $+1$ for an even permutation and $-1$ for an odd permutation. It is a homomorphism $S_n \to \{ \pm 1 \}$.
- [[Simple group|0YMYMNQ0QZ]]: a nontrivial group whose only normal subgroups are $\{e\}$ and the group itself.
- [[Solvable group|0FXJ7YFMMX]]: a group whose derived series reaches $\{e\}$ after finitely many steps.
- [[Sporadic group|PZ49Z4CBFZ]]: one of the $26$ finite simple groups that are not in the infinite families of the classification.
- Stabilizer: for an action of $G$ on $X$ and a point $x \in X$, the subgroup $G_x = \{ g \in G : g \cdot x = x \}$.
- [[Subgroup|0X1JXT47GA]]: a subset of $G$ that is a group under the operation of $G$.
- [[Subgroup generated by a subset|0RP7VWQ0YN]]: the smallest subgroup $\langle S \rangle$ of $G$ that contains $S$.
- [[Subnormal subgroup|FKTQ1WBMKM]]: a subgroup $H$ with a chain $H = H_0 \trianglelefteq H_1 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq H_n = G$.
- [[Supersolvable group|AWWM9RWETX]]: a group with a chain of normal subgroups of $G$ from $\{e\}$ to $G$ in which all factors are cyclic.
- [[Sylow p-subgroup|0JWZX6FKSH]]: a subgroup of order $p^k$ of a finite group $G$, where $p^k$ is the largest power of $p$ that divides $|G|$.
- [[Sylow theorems|FESTJ0K9TX]]: Sylow $p$-subgroups exist, any two of them are conjugate, and their number is $\equiv 1 \pmod p$ and divides $|G|$.
- [[Symmetric group|0515A6D2Y1]]: the group $S_n$ of all permutations of $\{1, \ldots, n\}$. It has order $n!$.
- [[Symmetry group|0ZG3Z5JZ85]]: the group of the isometries that map a figure onto itself.
- [[Torsion group|JZNQTTMG58]]: a group in which each element has finite order.
- [[Torsion subgroup|0DBWXQRXQJ]]: the subgroup of the elements of finite order in an abelian group.
- [[Torsion-free group|PEMNN32A43]]: a group in which only $e$ has finite order.
- [[Transitive action|02GK6HZYBT]]: an action on a nonempty set with only one orbit.
- [[Transposition|0FWVRHBRWM]]: a cycle $(a\, b)$ of length $2$. Each permutation of a finite set is a product of transpositions.
- [[Trivial group|0BNPC9850Y]]: the group $\{e\}$ with one element.
- [[Upper central series|0XW54DH28V]]: the chain $Z_0 = \{e\} \le Z_1 \le Z_2 \le \cdots$ with $Z_{i+1} / Z_i = Z(G / Z_i)$.
- [[Word problem for groups|PBGTTAVMMT]]: the question whether a given word in the generators of a presentation equals $e$. No algorithm solves it for all finite presentations.
- [[Wreath product|W1X984PKJ9]]: for a group $H$ that acts on a set $X$, the semidirect product $G^X \rtimes H$, in which $H$ permutes the coordinates.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A glossary is a short dictionary for one subject.
Group theory uses many special words, such as "subgroup", "coset" and "normal".
When a page uses a word that a reader does not know, the reader can look it up here.
For example, the "order" of a group is its number of elements.
The group of the turns of a square that keep the square in place has $4$ elements, so its order is $4$.

### L2 human

We use the glossary to describe the [[symmetric group|0515A6D2Y1]] $S_3$ of the permutations of $\{1, 2, 3\}$.

1. The order is $|S_3| = 3! = 6$. The elements are $e$, $(1\,2)$, $(1\,3)$, $(2\,3)$, $(1\,2\,3)$ and $(1\,3\,2)$.
2. The orders of the elements are $1$, $2$, $2$, $2$, $3$ and $3$. So $S_3$ is not cyclic, because no element has order $6$.
3. The conjugacy classes are $\{e\}$, the three transpositions, and the two $3$-cycles.
4. The class equation reads $6 = 1 + 3 + 2$, and the centre is $Z(S_3) = \{e\}$. So $S_3$ is centreless.
5. The alternating group $A_3 = \{e, (1\,2\,3), (1\,3\,2)\}$ has index $2$, so it is a normal subgroup.
6. The commutator subgroup of $S_3$ is $A_3$, and the abelianisation $S_3 / A_3$ is cyclic of order $2$.
7. The chain $\{e\} \trianglelefteq A_3 \trianglelefteq S_3$ has the abelian factors $A_3$ and $S_3 / A_3$. So $S_3$ is solvable.
8. A nilpotent group other than $\{e\}$ has a nontrivial centre. Since $Z(S_3) = \{e\}$, the group $S_3$ is not nilpotent.

The typical use of the glossary is this kind of check: each word of a statement is replaced by its definition, and then the definition is tested on the example.
A trap is to confuse terms with similar names.
The centre $Z(G)$ is one subgroup of $G$, but the centraliser $C_G(a)$ depends on the element $a$.
In $S_3$, the centre is $\{e\}$, but the centraliser of $(1\,2\,3)$ is $A_3$.

### L3 human

The terms of the glossary fall into a few families.
The first family describes one group: its order, its elements, its subgroups, and its centre.
The second family describes maps between groups: homomorphisms, kernels, images, automorphisms and quotients.
The third family describes the structure of a group by chains of subgroups: composition series, derived series and central series, with the classes of solvable, nilpotent and simple groups.
The fourth family describes groups acting on sets: orbits, stabilizers and the theorems of Burnside, Cauchy and Sylow.
Most of these terms have analogues in other parts of algebra, such as ideals and quotient rings in ring theory and submodules in module theory.
The sub-areas of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]] treat each family in depth, and the reading paths of the area overview give the order.
