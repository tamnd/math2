## Definition

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]] and let $a \in G$.
The conjugacy class of $a$ is the set $\operatorname{Cl}(a) = \{gag^{-1} : g \in G\}$.

## Remarks

The conjugacy classes are the equivalence classes of the relation "is [[conjugate to|~86de96b5]]", so they partition $G$.
They are the [[orbits|~401e0177]] of the [[action by conjugation|~d0188fe9]].
The class of $a$ is $\{a\}$ if and only if $a$ lies in the [[centre|~ff52f9c7]] of $G$.
In the symmetric group $S_n$, the conjugacy classes are given by the [[cycle types|~e5b7981c]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The conjugacy class of an element collects all the elements that look like it after a renaming by conjugation.
In an abelian group, each element is alone in its class.
In $S_3$, the three transpositions form one class, and the two $3$-cycles form another class.
