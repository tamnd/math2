## Summary

This topic is a part of [[analytic number theory|~124efb1e]].
It covers sifting a set of integers by primes with the combinatorial sieves of Brun and of Rosser and Iwaniec, the Selberg sieve and weighted sieves, and the limits that the parity problem sets for all sieves.
