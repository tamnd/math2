## Summary

Addition of natural numbers is the operation that sends two [[natural numbers|~6bc69be4]] $a$ and $b$ to their sum $a + b$.
The sum $a + b$ is the number that you get when you start at $a$ and apply the [[successor function|~849ef9dc]] $b$ times.
