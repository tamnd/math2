## Definition

Let $n$ be a natural number.
The $n$-th central binomial coefficient is the [[binomial coefficient|~b1f658a8]]
$$\binom{2n}{n} = \frac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2}.$$

## Remarks

The first values, for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4$, are $1, 2, 6, 20, 70$.
It is the middle entry of row $2n$ of [[Pascal's triangle|~9e20c12e]], and the largest entry of that row.
It counts the [[lattice paths|~a1e7b571]] from $(0, 0)$ to $(n, n)$ with unit steps right and up. The [[closed formula for Catalan numbers|~b7622ed6]] divides it by $n + 1$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The central binomial coefficient is the number in the middle of an even row of Pascal's triangle.
For $n = 2$ it is $\binom{4}{2} = 6$: the ways to choose 2 of 4 objects.
