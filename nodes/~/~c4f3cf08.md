## Summary

The multiplicative order of an integer $a$ modulo $n$, with $a$ coprime to $n$, is the smallest positive exponent $k$ such that $a^k \equiv 1 \pmod n$.
The [[definition of the multiplicative order|~d6aa568e]] gives the precise form.
