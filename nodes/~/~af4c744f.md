## Statement

The [[exponential function|~8680e2bf]] is strictly convex on $\mathbb{R}$.
That is, $e^{t x + (1 - t) y} < t e^x + (1 - t) e^y$ for all real $x \ne y$ and $t \in (0, 1)$.

## Remarks

The second derivative of $e^x$ is [[$e^x$ itself|~56e3e18f]], which is positive. So [[the second derivative test for convexity|~363d34dd]] applies, in its strict form.
The tangent line at $0$ gives the inequality [[$1 + x \le e^x$|~a5b5a59f]].
Mathlib states convexity as `convexOn_exp`.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The graph of $e^x$ bends upward everywhere.
Every chord of the graph lies above the graph.
