## Summary

This topic is a part of [[arithmetic geometry|~69c4d6cc]].
It covers analytic spaces over complete non-archimedean fields, such as rigid analytic spaces, Berkovich spaces, adic spaces and perfectoid spaces, and p-adic uniformization.
