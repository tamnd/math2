## Summary

A Dyck word is a balanced word of opening and closing brackets. Dyck words form the Dyck language of formal language theory, and those of semilength $n$ are counted by the Catalan number $C_n$. The node [[Dyck word|~de9fbb3f]] gives the precise definition.
