## Definition

Let $\varphi$ and $\psi$ be formulas of [[propositional logic|~5355a044]].
The formulas $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are logically equivalent when they have the same truth value for each assignment of truth values to their variables.
This is written $\varphi \equiv \psi$.

## Remarks

The formulas $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are logically equivalent exactly when $\varphi \leftrightarrow \psi$ is a [[tautology|~d592d671]].
For example, $p \to q \equiv \neg p \lor q$.
[[De Morgan's laws|~53574b2c]] give two more examples.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Logically equivalent formulas say the same thing in different words.
You can check an equivalence with a [[truth table|~77601110]]: the two columns must agree in every row.
Then you can replace one formula by the other in a proof.
