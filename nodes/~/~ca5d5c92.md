## Summary

A phase portrait of an autonomous system is a picture of a representative set of its [[trajectories|~8ab688bc]] in the phase space, usually the [[phase plane|~6ebe4c11]].
Arrows on the curves show the direction of time.
It shows the [[equilibrium points|~940e523e]], the periodic orbits and the long-time behaviour of the solutions.

## Remarks

The [[phase line|~c4019a16]] is the phase portrait of a scalar autonomous equation.
For the undamped [[harmonic oscillator|~f0623e61]] $x'' = -x$, the phase portrait in the $(x, x')$ plane consists of the origin and the circles around it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A phase portrait is a map of all the possible motions of a system.
One look shows where the system rests, where it cycles and where it goes in the long run.
