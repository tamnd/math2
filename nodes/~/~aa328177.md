## Summary

This topic is a part of [[Diophantine approximation and geometry of numbers|~db363e14]].
It covers the tools of transcendence proofs, such as auxiliary functions, Siegel's lemma, zero estimates and interpolation determinants, and the theories of E-functions, G-functions and Mahler functions.
