## Definition

Let $I$ be an open interval, let $a \in I$, and let $f \colon I \to \mathbb{R}$ be [[smooth|~61ef95c0]].
The Taylor series of $f$ at $a$ is the [[power series|~9b386670]]
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x - a)^n.$$

## Remarks

Its partial sums are the [[Taylor polynomials|~ef52d2a0]] of $f$ at $a$.
The Taylor series may diverge for each $x \ne a$. It may also converge to a function other than $f$, as for the [[smooth non-analytic function|~e21a0b09]].
The Taylor series converges to $f$ near $a$ exactly when $f$ is [[analytic|~fe69b7ef]] at $a$.
For $a = 0$ it is the [[Maclaurin series|~c87f36c5]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Taylor series is the Taylor polynomial of infinite degree.
It uses all the derivatives of $f$ at one point.
