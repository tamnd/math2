## Summary

A maximal ideal of a ring is a proper ideal that is not contained in a larger proper ideal.
The maximal ideals of $\mathbb{Z}$ are the ideals $p\mathbb{Z}$ for $p$ prime.
The [[definition of a maximal ideal|~3f9701c1]] gives the conditions.
