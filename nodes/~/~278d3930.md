## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the local Langlands conjectures, with parameters and L-packets, the correspondence for tori, for GL(n), for SL(2), for real groups and for classical groups, the local Jacquet-Langlands correspondence and the formal degree conjecture.
