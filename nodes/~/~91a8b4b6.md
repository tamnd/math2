## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the trace formula for reductive groups and its stabilization, with orbital integrals, the simple, invariant, stable and twisted trace formulas, endoscopic groups and transfer, the fundamental lemma, Arthur's conjectures and classification, and the relative trace formula.
