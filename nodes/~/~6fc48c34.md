## Statement

Let $v_1, \dots, v_n$ be vectors in $\mathbb{R}^m$. Let $A$ be the [[matrix|0N1MY1T0W9]] whose columns are $v_1, \dots, v_n$.

The vectors $v_1, \dots, v_n$ form a [[basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]] of $\mathbb{R}^m$ if and only if $A$ is [[row equivalent|095DDGZF16]] to the [[identity matrix|06DW9BAWB1]].

## Remarks

The identity matrix is square, so row equivalence to it forces $A$ to be an $m \times m$ matrix. Hence $n = m$. Every basis of $\mathbb{R}^m$ has exactly $m$ vectors.
