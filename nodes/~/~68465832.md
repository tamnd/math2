## Summary

The chi-squared distribution with $k$ degrees of freedom is the law of a sum of the squares of $k$ independent standard normal random variables. It is central in tests and confidence intervals for variances.
The node [[chi-squared distribution|~3aae6fc8]] gives the precise definition.
