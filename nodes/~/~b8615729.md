## Statement

Let $F$ be a field and let $V$ be a vector space over $F$.
Any two [[bases|~4af6ac42]] of $V$ have the same cardinality.

## Remarks

For finite bases, this follows from the [[Steinitz exchange lemma|~288533b7]].
For infinite bases, the proof uses cardinal arithmetic: each vector of one basis is a combination of finitely many vectors of the other basis.
The common cardinality is the [[dimension|~3d1088d4]] of $V$.
The theorem holds more generally for free modules over a commutative ring that is not the zero ring.

## Explanations

### L1 human

However we choose a basis of a space, it always has the same number of vectors.
So "the number of vectors in a basis" is a property of the space itself.
We call this number the dimension.
