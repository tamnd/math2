## Summary

This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|~1c787828]].
It covers the heat semigroup of Laplace-type operators on manifolds, its small-time asymptotics and the heat-kernel proof of the index theorem, Gaussian bounds and Brownian motion on manifolds.
