## Summary

A holomorphic function is a complex function that is complex differentiable at each point of an open subset of $\mathbb{C}$, see the [[definition|~1a187203]].
Holomorphic functions are the main objects of complex analysis.
They are infinitely differentiable and they are equal to their Taylor series near each point.
