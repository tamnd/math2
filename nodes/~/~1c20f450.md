## Summary

This topic is a part of [[automorphic forms and the Langlands program|~f7feaeb4]].
It covers the Jacobi forms of Eichler and Zagier, Ramanujan's mock theta functions and their completion by Zwegers, harmonic Maass forms, mock modular forms, Borcherds products and the traces of singular moduli.
