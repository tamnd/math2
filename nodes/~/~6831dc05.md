## Summary

A modular multiplicative inverse of an integer $a$ modulo $n$ is an integer $x$ such that $a x$ leaves the remainder $1$ on division by $n$.
The [[definition of the modular multiplicative inverse|~dd9a786f]] gives the precise form.
