## Statement

On the set of [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], the usual [[order|0Y9H52BT03]] $\le$ is a [[total order|0FRXBVVR03]].

That is, $\le$ is reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive on $\mathbb{N}$, and for all $a, b \in \mathbb{N}$ we have $a \le b$ or $b \le a$.
