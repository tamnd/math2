## Summary

This topic is a part of [[random structures and probabilistic models|~2d4dfb89]].
It covers the rigorous theory of disordered mean-field models, with the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick and p-spin models, the Parisi formula and ultrametricity, the cavity and replica methods, and their uses for random optimization problems.
