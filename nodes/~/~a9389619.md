## Proof

Write $V = \set{0, \dots, m - 1}$, $\neg x = x - 1 \bmod m$ and $x \lor y = \max(x, y)$, as in [[the definition|~fbe0fcc9]].
All terms below are built from $\neg$, $\lor$ and variables.
Let $S x = \neg^{m-1} x = x + 1 \bmod m$, so $S^k x = x + k \bmod m$.

Step 1: the top constant.

1. Put $\top(x) = \max(x, Sx, \dots, S^{m-1} x)$. The values $x + k \bmod m$ for $0 \le k \le m - 1$ are all of $V$, so $\top(x) = m - 1$.
2. Put $\bot(x) = S(\top(x)) = 0$.

Step 2: the tests $J_a$, with $J_a(x) = m - 1$ if $x = a$ and $J_a(x) = 0$ otherwise.

3. Put $M(x) = \max_{0 \le k \le m - 2} S^k x$. The values $S^k x$ with $k \le m - 2$ are all residues except $x - 1 \bmod m$.
4. So $M(x) = m - 1$ if $x - 1 \not\equiv m - 1$, that is if $x \ne 0$, and $M(0) = m - 2$.
5. Put $J_0(x) = S(M(x))$. Then $J_0(0) = S(m - 2) = m - 1$, and $J_0(x) = S(m - 1) = 0$ for $x \ne 0$.
6. Put $J_a(x) = J_0(S^{m-a} x)$. Since $S^{m-a} x = x - a \bmod m$, this is $0$ iff $x = a$, and step 5 gives the claim.

Step 3: conjunction of tests.
Let $u, v \in \set{0, m - 1}$.

7. Put $u \sqcap v = J_0(\max(J_0 u, J_0 v))$.
8. If $u = v = m - 1$, then $J_0 u = J_0 v = 0$, and $J_0(0) = m - 1$. So $u \sqcap v = m - 1$.
9. If $u = 0$ or $v = 0$, then the maximum is $m - 1$, and $J_0(m - 1) = 0$, since $m - 1 \ne 0$. So $u \sqcap v = 0$.
10. So $\sqcap$ is $\min$ on $\set{0, m - 1}$, and $\set{0, m - 1}$ is closed under $\sqcap$.

Step 4: scaling a test to a value $c$ with $1 \le c \le m - 1$.

11. For $e \in \set{0, m - 1}$ put $K_c(e) = S(\max(S^c e, J_0 e))$.
12. If $e = m - 1$: $S^c e = c - 1$ and $J_0 e = 0$, so the maximum is $c - 1$ and $K_c(e) = c$.
13. If $e = 0$: $S^c e = c$ and $J_0 e = m - 1$, so the maximum is $m - 1$ and $K_c(e) = 0$.

Step 5: normal form.
Let $f \colon V^n \to V$ with $n \ge 1$, and let $A = \set{a \in V^n : f(a) \ge 1}$.

14. If $A = \emptyset$, then $f(x_1, \dots, x_n) = \bot(x_1)$, by step 2.
15. Otherwise, for $a \in A$ put $T_a(x) = J_{a_1}(x_1) \sqcap \dots \sqcap J_{a_n}(x_n)$. By steps 6 and 10, $T_a(x) = m - 1$ if $x = a$ and $T_a(x) = 0$ otherwise.
16. Put $t(x) = \max_{a \in A} K_{f(a)}(T_a(x))$.
17. If $x = b \in A$, the term for $a = b$ is $K_{f(b)}(m - 1) = f(b)$ by step 12, and every other term is $0$ by step 13. So $t(b) = f(b)$.
18. If $x = b \notin A$, every term is $0$ by step 13, so $t(b) = 0 = f(b)$.
19. So $t$ is a term in $\neg$ and $\lor$ with $t = f$ on $V^n$.

## Idea

The proof copies disjunctive normal form.
The tests $J_a$ play the role of literals, $\sqcap$ plays the role of conjunction, and $\max$ joins the rows of the table.
The cyclic shift is what makes the tests possible: it moves every value, so a maximum over all but one shift detects one missing value.

## Variants

1. One can first define $\min$ on all of $V$ and then use $\min$ in place of $\sqcap$; the version above needs $\min$ only on $\set{0, m - 1}$.
2. The same proof works for any set of connectives that defines $S$ and $\max$; this gives [[Webb's Sheffer function|~02b9134b]].
