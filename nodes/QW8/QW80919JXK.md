## Example

Let $V = \R^2$ and $W = \R^3$, with [[direct product|0MM085RX00]] $V \times W$.
We check the [[dimension formula|06HN47VPTH]] $\dim(V \times W) = 5$ by finding a basis.

1. Vectors of $V \times W$ are pairs $((a, b), (c, d, e))$, with entrywise operations.
2. Let $f_1 = ((1, 0), 0)$, $f_2 = ((0, 1), 0)$, $f_3 = (0, (1, 0, 0))$, $f_4 = (0, (0, 1, 0))$ and $f_5 = (0, (0, 0, 1))$.
3. Spanning: $((a, b), (c, d, e)) = a f_1 + b f_2 + c f_3 + d f_4 + e f_5$.
4. Independence: if $a f_1 + \cdots + e f_5 = 0$, then $((a, b), (c, d, e)) = (0, 0)$, so all five scalars are $0$.
5. So $f_1, \dots, f_5$ is a [[basis|0VTQTPQGAV]], and the [[dimension|05G2G0P224]] of $V \times W$ is $5 = 2 + 3$.

The map $((a, b), (c, d, e)) \mapsto (a, b, c, d, e)$ is an isomorphism $\R^2 \times \R^3 \cong \R^5$, since it sends the basis $f_1, \dots, f_5$ to the standard basis.

## Remarks

The basis in step 2 is the union of a basis of $V \times 0$ and a basis of $0 \times W$.
These two subspaces meet only in $0$ and sum to $V \times W$, so $V \times W = (V \times 0) \oplus (0 \times W)$ as an internal direct sum.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A vector of $\R^2 \times \R^3$ is a list of two numbers followed by three numbers.
That is five numbers, so the product behaves like $\R^5$.

### L2 human

The proof of the dimension formula takes a basis of each factor and puts the vectors into the product with zero in the other factor.
Steps 3 and 4 check that the result spans and is independent.
