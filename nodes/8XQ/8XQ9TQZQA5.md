## Summary

In mathematics, $\mathbf{1 - 2 + 4 - 8 + \cdots}$ is the infinite series whose terms are the successive powers of two with alternating signs. As a [[geometric series|01X5CVV1Q5]], it is characterized by its first term, $1$, and its common ratio, $-2$.
