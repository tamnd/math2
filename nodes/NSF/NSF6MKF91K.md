## Summary

An elastic collision is a collision of bodies in which the total [[kinetic energy|C9YCMAPKMT]] after the collision equals that before it.
As in every collision of an isolated system, the total [[momentum|37Q4BTJMXW]] is also conserved.
For two particles moving on a line, these two conservation laws determine the outgoing velocities from the incoming ones.
Collisions of billiard balls and of gas molecules in the kinetic theory of gases are modelled as elastic.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of two billiard balls: when one hits the other head-on, the first stops and the second rolls on with the first one's speed.
No energy is lost to heat, sound or deformation in the ideal picture.
For two equal masses meeting head-on, they simply swap velocities.
A light ball bouncing off a very heavy wall comes back with the same speed.
