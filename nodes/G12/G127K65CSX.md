## Description

The complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak{f}_4$ is the [[simple Lie algebra|XDAXWHBNSZ]] over $\C$ whose [[root system|AVVV93CASN]] has type $F_4$.
The root system $F_4$ lies in $\R^4$ and consists of the $48$ vectors of the following three kinds:

1. the $8$ vectors $\pm e_i$;
2. the $24$ vectors $\pm e_i \pm e_j$ with $i < j$;
3. the $16$ vectors $(\pm \tfrac12, \pm \tfrac12, \pm \tfrac12, \pm \tfrac12)$.

The $24$ roots of the second kind are long, of squared length $2$, and the $24$ others are short, of squared length $1$.
Check of the count: $2 \cdot 4 = 8$, $\binom{4}{2} \cdot 4 = 24$ and $2^4 = 16$.
The rank is $4$, and the dimension is $4 + 48 = 52$.

## Remarks

The [[Dynkin diagram|CS73JZQS66]] of $F_4$ is a path of four vertices whose middle edge is double, with an arrow toward the short roots.
The [[Weyl group|YT1ZJ2S4EE]] of $F_4$ has order $1152$.
The algebra $\mathfrak{f}_4$ is the Lie algebra of derivations of the complex Albert algebra, the exceptional Jordan algebra of dimension $27$.
Its smallest nontrivial representation has dimension $26$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

$\mathfrak{f}_4$ is an exceptional simple Lie algebra of dimension $52$.
Its $48$ roots in four-dimensional space come in two lengths, half of each.
It is the symmetry algebra of a special $27$-dimensional algebra built from the octonions.
