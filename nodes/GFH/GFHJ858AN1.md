## Summary

The Mach number $\mathrm{M} = u / c$ is the ratio of the flow speed to the local [[speed of sound|SN4J6KP03C]].
It measures how important the compressibility of the fluid is.
Flows are called subsonic for $\mathrm{M} < 1$ and supersonic for $\mathrm{M} > 1$, and the two regimes are governed by equations of different type.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Sound is a small pressure wave that travels through the fluid at a fixed speed.
If an aircraft is slower than sound, the air ahead of it gets the warning waves and moves aside smoothly.
If it is faster, the air ahead receives no warning, and the waves pile up into a shock wave, heard on the ground as a sonic boom.
At sea level sound travels at about $340\ \mathrm{m/s}$, so a plane at $170\ \mathrm{m/s}$ flies at Mach $0.5$.
