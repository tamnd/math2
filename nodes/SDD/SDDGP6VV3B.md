## Summary

A Fisher market is a model of a market in which buyers come with budgets of money and sellers bring fixed amounts of divisible goods.
An equilibrium is a list of prices at which every buyer spends the budget on a best affordable bundle.
At these prices, all goods with positive price are sold.
It is a special case of an exchange economy and is named after Irving Fisher.
It is a central model in algorithmic game theory, because its equilibria can often be computed by convex optimization.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture an auction house with a fixed stock of goods and buyers holding fixed amounts of cash.
The seller wants to set prices so that everything is sold and everybody is happy with what they buy at those prices.
For example, take one good and two buyers holding $1$ and $2$ euros.
The price $3$ for the whole stock clears the market: the first buyer takes a third and the second takes two thirds.
Prices of popular goods go up until demand falls to the supply.
