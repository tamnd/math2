## Summary

The critical path method schedules a project made of activities with known durations and precedence constraints.
It computes the shortest possible duration of the project, which is the length of a longest path in an [[acyclic directed graph|0SXHBTNM9T]] of activities.
The activities on such a path are critical: delaying any of them delays the whole project.
The other activities have slack and can be shifted without harm.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Building a house needs many tasks, and some cannot start before others end.
The walls need the foundation, and the roof needs the walls, while the garden can be done at any time.
The longest chain of tasks that must follow one another fixes the earliest finishing date.
This chain is the critical path, and managers watch it closely because any delay on it delays everything.
For example, foundation $3$ days, walls $5$ days and roof $2$ days give a critical path of $10$ days, while a $4$-day garden has $6$ days of slack.
