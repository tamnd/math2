## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers linear isometries, orthogonal and unitary matrices, the orthogonal, special orthogonal, unitary and special unitary groups as groups of matrices, and reflections and rotations with their structure in low dimension.
It ends with the normal form of an orthogonal matrix, the Cartan-Dieudonné theorem and the Cayley transform.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Linear isometry|07SJXTWVQW]]: linear maps that keep norms, and [[the equivalent conditions|~d0f0e2fd]] for them.
2. [[Isometries preserve inner products|0R7VXTYHYZ]]: the polarization identities, and distance-preserving maps that fix $0$.
3. [[Orthogonal matrix|0W02QESPJQ]]: $Q^TQ = I$, [[the equivalent conditions|F7M8ZXB4P7]], and [[the determinant and eigenvalues|~d705bf24]].
4. [[Orthogonal group|~dc22fe40]]: $O(n)$ as the group of linear isometries of $\R^n$.
5. [[Special orthogonal group|~5781ca68]]: $SO(n)$ has [[index two|~dd100441]] in $O(n)$, and $SO(2)$ is the circle group.
6. [[Unitary matrix|0AR68JTMDT]]: $U^*U = I$, with [[its properties|~2220a569]].
7. [[Unitary group|~4244c18b]]: $U(n)$, and $U(1)$ as the circle group.
8. [[Special unitary group|~f67e0a03]]: $SU(n)$, and [[$SU(2)$ as the unit quaternions|~b83f5358]].
9. [[Rotation matrix|~8cba9603]]: the matrices $R(\theta)$ and the basic rotations of $\R^3$.
10. [[Reflection|06BRDF3F0E]]: the reflection in a hyperplane, Householder matrices, and [[generation of $O(n)$|~c91e3beb]].
11. [[Classification of O(2)|~233ca0a4]]: rotations and reflections of the plane.
12. [[Classification of O(3) and Euler's rotation theorem|2ZVG43GSKA]]: the axis and the angle of a rotation of space.
13. [[Normal form of an orthogonal matrix|~ff85d703]]: rotation blocks and entries $\pm 1$, from the [[real normal form|G6T1FNE3JC]].
14. [[Cartan-Dieudonné theorem|0VMWGYY35Q]]: at most $n$ reflections for each element of $O(n)$.
15. [[Cayley transform of matrices|~be1c3d3e]]: skew-symmetric matrices and [[the rotations without the eigenvalue $-1$|~1d91faa4]].

The topic on [[canonical forms under similarity|GEBYZKYV3D]] gives normal forms for matrices that are not normal.
The topic on [[positive definite matrices and the singular value decomposition|SS4EVPKT3A]] uses unitary matrices in the [[polar decomposition|0J2C7BDJQA]] and the [[singular value decomposition|000NPTMX6J]].
