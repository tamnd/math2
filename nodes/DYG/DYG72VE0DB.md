## Summary

This topic is a part of [[order, lattices and universal algebra|V1VVQR12AQ]].
It covers the classes of algebras that quasi-identities define, dualities for classes of algebras beyond the Boolean and distributive cases, varieties viewed as categories, and coalgebras as the dual view.
