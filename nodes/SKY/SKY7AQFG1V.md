## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the basic theory of propositional modal logic: the modal language, Kripke frames and models, and the normal modal logics from K to S5.
It also covers completeness by canonical models, filtration and decidability.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Modal logic|C3RZY20ETK]]: the box and the diamond, their readings, and the modal language.
2. [[Kripke semantics|NGHWZ9Y7ZV]]: frames, models, truth at a world, and validity in a model, a frame and a class of frames.
3. [[Accessibility relation|XBCYFDPS2F]]: the relation that reads possibility, and its properties.
4. [[Normal modal logic|D1TJYZZDCY]]: the logic $\mathsf{K}$, with axiom $\mathrm{K}$, necessitation and substitution.
5. [[Necessitation rule|G989VP2NGP]]: from $\vdash \varphi$ infer $\vdash \square\varphi$, and why it is not $\varphi \to \square\varphi$.
6. [[Common modal axiom schemata|FDFQFWPM4X]]: the axioms $\mathrm{T}$, $\mathrm{D}$, $\mathrm{B}$, $\mathrm{4}$ and $\mathrm{5}$ and their frame conditions.
7. [[Modal logics T, D and K4|XVYMEZKH7R]]: reflexive, serial and transitive frames.
8. [[Modal logic S4|N60RVXSA5W]]: preorders, the interior reading, and the link to intuitionistic logic.
9. [[Modal logic S5|VC3A2VWXND]]: equivalence relations, universal frames and the reduction of iterated modalities.
10. [[Brouwerian modal logic|2QPT4KCM0Q]]: the logic $\mathsf{KTB}$ of reflexive symmetric frames.
11. [[Strict implication|5JW54NP52K]]: Lewis's systems $\mathsf{S1}$ to $\mathsf{S5}$ and the history of the subject.
12. [[Canonical model|TVE4F04KME]]: maximal consistent sets, [[the truth lemma|NM4CB7QAYT]] and [[completeness of K to S5|FXTCKYR3AQ]].
13. [[Filtration|6ZZSHAKG5F]]: finite quotients of models and [[the filtration theorem|T99DK51S0W]].
14. [[Finite model property|3NT8RCA6HE]]: [[the FMP of K to S5|XNPJK6B921]] and [[Harrop's theorem|VSYQ1AZ9G7]] on decidability.
15. [[Standard translation|YW6YGBMVQY]]: modal formulas as first-order formulas, and [[its correctness|1HQ1W36J4V]].
16. [[Complexity of modal satisfiability|1P2B0C7CQ4]]: Ladner's theorem, PSPACE for $\mathsf{K}$ to $\mathsf{S4}$ and NP for $\mathsf{S5}$.
17. [[Neighborhood semantics|CN7HK9K1B6]]: neighbourhood frames and the classical logics $\mathsf{E}$, $\mathsf{M}$, $\mathsf{C}$ and $\mathsf{N}$.
18. [[Multimodal logic|ERG3KAQWYF]]: several boxes, fusions and interaction axioms.

### Prerequisites

1. [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: [[propositional calculus|09K9MWD2QQ]], tautologies and Hilbert systems.
2. [[Structures, satisfaction and truth|NJDME6F845]]: first-order structures and the satisfaction relation, used by the standard translation.
