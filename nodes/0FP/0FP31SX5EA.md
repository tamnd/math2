## Summary

A function is even when $f(-x) = f(x)$ and odd when $f(-x) = -f(x)$ for all $x$. Even functions have graphs symmetric about the vertical axis, and odd functions have graphs symmetric about the origin. The node [[even and odd functions|0XE690QF65]] gives the precise definition.
