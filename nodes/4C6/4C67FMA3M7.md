## Summary

Second-harmonic generation is the process in which light of frequency $\omega$ passing through a nonlinear medium produces light of frequency $2\omega$.
It comes from the second-order [[nonlinear susceptibility|7N71T3FKZ6]] $\chi^{(2)}$, so it needs a medium without a centre of inversion.
In the photon picture, two photons of energy $\hbar\omega$ merge into one photon of energy $2\hbar\omega$.
It was the first effect of nonlinear optics to be observed, by Franken and coworkers in 1961.
It is used to make green laser light from infrared lasers.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Shine an invisible infrared laser at $1064$ nm into a suitable crystal, and green light at $532$ nm comes out.
The crystal responds to the light a little unevenly, more in one direction than the other.
This uneven response contains a vibration at twice the frequency of the light, and it radiates new light at half the wavelength.
Common green laser pointers work this way.
