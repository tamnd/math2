## Description

The complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak{e}_7$ is the [[simple Lie algebra|XDAXWHBNSZ]] over $\C$ whose [[root system|AVVV93CASN]] has type $E_7$.
The root system $E_7$ is the set of roots of [[E8|9QT1JR4KNY]] orthogonal to one fixed root.
For the fixed root $v = (\tfrac12, \dots, \tfrac12)$ of $E_8$, these are the $56$ vectors $e_i - e_j$ with $i \ne j$ and the $70$ vectors $(\pm \tfrac12, \dots, \pm \tfrac12)$ with exactly four minus signs.
Check: $8 \cdot 7 = 56$ and $\binom{8}{4} = 70$, so there are $126$ roots, and they span the $7$-dimensional orthogonal complement of $v$.
The dimension of $\mathfrak{e}_7$ is $7 + 126 = 133$.

## Remarks

The [[Dynkin diagram|CS73JZQS66]] of $E_7$ is a tree with $7$ vertices: a branch vertex with arms of $1$, $2$ and $3$ further vertices.
The [[Weyl group|YT1ZJ2S4EE]] of $E_7$ has order $2\,903\,040$.
The smallest nontrivial representation of $\mathfrak{e}_7$ has dimension $56$.

### L1 human

$\mathfrak{e}_7$ is an exceptional simple Lie algebra of dimension $133$.
Its $126$ roots are the roots of $E_8$ at right angles to one chosen root.
