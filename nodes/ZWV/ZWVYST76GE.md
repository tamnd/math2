## Summary

The Maxwell stress tensor is a symmetric $3 \times 3$ tensor field built from the electric and magnetic fields.
It describes the flow of momentum in the electromagnetic field, in the same way that a stress tensor describes forces inside an elastic body.
The total electromagnetic force on the charges inside a closed surface can be computed from the tensor on that surface alone.
This makes it a practical tool for computing forces on bodies, such as the radiation pressure of light.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Faraday pictured field lines as rubber bands that pull along their length and push each other apart sideways.
The Maxwell stress tensor makes this picture exact.
At each point it says how much the field pulls or pushes across a small surface in every direction.
To find the force on a charged body, one only needs to know the field on a surface around the body, not the details inside.
