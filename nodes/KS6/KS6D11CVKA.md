## Problem

- [[Euler's Basel sum|0DMY75D6FV]], $\qquad \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6},$ can be demonstrated by considering the expected exit time of planar Brownian motion from an infinite strip. A number of other less well-known identities can be deduced in a similar manner.
