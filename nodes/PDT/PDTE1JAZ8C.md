## Summary

Asteroseismology is the study of the interiors of stars through their oscillation frequencies.
The frequencies of the [[pulsation modes|QW9QPV0HHG]] of a star depend on its internal structure, so measured frequencies constrain mass, radius, age and internal rotation.
For distant stars only modes of low degree are visible, since the disk is not resolved.
Space photometry missions have made it possible to measure such frequencies for many thousands of stars.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A bell of a different size or material rings with a different note.
In the same way a star of a different mass, size or age oscillates at different frequencies.
The oscillations show up as tiny periodic changes in the brightness of the star.
Measuring them is like hearing the star and working out how it is built inside.
For example, the spacing between consecutive overtones grows with the mean density of the star, so it gives the density directly.
