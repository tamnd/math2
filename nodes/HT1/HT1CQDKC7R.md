## Summary

This topic is a part of [[homological algebra and K-theory|43C9KPSDFS]].
It covers the homology and cohomology of a group with coefficients in a module, the standard resolutions, the meaning of the low degrees, and the maps that relate a group to its subgroups and quotients.
