## Summary

The integers modulo $n$ form the ring $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ of the $n$ residue classes modulo $n$, with the addition and the multiplication of representatives.
The [[definition of the integers modulo n|01N3AYAA6N]] gives the precise form.
