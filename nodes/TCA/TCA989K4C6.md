This topic is a part of [[thermodynamics and statistical mechanics|9A5DVGMPEB]].
It covers microstates and macrostates, the Gibbs ensembles and their partition functions, the Boltzmann and Gibbs entropies, the classical results that follow from them, and the attempts to justify the ensembles.
