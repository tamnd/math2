## Summary

The character group of a group $G$ is the set of homomorphisms from $G$ to the multiplicative group $\C^\times$, with pointwise multiplication.
It is always an abelian group.
For a finite abelian group it is isomorphic to the group itself, and its elements form an orthogonal basis of the functions on the group.
For locally compact abelian groups, its topological version with continuous unitary characters is the [[Pontryagin dual|FMW57WNPDB]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A [[character|H1WNVN5V7F]] turns the group law into multiplication of numbers.
Two characters can be multiplied value by value, and the result is again a character, so the characters form a group of their own.
For the cyclic group $\Z/4\Z$, a character is determined by the image of $1$, which must be one of $1, i, -1, -i$.
So the character group has four elements, and it is again cyclic of order $4$.
For the symmetric group $S_3$ there are only two characters, the trivial one and the sign.
