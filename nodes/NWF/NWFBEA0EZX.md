## Summary

This topic is a part of [[fluid mechanics|B8CZ8SX1G5]].
It covers the reduced models of large-scale flow in the atmosphere and the ocean, with the f-plane and beta-plane, geostrophic and related balances, quasi-geostrophic theory, Rossby and equatorial waves, baroclinic instability, and the general circulation.
