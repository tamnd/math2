## Summary

Population dynamics is the part of [[mathematical biology|YA4XXZZ6P8]] that studies how the size and the structure of populations change over time.
It uses births, deaths, migration and the interaction between species as the causes of change.
Its models predict growth, decline, oscillations and extinction, and they are used in ecology, fisheries, conservation and epidemiology.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Count the deer in a forest every year and you get a sequence of numbers.
Population dynamics asks which rules produce such a sequence.
If every year the herd grows by $10\%$, the numbers grow like $1.1^t$.
If food runs short when the herd is large, growth slows down and the herd settles near a fixed size.
With wolves in the forest, deer and wolves can rise and fall in cycles.
