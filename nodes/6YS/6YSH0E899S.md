## Definition

Let $R$ be a left [[Noetherian ring|0670T59HGE]], and let $\mathcal{M}(R)$ be the category of [[finitely generated|05QY1VEBYD]] left $R$-modules.
It is an abelian category because $R$ is Noetherian.

1. $G_0(R)$ is the [[Grothendieck group|2H7Y1XC93S]] $K_0(\mathcal{M}(R))$ of this abelian category.
2. For $n \ge 0$, $G_n(R) = \pi_{n+1}(BQ\mathcal{M}(R))$, where $Q\mathcal{M}(R)$ is the [[Quillen Q-construction|J9XP7CS943]] of $\mathcal{M}(R)$ and $B$ denotes the classifying space.

*G-theory* of $R$ is the sequence of groups $G_n(R)$, $n \ge 0$.

## Remarks

For $n = 0$, clause 2 gives back clause 1.
The same construction applied to coherent sheaves on a Noetherian scheme $X$ gives $G_n(X)$.
The inclusion of finitely generated projective modules into $\mathcal{M}(R)$ induces the Cartan map $K_n(R) \to G_n(R)$, an isomorphism when $R$ is regular by the [[resolution theorem|DYV91576BQ]].

Example: $G_0(\Z) \cong \Z$ through the rank.
The rank is additive on short exact sequences, so it defines a homomorphism $G_0(\Z) \to \Z$.
It is an isomorphism, because every finitely generated abelian group is $\Z^r \oplus T$ with $T$ finite, and $[\Z/m\Z] = [\Z] - [\Z] = 0$ from the sequence $0 \to \Z \to \Z \to \Z/m\Z \to 0$, with first map multiplication by $m$.

Non-example: for a non-Noetherian ring the finitely generated modules need not form an abelian category.
Over $R = k[x_1, x_2, \ldots]$, polynomials in infinitely many variables over a field $k$, the map $R \to k$ sending each $x_i$ to $0$ has kernel $(x_1, x_2, \ldots)$, which is not finitely generated.
