## Summary

In graph theory, a **graceful labeling** of a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] with $m$ edges is a labeling of its vertices with some subset of the [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] from 0 to $m$ inclusive, such that no two vertices share a label, and each edge is uniquely identified by the [[absolute difference|0KXJTZSW9B]] between its endpoints, such that this magnitude lies between 1 and $m$ inclusive. A graph which admits a graceful labeling is called a **graceful graph**.
