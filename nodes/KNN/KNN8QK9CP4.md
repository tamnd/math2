## Summary

In mathematics, , sometimes called the **golden field**, is a number system consisting of the [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] of all numbers $a + b\sqrt5$, where $a$ and $b$ are both rational numbers and $\sqrt5$ is the square root of 5, along with the basic arithmetical operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division). This arithmetic shares certain structural properties with the arithmetic of $\mathbb{Q}$, the field of rational numbers, making also a type of [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]]. More specifically, it is a real quadratic field, the [[extension field|0EQWVHF3W5]] of $\mathbb{Q}$ generated by combining rational numbers and $\sqrt5$ using arithmetical operations. The name comes from the [[golden ratio|0SC8T7548D]] $\varphi$, a positive number satisfying the equation $\textstyle \varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$, which is the fundamental unit of .
