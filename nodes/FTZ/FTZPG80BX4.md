## Summary

In mathematics, an **elliptic Gauss sum** is an analog of a Gauss sum depending on an elliptic curve with complex multiplication. The [[quadratic residue|0DKCECSYCF]] symbol in a Gauss sum is replaced by a higher residue symbol such as a cubic or quartic residue symbol, and the exponential function in a Gauss sum is replaced by an elliptic function. They were introduced by (txt), at least in the lemniscate case when the elliptic curve has complex multiplication by $i$, but seem to have been forgotten or ignored until the paper (Pinch 1988).
