## Summary

A scleronomous constraint is a constraint of a mechanical system whose equation does not contain the time explicitly.
The geometry of the allowed configurations then stays the same during the motion.
Take a system with ideal scleronomous [[holonomic constraints|AXA8HN8Y45]] and forces from a time-independent potential.
Then its total energy is conserved.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A bead on a fixed circular wire obeys $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ at every moment.
The wire does not move, so the rule does not change with time: the constraint is scleronomous.
If someone shakes or rotates the wire, the rule depends on the time and the constraint is no longer scleronomous.
