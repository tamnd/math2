## Summary

The Lindblad equation is the general form of a Markovian master equation for the [[density matrix|V1JTB52E8C]] of an [[open quantum system|6D9YCNZ7X9]].
It adds to the unitary Schrödinger evolution a dissipative part built from jump operators, which describe how the environment acts on the system.
Its generators are exactly the generators of continuous [[quantum dynamical semigroups|EQDPM8894B]] in finite dimension.
It is the standard model for decay, dephasing and [[decoherence|AN414B0M5X]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A closed quantum system evolves by a rotation of its state, and nothing is lost.
A real system leaks energy and information to its surroundings, so its state slowly becomes mixed.
The Lindblad equation describes this leak with a few "jump" operators, each one a way the environment can kick the system.
For an excited atom that emits light, one jump operator moves the atom from the excited level to the ground level.
The solution shows the excited population decaying like $e^{-\gamma t}$, the familiar law of radioactive decay.
