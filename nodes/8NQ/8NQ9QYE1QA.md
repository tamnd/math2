## Statement

Let $x$ be a nonzero element of the [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]], and let $m$ and $n$ be integers.
Then $x^m x^n = x^{m+n}$ and $(x^m)^n = x^{mn}$.

## Remarks

Here $x^0 = 1$, and a negative power is a power of the reciprocal, so $x^{-k} = (1/x)^k$ for $k > 0$.
The condition $x \neq 0$ is needed so that negative powers exist.
