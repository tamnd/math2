## Summary

This topic is a part of [[general topology|DQ77PZJBHV]].
It covers the conditions under which a topology comes from a metric, from Urysohn's theorem for second-countable spaces to the Nagata-Smirnov and Bing theorems, and the generalized metric spaces around them.
