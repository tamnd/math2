## Definition

*Hydrology* is the science of the water of the Earth on and below the land surface.
Its objects of study are:

1. the water cycle over land: precipitation, interception, infiltration into the soil, evaporation and transpiration;
2. the flow of water at the surface, in overland flow, rivers, lakes and catchments;
3. the flow and storage of water below the surface, in the unsaturated soil and in aquifers (hydrogeology), modelled with [[Darcy's law|DX8RV2XG6H]];
4. the quantity and quality of these waters, and their statistics in time and space.

## Remarks

Example: predicting the discharge of a river at a town from the rainfall over its catchment is a hydrological problem, under clauses 1 and 2.
Non-example: the circulation of the open ocean is studied by physical oceanography, not hydrology, although the two meet at estuaries and coasts.
Groundwater hydrology, surface water hydrology and hydrometeorology are its main branches.
