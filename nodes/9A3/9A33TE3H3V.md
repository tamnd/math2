## Statement

OR distributes over AND and AND distributes over OR. NOT does not distribute over AND or OR. See below about De Morgan's law:

- Distributive law for OR: ( c ∨ ( a & b) ) ≡ ( (c ∨ a) & (c ∨ b) )
- Distributive law for AND: ( c & ( a ∨ b) ) ≡ ( (c & a) ∨ (c & b) )
