## Summary

A **googolplex** is the large number $10^{10^{100}}$, that is, $10$ raised to the power of a googol. If written out in ordinary decimal notation, it would be $1$ followed by a googol (10<sup>100</sup>) zeroes—‌a physically impossible number to write explicitly.
