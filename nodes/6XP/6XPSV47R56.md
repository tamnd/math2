## Summary

Electric charge is the property of matter that makes it produce and feel electric and magnetic forces.
It is a real number, positive or negative, attached to a particle or spread over a region with some density.
Charge is conserved, and like charges repel while opposite charges attract.
In the mathematical theory, a charge distribution is the source term of the equations of electrostatics and of [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Rub a balloon on your hair and it can stick to a wall: it has picked up electric charge.
Think of charge as a signed amount, like money that can be a credit or a debt.
Two objects with charges of the same sign push each other away, and objects with opposite signs pull together.
An electron carries the charge $-e$ and a proton the charge $+e$, so a hydrogen atom has total charge $0$.
In SI units charge is measured in coulombs, and $e \approx 1.602 \times 10^{-19}$ coulomb.
