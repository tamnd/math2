## Summary

Nordström's theory of gravitation is a relativistic theory of gravity proposed by Gunnar Nordström in 1912 and 1913, before general relativity.
In it gravity is described by a single scalar field.
In the form found by Einstein and Fokker in 1914, spacetime is curved but conformally flat.
It is an [[alternative to general relativity|384JFR3K6X]] that was ruled out by the bending of light.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Before Einstein finished general relativity, Nordström tried to make Newton's gravity agree with special relativity in the simplest way.
He replaced Newton's potential by a single field that obeys a wave equation.
Einstein and Fokker then showed that this amounts to a curved spacetime whose metric is the flat one multiplied by a varying factor.
Such a change of scale does not change the paths of light rays.
So the theory predicts that the Sun does not bend starlight at all, which the 1919 eclipse observations contradicted.
