## Summary

Addition of natural numbers is the operation that sends two [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]] $a$ and $b$ to their sum $a + b$.
The sum $a + b$ is the number that you get when you start at $a$ and apply the [[successor function|0AK9TGSNXY]] $b$ times.
