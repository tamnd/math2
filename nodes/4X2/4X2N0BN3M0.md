## Summary

The Harish-Chandra c-function of a semisimple Lie group is a meromorphic function $c(\lambda)$ on the complexified dual of a maximal split abelian subalgebra.
It describes the behaviour at infinity of the [[spherical functions|BKZNC76RC8]] $\varphi_\lambda$.
Far out in the group, $\varphi_\lambda$ looks like a sum of plane waves with amplitudes $c(w \lambda)$.
Its inverse square $\abs{c(\lambda)}^{-2}$ is the density of the [[Plancherel theorem for spherical functions|PHYHP9TW29]].
The [[Gindikin–Karpelevich formula|5N2F22Y6AH]] computes it as a product of quotients of Gamma functions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

On the hyperbolic plane, a radial wave $\varphi_\lambda$ spreads out from the origin and, far away, looks like an outgoing plus an incoming plane wave.
The c-function records the size and phase of these plane waves.
It works like a scattering coefficient, and it tells how much each frequency counts when a radial function is rebuilt from its transform.
