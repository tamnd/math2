## Statement

The **axiom of non-choice**, also called **axiom of unique choice**, **axiom of function choice** or **function comprehension principle** is a function existence postulate. The difference to the [[axiom of choice|0HTGB13FE2]] is that in the antecedent, the existence of $y$ is already granted to be unique for each $x$.
