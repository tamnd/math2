## Summary

A **random *r*-regular graph** is a [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] selected from $\mathcal{G}_{n,r}$, which denotes the [[probability space|0NFPJVJJHX]] of all [[*r*-regular graphs|0KA4S8R39M]] on $n$ vertices, where $3 \le r < n$ and $nr$ is even. It is therefore a particular kind of random graph, but the regularity restriction significantly alters the properties that will hold, since most graphs are not regular.
