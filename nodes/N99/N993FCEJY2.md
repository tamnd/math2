## Summary

Superstring theory is [[string theory|GM58M1HFY0]] with [[supersymmetry|S847N922FY]]: the string carries fermionic degrees of freedom besides its position in spacetime.
Unlike the purely bosonic string, it describes spacetime fermions as well as bosons, and its consistent forms have no tachyon.
Its quantization is consistent only in ten spacetime dimensions.
There are five consistent perturbative superstring theories, and the dualities among them are a central theme of the subject.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In string theory the basic objects are tiny vibrating strings instead of point particles, and each way of vibrating looks like a different particle.
The simplest version, the bosonic string, only produces particles of the kind that carry forces, and it contains an unstable particle with negative squared mass.
Superstring theory adds to the string partner fields that obey the rules of fermions, the particles that make up matter.
A symmetry, supersymmetry, ties the two kinds of fields together.
The price is that the theory only works with ten spacetime dimensions, so six of them must be curled up small to match the world we see.
