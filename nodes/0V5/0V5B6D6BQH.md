## Summary

The characteristic function of a real random variable $X$ is the function $t \mapsto E[e^{itX}]$. It is the Fourier transform of the law of $X$, and it exists for every random variable.
The node [[characteristic function|0MYDNRT7PP]] gives the precise definition.
