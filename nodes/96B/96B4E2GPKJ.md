## Summary

A Lie group action is a [[group action|0WCY42EV9V]] of a [[Lie group|7JBRGCVJRB]] on a [[smooth manifold|F45VQ47GPV]] such that the action map is smooth.
It is the precise form of continuous symmetry of a geometric space.
Orbits of such actions are immersed submanifolds, and stabilisers are closed subgroups.
Differentiating the action turns elements of the Lie algebra into vector fields on the manifold.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Lie group action lets each element of a Lie group move the points of a smooth space.
The motion depends smoothly on both the element and the point.
Rotations of the plane about the origin are an example: rotating by $\theta$ moves each point smoothly, and rotating twice adds the angles.
The orbits are the circles centred at the origin, together with the origin itself, which is fixed by every rotation.
