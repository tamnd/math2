## Summary

This topic is a part of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]].
It covers the axioms of a group, their first consequences, the first examples of groups, and the order of a group and of its elements.
It starts with the laws that a binary operation can obey: associativity, commutativity, an identity element and inverses.
It then defines a group, proves the first rules of calculation in a group, and shows the first examples: numbers, roots of unity, symmetries and tables.
The last pages treat the logic of groups: the first-order theory of groups, Szmielew's decidability theorem for abelian groups, and Tarski's problems on [[free groups|0KTW26C80J]].
Three essays give the applications of groups, the history of group theory and a glossary of the whole area.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]]: the overview of the area and the map of its sub-areas.
2. [[Binary operation|0M418FE7BB]]: a rule that combines two elements of a set into one element of the set.
3. [[Associativity|0K8F7PVDMX]]: the law $(ab)c = a(bc)$ and why brackets can be dropped.
4. [[Commutativity|099E72KTF8]]: the law $ab = ba$ and the operations that break it.
5. [[Identity element|07WYQPB8E3]]: the element that changes nothing, on one side or on both sides.
6. [[Inverse element|0ZNZH0EXFX]]: the element that undoes another element.
7. [[Group|G5S1M9]]: the definition, the one-sided axioms, the first examples and non-examples.
8. [[Uniqueness of identity and inverses|0D9NDGE9KP]]: the first theorem about groups.
9. [[Cancellation laws|0VSNX4P2Q0]]: how to remove a common factor, and how to solve $ax = b$.
10. [[Laws of exponents|00DRK90Z6K]]: integer powers of an element and their rules.
11. [[Cayley table|0YB2NRP0T5]]: the multiplication table of a finite group, and the groups of order up to $4$.
12. [[Abelian group|082GHNPJ53]]: groups whose operation is commutative.
13. [[Trivial group|0BNPC9850Y]]: the group with one element.
14. [[Additive group of integers|0BSCJA5G53]]: the first infinite group.
15. [[Circle group|04EAFVFTF7]]: the complex numbers of absolute value $1$.
16. [[Group of nth roots of unity|0WX11EA3F3]]: the finite subgroups of the circle group.
17. [[Order of a group|0B85S2V2DR]]: the number of elements of a group.
18. [[Order of an element|0WE2NHZJ7B]]: the number of steps that an element needs to return to the identity.
19. [[Power is the identity if and only if the order divides the exponent|0F5QC101Q7]]: the basic test with the order of an element.
20. [[Order of a power|0VVPQNVM8S]]: the formula $n / \gcd(n, k)$ and the order of a product of commuting elements.
21. [[Symmetry group|0ZG3Z5JZ85]]: the group of the motions that map a figure onto itself.
22. [[Exponential map from the additive reals to the positive reals|7GEXM87S0H]]: a first isomorphism of groups.
23. [[Opposite group|D33V8HE0KY]]: the group with the reversed product.
24. [[Elementary theory of groups|RX9MB386C5]]: groups seen through first-order logic.
25. [[Szmielew's theorem|Y0753DF5R9]]: the theory of abelian groups is decidable.
26. [[Tarski's problems on free groups|3TZW7W8N6A]]: two questions on the theory of free groups, and [[their solution|NCWBT0BZC3]].
27. [[Groups in science and applications|1290GH695S]]: chemistry, crystals, physics, cryptography and puzzles.
28. [[History of group theory|ZWK8Q2N6YQ]]: from Lagrange and Galois to the classification of finite simple groups.
29. [[Glossary of group theory|FE26HZ5HCN]]: short definitions of the terms of the area.

It needs [[sets|0BSJ7RQ9BW]], [[functions|0EPAT51DXQ]], the [[Cartesian product|09CEDJYBKP]] and [[function composition|0THBK9V825]].
It needs the arithmetic of the [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]]: the [[division algorithm|0CHQKZFK5S]], [[divisibility|0PG2H7WKGG]] and the [[greatest common divisor|0C8WYW4KYQ]].
It uses [[mathematical induction|0ASJVEFA9J]] and the other methods of the topic [[proof methods|G9JFJGY4TQ]].
Some examples use the [[complex numbers|09FTAET5Z4]], [[matrix multiplication|032Q9FB8MT]] and the [[integers modulo n|03VFZ2WETT]].
Pages 24 to 26 also use the language of [[model theory|HFTBG5PMXC]].
