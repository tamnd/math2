## Summary

A power series is a series $\sum_{n \ge 0} a_n (z - c)^n$ with complex coefficients $a_n$, a center $c$ and a complex variable $z$, see the [[definition|0VARVQ9ZW1]].
It converges on a disc, and its sum is holomorphic there.
