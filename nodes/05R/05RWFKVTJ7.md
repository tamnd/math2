## Summary

A permutation of a set is a rearrangement of its elements, that is, a bijection from the set to itself. Permutations are the basic objects of enumerative combinatorics and of the theory of symmetric groups. The node [[permutation|0BQMWCNS8S]] gives the precise definition.
