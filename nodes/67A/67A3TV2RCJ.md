## Summary

In geometry, the **kappa curve** or **Gutschoven's curve** is a quartic plane algebraic curve resembling the Greek letter $\varkappa$ (kappa). The kappa curve was first studied by Gérard van Gutschoven around 1662. In the history of mathematics, it is remembered as one of the first examples of Isaac Barrow's application of rudimentary calculus methods to determine the [[tangent|01R1HW3Y42]] of a curve. Isaac Newton and Johann Bernoulli continued the studies of this curve subsequently.
