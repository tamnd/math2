## Statement

**Brocard's problem** is a problem in mathematics that seeks [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] values of $n$ such that $n!+1$ is a perfect square, where $n!$ is the [[factorial|0XWG0JGDFW]]. Only three values of $n$ are known — 4, 5, 7 — and it is not known whether there are any more. Though research has extended far beyond n > 7, no additional solutions to the equation n! + 1 = m<sup>2</sup> are known.
