## Summary

Weak gravitational lensing is the small distortion of the shapes of distant galaxies by the gravity of the matter along the line of sight.
Each galaxy is distorted by only about one per cent, far less than its natural shape varies.
So the signal is extracted statistically from many galaxies.
It maps the total mass, dark matter included, of clusters and of the large-scale structure of the universe.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Look at a wallpaper through a slightly uneven window: the pattern is stretched a little in places.
Matter between us and distant galaxies acts like such a window, stretching their images by a tiny amount.
One galaxy alone tells nothing, because galaxies come in many shapes.
But if thousands of galaxies in one part of the sky all lean slightly the same way, there must be mass in front of them.
Measuring this alignment over the sky gives a map of where the mass is.
