## Summary

a part of linear algebra concerned with the operations of vector addition and scalar multiplication, although it may also refer to vector operations of vector calculus, including the [[dot|0S52TNNTH9]] and [[cross product|0N5N15HWZ7]]. In this case it can be contrasted with geometric algebra which generalizes into higher dimensions.
