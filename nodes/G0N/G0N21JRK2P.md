## Summary

In [[combinatorics|0JKW7W7XFR]], the **Schröder–Hipparchus numbers** form an integer sequence that can be used to count the [[plane trees|062D9KXJB1]] with a given set of leaves, the ways of inserting parentheses into a sequence, and the ways of dissecting a convex polygon into smaller polygons by inserting diagonals.
