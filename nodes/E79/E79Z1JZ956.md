## Definition

Let $C \subseteq \F_2^n$ be the binary code of a parity-check matrix $H$.
Its [[Tanner graph|GTDMNAMPD9]] has variable nodes $v_1, \dots, v_n$ and check nodes $c_1, \dots, c_m$, and $N(\cdot)$ is the set of neighbours of a node.
A codeword is sent over a memoryless binary-input [[channel|B9C5RBFATB]] with transition probabilities $P(y \mid x)$, and $y_1, \dots, y_n$ are received.
Let $L_j = \ln \big( P(y_j \mid 0) / P(y_j \mid 1) \big) \in [-\infty, \infty]$ be the channel log-likelihood ratio of bit $j$.
*Belief propagation decoding* with $I \ge 1$ iterations is the following procedure.

1. Set $m^{(0)}_{c \to v} = 0$ for every edge $\set{v, c}$.
2. For $\ell = 1, \dots, I$ and every edge $\set{v_j, c}$, set
$$m^{(\ell)}_{v_j \to c} = L_j + \sum_{c' \in N(v_j) \setminus \set{c}} m^{(\ell - 1)}_{c' \to v_j},$$
and then, for every edge $\set{v, c}$,
$$m^{(\ell)}_{c \to v} = 2 \operatorname{artanh} \Big( \prod_{v' \in N(c) \setminus \set{v}} \tanh \big( m^{(\ell)}_{v' \to c} / 2 \big) \Big).$$
3. For each $j$, compute $\Lambda_j = L_j + \sum_{c \in N(v_j)} m^{(I)}_{c \to v_j}$, and output $\hat x_j = 0$ if $\Lambda_j \ge 0$ and $\hat x_j = 1$ otherwise.

## Remarks

The rule at a check node comes from the identity $P(x_1 + \cdots + x_r = 0) - P(x_1 + \cdots + x_r = 1) = \prod_i \big( P(x_i = 0) - P(x_i = 1) \big)$ for independent bits, together with $P(x = 0) - P(x = 1) = \tanh(L/2)$ when $L$ is the log-likelihood ratio of $x$.
Suppose the Tanner graph is a forest and $I$ is at least its diameter, and the sent codeword is uniformly chosen.
Then $\Lambda_j$ is the log of the ratio of the [[posterior probabilities|0KJ7ZS8AJA]] of $x_j = 0$ and $x_j = 1$.
So the output is the bitwise maximum a posteriori decision.
One often stops early once $H \hat x^T = 0$.
Each iteration costs a number of operations proportional to the number of edges, so the decoder is fast for sparse $H$.

Example: one check $x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 0$ over the binary erasure channel, with received word $(0, ?, 1)$.
Then $L = (+\infty, 0, -\infty)$, and the message to $v_2$ is $2 \operatorname{artanh}(\tanh(+\infty) \tanh(-\infty)) = 2 \operatorname{artanh}(-1) = -\infty$, so $\Lambda_2 = -\infty$ and $\hat x_2 = 1$, as it should.

Non-example: the min-sum decoder replaces the check rule by $\prod_{v'} \sgn\big(m_{v' \to c}\big) \cdot \min_{v'} \abs{m_{v' \to c}}$; it is a cheaper approximation and not belief propagation in the sense above.
