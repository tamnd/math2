## Summary

This topic is a part of [[order, lattices and universal algebra|V1VVQR12AQ]].
It covers groups and rings with a compatible partial, total or lattice order: the Archimedean and Hahn theory of ordered abelian groups, the structure theory of lattice-ordered groups, and partially ordered rings and f-rings.
