## Summary

In number theory, the **sum of squares function** is an [[arithmetic function|09115NQZ07]] that gives the number of representations for a given positive [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] *$n$* as the sum of *$k$* squares, where representations that differ only in the order of the summands or in the signs of the numbers being squared are counted as different. It is denoted by $r_k(n)$.
