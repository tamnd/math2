## Summary

The principal branch of a multivalued function is one branch of it that a standard convention chooses, see the [[definition|0YKY4M3KRQ]].
Its values are the principal values. An example is $\operatorname{Log} z = \ln |z| + i \operatorname{Arg} z$ with $\operatorname{Arg} z \in (-\pi, \pi]$.
