## Summary

Let $f$ be a real function and let $c$ be a point of its domain. The value $f(c)$ is a local (or relative) maximum of $f$ when it is at least as large as every value $f(x)$ for $x$ close to $c$. This is the notion in [[local maximum|00999TYPXB]].

The value $f(c)$ is a local (or relative) minimum of $f$ when it is at most every value $f(x)$ for $x$ close to $c$. This is the notion in [[local minimum|0NS8BP96QX]].

Here "close to $c$" means that $x$ lies in some open interval around $c$. A value that is either kind is called a local extremum.
