## Definition

Model a star as a self-gravitating fluid sphere.
Its internal structure, such as sound speed and density as functions of radius, is unknown, and so are its mass, radius and age.
*Asteroseismology* is the determination of this structure and these parameters from the observed frequencies of the [[pulsation modes|QW9QPV0HHG]] of the star.

## Remarks

Example: for solar-like oscillators, the large separation $\Delta\nu$ between modes of consecutive radial order scales as $\Delta\nu \propto \sqrt{M / R^3}$.
Here $M$ is the mass and $R$ the radius.
Together with the frequency of maximum power, $\Delta\nu$ gives estimates of $M$ and $R$.
The reason is that $\Delta\nu = \big(2 \int_0^R dr / c(r)\big)^{-1}$ is the inverse of the sound crossing time, which scales as the dynamical time $\sqrt{R^3 / (G M)}$.
Non-example: measuring the radius of a star by interferometry alone is not asteroseismology, because no oscillation frequencies are used.
The case of the Sun is called [[helioseismology|YFK8GRFF0Z]].
