## Summary

Numbers and arithmetic is the study of the number systems and of calculation with numbers.
A number system is a set of numbers with an addition and a multiplication, and in most cases an order.
The natural numbers, the integers, the rational numbers, the real numbers and the complex numbers form the main chain of number systems.
Arithmetic is the first mathematics that people learn, and the number systems are the base on which algebra and analysis are built.
Positional notation with a zero came from India and reached Europe through the Islamic world, and Dedekind and Cantor published rigorous constructions of the real numbers in 1872.
This topic collects the numbers and arithmetic pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Elementary number theory, elementary algebra, real analysis and field theory have their own topics.
