## Summary

In number theory, a **totative** of a given positive integer $n$ is an integer $k$ such that $0 < k \le n$ and $k$ is coprime to $n$. [[Euler's totient function|0DAMHCT17R]] φ(*n*) counts the number of totatives of *n*. The totatives under multiplication modulo *n* form the [[multiplicative group of integers modulo *n*|0F8X8ZRQ53]].
