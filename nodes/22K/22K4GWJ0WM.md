## Summary

The formulas (A → B) → ¬ (A → ¬ B) and (A → ¬ B) → ¬ (A → B) in [[propositional logic|09K9MWD2QQ]]; they are theorems in connexive logic but not in classical logic. See also Aristotle's theses.
