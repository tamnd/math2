This topic is a part of [[arithmetic geometry|73BKSN9KXY]].
It covers the arithmetic of iterated rational maps on varieties over number fields and non-archimedean fields, with preperiodic points, canonical heights, orbits, and dynamical analogues of Diophantine theorems.
