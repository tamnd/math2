## Summary

In mathematics, a filter on a set $X$ informally gives a notion of which [[subsets|0BJR9QR08E]] $A \subseteq X$ are "large". **Filter quantifiers** are a type of logical quantifier which, informally, say whether or not a statement is true for "most" elements of $X.$ Such quantifiers are often used in [[combinatorics|0JKW7W7XFR]], model theory (such as when dealing with ultraproducts), and in other fields of mathematical logic where (ultra)filters are used.
