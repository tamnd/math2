## Summary

The Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem says that every bounded sequence of reals has a convergent subsequence.
It is provable from arithmetical comprehension, and, in the other direction, the theorem itself proves arithmetical comprehension over a weak base.
Reverse mathematics is the program that finds, for each theorem of ordinary mathematics, the axioms that it needs, by proving the axioms back from the theorem.

The work takes place in subsystems of [[second-order arithmetic|DC60FYCSPM]], over the weak base system [[RCA0|XQENB067R3]].
A theorem $\tau$, coded as a sentence of $L_2$, is *equivalent over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$* to a subsystem $S \supseteq \mathsf{RCA}_0$ when $S \vdash \tau$ and $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \tau$ proves every axiom of $S$.
The second half is the *reversal*.
The [[formal definition|TQTDKMBJ0F]] states this exactly.

## Motivation

A proof of a theorem shows that some axioms suffice, but it does not show that weaker axioms fail.
Reverse mathematics shows that the axioms are optimal by deriving them from the theorem.
In second-order arithmetic the axioms that vary are comprehension axioms, which say which sets exist.
So an equivalence measures how much set existence a theorem needs, and also how far from computable its proofs must be.

The main finding is a surprise.
Most theorems of countable mathematics are either provable in $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ or equivalent over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ to one of four stronger systems: [[WKL0|7W9EG02T79]], [[ACA0|NAASTN80QN]], [[ATR0|MKB74TX3Z8]] and [[Π¹₁-CA0|T21WETAHN5]].
Together with $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ these are the *Big Five*.
The exceptions, led by [[Ramsey's theorem for pairs|ZT5KV6KJ3E]], form the [[reverse mathematics zoo|E3X5B175ZR]].

## Notation

For sentences $\tau$ and $\sigma$, "$\tau \equiv \sigma$ over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$" means $\mathsf{RCA}_0 \vdash \tau \leftrightarrow \sigma$.
The Big Five, in increasing strength, are $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, $\mathsf{WKL}_0$, $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ and $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.
The subscript $0$ marks restricted induction.
For example, $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ has induction only for $\Sigma^0_1$ formulas, and $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ has the induction axiom for sets but not the full induction scheme.

## Remarks

### The idea of a reversal

Here is how the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem proves arithmetical comprehension.
By the [[range lemma|3MQ39PRCYJ]], it is enough to show that every one-to-one function $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ has a range.
Form the partial sums
$$x_n = \sum_{i \le n} 2^{-f(i)}.$$
They increase and stay at most $\sum_k 2^{-k} = 2$, because the values $f(i)$ are distinct, so the theorem gives a convergent subsequence, and its limit $x$ is the limit of the whole sequence.
Now take any index $n$ with $x - x_n < 2^{-k}$.
Then $k$ is in the range of $f$ exactly when $k = f(i)$ for some $i \le n$.
A later value $f(i) = k$ would add $2^{-k}$ to the tail, so it cannot hide after $n$.
A search finds such an $n$, so the test is computable from $x$ and $f$, and $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ forms the range.
The [[Bolzano-Weierstrass equivalence|HV153Y95MQ]] gives the details.

### A ladder of results

At the bottom, $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ proves the [[intermediate value theorem|66WH4PQ392]] by bisection, and the [[infinite pigeonhole principle for two colors|9EB4DXQMWK]].
One step up, the [[Heine-Borel theorem for the unit interval|KRS3VTWCHJ]] is equivalent to $\mathsf{WKL}_0$, the system of weak König's lemma for binary trees.
Then the [[existence of Turing jumps|10NR4PGXHW]], the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem and [[König's lemma for finitely branching trees|3P2J5NWCM1]] are all equivalent to $\mathsf{ACA}_0$.
Higher still, comparability of countable well-orderings belongs to $\mathsf{ATR}_0$, and the Cantor-Bendixson theorem for closed sets belongs to $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.

### What is not a reverse mathematics result

An [[equivalence over a strong base|E007FEBHYD]] measures nothing.
$\mathsf{ACA}_0$ proves both the Heine-Borel and the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorems, so it proves that they are equivalent, although over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ the second is strictly stronger.
A theorem about arbitrary functions from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ is also outside the program, since the [[Heaviside function has no code|5W961M41F5]] in $L_2$.
Such theorems need [[higher-order reverse mathematics|NPDRXTBW5W]].

### The first properties

If $\tau$ is equivalent to $S$ over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, then the $\omega$-models of $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \tau$ are exactly the $\omega$-models of $S$.
So $\tau$ fails in the [[ω-model of the computable sets|F5T3F7E0H1]] whenever $S$ is $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ or stronger.
The first-order parts of the lower systems are known.
$\mathsf{RCA}_0$ and $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ are [[conservative|N238J43XNE]] over $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ for arithmetical sentences, and $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ is conservative over Peano arithmetic.
The proof-theoretic ordinals are $\omega^\omega$ for $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ and $\mathsf{WKL}_0$, $\varepsilon_0$ for $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, $\Gamma_0$ for $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ and $\psi(\Omega_\omega)$ for $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.

### Watch out

An equivalence over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ is a statement about provability in $\mathsf{RCA}_0$.
In the full model $(\mathbb{N}, \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{N}))$ the theorem and the axiom are both simply true.
The result also depends on the coding of reals and functions, so the coding must be fixed first, as in the [[definition of second-order arithmetic|Y92NXGEQBD]].
Last, the reversal must be carried out in $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \tau$.
A reversal that quietly forms the set of nodes of a tree with infinitely many extensions has used arithmetical comprehension, and it proves nothing.

### History

Harvey Friedman started the program in his address at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Vancouver in 1974.
Stephen Simpson and his students developed it into a systematic study.
Simpson's monograph *Subsystems of Second Order Arithmetic* (1999, second edition 2009) is the standard reference.
Earlier, Hilbert and Bernays developed analysis in second-order arithmetic, and Weyl developed a predicative analysis.

### Try it

First, $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ proves a theorem $\tau$.
Is $\tau$ equivalent to $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$?
Yes: $\mathsf{RCA}_0 \vdash \tau$, and $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \tau$ proves the axioms of $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ because it contains them.
So the classification of $\tau$ is "provable in the base".

Second, a statement $\tau$ fails in the $\omega$-model of the computable sets.
Can $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ prove $\tau$?
No, because that structure is a model of $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, and a theorem of $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ holds in every model of it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Usually one starts from axioms and proves theorems.
Reverse mathematics also goes the other way: it starts from a theorem and proves the axioms from it.
If both directions work over a weak base, then the theorem needs exactly those axioms.
For example, the theorem that a bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence turns out to need exactly the axiom that every arithmetically defined set exists.

### L2 human

A result in reverse mathematics has three parts, and the Heine-Borel theorem shows each one.

1. Code the theorem in $L_2$.
   An open cover of $[0, 1]$ is a sequence of open intervals with rational endpoints.
2. Prove the theorem in the target system $S$, here $\mathsf{WKL}_0$.
3. Prove the axioms of $S$ in $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ plus the theorem.
   Here one turns a binary tree into a cover by intervals, so that a finite subcover would give a level of the tree with no nodes.

The trap is in step 3: every set used in it must exist by $\Delta^0_1$ comprehension.
For example, the set of nodes of a tree with infinitely many extensions exists in $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, but $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ does not prove that it exists.

### L3 human

The Big Five match levels of computability.
$\mathsf{RCA}_0$ matches computable mathematics, $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ matches the degrees of complete extensions of Peano arithmetic, and $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ matches closure under the [[Turing jump|60P2XWY5QX]].
$\mathsf{ATR}_0$ matches transfinite iterations of the jump, and $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$ matches the hyperjump.
Two of the systems match foundational programs: $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ is a [[partial realization of Hilbert's program|Z26S516BKH]], and $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ is linked to [[predicative mathematics|43BSXNWQBG]] through $\Gamma_0$.
[[Weihrauch reducibility|4G2JQ13XZ0]] refines the implications over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ into uniform reductions between problems.
