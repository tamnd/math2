## Definition

Let $X$ be a [[Noetherian scheme|ND6XEY68P4]].
Let $F$ be the [[free abelian group|0WJX8MGG37]] on the isomorphism classes $[\mathcal{F}]$ of [[coherent sheaves|56RXEJ8Z6S]] $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$.
Let $R$ be the subgroup of $F$ generated by the elements $[\mathcal{F}] - [\mathcal{F}'] - [\mathcal{F}'']$, one for each [[short exact sequence|KJKNJMGKZZ]] $0 \to \mathcal{F}' \to \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{F}'' \to 0$ of coherent sheaves on $X$.
The *$K_0$ of coherent sheaves* on $X$ is the quotient group
$$G_0(X) = F / R.$$
It is the [[Grothendieck group|5FDMY7PDYY]] of the abelian category of coherent sheaves on $X$.

## Remarks

Example: for a field $k$ and $X = \operatorname{Spec} k$, coherent sheaves are finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces.
Every short exact sequence splits and dimension is additive, so $[V] \mapsto \dim_k V$ gives an isomorphism $G_0(X) \cong \Z$.

Example: for $X = \operatorname{Spec} \Z$, the exact sequence $0 \to \Z \xrightarrow{n} \Z \to \Z/n \to 0$ gives $[\Z/n] = 0$ for $n \ge 1$.
Every finitely generated abelian group is a sum of $\Z^r$ and finite cyclic groups, so the rank gives $G_0(\operatorname{Spec} \Z) \cong \Z$.

Non-example: the free group $F$ itself, without the relations from exact sequences, is not $G_0(X)$.
In $F$ for $X = \operatorname{Spec} k$, the class $[k^2]$ differs from $2[k]$, while in $G_0(X)$ they are equal.

Other notations are $K'_0(X)$ and $K_0(\operatorname{Coh} X)$.
If $X$ is regular and separated, the map from the K-group of vector bundles to $G_0(X)$ is an isomorphism, because every coherent sheaf has a finite resolution by vector bundles.
