## Summary

A generalized Cartan matrix is a square integer matrix with $2$ on the diagonal and nonpositive entries off the diagonal.
Its zero entries are placed symmetrically about the diagonal.
It is the input data of a [[Kac–Moody algebra|HD13X0CNBD]].
The [[Cartan matrices|H59F1XBDZP]] of finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebras are the generalized Cartan matrices of finite type.
The matrices of affine type give the affine Lie algebras.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Cartan matrix of a semisimple Lie algebra records the angles and length ratios between its simple roots.
A generalized Cartan matrix keeps only the simplest integer rules that such a matrix obeys and drops the condition that it comes from a finite root system.
For example, the matrix with rows $(2, -2)$ and $(-2, 2)$ is a generalized Cartan matrix.
It is not the Cartan matrix of any finite-dimensional Lie algebra, because its determinant is $0$.
It belongs to the affine Lie algebra built from $\mathfrak{sl}_2$.
