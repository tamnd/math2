## Summary

A perfect fluid is a model of matter that has, in its own rest frame, an energy density and an isotropic pressure.
It has no viscosity, shear stress or heat flow.
Its [[stress–energy tensor|REDZ92MKXX]] is determined by the density, the pressure and the [[four-velocity|Y59V040THG]] of the fluid.
Perfect fluids model the interiors of stars, the matter and radiation in cosmology, and many exact solutions of the Einstein equations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a gas or a liquid that has no friction at all and does not conduct heat.
An observer moving with the fluid sees only two numbers at each point.
They are the energy per unit volume and the pressure, which pushes equally in all directions.
For example, the light filling an oven behaves as a perfect fluid whose pressure is one third of its energy density.
Pressureless dust, such as galaxies on cosmic scales, is the simplest perfect fluid.
