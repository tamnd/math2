## Definition

Let $R$ be a ring with identity, let $K(R)$ be its [[K-theory spectrum|2C1M0NWPB0]], and let $\ell \ge 2$ be an integer.
Let $K(R)/\ell$ be the cofibre, or [[mapping cone|6CXPGB13WD]], of the map $\ell \colon K(R) \to K(R)$, multiplication by $\ell$ on the [[spectrum|1GHF4EKBXS]].
For $n \ge 0$, the *K-group of $R$ with coefficients in $\Z/\ell$* is the [[homotopy group|P976WYEFMB]]
$$K_n(R; \Z/\ell) = \pi_n \big( K(R)/\ell \big).$$
The same definition applies to any exact or Waldhausen category, or scheme, in place of $R$.

## Remarks

The cofibre sequence $K(R) \xrightarrow{\ell} K(R) \to K(R)/\ell$ gives the universal coefficient sequence
$$0 \to K_n(R)/\ell \to K_n(R; \Z/\ell) \to K_{n-1}(R)[\ell] \to 0,$$
where $A[\ell]$ is the subgroup of elements of $A$ killed by $\ell$, part of the [[torsion subgroup|0DBWXQRXQJ]].
For $n \ge 2$, one can also write $K_n(R; \Z/\ell) = [P^n(\ell), BGL(R)^+]$, pointed homotopy classes of maps from the mod $\ell$ [[Moore space|68MA0RM5P6]] $P^n(\ell) = S^{n-1} \cup_\ell e^n$ into the [[plus construction|XFGZFM8PC6]].

Example: for a field $F$, $K_0(F) = \Z$ is torsion-free, so $K_1(F; \Z/\ell) \cong K_1(F)/\ell = F^\times/(F^\times)^\ell$.
For $F = \Q$ and $\ell = 2$ this is infinite, generated by the classes of $-1$ and of the primes.

Non-example: $K_n(R; \Z/\ell)$ is in general not $K_n(R) \otimes \Z/\ell$.
For $R = \C$, the group $K_2(\C)$ is divisible, since $\set{a, b} = \ell \set{c, b}$ when $a = c^\ell$, so $K_2(\C)/\ell = 0$.
But $K_1(\C)[\ell]$ is the group $\mu_\ell$ of $\ell$-th roots of unity, so $K_2(\C; \Z/\ell) \cong \mu_\ell \cong \Z/\ell$.
