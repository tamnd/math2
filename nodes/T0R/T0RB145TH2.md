## Summary

An Akivis algebra is a vector space with an antisymmetric bilinear bracket and a trilinear operation, linked by the Akivis identity.
The identity says that the Jacobi expression of the bracket equals an alternating sum of the trilinear operation.
Every [[nonassociative algebra|60W3THKGA7]] gives an Akivis algebra, with the commutator as bracket and the [[associator|RT4YV20367]] as trilinear operation.
This parallels the way every associative algebra gives a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]].
Akivis algebras arose in the differential geometry of webs.
The tangent space at the identity of a local smooth loop is an Akivis algebra.
This parallels the Lie algebra of a Lie group.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When an algebra is associative, its commutator satisfies the Jacobi identity and gives a Lie algebra.
When the algebra is not associative, the Jacobi identity fails, and the failure is measured by the associator $(xy)z - x(yz)$.
An Akivis algebra keeps both the commutator and the associator, and the one rule that always links them.
A Lie algebra is an Akivis algebra whose trilinear operation is zero.
