## Summary

Contact mechanics studies the deformation and the stresses of solid bodies that touch each other.
Its basic difficulty is that the region of contact is not known in advance.
It depends on the loads and the deformation, so the boundary conditions are unilateral inequalities.
Classical results include [[Hertz's theory|XNCND3KYNP]] of elastic spheres and the [[Boussinesq problem|QJW6NMNXJM]] of a point load on a half-space.
Mathematically the subject leads to the [[Signorini problem|QFQAB091MQ]] and to variational inequalities.
It is the basis of the design of bearings, gears, wheels on rails and of the study of friction and adhesion.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Press a ball onto a table.
Where they touch, both flatten a little and a small round patch of contact forms.
The size of the patch and the pressure in it depend on the force and on how stiff the ball and the table are.
Contact mechanics computes such patches and pressures, and it must also decide where the bodies touch and where they stay apart.
