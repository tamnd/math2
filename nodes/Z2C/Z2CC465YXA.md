## Summary

The Prandtl stress function is a scalar function on the cross-section of a twisted bar whose derivatives give the shear stresses.
It turns the [[torsion of prismatic bars|HWW08Q7372]] into a Poisson equation with zero boundary values.
The torque is twice its integral over the cross-section, and the shear stress is tangent to its level curves.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Stretch a soap film over a hole shaped like the cross-section and blow on it gently from below.
Prandtl observed that the height of the film behaves like his stress function.
The steepness of the film gives the size of the shear stress, and the volume under it gives the torque the bar carries.
For a round bar the film is a dome, steepest at the edge, which is where the bar is most stressed.
