## Description

Let $I$ be the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix and let $X, Y, Z$ be the [[Pauli matrices|XAQCW3D4QQ]].
Let $n \ge 1$.
The *Pauli group* on $n$ qubits is the set of $2^n \times 2^n$ matrices
$$\mathcal{P}_n = \set{ i^k \, P_1 \otimes \cdots \otimes P_n : k \in \set{0, 1, 2, 3}, \ P_j \in \set{I, X, Y, Z} },$$
with matrix multiplication as the [[group|G5S1M9]] operation.
Here $\otimes$ is the [[Kronecker product|0N7ENQRCXN]].
It has $4 \cdot 4^n = 4^{n+1}$ elements.

## Remarks

It is closed under products because the product of two Pauli matrices is a Pauli matrix times a power of $i$, for example $XY = iZ$.
For $n = 1$, $\mathcal{P}_1 = \set{\pm I, \pm iI, \pm X, \pm iX, \pm Y, \pm iY, \pm Z, \pm iZ}$, of order $16$.

Any two elements of $\mathcal{P}_n$ either commute or anticommute.
The center of $\mathcal{P}_n$ is $\set{\pm I, \pm iI}$, where $I$ now denotes the identity on $(\C^2)^{\otimes n}$.
The [[quotient group|0KYX2N69J0]] of $\mathcal{P}_n$ by its center is abelian and isomorphic to $\F_2^{2n}$, where $\F_2$ is the [[field with two elements|0ZXZ82FTX2]].
Under this isomorphism, $X^{a_1}Z^{b_1} \otimes \cdots \otimes X^{a_n}Z^{b_n}$ maps to $(a, b) \in \F_2^{2n}$, up to phase.
Two elements commute exactly when the symplectic form $a \cdot b' + a' \cdot b$ of their images is $0$ in $\F_2$.

The Pauli group is a finite $2$-group.
It is the basic error group of quantum error correction, and stabilizer codes are defined by its abelian subgroups.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Pauli group collects all ways to apply a bit flip, a phase flip, both, or nothing to each qubit of a register, together with overall phases $\pm 1, \pm i$.
It is small and finite, and its elements behave very simply: any two either commute or anticommute.
Quantum error correction describes the typical errors on qubits by elements of this group.
