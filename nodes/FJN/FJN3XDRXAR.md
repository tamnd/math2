## Summary

**Conjunction introduction** (often abbreviated simply as **conjunction** and also called **and introduction** or **adjunction**) is a valid rule of inference of propositional logic. The rule makes it possible to introduce a [[conjunction|025YZN3VJW]] into a logical proof. It is the inference that if the [[proposition|0ZGC7ZESYN]] $P$ is true, and the proposition $Q$ is true, then the logical conjunction of the two propositions $P$ and $Q$ is true.
