## Summary

Entanglement distillation is the conversion of many copies of a weakly or noisily [[entangled|CHNXT8CCK7]] state into fewer copies of nearly perfect [[Bell states|CA57HMCRQQ]].
The parties may use only [[LOCC|F5EB23T03M]], local operations and classical communication.
The best asymptotic rate of this conversion is the distillable entanglement of the state.
Distillation is needed because protocols such as teleportation require pure maximally entangled pairs, while real sources produce noisy ones.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Suppose two friends share many pairs of particles, each only partly entangled because of noise.
By working on many pairs together and comparing notes by phone, they can sacrifice some pairs to purify the others.
At the end they hold fewer pairs, but these are almost perfectly entangled.
It is like refining ore: much raw material goes in and a smaller amount of pure metal comes out.
Some entangled states cannot be refined at all; their entanglement is called bound.
