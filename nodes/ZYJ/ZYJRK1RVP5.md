## Summary

The Biot number $\mathrm{Bi} = h L / k$ is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that compares heat resistance inside a solid body with heat resistance at its surface.
Here $h$ is the [[heat transfer coefficient|J821AD6YTE]] at the surface and $k$ is the [[thermal conductivity|XBQBT9E7JT]] of the solid.
A small Biot number means that the temperature inside the body is nearly uniform.
This justifies the [[lumped-element thermal model|EB1DQP2E0K]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take a hot potato out of the oven and let it cool in the air.
Heat must first travel from the centre to the skin, and then leave the skin into the air.
The Biot number says which of these two steps is the bottleneck.
If the step into the air is the slow one, the Biot number is small and the whole potato cools almost evenly.
If the step inside is the slow one, the Biot number is large and the centre stays hot while the skin cools.
