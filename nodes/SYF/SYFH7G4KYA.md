## Summary

In number theory, **primes in arithmetic progression** are any [[sequence|0G9EKM0E15]] of at least three [[prime numbers|01826KMG2H]] that are consecutive terms in an arithmetic progression. An example is the sequence of primes (3, 7, 11), which is given by $a_n = 3 + 4n$ for $0 \le n \le 2$.
