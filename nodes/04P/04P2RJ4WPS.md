## Summary

A continued fraction is an expression $b_0 + a_1 / (b_1 + a_2 / (b_2 + \cdots))$, a fraction whose denominator contains a fraction, and so on.
The [[definition of a continued fraction|0BK6JBK58W]] gives the precise form.
