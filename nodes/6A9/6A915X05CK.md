## Summary

In cosmology, the equation of state of a component of the universe is the ratio $w = p/\rho$ of its pressure to its energy density.
Each kind of matter has its own typical value: $w = 0$ for cold matter, $w = 1/3$ for radiation, and $w = -1$ for a [[cosmological constant|WVKKDY9V61]].
The value of $w$ decides how fast the density of the component dilutes as the universe expands, and so which component dominates at each epoch.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When the universe expands, a box of cold matter keeps its particles, so its density drops like the volume.
A box of light also loses energy because each photon is stretched, so its density drops faster.
The energy of empty space does not dilute at all.
The single number $w$ captures these three behaviours and everything in between.
