## Summary

Equivariant K-theory is the version of [[complex K-theory|9E4JE4902G]] for spaces with an action of a [[compact Lie group|9TFC6ADC1C]] $G$.
It is built from vector bundles on which $G$ acts compatibly with its action on the base.
For a point it gives the [[representation ring|RKTX9MN7Q3]] of $G$, and for a free action it gives the K-theory of the orbit space.
It is the setting of the equivariant index theorem and of the [[Atiyah–Segal completion theorem|QKS024G8HQ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a group acts on a space, one can ask for vector bundles that are symmetric under the group.
Each symmetry of the space should lift to a linear map between fibres, compatible with the group law.
Over a single point such a bundle is just a vector space with a group action, that is, a representation.
So equivariant K-theory mixes the topology of the space with the representation theory of the group.
