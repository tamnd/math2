## Summary

The lower central series of a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] $\mathfrak g$ is the decreasing chain of ideals $\mathfrak g \supseteq [\mathfrak g, \mathfrak g] \supseteq [\mathfrak g, [\mathfrak g, \mathfrak g]] \supseteq \cdots$.
Each term is obtained by bracketing the previous one with the whole algebra.
A Lie algebra is nilpotent exactly when this chain reaches $0$.
It is the Lie algebra analogue of the [[lower central series|0K98R7A4VC]] of a group.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Start with the whole Lie algebra.
At each step, keep only what can be written as sums of brackets of the last step with arbitrary elements.
Each step removes a layer that commutes with everything modulo the next layer.
For the Heisenberg algebra with $[x, y] = z$, the chain is the whole algebra, then the line spanned by $z$, then $0$.
