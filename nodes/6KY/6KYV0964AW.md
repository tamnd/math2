## Statement

Let $f:V\rightarrow W$ to be the fiber space from m-dimensional variety $V$ to n-dimensional variety $W$ and each fibers $V_w=f^{-1}(w)$ connected. Then

$$\kappa(V)\ge\kappa(V_w)+\kappa(W).$$
