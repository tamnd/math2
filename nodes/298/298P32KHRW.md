## Summary

In a first-price sealed-bid auction, all bidders submit sealed bids at the same time, and the highest bidder wins and pays her own bid.
Bidders have a reason to bid below their values, and how much they shade their bids depends on what they believe about the other bidders.
It is studied as a Bayesian game in [[auction theory|86ZWXGHNGB]], and under standard assumptions it yields the same expected revenue as the [[Vickrey auction|75GR8RZSFP]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Each bidder writes one bid in a sealed envelope, and the highest bid wins and is paid in full.
Bidding exactly your value gains nothing, since you would pay all that the item is worth to you.
So you bid less, trading a lower chance of winning for a better deal when you win.
With two bidders whose values are uniformly random between $0$ and $1$, the equilibrium is to bid half of one's value.
