## Statement

Let $F$ be a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] of [[characteristic|1ZB13N78XW]] different from $2$, and let $n \ge 0$.
Then the [[Galois symbol|N4NNC0BB7A]]
$$K_n^M(F)/2 \longrightarrow H^n(F, \Z/2\Z), \qquad \set{a_1, \ldots, a_n} \mapsto \delta(a_1) \cup \cdots \cup \delta(a_n),$$
from [[Milnor K-theory|2YXDQ7Q445]] modulo $2$ to [[Galois cohomology|9MPCZ3EAJM]] with coefficients in $\Z/2\Z = \mu_2^{\otimes n}$, is an isomorphism.
Here $\delta \colon F^\times \to H^1(F, \Z/2\Z)$ is the Kummer map.

## Remarks

Milnor stated this in 1970, and Voevodsky proved it using motivic cohomology and motivic Steenrod operations.
Before that, the case $n \le 1$ followed from Kummer theory, the case $n = 2$ was proved by Merkurjev, and the case $n = 3$ by Merkurjev–Suslin and, independently, by Rost.
It is the case $\ell = 2$ of the [[norm residue isomorphism theorem|5Q5VN56QH4]].

Example: $F = \R$.
Since $\R^\times / (\R^\times)^2 = \set{\pm 1}$, the group $K_n^M(\R)/2$ is generated by $\set{-1, \ldots, -1}$.
The cohomology ring $H^*(\R, \Z/2\Z)$ is $\F_2[x]$ with $x = \delta(-1)$, so the symbol maps to $x^n \ne 0$, and both sides are $\Z/2\Z$.

The hypothesis on the characteristic is needed for the Kummer map.
In characteristic $2$, squaring is not a surjective map of $(F^s)^\times$ with kernel $\mu_2$, and the Galois symbol is not defined in this form.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Milnor K-theory modulo $2$ is built from symbols of field elements by simple rules.
Galois cohomology with coefficients in $\Z/2\Z$ is built from the symmetries of the field extensions.
The theorem says that they agree in every degree.

### L2 human

Combined with the work of Orlov, Vishik and Voevodsky, it also gives the Milnor conjecture on quadratic forms.
That result says that the graded ring of the Witt ring of $F$ for its fundamental ideal is isomorphic to $K_*^M(F)/2$.
So quadratic forms over $F$ are classified, up to a filtration, by Galois cohomology invariants.
