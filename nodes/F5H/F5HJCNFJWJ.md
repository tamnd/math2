## Statement

Let $A$ and $B$ be square matrices of the same size $n \times n$, with real or complex entries.
Then the [[determinant|00KDZRW4C0]] of their [[matrix product|032Q9FB8MT]] is the product of their determinants:
$$\det(AB) = \det(A)\,\det(B).$$
