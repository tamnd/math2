## Summary

In computational number theory, **Cornacchia's algorithm** is an algorithm for solving the [[Diophantine equation|08B1RTW7VZ]] $x^2+dy^2=m$, where $1\le d<m$ and *d* and *m* are coprime. The algorithm was described in 1908 by Giuseppe Cornacchia.
