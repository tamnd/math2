## Summary

A map between metric spaces is Hölder continuous with exponent $\alpha$ when $d(f(x), f(y)) \le C \, d(x, y)^\alpha$ for some constant $C$ and all $x, y$.
For $\alpha = 1$ this is the Lipschitz condition.
