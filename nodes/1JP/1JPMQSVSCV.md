## Summary

This topic is a part of [[classical mechanics|9X01KNW495]].
It covers the integral and differential principles that give the equations of motion, such as Hamilton's principle, the least action principle of Maupertuis and Jacobi, and the principles of Gauss, Hertz and Jourdain.
