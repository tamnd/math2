## Summary

In the theory of elliptic curves, **Tate's algorithm** takes as input an integral model of an elliptic curve *E* over $\mathbb{Q}$, or more generally an algebraic number field, and a prime or [[prime ideal|00MHHZ9SNE]] *p*. It returns the exponent *f*<sub>*p*</sub> of *p* in the conductor of *E*, the type of reduction at *p*, the local index
