## Statement

The identity is used in a proof of **Hua's theorem**, which states that if $\sigma$ is a [[function|0EPAT51DXQ]] between division rings satisfying

$$\sigma(a + b) = \sigma(a) + \sigma(b), \quad \sigma(1) = 1, \quad \sigma(a^{-1}) = \sigma(a)^{-1},$$

then $\sigma$ is a [[homomorphism|0GGE0Z0JQ1]] or an antihomomorphism. This theorem is connected to the fundamental theorem of projective geometry.
