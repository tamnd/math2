## Summary

The tide-generating potential is the potential whose gradient is the [[tidal acceleration|48JY6FBEHB]] produced by an external body such as the Moon or the Sun.
It is the gravitational potential of the body with its constant and linear parts removed.
Its expansion in [[Legendre polynomials|QFYGJVA996]] begins at degree two, and the degree two term carries almost all of the effect on the Earth.
Expanding it in time gives the [[tidal harmonic constituents|Y07010W5HJ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A potential is a landscape whose slope gives a force.
The tide-generating potential is the landscape whose slope gives the stretching force of the Moon or Sun, after the uniform pull on the whole Earth has been taken away.
On the Earth's surface it is highest at the points facing towards and away from the Moon and lowest on the circle halfway between.
As the Earth turns under this pattern, each place passes two highs and two lows a day.
