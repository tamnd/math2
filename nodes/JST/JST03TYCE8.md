## Statement

In number theory, the **second Hardy–Littlewood conjecture** concerns the number of [[primes|01826KMG2H]] in [[intervals|0Q1D85B8G6]]. Along with the first Hardy–Littlewood conjecture, the second Hardy–Littlewood conjecture was proposed by G. H. Hardy and John Edensor Littlewood in 1923.
