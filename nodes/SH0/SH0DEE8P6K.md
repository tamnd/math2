## Summary

Ashtekar variables are canonical variables for general relativity in which the configuration variable is a connection rather than a metric.
Abhay Ashtekar introduced them in 1986.
They make general relativity look like a gauge theory of the [[Yang–Mills|JJWETW5AT8]] type, with gauge group $SU(2)$.
In their original complex form the constraints of the [[ADM formalism|CFAFAPV7HB]] become polynomial.
A real version, due to Fernando Barbero (1995), is the starting point of [[loop quantum gravity|9C3ECSDRTP]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

General relativity is usually written in terms of the metric, which measures lengths and angles.
Ashtekar found that one can instead use a rule for rotating frames as one moves around space, a connection, as in the theory of the strong and weak forces.
Its partner variable is a field of three orthogonal directions at each point, scaled by the volume.
In these variables the equations of gravity become simpler polynomial expressions.
This made new quantization methods possible, based on loops and holonomies.
