## Summary

The Wronskian of $n$ functions is the determinant of the matrix of the functions and their derivatives up to order $n - 1$.
It is a test for the linear independence of solutions of a linear differential equation. See the [[definition|0N5NF1RGBP]].

## Remarks

It is named after Józef Hoene-Wroński.
