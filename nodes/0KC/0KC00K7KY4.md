## Summary

The Bernoulli numbers are a sequence of rational numbers $B_n$, the coefficients of $t^n / n!$ in the expansion of $t / (e^t - 1)$.
The [[definition of the Bernoulli numbers|0RAS24E1R5]] gives the precise form.
