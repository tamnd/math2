## Summary

In number theory, the **generalized taxicab number** $\operatorname{Taxicab}(k, j, n)$ is the smallest number — if it exists — that can be expressed as the sum of $j$ numbers to the $k$th positive power in $n$ different ways. For $k = 3$ and $j = 2$, they coincide with the taxicab number.
