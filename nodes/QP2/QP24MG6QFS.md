## Summary

Nutation is the nodding of the axis of a spinning body: an oscillation of the angle between the axis and a fixed direction.
It usually comes on top of [[precession|CFE9SNEHMP]], so that the axis traces a wavy path around the fixed direction.
For a heavy symmetric top it is periodic and can be computed exactly.
The Earth's axis also nutates, mainly with a period of about $18.6$ years, due to the changing torque of the Moon.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Watch a spinning top that has just been tilted: its axis circles around the vertical, but it also bobs up and down a little as it goes.
This bobbing is nutation.
If you draw the path of the tip of the axis, you see loops or waves instead of a smooth circle.
For a fast top the nodding is small and quick, and friction soon damps it.
