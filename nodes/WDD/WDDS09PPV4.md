## Summary

A gravity assist is a close flyby of a planet that changes the speed and direction of a spacecraft relative to the Sun without fuel.
Relative to the planet, the craft leaves with the same speed as it arrived, only turned.
Adding the planet's own motion turns this rotation into a gain or loss of speed relative to the Sun.
Gravity assists made possible the missions to the outer planets.
See the [[definition|51CJVWZPY6]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A ball thrown at an approaching train bounces back faster than it was thrown.
A spacecraft passing behind a moving planet is similar, with gravity in place of the bounce.
Seen from the planet, the craft just swings around and leaves as fast as it came.
Seen from the Sun, it has borrowed a little of the planet's orbital speed.
