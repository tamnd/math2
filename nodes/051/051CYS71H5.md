## Summary

Suppose that all cases are equally likely.
Then the classical definition says that the probability of an event is the number of favourable cases divided by the number of all cases.
It goes back to Jacob Bernoulli and Pierre-Simon Laplace. The node [[classical definition of probability|0AKNPYQ04R]] gives the precise form.
