## Statement

The set $\mathbb{R}$ of real numbers is [[uncountable|0X55M9XET4]].

## Remarks

Cantor proved it in his article of 1874. That proof uses nested intervals.
A later proof uses [[Cantor's diagonal argument|0XCH8QVDCQ]] on decimal or binary expansions.
Since the algebraic numbers are countable, the result shows that transcendental numbers exist. Cantor gave this application in the same article.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Each list of real numbers misses some real number.
So there are more real numbers than natural numbers, although both sets are infinite.
