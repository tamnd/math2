## Summary

A Cauchy elastic material is a material whose stress at each point depends only on the current state of deformation there, through the [[deformation gradient|HTYRR2WDQ6]].
The stress does not depend on the path by which the deformation was reached, nor on time or on strain rate.
It is the most general notion of an elastic solid in nonlinear continuum mechanics, and it contains hyperelastic materials as a special case.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Stretch a rubber band, let it go, and stretch it again to the same length: it pulls back with the same force both times.
For a Cauchy elastic material this is always true: the force depends only on the present shape, not on the history.
Materials that creep or flow, such as putty or warm wax, are not like this.
