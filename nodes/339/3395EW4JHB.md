## Statement

The [[complete bipartite graph|0V0GC7BQAN]] $K_{3,3}$ is not [[planar|029JQEK15B]]. No drawing of it in the plane avoids edge crossings.

## Remarks

The graph has $6$ vertices and $9$ edges. It has no cycle of length $3$, so a planar drawing would force $e \le 2v - 4 = 8$. This is a contradiction.
