## Summary

In graph theory, a **$L(h, k)$-labelling**, **$L(h, k)$-coloring** or sometimes **$L(p, q)$-coloring** is a [[(proper) vertex coloring|0NCPXH194Y]] in which every pair of adjacent vertices has color numbers that differ by at least $h$, and any nodes connected by a 2 length path have their colors differ by at least $k$. The parameters $h, k$ are understood to be non-negative [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]].
