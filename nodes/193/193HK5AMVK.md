## Summary

Fractional viscoelastic models are linear [[viscoelastic|GCX1B3DAJN]] constitutive laws in which ordinary time derivatives are replaced by derivatives of non-integer order from [[fractional calculus|T73XK15SA2]].
Their basic element, the springpot, interpolates between a spring and a dashpot and has a power-law [[relaxation modulus|JSSYGY0V87]].
Such models fit the broad, power-law relaxation of polymers and biological tissue with very few parameters, where spring–dashpot models need many terms.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A spring answers to how far it is stretched, and a dashpot answers to how fast it is stretched.
A springpot answers to something in between, a derivative of order between $0$ and $1$.
Its stress after a sudden stretch decays like a power of time, $t^{-\alpha}$, rather than exponentially.
Many soft materials, such as gels and living tissue, relax in just this power-law way.
