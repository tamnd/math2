## Summary

The cyclotomic trace is a natural map $K(R) \to TC(R)$ from the [[algebraic K-theory|5GVFZWMWC8]] spectrum of a ring to its [[topological cyclic homology|6RQS843SE5]].
It was constructed by Bökstedt, Hsiang and Madsen.
It refines the [[Dennis trace map|4YDCBSAJZ0]] and its topological version $K(R) \to THH(R)$.
For nilpotent extensions it detects relative K-theory exactly, which makes it the main tool for computing K-groups of local and $p$-adic rings.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A trace turns matrices into something simpler that does not see conjugation.
The cyclotomic trace is a very refined trace: its target keeps track of the circle symmetry and of Frobenius maps.
Because of this extra structure, its target is close to K-theory.
Bökstedt, Hsiang and Madsen first used it to prove the algebraic K-theory Novikov conjecture for groups whose homology is finitely generated in each degree.
