## Statement

A *Kirchberg algebra* is a [[C*-algebra|M2WFY1D3SZ]] that is separable, simple, [[nuclear|D2M4VXJ7PA]] and purely infinite.
Here purely infinite means that every nonzero hereditary C*-subalgebra contains an infinite projection.
Let $A$ and $B$ be Kirchberg algebras.

1. $A \otimes \mathcal{K} \cong B \otimes \mathcal{K}$ if and only if $A$ and $B$ are [[KK-equivalent|1W9Q7W378J]], where $\mathcal{K}$ is the algebra of compact operators on a separable infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.
2. For unital $A$ and $B$: $A \cong B$ if and only if some KK-equivalence between them induces a map on $K_0$ sending $[1_A]$ to $[1_B]$.
3. Suppose moreover that $A$ and $B$ satisfy the universal coefficient theorem.
   Then $A \otimes \mathcal{K} \cong B \otimes \mathcal{K}$ if and only if $K_0(A) \cong K_0(B)$ and $K_1(A) \cong K_1(B)$.
   If they are unital, then $A \cong B$ if and only if $K_1(A) \cong K_1(B)$ and some isomorphism $K_0(A) \cong K_0(B)$ sends $[1_A]$ to $[1_B]$.

## Remarks

The theorem was proved independently by Kirchberg and by Phillips in the mid-1990s.
Two key inputs are Kirchberg's theorems $A \otimes \mathcal{O}_2 \cong \mathcal{O}_2$ and $A \otimes \mathcal{O}_\infty \cong A$ for every unital Kirchberg algebra $A$, where $\mathcal{O}_n$ are the [[Cuntz algebras|36Q7XH9P7W]].
Example: $\mathcal{O}_n$ is a unital Kirchberg algebra in the UCT class with $K_0(\mathcal{O}_n) \cong \Z/(n-1)\Z$, $[1] \mapsto 1$, and $K_1(\mathcal{O}_n) = 0$.
So every unital Kirchberg algebra in the UCT class with these invariants is isomorphic to $\mathcal{O}_n$.
The range of the invariant is known.
For countable abelian groups $G_0$, $G_1$ and $g \in G_0$, some unital Kirchberg algebra in the UCT class has $(K_0, [1], K_1) \cong (G_0, g, G_1)$.
Whether every separable nuclear C*-algebra satisfies the universal coefficient theorem is an open problem, so the UCT hypothesis in part 3 cannot at present be removed.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Kirchberg algebras are the "infinite" simple nuclear C*-algebras, such as the Cuntz algebras.
The theorem says that, among them, K-theory is a complete invariant.
Two such algebras that satisfy a standard technical condition are the same exactly when their K-groups and unit classes agree.
So their classification reduces to a list of pairs of countable abelian groups.

### L2 human

It is the purely infinite half of the Elliott classification program, and it was settled long before the stably finite half.
The strategy is to lift KK-equivalences to isomorphisms, using that these algebras absorb $\mathcal{O}_\infty$ tensorially.
The UCT turns KK-equivalence into an isomorphism of K-groups, which is how part 3 follows from parts 1 and 2.
