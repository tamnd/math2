## Summary

This topic is a part of [[matrix theory and multilinear algebra|WMBK7M313V]].
It covers matrices with entries in commutative rings, principal ideal domains, the integers, finite fields and the quaternions, and the normal forms and invariants that replace eigenvalues there.
