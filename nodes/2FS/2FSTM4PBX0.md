## Summary

Conformal gravity is a family of theories of gravity whose action is unchanged when the metric is multiplied by an arbitrary positive function.
The main example in four dimensions is Weyl gravity, whose Lagrangian is the square of the [[Weyl tensor|SBHY31HESP]].
Its field equations are of fourth order in the metric, unlike the second-order equations of [[general relativity|7M7M55TKXA]].
It is one of the [[alternatives to general relativity|384JFR3K6X]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A conformal change of a metric stretches all lengths at a point by the same factor, but the factor may vary from point to point.
Angles and light cones stay the same, only the scale changes.
Conformal gravity asks that the laws of gravity see only angles and light cones, not scales.
The Weyl tensor is the part of curvature that does not change under such stretching, so it is the natural building block.
For example, flat spacetime and any spacetime obtained from it by a conformal change are equally good solutions.
