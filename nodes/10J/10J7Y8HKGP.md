## Summary

A process function is a quantity that belongs to a process, that is, to a path between states, and not to a single state.
Its value depends on the whole path, not only on its end points.
[[Heat|CY4GJQGF06]] and [[work|S8H21RWQ86]] are the main process functions, in contrast with [[state functions|T8J5XRYBZZ]] such as internal energy.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The distance you drive from one town to another depends on the road you choose.
It makes no sense to ask how much distance a town has; distance belongs to the trip.
Heat and work are like this: a gas does not contain a certain amount of work or heat.
They measure energy that crosses the boundary during a process, and their amounts depend on how the process is carried out.
