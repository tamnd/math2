## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, a **distance-regular graph** is a [[regular graph|0KA4S8R39M]] such that for any two vertices $v$ and $w$, the number of vertices at [[distance|0QV9VNNB4Z]] $j$ from $v$ and at distance $k$ from $w$ depends only upon $j$, $k$, and the distance between $v$ and $w$.
