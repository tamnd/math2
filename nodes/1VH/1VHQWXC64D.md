## Summary

This topic is a part of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]].
It covers cosets, the index of a subgroup, Lagrange's theorem and its consequences, products of subgroups and double cosets.
The cosets of a subgroup cut a group into pieces of equal size.
This one fact gives Lagrange's theorem: the order of a subgroup of a finite group divides the order of the group.
The consequences include Fermat's little theorem and the fact that each group of prime order is cyclic.
The converse of Lagrange's theorem is false, and the last pages study when it holds.
