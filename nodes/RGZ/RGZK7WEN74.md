## Statement

Let $T$ be a [[tree|0MJ0JS72DZ]] with at least two vertices.
Then $T$ has at least two distinct vertices whose [[degree|0T0F6CCS9W]] is $1$.

## Remarks

A vertex of degree $1$ is a leaf.
As in the source, a graph here is a finite graph, as in a text on discrete mathematics. This is a convention of the setting and not an added hypothesis.
The two ends of a longest path in $T$ are such vertices.
