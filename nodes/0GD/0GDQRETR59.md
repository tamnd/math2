## Summary

The Prüfer sequence encodes a labelled tree on $n$ vertices as a sequence of $n - 2$ labels. This encoding is a bijection, and it proves Cayley's formula. The node [[Prüfer sequence|09PV9JSY6E]] gives the precise definition.
