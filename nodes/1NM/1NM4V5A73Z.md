## Summary

In mathematics, an **algebraic function field** (often abbreviated as **function field**) of $n$ variables over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $k$ is a finitely generated [[field extension|0EQWVHF3W5]] $K/k$ which has transcendence degree $n$ over $k$. Equivalently, an algebraic function field of $n$ variables over $k$ may be defined as a finite field extension of the field $K=k(x_1,\dots,x_n)$ of rational functions in $n$ variables over $k$.
