## Summary

The magnetic field $B$ is the [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] that describes the force on moving electric [[charges|6XPSV47R56]], beyond the force of the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]].
The force is perpendicular both to the velocity of the charge and to the field.
Magnetic fields are produced by electric currents and by magnetic materials.
Together with the electric field, $B$ is one of the two unknowns of [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A compass needle turns to point along the magnetic field of the Earth.
Near a bar magnet, the field lines run out of the north pole and back into the south pole, and they never start or end.
A charged particle moving through a magnetic field is pushed sideways, not forward, so its path curves.
In a uniform field, a charge moving across the field travels in a circle.
