## Summary

A stream function is a scalar function $\psi$ that encodes a two-dimensional [[incompressible flow|2JZ0C15A9C]] through $u = \partial \psi / \partial y$ and $v = -\partial \psi / \partial x$.
The curves of constant $\psi$ are the [[streamlines|ZWF8B7T5NX]] of the flow.
The difference of $\psi$ between two points is the volume flux through any curve that joins them.
It reduces the two unknown velocity components to one scalar unknown.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Draw a contour map of a function $\psi$ on a sheet of paper.
Now let water flow along the contour lines, keeping the higher ground on its left, and flowing faster where the contours are close together.
This is the flow whose stream function is $\psi$.
For example, $\psi = y$ has horizontal contour lines equally spaced, and its flow is the uniform flow $(1, 0)$ to the right.
