## Summary

In number theory, a **Williams number base *b*** is a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] of the form $(b-1) \cdot b^n-1$ for integers *b* ≥ 2 and *n* ≥ 1. The Williams numbers base 2 are exactly the Mersenne numbers.
