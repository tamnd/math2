This topic is a part of [[integral transforms and asymptotic methods|4VR0XYQ63R]].
It covers the transforms whose kernels are Bessel, Legendre, Jacobi, Whittaker or Meijer functions, the index transforms, and the Abel transform of radially symmetric data.
