## Summary

**Dependence logic** is a logical formalism, created by Jouko Väänänen, which adds *dependence atoms* to the language of first-order logic. A dependence atom is an expression of the form $=\!\!(t_1 \ldots t_n)$, where $t_1 \ldots t_n$ are terms, and corresponds to the statement that the value of $t_n$ is functionally dependent on the values of $t_1,\ldots, t_{n-1}$.
