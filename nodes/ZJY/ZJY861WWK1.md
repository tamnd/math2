## Summary

Let $f$ be a differentiable function on an open interval $(a,b)$. This is the calculus meaning of the two shapes of a graph.

The function $f$ is concave up on $(a,b)$ exactly when its [[derivative function|0QKJ6NT0R9]] $f'$ is increasing on $(a,b)$. The graph then bends upward, and the slopes of the tangent lines grow as $x$ grows.

The function $f$ is concave down on $(a,b)$ exactly when $f'$ is decreasing on $(a,b)$. The graph then bends downward, and the slopes of the tangent lines shrink as $x$ grows.

For differentiable functions this matches the notions of a [[convex function|07Q7CNM5WF]] and a [[concave function|0GCM7B6PBK]].
