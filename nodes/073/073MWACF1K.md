## Summary

The order of a pole $c$ of $f$ is the integer $m \ge 1$ with the following property. The function $(z - c)^m f(z)$ extends to a holomorphic function near $c$ that is not $0$ at $c$, see the [[definition|0YK27TJ4WF]].
