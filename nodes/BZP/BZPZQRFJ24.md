## Summary

Magnetostriction is the change of shape or size of a ferromagnetic material when its magnetization changes.
It comes from the coupling between the directions of the atomic magnetic moments and the crystal lattice.
The strains are small, typically millionths, but some alloys such as Terfenol-D show a much larger effect.
It causes the hum of transformers and is used in sonar transducers, actuators and sensors, together with its inverse, the change of magnetization under stress.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put an iron bar in a coil and switch on the current: the bar changes its length a tiny bit.
Inside the bar, small magnetic regions turn to line up with the field, and as they turn they stretch the crystal slightly in their direction.
With alternating current the bar lengthens and shortens twice per cycle, and this vibration is the hum you hear near a transformer.
Some materials get longer along the field and others, such as nickel, get shorter.
