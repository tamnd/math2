## Summary

A system of imprimitivity is a unitary representation of a group together with a projection-valued measure on a space on which the group acts.
The group must move the projections in the same way as it moves the sets.
Induced representations come with a canonical system of imprimitivity.
The [[Mackey imprimitivity theorem|CTYQQWJPMB]] says that, for transitive actions, every system of imprimitivity is of this form.
This is the basis of the Mackey machine for classifying representations of group extensions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a Hilbert space split into pieces labelled by points of a space $X$, such as the positions of a quantum particle.
A group acts on $X$, and it also acts on the Hilbert space.
The two actions fit together when moving a state by $g$ moves the piece labelled by a set $E$ to the piece labelled by $gE$.
Such a compatible pair is a system of imprimitivity.
A simple case is a group permuting a set $X$ and permuting the basis vectors $\delta_x$ of $\ell^2(X)$ in the same way.
