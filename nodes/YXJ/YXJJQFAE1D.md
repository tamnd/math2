## Summary

In algebra, specifically in the theory of [[commutative rings|0310REJJY8]], a **quasi-unmixed ring** (also called a **formally equidimensional ring** in EGA) is a [[Noetherian ring|0670T59HGE]] $A$ such that for each [[prime ideal|00MHHZ9SNE]] *p*, the completion of the localization *A<sub>p</sub>* is equidimensional, i.e. for each minimal prime ideal *q* in the completion $\widehat{A_p}$, $\dim \widehat{A_p}/q = \dim A_p$ = the Krull dimension of *A<sub>p</sub>*.
