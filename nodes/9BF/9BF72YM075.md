## Summary

One strategy dominates another when it does at least as well whatever the other players do.
A strictly dominated strategy is never played by a rational player and can be removed from the game.
Repeating this removal, called iterated elimination of dominated strategies, often simplifies a game a lot.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Suppose that whatever your rival does, choice A pays you more than choice B.
Then choice B is strictly dominated, and there is no reason ever to make that choice.
In the prisoner's dilemma, confessing pays more than staying silent whether the partner stays silent or confesses.
So confessing dominates staying silent, even though both prisoners would be better off if both stayed silent.
