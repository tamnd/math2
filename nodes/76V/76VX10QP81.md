## Proof

We sort the columns of the reduced form into pivot columns and free columns, and we show that the two counts are the rank and the nullity.
Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix over a field $F$, with rank $r$ and nullity $k$.

1. By the [[existence of the reduced form|FCM29NQ6DN]], $A$ is row equivalent to a matrix $R$ in [[reduced row echelon form|0NNR68BFRY]].
2. Let $p$ be the number of columns of $R$ that contain a [[pivot|0BCMGDHZGR]], and let $f$ be the number of the other columns, the free columns.
3. Each of the $n$ columns of $R$ is either a pivot column or a free column, and not both, so $p + f = n$.
4. The pivot columns of $A$ form a basis of $\operatorname{Col}(A)$, by the [[theorem on the column space|11CSKXQNG3]].
So $\dim \operatorname{Col}(A) = p$, and $r = p$ by the [[definition of rank|0EWQEYRVA8]].
5. The systems $Ax = 0$ and $Rx = 0$ have the same solutions, and the [[theorem on the free variables|5NKND0CPKP]] gives one basis vector of $\operatorname{Null}(A)$ for each free column.
So $k = f$.
6. Steps 3, 4 and 5 give $r + k = p + f = n$.

## Idea

Row reduction sorts the columns into two kinds.
A pivot column carries a new direction, so the pivot columns count the rank.
A free column carries a free variable, and each free variable gives one basis vector of the solutions of $Ax = 0$, so the free columns count the nullity.
For example, a $3 \times 4$ matrix with pivots in columns 1 and 3 has rank $2$, free variables $x_2$ and $x_4$, and nullity $2$.

## Variants

The [[rank-nullity theorem for linear maps|0W4FSW1TVB]], applied to $x \mapsto Ax$, gives the same result without row reduction: extend a basis of the kernel to a basis of $F^n$, and show that the images of the added vectors form a basis of the image.
Over $\mathbb{R}$, the [[four fundamental subspaces|73DVECPAT6]] give the orthogonal decomposition $\mathbb{R}^n = \operatorname{Row}(A) \oplus \operatorname{Null}(A)$, and since $\dim \operatorname{Row}(A) = r$, this gives $n = r + k$ once more.
