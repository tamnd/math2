## Summary

An algebra over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $K$ is a [[vector space|000AYPA9AJ]] over $K$ with a bilinear multiplication.
It is the case of a [[nonassociative algebra|60W3THKGA7]] where the ring of scalars is a field.
Many authors also require the product to be associative and to have a unit; in the theory of nonassociative algebras neither is assumed.
Once a [[basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]] is chosen, the product is described by its [[structure constants|HFXWYNFB9A]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

An algebra over a field is a space of vectors in which you may also multiply two vectors and get a vector.
The multiplication must respect addition and scaling in each factor.
The complex numbers form an algebra over $\R$ of dimension $2$.
The $n \times n$ real matrices form an algebra over $\R$ of dimension $n^2$.
The vectors of $\R^3$ with the cross product form an algebra that is not associative.
