## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices and endomorphisms, the characteristic polynomial, multiplicities, polynomials in an endomorphism, the minimal polynomial, and the Cayley-Hamilton theorem.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Eigenvalue|0BQAA2BK3Q]]: a scalar $\lambda$ with $Tv = \lambda v$ for some $v \ne 0$, and zero as an eigenvalue of a singular map.
2. [[Eigenvector|0N6960W0BV]]: invariant lines, with rotations, projections, reflections and shears.
3. [[Eigenspace|0G0RFPG8HR]]: the kernel of $T - \lambda I$ and a basis by row reduction.
4. [[Spectrum of a matrix|0XPX5NC67E]]: the set of eigenvalues, and how it depends on the field.
5. [[Characteristic polynomial|0VSSPAFM2C]]: $\det(tI - A)$, monic of degree $n$, and the other sign convention.
6. [[Characteristic polynomial of an endomorphism|~756cfc1f]]: independence of the basis, direct sums and block triangular matrices.
7. [[Eigenvalues are the roots of the characteristic polynomial|053QVHF6N7]]: at most $n$ eigenvalues.
8. [[Computing eigenvalues and eigenvectors by hand|~7d332ef3]]: $2 \times 2$ and $3 \times 3$ examples, complex and triangular cases.
9. [[Coefficients of the characteristic polynomial|0FRYPZ6D0X]]: sums of principal minors, the trace and the determinant.
10. [[Trace is the sum of the eigenvalues|0NMXDCNNKR]]: counted with algebraic multiplicity.
11. [[Determinant is the product of the eigenvalues|0C2F40MEBC]]: counted with algebraic multiplicity.
12. [[Eigenvectors with distinct eigenvalues are linearly independent|0HNMFG8F8K]]: the sum of eigenspaces is direct.
13. [[Algebraic multiplicity|0FY42N02DV]]: the multiplicity of a root of the characteristic polynomial.
14. [[Geometric multiplicity|099TWVJ909]]: the dimension of the eigenspace, and defective eigenvalues.
15. [[Existence of an eigenvalue over an algebraically closed field|08CJB2NQ92]]: Axler's proof without determinants.
16. [[Invariant subspaces of dimension 1 or 2 for real operators|~9af39e83]]: real eigenvalues in odd dimension.
17. [[Eigenvalues of AB and BA|~4971526f]]: the same nonzero eigenvalues with multiplicities.
18. [[Left eigenvector|~d0c39701]]: eigenvectors of the transpose and biorthogonality.
19. [[Polynomial of an endomorphism|0E3MV6E6MG]]: the evaluation homomorphism and the eigenvalues of $p(T)$.
20. [[Annihilating polynomial|0PBN8VEKY8]]: existence in finite dimension and the ideal of annihilating polynomials.
21. [[Minimal polynomial|03NDVCSHSB]]: the monic generator of that ideal.
22. [[Minimal polynomial divides every annihilating polynomial|0VTAD25A3G]]: division with remainder.
23. [[Eigenvalues are the roots of the minimal polynomial|0QF4ZEFG52]]: a proof without the Cayley-Hamilton theorem.
24. [[Cayley-Hamilton theorem|0F765KFC2J]]: $p_A(A) = 0$, the adjugate proof, and the false one-line proof.
25. [[Minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial|093X6MN6Y6]]: and the characteristic polynomial divides a power of the minimal polynomial.
26. [[Inverse and powers by the Cayley-Hamilton theorem|~1ddc1825]]: $A^{-1}$ and $A^k$ as polynomials in $A$.
