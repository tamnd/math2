## Summary

The Coulomb gas method is a technique for two-dimensional critical [[lattice models|PAEME5EQRM]].
It rewrites the partition function of a model as that of a gas of point charges with logarithmic interactions.
Equivalently, it uses a [[Gaussian free field|EGCDMWYKQB]] with charges.
The Gaussian theory can be solved exactly, so one can read off critical exponents and other universal quantities.
It explains the [[Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition|MN952HPZ50]] of the [[XY model|J53AT590R9]] and gives exact predictions for loop models, Potts models and self-avoiding walks.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In the plane, two electric charges attract or repel with an energy that grows like the logarithm of their distance.
Many two-dimensional models, such as spins that rotate in a plane, have defects like little whirlpools that behave just like such charges.
The method replaces the original model by this gas of charges, whose large-scale behaviour is understood.
For example, in the XY model a whirlpool and an anti-whirlpool are bound in pairs at low temperature and break free at high temperature.
This unbinding is the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless phase transition.
