## Statement

Let $\det$ be the [[determinant|00KDZRW4C0]] of square matrices. It has three properties.

1. If $A$ is a [[triangular matrix|GTG8MJGDKC]], then $\det A$ is the product of the diagonal entries of $A$.
2. Let $P$ be a matrix that comes from the identity matrix by swapping two of its rows. Then $\det P = -1$.
3. For square matrices $A$ and $B$ of the same size, $\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)$.
