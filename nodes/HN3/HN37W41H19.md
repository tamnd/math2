## Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a [[category|A2DE66937D]] with a zero object $0$.
A *Waldhausen category* structure on $\mathcal{C}$ consists of two subcategories of $\mathcal{C}$ that contain all objects.
They are the *cofibrations*, written $A \rightarrowtail B$, and the *weak equivalences*, written $A \xrightarrow{\sim} B$.
They must satisfy the following conditions.

1. Every isomorphism of $\mathcal{C}$ is both a cofibration and a weak equivalence.
2. For every object $A$, the unique map $0 \to A$ is a cofibration.
3. For every cofibration $A \rightarrowtail B$ and every morphism $A \to C$, the pushout $B \cup_A C$ exists in $\mathcal{C}$, and the map $C \to B \cup_A C$ is a cofibration.
4. (Gluing lemma.) Consider a commutative diagram
$$\begin{array}{ccccc} B & \leftarrow & A & \rightarrowtail & C \\ \downarrow & & \downarrow & & \downarrow \\ B' & \leftarrow & A' & \rightarrowtail & C' \end{array}$$
in which the horizontal maps on the right are cofibrations and the vertical maps are weak equivalences.
Then the induced map $B \cup_A C \to B' \cup_{A'} C'$ is a weak equivalence.

## Remarks

For a cofibration $A \rightarrowtail B$, the *quotient* is $B/A = B \cup_A 0$, which exists by clause 3, and $A \rightarrowtail B \twoheadrightarrow B/A$ is called a cofibration sequence.

Example: let $\mathcal{E}$ be a small [[exact category|TFADSC9YPZ]], with the admissible monomorphisms as cofibrations and the isomorphisms as weak equivalences.
Clauses 1 and 2 hold by the axioms of an exact category.
Clause 3 holds because the pushout of an admissible monomorphism along any morphism exists and is an admissible monomorphism.
Clause 4 holds because isomorphisms of diagrams induce isomorphisms of pushouts.
Another example: bounded [[chain complexes|WQ82C0M3QB]] of finitely generated projective modules over a ring, with the degreewise split monomorphisms as cofibrations and the [[quasi-isomorphisms|C9JEC2WQKV]] as weak equivalences.

Non-example: take pointed topological spaces, declare every map a cofibration, and take the [[homotopy equivalences|XZG6MTSWCN]] as weak equivalences.
Clauses 1 to 3 hold, but clause 4 fails.
Compare the diagrams $D^2 \leftarrow S^1 \to \ast$ and $\ast \leftarrow S^1 \to \ast$, linked by the maps $D^2 \to \ast$, $\id_{S^1}$ and $\id_\ast$, which are homotopy equivalences.
The pushouts are $D^2 \cup_{S^1} \ast = S^2$ and $\ast$, and the map $S^2 \to \ast$ is not a homotopy equivalence.

A Waldhausen category is also called a category with cofibrations and weak equivalences.
