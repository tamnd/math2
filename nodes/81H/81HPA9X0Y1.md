## Summary

Cosmic inflation is a hypothesized epoch of accelerated expansion in the very early universe, before the hot Big Bang phase.
During inflation the [[scale factor|J70F32P86W]] grows by a huge factor in a short time, close to the exponential growth of the [[de Sitter universe|55ZQST8RG5]].
Inflation was proposed to solve the [[horizon problem|9SMM2A494H]] and the [[flatness problem|D7XJTSZZEA]].
Quantum fluctuations during inflation give a mechanism for the origin of the density perturbations that grew into galaxies.
It is usually modelled by a scalar field, the inflaton, slowly rolling down a potential.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Inflation is a very brief moment at the start of the universe when space grew enormously fast, doubling in size again and again.
A tiny patch, small enough to have evened out its temperature, was blown up to a size larger than everything we can see.
That explains why the sky looks so uniform, and why space looks flat, just as a small piece of a huge balloon looks flat.
Small quantum jitters were stretched along with space and became the seeds of galaxies.
