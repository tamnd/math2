## Summary

The electromagnetic stress–energy tensor is a symmetric $4 \times 4$ tensor field on spacetime.
It collects the energy density, the energy flux, the momentum density and the momentum flux of the electromagnetic field.
Its components are the field energy density, the [[Poynting vector|P3PK0Q02CZ]] and the [[Maxwell stress tensor|ZWVYST76GE]].
Its conservation law contains both Poynting's theorem and the balance of momentum.
In general relativity it is the source term of the Einstein field equations for an electromagnetic field.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Different observers moving relative to each other disagree about how much energy and how much momentum a light beam carries.
The stress–energy tensor packs energy, energy flow, momentum and momentum flow into one object that all observers agree on.
Each observer reads off the quantities they measure by picking out its entries in their own coordinates.
Because energy curves spacetime, this tensor is also what tells gravity about an electromagnetic field.
