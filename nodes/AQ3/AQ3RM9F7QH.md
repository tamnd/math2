## Summary

The galactic coordinate system is the [[celestial coordinate system|YT0G7RE2GM]] built on the plane of the Milky Way.
Its coordinates are galactic latitude $b$ and galactic longitude $l$, with $l = 0$ in the direction of the centre of the Galaxy.
It is the natural frame for the study of the structure of the Milky Way.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Milky Way is a flat disc of stars, and we live inside it.
From inside, the disc looks like a band of light around the whole sky.
The galactic system takes that band as its equator and starts counting longitude in the direction of the galactic centre, in the constellation Sagittarius.
Stars of the disc have small latitude, while distant galaxies are easiest to see at large latitude, away from the dust of the disc.
