## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the arithmetization of syntax, the diagonal lemma, Gödel's two incompleteness theorems and Rosser's form of the first, Tarski's undefinability theorem, Löb's theorem and the reflection principles.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Gödel numbering|MFJXPAF0R0]]: codes for symbols, formulas and proofs.
2. [[Arithmetization of syntax|~f1d9d517]]: syntactic relations are decidable and representable in $\mathsf{Q}$.
3. [[Provability predicate|NB2BCZR40F]]: the $\Sigma_1$ formula $\mathrm{Prov}_T(x)$.
4. [[Derivability conditions|~abdd9e6a]]: the three conditions D1, D2 and D3 for $\mathrm{Prov}_T$.
5. [[Diagonal lemma|3PRW76471J]]: every formula with one free variable has a fixed point.
6. [[ω-consistency|~08020cc1]]: the hypothesis of Gödel's original first theorem.
7. [[Gödel's first incompleteness theorem|X9WYNYE29C]]: the Gödel sentence is undecided, with three proofs.
8. [[Rosser's theorem|~71fdf6fd]]: incompleteness from consistency alone.
9. [[Negation completeness|319P85G2C1]]: the property that the first theorem refutes.
10. [[Gödel's second incompleteness theorem|~c79a35b5]]: $T$ does not prove $\mathrm{Con}(T)$.
11. [[Consistency statement|~3f19fcb2]]: $\mathrm{Con}(T)$ as a $\Pi_1$ sentence, and its dependence on the axiom formula.
12. [[Tarski's undefinability theorem|YRV6SYK2BY]]: arithmetical truth is not arithmetically definable.
13. [[Partial truth predicates|~2f2c217a]]: truth for $\Sigma_n$ sentences is definable.
14. [[Löb's theorem|3HD30YG3DG]]: $T$ proves $\square \varphi \to \varphi$ only when $T$ proves $\varphi$.
15. [[Henkin sentence|SBMQ5QHG9W]]: a sentence that says it is provable is provable.
16. [[Reflection principles|~506670c9]]: soundness schemes that strengthen $\mathrm{Con}(T)$.
17. [[Turing and Feferman progressions|~103c3d12]]: iterating consistency and reflection along ordinal notations.
18. [[Gödel's speed-up theorem|~005bbed5]]: a stronger theory can shorten proofs without a computable bound.
19. [[Self-verifying theories|~c4c4fa15]]: Willard's weak theories that prove their own tableau consistency.
20. [[Significance of the incompleteness theorems|~68884519]]: what the theorems say and what they do not say.

### Prerequisites

1. [[First-order languages and syntax|PSRDJXRW53]]: terms, formulas, sentences and proofs.
2. [[Gödel's completeness theorem|WQ1ZE255SF]]: provability and truth in all models agree.
3. [[Formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]]: [[Robinson arithmetic|3K1AKGXF8A]] $\mathsf{Q}$ and Peano arithmetic.
4. [[Computably enumerable sets|TN9G24VABN]] and the [[undecidability of the halting problem|Z7TN2B35B0]].
