## Summary

In number theory, two positive [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] *a* and *b* are said to be **multiplicatively independent** if their only common integer power is 1. That is, for integers *n* and *m*, $a^n=b^m$ implies $n=m=0$. Two integers which are not multiplicatively independent are said to be multiplicatively dependent.
