## Summary

The residue of a function at an isolated singularity $c$ is the coefficient of $(z - c)^{-1}$ in its Laurent series, see the [[definition|0JFGKKWWFK]].
It is $\frac{1}{2\pi i}$ times the integral of the function around a small circle around $c$.
