## Summary

The momentum of a particle is its mass times its [[velocity|K160D2Z0CS]], a vector that points in the direction of motion.
In [[classical mechanics|68W1NWECX8]], the net force on a particle equals the rate of change of its momentum.
The total momentum of an isolated system stays constant, which makes momentum one of the basic conserved quantities of mechanics.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Momentum measures how hard it is to stop a moving body.
A truck and a bicycle at the same speed have very different momenta, because the truck is much heavier.
A ball of mass $0.5$ kg moving at $4$ m/s has momentum $2$ kg m/s.
When two bodies collide, the momentum lost by one is gained by the other.
