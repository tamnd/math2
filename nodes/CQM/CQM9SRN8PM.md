## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers modal logics of time, knowledge, belief, obligation, conditionals, agency and justification as mathematical systems.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Temporal logic|8XB117FKKV]]: Prior's tense operators and frame classes.
2. [[Linear temporal logic|RMJ56ZPA63]]: next and until over the natural numbers.
3. [[Computation tree logic|F6JA5C5BJX]]: path quantifiers over branching time.
4. [[CTL*|Y4XZ94GRXV]]: the full branching-time logic.
5. [[Kamp's theorem|CQ0QCQK5JT]]: since and until are expressively complete.
6. [[Interval temporal logics|991B5RR6X4]]: Halpern and Shoham's logic and its undecidability.
7. [[Epistemic logic|EA34W4ERRE]]: knowledge as a box, and the introspection axioms.
8. [[Common knowledge|7BCMKF2P7X]]: iterated mutual knowledge as a fixed point.
9. [[Muddy children puzzle|BG9TYCJ7HP]]: the puzzle solved by induction.
10. [[Doxastic logic|RTP4HTZYK3]]: belief as KD45, and Moore's paradox.
11. [[Dynamic epistemic logic|9465T2PWZT]]: announcements, reduction axioms and action models.
12. [[Deontic logic|7B74J6J5JV]]: obligation as the logic KD, and its paradoxes.
13. [[Conditional logic|ZGYG7WN7EC]]: counterfactuals in sphere semantics.
14. [[STIT logic|WYY2JXWMYY]]: agency as choice in branching time.
15. [[Justification logic|5HKWCXEFG1]]: explicit proofs and the realization theorem.
16. [[AGM belief revision|Y05YQN7HGQ]]: the postulates and Grove's spheres.
17. [[Gärdenfors's triviality theorem|WMZ1QP7KQY]]: the Ramsey test against preservation.
18. [[Fitch's paradox of knowability|HVCW7QYXSW]]: knowable truths are known truths.
19. [[Inquisitive logic|EX0ND8DCTN]]: questions in support semantics.
20. [[Description logic|TXQ80B8ZSA]]: ALC as multimodal K.
21. [[Axiological logic|4BRXSBXXWG]]: good, bad and better.
