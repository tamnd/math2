## Summary

A harmonic function is a twice continuously differentiable function on an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ whose [[Laplacian|0E0B6RA8K0]] is zero at each point.
Harmonic functions describe steady states, such as equilibrium temperatures and electrostatic potentials in empty space.
