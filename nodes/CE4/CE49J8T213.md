## Summary

Shamir's secret sharing is the threshold [[secret sharing|RF8K6DV805]] scheme in which the shares are values of a random polynomial whose constant term is the secret.
Any $t$ shares determine the polynomial of degree less than $t$ by [[Lagrange interpolation|237KR6FRCK]], and so the secret.
Fewer than $t$ shares are consistent with every possible secret, with equal probability.
Adi Shamir published it in 1979.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Two points determine a line.
Put the secret as the height where a random line crosses the vertical axis, and give each person one other point of the line.
Any two people can draw the line and read off the secret.
One person alone has one point, and lines through it cross the axis at every height equally often, so the point tells nothing.
With polynomials of higher degree the same idea gives any threshold.
