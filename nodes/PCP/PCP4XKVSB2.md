## Summary

In algebra, a **Cohen ring** is a field or a complete discrete valuation ring of mixed characteristic $(0, p)$ whose [[maximal ideal|09J6KGT65G]] is generated by *p*. Cohen rings are used in the Cohen structure theorem for complete [[Noetherian|0670T59HGE]] local rings.
