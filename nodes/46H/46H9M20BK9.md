## Definition

Let $(M, g)$ be a [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]] of dimension $D$, with [[scalar curvature|ZHX2VYQDZ3]] $R$, [[Ricci tensor|4GTNM0WHDH]] $R_{ab}$ and [[Riemann tensor|JA29ETCM02]] $R_{abcd}$.
The *Gauss–Bonnet term* is
$$\mathcal{G} = R^2 - 4 R_{ab}R^{ab} + R_{abcd}R^{abcd}.$$
Let $\kappa > 0$, $\Lambda$ and $\alpha$ be real constants.
*Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet gravity* is the [[Lovelock theory|AS38SRETPQ]] with action
$$S = \frac{1}{2\kappa} \int \left( R - 2\Lambda + \alpha \, \mathcal{G} \right) \sqrt{-g}\, d^D x + S_m,$$
where $S_m$ is the action of matter.

## Remarks

The field equations are $G_{ab} + \Lambda g_{ab} + \alpha H_{ab} = \kappa T_{ab}$, where $G_{ab}$ is the Einstein tensor and
$$H_{ab} = 2\left(R R_{ab} - 2R_{ac}R^c{}_b - 2R_{acbd}R^{cd} + R_{acde}R_b{}^{cde}\right) - \frac12 g_{ab}\,\mathcal{G}.$$
Example: for $D \ge 5$ and $\alpha \ne 0$ the tensor $H_{ab}$ is not identically zero, and the theory differs from general relativity.
Non-example: the action with $\alpha R^2$ in place of $\alpha\,\mathcal{G}$ is not of this form, and its field equations are of fourth order.
For $D = 4$, $H_{ab}$ vanishes identically and the theory is general relativity; on a compact oriented Riemannian $4$-manifold the [[Chern–Gauss–Bonnet theorem|GG6NYPKMHX]] gives $\int \mathcal{G}\, dV = 32\pi^2 \chi(M)$, where $\chi$ is the [[Euler characteristic|RNDDWDAFJ5]].
Boulware and Deser found its static spherically symmetric black hole solutions in 1985.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet gravity adds one carefully chosen square of the curvature to Einstein's action.
The square is chosen so that the equations still have only second derivatives, and it only matters in more than four dimensions.
