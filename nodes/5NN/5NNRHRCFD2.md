## Summary

the symbol in number theory

In number theory, the **Kronecker symbol**, written as $\left(\frac an\right)$ or $(a|n)$, is a generalization of the [[Jacobi symbol|0ADTQBXRMC]] to all [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] $n$. It was introduced by (txt, page 770).
