## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|G7N6BDTEZ2]].
It covers the homological invariants of noncommutative rings: global and weak dimension, hereditary rings, Koszul algebras and Koszul duality, Gorenstein and Calabi-Yau algebras, and the homological conjectures for finite-dimensional algebras.
