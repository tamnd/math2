## Summary

The flux of a vector field through an [[oriented surface|0TD904MG06]] measures how much of the field passes through the surface. It counts flow in the direction of the chosen normal.
It is the [[surface integral|065HE5V98E]] of the normal component of the field. For a velocity field of a fluid, it is the volume of fluid that crosses the surface per unit time.
