## Summary

In mathematics, the **Koszul cohomology** groups $K_{p,q}(X,L)$ are groups associated to a projective variety *X* with a line bundle *L*. They were introduced by (txt), and named after Jean-Louis Koszul as they are closely related to the Koszul complex.
