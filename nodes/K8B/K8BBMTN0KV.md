## Summary

Plasticity is the ability of a solid to undergo permanent deformation once the stress exceeds a threshold, the yield stress.
Below the threshold the material behaves elastically and returns to its shape when unloaded; beyond it, part of the deformation remains.
Mathematical models split the strain into an elastic and a plastic part.
The stresses must stay in a set bounded by a [[yield surface|CWWCEXZ8BH]], and a rule says how the plastic strain grows.
It is central to metal forming, structural safety and soil mechanics.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Bend a paper clip slightly and it springs back; bend it further and it stays bent.
The first response is elastic, the second is plastic.
The stress at which the change happens is the yield stress, and the bend that remains is the plastic deformation.
A steel bar pulled past its yield stress and released is a little longer than before.
