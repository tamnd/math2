## Summary

In number theory, a **$k$-hyperperfect number** is a [[natural number|0MWKJ44V5A]] $n$ for which the equality $n = 1+k(\sigma(n)-n-1)$ holds, where $\sigma(n)$ is the [[divisor function|04J4Z73J5B]] (i.e., the sum of all positive [[divisors|0PG2H7WKGG]] of $n$). A **hyperperfect number** is a $k$-hyperperfect number for some integer $k$. Hyperperfect numbers generalize [[perfect numbers|0MXGYCH4SY]], which are 1-hyperperfect.
