## Definition

Let $AP$ be a set of atomic propositions.
The formulas of *linear temporal logic* over $AP$ are given by the grammar
$$\varphi ::= p \mid \neg \varphi \mid \varphi \land \varphi \mid X \varphi \mid \varphi \, U \, \varphi, \qquad p \in AP.$$
A formula is interpreted on an infinite word $w = w_0 w_1 w_2 \cdots$ with each $w_i \subseteq AP$.
For $i \ge 0$, let $w^{i} = w_i w_{i+1} \cdots$ be the suffix of $w$ from position $i$.
The relation $w \models \varphi$ is defined by induction on $\varphi$:

1. $w \models p$ if and only if $p \in w_0$;
2. $w \models \neg\varphi$ if and only if $w \not\models \varphi$, and $w \models \varphi \land \psi$ if and only if $w \models \varphi$ and $w \models \psi$;
3. $w \models X\varphi$ if and only if $w^{1} \models \varphi$;
4. $w \models \varphi \, U \, \psi$ if and only if there is $j \ge 0$ with $w^{j} \models \psi$ and $w^{i} \models \varphi$ for all $0 \le i < j$.

Let $s$ be a state of a [[Kripke structure|NGHWZ9Y7ZV]] with a total transition relation.
Then $s$ satisfies $\varphi$ if the word of labels of every infinite path from $s$ satisfies $\varphi$.

## Remarks

The usual abbreviations are $F\varphi = \top \, U \, \varphi$ ("eventually") and $G\varphi = \neg F \neg \varphi$ ("always"); the other connectives of [[propositional logic|09K9MWD2QQ]] are defined as usual.

Example: let $AP = \set{p}$ and $w = \emptyset \, \emptyset \, \set{p} \, \set{p} \, \set{p} \cdots$.
Then $w \models F G p$, because $w^{2} \models G p$: every suffix of $w^{2}$ starts with a letter containing $p$.

Non-example: the word $w' = \set{p} \, \emptyset \, \set{p} \, \emptyset \cdots$ does not satisfy $F G p$.
Every suffix of $w'$ has a later position without $p$, so no suffix satisfies $G p$.
