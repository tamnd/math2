## Summary

In logic and computer science, the **Davis–Putnam–Logemann–Loveland** (**DPLL**) **algorithm** is a complete, backtracking-based search algorithm for deciding the satisfiability of [[propositional logic formulae|09K9MWD2QQ]] in [[conjunctive normal form|07C4A8Y18K]], i.e. for solving the CNF-SAT problem.
