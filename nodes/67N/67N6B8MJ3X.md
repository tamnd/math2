## Summary

The four-momentum of a particle is the [[four-vector|S8RKKKMSTG]] that combines its energy and its momentum.
For a particle of mass $m$ it is $p = m u$, where $u$ is the [[four-velocity|Y59V040THG]].
In an inertial frame its time component is $E/c$ and its space part is the relativistic momentum $\vec p$.
The total four-momentum of an isolated system is conserved, which unites the conservation of energy and of momentum in one law.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In relativity energy and momentum are not separate things; a change of inertial frame mixes them, as it mixes time and space.
The four-momentum puts them into one arrow in spacetime.
Its length does not depend on the observer and gives the mass of the particle.
For a particle at rest, the arrow points straight along the time direction and has time component $mc$, so its energy is $mc^2$.
