## Summary

This topic is a part of [[philosophy of mathematics|NTJ5XW0PVD]].
It covers the philosophy of logic as it concerns mathematics, with logical consequence, logical pluralism, the status of second-order logic, conventionalism, and the philosophical readings of incompleteness and of the Church-Turing thesis.
