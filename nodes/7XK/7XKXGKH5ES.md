## Statement

Consider the [[ordered pairs|0JQWWFCS9R]] of [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], used in the [[construction of the integers|0NS58JP68E]].
Write $(a, b) \cong (c, d)$ when $a + d = b + c$.
Then this relation is symmetric.
That is, if $(a, b) \cong (c, d)$, then $(c, d) \cong (a, b)$.

## Remarks

This is one of three parts of the proof that $\cong$ is an [[equivalence relation|0SGTMBEWRF]]. The other two parts are reflexivity and transitivity.
