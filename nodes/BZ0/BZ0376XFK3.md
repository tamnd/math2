## Summary

In mathematics, a **Sheffer sequence** or **poweroid** is a polynomial sequence, i.e., a [[sequence|0G9EKM0E15]] $( p_{n}(x) : n = 0, 1, 2, 3, ... )$ of polynomials in which the index of each polynomial equals its [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]], satisfying conditions related to the umbral calculus in [[combinatorics|0JKW7W7XFR]]. They are named for Isador M. Sheffer.
