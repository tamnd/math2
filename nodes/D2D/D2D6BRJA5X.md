## Summary

The energy release rate $G$ is the decrease of the potential energy of a cracked body per unit area of crack growth.
It is the energy that is available to break the material at the crack front.
In linear elastic [[fracture mechanics|SNRXGRZWR5]] it is determined by the [[stress intensity factors|8D619PN75J]] through Irwin's relation.
Griffith's criterion compares it with the energy needed to create new crack surfaces.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A loaded body stores elastic energy, like a stretched spring.
When the crack inside it grows a little, the body becomes more flexible and gives up some of that energy.
The energy given up per unit of new crack area is the energy release rate.
If it is larger than what the material needs to break its bonds, the crack keeps growing.
