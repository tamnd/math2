## Summary

Permittivity is the constant of proportion between the [[electric displacement field|NJVXQZ780B]] and the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] in a linear medium.
It measures how strongly a material polarizes in response to an electric field.
The vacuum permittivity $\varepsilon_0$ is the constant in Coulomb's law, and the relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r = \varepsilon / \varepsilon_0$ is a dimensionless property of a material.
In anisotropic materials it is a tensor, symmetric for lossless media, and for time-varying fields it depends on the frequency.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put two charges inside water instead of air, and the force between them becomes about $80$ times weaker.
The water molecules turn to partly shield each charge.
Permittivity measures this shielding: a larger permittivity means a stronger shield.
Air is close to vacuum, with relative permittivity about $1$.
Placing a material of high permittivity between capacitor plates raises the capacitance by the same factor.
