## Summary

The stress–energy tensor is a symmetric [[tensor field|WX302T93TW]] of rank two on spacetime.
It describes the density and the flow of energy and momentum of matter and fields.
For an observer it gives the energy density, the momentum density and the stresses, such as pressure and shear, that this observer measures.
It is the source term of the [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]], so it tells spacetime how to curve.
Local conservation of energy and momentum is the statement that its divergence vanishes.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Matter has energy, it moves, and it pushes on its surroundings.
The stress–energy tensor stores all of this at each point in a $4 \times 4$ symmetric table.
The top-left entry is the energy density, the rest of the first row is the flow of energy, and the lower $3 \times 3$ block holds the pressures and stresses.
For dust at rest, a cloud of particles that do not touch, only the top-left entry, the energy density, is nonzero.
