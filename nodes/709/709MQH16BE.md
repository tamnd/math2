## Statement

In graph theory, the **Kelmans–Seymour conjecture** states that every 5-vertex-connected graph that is not [[planar|029JQEK15B]] contains a subdivision of the 5-vertex [[complete graph|03WKB2H474]] $K_{5}$. It is named for Paul Seymour and Alexander Kelmans, who independently described the conjecture; Seymour in 1977 and Kelmans in 1979. A proof was announced in 2016, and published in four papers in 2020.
