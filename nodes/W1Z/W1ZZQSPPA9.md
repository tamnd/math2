## Summary

In mathematics, an **Igusa curve** is (roughly) a coarse moduli space of elliptic curves in characteristic *p* with a level *p* **Igusa structure**, where an Igusa structure on an elliptic curve *E* is roughly a point of order *p* of *E*<sup>(*p*)</sup> generating the kernel of *V* : *E*<sup>(*p*)</sup> → *E*. An **Igusa variety** is a higher-dimensional analogue of an Igusa curve. Igusa curves were studied by Igusa and Igusa varieties were introduced by Harris and Taylor for studying the bad reduction of some Shimura varieties: the $\ell$-adic cohomology of Igusa varieties sheds some light on that of Shimura varieties.
