## Summary

This topic is a part of [[partial differential equations|2D684V8321]].
It covers nonlinear elliptic equations in divergence form and with nonlinear lower-order terms, with existence by sub- and supersolutions, degree and variational methods, the p-Laplacian, the prescribed mean curvature equation and the Ginzburg-Landau equation.
