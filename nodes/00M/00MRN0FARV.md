## Summary

A family of random variables is independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) when the variables are mutually independent and all have the same distribution.
It is the standard model of repeated trials of one experiment. The node [[i.i.d. random variables|0AJN7WHBJZ]] gives the precise definition.
