## Summary

A prime ideal of a commutative ring is a proper ideal with the following property. If it contains a product, then it contains one of the factors.
The prime ideals of $\mathbb{Z}$ are the ideals $p\mathbb{Z}$ for $p$ prime and the zero ideal.
The [[definition of a prime ideal|06HC6HK0Z8]] gives the conditions.
