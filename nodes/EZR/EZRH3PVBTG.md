## Summary

This topic is a part of [[fluid mechanics|B8CZ8SX1G5]].
It covers the dynamics of rotating fluids and of density-stratified fluids without planetary geometry, with the Taylor-Proudman theorem, Ekman layers, inertial waves, buoyancy oscillations, and the stability of stratified shear flows.
