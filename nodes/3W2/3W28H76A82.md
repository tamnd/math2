## Summary

In thermodynamics, conjugate variables are pairs made of an intensive and an extensive variable whose product has the dimension of energy.
The main pairs are temperature and entropy, pressure and volume, and chemical potential and number of particles.
Up to sign, each intensive variable of a pair is a partial derivative of the internal energy with respect to its extensive partner.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Energy can enter a system in several ways, and each way comes with a pair of quantities.
To push a piston, the pressure acts like a force and the change of volume like a distance; their product is the work.
For heat, temperature plays the role of the force and entropy the role of the distance.
The intensive member of the pair says how hard the system pushes; the extensive member says how much of something changes.
