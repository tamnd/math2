## Summary

Electromagnetic radiation is the part of the electromagnetic field that travels away from its sources and carries energy and momentum to arbitrarily large distances.
It consists of waves of the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] and the [[magnetic field|XRED3C547P]] that solve the [[electromagnetic wave equation|FSDEQM9SCV]].
In vacuum these waves move at the speed of light.
It is produced by accelerated charges and changing currents, and it includes radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays.
Mathematically it is the part of the field that decays like $1/r$.
Its energy flux through a large sphere can therefore stay nonzero as the sphere grows.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Light is a wave of electric and magnetic fields that carries energy through empty space.
Wiggle a charge and ripples spread out from it at the speed of light, like ripples on a pond.
Close to the charge the field is dominated by its ordinary Coulomb pull, but far away only the ripples remain.
The colour of light, or the type of radiation, is set by how fast the fields oscillate.
