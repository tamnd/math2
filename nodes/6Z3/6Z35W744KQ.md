## Summary

A Lie-admissible algebra is a [[nonassociative algebra|60W3THKGA7]] whose commutator $xy - yx$ is a Lie bracket.
Thus every Lie-admissible algebra $A$ gives a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] $A^-$ on the same vector space.
Associative algebras and pre-Lie algebras are Lie-admissible.
The notion singles out those algebras whose commutator is a Lie bracket, as it is for an associative algebra.

## Explanations

### L1 human

From any associative algebra, such as the square matrices, one gets a Lie algebra by taking the commutator $xy - yx$ as the new product.
For a nonassociative algebra, the commutator is still antisymmetric, but it may fail the Jacobi identity.
Lie-admissible algebras are those for which it does not fail.
They form a large class that contains associative algebras as a special case.
