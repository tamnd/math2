## Summary

**STIT logic** (from *seeing to it that*) is a family of modal and branching-time logics for reasoning about agency and choice. A typical STIT operator has the form $[i\ \mathsf{stit}:\varphi]$, usually read as "agent $i$ sees to it that $\varphi$", and is interpreted in models where agents choose between alternative possible futures.
