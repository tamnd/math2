## Summary

A supersonic flow is a [[compressible flow|FJC2GMVARF]] in which the fluid moves faster than the local [[speed of sound|SN4J6KP03C]].
So its [[Mach number|GFHJ858AN1]] exceeds $1$.
Small disturbances cannot travel upstream in such a flow: they stay inside a cone downstream of their source.
Its steady equations are hyperbolic, so they are solved by marching downstream, for instance with the method of characteristics.
Shock waves and expansion fans are its typical features, and it governs the design of supersonic aircraft, rockets and wind tunnels.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A boat that moves faster than its own waves leaves a V-shaped wake behind it.
A body moving faster than sound in air does the same in three dimensions: the sound it makes is left behind inside a cone.
Nothing ahead of the body "hears" it coming, so the air must change direction suddenly when the body arrives.
For example, at Mach $2$ the cone has a half-angle of $30$ degrees.
