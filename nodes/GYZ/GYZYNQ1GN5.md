## Summary

A food web is the network of feeding relations among the species of an ecological community.
Mathematically it is a [[directed graph|0KFYGSHHF5]] whose vertices are species and whose arcs go from each food species to each species that eats it.
Its structure is related to the stability and the dynamics of the community.
Examples of structural features are the number of links per species and the lengths of food chains.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Grass is eaten by rabbits and by deer, rabbits are eaten by foxes and owls, and deer are eaten by wolves.
Draw a dot for each species and an arrow from each food to its eater: the picture is a food web.
Plants are at the bottom, with no arrows coming in, and top predators are at the top, with no arrows going out.
A food chain is a path through the web, such as grass, rabbit, fox.
