## Summary

A baric algebra is a possibly [[nonassociative algebra|60W3THKGA7]] over a field together with a nonzero algebra homomorphism to the field, called its weight.
Baric algebras are the common setting of the algebras of mathematical genetics, such as [[genetic algebras|S9Z7YX9D9K]] and [[Bernstein algebras|S8GFADSZ7M]].
In these models the weight of a population vector is its total frequency, so the vectors of weight $1$ are the populations.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Describe a population by the frequencies of a few types of genes, and let the product of two vectors describe the offspring of a random mating.
The sum of all frequencies is the weight of the vector.
Offspring frequencies of two populations again add up to the product of the totals, so the weight respects the product.
For example, with two alleles $A$ and $a$, the weight of $pA + qa$ is $p + q$, and a population has $p + q = 1$.
