## Summary

The Jacobi metric of a mechanical system with fixed total energy $E$ is a Riemannian metric on part of the configuration space.
This part is the region where the potential energy is less than $E$.
It is the kinetic-energy metric multiplied by twice the difference between $E$ and the potential.
By [[Maupertuis's principle|ZXY0AYKMXZ]], the paths of the motions of energy $E$ are exactly its [[geodesics|NSYMQMHPDN]].
So it turns questions about dynamics into questions of Riemannian geometry.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A ball rolling in a landscape of hills and valleys, with fixed total energy, follows curved paths.
The Jacobi metric is a way of measuring lengths in the plane so that these curved paths become the shortest paths.
Places where the ball would be fast, deep in the valleys, count as long; places where it would be slow count as short.
For a ball thrown in uniform gravity, the short pieces of shortest paths for this new length are pieces of the familiar parabolas.
