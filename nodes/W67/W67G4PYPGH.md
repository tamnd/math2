## Summary

GENERIC, the "general equation for the non-equilibrium reversible-irreversible coupling", is a framework for the equations of [[non-equilibrium thermodynamics|WC6CK8PXPX]] proposed by Grmela and Öttinger.
It writes the time evolution as the sum of a reversible part and an irreversible part.
The energy generates the reversible part through a [[Poisson bracket|2S0MXDT4YH]].
The entropy generates the irreversible part through a symmetric dissipative bracket.
Two degeneracy conditions make the energy conserved and the entropy non-decreasing.
Many models fit this form, including the Navier–Stokes–Fourier equations and models of complex fluids.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A closed physical system has two kinds of motion: reversible motion, as in a frictionless pendulum, and irreversible motion, as in friction.
GENERIC gives a single recipe for both.
The energy drives the reversible part, the entropy drives the irreversible part, and the two are kept apart so that energy is conserved and entropy never decreases.
A pendulum with friction that heats its surroundings is the simplest example.
