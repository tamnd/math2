## Summary

The vibration of plates is the study of the time-dependent transverse motion of thin elastic plates.
In [[Kirchhoff–Love plate theory|9E6E0SCEYA]] the motion obeys a fourth-order equation in space and second order in time.
Free vibrations split into normal modes, each with a natural frequency and a mode shape, found from an eigenvalue problem for the biharmonic operator.
The lines where a mode shape vanishes are the nodal lines, made visible by [[Chladni figures|TK7T133JQ4]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Strike a metal tray and it rings with a few clear tones.
Each tone belongs to a pattern of motion in which parts of the tray move up and down while some lines on it stay still.
Higher tones have more of these still lines, dividing the plate into more pieces that move in opposite directions.
The pitch of each tone depends on the stiffness, the mass, the shape of the plate and how its edges are held.
