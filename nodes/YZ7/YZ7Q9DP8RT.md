## Summary

The electric field is a [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] on space.
At each point it gives the force per unit charge on a small test charge at rest there.
It lets one describe the effect of a charge distribution without naming the charge it acts on.
In electrostatics it is computed from [[Coulomb's law|4RM18X9CXG]] by superposition.
Together with the magnetic field it is one of the two unknowns of [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture each point of space carrying a small arrow.
The arrow shows the direction in which a positive charge put there would be pushed, and its length shows how hard.
Around a single positive charge the arrows point straight outward and get shorter with distance.
Around a negative charge they point inward.
Field lines drawn along these arrows start on positive charges and end on negative charges.
