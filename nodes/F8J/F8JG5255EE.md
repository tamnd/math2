## Summary

The equation of time is apparent solar time minus mean solar time.
Apparent time is read from the true Sun, and mean time from a fictitious Sun moving uniformly.
It varies over the year between about $-14$ and $+16$ minutes.
It comes from the eccentricity of the Earth's orbit and from the tilt of the Earth's axis to the orbit.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A sundial and a good clock do not agree, and the gap changes during the year.
In early November the sundial is about a quarter of an hour ahead of the clock, and in mid-February it is about a quarter of an hour behind.
The reason is that the Sun does not move across the sky at a perfectly steady pace.
The Earth moves faster in its orbit near January than near July, and the tilted axis adds a second wobble.
