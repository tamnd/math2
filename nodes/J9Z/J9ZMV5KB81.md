## Summary

In algebra and network theory, a **Wang algebra** is a commutative algebra $A$, over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] or (more generally) a [[commutative unital ring|0310REJJY8]], in which $A$ has two additional properties: (Rule i) For all elements *x* of $A$, *x* + *x* = 0 (universal additive [[nilpotency|0N9R5NN6TW]] of degree 1). (Rule ii) For all elements *x* of $A$, *x***⋅***x* = 0 (universal multiplicative nilpotency of degree 1).
