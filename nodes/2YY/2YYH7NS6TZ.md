## Summary

In mathematics, a **Redheffer matrix**, often denoted $A_n$ as studied by (Redheffer 1977), is a [[square|0CP7YY6NAG]] (0,1) matrix whose entries *a*<sub>*ij*</sub> are 1 if *i* divides *j* or if *j* = 1; otherwise, *a*<sub>*ij*</sub> = 0. It is useful in some contexts to express [[Dirichlet convolution|037F589D1D]], or convolved divisors sums, in terms of matrix products involving the [[transpose|09DXWYRD43]] of the $n^{th}$ Redheffer matrix.
