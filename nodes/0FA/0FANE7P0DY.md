## Summary

The Möbius function $\mu$ is an arithmetic function that is $0$ on integers with a square factor greater than $1$.
On a squarefree integer, its value $1$ or $-1$ depends on the parity of the number of prime factors.
The [[definition of the Möbius function|0C8QENHYZW]] gives the precise form.
