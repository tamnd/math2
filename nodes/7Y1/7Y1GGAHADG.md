## Summary

A quadratic Jordan algebra is a version of a [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]] built on the quadratic operators $U_x$ instead of the bilinear product.
In an associative algebra, $U_x y = xyx$.
The notion, due to Kevin McCrimmon, works over any commutative ring, including rings in which $2$ is not invertible.
When $\frac{1}{2}$ is available it is equivalent to the usual notion of a unital Jordan algebra.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The usual Jordan product $\frac{1}{2}(xy + yx)$ needs division by $2$.
In characteristic $2$ that is impossible, and the theory breaks down.
The operation $y \mapsto xyx$ makes sense in every associative algebra without any division.
A quadratic Jordan algebra takes this operation as the basic one, and keeps the rules that it obeys in associative algebras.
