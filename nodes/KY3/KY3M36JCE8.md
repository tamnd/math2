## Summary

A Satake diagram is the Dynkin diagram of a complex semisimple Lie algebra decorated to describe one of its real forms.
Some vertices are painted black, and some pairs of white vertices are joined by double-headed arrows.
Satake diagrams classify the real semisimple Lie algebras up to isomorphism, and they also show the restricted root system of the real form.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A complex Lie algebra can have several real versions, its real forms.
A Satake diagram tells them apart by colouring the dots of the Dynkin diagram and by drawing arrows between some of them.
Black dots mark directions in which the real form behaves like a compact group of rotations.
The compact real form has all dots black, and the split real form, such as $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\R)$, has all dots white and no arrows.
