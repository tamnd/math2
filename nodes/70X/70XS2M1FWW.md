## Summary

In graph theory, a branch of mathematics, two graphs *G* and *H* are called **homomorphically equivalent** if there exists a [[graph homomorphism|052JJ9YVNM]] $f\colon G \to H$ and a graph homomorphism $g\colon H \to G$. An example usage of this notion is that any two cores of a graph are homomorphically equivalent.
