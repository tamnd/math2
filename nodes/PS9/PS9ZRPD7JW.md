## Definition

A process of a closed thermodynamic system is *adiabatic* if the system exchanges no [[heat|CY4GJQGF06]] with its surroundings during the process.
So $Q = 0$ for the whole process and for each of its parts.

## Remarks

Then the change of [[internal energy|F6JVQ12X43]] equals the work done on the system: $\Delta U = W$.
For a reversible adiabatic process of an [[ideal gas|N7R0H2VNDJ]] with constant heat capacities, $p V^\gamma$ is constant.
Here $\gamma = C_p / C_V$ is the ratio of the heat capacities at constant pressure and constant volume.
The free expansion of a gas in an insulated container is adiabatic, but it is not reversible.
The isothermal expansion of an ideal gas is not adiabatic: the gas absorbs heat equal to the work it does.
