## Summary

In mathematics, a multivariate polynomial defined over the [[rational numbers|0NCJ5WXN6Z]] is **absolutely irreducible** if it is [[irreducible|00X2A9G9W3]] over the complex field. For example, $x^2+y^2-1$ is absolutely irreducible, but while $x^2+y^2$ is irreducible over the integers and the reals, it is reducible over the [[complex numbers|09FTAET5Z4]] as $x^2+y^2 = (x+iy)(x-iy),$ and thus not absolutely irreducible.
