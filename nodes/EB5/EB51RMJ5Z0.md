## Summary

Gravitational radiation reaction is the effect on a source of the [[gravitational waves|SJ0A4TZTFR]] it emits.
The waves carry away energy and angular momentum, and the source loses them.
For a binary system the orbit shrinks and the two bodies spiral together.
In the [[post-Newtonian expansion|7HC8RM1EH9]] it first appears at order $(v/c)^5$ beyond Newtonian gravity, as a dissipative force consistent with the [[quadrupole formula|F541Z4R4ZX]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A boat moving through water makes waves, and making them slows the boat down.
In the same way, two stars orbiting each other make gravitational waves, and pay for them with orbital energy.
They slowly fall closer, orbit faster, and radiate even more.
This runaway ends in a collision, which is what detectors such as LIGO observe when two black holes merge.
