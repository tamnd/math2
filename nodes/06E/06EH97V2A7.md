## Summary

The Fréchet derivative of a map $f$ between normed vector spaces at a point $x$ is a bounded linear map. It best approximates $f(x + h) - f(x)$ for small $h$.
It extends the derivative of one-variable calculus to maps between normed spaces, which can have infinite dimension.
For maps between Euclidean spaces it is the [[total derivative|0FNKFT8H6E]].
