## Statement

Let $m$ and $n$ be any [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], where $0$ counts as a natural number.
Then, with the [[addition of natural numbers|0W5DSDFQ4G]], adding $1$ to the second summand gives the same sum as adding $1$ to the first summand:

$$m + (n + 1) = (m + 1) + n.$$

## Remarks

The statement is a step toward the commutativity of addition. It shows that a one can be moved from one summand to the other without changing the sum.
