## Summary

In mathematics, and more specifically in combinatorial commutative algebra, a **zero-divisor graph** is an undirected graph representing the [[zero divisors|0K26YKNBSA]] of a [[commutative ring|0310REJJY8]]. It has elements of the [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] as its vertices, and pairs of elements whose product is zero as its edges.
