## Summary

Cooperative game theory is the part of [[game theory|J8A9GBFSTY]] that studies what groups of players, called coalitions, can achieve together.
Its basic model assigns to each coalition the total payoff its members can secure by cooperating.
The main question is how the payoff of the whole group should be shared among the players.
Solution concepts such as the core, the Shapley value and the nucleolus give different answers to this question.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of several firms that can save money by sharing a pipeline, a road or a computer.
Each group of firms can save a certain amount on its own, and all of them together save the most.
Cooperative game theory asks for a fair or stable way to split the total savings.
For example, if two people together earn $10$ and each alone earns nothing, an equal split gives $5$ to each.
Other splits are possible, and the theory compares them through rules that a good split should satisfy.
