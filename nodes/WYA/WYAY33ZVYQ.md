## Summary

An isobaric process is a process at constant pressure.
The work done on a fluid is then $-p \, \Delta V$, and the heat absorbed equals the change of enthalpy.
Many processes in chemistry and daily life, such as boiling water in an open pot, happen at the constant pressure of the atmosphere.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Heat the air in a cylinder closed by a freely sliding, weighted piston.
The weight keeps the pressure fixed, so the air pushes the piston up as it warms.
Part of the heat raises the temperature of the air, and part of it is spent lifting the piston.
In the pressure-volume plane, the process is a horizontal line.
