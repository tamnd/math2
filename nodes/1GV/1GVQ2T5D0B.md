## Summary

Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory is a time-symmetric formulation of the classical electrodynamics of point charges, proposed by John Wheeler and Richard Feynman.
Each charge acts on the others through half the sum of its retarded and advanced fields, and no charge acts on itself.
If the universe absorbs all radiation, the theory gives the same equations of motion as ordinary retarded electrodynamics, including the radiation reaction force.
It thus explains radiation reaction without self-fields and shows how the arrow of radiation can come from boundary conditions rather than from the laws.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Maxwell's equations allow waves that go forward in time and waves that go backward in time, but we only ever see the forward ones.
Wheeler and Feynman supposed that each charge sends out both kinds equally.
When a charge shakes, the far-away matter that absorbs its light answers with backward waves that arrive exactly when the charge shakes.
The sum of all these answers looks like ordinary forward radiation plus a braking force on the shaking charge, which is the observed radiation reaction.
