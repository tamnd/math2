## Summary

The adjacency matrix of a finite graph records adjacency as a matrix of zeros and ones. It lets linear algebra study graphs, for example through eigenvalues and powers that count walks. The node [[adjacency matrix|0NZ8MDBN6Q]] gives the precise definition.
