## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **friendship graph** (or **Dutch windmill graph** or **$n$-fan**) $F_{n}$ is a [[planar|029JQEK15B]], undirected graph with $2n + 1$ vertices and $3n$ edges.
