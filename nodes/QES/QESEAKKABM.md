## Summary

The Plancherel measure of a second countable unimodular [[locally compact group|AHV05ZBCYW]] of type I is a measure on its [[unitary dual|95Q8WZMN0W]].
It tells how the regular representation on $L^2(G)$ is built from irreducible representations.
With it, the $L^2$ norm of a function equals an integral of the Hilbert–Schmidt norms of its [[Fourier transform|N8E0GVVWG1]].
Its support is the reduced dual, the set of irreducible representations weakly contained in the regular representation on $L^2(G)$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

For the real line, $L^2(\R)$ is built from all the waves $e^{2 \pi i \xi x}$, and the Plancherel measure is the ordinary length measure $d\xi$ on frequencies.
For a compact group, $L^2(G)$ is a direct sum, and the Plancherel measure gives each irreducible representation $\pi$ the weight $\dim \pi$.
For larger groups such as $\mathrm{SL}(2, \R)$ the measure has a discrete part and a continuous part.
In every case it is the weight that makes "energy of $f$ equals the total energy of its frequency parts" true.
