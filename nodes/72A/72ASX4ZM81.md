## Summary

Eta conversion identifies a term $M$ with the abstraction $\lambda x.\, M\, x$, when $x$ does not occur free in $M$.
It expresses extensionality in the [[lambda calculus|BD756KR8FM]]: two functions that give the same result on every argument are equal.
Added to [[beta reduction|Y9B9CVY6SJ]], it gives the $\beta\eta$-calculus, whose convertibility is the extensional equality of terms.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The function "take $x$ and return $f(x)$" is just the function $f$ itself.
Eta conversion is the rule that says so: $\lambda x.\, f\, x$ may be replaced by $f$.
The condition that $x$ does not appear inside $f$ is needed.
For example, $\lambda x.\, g\, x\, x$ is not the same as $g\, x$, since there the $x$ inside would change meaning.
In programming this rule removes needless wrapper functions.
