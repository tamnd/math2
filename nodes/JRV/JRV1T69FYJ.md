## Summary

A vacuum solution is a spacetime that satisfies the [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]] with no matter and no cosmological constant.
In four dimensions this means that the [[Ricci curvature|4GTNM0WHDH]] of the metric vanishes, so the only curvature left is the [[Weyl tensor|SBHY31HESP]].
Vacuum solutions describe the gravitational field outside stars and black holes and gravitational waves in empty space.
Minkowski space, the Schwarzschild metric and the Kerr metric are vacuum solutions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Gravity does not stop where matter stops: outside the Sun, space is empty, but it is still curved.
A vacuum solution describes such a region, where there is no matter at all and the curvature is due only to distant sources or to waves.
In such a region, a small cloud of free particles keeps its volume at first but is stretched in one direction and squeezed in another by tidal forces.
The Schwarzschild metric, which describes the space around a non-rotating star or black hole, is the simplest vacuum solution with curvature.
