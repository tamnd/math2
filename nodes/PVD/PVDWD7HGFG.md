## Summary

A quasistatic process is a process that happens so slowly that the system stays in [[equilibrium|5EKTEE00FZ]] at every moment.
It can be drawn as a curve in the space of equilibrium [[states|TD4C13V3J6]].
Along such a curve the state variables are defined at all times, so work and heat can be written as integrals such as $-\int p \, dV$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine compressing a gas by adding grains of sand, one at a time, on top of a piston.
After each grain the gas settles before the next grain arrives.
The gas then passes through a chain of calm states, each with a well-defined pressure and temperature.
A sudden push on the piston is different: for a moment the gas swirls and has no single pressure.
