## Summary

Mathematics education is the study of the teaching and learning of mathematics.
It is both a field of research and the professional practice of teachers, teacher educators and curriculum designers.
Its research uses methods from psychology, sociology and mathematics, and its results shape school curricula, textbooks and the education of teachers.
The International Commission on Mathematical Instruction was founded in 1908 with Felix Klein as its first president, and the field grew into a research discipline in the second half of the 20th century.
This topic collects the mathematics education pages of math2: the theories of learning and of didactics, mathematical cognition and affect, the social dimensions of learning, teaching methods, assessment, curricula and international comparisons, teacher education, university teaching, technology and research methods.
The teaching of each content field, such as algebra, geometry or calculus, is in the topic of that field, and problem solving and competitions, recreational mathematics, and mathematics and culture have their own topics.
