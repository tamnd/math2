## Summary

The S-construction turns a [[Waldhausen category|X7JG1QEB9Q]] $\mathcal{C}$ into a simplicial category $S_\bullet \mathcal{C}$, whose $n$-simplices are chains of $n$ cofibrations together with chosen quotients.
The K-theory space of $\mathcal{C}$ is the loop space of the realization of the weak equivalences $wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}$, and the K-groups are its homotopy groups.
For an exact category with the isomorphisms as weak equivalences, it gives the same K-theory as Quillen's [[Q-construction|J9XP7CS943]].
Iterating it gives the [[K-theory spectrum|2C1M0NWPB0]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

The rule behind $K_0$ is that an object built from a sub $A$ and a quotient $B/A$ counts as $[A] + [B/A]$.
The S-construction turns this rule into geometry.
Objects become points, cofibration sequences $A \rightarrowtail B \twoheadrightarrow B/A$ become triangles with edges $A$, $B$ and $B/A$, and longer filtrations become higher simplices.
Loops in the resulting space are generated by objects, and the triangles impose the additivity rule.
The higher homotopy groups of this space are the higher K-groups.
