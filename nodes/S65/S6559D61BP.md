## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers the dictionary between linear maps and matrices: the matrix of a map with respect to bases, the four fundamental subspaces, rank, change of basis, similarity, and the trace of an endomorphism.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Matrix of a linear map|0YQSGNYE3K]]: the columns are the coordinates of the images of the basis vectors, and $[f(v)]_C = [f]_{C,B}[v]_B$.
2. [[Linear map of a matrix|06HMCTAQH3]]: $\Hom(V, W) \cong M_{m \times n}(F)$ once bases are fixed.
3. [[Matrix of a composition|05Q1B7MH0M]]: $[g \circ f] = [g][f]$, and $\End(V) \cong M_n(F)$ as algebras.
4. [[Column space|03AJKXH1AV]]: the span of the columns, with the pivot columns of $A$ as a basis.
5. [[Row space|069MCGX635]]: row operations keep it, and the nonzero rows of the echelon form are a basis.
6. [[Null space|0PHDYNR24D]]: the solutions of $Ax = 0$, with a basis from the free variables.
7. [[Four fundamental subspaces|~c3d8720b]]: dimensions $r$, $n - r$, $r$, $m - r$, and orthogonality over $\R$.
8. [[Rank of a matrix|0ZC9YTXZRY]]: the common dimension of the row and column spaces.
9. [[Row rank equals column rank|0TZAG60ZYT]]: proofs by row reduction, rank factorization and orthogonality.
10. [[Rank-nullity theorem for matrices|TR48YQGCCA]]: rank plus nullity equals the number of columns.
11. [[Rank of a product of matrices|02FDWY55AS]]: $\rank(AB) \le \min(\rank A, \rank B)$, with the inequalities of Sylvester and Frobenius.
12. [[Rank factorization and rank-one matrices|~5d7700d1]]: $A = CR$, and the rank-one matrices are $uv^\top$.
13. [[Change of basis matrix|0MKRHZB3WS]]: the columns are the new basis vectors in old coordinates, with a warning on conventions.
14. [[Change of basis formula|0MQXVZEBMT]]: $[T]_{C',B'} = Q^{-1}[T]_{C,B}P$.
15. [[Similar matrices|0E89HQATM8]]: $B = P^{-1}AP$, an equivalence relation.
16. [[Invariants that do not decide similarity|~d351ee64]]: two matrices that share five invariants and are not similar.
17. [[Trace of a linear map|0KZDNGA561]]: independent of the basis, and equal to the rank for a projection in characteristic $0$.
18. [[Fredholm alternative|4HH8C9V8JQ]]: $Ax = b$ is solvable exactly when $b$ is orthogonal to the null space of $A^\top$.
