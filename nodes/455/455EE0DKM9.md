## Definition

Let $\C[\partial]$ be the [[polynomial ring|0SDC934R52]] over $\C$ in one variable $\partial$, and let $R$ be a [[module|0NZ8JA25WK]] over $\C[\partial]$.
Let $\lambda$ and $\mu$ be further variables, and write $\C[\lambda] \otimes R$ for the polynomials in $\lambda$ with coefficients in $R$.
A *Lie conformal algebra* structure on $R$ is a $\C$-bilinear map $R \times R \to \C[\lambda] \otimes R$, written $(a, b) \mapsto [a_\lambda b]$, such that for all $a, b, c \in R$:

1. $[\partial a_\lambda b] = -\lambda [a_\lambda b]$ and $[a_\lambda \partial b] = (\partial + \lambda) [a_\lambda b]$;
2. $[b_\lambda a] = -[a_{-\lambda - \partial} b]$;
3. $[a_\lambda [b_\mu c]\,] = [\,[a_\lambda b]_{\lambda + \mu} c] + [b_\mu [a_\lambda c]\,]$.

In clause 2, if $[a_\lambda b] = \sum_j \lambda^j c_j$ with $c_j \in R$, then $[a_{-\lambda - \partial} b]$ means $\sum_j (-\lambda - \partial)^j c_j$.
Clause 3 is an identity of polynomials in $\lambda$ and $\mu$ with coefficients in $R$.

## Remarks

Example: the Virasoro conformal algebra $R = \C[\partial] L$, free of rank one, with $[L_\lambda L] = (\partial + 2\lambda) L$, extended to $R$ by clause 1.
Check of clause 2: $-[L_{-\lambda - \partial} L] = -(\partial + 2(-\lambda - \partial)) L = (\partial + 2\lambda) L$.

Example: for a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, the current conformal algebra $\C[\partial] \otimes \mathfrak{g}$ with $[a_\lambda b] = [a, b]$ for $a, b \in \mathfrak{g}$.

Non-example: $R = \C[\partial] L$ with $[L_\lambda L] = \lambda L$ fails clause 2, since $-[L_{-\lambda - \partial} L] = (\lambda + \partial) L \ne \lambda L$.

The coefficients $a_{(j)} b$ in $[a_\lambda b] = \sum_j \frac{\lambda^j}{j!} a_{(j)} b$ are called the $j$-th products.
