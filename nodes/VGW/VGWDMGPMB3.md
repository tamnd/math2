## Proof

Two results are cited without proof.

- (C1) Finite model property of $\mathsf{S4}$: if $\mathsf{S4} \nvdash \varphi$, then $\varphi$ is false at the root of some finite rooted preorder under some valuation. This follows from completeness for preorders, filtration, and passing to the subframe generated by a world.
- (C2) For every finite rooted preorder $(W, \leq)$, there is a [[continuous|0VGFHTM75Y]] and [[open|0YCG648KDD]] map from $\R$ onto $W$ with its Alexandrov topology of up-sets.

Part A: soundness (items 1 and 3, one direction).

1. Let $X$ be a space. For all $A, B \subseteq X$: $\mathrm{Int}\, A \subseteq A$; $\mathrm{Int}\, A$ is open, so $\mathrm{Int}\, \mathrm{Int}\, A = \mathrm{Int}\, A$; and $\mathrm{Int}(A \cap B) = \mathrm{Int}\, A \cap \mathrm{Int}\, B$.
2. So $\square p \to p$ and $\square p \to \square \square p$ are valid. The axiom $\square(p \to q) \to (\square p \to \square q)$ is valid, since $\mathrm{Int}((X \setminus A) \cup B) \cap \mathrm{Int}\, A = \mathrm{Int}(((X \setminus A) \cup B) \cap A) \subseteq \mathrm{Int}\, B$.
3. Tautologies are valid, since the connectives are computed by Boolean operations on sets. Modus ponens preserves validity.
4. Necessitation preserves validity, since $\mathrm{Int}\, X = X$.
5. So every theorem of $\mathsf{S4}$ is valid in $X$. This is item 1, and the inclusion $\mathsf{S4} \subseteq \mathrm{Log}(\R)$.

Part B: interior maps preserve refutations.

6. Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be continuous and open. Claim: $f^{-1}(\mathrm{Int}\, B) = \mathrm{Int}\, f^{-1}(B)$ for every $B \subseteq Y$.
7. The set $f^{-1}(\mathrm{Int}\, B)$ is open by continuity, and it lies inside $f^{-1}(B)$. So it lies inside $\mathrm{Int}\, f^{-1}(B)$.
8. Conversely, let $x \in \mathrm{Int}\, f^{-1}(B)$, with $x \in U \subseteq f^{-1}(B)$ and $U$ open. Then $f(U)$ is open, contains $f(x)$, and lies inside $B$. So $f(x) \in \mathrm{Int}\, B$.
9. Let $V$ be a valuation on $Y$, and let $V'(p) = f^{-1}(V(p))$ on $X$. By induction on $\varphi$, $V'(\varphi) = f^{-1}(V(\varphi))$. Preimages commute with Boolean operations, and step 6 gives the case of $\square$.
10. If $f$ is onto and $V(\varphi) \neq Y$, then $V'(\varphi) = f^{-1}(V(\varphi)) \neq X$. So a formula refuted in $Y$ is refuted in $X$.

Part C: completeness for $\R$ (item 2).

11. Let $\mathsf{S4} \nvdash \varphi$. By (C1), $\varphi$ fails at the root of a finite rooted preorder $(W, \leq)$ under some valuation.
12. By [[Alexandrov spaces give Kripke semantics|CWZ1K5AN1N]], $\varphi$ is refuted in $W$ with the topology of up-sets, under the same valuation.
13. By (C2), there is a continuous open map from $\R$ onto this space. By step 10, $\varphi$ is refuted in $\R$.
14. So $\mathrm{Log}(\R) \subseteq \mathsf{S4}$. With step 5, $\mathrm{Log}(\R) = \mathsf{S4}$, which is item 2.
15. For item 3, the logic of all spaces is contained in $\mathrm{Log}(\R) = \mathsf{S4}$, and it contains $\mathsf{S4}$ by step 5.

## Idea

Soundness is the list of Kuratowski's axioms for the interior operator.
For completeness, a non-theorem of $\mathsf{S4}$ is refuted on a finite preorder, which is a finite topological space.
Continuous open maps pull refutations back along preimages, so it suffices to map $\R$ onto every finite rooted preorder in this way.
The cited lemma (C2) divides $\R$ into pieces, one for each point $w$ of the preorder.
Each point of the piece of $w$ has a neighbourhood inside the pieces of the points above $w$.
Each of its neighbourhoods meets all these pieces.

## Variants

McKinsey and Tarski gave an algebraic proof: every finite well-connected closure algebra embeds into the closure algebra of a dense-in-itself separable metric space.
This embedding is the algebraic dual of the onto interior map of (C2).
Other proofs pass through trees: a finite rooted preorder is the image of a finite tree of clusters under a bounded morphism, and the Cantor space or $\R$ is mapped onto such trees.
Kremer's proof of strong completeness extends the method from single formulas to infinite sets of formulas.
