## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the modal logics that describe provability and interpretability in arithmetic, their arithmetical completeness theorems and their algebraic semantics.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Provability logic|WJK0YA4GP2]]: the logic $\mathsf{GL}$, with $\square$ read as provability in $\mathsf{PA}$.
2. [[Löb's axiom in provability logic|QQ6PZFGGB3]]: axiom 4 follows from Löb's axiom, and the frame condition of the axiom.
3. [[Kripke completeness of GL|~d95bfe82]]: $\mathsf{GL}$ is complete for finite transitive irreflexive frames, so it is decidable.
4. [[Solovay's arithmetical completeness theorem|~5cc205a1]]: $\mathsf{GL}$ is exactly the provability logic of $\mathsf{PA}$, and $\mathsf{GLS}$ is the logic of truth.
5. [[De Jongh-Sambin fixed-point theorem|~2d32d967]]: self-referential modal sentences have unique explicit fixed points.
6. [[Grzegorczyk logic|~18306319]]: the logic of "true and provable" and of finite partial orders.
7. [[Japaridze's polymodal logic GLP|~fc4764bd]]: a hierarchy of provability operators with no complete Kripke semantics.
8. [[Magari algebra|~f637853d]]: the algebraic models of $\mathsf{GL}$.
9. [[Interpretability logic|~f60c136a]]: the binary operator $\rhd$, Veltman semantics and the logics $\mathsf{ILM}$ and $\mathsf{ILP}$.
10. [[Quantified provability logic|~b94ba81d]]: with quantifiers, the provability logic of $\mathsf{PA}$ is $\Pi^0_2$-complete.

### Prerequisites

1. [[Gödel's incompleteness theorems and related limits|HXHHWY3328]]: the provability predicate, the derivability conditions and [[Löb's theorem|3HD30YG3DG]].
2. [[Formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]]: Peano arithmetic and its $\Sigma_1$-completeness.
3. [[Syntax, Kripke semantics and completeness of modal logic|SKY7AQFG1V]]: [[modal logic|C3RZY20ETK]] and [[Kripke semantics|NGHWZ9Y7ZV]].
