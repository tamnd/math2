## Summary

In number theory, a **sphenic number** (from σφήν, 'wedge') is a [[positive integer|0MWKJ44V5A]] that is the product of three distinct [[prime numbers|01826KMG2H]].p. 147 For example, since 2, 3, and 73 are all prime, 438 is a sphenic number because $2 \times 3 \times 73 = 438$.p. 147 Because there are [[infinitely many prime numbers|0292EPPPT0]], there are also infinitely many sphenic numbers.
