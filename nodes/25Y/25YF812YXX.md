## Definition

Let $F$ be a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] and let $T$ be a [[vector space|000AYPA9AJ]] over $F$.
A *Lie triple system* on $T$ is a trilinear map $T \times T \times T \to T$, written $(x, y, z) \mapsto [x, y, z]$, such that for all $u, v, x, y, z \in T$:

1. $[x, x, z] = 0$;
2. $[x, y, z] + [y, z, x] + [z, x, y] = 0$;
3. $[u, v, [x, y, z]\,] = [\,[u, v, x], y, z] + [x, [u, v, y], z] + [x, y, [u, v, z]\,]$.

## Remarks

Example: let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] with bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]$ and put $[x, y, z] = [\,[x, y], z]$.
Clause 1 holds because $[x, x] = 0$.
Clause 2 is the Jacobi identity.
Clause 3 says that the map $z \mapsto [\,[u, v], z]$ acts as a derivation of the triple product, and this holds because $\mathrm{ad}_{[u, v]}$ is a derivation of the bracket.
The same formulas hold on any subspace of $\mathfrak{g}$ closed under the triple product, for example on the $-1$ eigenspace of an involutive automorphism of $\mathfrak{g}$.

Non-example: on $\R^n$ with the dot product, the trilinear map $[x, y, z] = \ip{x}{y} z$ fails clause 1, since $[x, x, z] = \abs{x}^2 z \ne 0$ for nonzero $x$ and $z$.

Clause 1 implies the skew-symmetry $[x, y, z] = -[y, x, z]$, by expanding $[x + y, x + y, z] = 0$.
