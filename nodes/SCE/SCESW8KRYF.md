## Summary

This topic is a part of [[order, lattices and universal algebra|V1VVQR12AQ]].
It covers sets of operations closed under composition, their Galois connection with relations, the lattice of clones on a finite set, the functional completeness of algebras, and the use of polymorphisms to classify constraint problems.
