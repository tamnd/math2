## Summary

The limit of a function of several variables at a point $a$ is the value that $f(x)$ approaches when $x$ approaches $a$ in [[Euclidean space|0XPR1E85W0]]. The approach can be from any direction and along any route.
The distance in $\mathbb{R}^n$ measures how close $x$ is to $a$.
