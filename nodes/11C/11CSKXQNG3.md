## Statement

Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix with real entries.
Then the [[column space|03AJKXH1AV]] of $A$ is a [[subspace|00YC1D652K]] of $\mathbb{R}^m$.
Its [[dimension|03GSZ8RTZJ]] is equal to the [[rank|0ZC9YTXZRY]] of $A$.
Moreover, the columns of $A$ that hold [[pivot|0BCMGDHZGR]] positions form a [[basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]] of the column space.

## Remarks

So the number of pivot columns of $A$ is the rank of $A$.
