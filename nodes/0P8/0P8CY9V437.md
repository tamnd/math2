## Example

Let $G$ be a [[group|G5S1M9]].
The map $G \times G \to G$, $(g, x) \mapsto gxg^{-1}$, is an [[action|0WCY42EV9V]] of $G$ on the set $G$.
Indeed, $exe^{-1} = x$ and $g(hxh^{-1})g^{-1} = (gh)x(gh)^{-1}$.
The [[orbits|0YQPZB5Q1G]] of this action are the [[conjugacy classes|0DEBXAJ5SA]] of $G$.
The [[stabiliser|0AYMP41THN]] of $x$ is the [[centraliser|02YYV36BFM]] $C_G(x)$.
The [[fixed points|0RRWNMYRXC]] are the elements of the [[centre|0DZ93493VN]] $Z(G)$.
For an [[abelian group|082GHNPJ53]], each element is a fixed point, and each orbit has one element.
