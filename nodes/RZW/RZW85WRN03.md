## Summary

In number theory, a ***k*th root of unity modulo *n*** for positive [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] *k*, *n* ≥ 2, is a [[root of unity|0S61N75H45]] in the ring of integers modulo *n*; that is, a solution *x* to the equation (or *congruence*) $x^k \equiv 1 \pmod{n}$. If *k* is the smallest such exponent for *x*, then *x* is called a **primitive *k*th root of unity modulo *n***. See modular arithmetic for notation and terminology.
