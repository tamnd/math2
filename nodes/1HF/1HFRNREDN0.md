## Summary

In general, exponentiation fails to be [[commutative|099E72KTF8]]. However, the equation $x^y = y^x$ has an infinity of solutions, consisting of the line $x=y$ and a smooth curve intersecting the line at $(e,e)$, where $e$ is Euler's number. The only integer solution that is on the curve is $2^4=4^2$.
