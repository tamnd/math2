## Summary

A Euclidean domain is an integral domain with a division with remainder, in which the remainder is smaller than the divisor for some size function.
The integers and the polynomials over a field are the main examples.
The [[definition of a Euclidean domain|0GJ2HRG95T]] gives the conditions.
