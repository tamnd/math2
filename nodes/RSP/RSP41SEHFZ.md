## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|G7N6BDTEZ2]].
It covers rings with the extra structure that ring theory uses: group gradings and superalgebras, filtrations, involutions, derivations, group actions with their fixed rings, and Rota-Baxter operators.
