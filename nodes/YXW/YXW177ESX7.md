## Summary

A **fish curve** is an ellipse negative pedal curve that is shaped like a fish. In a fish curve, the pedal point is at the focus for the special case of the squared eccentricity $e^2=\tfrac{1}{2}$. The parametric equations for a fish curve correspond to those of the associated [[ellipse|0VKCCEXBJ1]].
