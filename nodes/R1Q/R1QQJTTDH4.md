## Summary

In number theory, **Jordan's totient function**, denoted as $J_k(n)$, where $k$ is a positive integer, is a function of a positive integer, $n$, that equals the number of $k$-[[tuples|9HFER6N708]] of positive integers that are less than or equal to $n$ and that together with $n$ form a [[coprime set|0VZ48N083F]] of $k+1$ integers.
