## Summary

The exergy of a system is the largest useful work that can be extracted from it.
The extraction ends when the system is in equilibrium with a given environment.
Unlike energy, exergy is not conserved: every irreversible process destroys some of it.
Exergy analysis uses this to locate and measure the losses in power plants, engines and chemical processes.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Energy is never lost, but it can become useless.
Take a cup of hot coffee and the air around it, before and after the coffee cools.
The total energy is the same, yet only before can you run a small engine between them.
Exergy measures the part of the energy that can still be turned into work, given the temperature and pressure of the surroundings.
A tank of air at the same temperature and pressure as the room has zero exergy, however much energy it contains.
