## Example

A root world $w$ sees two dead-end worlds $u$ and $v$, and $w$ lets $u$ jump to $v$.
Precisely, let $W = \{w, u, v\}$ with $R = \{(w, u), (w, v)\}$, so $R[w] = \{u, v\}$ and $R[u] = R[v] = \emptyset$.
Let $S_w = \{(u, u), (v, v), (u, v)\}$, and let $S_u$ and $S_v$ be empty.
Let $p$ be true only at $u$, and $q$ true only at $v$.
We first check that $(W, R, \{S_x\})$ is a Veltman frame, with the conditions of [[interpretability logic|BGKZTSQN3W]].

1. $W$ is finite and non-empty.
2. $R$ is irreflexive, since none of the pairs $(w, w)$, $(u, u)$, $(v, v)$ lies in $R$.
3. $R$ is [[transitive|0GJZW9JFZQ]], since a chain $xRyRz$ needs $y \in \{u, v\}$ with an $R$-successor, and neither $u$ nor $v$ has one.
4. $S_w$ is a relation on $R[w] = \{u, v\}$, and it contains $(u, u)$ and $(v, v)$, so it is reflexive on $R[w]$.
5. $S_w$ is transitive.
   Composing $(u, v)$ with an identity pair on either side gives back $(u, v)$, and no pair starts at $v$ except $(v, v)$.
6. $S_u$ and $S_v$ are relations on $R[u] = R[v] = \emptyset$, and the empty relation is reflexive and transitive there.
7. The condition "$xRyRz$ implies $y S_x z$" holds, because by step 3 there is no chain $xRyRz$.

Now we compute two formulas at $w$.

8. $w \Vdash p \rhd q$.
   The only $R$-successor of $w$ where $p$ holds is $u$, and $u S_w v$ with $v \Vdash q$.
9. $w \nVdash \square(p \to q)$.
   We have $wRu$, $u \Vdash p$ and $u \nVdash q$, so $u \nVdash p \to q$.

So $p \rhd q \to \square(p \to q)$ fails at $w$, and by the soundness of $\mathsf{IL}$ for Veltman models it is not a theorem of $\mathsf{IL}$.

## Remarks

### The stronger logics

The model also meets the frame conditions of $\mathsf{ILM}$ and $\mathsf{ILP}$.
The condition of M, "$x S_y z R t$ implies $x R t$", holds because every world $z$ in $R[w]$ is a dead end.
The condition of P, "$y R y' R x S_y z$ implies $x S_{y'} z$", holds because there is no chain $y R y' R x$.
So $p \rhd q \to \square(p \to q)$ is not a theorem of $\mathsf{ILM}$ or $\mathsf{ILP}$ either.

### The arithmetical counterpart

The example shows that the converse of axiom J1 fails, so interpretability is weaker than provable implication.
In arithmetic, $T + A$ can interpret $T + B$ without proving $B$.
For instance, $\mathsf{PA} + \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$ interprets $\mathsf{PA} + \neg \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$.
It does not prove $\neg \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$, because it is a consistent theory that proves $\mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$.

The interpretation comes from J5 with $A = \neg \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$.
The formalized second incompleteness theorem gives $\mathsf{PA} \vdash \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA}) \to \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA} + A)$.
So $\mathsf{PA} + \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$ interprets $\mathsf{PA} + \mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA} + A)$ by J1, and that theory interprets $\mathsf{PA} + A$ by J5.

### Small changes to the model

If we remove the pair $(u, v)$ from $S_w$, then $w \nVdash p \rhd q$, since the only $S_w$-partner of $u$ is $u$, where $q$ fails.
If we add $(v, u)$ to $S_w$, the frame stays a Veltman frame, and $w \Vdash q \rhd p$ holds as well.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a root world $w$ that looks at two worlds $u$ and $v$.
At $u$ the statement $p$ holds, and at $v$ the statement $q$ holds.
From $w$, the world $u$ is allowed to "jump" to $v$.
"$p$ interprets $q$" holds at $w$, because every $p$-world seen from $w$ can jump to a $q$-world.
But "$p$ implies $q$ in every seen world" fails, because at $u$ we have $p$ without $q$.

### L2 human

We evaluate J3 with $r = q$ at $w$, as a further check of the model.

1. $w \Vdash p \rhd q$ by step 8.
2. $w \Vdash q \rhd q$, because the only successor where $q$ holds is $v$, and $v S_w v$.
3. $w \Vdash (p \vee q) \rhd q$, because the successors with $p \vee q$ are $u$ and $v$, and both have the partner $v$.

So J3 holds at $w$ in this instance, as soundness says it must.
The typical use of such a model is to separate two formulas that look alike.
The trap is to think the jump relation is global.
$S_w$ belongs to the world $w$, and a different root world could carry a different relation on its successors.
