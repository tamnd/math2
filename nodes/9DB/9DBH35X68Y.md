## Summary

The fundamental representations of a complex [[semisimple Lie algebra|EY9DN7QAX1]] are the irreducible representations whose [[highest weights|C5W8BE1DRY]] are the fundamental weights.
There is one for each simple root, so their number is the rank.
Every finite-dimensional irreducible representation occurs inside a tensor product of fundamental ones.
For $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\C)$ they are the exterior powers $\Lambda^k \C^n$ for $1 \le k \le n - 1$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Irreducible representations are labelled by highest weights, which are combinations of a few basic weights with nonnegative integer coefficients.
The fundamental representations are the ones labelled by the basic weights themselves.
They act as generators: the other irreducible representations sit inside tensor products of them.
In physics, "fundamental representation" often means more loosely the defining representation, such as $\C^n$ for $SU(n)$.
