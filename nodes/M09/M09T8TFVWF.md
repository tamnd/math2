## Summary

In mathematics, the **Turán number** $T(n,k,r)$ for $r$-uniform hypergraphs of order $n$ is the smallest number of $r$-edges such that every [[induced subgraph|0EREPBVG2G]] on $k$ vertices contains an edge. This number was determined for $r = 2$ by (Turán 1941), and the problem for general $r$ was introduced in (Turán 1961). The paper (Sidorenko 1995) gives a survey of Turán numbers.
