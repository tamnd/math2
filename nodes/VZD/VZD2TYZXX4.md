## Statement

Let $m$, $n$ and $p$ be any [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], including $0$. Then multiplying $n$ by $p$ first and then by $m$ gives the same result as multiplying $m$ by $n$ first and then by $p$:

$$m \cdot (n \cdot p) = (m \cdot n) \cdot p.$$

Here $\cdot$ is the [[multiplication of natural numbers|06J5DE801P]].

## Remarks

Because of this law, a product of three factors can be written without parentheses.
