## Summary

A set of simple roots, or a base, of a root system is a basis of the ambient space made of roots.
Every root must be an integer combination of them with all coefficients of the same sign.
It splits the roots into positive and negative roots.
The simple roots determine the whole root system, and the Cartan matrix and the Dynkin diagram are built from them.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A root system can have many roots, but a few of them are enough to rebuild all the others.
Every root is then a sum of simple roots, or minus such a sum, with no mixing of signs.
In the hexagon system $A_2$ with roots $\pm \alpha, \pm \beta, \pm(\alpha + \beta)$, the two roots $\alpha$ and $\beta$ at an angle of $120^\circ$ are simple roots.
