## Summary

A conservative vector field is a [[vector field|0299VCW1TH]] that is the [[gradient|0ABK9V9Y64]] of a scalar function, called a [[scalar potential|0H7C3T1GJY]] of the field.
Its line integrals depend only on the end points of the path, and they vanish around each closed path.
