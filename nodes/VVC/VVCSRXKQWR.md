## Summary

The Grashof number is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that compares buoyancy with viscous forces in natural convection.
It plays for natural [[convection|RK24AYNG4Z]] the role that the [[Reynolds number|JH9EJ0HA0R]] plays for forced convection.
Its product with the [[Prandtl number|C4KSBMR413]] is the [[Rayleigh number|CEKBV27MP0]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Air next to a hot wall warms, expands and becomes lighter, so it rises.
[[Viscosity|NM5Q4845Z4]] resists this motion.
The Grashof number compares the lift of the warm air with the drag of viscosity.
It grows with the cube of the height of the wall, so tall hot surfaces drive much stronger flows than short ones.
