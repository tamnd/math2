## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers matrices over a field or a commutative ring, the operations on them, and the standard named types of matrices that a first course uses.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Matrix|0VD9ZSWWJG]]: a rectangular array of entries, its size and its entries.
2. [[Square matrix|0JXZKX526R]]: equal numbers of rows and columns, and the main diagonal.
3. [[Zero matrix|~d9ded4cd]]: the identity for addition.
4. [[Identity matrix|~495c2486]]: the identity for multiplication.
5. [[Matrix addition and scalar multiplication|0Y7H3SAVA1]]: the vector space of matrices of one size.
6. [[Matrix-vector product|0PDVQEP0ZC]]: the row view and the column view of $Ax$.
7. [[Matrix multiplication|032Q9FB8MT]]: the row-column rule and the composition of linear maps.
8. [[Associativity of matrix multiplication|073E4XC016]]: a proof by sums and a proof by composition.
9. [[Non-commutativity of matrix multiplication|0P506SPZP2]]: two matrices with $AB \neq BA$.
10. [[Matrix ring|~de637a64]]: the ring $M_n(R)$, its centre and its ideals.
11. [[Scalar matrix|067NKSBEWT]]: the matrices $cI$ and the matrices that commute with all others.
12. [[Diagonal matrix|044JNC7ZAA]]: sums, products and powers entry by entry.
13. [[Upper and lower triangular matrices|0N4XY7Z3NN]]: closed under products, and strictly triangular matrices are nilpotent.
14. [[Transpose|09DXWYRD43]]: rows become columns, and the notations for it.
15. [[Transpose of a product|09GK5PS824]]: the rule $(AB)^{\mathsf{T}} = B^{\mathsf{T}} A^{\mathsf{T}}$.
16. [[Symmetric matrix|0VR671PHE5]]: $A^{\mathsf{T}} = A$, and when a product is symmetric.
17. [[Skew-symmetric matrix|0PBY4E9GQF]]: $A^{\mathsf{T}} = -A$, and the symmetric plus skew-symmetric decomposition.
18. [[Conjugate transpose|0DXKE10PHF]]: the transpose with complex conjugate entries.
19. [[Hermitian and skew-Hermitian matrices|0P17NGQQMQ]]: $A^* = A$, and the Cartesian decomposition.
20. [[Block matrix|0ASDFEQS6P]]: partitions and the block multiplication rule.
21. [[Block diagonal matrix|001T5FJDC1]]: square blocks on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere.
22. [[Trace of a matrix|0XC1RDBY7A]]: the sum of the diagonal entries.
23. [[Cyclic property of the trace|0Y4NF1DRKD]]: $\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)$, and no solution of $AB - BA = I$.
24. [[Nilpotent matrix|07BSPZPSMF]]: $N^k = 0$, and the inverse of $I - N$.
25. [[Idempotent matrix|0KCE8CG86P]]: $P^2 = P$, and projections.
26. [[Involutory matrix|~c8443172]]: $A^2 = I$, and reflections.
27. [[Commuting matrices|8G3X8MPG7E]]: $AB = BA$, and the binomial theorem.
28. [[Matrix units|~2c685e2c]]: the matrices $E_{ij}$ and the rule $E_{ij} E_{kl} = \delta_{jk} E_{il}$.
