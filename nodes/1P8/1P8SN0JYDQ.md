## Summary

In mathematics, the **constant sheaf** on a [[topological space|0CQHFPGJ45]] $X$ associated to a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] $A$ is a sheaf of sets on $X$ whose stalks are all equal to $A$. It is denoted by $\underline{A}$ or $A_X$. The **constant presheaf** with value $A$ is the presheaf that assigns to each [[open subset|01ZQQDCERP]] of $X$ the value $A$, and all of whose restriction maps are the identity map $A\to A$. The constant sheaf associated to $A$ is the sheafification of the constant presheaf associated to $A$. This sheaf may be identified with the sheaf of locally constant $A$-valued functions on $X$.
