## Summary

A Moufang loop is a loop, that is, a [[quasigroup|ZJD31SPPFP]] with an identity element, that satisfies the Moufang identity $z(x(zy)) = ((zx)z)y$.
Moufang loops are the most studied class of loops close to groups.
Every group is one, and in each of them any two elements generate a subgroup.
The nonzero octonions under multiplication form a Moufang loop that is not a group.
Ruth Moufang introduced these loops in her work on projective planes.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A loop is like a group in which the associative law may fail.
In a Moufang loop it fails only in a controlled way: certain products in which one element appears twice can be regrouped.
The octonions give the main picture: their multiplication is not associative, yet two octonions always behave as if they lived in an associative world.
For this reason the nonzero octonions, or the octonions of length $1$, are the standard example of a Moufang loop that is not a group.
