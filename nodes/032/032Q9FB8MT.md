## Summary

Matrix multiplication is the operation that takes an $m \times n$ matrix and an $n \times p$ matrix and gives an $m \times p$ matrix.
Entry $(i, k)$ of the product combines row $i$ of the first matrix with column $k$ of the second matrix.
