## Summary

The beta plane is a local flat approximation of a rotating sphere in which the Coriolis parameter varies linearly with the northward coordinate.
It keeps the one effect of the Earth's curvature that matters most for large-scale flow: the change of the Coriolis parameter with latitude.
It is the simplest setting that supports [[Rossby waves|6N1MQMA9TM]] and explains the westward drift of large weather and ocean patterns.

## Explanations

### L1 human

On a spinning globe, the sideways deflection of moving air is strongest at the poles and vanishes at the equator.
The beta plane replaces a patch of the globe by a flat sheet, but keeps the fact that this deflection changes as one goes north.
It does this in the simplest possible way, by a straight-line rule $f = f_0 + \beta y$.
This small change is enough to create Rossby waves, the slow meanders of the jet stream.
