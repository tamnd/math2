## Summary

In mathematics, a **delta operator** is a shift-equivariant linear operator $Q\colon\mathbb{K}[x] \longrightarrow \mathbb{K}[x]$ on the [[vector space|000AYPA9AJ]] of polynomials in a variable $x$ over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $\mathbb{K}$ that reduces [[degrees|0DPF6WRZW6]] by one.
