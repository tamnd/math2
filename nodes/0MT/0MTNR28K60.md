## Summary

A Poisson point process is a random set of points in which the numbers of points in disjoint regions are independent Poisson random variables. On the time axis, it models events that occur at random times with a constant rate.
The node [[Poisson point process|05TG0SE4HT]] gives the precise definition of the homogeneous process on $[0, \infty)$.
