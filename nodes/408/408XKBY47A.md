## Definition

Let $K$ be a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] and let $A$ be a commutative [[nonassociative algebra|60W3THKGA7]] over $K$ of finite dimension $n + 1$.
The algebra $A$ is a *genetic algebra* if it has a basis $c_0, c_1, \ldots, c_n$ with [[structure constants|HFXWYNFB9A]] $\lambda_{ijk} \in K$, defined by $c_i c_j = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \lambda_{ijk} c_k$, such that:

1. $\lambda_{000} = 1$.
2. $\lambda_{0jk} = 0$ for all $j \ge 1$ and $k < j$.
3. $\lambda_{ijk} = 0$ for all $i, j \ge 1$ and $k \le \max(i, j)$.

## Remarks

Several closely related definitions are in use; this one asks for a basis in which the multiplication is triangular.
The linear map $w$ with $w(c_0) = 1$ and $w(c_i) = 0$ for $i \ge 1$ is then a weight, so $(A, w)$ is a [[baric algebra|A6HRHW6CBY]].
Indeed, the conditions force $c_0 c_0$ to have weight $1$ and all other products of basis vectors to have weight $0$.

Example: the gametic algebra with basis $A_1, A_2$ over $\Q$, $A_1^2 = A_1$, $A_2^2 = A_2$, $A_1 A_2 = \tfrac12(A_1 + A_2)$.
Take $c_0 = A_1$ and $c_1 = A_1 - A_2$.
Then $c_0^2 = c_0$, $c_0 c_1 = A_1 - \tfrac12(A_1 + A_2) = \tfrac12 c_1$, and $c_1^2 = A_1 + A_2 - (A_1 + A_2) = 0$, so all three clauses hold.

Non-example: $K^2$ with the componentwise product is not genetic.
For a basis as above, $w$ is a weight and clause 3 gives $c_1^2 = 0$ for the vector $c_1$ that spans $\ker w$.
But the only weights on $K^2$ are the two coordinate projections, and the kernel of each is spanned by an idempotent $e$, with $e^2 = e \ne 0$.
So $c_1 = t e$ with $t \ne 0$ and $c_1^2 = t^2 e \ne 0$, a contradiction.
