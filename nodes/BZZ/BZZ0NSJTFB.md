## Summary

Optimization is the study of how to find the best point of a set with respect to a given objective.
An optimization problem asks for a point that minimizes a function over a feasible set, and equations and inequalities usually describe this set.
Optimization problems arise in engineering, economics, statistics and machine learning, and convexity often decides whether a problem is tractable.
Fermat and Lagrange gave the first conditions for extrema, and the work of Kantorovich and Dantzig on linear programming in the 1940s started the modern subject.
This topic collects the optimization pages of math2: convex analysis and duality, optimality conditions, linear, integer, conic and polynomial programming, combinatorial optimization, the algorithms and their complexity, and optimization under uncertainty and with several objectives.
Operations research, game theory, the calculus of variations and optimal control, and convex and discrete geometry have their own topics.
