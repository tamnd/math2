## Summary

The Marshallian demand function gives the bundle of goods that a consumer buys at given prices and wealth.
It is the solution of the [[utility maximization problem|A56BG7XYM6]], seen as a function of the prices and the wealth.
It is the demand that one observes in markets, and it is homogeneous of degree zero in prices and wealth together.
Its changes with prices split into substitution and income effects by the Slutsky equation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Tell a consumer the prices of all goods and how much money they have.
The Marshallian demand is the basket they then choose, the best one they can afford.
For example, a consumer who always spends half of their money on bread buys $w / (2 p)$ loaves when they have $w$ euros and bread costs $p$ euros.
If all prices and the wealth double, the basket does not change.
