## Summary

A Jordan operator algebra is a norm-closed space of [[self-adjoint|0NEGN5CRSC]] bounded operators on a [[Hilbert space|HM2HHE3J51]].
It is closed under the Jordan product $\frac{1}{2}(ab + ba)$.
Such algebras are also called JC-algebras, and they are concrete examples of [[JB-algebras|NXY6YN56XR]].
They model the observables of a quantum system.
Self-adjoint operators are not closed under the ordinary product, but they are closed under the Jordan product.
The theory is the Jordan analogue of the theory of C*-algebras and [[von Neumann algebras|PSFWQVZ0FS]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

In quantum mechanics, measurable quantities are represented by self-adjoint operators.
The product $ab$ of two self-adjoint operators is self-adjoint only when they commute.
The averaged product $\frac{1}{2}(ab + ba)$ is always self-adjoint, and it can be written with squares only, as $\frac{1}{4}((a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2)$.
A Jordan operator algebra is a closed family of self-adjoint operators that is stable under this product, for example all real symmetric $n \times n$ matrices.
