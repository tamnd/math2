## Summary

An isentropic process is a process in which the [[entropy|SPY89W0RJ7]] of the system stays constant.
A reversible [[adiabatic process|W1RS8DA2H6]] is isentropic, and in engineering the two terms are often used as synonyms.
Isentropic processes are the ideal reference for compressors, turbines and nozzles.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a gas is compressed smoothly and without friction in a perfectly insulated cylinder, no heat enters or leaves and nothing is wasted.
Its entropy then stays the same, and the process is isentropic.
Real machines lose a little to friction, so their entropy rises.
Engineers compare a real turbine with the ideal isentropic one to measure how good it is.
