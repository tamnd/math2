## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers Tarski's semantics for first-order logic, with structures, assignments, satisfaction, truth, models, validity and the basic lemmas.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Structure|983GZBQYT4]]: a domain with interpretations of the symbols of a signature.
2. [[Satisfaction|2RBQPV02AS]]: Tarski's recursive clauses for $\mathcal{M} \models \varphi[s]$.
3. [[Truth in a structure|K7XDVK5854]]: sentences true in $\mathcal{M}$ and the theory $\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal{M})$.
4. [[Model|MVCH6N9SNW]]: structures in which every sentence of a theory is true.
5. [[Validity|5SDS9T8E7J]]: formulas true in every structure.
6. [[Coincidence lemma|~7b107606]]: satisfaction depends only on the free variables and the symbols used.
7. [[Substitution lemma (semantic)|~761225ef]]: substitution of a term matches a change of the assignment.
8. [[Isomorphism invariance of truth|~5166a0b3]]: isomorphic structures satisfy the same formulas.
9. [[Herbrand universe|~b97c4e92]]: the closed terms of a signature.
10. [[Herbrand structure|HKW429DSDK]]: structures built from closed terms, and Herbrand models of universal sentences.
11. [[Game-theoretic semantics|D45Z7HETDA]]: truth as the existence of a winning strategy for the verifier.
12. [[Definable relations|~11b21939]]: relations defined by formulas, and non-definability by automorphisms.
13. [[Drinker paradox|~c90af64d]]: a valid sentence that sounds false.

### Prerequisites

- [[First-order languages and syntax|PSRDJXRW53]]: signatures, terms, formulas, free variables and substitution.
- [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: truth tables and tautologies, which the clauses for the connectives extend.
- [[Reasoning and proof|SGSVYFDSYK]]: proof by cases and proof by induction, used in the proofs of the lemmas.
