## Summary

The world line of a point particle is the curve in spacetime made of all the events where the particle is present.
In [[Minkowski space|G49G4R1K04]] the world line of a massive particle is a curve whose tangent always points into the future light cone.
The world line of a light pulse is a null line.
World lines turn motion into geometry.
A particle at rest has a vertical straight world line, a uniformly moving one a tilted straight line, and an accelerated one a curved line.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Draw time going up and space going sideways.
A particle sitting still traces a vertical line in this picture, because it stays at one place as time goes on.
A moving particle traces a slanted line, and a speeding-up particle traces a curve.
For example, with $c = 1$ a particle moving at speed $0.5$ from the origin has world line $x = 0.5 t$, which is steeper than the light ray $x = t$.
