## Summary

In mathematics, a **torsion sheaf** is a sheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal{F}$ on a site for which, for every object *U*, the space of sections $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{F})$ is a torsion abelian group. Similarly, for a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] *p*, we say a sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ is ***p*-torsion** if every section over any object is killed by a power of *p*.
