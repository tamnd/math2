## Summary

In [[combinatorics|0JKW7W7XFR]], a **square-free word** is a word (a sequence of symbols) that does not contain any squares. A **square** is a word of the form $XX$, where $X$ is not empty. Thus, a square-free word can also be defined as a word that avoids the pattern $XX$.
