## Summary

In abstract algebra, an associative algebra $A$ over a [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] $R$ is called **finite** if it is [[finitely generated|05QY1VEBYD]] as an $R$-[[module|0NZ8JA25WK]]. An $R$-algebra can be thought as a homomorphism of [[rings|0B1261B5ED]] $f: R \to A$, in this case $f$ is called a **finite morphism** if $A$ is a finite $R$-algebra.
