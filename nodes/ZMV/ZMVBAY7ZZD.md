## Summary

In mathematical logic, the **implicational propositional calculus** is a version of classical propositional calculus that uses only one connective, called [[implication or conditional|0NMG7W7Q11]]. In formulas, this [[binary operation|0580ZJSA3Q]] is indicated by "implies", "if ..., then ...", "→", "$\rightarrow$", etc..
