## Summary

In mathematics, a **pointed set** (also **based set** or **rooted set**) is an [[ordered pair|0JQWWFCS9R]] $(X, x_0)$ where $X$ is a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] and $x_0$ is an element of $X$ called the **base point**  (also spelled **basepoint**).
