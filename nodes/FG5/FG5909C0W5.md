## Definition

Let $A$ be a [[Jordan algebra|6C2MCVB8C7]] over $\R$, with product $(a, b) \mapsto a \circ b$, and write $a^2 = a \circ a$.
A *JB-algebra* structure on $A$ is a [[norm|03J5RAKB30]] $\norm{\cdot}$ on $A$ such that for all $a, b \in A$:

1. $A$ is [[complete|0BY52JW6NK]] for the metric $\norm{a - b}$;
2. $\norm{a \circ b} \le \norm{a} \norm{b}$;
3. $\norm{a^2} = \norm{a}^2$;
4. $\norm{a^2} \le \norm{a^2 + b^2}$.

## Remarks

Example: the real symmetric $n \times n$ matrices with $a \circ b = \frac{1}{2}(ab + ba)$ and the operator norm.
Clause 1 holds in finite dimension, and clause 2 follows from $\norm{ab} \le \norm{a}\norm{b}$.
For clauses 3 and 4, $\norm{a}$ is the largest absolute value of an eigenvalue, so $\norm{a^2} = \norm{a}^2$, and $a^2 \le a^2 + b^2$ as positive semidefinite matrices.

Non-example: $\R^2$ with the componentwise product and the norm $\norm{(x_1, x_2)} = \abs{x_1} + \abs{x_2}$ fails clause 3.
For $a = (1, 1)$ one has $\norm{a^2} = 2$ but $\norm{a}^2 = 4$.
With the norm $\max(\abs{x_1}, \abs{x_2})$ instead, it is a JB-algebra.

The self-adjoint part of a C*-algebra, with $a \circ b = \frac{1}{2}(ab + ba)$, is a JB-algebra.
