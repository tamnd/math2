## Summary

A compact real form of a complex semisimple Lie algebra is a real form whose Killing form is negative definite.
These are exactly the real forms that are Lie algebras of compact Lie groups.
Every complex semisimple Lie algebra has a compact real form, unique up to conjugation by an automorphism.
This underlies Weyl's unitary trick and the Cartan decomposition.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Among the real forms of a complex semisimple Lie algebra there is always one that comes from a compact group of symmetries, such as a group of rotations.
For $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\C)$ it is $\mathfrak{su}(n)$, the skew-Hermitian matrices of trace $0$, the Lie algebra of the special unitary group.
Working with this compact form lets one average over a compact group, which makes many proofs easier.
