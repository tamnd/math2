## Summary

A Jordan pair is a pair of vector spaces $(V^+, V^-)$ with two triple products.
Each product takes two elements of one space and one element of the other.
It generalizes the [[Jordan triple system|M13DZ6YXH1]], where the two spaces coincide.
Through the [[Tits–Kantor–Koecher construction|FZM1A8HAHD]], Jordan pairs match Lie algebras with a grading $\mathfrak{g}_{-1} \oplus \mathfrak{g}_0 \oplus \mathfrak{g}_1$.
The theory was developed systematically by Ottmar Loos.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A $p \times q$ matrix and a $q \times p$ matrix can be multiplied in either order.
So with $x, z$ of size $p \times q$ and $y$ of size $q \times p$, the product $xyz$ is again $p \times q$.
Swapping the roles gives a product of two $q \times p$ matrices around one $p \times q$ matrix.
A Jordan pair keeps the two spaces apart and records both triple products and their rules.
