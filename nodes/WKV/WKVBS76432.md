## Summary

The Lie algebra of a [[Lie group|7JBRGCVJRB]] $G$ is the tangent space of $G$ at the identity.
Its bracket comes from the bracket of left-invariant vector fields.
It is a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] that records the group near the identity.
By the [[Lie group–Lie algebra correspondence|M13HAQSHEM]], it determines a connected and simply connected Lie group up to isomorphism.
Lie group homomorphisms are studied through the linear maps they induce on Lie algebras.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Lie group is a group that is also a smooth space, such as the group of rotations of space.
Its Lie algebra consists of the velocity vectors of curves through the identity element, the infinitesimal motions.
These vectors form a vector space, and the bracket measures how much two infinitesimal motions fail to commute.
For the group $\mathrm{GL}_n(\R)$ of invertible matrices, the Lie algebra is the space of all $n \times n$ real matrices with bracket $[X, Y] = XY - YX$.
