## Summary

The Beilinson regulator is a map from the rational algebraic K-theory of a variety, split by weight with the [[Adams operations|KQP64TH307]], to its Deligne–Beilinson cohomology.
It generalises the [[Borel regulator|D22EZ3CNAF]] of number fields and the logarithm of the absolute value of units.
Beilinson used it to formulate his conjectures relating special values of L-functions of varieties to K-theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

For a number field, the logarithm of the absolute value turns units into real vectors.
The volume of the resulting lattice enters the formula for the zeta function at $s = 1$.
Beilinson found a construction that does the same for higher K-groups of any smooth variety.
Its target is a cohomology theory built from both the topology of the complex points and their holomorphic differential forms.
The idea is that the volume of the image should control values of L-functions, just as in the classical case.
