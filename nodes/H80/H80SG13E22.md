## Summary

A Zel'dovich pancake is a thin sheet of high density that forms when a region of matter collapses along one axis first.
In the [[Zel'dovich approximation|M8BWRDPA5Y]] a generic overdense region contracts fastest along one direction, so the first structures to form are flattened sheets rather than points.
Pancakes are [[caustics|GMRYG4S3G0]] of the flow of matter, and they explain the sheets, filaments and voids of the cosmic web.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Squeeze a ball of soft dough unevenly: it flattens first in the direction where you push hardest.
Gravity does the same to a lump of matter in the young universe, because a random lump is never perfectly round.
The lump collapses along its shortest axis and becomes a flat pancake, then later collapses into filaments and knots.
In the one-dimensional model $x = q - DA\sin(kq)/k$, the pancake appears at $q = 0$ at the moment $DA = 1$.
