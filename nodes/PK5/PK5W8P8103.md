This topic is a part of [[convex and discrete geometry|SKBWD5C2ZG]].
It covers polyhedra in three-dimensional space as combinatorial and metric objects, with Euler's formula, angle defects, duality, Steinitz's theorem, unfoldings and the named families of polyhedra.
