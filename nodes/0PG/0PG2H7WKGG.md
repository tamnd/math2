## Summary

Divisibility is the relation between two integers $a$ and $b$ that holds when $b$ is an integer multiple of $a$.
Then $a$ is a divisor of $b$, and we write $a \mid b$.
The [[definition of divisibility|096PW7Q014]] gives the precise form.
