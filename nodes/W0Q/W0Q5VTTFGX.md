## Summary

The coefficient of performance (COP) measures how well a refrigerator or a heat pump uses the work put into it.
For a refrigerator it is the heat removed from the cold side per unit of work.
For a heat pump it is the heat delivered to the warm side per unit of work.
Unlike a [[thermal efficiency|Z686C32X8S]], it is usually larger than $1$.
Its largest possible value, reached by a reversible cycle, is fixed by the two temperatures.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A heat pump does not make heat; it moves heat from the cold outdoors into a warm house, using electricity to drive the motor.
Because it moves heat rather than making it, it can deliver three or four units of heat for each unit of electricity.
That ratio is its coefficient of performance.
The smaller the temperature gap it has to pump across, the higher the COP can be.
