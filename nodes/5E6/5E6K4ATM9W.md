## Summary

There are two ways to describe the motion of a fluid.
The Lagrangian specification follows each fluid parcel and records its position in time.
The Eulerian specification records the [[velocity field|0299VCW1TH]] at each fixed point of space and each time.
The two are linked by the [[flow|06738GGWWK]] of the velocity field, and the [[material derivative|CJW50K5HZB]] translates rates of change from one to the other.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a river.
The Lagrangian view is a person in a boat drifting with the water and writing down where the boat is.
The Eulerian view is a person standing on a bridge and measuring the speed of whatever water passes below.
Take a river that flows at the same speed $c$ everywhere.
Its Eulerian velocity is the constant $c$, and the Lagrangian path of a parcel that starts at $a$ is $a + c t$.
