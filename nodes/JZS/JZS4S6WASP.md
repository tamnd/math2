## Summary

This topic is a part of [[matrix theory and multilinear algebra|WMBK7M313V]].
It covers the eigenvalues of Hermitian matrices as extreme values, the inequalities between the eigenvalues of related Hermitian matrices, and the regions of the plane that contain the eigenvalues of any matrix.
