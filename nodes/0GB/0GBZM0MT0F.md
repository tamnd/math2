## Summary

The binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ is the number of ways to choose $k$ elements from an $n$-element set. These numbers are the coefficients in the expansion of $(x + y)^n$. The node [[binomial coefficient|092GEF38YD]] gives the precise definition.
