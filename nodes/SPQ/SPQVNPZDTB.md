## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the model theory and correspondence theory of modal logic, completeness and incompleteness results, and the extensions of modal logic by fixed points, programs, nominals, quantifiers and topological semantics.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Bisimulation|X27BR4YP2Y]]: the zig and zag conditions, and the invariance of modal formulas under bisimulation.
2. [[Hennessy-Milner theorem|~613466ad]]: on image-finite models, modal equivalence is bisimilarity.
3. [[Van Benthem characterization theorem|~bf053ff5]]: modal logic is the bisimulation-invariant fragment of first-order logic.
4. [[Modal correspondence theory|~c216e98c]]: modal formulas as second-order frame conditions, and the first-order ones among them.
5. [[Sahlqvist's theorem|~c09fa1e0]]: Sahlqvist formulas have first-order correspondents and are canonical.
6. [[Goldblatt-Thomason theorem|~4509d116]]: the closure conditions that characterize modally definable elementary frame classes.
7. [[General and descriptive frames|~84a9e3fa]]: frames with admissible sets, and the duals of modal algebras.
8. [[Kripke incompleteness|~481a531d]]: a consistent normal logic that no class of Kripke frames characterizes.
9. [[McKinsey formula|~b9536ed1]]: a modal formula whose frame class is not first-order definable.
10. [[Lattice of normal modal logics|~d43c867f]]: $\mathrm{NExt}(\mathsf{K})$, Makinson's theorem and splittings.
11. [[Quantified modal logic|D2H3S12V7X]]: Kripke models with domains, de re and de dicto, and incompleteness.
12. [[Barcan formula|~10c81368]]: the exchange of $\forall x$ and $\square$, and the frame conditions on domains.
13. [[Modal mu-calculus|MZ0M2KR9S0]]: least and greatest fixed points, and the evaluation game.
14. [[Propositional dynamic logic|VJ78A0Q22R]]: a box for each regular program, Segerberg's axioms and the Fischer-Ladner closure.
15. [[Hybrid logic|~be12fd35]]: nominals, satisfaction operators and the binder, with completeness for pure axioms.
16. [[Topological semantics of modal logic|~4bc1b229]]: the box as interior, and $\mathsf{S4}$ as the logic of all spaces.
17. [[McKinsey-Tarski theorem|~f8b60591]]: $\mathsf{S4}$ is the logic of the real line.
18. [[Gödel-McKinsey-Tarski translation|~15f8f42e]]: a faithful embedding of intuitionistic logic into $\mathsf{S4}$.
19. [[Blok-Esakia theorem|~6f2381b8]]: superintuitionistic logics correspond one to one to normal extensions of $\mathsf{Grz}$.
20. [[Products of modal logics|~65e11534]]: two-dimensional frames, commutativity and the undecidability of three-dimensional products.
21. [[Impossible world|8PAJMAN8DM]]: worlds where logical laws fail, non-normal worlds and logical omniscience.

2. [[Structures, satisfaction and truth|NJDME6F845]]: first-order structures and the satisfaction relation.

4. [[Algebraic logic|QYA4H5Y7VR]] and [[intuitionistic and intermediate logics|38EQ20GR7M]]: Boolean, Heyting and interior algebras, used in rows 7, 18 and 19.
