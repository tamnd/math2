## Summary

This topic is a part of [[order, lattices and universal algebra|V1VVQR12AQ]].
It covers the representation of Boolean algebras by the clopen sets of compact zero-dimensional spaces, the duality between the two categories, and the translation of algebraic notions into topological ones.
