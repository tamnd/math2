## Summary

In graph theory, a **uniquely colorable graph** is a $k$-chromatic [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] that has only one possible (proper) $k$-[[coloring|0NCPXH194Y]] up to [[permutation|05RWFKVTJ7]] of the colors. Equivalently, there is only one way to partition its vertices into $k$ [[independent sets|08ANQHHG3P]] and there is no way to partition them into $k - 1$ independent sets.
