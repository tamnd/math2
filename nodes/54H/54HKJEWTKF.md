## Summary

This topic is a part of [[rings and modules|G7N6BDTEZ2]].
It covers modules over an arbitrary ring as the common setting of abelian groups, vector spaces and ideals: submodules, homomorphisms, quotients, direct sums, free modules, groups of homomorphisms, bimodules, and tensor products over noncommutative rings.
