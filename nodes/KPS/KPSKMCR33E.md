## Summary

This topic is a part of [[matrix theory and multilinear algebra|WMBK7M313V]].
It covers matrices with nonnegative entries, the Perron-Frobenius theorem on their dominant eigenvalue, and the related classes of M-matrices and of maps that preserve a cone.
