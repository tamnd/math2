## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers weak fragments of arithmetic whose provably total functions form complexity classes, their witnessing theorems, and their links to open problems of complexity theory.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Bounded arithmetic|~55a002f3]]: theories of arithmetic with bounded induction, whose provable functions are complexity classes.
2. [[IΔ0 and the linear-time hierarchy|~38fa6f6e]]: the $\Delta_0$-definable sets are the sets in the linear-time hierarchy.
3. [[Parikh's theorem|~5b376b4b]]: if $\mathsf{I}\Delta_0$ proves $\forall x \, \exists y \, \varphi(x, y)$ with $\varphi$ bounded, then a witness $y$ exists below a polynomial in $x$, provably in $\mathsf{I}\Delta_0$.
4. [[Cook's theory PV|~98af8889]]: an equational theory with a symbol for every polynomial-time function.
5. [[Buss's witnessing theorem|~ad7bce8a]]: the $\Sigma^b_1$-definable functions of $S^1_2$ are the polynomial-time functions.
6. [[Krajíček-Pudlák-Takeuti theorem|~2d7d49f7]]: a collapse of the theories gives a collapse of the polynomial hierarchy.
7. [[Paris-Wilkie translation|~c9c38d6d]]: from bounded formulas to families of constant-depth propositional formulas.
8. [[Pigeonhole principles in bounded arithmetic|~55b2a273]]: which weak theories prove which forms of the pigeonhole principle, with open cases.
9. [[Separating the hierarchy of bounded arithmetic|~4ddef1ef]]: the open problem whether the theories $S^i_2$ and $T^i_2$ differ.
10. [[Approximate counting in bounded arithmetic|~a1e5c7e9]]: Jeřábek's theory of approximate sizes from the dual weak pigeonhole principle.

### Prerequisites

1. [[Formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]]: Robinson arithmetic, Peano arithmetic and its fragments by induction.
2. [[Structural proof theory|R10RMQCJQX]]: the sequent calculus and cut elimination, used in the witnessing proofs.
3. [[Natural independence results and fast-growing functions|T6FK1E7GJ1]]: provably total functions of stronger theories, for comparison.
4. [[P|GVT2NKN37K]] and [[NP|QQRA9GBW51]]: the complexity classes that the theories capture.
5. [[Polynomial hierarchy|TDQ7AXGM6P]]: the classes $\Sigma^p_i$ that match the formulas $\Sigma^b_i$.
