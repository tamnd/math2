## Summary

Muller's ratchet is the irreversible build-up of harmful mutations in a finite asexual population without recombination.
The class of individuals with the fewest mutations can be lost by [[genetic drift|AFJ02FJ1KR]], and without recombination or back mutation it can never be rebuilt.
Each such loss is a click of the ratchet, and the clicks lower the fitness of the population step by step.
It is one of the classical arguments for an advantage of sexual reproduction.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a photocopier that sometimes adds a smudge but never removes one.
If you always copy copies, the cleanest copy can get lost by bad luck, and from then on every copy has at least one smudge.
Then the cleanest copy with one smudge can get lost too, and so on.
In an asexual population, harmful mutations play the role of smudges.
Sex can rebuild a clean genome by combining clean parts of two parents, which stops the ratchet.
