## Summary

Spacetime is the four-dimensional continuum of events, with three dimensions of space and one of time, in which physical processes happen.
In relativity it is modelled by a four-dimensional [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]] with a choice of future direction of time.
Its metric determines lengths, durations, the light cones that bound causal influence, and, in general relativity, gravitation.
The flat case is [[Minkowski space|G49G4R1K04]], the spacetime of special relativity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An event is a place together with a moment, such as "here, at noon".
Spacetime is the set of all events.
Relativity showed that space and time cannot be separated in a way that all observers agree on, so they form one geometric object.
A particle traces a curve in spacetime, its world line, and a flash of light spreads out along a cone.
