## Summary

A finite measure is a measure that gives the whole space a finite value.
Every measurable set then has finite measure.
Probability measures are the finite measures of total mass $1$, and every nonzero finite measure is a multiple of a probability measure.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A finite measure is a way of measuring in which the total amount is finite.
Length on the interval $[0, 1]$ is a finite measure, with total $1$.
Length on the whole real line is not, since the line is infinitely long.
Dividing a nonzero finite measure by its total gives a probability measure.
