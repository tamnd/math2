## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers prime and atomic models, the countable models of a complete theory, countable categoricity, the Ryll-Nardzewski theorem, Vaught's never-two theorem, categoricity in power and the Łoś-Vaught test.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Categorical theory|1JJCGNX240]]: one model of a given size, up to isomorphism.
2. [[Łoś-Vaught test|ZR7Y5C235P]]: categoricity in one infinite size gives completeness.
3. [[Theory of dense linear orders|2W4S2H4VYA]]: the first $\omega$-categorical theory.
4. [[Standard examples of categoricity|20QJANNKP7]]: the four patterns of categoricity, with a table.
5. [[Atomic model|HSJ4F9J8SM]]: every tuple realizes an isolated type.
6. [[Prime model|S8W40PYNHX]]: a model that embeds elementarily into all models.
7. [[Countable saturated models|7GN6XF590M]]: they exist exactly for small theories.
8. [[Ryll-Nardzewski theorem|1513J40TKA]]: $\omega$-categoricity by counting types or orbits.
9. [[Omega-categorical theory|A22K1X8GKD]]: examples, structure and finite axiomatizability.
10. [[Vaught's never-two theorem|E5W3RRSY5Z]]: never exactly two countable models.
11. [[Ehrenfeucht's theory|XT2D23G98S]]: exactly three countable models.
12. [[Vaught conjecture|YVBHE2SEE6]]: countably many or continuum many countable models.
13. [[Morley's theorem on countable models|JFG783DEG8]]: at most $\aleph_1$ or exactly $2^{\aleph_0}$.
14. [[Small theory|W6XM5NVNRP]]: countably many types over the empty set.

- [[Back-and-forth methods|N64R1MGDBN]], for the isomorphism arguments between countable models.
- [[Elementary extensions|2RE7GWWXM5]] and the Löwenheim-Skolem theorem.
- [[Basic notions of model theory|AY09KYSW81]]: structures, theories and complete theories.
