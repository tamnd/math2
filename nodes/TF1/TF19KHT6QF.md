## Summary

This topic is a part of [[geometric group theory|8ER1DMV2ED]].
It covers the automorphism and outer automorphism groups of free groups as groups: generators, Whitehead's algorithm, outer space, train tracks, and the analogy with mapping class groups and with the general linear groups over the integers.
