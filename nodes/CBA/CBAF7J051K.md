## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the length of proofs of propositional tautologies in proof systems, the hierarchy of proof systems, and the known lower bounds.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Proof complexity|1Q046J0AW2]]: the measures of proofs and the aims of the field.
2. [[Propositional proof system|3V24CYWDT6]]: the definition of Cook and Reckhow, and p-simulation.
3. [[Cook–Reckhow theorem|YS1T29B00B]]: polynomially bounded systems and the question NP versus coNP.
4. [[Frege system|J2BAR09TPF]]: Hilbert-style systems, and Reckhow's theorem that they are p-equivalent.
5. [[Extended Frege system|5YSE04SHFH]]: abbreviations by new variables.
6. [[Lower bounds for Frege systems|ANSV13M4FS]]: the open problem and its barriers.
7. [[Resolution as a proof system|6JSH907W5R]]: tree-like, regular and dag-like refutations, and SAT solvers.
8. [[Haken's theorem|4PCEYEW3JG]]: the first exponential lower bound for resolution.
9. [[Size-width relation for resolution|GWDGRPAQSM]]: lower bounds for size from lower bounds for width.
10. [[Tseitin formulas|JXTCC6KXNG]]: parity on graphs, hard for resolution on expanders.
11. [[Pigeonhole formulas|RJ571F7FF0]]: the main hard examples, and the systems that refute them.
12. [[Bounded-depth Frege|41QJ1WEMJ3]]: lines of constant depth, and Ajtai's lower bound.
13. [[Cutting planes|KKCX2HQP2K]]: linear inequalities, and Pudlák's lower bound.
14. [[Algebraic proof systems|ZERBQSTZB5]]: Nullstellensatz and polynomial calculus, measured by degree.
15. [[Feasible interpolation|59GJCM8C66]]: circuits from proofs, and lower bounds from circuit lower bounds.
16. [[Automatizability|WBECDG163W]]: finding proofs, and the theorem of Atserias and Müller.

### Prerequisites

- [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: formulas, tautologies, Hilbert systems and resolution.
- [[Theory of computation|52TNMMSXD3]]: polynomial time, NP, coNP and Boolean circuits.
- [[Bounded arithmetic|RA380RWEC0]]: the theories that correspond to the proof systems, for the remarks on logic.
