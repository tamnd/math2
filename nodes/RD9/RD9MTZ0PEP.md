## Summary

T-duality is an equivalence between [[string theories|GM58M1HFY0]] on different backgrounds.
Let one direction of spacetime be curled into a [[circle|060W5CBZP6]] of radius $R$.
Then closed strings give the same physics as on a circle of radius $\alpha'/R$, where $\alpha'$ is the Regge slope.
The duality exchanges the momentum of a string around the circle with the number of times the string winds around it.
It shows that string theory has a minimal length scale of order $\sqrt{\alpha'}$.
In [[superstring theory|N993FCEJY2]] it relates the type IIA and type IIB theories.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A point particle on a circle can only move around it, and its momentum comes in whole multiples of $1/R$.
A closed string can also wrap around the circle a whole number of times, and stretching it costs energy proportional to $R$.
Shrinking the circle makes the momentum states expensive and the wrapped states cheap, and growing it does the opposite.
T-duality says these two pictures match exactly.
The list of states on a circle of radius $R$ is the same as on a circle of radius $\alpha'/R$, with momentum and winding swapped.
So a very small circle cannot be told apart from a very large one.
