## Summary

A [[summability method|8X25Q4XYMN]] is regular when it sums every convergent sequence to its ordinary limit.
Regularity is the basic requirement for a method to count as a generalization of convergence.
For matrix methods it is characterized by the [[Silverman–Toeplitz theorem|S4J7SRPXMZ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A new way of summing series should not change the answer for series that already have a sum.
Regular methods have this property: on convergent series they agree with the ordinary sum, and they only add new values for some divergent series.
Cesàro summation is regular, because the running averages of a convergent sequence tend to the same limit.
