## Example

We solve the system over $\R$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 &= 2, \\ 2x_1 + x_2 + 3x_3 + x_4 &= 5, \\ x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_4 &= -1 \end{aligned}$$
by [[Gauss-Jordan elimination|098D6FQN3N]].

1. The augmented matrix is $\left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 3 & 1 & 5 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 & 3 & -1 \end{array}\right]$.
2. $R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$ gives $(0, -1, 1, -1 \mid 1)$.
3. $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - R_1$ gives $(0, 1, -1, 2 \mid -3)$.
4. $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 + R_2$ gives $(0, 0, 0, 1 \mid -2)$; the forward phase is complete.
5. $R_2 \leftarrow -R_2$ gives $(0, 1, -1, 1 \mid -1)$, so every pivot is $1$.
6. $R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - R_3$ gives $(0, 1, -1, 0 \mid 1)$.
7. $R_1 \leftarrow R_1 - R_3$ gives $(1, 1, 1, 0 \mid 4)$.
8. $R_1 \leftarrow R_1 - R_2$ gives $(1, 0, 2, 0 \mid 3)$.
9. The [[RREF|0WF5AQYTW6]] is $\left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r} 1 & 0 & 2 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & -2 \end{array}\right]$.
10. The [[pivots|0J0BBCMJ5W]] are in columns 1, 2 and 4, so $x_3$ is free; put $x_3 = t$.
11. The rows give $x_1 = 3 - 2t$, $x_2 = 1 + t$ and $x_4 = -2$.

So the solution set is $\set{(3, 1, 0, -2) + t(-2, 1, 1, 0) : t \in \R}$, a line in $\R^4$.

Check, for every $t$:

1. $(3 - 2t) + (1 + t) + t + (-2) = 2$.
2. $2(3 - 2t) + (1 + t) + 3t + (-2) = 5$.
3. $(3 - 2t) + 2(1 + t) + 0 + 3(-2) = -1$.

## Remarks

The [[parametric form|KQT68YPHD1]] has one parameter, because there are $4$ unknowns and $3$ pivots.
The vector $(-2, 1, 1, 0)$ solves the homogeneous system, and $(3, 1, 0, -2)$ is one particular solution.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Three equations in four unknowns usually leave one unknown free.
After the reduction, each equation names one unknown in terms of the free one.
Every choice of the free unknown gives one solution.

### L2 human

1. Do the forward phase to reach echelon form.
2. Scale each pivot row so that the pivot is $1$.
3. Clear the entries above each pivot, from the bottom pivot up.
4. Give each free variable a parameter and read the pivot variables from the rows.
5. Check the answer with the parameter still in it.

The typical use is to find the general solution of an underdetermined system.
A trap: column 3 has nonzero entries in the RREF, but no pivot; it is the column of a free variable.
