## Summary

In number theory and arithmetic geometry, the adelic points of an algebraic group $G$ over a global field $K$ form a topological group denoted $G(\mathbb A_K)$, where $\mathbb A_K$ is the adele ring of $K$. For a linear algebraic group, $G(\mathbb A_K)$ may be described as the restricted product of the local groups $G(K_v)$ over all places $v$ of $K$, with respect to compact [[open|01ZQQDCERP]] [[subgroups|0X1JXT47GA]] $G(\mathcal O_v)$ at [[almost all|N1XSAXCQY3]] non-archimedean places.
