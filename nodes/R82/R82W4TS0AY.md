## Summary

A **powerful number** is a positive integer *m* such that for every [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] *p* dividing *m*, *p*<sup>2</sup> also divides *m*. Equivalently, a powerful number is the product of a square and a cube, that is, a number *m* of the form *m* = *a*<sup>2</sup>*b*<sup>3</sup>, where *a* and *b* are positive integers. Paul Erdős and George Szekeres studied such numbers and Solomon W. Golomb named such numbers *powerful*. Powerful numbers are also known as **squarefull**, **square-full**, or **2-full**. (Not to be confused with the term **squareful**, which refers to numbers that are not [[square-free|004JW7A2N5]].)
