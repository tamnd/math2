## Definition

Let $k > 0$ and $r_c > 0$ be constants and let $\eta_{\mu\nu}$ be the Minkowski metric on $\R^4$.
The *Randall–Sundrum model* RS1 is five-dimensional gravity with negative cosmological constant on $\R^4 \times (S^1 / \Z_2)$, where the orbifold $S^1/\Z_2$ is the interval $0 \le y \le \pi r_c$.
It has two [[branes|DJAATX710N]] with opposite tensions at the fixed points $y = 0$ and $y = \pi r_c$, and the background metric
$$ds^2 = e^{-2k\abs{y}} \eta_{\mu\nu} \, dx^\mu dx^\nu + dy^2.$$
The *RS2 model* is the limit $r_c \to \infty$, with one brane at $y = 0$ and an infinite extra dimension.

## Remarks

Between the branes the metric is a slice of five-dimensional [[anti-de Sitter space|EQX6M75VB1]] of curvature radius $1/k$, which solves the bulk [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]] with negative cosmological constant.
The jumps of $\partial_y$ of the warp factor at the branes are matched by the brane tensions, which must be tuned against the bulk cosmological constant.
Example: a mass parameter $m_0$ on the brane at $y = \pi r_c$ appears to a four-dimensional observer as $m = e^{-k \pi r_c} m_0$; with $k r_c \approx 11$ to $12$ this lowers the Planck scale to the weak scale.
Non-example: the product metric $\eta_{\mu\nu} dx^\mu dx^\nu + dy^2$ on $\R^4 \times S^1$, as in [[Kaluza–Klein theory|E55CCQ3K4S]], is not warped, so it gives no exponential hierarchy.
