## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers paraconsistent logics and dialetheism, free and partial logics, connexive logic, non-monotonic logics, combinations of logics, the logic of natural language, quantum logic and computability logic.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Non-classical logic|~b095f207]]: what makes a logic non-classical, and the main families.
2. [[Explosion|TTKZGJEEK7]]: the principle that a contradiction entails every formula.
3. [[Paraconsistent logic|C7YWCR87QJ]]: logics in which a contradiction does not entail everything.
4. [[Logic of paradox|~26a0362d]]: Priest's three-valued logic with a glut value.
5. [[Dialetheism|3P0FM0AQF3]]: the view that some contradictions are true.
6. [[Da Costa's systems|~3c6ff4aa]]: the hierarchy of logics with well-behaved formulas.
7. [[Jaśkowski's discussive logic|~3a0900b2]]: a paraconsistent logic defined by a translation into S5.
8. [[Adaptive logic|~132437fd]]: logics that apply classical rules except where abnormalities occur.
9. [[Free logic|~5d51b6ee]]: logic without the assumption that every term denotes.
10. [[Partial logic|F5SP7HQFFS]]: logic with undefined terms and truth-value gaps.
11. [[Connexive logic|NQFYY76BSM]]: logics that contain Aristotle's and Boethius' theses.
12. [[Multiple-conclusion logic|3AJTDAP93H]]: consequence relations with sets of conclusions.
13. [[Nonmonotonic logic|~37275fd2]]: reasoning in which new premises can remove conclusions.
14. [[Default logic|~1470d90b]]: Reiter's default rules and their extensions.
15. [[Preferential consequence relation|~5ba6fe6b]]: the KLM rules and their preferential models.
16. [[Combining logics|~d4f6223a]]: fusion, products, fibring and the collapse problem.
17. [[Montague grammar|~9ed593dc]]: the semantics of natural language by typed intensional logic.
18. [[Quantum logic|2E9DMB7A6Q]]: the lattice of closed subspaces of a Hilbert space.
19. [[Computability logic|~f181db4f]]: formulas as interactive computational problems.

### Prerequisites

- [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: classical connectives, valuations and consequence.
- [[Many-valued and fuzzy logics|WZP584JQ06]]: matrices and truth values other than true and false.
- [[Syntax, Kripke semantics and completeness of modal logic|SKY7AQFG1V]]: Kripke models, used in discussive logic and in combined logics.
