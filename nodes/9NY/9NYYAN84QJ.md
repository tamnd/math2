## Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a [[Waldhausen category|X7JG1QEB9Q]] with zero object $0$.
For $n \ge 0$, let $\operatorname{Ar}[n]$ be the poset of pairs $(i, j)$ of integers with $0 \le i \le j \le n$, ordered by $(i, j) \le (i', j')$ when $i \le i'$ and $j \le j'$.
The category $S_n \mathcal{C}$ has as objects the [[functors|KJB47D7Z05]] $A \colon \operatorname{Ar}[n] \to \mathcal{C}$, $(i, j) \mapsto A_{i,j}$, such that:

1. $A_{i,i} = 0$ for $0 \le i \le n$.
2. For $0 \le i \le j \le k \le n$, the map $A_{i,j} \to A_{i,k}$ is a cofibration, and the square formed by $A_{i,j} \to A_{i,k}$, $A_{i,j} \to A_{j,j} = 0$, $A_{i,k} \to A_{j,k}$ and $0 \to A_{j,k}$ is a pushout.

The morphisms of $S_n \mathcal{C}$ are the natural transformations.
A morphism is a weak equivalence when each of its components is a weak equivalence of $\mathcal{C}$; these form the subcategory $wS_n \mathcal{C}$.
An order-preserving map $[m] \to [n]$ induces a functor $\operatorname{Ar}[m] \to \operatorname{Ar}[n]$, and composition with it gives $S_n \mathcal{C} \to S_m \mathcal{C}$.
So $[n] \mapsto wS_n \mathcal{C}$ is a [[simplicial object|WK0A32TZHB]] in categories, the *S-construction* $wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}$.

The *K-theory space* of $\mathcal{C}$ is $\Omega \abs{wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}}$, the [[loop space|2JFF5F3EGF]] of the [[geometric realization|TG6WHHR7CF]] of the nerves, and for $n \ge 0$ the K-groups are $K_n(\mathcal{C}) = \pi_{n+1} \abs{wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}}$.

## Remarks

Clause 2 says that $A_{i,j} \rightarrowtail A_{i,k} \twoheadrightarrow A_{j,k}$ is a cofibration sequence, so $A_{j,k}$ is a chosen quotient $A_{i,k}/A_{i,j}$.
An object of $S_n \mathcal{C}$ is thus a chain $0 = A_{0,0} \rightarrowtail A_{0,1} \rightarrowtail \cdots \rightarrowtail A_{0,n}$ with all its subquotients chosen.
The category $S_0 \mathcal{C}$ has one object, $S_1 \mathcal{C}$ is equivalent to $\mathcal{C}$, and $S_2 \mathcal{C}$ is the category of cofibration sequences.

Example: $\pi_1 \abs{wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}} = K_0(\mathcal{C})$ is the abelian group generated by the objects of $\mathcal{C}$, with $[A] = [A']$ for each weak equivalence $A \xrightarrow{\sim} A'$ and $[B] = [A] + [B/A]$ for each cofibration sequence.
Each object $A$ gives a $1$-simplex, a loop, and each cofibration sequence gives a $2$-simplex whose three edges are $A$, $B$ and $B/A$.
For an exact category with the isomorphisms as weak equivalences, Waldhausen showed that $\abs{wS_\bullet \mathcal{C}}$ is homotopy equivalent to the classifying space of the [[Q-construction|J9XP7CS943]].

Non-example: a functor $A \colon \operatorname{Ar}[1] \to \mathcal{C}$ with $A_{0,0} = 0$, $A_{1,1} \ne 0$ is not an object of $S_1 \mathcal{C}$, since it fails clause 1.
