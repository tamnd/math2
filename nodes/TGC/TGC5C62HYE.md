## Summary

A cyclic coordinate, or ignorable coordinate, of a Lagrangian system is a generalized coordinate on which the Lagrangian does not depend.
The Lagrangian may still depend on the velocity of this coordinate.
The generalized momentum conjugate to a cyclic coordinate is conserved during the motion.
This is the simplest form of the link between symmetries and conservation laws given by [[Noether's theorem|XHPDM5T2JG]].
Cyclic coordinates can be eliminated from the equations, which reduces the number of degrees of freedom.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If moving a system along some coordinate does not change its Lagrangian, that coordinate is called cyclic.
For a planet around the Sun, turning the whole orbit by an angle changes nothing, so the polar angle is cyclic.
The quantity attached to this angle, the angular momentum, then stays constant.
This is why Kepler's second law holds: equal areas are swept in equal times.
