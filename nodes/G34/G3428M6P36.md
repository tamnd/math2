## Summary

The quasispecies model describes a large population of replicating sequences, such as RNA viruses, in which copying errors are frequent.
Its equations combine selection by replication rate with mutation by a matrix of copying probabilities.
At equilibrium the population is a cloud of related sequences, the quasispecies, around the fittest one, rather than a single genotype.
It leads to the notion of an [[error threshold|XQV0KWHJE3]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine many copies of a short text that copy themselves, with a typo now and then.
Faster copiers become more common, but each copy may carry typos, so the population spreads out around the best text.
The stable state is a mixture: the best text together with its near-variants in fixed proportions.
If typos are too frequent, the best text is lost in the cloud.
