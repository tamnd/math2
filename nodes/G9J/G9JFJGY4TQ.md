## Summary

This topic collects the standard methods of [[mathematical proof|0B94TKFQJX]], such as direct proof, proof by contradiction, proof by cases and induction.
A proof node names its methods with the method edge, and each target of that edge is a concept in this topic.
The topic lets a reader find all the proofs that use one method.
