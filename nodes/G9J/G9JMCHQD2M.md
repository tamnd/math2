## Summary

Informally, a **definable real number** is a [[real number|08J2M1JYAG]] that can be uniquely specified by its description. The description may be expressed as a construction or as a formula of a formal language. For example, the positive square root of 2, $\sqrt{2}$, can be defined as the unique positive solution to the equation $x^2 = 2$, and it can be constructed with a compass and straightedge.
