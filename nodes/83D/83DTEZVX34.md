## Summary

In mathematics, the **Barnes–Wall lattice** $BW_{16}$, discovered by Eric Stephen Barnes and G. E. (Tim) Wall, is the 16-dimensional positive-definite even integral lattice of discriminant 2<sup>8</sup> with no norm-2 vectors. It is the sublattice of the Leech lattice fixed by a certain automorphism of order 2, and is analogous to the Coxeter&ndash;Todd lattice.
