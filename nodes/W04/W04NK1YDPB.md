## Summary

This topic is a part of [[low-dimensional topology and knot theory|P9PPWXE9DJ]].
It covers invariants of three-manifolds from counts of flat connections and from Floer theories, such as the Casson and Rokhlin invariants, homology cobordism, and the instanton, monopole and Heegaard Floer homologies.
