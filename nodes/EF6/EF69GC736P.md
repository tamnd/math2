## Summary

The Euler force is the [[fictitious force|ZN2Z2HJY9Z]] that appears in a [[rotating reference frame|HNXK6CJ5B2]] whose [[angular velocity|2FTZ7D8Z0F]] changes in time.
For a particle of mass $m$ at position $r$ it equals $-m\, \dot\omega \times r$, where $\dot\omega$ is the [[angular acceleration|7M4AZSA2MN]] of the frame.
It vanishes when the frame turns at a constant rate.
Together with the [[centrifugal force|BSV03EAVYT]] and the Coriolis force it completes the list of fictitious forces in a rotating frame with a fixed origin.

## Explanations

### L1 human

When a merry-go-round speeds up, a rider is pressed backward, against the direction of turning.
When it slows down, the rider is pressed forward.
This push is the Euler force: it is felt only while the spin rate changes.
If the platform's spin rate grows by $1$ radian per second every second, a $2$ kg mass at $3$ m from the axis feels a sideways force of $2 \cdot 1 \cdot 3 = 6$ newtons.
