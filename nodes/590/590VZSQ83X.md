## Summary

The Steinberg group $St(R)$ of a ring $R$ is the group generated by symbols $x_{ij}(r)$ subject only to the Steinberg relations.
It maps onto the group $E(R)$ generated by elementary matrices, and the kernel of this map is $K_2(R)$.
This map is the universal central extension of $E(R)$, which makes the Steinberg group the natural home of $K_2$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Elementary matrices satisfy some obvious rules, the Steinberg relations.
The Steinberg group is what one gets by keeping only these rules and forgetting all other coincidences between products of elementary matrices.
Each product of generators maps to an actual matrix.
The products that map to the identity matrix, although the rules do not force them to be trivial, are the elements of $K_2(R)$.
