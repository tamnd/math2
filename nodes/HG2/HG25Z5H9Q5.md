## Summary

In birational geometry, the **Cremona group**, named after Luigi Cremona, is the group of birational automorphisms of the $n$-dimensional projective space over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] $k$, also known as **Cremona transformations**. It is denoted by $Cr(\mathbb{P}^n(k))$, $Bir(\mathbb{P}^n(k))$ or $Cr_n(k)$.
