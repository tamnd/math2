## Statement

In mathematics, specifically the field of abstract algebra, **Bergman's Diamond Lemma** (after George Bergman) is a method for confirming whether a given set of monomials of an algebra forms a $k$-basis. It is an extension of Gröbner bases to non-commutative [[rings|0B1261B5ED]]. The proof of the lemma gives rise to an algorithm for obtaining a non-commutative Gröbner basis of the algebra from its defining relations. However, in contrast to Buchberger's algorithm, in the non-commutative case, this algorithm may not terminate.
