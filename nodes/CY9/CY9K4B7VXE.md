## Summary

In mathematics, the **Tate curve** is a curve defined over the ring of formal power series $\mathbb{Z}[[q]]$ with [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] coefficients. Over the open subscheme where *q* is invertible, the Tate curve is an elliptic curve.
