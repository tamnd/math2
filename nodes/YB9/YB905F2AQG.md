## Summary

Diophantine approximation is the study of how well real numbers can be approximated by rational numbers, and the geometry of numbers is the study of lattice points in convex bodies.
A lattice in n-dimensional space is the set of all integer combinations of n linearly independent vectors.
The two parts meet because a good rational approximation is a lattice point in a thin region, and Minkowski's convex body theorem finds such points.
Dirichlet proved his approximation theorem in 1842, Liouville constructed the first transcendental numbers in 1844, and Minkowski founded the geometry of numbers in the 1890s.
This topic collects the Diophantine approximation and geometry of numbers pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Algebraic number theory, analytic number theory, arithmetic geometry, ergodic theory and convex and discrete geometry have their own topics.
