## Summary

This area studies partial differential equations, which relate an unknown function of several variables to its partial derivatives.
The Laplace equation, the heat equation and the wave equation are the model elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic equations, and the classification of equations follows these three types.
Partial differential equations are the language of continuum physics, of fluid flow, of electromagnetism, of general relativity and of quantum mechanics, and they are central tools of geometry.
D'Alembert found the wave equation for the vibrating string in 1747, and Fourier solved the heat equation by trigonometric series in the early nineteenth century.
This topic collects the partial differential equations pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Potential theory, numerical methods for differential equations, fluid mechanics, and the calculus of variations and optimal control have their own topics.
