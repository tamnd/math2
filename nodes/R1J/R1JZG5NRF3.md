## Summary

In mathematics, a **twisted polynomial** is a polynomial over a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] of [[characteristic|0F6ES3BRYP]] $p$ in the variable $\tau$ representing the Frobenius map $x\mapsto x^p$. In contrast to normal polynomials, multiplication of these polynomials is not commutative, but satisfies the commutation rule
