## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|KKBKTW8YB8]].
It covers ideals and rings described by combinatorial data: monomial and Stanley-Reisner ideals and their resolutions, toric and binomial ideals, affine semigroup rings, rings from posets and graphs, lex ideals and cluster algebras.
