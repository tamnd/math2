## Summary

This topic is a part of [[manifolds and differential topology|15PK587CVP]].
It covers the theory of topological manifolds that are not smooth or PL, with the Schoenflies problem, the annulus theorem, Kirby-Siebenmann theory, wild embeddings and decomposition space theory.
