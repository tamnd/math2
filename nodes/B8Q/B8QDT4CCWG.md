## Summary

A SIC-POVM, or symmetric informationally complete POVM, is a measurement on $\C^d$ built from $d^2$ unit vectors with equal pairwise overlaps.
Its outcome probabilities determine the quantum state completely.
It is the most symmetric such measurement, and it is used in state tomography and in the foundations of quantum theory.
Whether one exists in every dimension is [[Zauner's conjecture|446JHJA85V]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A [[POVM|33BRW6TMY4]] is the most general kind of quantum measurement.
A SIC-POVM uses $d^2$ outcomes, the smallest number from which the state can be rebuilt.
Its vectors are spread out as evenly as possible: every pair makes the same angle.
For a qubit the four vectors point to the corners of a regular tetrahedron inscribed in the [[Bloch sphere|A4Q88T5695]].
