## Summary

Piezoelectricity is the coupling, in certain crystals and ceramics, between mechanical stress and electric polarization.
In the direct effect, a stress produces an electric charge on the surfaces; in the converse effect, an applied electric field produces a strain.
In the linear theory the coupling is a third-order tensor, which vanishes in every crystal with a centre of symmetry.
Piezoelectric materials such as quartz and lead zirconate titanate are used in sensors, actuators, ultrasound transducers and quartz clocks.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Squeeze a quartz crystal and a small voltage appears across it.
Put a voltage across it and it changes shape very slightly.
A gas lighter uses the first effect: a spring hammer hits a crystal and the voltage makes a spark.
A quartz watch uses the second: an electric signal makes a tiny crystal vibrate at a very steady frequency.
