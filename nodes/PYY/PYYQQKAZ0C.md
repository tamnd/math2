## Summary

The spinor bundle of a spin manifold is the complex [[vector bundle|P4DZM975BV]] associated with its [[spin structure|RSPZ9TCFQ8]] and the spin representation of the [[spin group|JJSXXJQGEH]].
Its sections are the spinor fields.
It carries a Hermitian metric, a connection induced by the Levi-Civita connection and a Clifford multiplication by tangent vectors, and these define the Dirac operator.

## Explanations

### L1 human

On a spin manifold, each point has a small complex vector space of spinors, and these spaces fit together into a bundle.
A tangent vector acts on spinors, and acting twice by a unit vector gives minus the identity.
On flat space $\R^4$ the spinor bundle is the trivial bundle with fibre $\C^4$, matching the four complex components of a spinor in Dirac's equation for the electron.
