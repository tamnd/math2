## Summary

The field of fractions of an integral domain is the smallest field that contains it.
Its elements are the fractions $a/b$ with $b \neq 0$.
The rational numbers are the field of fractions of the integers.
The [[definition of the field of fractions|0VF11ZTTMD]] gives the construction.
