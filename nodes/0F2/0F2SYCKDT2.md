## Summary

An equilibrium point of a dynamical system is Lyapunov stable if all solutions that start near it stay near it for all future time.
The theory goes back to Aleksandr Lyapunov. See the [[definition|092S6VBECQ]] and the stronger notion of [[asymptotic stability|05Y1JAM3Y5]].
