## Summary

This topic is a part of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]].
It covers subgroups, the subgroup generated by a set, the lattice of subgroups, and the structure of cyclic groups.
A subgroup is a subset of a group that is a group under the same operation.
The subgroups generated by one element are the cyclic subgroups, and the groups generated by one element are the cyclic groups.
Every cyclic group is isomorphic to the integers or to the integers modulo $n$, and its subgroups are known exactly.
The topic ends with the exponent of a group, cyclic subgroups of the units of a field, maximal subgroups and locally cyclic groups.
