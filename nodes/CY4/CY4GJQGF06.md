## Summary

Heat is energy transferred between a system and its surroundings because of a difference of temperature, and not as work or with matter.
It is a [[process function|10J7Y8HKGP]]: a system does not contain heat, it exchanges heat during a process.
The first law of thermodynamics balances heat, [[work|S8H21RWQ86]] and the change of [[internal energy|F6JVQ12X43]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put a cold spoon in hot soup, and energy passes from the soup to the spoon; that energy in transit is heat.
It flows by itself from hot to cold, never the other way round.
You can warm your hands by holding them near a fire, which is heat, or by rubbing them together, which is work.
Both raise the energy of your hands, but they are different ways of transferring it.
