## Summary

Time reversal symmetry is the invariance of the laws of motion under reversing the direction of time.
In quantum mechanics it is implemented by an antiunitary operator $\Theta$, which reverses momenta and spins but keeps positions.
A system is time-reversal symmetric when $\Theta$ commutes with its Hamiltonian.
Then the time reverse of every solution of the Schrödinger equation is again a solution.
For particles of half-integer spin $\Theta^2 = -1$, and this forces the degeneracy of energy levels stated by [[Kramers' theorem|C5P4ZK0P26]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Film a collision of billiard balls and play it backwards: the reversed film also shows a possible motion.
This is time reversal symmetry.
In quantum mechanics, reversing time means flipping the sign of the momentum and taking the complex conjugate of the wave function.
A magnetic field breaks the symmetry, because a charged particle curving one way in the field does not curve back along the reversed path.
