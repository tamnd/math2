## Summary

The **square root of 2** (approximately 1.4142) is the positive [[real number|08J2M1JYAG]] that, when multiplied by itself or squared, equals the number 2. It may be written as $\sqrt{2}$ or $2^{1/2}$. It is an [[algebraic number|0MB4TGMXG6]], and therefore not a [[transcendental number|079EGSD32Y]]. Technically, it should be called the *principal* [[square root|0F7FQS6XY6]] of 2, to distinguish it from the negative number with the same property.
