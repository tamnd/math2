## Statement

For $k \ge 0$, let $C_k$ be the real [[Clifford algebra|A38B1QFK20]] of $\R^k$ with $v^2 = -\abs{v}^2$ for $v \in \R^k$, graded by even and odd elements.
Let $M_k$ be the [[Grothendieck group|5FDMY7PDYY]] of finitely generated $\Z/2$-graded [[Clifford modules|31Q0RDPZ00]] over $C_k$, and let $i^* \colon M_{k+1} \to M_k$ be restriction along the inclusion $C_k \subseteq C_{k+1}$.
Let $W = W^0 \oplus W^1$ be a graded $C_k$-module.
For $x$ in the unit sphere $S^{k-1} \subseteq \R^k$, the map $W^0 \to W^1$, $w \mapsto x \cdot w$, is an isomorphism.
Write $S^k$ as the union of two closed hemispheres, each a copy of $D^k$, meeting in the equator $S^{k-1}$.
Glue the trivial bundle with fibre $W^0$ over one hemisphere to that with fibre $W^1$ over the other, using these maps on the equator.
The class of the resulting real vector bundle minus $\dim W^0$ is an element $\varphi(W) \in \widetilde{KO}(S^k)$.
Then $\varphi$ induces an isomorphism of groups
$$M_k / i^* M_{k+1} \xrightarrow{\cong} \widetilde{KO}(S^k),$$
where $\widetilde{KO}$ is reduced [[real K-theory|P7MN6MQV7P]].
The same construction with complex Clifford algebras gives an isomorphism onto $\tilde{K}(S^k)$, reduced [[complex K-theory|9E4JE4902G]].

## Remarks

The left side is computed by pure algebra from the classification of Clifford algebras.
Each $C_k$ is a matrix algebra over $\R$, $\C$ or the quaternions, or a sum of two such algebras, and the pattern has period $8$ in $k$.
For $k = 1, 2, \ldots, 8$ it is $\Z/2, \Z/2, 0, \Z, 0, 0, 0, \Z$, which matches the values of $\widetilde{KO}(S^k)$ given by Bott periodicity.
So the period $8$ of real K-theory is the period $8$ of Clifford algebras.

Done fibrewise on a vector bundle, the construction produces Thom classes from spinor bundles.
A real vector bundle of rank $8m$ with a [[spin structure|RSPZ9TCFQ8]] gets a Thom class in real K-theory.
A real bundle of even rank with a spin$^c$ structure gets a [[K-orientation|HD9ZST2640]].

Example: for $k = 2$ in the complex case, take the complexified Clifford algebra $\C l_2 \cong M_2(\C)$ acting on $W = \C \oplus \C$, with $x = x_1 + i x_2 \in S^1$ acting from $W^0$ to $W^1$ by multiplication by $x$.
The clutching function is $z \mapsto z$ on $S^1$, which gives a generator of $\tilde{K}(S^2) \cong \Z$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Clifford algebras are algebras generated by square roots of $-1$ that anticommute.
Atiyah, Bott and Shapiro found that modules over these algebras produce vector bundles over spheres in a direct way.
A vector of the sphere acts on the module and serves as a gluing map.
They showed that this gives exactly the K-theory of spheres, once one removes the modules that come from the next Clifford algebra.

### L2 human

The result turns the deep Bott periodicity theorem into the elementary algebraic periodicity of Clifford algebras, at least at the level of the groups.
Its fibrewise form gives Thom classes for spin and spin$^c$ bundles, and so it identifies the symbol of the Dirac operator with the K-theory Thom class.
This is why the Dirac operator plays a central role in the Atiyah–Singer index theorem.
