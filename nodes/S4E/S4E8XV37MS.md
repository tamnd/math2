## Summary

A Bol loop is a loop, that is, a [[quasigroup|ZJD31SPPFP]] with an identity element, that satisfies one of the Bol identities.
A left Bol loop satisfies $a(b(ac)) = (a(ba))c$, and a right Bol loop satisfies the mirror identity $((ca)b)a = c((ab)a)$.
The loops that are both left and right Bol are exactly the [[Moufang loops|H0TJPNCMXD]].
Bol loops are named after Gerrit Bol, who met them in the study of webs in differential geometry.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Bol loop weakens associativity in a one-sided way.
In a left Bol loop, a product of the form $a(b(ac))$, with the same element $a$ used twice, can be regrouped as $(a(ba))c$.
Every group satisfies the identity, and so does every Moufang loop.
There are also Bol loops that are not Moufang, so the class is strictly larger.
