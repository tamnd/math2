## Summary

The Borel σ-algebra of a topological space is the smallest σ-algebra that contains all open sets. Its elements are the Borel sets.
On the real line it is the usual family of events for real random variables. The node [[Borel sigma-algebra|0BVHZXWKCD]] gives the precise definition.
