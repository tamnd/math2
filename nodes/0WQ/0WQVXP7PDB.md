## Summary

The moment-generating function of a real random variable $X$ is the function $t \mapsto E[e^{tX}]$. When it is finite near $0$, its derivatives at $0$ are the moments of $X$.
The node [[moment-generating function|0NNRNGKMFM]] gives the precise definition.
