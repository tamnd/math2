## Summary

In graph theory, the **hypercube graph** $Q_n$ is the edge graph of the $n$-dimensional hypercube, that is, it is the graph formed from the vertices and edges of the hypercube. For instance, the cube graph $Q_3$ is the graph formed by the 8 vertices and 12 edges of a three-dimensional cube. $Q_n$ has $2^n$ vertices, $2^{n-1}n$ edges, and is a [[regular graph|0KA4S8R39M]] with $n$ edges touching each vertex.
