## Summary

Quantum information is information carried by the state of a quantum system, and quantum information theory studies how it can be stored, transmitted and processed.
Its basic objects are [[density matrices|V1JTB52E8C]] for states, [[quantum channels|10VZYJ4552]] for processes and [[POVMs|33BRW6TMY4]] for measurements.
Its basic unit is the qubit, a two-level quantum system.
The theory explains tasks with no classical analogue, such as teleportation, and limits such as the impossibility of copying an unknown state.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Classical information is made of bits, each $0$ or $1$.
Quantum information is made of qubits, which can be in superpositions of $0$ and $1$ and can be entangled with each other.
These features allow new tasks, like sending a quantum state using entanglement and two classical bits.
They also bring new limits: an unknown quantum state cannot be copied, and a measurement disturbs it.
Quantum information theory is the mathematics of what is possible and impossible with such information.
