## Summary

The chemical potential of a species is the change of the energy of a system per unit of that species added.
The other natural variables are held fixed.
It is the intensive variable conjugate to the amount of the species.
Matter flows from regions of higher chemical potential to regions of lower chemical potential.
Equality of chemical potentials is the condition for phase equilibrium and for chemical equilibrium.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Temperature tells which way heat flows, and pressure tells which way a wall moves.
The chemical potential tells which way particles move.
If water vapour in a room has a higher chemical potential than the liquid in a glass, vapour condenses; if lower, the water evaporates.
At equilibrium the two chemical potentials are equal and nothing changes on average.
For an ideal gas the chemical potential grows with the logarithm of the pressure, so particles move from high to low pressure.
