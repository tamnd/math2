## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers quadratic forms over a field and their polar forms, diagonalization, Sylvester's law of inertia, the classification over the real and the complex numbers, and the principal axis theorem with its use for conics and quadric surfaces.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Quadratic form|0RBQS1JJCR]]: homogeneous polynomials of degree $2$ and their matrices.
2. [[Polar form|0HYFWKKY3C]]: the symmetric bilinear form of a quadratic form, and characteristic $2$.
3. [[Diagonalization of quadratic forms|0Z3FEWZ8HA]]: orthogonal bases and Lagrange's method of completing squares.
4. [[Sylvester's law of inertia|0WFED3K3DQ]]: the numbers of positive and negative squares do not change.
5. [[Signature and inertia|07EZA80NEH]]: the triple $(p, q, r)$ and the classification of real forms.
6. [[Classification over C and algebraically closed fields|0CEX9BRXRZ]]: the rank is the only invariant.
7. [[Quadratic forms over finite fields|2HBR7137AD]]: the dimension and the discriminant classify the forms.
8. [[Discriminant of a quadratic form|MZFRXECCMC]]: the determinant modulo nonzero squares.
9. [[Positive definite quadratic form|0RP20HFYM3]]: the tests by eigenvalues, minors and squares.
10. [[Definite, semidefinite and indefinite forms|01RBSSGMY5]]: the five types and the second derivative test.
11. [[Principal axis theorem|0PR5P9Z4KM]]: an orthogonal change of variables gives a sum of $\lambda_i y_i^2$.
12. [[Conics by quadratic forms|1DGT7X9E35]]: rotation and translation of plane conics.
13. [[Quadric surfaces by quadratic forms|F42W4DQHBK]]: surfaces in space by the signs of the eigenvalues.
14. [[Extreme values on unit vectors|YS4HT1EYE5]]: the largest and smallest eigenvalues.
