## Summary

Stochastic dominance is a partial order on the probability distributions of random outcomes.
It says that one gamble is better than another for a whole class of decision makers at once.
First-order dominance means that the first gamble is at least as likely as the second to exceed every threshold.
Then every agent who prefers more to less agrees on the ranking.
Second-order dominance is weaker, and every risk-averse agent who prefers more to less agrees on it.
These orders compare risky prospects without fixing a particular utility function.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Suppose two lottery tickets pay random amounts.
If, for every target amount, the first ticket is at least as likely as the second to pay at least that much, the first ticket dominates.
Anyone who likes more money should then prefer it.
For example, take a ticket paying $1$ or $3$ euros with equal chances, and a ticket paying $0$ or $2$ euros with equal chances.
The first dominates the second, since it is just the second ticket plus one euro.
