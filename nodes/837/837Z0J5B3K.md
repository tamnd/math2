## Summary

This area studies the mathematics of learning from data, with the models of learning, the theorems that bound what a learner can do, and the analysis of the main families of learning methods.
In the basic model, a learner receives a sample of labelled examples drawn from an unknown distribution and must choose a hypothesis whose expected loss on new examples is small.
Machine learning methods are now in use across science and industry, and their mathematics joins probability, statistics, optimization, approximation theory and the theory of computation.
Vapnik and Chervonenkis introduced their measure of the capacity of a class of sets around 1970, and Valiant defined probably approximately correct learning in 1984.
This topic collects the mathematics of machine learning pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Statistical inference, statistical models and methods, and optimization have their own topics.
