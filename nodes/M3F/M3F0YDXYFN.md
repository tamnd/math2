## Summary

Capacitance measures how much electric charge a conductor holds per unit of [[electric potential|SAEDXNM3Y1]].
For an isolated conductor it is the ratio of its charge to its potential.
For a capacitor it is the charge on one plate divided by the voltage between the plates.
It depends only on the shape and position of the conductors and on the medium around them.
In potential theory it corresponds to the Newtonian [[capacity of a set|4YZVFGJVSZ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A conductor is like a container for charge, and capacitance says how big the container is.
Push more charge onto it and its potential rises in proportion; the capacitance is the constant of proportion.
A larger metal sphere holds more charge at the same voltage than a smaller one.
Two plates close together hold a lot of charge at a small voltage, which is why capacitors are built that way.
