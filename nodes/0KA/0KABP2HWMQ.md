## Summary

The upper Darboux integral of a bounded function is the infimum of its upper Darboux sums. The lower one is the supremum of its lower Darboux sums. A function is integrable when the two agree. The node [[upper and lower Darboux integrals|09FQT057T7]] gives the precise definition.
