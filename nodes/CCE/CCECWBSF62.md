## Summary

In number theory, a **congruum** (plural *congrua*) is the difference between successive square numbers in an arithmetic progression of three squares. The **congruum problem** is the problem of finding squares in arithmetic progression and their associated congrua. It can be formalized as a [[Diophantine equation|08B1RTW7VZ]].
