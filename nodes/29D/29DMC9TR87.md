## Statement

Consider the quantized electromagnetic field between two parallel, perfectly conducting plates at distance $a$, in the limit of large plate area $A$.
Define the vacuum energy as the regularized sum of the zero-point energies $\tfrac12\hbar\omega$ of all modes, minus the same sum without the plates.
Then the energy per unit area is
$$\frac{E}{A} = -\frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720\, a^3}.$$
So the plates attract each other with the pressure $\pi^2\hbar c/(240\, a^4)$.

## Remarks

The idea of the computation: between the plates, the momentum normal to the plates takes only the values $n\pi/a$.
The sum $\sum_n \tfrac12\hbar\omega_n$ diverges, but its dependence on $a$ is finite after a cutoff, and the limit does not depend on the choice of a smooth cutoff.
With [[zeta function regularization|VJCJYXGTDY]], the computation uses the value $\zeta(-3) = 1/120$.

Example: a one-dimensional massless field on an interval of length $a$ with vanishing boundary values has modes $\omega_n = n\pi c/a$.
Zeta regularization gives $\tfrac12\sum_{n \ge 1} \hbar n\pi c/a = \frac{\hbar\pi c}{2a}\zeta(-1) = -\frac{\hbar\pi c}{24a}$, using $\zeta(-1) = -1/12$.

The result depends on the boundary conditions.
Real metals are not perfect conductors at high frequencies, which gives corrections that the Lifshitz theory describes.
The force has been measured, and it agrees with the prediction.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Even empty space has a quantum field with a lowest energy.
Two metal plates placed close together restrict which waves of the field can exist between them.
This changes the energy of empty space, and the change pulls the plates together.
The force is tiny, but measurable at distances below a micrometre.

### L2 human

The Casimir effect is the standard example of how vacuum energy differences, rather than the divergent vacuum energy itself, have physical meaning.
It is a test case for regularization methods, since zeta regularization, exponential cutoffs and point splitting give the same finite answer.
Similar computations give Casimir energies in quantum field theory on compact spaces and in string theory.
