## Summary

The principle of relativity requires that the laws of physics have the same form in every inertial frame of reference.
It turns a physical idea into a symmetry requirement: the equations of a theory must be invariant under a group of changes of frame.
With the Galilean group it gives Newtonian mechanics; with the Lorentz group it gives special relativity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a passenger in a smooth train with closed windows.
A ball dropped in the train falls straight down, just as on the platform, so no experiment inside tells whether the train moves.
The principle of relativity says this holds for all laws of physics, not only for falling balls.
So only relative motion has a meaning, not absolute motion.
