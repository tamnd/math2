## Summary

A Bernoulli equation is a first-order equation $y' + P(x) y = Q(x) y^n$.
The substitution $v = y^{1-n}$ changes it into a linear equation. See the [[definition|0ZB84QCDKR]].

## Remarks

Jacob Bernoulli discussed the equation in 1695. Leibniz published a solution in the same year.
