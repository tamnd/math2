## Statement

In number theory, **Rosser's theorem** states that the $n$th [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] is greater than $n \log n$, where $\log$ is the [[natural logarithm|0044CPHJBV]] function. It was published by J. Barkley Rosser in 1939.
