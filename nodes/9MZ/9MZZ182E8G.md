## Definition

Let $N \ge 1$, and let $\mathcal{A}_N$ be the set of normalised antisymmetric functions $\Psi$ in $L^2((\R^3 \times \set{\uparrow, \downarrow})^N)$ with finite kinetic energy.
These are the states of $N$ electrons, [[identical particles|Y64G7W4BEC]] that are fermions.
The *density* of $\Psi \in \mathcal{A}_N$ is
$$\rho_\Psi(x) = N \sum_{\sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_N} \int_{\R^{3(N-1)}} \abs{\Psi(x, \sigma_1, x_2, \sigma_2, \ldots, x_N, \sigma_N)}^2 \, dx_2 \cdots dx_N,$$
so that $\rho_\Psi \ge 0$ and $\int_{\R^3} \rho_\Psi = N$.
In atomic units, let $T = -\frac12 \sum_i \Delta_i$ and $W = \sum_{i < j} \abs{x_i - x_j}^{-1}$.
The *Levy–Lieb functional* of a density $\rho$ is
$$F[\rho] = \inf \set{ \ip{\Psi}{(T + W)\Psi} : \Psi \in \mathcal{A}_N, \ \rho_\Psi = \rho }.$$
For an external potential $v$, let $E_0(v)$ be the ground state energy of the [[Hamiltonian|VXDEQB46GV]] $H_v = T + W + \sum_i v(x_i)$.
*Density functional theory* is the computation of $E_0(v)$ through the identity
$$E_0(v) = \inf_{\rho} \left( F[\rho] + \int_{\R^3} v \rho \right).$$
Here $\rho$ runs over the densities of the elements of $\mathcal{A}_N$.

## Remarks

The identity holds because the infimum over $\Psi$ in the [[variational method|EYARCVGB0V]] can be split into an infimum over $\Psi$ with given density, then over the densities, and $\ip{\Psi}{\sum_i v(x_i)\Psi} = \int v \rho_\Psi$.
The densities of the elements of $\mathcal{A}_N$ are exactly the functions $\rho \ge 0$ with $\int \rho = N$ and $\sqrt{\rho} \in H^1(\R^3)$.
The functional $F$ does not depend on $v$; it is universal but not known in closed form.
In the Kohn–Sham scheme, $F[\rho]$ is split into three parts.
They are the kinetic energy of non-interacting electrons with density $\rho$, the classical Coulomb energy $\frac12 \iint \frac{\rho(x)\rho(y)}{\abs{x - y}}\, dx\, dy$, and a remainder.
The remainder is the exchange-correlation energy, and it is the part that is approximated.

Example: for $N = 1$ there is no interaction, and $F[\rho] = \frac12 \int \abs{\nabla \sqrt{\rho}}^2$, the von Weizsäcker functional, attained by $\Psi = \sqrt{\rho}$ (with a fixed spin).

Non-example: a function $\rho \ge 0$ with $\int \rho \ne N$ is not the density of any $N$-electron state, so $F[\rho]$ is the infimum of the empty set and $\rho$ plays no role.
