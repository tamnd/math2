## Definition

Let $X \subseteq \R^n$ be a [[compact|0R6EWFKRCF]] [[convex set|02AS45T3H9]] and let $f \colon \R^n \to \R$ be a [[convex function|07Q7CNM5WF]].
Consider the problem of minimizing $f$ over $X$, and let $X^*$ be its set of minimizers.
A vector $g \in \R^n$ is a *subgradient* of $f$ at $x$ if $f(z) \ge f(x) + g^T (z - x)$ for all $z \in \R^n$.
A *cutting-plane method* keeps a convex set $P_k \supseteq X^*$, starting from $P_0 = X$, and for $k \ge 0$:

1. chooses a query point $x_k$ by a rule that defines the particular method;
2. obtains a nonzero vector $g_k$ such that $g_k^T (z - x_k) \le 0$ for every $z \in X^*$, called a *cut*;
3. sets $P_{k+1} = P_k \cap \set{z \in \R^n : g_k^T (z - x_k) \le 0}$, or a convex set that contains it.

## Remarks

A nonzero [[subgradient|9C5WXYYQEK]] $g$ of $f$ at $x_k$ is a cut.
Indeed, if $g^T (z - x_k) > 0$, then $f(z) > f(x_k)$, so $z$ is not a minimizer when $x_k \in X$.
If $x_k \notin X$, a hyperplane that separates $x_k$ from $X$ gives a cut.

Example: *Kelley's method* uses subgradients $g_0, \dots, g_k$ at $x_0, \dots, x_k$ and the model $\hat f_k(x) = \max_{i \le k} \left( f(x_i) + g_i^T (x - x_i) \right)$.
It takes $x_{k+1}$ as a minimizer of $\hat f_k$ over $X$, which is a linear program when $X$ is a polyhedron.
Since $\hat f_k \le f$, the value $\min_X \hat f_k$ is a lower bound for $\min_X f$.

Other choices of $x_k$ in clause 1 are the center of gravity of $P_k$ and the center of an ellipsoid containing $P_k$, the ellipsoid method.

Non-example: the vector $-g$, for a nonzero subgradient $g$ at $x_k \in X$, is not a cut in general.
For $f(x) = x^2$ on $X = [-1, 1]$ and $x_k = 1$, one has $g = 2$, and $-g (0 - 1) = 2 > 0$ for the minimizer $z = 0$.
