## Example

Let $P = \set{p, q}$ and $T = \set{p}$, and let $\mathbf{L}_T$ be the [[Lindenbaum-Tarski algebra|MGCVQXXQJH]] of $T$.
We show that $\mathbf{L}_T$ has exactly four elements: $[\bot]$, $[q]$, $[\neg q]$, $[\top]$.

1. By the [[deduction theorem|9X08XK5RV2]] and MP, $\set{p} \vdash \alpha$ iff $\vdash p \to \alpha$.
2. By [[the algebraic completeness theorem|J2GTJ8FKF2]], $\vdash p \to \alpha$ iff $\alpha$ is true in every row where $p$ is true.
3. So $\varphi \sim_T \psi$ iff $\varphi$ and $\psi$ agree in the two rows $(p, q) = (1, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$.
4. A pair of values in these two rows is one of $11$, $10$, $01$, $00$.
5. The formulas $\top$, $q$, $\neg q$, $\bot$ realize these four pairs, in this order.
6. So there are exactly four classes, and $[p] = [\top] = 1$.
7. The order is $0 < [q], [\neg q] < 1$, with $[q] \land [\neg q] = 0$.

So $\mathbf{L}_T$ is the four-element Boolean algebra with atoms $[q]$ and $[\neg q]$.

## Remarks

1. Adding $p$ to the theory merges the sixteen classes of [[the empty theory|A7CTMV6M39]] in groups of four.
   The quotient map sends $[\varphi]_\emptyset$ to $[\varphi]_T$, and it is a homomorphism onto $\mathbf{L}_T$.
2. With $T = \set{p \lor q}$, three rows make $p \lor q$ true, so there are $2^3 = 8$ classes.
3. With $T = \set{p, \neg p}$, every formula is derivable, all classes coincide, and $\mathbf{L}_T$ has one element.
   This is the case where $T$ is inconsistent.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Here we accept $p$ as true from the start.
Then only the rows of the truth table with $p$ true matter.
There are two such rows, so a formula gives a pattern of two answers.
There are four patterns, so there are four boxes.

### L2 human

1. Take $\varphi = p \land q$ and $\psi = q$.
2. In the row $(1, 1)$ both are true, and in the row $(1, 0)$ both are false.
3. So $[p \land q] = [q]$ in $\mathbf{L}_T$.
4. In the empty theory they differ, since at the row $(0, 1)$ the first is false and the second is true.

The typical use: the algebra of a theory is the algebra of the empty theory with the axioms set equal to $1$.
The trap: the elements of $\mathbf{L}_T$ are classes for $\sim_T$, not for $\sim_\emptyset$.
