## Summary

The Nusselt number $\mathrm{Nu} = h L / k$ is the [[dimensionless number|T3R5FWV91P]] that measures how much [[convection|RK24AYNG4Z]] improves heat transfer over pure conduction through a fluid layer.
Here $h$ is the [[heat transfer coefficient|J821AD6YTE]] and $k$ is the conductivity of the fluid.
Results on convection are usually stated as formulas for $\mathrm{Nu}$ in terms of the [[Reynolds|JH9EJ0HA0R]], [[Prandtl|C4KSBMR413]], [[Grashof|VVCSRXKQWR]] or [[Rayleigh|CEKBV27MP0]] numbers.
The [[Dittus–Boelter equation|5X9Q3AABF3]] is one such formula.

## Explanations

### L1 human

If a fluid layer did not move, heat would cross it only by conduction.
Stirring the fluid carries heat across much faster.
The Nusselt number says how many times faster.
A value of $1$ means no gain from the motion, and values in the hundreds are common in turbulent flows.
