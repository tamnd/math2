## Summary

A link is a finite collection of disjoint closed curves in three-dimensional space, considered up to deformation of the space.
Each curve is a component, and a link with one component is a [[knot|QYEAZTYGPT]].
Links show phenomena that single knots cannot, such as two components that cannot be pulled apart although each is unknotted.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take several closed loops of string, possibly tangled together.
The [[Hopf link|JMWHKWD7EX]] is two rings linked like the links of a chain.
The [[Borromean rings|WX1Z2F3DCB]] are three rings that hold together although no two of them are linked.
Two links are the same if one can be moved to the other without cutting any string.
