## Summary

A polycyclic group is a group with a subnormal series whose factors are all cyclic.
Equivalently, it is a solvable group in which every subgroup is finitely generated.
Polycyclic groups form a well-behaved class of infinite groups: they are finitely presented, and many algorithmic questions about them can be decided.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A polycyclic group is built by stacking cyclic groups one on top of another.
Each layer is generated by a single element, so the whole group is described by finitely many elements and the way they act on each other.
The group $\Z^2$ is polycyclic: the series $0 \le \Z \times 0 \le \Z^2$ has factors $\Z$ and $\Z$.
Every finite solvable group is polycyclic, but the additive group of the rational numbers is not, since it is not finitely generated.
