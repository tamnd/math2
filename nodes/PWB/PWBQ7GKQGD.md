## Summary

A life table records, age by age, how many members of a starting group are expected to be alive.
It also records how many of them die before the next birthday.
From it one reads death probabilities, survival probabilities and life expectancies.
It is the discrete form of the [[survival function|QTM1HZM53Q]] of a lifetime.
It is the basic tool for pricing life insurance and annuities, and a basic tool of demography.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Start with $100000$ newborns and follow them through life on paper.
The table lists how many are left at each birthday: maybe $99500$ at age $1$, $99000$ at age $20$, and so on.
The drop between two rows is the number of deaths in that year of age.
Dividing that drop by the number alive at the start of the year gives the chance of dying during the year.
Insurers use these numbers to set the price of a life policy.
