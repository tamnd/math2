## Summary

The determinant of a [[finitely generated projective module|HQA9YHDSH5]] $P$ of constant rank $n$ is its top exterior power $\Lambda^n P$.
It is an [[invertible module|HYRK3K52ET]], the algebraic analogue of the determinant line bundle of a vector bundle.
It turns direct sums into tensor products.
When the spectrum of $R$ is connected, it gives a group homomorphism from [[$K_0(R)$|Q14JS2MA1C]] to the [[Picard group|3Z9DCWYXQZ]] of $R$.
For a [[Dedekind domain|7NPY2CN5KB]] this homomorphism detects the ideal class of a projective module.

## Explanations

### L1 human

For a free module $R^n$, the top exterior power is $R$ itself, spanned by $e_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge e_n$; this is where determinants of matrices live.
For a twisted projective module, the top exterior power is a twisted module of rank one.
It keeps one piece of information about the twisting, in the simplest possible form.
Over a Dedekind domain, the module $R \oplus I$ has determinant $I$, which records the ideal class of $I$.
