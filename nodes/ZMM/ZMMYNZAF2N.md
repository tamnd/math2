## Statement

Theorem provers use automated reasoning techniques to determine [[proofs|0B94TKFQJX]] of mathematical theorems. They may also be used to verify existing proofs. In addition to academic use, typical applications of theorem provers include verification of the correctness of integrated circuits, software programs, engineering designs, etc.
