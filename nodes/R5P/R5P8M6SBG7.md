## Summary

Consider the [[plane waves|NM024G378A]] that solve a linear wave equation.
A dispersion relation is the relation between their angular frequency $\omega$ and their wave vector $k$.
It determines how waves of different wavelengths travel: their phase velocity and their group velocity.
A medium is called dispersive when waves of different frequencies travel at different speeds, so that a pulse spreads out.
For light in vacuum the relation is $\omega = c \abs{k}$, and in a medium it is $\abs{k} = n(\omega) \omega / c$ with refractive index $n$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Try a wave of the form $\cos(kx - \omega t)$ in a wave equation.
It is a solution only for special pairs of $k$ and $\omega$, and the rule that picks these pairs is the dispersion relation.
For a guitar string the rule is $\omega = ck$, so all wavelengths move at the same speed $c$.
In glass the speed of light depends on its colour, which is why a prism splits white light into a rainbow.
