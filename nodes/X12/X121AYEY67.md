## Summary

Infinitesimal strain theory is the approximation of the kinematics of deformation in which displacement gradients are small.
Strain is then described by the linear [[strain tensor|8QS6QMJF5Z]] $\varepsilon = \tfrac12 (\nabla u + (\nabla u)^T)$, and the reference and deformed shapes are not distinguished.
It is the kinematic basis of linear elasticity and of most engineering calculations for stiff materials such as steel and concrete.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A steel bridge under traffic bends by millimetres over tens of metres.
For such small deformations the squares of small quantities are negligible, and every formula becomes linear.
Infinitesimal strain theory makes this simplification systematically.
For example, a bar of length $L$ stretched by $\Delta L$ has strain $\Delta L / L$, with no correction terms.
