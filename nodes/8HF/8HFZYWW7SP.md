## Summary

The crack tip opening displacement, or CTOD, is the distance between the two faces of a crack at its tip.
It is positive because plastic deformation blunts the tip.
It measures the deformation at the crack tip.
It stays meaningful when the plastic zone is too large for the [[stress intensity factor|8D619PN75J]] to apply.
A crack starts to grow when the CTOD reaches a critical value of the material, which makes it a fracture parameter of elastic–plastic [[fracture mechanics|SNRXGRZWR5]].
In linear elastic fracture with small plastic zones, it is proportional to $K_I^2$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In a tough metal, the tip of a crack is not sharp: it is rounded off because the metal yields before it breaks.
The two faces of the crack therefore have a small gap even at the tip.
That gap is the crack tip opening displacement.
The more the metal is loaded, the wider the gap, and the crack starts to grow once the gap reaches a value typical of the metal.
