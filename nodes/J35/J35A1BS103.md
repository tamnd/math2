## Definition

Consider a river reach with inflow $I(t)$ at its upper end, outflow $O(t)$ at its lower end and stored water volume $S(t)$.
Fix a storage constant $K > 0$, with units of time, and a dimensionless weight $X$ with $0 \le X \le 1/2$.
The *Muskingum model* of the reach consists of two equations.

1. Continuity: $\dfrac{dS}{dt} = I - O$.
2. Linear storage: $S = K\big(X I + (1 - X) O\big)$.

The *Muskingum routing method* is the scheme obtained from these equations with a time step $\Delta t$.
It replaces $dS/dt$ by $(S_2 - S_1)/\Delta t$ and $I - O$ by the mean of its values at the two ends of the step.
With $I_1, O_1$ at the start and $I_2, O_2$ at the end of the step, it gives
$$O_2 = C_0 I_2 + C_1 I_1 + C_2 O_1,$$
where, with $d = 2K(1 - X) + \Delta t$,
$$C_0 = \frac{\Delta t - 2KX}{d}, \quad C_1 = \frac{\Delta t + 2KX}{d}, \quad C_2 = \frac{2K(1 - X) - \Delta t}{d}.$$

## Remarks

The coefficients satisfy $C_0 + C_1 + C_2 = 1$, since the numerators add up to $2K(1 - X) + \Delta t = d$.
Example: with $K = 2$ h, $X = 0.2$ and $\Delta t = 1$ h, $d = 4.2$ and $C_0 = 0.2/4.2$, $C_1 = 1.8/4.2$, $C_2 = 2.2/4.2$, which are all positive and add up to $1$.
The coefficients are all non-negative exactly when $2KX \le \Delta t \le 2K(1 - X)$, and this is the usual rule for choosing the time step.
For $X = 0$ the model is a linear reservoir $S = KO$, an [[ordinary differential equation|0NT64JV96A]] $K\,dO/dt = I - O$ whose solution with $I = 0$ is $O(t) = O(0) e^{-t/K}$.
A non-example is a reach with nonlinear storage $S = K O^m$ with $m \ne 1$: it satisfies the [[continuity equation|FD4NR553YR]] but fails the linear storage clause, so it is not a Muskingum model.
