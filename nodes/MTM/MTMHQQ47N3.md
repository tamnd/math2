## Summary

Electromagnetic duality is a symmetry of the source-free [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]].
It exchanges the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] and the [[magnetic field|XRED3C547P]], up to a sign and a factor $c$.
More generally, the equations are invariant under a rotation of the pair $(E, cB)$ by any angle.
With sources the symmetry holds only if [[magnetic monopoles|QQ7HF9X1GQ]] are allowed and their charges are rotated together with the electric ones.
Duality also relates many formulas of electricity to formulas of magnetism, and it has deep analogs in gauge theory and string theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In empty space, electric and magnetic fields play almost the same role in Maxwell's equations.
If one swaps them, with a sign change and a factor of the speed of light, every solution turns into another solution.
For a light wave, this turns the direction of the electric field into the direction of the magnetic field: a wave polarized up and down becomes one polarized sideways.
Energy and the flow of energy do not change under the swap.
