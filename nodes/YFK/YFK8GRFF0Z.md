## Summary

Helioseismology is the study of the interior of the Sun through its oscillations.
The surface of the Sun moves in millions of [[pulsation modes|QW9QPV0HHG]], mainly acoustic p modes with periods near five minutes.
Their measured frequencies are inverted to give the sound speed, the density and the rotation rate as functions of depth.
It is the solar case of [[asteroseismology|PDTE1JAZ8C]], and the most precise one, because individual modes of high degree can be resolved on the solar disk.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Earthquakes send waves through the Earth, and their echoes reveal its inner layers.
The Sun rings all the time, driven by turbulent convection near its surface, and its surface moves up and down by tiny amounts.
Each sound wave travels down to a depth that depends on its pattern, so different waves sample different layers.
From thousands of measured frequencies one can map the inside of the Sun.
For example, helioseismology located the base of the solar convection zone at about $0.71$ of the solar radius and showed how the rotation rate changes with depth and latitude.
