## Summary

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be a smooth hypersurface, let $p \in M$, and let $r$ be a defining function of $M$ at $p$. So $r$ is a smooth real function near $p$ with $M = \{r = 0\}$ near $p$ and $dr \neq 0$ at $p$.

A vector $X_p = \sum_k a_k \, \partial/\partial x_k |_p$ at $p$ is tangent to $M$ when it kills $r$, that is, when $X_p r = \sum_k a_k \, \partial r/\partial x_k (p) = 0$.

The tangent space $T_p M$ is the set of all tangent vectors to $M$ at $p$. It is the subspace of the tangent space of $\mathbb{R}^n$ at $p$ cut out by the one linear condition $X_p r = 0$.
