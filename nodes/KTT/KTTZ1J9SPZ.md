## Summary

A Leontief production function describes production with fixed proportions of inputs.
Output is the minimum of the input amounts, each scaled by the amount needed per unit of output.
Inputs cannot be substituted for one another, so extra amounts of one input are wasted unless the others grow too.
It is the technology behind the [[input–output model|PF15E9SVQV]] and the limit case of no substitution among production functions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of making bicycles: each bicycle needs one frame and two wheels.
With $10$ frames and $14$ wheels, one can build only $\min(10, 14/2) = 7$ bicycles.
Buying more frames does not help until more wheels arrive.
The isoquants, the curves of equal output, are right angles, with their corners on the ray where inputs come in the exact proportion.
