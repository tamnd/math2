## Summary

Natural convection, also called free convection, is [[convective heat transfer|RK24AYNG4Z]] in which the fluid moves only because of buoyancy.
Temperature differences make the density of the fluid uneven, and gravity then lifts the lighter parts and sinks the heavier ones.
It cools electronics without fans, heats rooms from radiators, and drives flows in the atmosphere, the oceans and the Earth's mantle.
The heating of a fluid layer from below, [[Rayleigh–Bénard convection|GKVRHF9E43]], is its model problem.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Air next to a hot radiator warms up, becomes lighter than the air around it, and rises.
Cooler air flows in to take its place, warms in turn, and the cycle continues.
Nobody pushes the air: the motion is made by the heat itself.
This is why the warm air collects near the ceiling of a heated room.
