## Summary

An isochoric process is a process at constant volume.
A fluid then does no pressure-volume work, so all the heat it absorbs goes into its internal energy.
Heating a gas in a sealed rigid container is the standard example.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Put a closed steel can of air on a stove.
The can cannot grow, so the air keeps its volume while it warms.
Since nothing moves, no work is done, and all the heat raises the energy of the air.
Its pressure goes up, which is why heated sealed cans can burst.
In the pressure-volume plane, the process is a vertical line.
