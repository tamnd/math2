## Summary

The view factor $F_{i \to j}$ from a surface $A_i$ to a surface $A_j$ is the fraction of the radiation leaving $A_i$ that reaches $A_j$ directly.
This reading assumes that $A_i$ emits and reflects diffusely.
It depends only on the geometry: the sizes, distance and orientation of the two surfaces.
View factors satisfy the reciprocity rule $A_i F_{i \to j} = A_j F_{j \to i}$ and, in a closed enclosure, sum to $1$ over all target surfaces.
They are the geometric input of every enclosure calculation in [[thermal radiation|S6XR2K4N8G]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Stand in a room and ask how much of your field of view is filled by a hot fireplace.
The view factor makes this precise: it is the share of the heat radiation leaving one surface that lands on another.
A small ball inside a big hollow sphere sends all its radiation to the sphere, so its view factor to the sphere is $1$.
The big sphere sends most of its radiation to itself, and only a small share hits the ball.
