## Summary

**Absorption** is a valid argument form and rule of inference of [[propositional logic|09K9MWD2QQ]]. The rule states that if $P$ implies $Q$, then $P$ implies $P$ and $Q$. The rule makes it possible to introduce [[conjunctions|025YZN3VJW]] to proofs. It is called the law of absorption because the term $Q$ is "absorbed" by the term $P$ in the consequent.
