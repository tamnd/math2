## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical cryptography|BR222BKW7C]].
It covers cryptography in the group of points of an elliptic curve or the Jacobian of a curve over a finite field, the discrete logarithm problem in these groups, and the attacks that rule out weak curves.
