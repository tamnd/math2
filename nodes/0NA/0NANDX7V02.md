## Summary

The harmonic numbers are the sums $1 + 1/2 + \cdots + 1/n$ of the reciprocals of the first positive integers. They grow like the natural logarithm of $n$. The node [[harmonic number|0D2Y6G4C4S]] gives the precise definition.
