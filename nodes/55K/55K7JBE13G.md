## Statement

Let $F$ be a field and let $A$ be a [[matrix|0N1MY1T0W9]] over $F$.
The columns of $A$ are [[linearly independent|0CRMGPAH83]] if and only if each column of $A$ contains a [[pivot|0J0BBCMJ5W]] position.

## Remarks

Equivalently, the columns are dependent exactly when some column has no pivot.
A column without a pivot gives a free variable of the system $Ax = 0$, and then this system has a nonzero solution.
