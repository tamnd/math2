## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers combinatorial statements that are true but not provable in Peano arithmetic or in stronger systems, and the hierarchies of fast-growing functions that measure the strength of theories.
Each page names the theory that does not prove the statement and a stronger theory that proves it.
The functions $f_\alpha$ and $H_\alpha$ measure the growth that makes these statements unprovable.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Goodstein's theorem|XQMNJF241J]]: hereditary base notation, a proof by ordinals below $\varepsilon_0$, and unprovability in $\mathsf{PA}$.
2. [[Kirby-Paris hydra|C028FF4TWM]]: every strategy kills the hydra, and $\mathsf{PA}$ does not prove it.
3. [[Buchholz hydra theorem|9APRFM0G7F]]: labelled hydras, unprovable in $(\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}) + \mathsf{BI}$.
4. [[Strengthened finite Ramsey theorem|4P2BAT6YC3]]: relatively large homogeneous sets, proved from the infinite Ramsey theorem by compactness.
5. [[Paris-Harrington theorem|509ZWA0R26]]: the strengthened finite Ramsey theorem is unprovable in $\mathsf{PA}$, by indicators.
6. [[Kanamori-McAloon theorem|NM5WYBK6BG]]: regressive colourings and min-homogeneous sets, unprovable in $\mathsf{PA}$.
7. [[Friedman's finite form of Kruskal's theorem|12XRGBWH59]]: finite sequences of trees, unprovable in $\mathsf{ATR}_0$.
8. [[Unprovability of the graph minor theorem|WXNTPQGJ38]]: finite forms unprovable in $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.
9. [[Indicators|JWTZ8NVVGK]]: functions that detect cuts in non-standard models, the template for unprovability proofs.
10. [[Provably total function|Z742YPHPNS]]: the functions that a theory proves total, for $\mathsf{I}\Sigma_1$ and $\mathsf{PA}$.
11. [[Fast-growing hierarchy|R6ZC4BJGXV]]: the functions $f_\alpha$, and the bound $f_{\varepsilon_0}$ for $\mathsf{PA}$.
12. [[Hardy hierarchy|X8RKXQASR9]]: the functions $H_\alpha$, with $H_{\omega^\alpha} = f_\alpha$, and the lengths of Goodstein sequences.
13. [[Slow-growing hierarchy|VDF6R9EVJ2]]: the functions $g_\alpha$, and their dependence on the fundamental sequences.
14. [[Beklemishev's worm principle|MHDZGDNEPS]]: worms from provability logic, whose termination is unprovable in $\mathsf{PA}$.
15. [[Concrete mathematical incompleteness|E2H655A7BS]]: the general program, from $\mathsf{PA}$ up to large cardinals.

### Prerequisites

- [[Gödel's incompleteness theorems and related limits|HXHHWY3328]]: the incompleteness theorems and consistency statements.
- [[Ordinal analysis and ordinal notations|XTTKZTYDSD]]: [[epsilon nought|KT50SMH3EC]], Cantor normal form and transfinite induction.
- [[Formal arithmetic and its fragments|QHCRN4JVG0]]: [[Peano arithmetic|MPSCREYC3M]], its fragments and [[non-standard models|1QWN5CV4CG]].
