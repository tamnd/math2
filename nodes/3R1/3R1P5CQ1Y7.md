## Summary

The interaction picture is a description of quantum dynamics between the [[Schrödinger picture|GEHZ2CCH2J]] and the [[Heisenberg picture|TAFA5VK0ST]].
The [[Hamiltonian|VXDEQB46GV]] is split as $H = H_0 + V$, where $H_0$ is a simple part with known dynamics and $V$ is the interaction.
[[Observables|DF5RN297GE]] evolve with $H_0$, and state vectors evolve only under the effect of $V$.
It is the starting point of time-dependent perturbation theory and of the Dyson series in quantum field theory.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Often the motion of a system is easy to describe except for a small extra force.
The interaction picture follows the easy motion exactly, by moving the observables with it.
What is left for the state to do is to respond to the extra force alone, which is slow when the force is weak.
This makes it possible to compute the effect of the extra force step by step, as a series in its strength.
