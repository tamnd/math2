## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers the theorems that match a semantic property of a class of models with a syntactic form of its axioms, and the theorems that change implicit definitions into explicit definitions.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Preservation theorems|T69D2NS1HP]]: the general pattern that links closure of a class under a construction with a syntactic form of its axioms.
2. [[Łoś-Tarski preservation theorem|9RN0KYXNT1]]: preservation under substructures and universal sentences.
3. [[Chang-Łoś-Suszko theorem|RK7NK2P75J]]: unions of chains and $\forall\exists$ sentences.
4. [[Lyndon's homomorphism preservation theorem|8DJSRWME2X]]: surjective homomorphisms and positive sentences.
5. [[Universal class|ZSTEZ3PRT9]] and its [[characterization|JPGDSW2TAP]], with examples from graphs and orders.
6. [[Universal Horn class|9A4CYWXPEA]] and its [[characterization|VCJX55N45K]] by isomorphisms, substructures and reduced products.
7. [[Horn sentences and reduced products|1FF2GRE6VX]]: the sentences preserved under all reduced products.
8. [[Beth definability theorem|RJBDSA2ETP]]: implicit definitions are explicit, and Padoa's method.
9. [[Svenonius's theorem|JNXY3WRYFY]]: definability through automorphisms of models.
10. [[Chang-Makkai theorem|MFR9Z5PPH4]]: definability through the number of automorphic images in saturated models.
11. [[Pseudo-elementary class|YEC1Q3A490]]: classes of reducts, and how interpolation separates them.

### Prerequisites

- [[Elementary extensions and the Löwenheim-Skolem theorems|2RE7GWWXM5]]: elementary chains, diagrams and downward Löwenheim-Skolem.
- [[Ultraproducts and product constructions|HZG1YP26AB]]: filters, reduced products and Łoś's theorem.
- The [[Craig interpolation theorem|E2CBFNAVZY]], which the definability proofs use.
