## Summary

The flattening of an [[ellipse|0VKCCEXBJ1]] or of an ellipsoid of revolution measures how far it is squashed compared with a circle or a sphere.
It is the number $f = (a - b)/a$, where $a$ is the semi-major axis and $b$ the semi-minor axis.
In geodesy it is the standard shape parameter of a [[reference ellipsoid|R9XX7TNJCH]], together with $a$.
For the Earth, $f \approx 1/298.257$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Press a ball from top and bottom and it gets a little lower and a little wider.
The flattening says by what fraction the short radius is shorter than the long one.
A circle has flattening $0$, and a segment squashed to a line has flattening $1$.
For an ellipse with radii $a = 5$ and $b = 4$, the flattening is $(5 - 4)/5 = 1/5$.
The Earth is very close to a sphere: its flattening is about $1/298$.
