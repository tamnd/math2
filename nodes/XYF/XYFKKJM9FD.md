## Summary

The Bondi mass is the mass of an isolated system in general relativity measured at future null infinity, the place reached by outgoing light rays.
It depends on the retarded time $u$, that is, on the cut of null infinity where it is measured.
It was introduced by Bondi, van der Burg and Metzner and by Sachs in their study of gravitational waves.
Its main property is the Bondi mass-loss formula: the Bondi mass never increases, and it decreases exactly when gravitational waves are emitted.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The [[ADM energy|XQNV97Z79Q]] counts all the energy, including the energy of gravitational waves that have left the system long ago.
The Bondi mass instead measures what remains at a given retarded time, as seen by distant observers who receive light signals from the system.
When the system emits gravitational waves, these carry energy away, and the Bondi mass goes down.
This showed, within the full nonlinear theory, that gravitational waves carry energy away.
For the Schwarzschild metric nothing is radiated, and the Bondi mass is the constant $M$.
