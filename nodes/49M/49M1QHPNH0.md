## Summary

In number theory, the **aliquot sum** $s(n)$ of a positive integer $n$ is the sum of all proper divisors of $n$, that is, all divisors of $n$ other than $n$ itself. That is,

$$s(n)=\sum_{{d|n,} \atop {d\ne n}} d \, .$$
