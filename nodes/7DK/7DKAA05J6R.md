## Summary

Heritability is the fraction of the [[variance|0477R9PYAE]] of a trait in a population that is due to genetic differences between individuals.
Broad-sense heritability counts all genetic variance; narrow-sense heritability counts only the additive part, which is what parents pass on to offspring on average.
Narrow-sense heritability predicts the response of a trait to selection through the [[breeder's equation|FQ1EK70S68]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

People differ in height partly because of their genes and partly because of food, health and other conditions.
Heritability asks what share of these differences, in a given population and environment, comes from the genes.
It is a statement about variation in a group, not about one person: a heritability of $0.8$ does not mean that $80\%$ of your height comes from your genes.
It can also change when the environment changes, even if the genes do not.
