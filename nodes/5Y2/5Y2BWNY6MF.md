## Summary

The geopotential is the potential of the Earth's gravity, as felt by a body that rotates with the Earth.
It is the sum of the gravitational potential of the Earth's masses and the potential of the [[centrifugal force|BSV03EAVYT]].
Its gradient is the gravity vector, and its level surfaces are horizontal surfaces, such as the [[geoid|999BYZY0ZG]].
Differences of geopotential measure how much work is needed to lift a unit mass from one level to another.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Water at rest settles so that its surface has the same geopotential everywhere.
So the geopotential says which way is "level" and which way is "down".
It combines two effects: the pull of the Earth's mass and the outward push felt on a spinning planet.
At the equator the push is largest, which is one reason why things weigh slightly less there.
