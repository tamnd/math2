## Summary

An integrate-and-fire model is a [[neuron model|RMWR3MDW0T]] in which the membrane potential obeys a simple differential equation below a threshold.
A spike is an instantaneous event: when the potential reaches the threshold, a spike is recorded and the potential is reset.
The leaky integrate-and-fire model, with a linear leak towards rest, is the most common form.
These models ignore the shape of the spike and keep only its timing.
This makes them cheap to simulate in large networks and easy to analyse.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a bucket with a small hole, filled by a tap.
The water level is the voltage, the tap is the input current and the hole is the leak.
When the water reaches a mark, the bucket is emptied at once, and that moment counts as a spike.
With a strong tap the bucket fills and empties regularly; with a weak tap the leak wins and the mark is never reached.
