## Summary

The infinite alleles model is a mutation model of [[population genetics|CCN582E2C4]] in which every mutation creates an allele that has never existed before.
Alleles are then only "same" or "different", with no notion of how different.
It is the setting of classical results on the number and frequencies of alleles in a sample, such as [[Ewens's sampling formula|ES34NVS9D5]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of each mutation as handing out a brand new colour that nobody has used yet.
A gene copies the colour of its parent, unless a mutation gives it a fresh colour.
Two genes have the same colour only if they inherited it from a common ancestor with no mutation on the way.
For example, in a sample of five genes one might see the colours red, red, blue, red, green: three alleles, one of them carried three times.
