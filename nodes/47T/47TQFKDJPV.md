## Summary

In mathematics, a **singleton** (also known as a **unit set** or **one-point set**) is a [[set|0BSJ7RQ9BW]] with [[exactly one|0QAJHFGZZJ]] element. For example, the set $\{0\}$ is a singleton whose single element is $0$.
