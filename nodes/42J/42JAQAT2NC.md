## Summary

In algebra, the **free product** (**coproduct**) of a family of associative algebras $A_i, i \in I$ over a [[commutative|0310REJJY8]] [[ring|0B1261B5ED]] *R* is the associative algebra over *R* that is, roughly, defined by the generators and the relations of the $A_i$'s. The free product of two algebras *A*, *B* is denoted by *A* ∗ *B*. The notion is a ring-theoretic analog of a free product of groups.
