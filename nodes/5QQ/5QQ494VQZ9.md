## Summary

In algebraic geometry, a **lemniscate** ( or ) is any of several figure-eight or $\infty$-shaped curves. The word comes from the Latin la, meaning "decorated with ribbons", from the Greek grc (λημνίσκος), meaning "ribbon", or which alternatively may refer to the wool from which the ribbons were made.
