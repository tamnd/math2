## Summary

A root of unity is a number whose power with some positive integer exponent is $1$.
In $\mathbb{C}$, the $n$th roots of unity are the $n$ points $e^{2 \pi i k / n}$ on the unit circle. They are the vertices of a regular $n$-gon.
