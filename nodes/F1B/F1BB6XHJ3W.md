## Statement

In mathematics, the **grand Riemann hypothesis** is a generalisation of both the Riemann hypothesis and the generalized Riemann hypothesis. It states that the non-trivial zeros of all automorphic *L*-functions lie on the critical line $1/2 + it$ with $t$ a real number variable and $i$ the [[imaginary unit|0ESCX36MMC]].
