## Summary

A Lie subalgebra of a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] $\mathfrak g$ is a linear subspace that is closed under the Lie bracket.
With the restricted bracket it is a Lie algebra in its own right.
Subalgebras of the matrix Lie algebra $\mathfrak{gl}_n$ give most of the classical examples.
For a Lie group, the connected Lie subgroups correspond to the Lie subalgebras of its Lie algebra.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Inside a Lie algebra, a subalgebra is a smaller space where brackets never lead outside.
Take any two elements of the subspace, bracket them, and the result is again in the subspace.
For example, the upper triangular $n \times n$ matrices form a subalgebra of all $n \times n$ matrices with $[A, B] = AB - BA$.
Indeed the product of two upper triangular matrices is upper triangular, so their commutator is too.
