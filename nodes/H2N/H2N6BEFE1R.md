## Summary

This topic is a part of [[electromagnetism and optics|FW8EASGZZW]].
It covers light in anisotropic and gyrotropic media, with the dielectric tensor, birefringence, the index ellipsoid and wave surfaces, conical refraction, optical activity, and the electro-optic and magneto-optic effects.
