## Summary

In graph theory, the **Möbius ladder** $M_{n}$, for even numbers $n$, is formed from an $n$-[[cycle|0QQ0F12962]] by adding edges (called "rungs") connecting opposite pairs of vertices in the cycle. It is a cubic, circulant graph, so-named because (with the exception of $M_{6}$ (the utility graph $K_{3,3}$), $M_{n}$ has exactly $n/2$ four-cycles which link together by their shared edges to form a topological [[Möbius strip|04AJE0M271]].
