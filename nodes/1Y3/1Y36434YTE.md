## Summary

The heat flux is a vector field that describes the flow of [[heat|CY4GJQGF06]].
At each point and time, it gives the direction of the flow and the heat that crosses a unit area per unit time.
It is measured in watts per square metre.
Laws of heat transfer such as [[Fourier's law|9YJZ1PP2Y8]] are statements about the heat flux.
The [[heat equation|VP2HN86VAG]] comes from balancing the flux through the boundary of a small region against the change of energy inside it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture heat as an invisible fluid that streams through a body.
At each point, an arrow shows where the stream goes, and its length shows how strong it is.
To know how much heat leaves through a window, add up the part of the arrows that points straight through the glass, over the whole window.
For example, let a uniform heat flux of $50$ watts per square metre cross a wall of area $10$ square metres at right angles.
Then $500$ watts of heat pass through the wall.
