## Summary

The thermodynamic square is a mnemonic diagram for the four common [[thermodynamic potentials|MKZW03Q590]] of a closed simple system.
The potentials sit on the sides of a square and the variables $S, V, T, p$ sit at the corners.
From the diagram one can read the natural variables of each potential, its differential and the [[Maxwell relations|MAHDE6JZYT]].
It is attributed to Max Born.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The square is a memory aid, not a theorem.
Each side carries a potential, and its two neighbouring corners are its natural variables.
The corner across the square from a variable gives the coefficient of that variable in the differential.
For example, the internal energy $U$ sits between $S$ and $V$, and across from them sit $T$ and $-p$, which gives $dU = T \, dS - p \, dV$.
