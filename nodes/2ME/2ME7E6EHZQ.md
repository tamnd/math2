## Summary

In mathematics, the **generalized symmetric group** is the wreath product $S(m,n) := C_m \wr S_n$ of the [[cyclic group|0B85Q41RP6]] of [[order|0B85S2V2DR]] *m* and the [[symmetric group|0515A6D2Y1]] of order *n*.
