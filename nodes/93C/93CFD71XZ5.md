## Summary

Stellar parallax is the small yearly shift in the apparent direction of a nearby star caused by the motion of the Earth around the Sun.
The parallax angle is the angle that the radius of the Earth's orbit subtends at the star.
It gives the distance of the star by plain trigonometry, and it defines the parsec.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Hold a finger in front of your face and close one eye, then the other: the finger seems to jump against the background.
The Earth's orbit plays the role of your two eyes, six months apart.
A nearby star seems to move a little against much more distant stars.
The closer the star, the larger the shift, so measuring the shift gives the distance.
Even for the nearest star the shift is less than one arcsecond.
