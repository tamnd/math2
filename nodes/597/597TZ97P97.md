## Example

Take a homogeneous, incompressible, self-gravitating fluid of constant density $\rho$ that fills the ellipsoid $x_1^2/a_1^2 + x_2^2/a_2^2 + x_3^2/a_3^2 \le 1$ with semi-axes $a_1, a_2, a_3 > 0$.
Let the ellipsoid rotate rigidly with angular velocity $\Omega$ about the $x_3$-axis.
In the rotating frame, let the fluid move with the velocity field
$$u = \lambda \left( \frac{a_1}{a_2} x_2, \; -\frac{a_2}{a_1} x_1, \; 0 \right),$$
where $\lambda$ is a real constant.
This flow is tangent to the ellipses $x_1^2/a_1^2 + x_2^2/a_2^2 = \text{const}$, since $\frac{d}{dt}\big(x_1^2/a_1^2 + x_2^2/a_2^2\big) = 2\lambda\big(x_1 x_2/(a_1 a_2) - x_1 x_2/(a_1 a_2)\big) = 0$, so the surface keeps its shape.
It has divergence zero and uniform [[vorticity|B3SXT9TWFA]] $\zeta = -\lambda (a_1^2 + a_2^2)/(a_1 a_2)$ along the $x_3$-axis.
A *Riemann ellipsoid of type S* is such a configuration with suitable $a_1, a_2, a_3$, $\Omega$ and $\zeta$, chosen as follows.
The pressure given by the [[Euler equations|RW0JK9VDZE]] with the gravity of the ellipsoid vanishes on the boundary.
Then the figure is a steady equilibrium.

## Remarks

The family contains the classical figures as special cases.
With $\zeta = 0$ it gives the [[Jacobi ellipsoids|WBSY27NTD1]], which rotate rigidly.
With $\Omega = 0$ it gives the [[Dedekind ellipsoids|49CZM6QZD1]], which are at rest in space but carry internal flow.
With $a_1 = a_2$ and no internal flow it gives the [[Maclaurin spheroids|JVFQZ155S9]].
Riemann also found equilibria in which the rotation and the vorticity are not parallel but lie in a principal plane of the ellipsoid.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A ball of liquid held together by its own gravity can have the shape of an ellipsoid with three different axes.
The shape can turn like a rigid body, and the liquid can also circulate inside it along ellipses.
Riemann ellipsoids are the shapes where both motions happen at once and still balance gravity and pressure.
They are simple models of rapidly spinning stars and of the stability of rotating bodies.
