## Summary

A homogeneous linear system with constant coefficients is a system $x' = A x$ with a constant square matrix $A$.
Its solutions are given by the matrix exponential, and the eigenvalues of $A$ control their behaviour. See the [[definition|0VJF99CZ0Z]].
