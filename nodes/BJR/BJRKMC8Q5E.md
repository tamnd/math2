## Summary

Horndeski's theory is the most general [[scalar–tensor theory|MWX9XPYEYY]] in four dimensions with second-order field equations.
Its Lagrangian is built from the metric, a scalar field and their derivatives.
Gregory Horndeski found it in 1974.
It contains Brans–Dicke theory, $f(R)$ gravity and many models of dark energy as special cases.
This makes it a common framework for testing gravity with cosmology.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Adding a scalar field to gravity can be done in many ways, and most of them lead to equations with too many derivatives, which bring unstable "ghost" modes.
Horndeski's theory lists all the ways that avoid this problem in four dimensions.
It is described by four free functions of the scalar field and of its rate of change.
Choosing these functions gives a specific theory; for example, one choice gives general relativity with an ordinary scalar field.
