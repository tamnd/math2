## Summary

The torque of a force about a point measures how strongly the force tends to turn a body about that point.
It is the [[cross product|0N5N15HWZ7]] of the position of the point where the force acts, relative to the reference point, with the force.
The rate of change of [[angular momentum|7147M98XNR]] equals the total torque, just as the rate of change of momentum equals the total force.
Torque is also called the moment of a force.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Pushing a door near its handle opens it easily, while pushing near the hinges hardly moves it.
Torque captures this: it grows with the force and with the distance from the pivot.
Only the part of the force across the lever arm counts; pushing straight towards the hinge does nothing.
A force of $10$ newtons applied at right angles $0.8$ metres from the hinge gives a torque of $8$ newton metres.
