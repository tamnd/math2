## Summary

The velocity of a moving point is the rate of change of its position with respect to time.
It is a [[vector|0QN3D80AWP]]: it has a direction, the direction of motion, and a size, the speed.
Mathematically, it is the [[derivative|0V1EJCT8G6]] of the position as a function of time.
Velocity is the first notion of kinematics, and acceleration and momentum are built from it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A car that drives $100$ km north in $2$ hours has an average velocity of $50$ km/h to the north.
The velocity at one instant is what this average becomes over shorter and shorter time intervals.
The speedometer shows the speed, the size of the velocity, but not its direction.
Two cars with the same speed going in opposite directions have different velocities.
