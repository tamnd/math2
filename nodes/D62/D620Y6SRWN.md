## Summary

The **square root of 5**, denoted $\sqrt5$, is the positive [[real number|08J2M1JYAG]] that, when multiplied by itself, gives the natural number 5. Along with its conjugate $-\sqrt5$, it solves the quadratic equation $x^2 - 5 = 0$, making it a quadratic integer, a type of [[algebraic number|0MB4TGMXG6]]. $\sqrt5$ is an [[irrational number|080T2TXVQV]], meaning it cannot be written as a fraction of [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]].
