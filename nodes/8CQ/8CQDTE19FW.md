## Summary

An Alfvén wave is a transverse wave of [[ideal MHD|ZSA13P8P81]] in which the fluid and the magnetic field oscillate together across a background field.
Its restoring force is the tension of the magnetic field lines.
It travels along the background field at the Alfvén speed $v_A = B_0 / \sqrt{\mu_0 \rho_0}$, whatever its wavelength, so it is not dispersive.
Alfvén waves carry energy in the solar corona, the solar wind and fusion plasmas.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A plucked guitar string vibrates because it is under tension.
In a conducting fluid, magnetic field lines are under tension too, and the fluid is attached to them.
Shake the fluid sideways and the field lines bend, then pull back, and a wave runs along the field lines.
The wave is faster when the field is stronger and slower when the fluid is heavier, just as for a string.
