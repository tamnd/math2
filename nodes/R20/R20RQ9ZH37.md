## Definition

Let $\Sigma$ be a closed [[thermodynamic system|GYNNJBMTCP]] enclosed by adiabatic walls, which let no heat through.
Assume the following postulate.
For any two equilibrium [[states|TD4C13V3J6]] $A$ and $B$, one of them can be reached from the other by a process in these walls.
The [[work|S8H21RWQ86]] done on $\Sigma$ is the same for every such process.
Write $W_{\mathrm{ad}}(A \to B)$ for this work.
The *internal energy* is the [[state function|T8J5XRYBZZ]] $U$ with $U(B) - U(A) = W_{\mathrm{ad}}(A \to B)$.

## Remarks

If only $A$ can be reached from $B$, set $W_{\mathrm{ad}}(A \to B) = -W_{\mathrm{ad}}(B \to A)$.
The postulate determines $U$ up to an additive constant, fixed by choosing $U$ at one reference state.
For $n$ moles of a monatomic ideal gas at thermodynamic temperature $T$, $U = \tfrac{3}{2} nRT + U_0$, where $R$ is the gas constant.
A ball of gas thrown through the air has extra kinetic energy as a whole.
That bulk kinetic energy is not part of $U$, since it does not depend on the thermodynamic state.
