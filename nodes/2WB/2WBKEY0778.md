## Summary

Parametric programming studies a family of [[optimization problems|NS0Q42Y3FX]] that depend on a parameter.
It describes the optimal value and the optimal solutions as functions of that parameter.
Instead of one answer it gives a map from parameters to answers.
Take linear programs whose right-hand side depends affinely on the parameter.
There the optimal value is a convex piecewise affine function on a convex polyhedron of parameters.
This is the basis of sensitivity analysis and of explicit model predictive control.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A company plans its production, but the price of a raw material is not known yet.
Instead of solving the planning problem once for each possible price, parametric programming solves it for all prices at once.
The answer is a formula, often given piece by piece, that says the best plan and the best profit for each price.
For example, the least $x$ with $x \ge 0$ and $x \ge t$ is $\max(t, 0)$, a function of the parameter $t$ made of two straight pieces.
