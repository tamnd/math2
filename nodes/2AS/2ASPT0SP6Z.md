## Summary

A Bravais lattice is the set of all integer combinations of $n$ linearly independent vectors in $\R^n$.
Physics uses it to describe the translation symmetry of a crystal.
As a set it is a [[lattice|WN9V7WSJKF]] in $\R^n$, and the crystal is obtained by placing the same group of atoms at every lattice point.
Bravais lattices are sorted into types by their symmetry: there are $5$ types in the plane and $14$ in space.
The reciprocal lattice and the Brillouin zone are built from it.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Think of a wallpaper: one motif repeated by shifting it along two directions.
The points where copies of a chosen corner of the motif land form a Bravais lattice.
The square grid $a\Z^2$ of points $(ma, na)$ with $m, n$ integers is the simplest example in the plane.
In a crystal the same thing happens in three directions, and the lattice says which shifts leave the crystal unchanged.
Lattices with the same kind of symmetry, such as all square grids, belong to one Bravais type.
