## Definition

Let $\Lambda \subset \R^n$ be a Bravais lattice with primitive cell $\Omega$ of volume $\abs{\Omega}$, reciprocal lattice $\Lambda^*$ and [[Brillouin zone|EKTERAYFJ2]] $B$.
Let $H = -\Delta + V$ be a periodic Schrödinger operator, and let $E_m$ be a band of its [[band structure|QP0TK2M29V]] that does not meet the other bands.
Choose Bloch functions $\psi_k$ of this band for $k \in \R^n$, measurable in $k$, such that:

1. $\psi_k(x + R) = e^{ik \cdot R} \psi_k(x)$ for all $R \in \Lambda$, and $H\psi_k = E_m(k)\psi_k$;
2. $\int_\Omega \abs{\psi_k(x)}^2 \, dx = 1$;
3. $\psi_{k + G} = \psi_k$ for all $G \in \Lambda^*$.

The *Wannier function* of the band at the lattice site $R \in \Lambda$ is
$$w_R(x) = \frac{\abs{\Omega}}{(2\pi)^n} \int_B e^{-ik \cdot R} \, \psi_k(x) \, dk.$$

## Remarks

Clause 1 gives $w_R(x) = w_0(x - R)$, and the functions $w_R$, $R \in \Lambda$, form an orthonormal basis of the spectral subspace of the band.
The Bloch functions are recovered as $\psi_k(x) = \sum_{R \in \Lambda} e^{ik \cdot R} w_R(x)$.
The choice of phases $\psi_k \mapsto e^{i\varphi(k)}\psi_k$ changes the Wannier functions.
If the phases depend smoothly on $k$, then $w_0$ decays fast; the existence of exponentially decaying Wannier functions is obstructed by a nonzero Chern number of the band.

Example: for the one-band [[tight-binding model|57Z8F1Q74B]] on $\ell^2(\Z)$, the lattice analogue has $\Omega = \set{0}$ and $\psi_k(n) = e^{ikn}$, and
$$w_R(n) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} e^{-ikR} e^{ikn} \, dk = \delta_{nR},$$
the function concentrated on the site $R$.

Non-example: a Bloch function $\psi_k$ itself is not a Wannier function: it has $\abs{\psi_k(x + R)} = \abs{\psi_k(x)}$ for all $R$, so it is not square-integrable on $\R^n$.
