## Statement

In number theory, a **Pierpont prime** is a [[prime number|01826KMG2H]] of the form

$$2^u\cdot 3^v + 1\,$$

for some nonnegative [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]] $u$ and $v$. That is, they are the prime numbers $p$ for which $p - 1$ is 3-smooth. They are named after the mathematician James Pierpont, who used them to characterize the [[regular polygons|01KJJ9A400]] that can be constructed using [[conic sections|00HJCMC2CD]]. The same characterization applies to polygons that can be constructed using ruler, compass, and [[angle trisector|00J3DCCEK5]], or using paper folding.
