## Definition

Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a complex [[semisimple Lie algebra|EY9DN7QAX1]] with [[Cartan subalgebra|BB098JPWGA]] $\mathfrak{h}$.
Choose [[simple roots|CSBM217V1N]] $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_r$, with [[coroots|XV6Q01FVAS]] $h_1, \dots, h_r$.
The *fundamental weights* are the $\omega_1, \dots, \omega_r \in \mathfrak{h}^*$ with $\omega_i(h_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
The *fundamental representations* of $\mathfrak{g}$ are the finite-dimensional [[irreducible|HFW8642GBK]] modules $L(\omega_1), \dots, L(\omega_r)$ of [[highest weight|C5W8BE1DRY]] $\omega_i$.

## Remarks

They exist and are unique up to isomorphism by the [[theorem of the highest weight|J1EFY61K87]].
Example: for $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\C)$ with diagonal $\mathfrak{h}$, $h_j = E_{jj} - E_{j+1,j+1}$, and $\omega_k = \varepsilon_1 + \dots + \varepsilon_k$, where $\varepsilon_i$ reads the $i$-th diagonal entry.
The module $\Lambda^k \C^n$ has highest weight vector $e_1 \wedge \dots \wedge e_k$ of weight $\omega_k$, so $L(\omega_k) \cong \Lambda^k \C^n$.
Non-example: the adjoint representation of $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\C)$ is irreducible of highest weight $\varepsilon_1 - \varepsilon_3 = \omega_1 + \omega_2$, which is not a fundamental weight.
Every dominant integral weight is $\lambda = \sum_i n_i \omega_i$ with $n_i \in \Z_{\ge 0}$.
Then $L(\lambda)$ is the submodule of $\bigotimes_i L(\omega_i)^{\otimes n_i}$ generated by the tensor product of highest weight vectors.
