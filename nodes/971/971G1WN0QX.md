## Summary

Landau–de Gennes theory is the [[Landau theory|XJ4NRM74W6]] of nematic liquid crystals.
Its [[order parameter|XSA203109Z]] is the Q-tensor, a symmetric traceless $3 \times 3$ matrix that records both the preferred direction of the molecules and the degree of alignment.
The free energy is a polynomial in invariants of the Q-tensor plus elastic terms in its gradient.
Because a cubic invariant is allowed, the theory predicts a first-order transition between the isotropic and nematic phases.
It can also describe defects where the director of the [[Oseen–Frank|BYWG3ADRF3]] theory is undefined.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A director only says which way the molecules point, not how well they agree.
The Q-tensor stores both: it is zero when the molecules point every which way, and it is large when they are well aligned.
The theory writes the free energy as a simple polynomial in the Q-tensor, with a coefficient that changes sign as the temperature drops.
At high temperature the minimum is at zero, the isotropic liquid.
At low temperature the minimum jumps to an aligned state, the nematic, and the jump is sudden because of a cubic term.
