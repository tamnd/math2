## Summary

A finitely generated projective module over a ring is a direct summand of a free module $R^n$ of finite rank.
These modules are the building blocks of [[$K_0$ of a ring|Q14JS2MA1C]].
Over a field they are the finite-dimensional vector spaces, and over a local ring they are the free modules of finite rank.
By the [[Serre–Swan theorem|B31RNWHHAY]], over a ring of continuous functions they correspond to vector bundles.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A free module $R^n$ is like a space with coordinates.
A finitely generated projective module is a piece of such a space that splits off cleanly, with a complement.
It looks like a free module locally, but it may be twisted globally, as a vector bundle can be twisted.
Over $R = \Z/6\Z$, the ring splits as $\Z/2\Z \times \Z/3\Z$, and $\Z/2\Z$ is a finitely generated projective module that is not free.
