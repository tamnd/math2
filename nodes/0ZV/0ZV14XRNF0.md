## Summary

A [[differential form|0E3G0GRPZ6]] is closed if its [[exterior derivative|0ZHSTAG8ZY]] is zero, and exact if it is the exterior derivative of another form.
Each exact form is closed. Whether each closed form is exact depends on the shape of the domain, so these notions detect holes in the domain.
