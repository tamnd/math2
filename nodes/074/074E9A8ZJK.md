## Summary

A separable equation is a first-order equation $y' = g(x) h(y)$ whose right side is a product of a function of $x$ and a function of $y$.
One solves it by separation of variables: divide by $h(y)$ and integrate both sides. See the [[definition|0V4E623THY]].
