## Summary

In mathematics, the **Gould polynomials** $G_n$, $n\in\mathbb{N}$, are polynomials introduced by Henry W. Gould. They are defined in terms of the falling factorial by Roman, Steven. (1984). "The Umbral Calculus". 111. Academic Press. isbn:0-12-594380-6. mr:741185
