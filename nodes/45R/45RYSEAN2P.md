## Summary

Donald A. Martin

Martin's axiom for a cardinal κ states that for any partial order *P* satisfying the countable chain condition and any family *D* of dense sets in *P* of cardinality at most κ, there is a filter *F* on *P* such that $F \cap d$ is non-empty for every *d* in *D*

Martin's maximum states that if *D* is a collection of $\aleph_1$ dense subsets of a notion of forcing that preserves stationary subsets of ω<sub>1</sub>, then there is a *D*-generic filter
