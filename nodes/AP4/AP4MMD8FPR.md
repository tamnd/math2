## Summary

A Borel subalgebra of a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] is a maximal [[solvable|W7A2R0GR3T]] subalgebra.
In $\mathfrak{gl}_n(\C)$ the upper triangular matrices form a Borel subalgebra, and every Borel subalgebra is conjugate to it.
For a complex semisimple Lie algebra, a Borel subalgebra is a Cartan subalgebra together with the root spaces of a choice of positive roots.
Borel subalgebras are the starting point of highest weight theory and correspond to Borel subgroups of algebraic groups.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Among all subalgebras that can be put in upper triangular form, take one that is as large as possible.
That is a Borel subalgebra.
In $2 \times 2$ matrices of trace zero, the upper triangular ones, spanned by $\operatorname{diag}(1, -1)$ and the matrix with a single $1$ in the top right corner, form a Borel subalgebra.
Every Borel subalgebra there is the set of trace zero matrices that map some fixed line in $\C^2$ into itself.
