## Summary

Take the [[graph|0JBJYT69ZV]] of a [[differentiable|0HHHDCXZV4]] function of two variables. Its tangent plane at a point is the plane through that point that best approximates the graph nearby.
It is the graph of the linear approximation of the function at the point.
