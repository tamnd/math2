## Statement

Wade and Wade first introduced the categorization of Pythagorean triples by their height, defined as $c - b$, linking 3,4,5 to 5,12,13 and 7,24,25 and so on.

McCullough and Wade extended this approach, which produces all Pythagorean triples when $k > h\sqrt{2}/d$: Write a positive integer $h$ as $pq^{2}$ with $p$ square-free and $q$ positive. Set $d = 2pq$ if $p$ is odd, or $d= pq$ if $p$ is even. For all pairs $(h,k)$ of positive integers, the triples are given by

$$\left(h+dk, dk+\frac{(dk)^2}{2h}, h+dk+\frac{(dk)^2}{2h}\right).$$

The primitive triples occur when $\gcd(h,k) = 1$ and either $h = q^{2}$ with $q$ odd or $h=2q^{2}$.
