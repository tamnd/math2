## Summary

In mathematics, a **polynomial decomposition** expresses a polynomial *f* as the functional composition $g \circ h$ of polynomials *g* and *h*, where *g* and *h* have [[degree|0DPF6WRZW6]] greater than 1; it is an algebraic functional decomposition. Algorithms are known for decomposing univariate polynomials in polynomial time.
