## Summary

A Cauchy surface in a spacetime is a subset that every inextendible timelike curve crosses exactly once.
It plays the role of an instant of time.
Data on it determine the solution of the field equations everywhere, both to the future and to the past.
A spacetime has a Cauchy surface exactly when it is [[globally hyperbolic|PZGHVCQB13]].
Cauchy surfaces are where initial data are posed in the initial value formulation of general relativity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine every possible observer, each following their own path through spacetime from the infinite past to the infinite future.
A Cauchy surface is a slice that each of them passes through once, and only once.
In flat spacetime, the set of all events at time $t = 0$ is such a slice.
Knowing everything on this slice is enough to predict everything that happens, because every observer and every signal must cross it.
