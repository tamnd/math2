## Summary

Added mass is the extra inertia that a body appears to have when it accelerates through a fluid.
To speed up the body, one must also speed up the surrounding fluid, and this costs force.
In ideal potential flow the effect is a symmetric matrix, the added mass tensor.
It depends only on the shape of the body and the density of the fluid.
It matters for ships, submarines, bubbles and anything that moves quickly back and forth in water.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Try to shake a plate quickly under water: it feels much heavier than in air.
The plate has to push water out of the way, and that water must be set moving as well.
The body behaves as if it carried part of the fluid with it.
For a sphere in an ideal fluid, the extra mass is exactly half the mass of the water that the sphere displaces.
