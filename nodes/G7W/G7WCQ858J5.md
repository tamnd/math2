## Statement

In mathematics, the **Hilbert–Speiser theorem** is a result on cyclotomic fields, characterising those with a normal integral basis. More generally, it applies to any finite abelian extension of $\mathbf{Q}$, which by the Kronecker–Weber theorem are isomorphic to subfields of cyclotomic fields.
