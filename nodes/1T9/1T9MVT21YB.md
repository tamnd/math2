## Summary

A transverse mode is a pattern of the electromagnetic field across the direction of propagation.
This pattern keeps its shape as the wave travels.
Modes in waveguides are classified by which field components along the axis vanish: TEM, TE, TM or hybrid.
In laser beams, transverse modes are the Hermite–Gaussian or Laguerre–Gaussian beam profiles.
Each mode has its own propagation constant and, in a hollow guide, its own cutoff frequency.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Look at a cross-section of a waveguide or a laser beam while the wave passes through.
The field there forms a pattern: one bright spot, or two lobes, or a ring, and so on.
A transverse mode is one such pattern that travels down the guide without changing, only shifting in phase.
Any field in the guide can be written as a mix of these modes, like a sound as a mix of pure tones.
