## Summary

The order of a zero $z_0$ of a holomorphic function $f$ is the smallest $m$ with $f^{(m)}(z_0) \neq 0$, see the [[definition|064N002TX4]].
Near such a zero, $f(z) = (z - z_0)^m g(z)$ with $g(z_0) \neq 0$.
