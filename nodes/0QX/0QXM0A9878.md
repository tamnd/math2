## Summary

The Picard–Lindelöf hypotheses on a vector field are continuity in time, a Lipschitz condition in space and a bound near the initial point. They also ask for a short enough time interval.
They are the conditions of the [[Picard–Lindelöf theorem|0P6KRD5B7A]]. See the [[definition|0J7HF2PF1H]].
