## Summary

A linear first-order equation is an equation $y' + p(x) y = q(x)$ with given functions $p$ and $q$.
It is the simplest [[linear differential equation|0QQRP17Z7S]], and an [[integrating factor|07DJAZW32T]] solves it explicitly. See the [[definition|0Z3YKRQZ48]].
