## Summary

In mathematics, the **Pythagoras number** or **reduced height** of a [[field|04C5ZBG1WT]] describes the structure of the set of squares in the field. The Pythagoras number $p(K)$ of a field $K$ is the smallest positive [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] $p$ such that every sum of squares in $K$ is a sum of $p$ squares.
