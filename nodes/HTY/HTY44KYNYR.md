## Statement

In graph theory, **Robbins' theorem**, named after (txt), states that the graphs that have strong orientations are exactly the 2-edge-connected graphs. That is, it is possible to choose a direction for each edge of an undirected graph $G$, turning it into a [[directed graph|0KFYGSHHF5]] that has a path from every vertex to every other vertex, if and only if $G$ is connected and has no [[bridge|0BANA3XYPF]].
