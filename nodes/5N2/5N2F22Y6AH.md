## Statement

Let $G$ be a connected noncompact [[semisimple Lie group|XJS07AJ50Y]] with finite centre, and $c$ its [[Harish-Chandra c-function|AYN9675FTS]].
Let $G = K A N$ be the Iwasawa decomposition used to define $c$, with $\mathfrak a$ the Lie algebra of $A$.
Let $\Sigma^+$ be the positive restricted roots, those occurring in $N$, and $\Sigma_0^+$ the positive roots $\alpha$ such that $\alpha / 2$ is not a root.
For a root $\alpha$ let $m_\alpha$ be its multiplicity, with $m_{2\alpha} = 0$ if $2\alpha$ is not a root.
Let $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ be the form on $\mathfrak a^*_\C$ induced by the Killing form, and $\alpha_0 = \alpha / \langle \alpha, \alpha \rangle$.
Let $\Gamma$ be the [[Gamma function|0H5SYBSED9]].
Then for $\lambda \in \mathfrak a^*_\C$,
$$c(\lambda) = c_0 \prod_{\alpha \in \Sigma_0^+} \frac{2^{-\langle i \lambda, \alpha_0 \rangle} \, \Gamma(\langle i \lambda, \alpha_0 \rangle)}{\Gamma\Big(\tfrac12 \big(\tfrac12 m_\alpha + 1 + \langle i \lambda, \alpha_0 \rangle\big)\Big) \, \Gamma\Big(\tfrac12 \big(\tfrac12 m_\alpha + m_{2\alpha} + \langle i \lambda, \alpha_0 \rangle\big)\Big)},$$
where the constant $c_0$ is fixed by $c(-i \rho) = 1$.

## Remarks

The proof writes $\bar N$ as a product of the subgroups $\bar N_\alpha$ along a reduced decomposition of the longest Weyl group element.
The integral defining $c$ then factors into rank one integrals, $c = \prod_{\alpha \in \Sigma_0^+} c_\alpha$.
Here $c_\alpha$ is the c-function of the rank one subgroup attached to $\alpha$, and each rank one integral is a Beta-type integral that gives the Gamma quotient.

Example: if $G$ is a complex semisimple Lie group, viewed as a real Lie group, then all $m_\alpha = 2$ and $m_{2\alpha} = 0$.
By the duplication formula each factor reduces to a constant times $1 / \langle i \lambda, \alpha_0 \rangle$, and the normalization gives
$$c(\lambda) = \prod_{\alpha \in \Sigma^+} \frac{\langle \rho, \alpha \rangle}{\langle i \lambda, \alpha \rangle}.$$
For $G = \mathrm{SL}(2, \R)$ there is one root with $m_\alpha = 1$, and the formula gives $c(s \rho) = \Gamma(i s / 2) / (\sqrt{\pi} \, \Gamma((1 + i s)/2))$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The c-function is defined by an integral over a nilpotent group, which looks hard to compute.
The formula says that it splits into one simple factor for each root, and each factor is a ratio of Gamma functions.
This makes the Plancherel density of a symmetric space completely explicit.

### L2 human

The formula reduces questions about the [[Plancherel theorem for spherical functions|PHYHP9TW29]] to rank one, where the groups are $\mathrm{SO}(n, 1)$, $\mathrm{SU}(n, 1)$, $\mathrm{Sp}(n, 1)$ and $F_{4(-20)}$, up to covering and centre.
Langlands used a $p$-adic analogue in his work on Eisenstein series, where the factors become quotients of local zeta functions.
