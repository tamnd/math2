## Summary

In mathematics, the (**signed and unsigned**) **Lah numbers** are coefficients expressing rising factorials in terms of falling factorials and vice versa. They were discovered by Ivo Lah in 1954. Explicitly, the unsigned Lah numbers $L(n, k)$ are given by the formula involving the [[binomial coefficient|092GEF38YD]]
