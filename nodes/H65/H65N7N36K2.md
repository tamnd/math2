## Summary

The covariant formulation writes [[classical electromagnetism|A34AF4ZVKM]] in terms of tensors on Minkowski spacetime.
The fields become the [[electromagnetic tensor|WRZJRJS44W]], the potentials become the [[four-potential|BQFCNWFV9Q]], and the sources become the four-current.
In this form the laws look the same in every inertial frame, which makes their invariance under Lorentz transformations evident.
It is also the starting point for electromagnetism in curved spacetime and for gauge theories.

## Explanations

### L1 human

In the usual form, Maxwell's equations treat space and time separately, and they look different to observers who move relative to each other, even though the physics is the same.
The covariant form treats space and time together, as one four-dimensional spacetime.
Then the four Maxwell equations shrink to two short equations, and the Lorentz force law to one.
Their form is the same for every observer moving at constant velocity, which is exactly what special relativity requires.
