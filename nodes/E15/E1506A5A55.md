## Summary

This topic is a part of [[group theory|W9RZSPR6CP]].
It covers the maps between groups that keep the group operation, and the subgroups and quotients that these maps produce.
A group homomorphism has a kernel and an image.
The kernel is a normal subgroup, and each normal subgroup is a kernel.
The cosets of a normal subgroup form a new group, the quotient group.
The first isomorphism theorem says that the quotient of a group by the kernel of a homomorphism is isomorphic to the image.
The second, third and correspondence theorems compare the subgroups and the quotients of a group with those of its quotient groups.
These results are the basic tools to compare two groups and to break a group into smaller pieces.
