## Statement

The **Erdős–Straus conjecture** is an unproven statement in number theory. The conjecture is that, for every [[integer|0C4JS9RF5M]] $n$ that is greater than or equal to 2, there exist positive integers $x$, $y$, and $z$ for which

$$\frac{4}{n}=\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}.$$

In other words, the number $4/n$ can be written as a sum of three positive unit fractions.
