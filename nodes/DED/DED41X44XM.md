## Summary

Mohr's circle is a graphical picture of how the normal and shear [[stress|HXGCGHS070]] on a plane through a point change as the plane is rotated.
In two dimensions, the pairs (normal stress, shear stress) for all orientations lie on one circle.
Its centre is the mean normal stress and its radius is the maximum shear stress.
The circle meets the horizontal axis at the [[principal stresses|M6ZWV4Y29A]], so it gives them, and the maximum shear stress, at a glance.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Take a small square inside a loaded plate and turn it slowly.
The push and the sideways drag on one of its faces change as it turns.
Plot the push horizontally and the drag vertically: the point runs around a circle, twice for each full turn of the square.
For a plate pulled with stress $s$ in one direction only, the circle passes through $0$ and $s$.
The largest shear stress is its radius $s/2$, reached on planes at $45$ degrees.
