## Summary

An octonion algebra over a field $K$ is a unital [[composition algebra|1Q9G6AST10]] of dimension $8$ over $K$.
It is [[alternative|N8K9SSVMRN]] and not associative, and it is the [[Cayley–Dickson double|T9XW7RBEHY]] of a [[quaternion algebra|ZWXW0T3EAC]].
The [[octonions|1HRMT3DSX2]] and the [[split-octonions|V17Y6PNA13]] are the two octonion algebras over $\R$.
Octonion algebras are tied to the exceptional Lie group $G_2$, which is their automorphism group, and to the exceptional Jordan algebras.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The octonions are an eight-dimensional number system over the reals in which the length of a product is the product of the lengths.
An octonion algebra is the same structure over any field.
Over some fields there is only one, over others several, and they are told apart by their norm forms.
