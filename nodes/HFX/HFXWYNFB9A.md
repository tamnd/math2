## Summary

The structure constants of an [[algebra over a field|1SENAHZW5A]] are the coefficients that express the product of two basis vectors in the [[basis|0KCQ9Y3QKA]].
By bilinearity they determine the whole multiplication.
They turn questions about an abstract algebra into computations with numbers, and they are the usual way to write down [[Lie algebras|5JJYBJ6A8K]] in physics.
Under a change of basis they transform as a tensor with two lower indices and one upper index.

## Explanations

### L1 human

To multiply in an algebra, it is enough to know how to multiply basis vectors.
Each such product is again a combination of basis vectors, and its coefficients are the structure constants.
For the complex numbers with basis $1, i$, the only nontrivial rule is $i \cdot i = -1 \cdot 1 + 0 \cdot i$.
A table of these coefficients is a complete description of the multiplication.
