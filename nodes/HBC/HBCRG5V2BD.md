## Summary

Transformation optics designs optical media from changes of coordinates.
[[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]] keep their form under a smooth change of coordinates if the [[permittivity|F768YN0P2V]] and [[permeability|5MJQ5J5H8Z]] are changed by an explicit rule.
So a deformation of space can be realised by a material with the transformed parameters.
It is the basis of [[metamaterial cloaking|XAKEYEGSTC]] and of many lens designs with [[metamaterials|CQ9A45JF68]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a grid drawn on a rubber sheet, and light that follows the grid lines.
If you stretch the sheet, the light still follows the stretched lines.
Transformation optics finds a material that makes light behave as if space itself were stretched in this way.
For example, stretching all of space by a factor $2$ corresponds to a material of refractive index $1/2$.
Then each path keeps its optical length.
