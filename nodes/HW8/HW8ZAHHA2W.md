## Summary

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the **bondage number** of a nonempty [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] $G$ is the [[cardinality|0G0ASKNA6Z]] of the smallest set of edges whose removal results in a domination number strictly greater than the domination number $\gamma(G)$ of $G$. The bondage number is denoted $b(G)$.
