## Summary

In mathematics, the **Plücker map** embeds the Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}(k,V)$, whose elements are *k*-dimensional [[subspaces|00YC1D652K]] of an *n*-dimensional [[vector space|000AYPA9AJ]] *V*, either real or complex, in a projective space, thereby realizing it as a projective algebraic variety. More precisely, the Plücker map embeds $\mathrm{Gr}(k,V)$ into the projectivization $\mathbb{P}({\textstyle\bigwedge}^k V)$ of the $k$-th exterior power of $V$. The image is algebraic, consisting of the intersection of a number of quadrics defined by the § Plücker relations (see below).
