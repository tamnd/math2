## Summary

In graph theory, a branch of mathematics, the **cyclomatic number**, **circuit rank**, **cycle rank**, **corank** or **nullity** of an undirected graph is the minimum number of edges that must be removed from the graph to break all its [[cycles|0Z05VCBQ7Y]], making it into a [[tree|062D9KXJB1]] or forest.
