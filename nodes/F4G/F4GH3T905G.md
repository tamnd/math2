## Summary

A correlated equilibrium is a probability distribution over strategy profiles, used by a mediator who privately tells each player what to play.
It is an equilibrium when no player gains by disobeying her recommendation, given what the recommendation tells her.
Robert Aumann introduced it in 1974.
Every Nash equilibrium is a correlated equilibrium, and the set of correlated equilibria is a convex polytope.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A traffic light is the standard picture.
Two drivers reach a crossing; the light shows green to one and red to the other, each with probability $1/2$.
A driver who sees red knows the other has green, so stopping is best; a driver who sees green knows the other will stop, so going is best.
Nobody wants to disobey, and crashes are avoided without any agreement between the drivers.
