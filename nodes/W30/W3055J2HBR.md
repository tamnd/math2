## Summary

Let $X$ be a subvariety of an open set $U \subset \C^n$, that is, a closed [[analytic subset|VMDTGTMN9P]] of $U$.
A point $p \in X$ is regular when, after renaming the coordinates, $X$ is near $p$ the graph of a holomorphic map.
More exactly, there are a number $k$ with $0 \le k \le n$, a neighbourhood $U' \times U''$ of $p$ in $\C^k \times \C^{n-k}$ and a holomorphic map $f \colon U' \to \C^{n-k}$ with $X \cap (U' \times U'') = \Gamma_f$.
Then $k$ is the dimension of $X$ at $p$, written $\dim_p X = k$, and $n-k$ is the codimension of $X$ at $p$.
A point of $X$ that is not regular is singular.
The sets of regular and singular points are written $X_{reg}$ and $X_{sing}$.
If every point of $X$ is regular of dimension $k$, then $X$ is a [[complex manifold|JSGWAN2RJV]] (a complex submanifold of $U$) of dimension $k$.
