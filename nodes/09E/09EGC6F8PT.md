## Summary

A partial derivative of a [[function of several variables|0JPCHTXWG0]] is its derivative with respect to one variable, while all other variables stay fixed.
It measures the rate of change of the function along one coordinate axis.
For $f(x, y) = x^2 y$, the partial derivatives are $\partial f / \partial x = 2xy$ and $\partial f / \partial y = x^2$.
