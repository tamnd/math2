## Summary

The Grad–Shafranov equation is the equation of equilibrium of an axisymmetric plasma in [[ideal MHD|ZSA13P8P81]].
It reduces the force balance between pressure and magnetic force to one scalar nonlinear elliptic equation for the poloidal flux function.
The pressure and the toroidal field enter as two free functions of the flux.
It is the basic tool for designing and reconstructing equilibria in tokamaks, and it is named after Harold Grad and Vitalii Shafranov.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A tokamak holds a hot plasma in a ring-shaped magnetic cage.
For the plasma to sit still, its outward pressure must be balanced at every point by the magnetic force.
Because the device is symmetric around its central axis, this balance boils down to one equation for one function on a cross-section of the ring.
The level curves of that function are the nested magnetic surfaces on which the plasma pressure is constant.
