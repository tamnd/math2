## Summary

The **history of Lorentz transformations** comprises the development of [[linear transformations|0NRYHJPQG2]] forming the Lorentz group or Poincaré group preserving the Lorentz interval $-x_{0}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}$ and the Minkowski inner product $-x_{0}y_{0}+\cdots+x_{n}y_{n}$.
