## Summary

**Fuzzy differential equation** are general concept of [[ordinary differential equation|0NT64JV96A]] in mathematics defined as differential inclusion for non-uniform upper hemicontinuity [[convex set|02AS45T3H9]] with compactness in fuzzy set.

$$dx(t)/dt= F(t,x(t),\alpha),$$

for all $\alpha \in [0,1]$.
