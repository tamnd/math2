## Summary

Cylindrical coordinates describe a point of space by the [[polar coordinates|0BSW4T7THS]] of its projection to the $xy$-plane and by its height $z$ above that plane.
They simplify problems with symmetry about an axis, such as integrals over cylinders and cones.
