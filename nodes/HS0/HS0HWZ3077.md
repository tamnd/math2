## Definition

Let a straight beam occupy $0 \le x \le L$, with [[Young's modulus|T8ENJX1905]] $E$, [[shear modulus|J2GWW1NPTC]] $G$, density $\rho$, cross-sectional area $A$ and [[second moment of area|1QCSARFXQ0]] $I$.
Let $\kappa > 0$ be the shear coefficient of the cross-section.
The unknowns are the deflection $w(x, t)$ and the rotation $\varphi(x, t)$ of the cross-section.
The load $q(x, t)$ per unit length acts in the direction of $w$.
The [[bending moment|Q6XAPXMGH0]] and the shear force are
$$M = -E I \frac{\partial \varphi}{\partial x}, \qquad V = \kappa A G \left( \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} - \varphi \right).$$
The *Timoshenko–Ehrenfest beam equations* are
$$\rho A \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial t^2} - \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[ \kappa A G \left( \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} - \varphi \right) \right] = q,$$
$$\rho I \frac{\partial^2 \varphi}{\partial t^2} - \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left( E I \frac{\partial \varphi}{\partial x} \right) - \kappa A G \left( \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} - \varphi \right) = 0.$$

## Remarks

In the static case the equations are the equilibrium equations $V' = -q$ and $M' = V$.
Example: a cantilever with constant coefficients, clamped at $x = 0$ with $w = \varphi = 0$ and loaded by a force $P$ at $x = L$, has $V = P$ and $M = P(x - L)$.
Then $\varphi = P(Lx - x^2/2)/(EI)$ and $w = P(Lx^2/2 - x^3/6)/(EI) + Px/(\kappa A G)$.
The tip deflection $PL^3/(3EI) + PL/(\kappa AG)$ is the Euler–Bernoulli value plus a shear term.

Non-example: the pair $\varphi = w'$ with $M = -EIw''$ is the Euler–Bernoulli model; it does not satisfy the shear law above unless $V = 0$, since then $w' - \varphi = 0$.

The value of $\kappa$ depends on the shape of the section; a common value for a rectangle is $5/6$.
