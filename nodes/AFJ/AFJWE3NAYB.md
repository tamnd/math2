## Summary

In mathematics, the **Angelescu polynomials** π<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are a series of polynomials generalizing the Laguerre polynomials introduced by Aurel Angelescu. The polynomials can be given by the [[generating function|0HH6JX5WZZ]]

$$\phi\left(\frac t{1-t}\right)\exp\left(-\frac{xt}{1-t}\right)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\pi_n(x)t^n.$$
