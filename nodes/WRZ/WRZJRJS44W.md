## Summary

The electromagnetic tensor, or field strength tensor, is an antisymmetric tensor field of rank two on spacetime.
It combines the [[electric field|YZ7Q9DP8RT]] and the [[magnetic field|XRED3C547P]] into one object.
Under a Lorentz transformation it transforms as a tensor, which explains how electric and magnetic fields mix for moving observers.
In terms of it, [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]] become two short tensor equations.
In the language of differential forms it is a closed $2$-form, the curvature of the electromagnetic potential.

## Explanations

### L1 human

An observer at rest next to a charge sees only an electric field.
An observer running past the same charge sees a moving charge, which is a current, and so also sees a magnetic field.
So electric and magnetic fields are two faces of one thing, and which face one sees depends on how one moves.
The electromagnetic tensor is that one thing: a table of six numbers at each point of spacetime, three electric and three magnetic.
