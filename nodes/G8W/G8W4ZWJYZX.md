## Summary

The [[number $e$|0MVCFMW7S4]] was introduced by Jacob Bernoulli in 1683. More than half a century later, Euler, who had been a student of Jacob's younger brother Johann, proved that $e$ is [[irrational|080T2TXVQV]]; that is, that it cannot be expressed as the quotient of two integers.
