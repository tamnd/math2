## Summary

In axiomatic set theory, **Shelah cardinals** are a kind of large cardinals. A [[cardinal|0EBACQFAZW]] $\kappa$ is called Shelah iff for every $f:\kappa\rightarrow\kappa$, there exists a transitive class $N$ and an elementary embedding $j:V\rightarrow N$ with critical point $\kappa$; and $V_{j(f)(\kappa )}\subset N$.
