## Summary

A semisimple Lie algebra is a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] with no nonzero [[solvable|W7A2R0GR3T]] ideals.
In characteristic $0$ these are exactly the direct sums of simple Lie algebras, and also exactly the Lie algebras with nondegenerate Killing form.
Over $\C$ they are classified by Dynkin diagrams, and all their finite-dimensional representations are completely reducible.
They form the semisimple part of every finite-dimensional Lie algebra in the Levi decomposition.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A semisimple Lie algebra has no commutative pieces hidden inside, not even deep down in the form of a solvable ideal.
In characteristic $0$ it splits as a direct sum of simple Lie algebras, which cannot be broken further.
The Lie algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\C)$ of trace zero matrices is semisimple, while $\mathfrak{gl}_n(\C)$ is not, because the scalar matrices form a commutative ideal.
