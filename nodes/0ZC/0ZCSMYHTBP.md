## Summary

The Jacobian determinant of a map from an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ to $\mathbb{R}^n$ at a point is the determinant of its [[Jacobian matrix|047NDTEPCV]] there.
Its absolute value is the factor by which the map scales small volumes near the point. Its sign tells whether the map keeps or reverses orientation.
