## Summary

$\mathrm{AC}^0$ is the [[complexity class|ESPYW94XGR]] of languages decided by families of [[Boolean circuits|SQXNFGVADX]] of polynomial size and constant depth.
In these circuits the AND and OR gates may have any number of inputs.
It models computations that can be done in constant time by polynomially many parallel processors.
It is one of the few classes for which strong lower bounds are known: the parity function is not in $\mathrm{AC}^0$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a circuit with only a fixed number of layers, say five, where each gate may combine as many wires as it likes.
Such circuits can add two numbers and compare two numbers.
They cannot tell whether the number of ones in the input is even or odd, unless they are exponentially large.
This shows that a simple-looking task can be impossible for a natural model of very fast parallel computation.
