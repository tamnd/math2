## Summary

In mathematics, informally speaking, **Euclid's orchard** is an array of one-dimensional "trees" of unit height planted at the lattice points in one quadrant of a square lattice. More formally, Euclid's orchard is the set of line segments from $(x, y, 0)$ to $(x, y, 1)$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive [[integers|0C4JS9RF5M]].
