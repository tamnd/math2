## Summary

An approach to nonlinear congruential methods of generating uniform pseudorandom numbers in the interval \[0,1) is the Inversive congruential generator with prime modulus. A generalization for arbitrary composite moduli $m=p_1,\dots p_r$ with arbitrary distinct [[primes|01826KMG2H]] $p_1,\dots ,p_r \ge 5$ will be present here.
