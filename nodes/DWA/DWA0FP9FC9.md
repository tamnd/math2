## Summary

The nucleolus of a [[cooperative game|39HGM44CFZ]] is a sharing of the total worth chosen to please the most dissatisfied coalition as much as possible.
Ties are broken by the next most dissatisfied coalition, and so on.
David Schmeidler introduced it and proved that it exists and is unique whenever the game has an imputation.
It always lies in the core when the core is nonempty, so it picks out one stable allocation.

## Explanations

### L1 human

For a proposed split, measure the complaint of each group as what it could earn alone minus what it receives.
The nucleolus first makes the largest complaint as small as possible.
Among the splits that do this, it makes the second largest complaint as small as possible, and so on.
Suppose any two of three players can win a prize of $1$.
Then the nucleolus gives $1/3$ to each player, since every unequal split makes some pair complain more.
