## Summary

Complex geometry and several complex variables is the study of holomorphic functions of several complex variables and of complex manifolds and complex analytic spaces.
A complex manifold is a space that looks locally like a domain in n-dimensional complex space, with holomorphic changes of coordinates.
Holomorphic functions of several variables behave differently from those of one variable: by Hartogs' theorem, a holomorphic function on a ball in complex space of dimension two or more with one point removed extends across that point.
Hartogs found this phenomenon in 1906, and Oka, Cartan and Grauert built the theory of Stein manifolds and coherent analytic sheaves in the 1940s and 1950s.
This topic collects the complex geometry and several complex variables pages of math2.
Its sub-areas are listed below, in reading order.
Complex analysis, algebraic geometry, differential geometry and symplectic, contact and Poisson geometry have their own topics.
