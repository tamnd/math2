## Summary

The density of the normal distribution is the bell-shaped function $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}$.
The node [[density of the normal distribution|027JF1FBB0]] gives the precise definition.
