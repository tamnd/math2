## Summary

A parabolic subalgebra of a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $0$ is a subalgebra that contains a [[Borel subalgebra|AP4MMD8FPR]].
In $\mathfrak{gl}_n(\C)$ the parabolic subalgebras containing the upper triangular matrices are the block upper triangular matrices.
They are the Lie algebras of parabolic subgroups, and they are used to build induced representations and generalized flag varieties.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A Borel subalgebra of $n \times n$ matrices is the set of matrices that keep a full chain of subspaces in place.
A parabolic subalgebra keeps only part of such a chain in place.
In matrix form this means block upper triangular: square blocks on the diagonal can be arbitrary, everything above them is allowed, and everything below them is zero.
For example, the $3 \times 3$ matrices whose last row is $(0, 0, *)$ form a parabolic subalgebra of $\mathfrak{gl}_3$.
