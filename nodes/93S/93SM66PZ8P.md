## Summary

In mathematics, a **biquadratic field** is a number field $K$ of a particular kind, which is a [[Galois extension|0DS2F7GK05]] of degree 4 of the rational number field $\mathbb{Q}$ with [[Galois group|0XTY86BZKY]] isomorphic to the [[Klein four-group|0PMRQJVTME]].
