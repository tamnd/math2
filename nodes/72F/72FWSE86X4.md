## Summary

In mathematics, the **Gauss–Manin connection** is a connection on a certain vector bundle over a base space *S* of a family of algebraic varieties $V_s$. The fibers of the vector bundle are the de Rham cohomology groups $H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(V_s)$ of the fibers $V_s$ of the family. It was introduced by (txt) for curves *S* and by (txt) in higher dimensions.
