## Summary

This topic is a part of [[representation theory|S60Z63NM98]].
It covers highest weight representations of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras: integrable modules of affine algebras, the Kac-Weyl character formula, modules over the Virasoro algebra, and the vertex operator constructions that realize them.
