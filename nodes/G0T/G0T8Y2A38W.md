## Definition

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] over a field $k$.
For subspaces $\mathfrak a, \mathfrak b \subseteq \mathfrak g$, let $[\mathfrak a, \mathfrak b]$ be the subspace spanned by all $[a, b]$ with $a \in \mathfrak a$ and $b \in \mathfrak b$.
The *lower central series* of $\mathfrak g$ is the sequence of subspaces given by
$$\mathfrak g^1 = \mathfrak g, \qquad \mathfrak g^{i+1} = [\mathfrak g, \mathfrak g^i] \quad (i \ge 1).$$

## Remarks

Each $\mathfrak g^i$ is a [[Lie ideal|JWMTXQ8A3A]] of $\mathfrak g$, and $\mathfrak g^{i+1} \subseteq \mathfrak g^i$.
The quotient $\mathfrak g^i / \mathfrak g^{i+1}$ lies in the center of $\mathfrak g / \mathfrak g^{i+1}$.
Other notations are $C^i \mathfrak g$ and $\mathcal{C}^i(\mathfrak g)$, and some authors start the numbering at $0$.

Example: the [[Heisenberg algebra|HBVZGRKCWQ]] with basis $x, y, z$ and $[x, y] = z$, $[x, z] = [y, z] = 0$.
Then $\mathfrak g^2$ is spanned by $[x, y] = z$, and $\mathfrak g^3 = [\mathfrak g, kz] = 0$.

A series that never reaches $0$: take the [[special linear Lie algebra|1ZAT1Z967A]] $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ over a field of characteristic not $2$.
With $[h, e] = 2e$, $[h, f] = -2f$ and $[e, f] = h$, the elements $e$, $f$ and $h$ all lie in $\mathfrak g^2$.
So $\mathfrak g^i = \mathfrak{sl}_2$ for all $i$.

The same construction works for a [[representation|TZAZ163QGK]] $M$ of $\mathfrak g$: $M_0 = M$ and $M_{i+1} = [\mathfrak g, M_i]$, the span of all $x \cdot m$ with $x \in \mathfrak g$ and $m \in M_i$.
For $M = \mathfrak g$ with the adjoint action, this gives back the series above, shifted by one index.
