## Summary

This topic is a part of [[linear algebra|GN8GYSDZFX]].
It covers positive definite and positive semidefinite matrices, their tests, square roots, and the Cholesky and polar decompositions.
It also covers singular values, the singular value decomposition, the pseudoinverse, and low-rank approximation.
