## Summary

A Rajchman measure is a finite measure on a locally compact abelian group whose [[Fourier–Stieltjes transform|5X8PK7H6W3]] tends to zero at infinity.
On the circle, this means that the Fourier coefficients $\hat\mu(n)$ tend to $0$ as $\abs{n} \to \infty$.
Every absolutely continuous measure is Rajchman by the [[Riemann–Lebesgue lemma|WVKDC011KP]], but some singular measures are Rajchman too.
These measures are central in the study of sets of uniqueness and multiplicity for trigonometric series.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A measure on the circle spreads mass around the circle, and its Fourier coefficients measure how much it resonates with each frequency.
For a smooth density, the resonance with very high frequencies dies out.
A point mass resonates equally with every frequency, so its coefficients never die out.
A Rajchman measure is one whose resonance dies out at high frequencies.
The surprise is that a measure can live on a set of length zero and still be Rajchman.
