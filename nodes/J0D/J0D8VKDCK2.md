## Summary

The four-acceleration of a massive particle is the derivative of its [[four-velocity|Y59V040THG]] with respect to proper time.
It is a four-vector orthogonal to the four-velocity in the Minkowski metric, so it is spacelike or zero.
Its Minkowski length is the proper acceleration, the acceleration felt by the particle and measured by an accelerometer it carries.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Four-acceleration measures how the direction of a world line bends in spacetime.
A particle in free, uniform motion has zero four-acceleration.
Its size is what the passenger feels: the push into the seat of an accelerating rocket.
A rocket that feels a constant push of $g$ forever moves along a hyperbola in spacetime, even though its speed never reaches $c$.
