## Summary

The Riemann sphere is the complex plane with one added point $\infty$, see the [[definition|0CDMXGPSES]].
Stereographic projection identifies it with the unit sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
It is the natural domain and target of [[Möbius transformations|0SG74WBFA2]] and of meromorphic functions.
