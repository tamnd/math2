## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers intuitionistic logic as a formal logic with its semantics and metatheory, minimal logic, and the logics between intuitionistic and classical logic.
The main tools are Kripke, topological and algebraic semantics, Jankov formulas, and the Rieger-Nishimura ladder.
The BHK interpretation, realizability and Heyting arithmetic are treated with type theory and constructive mathematics.
Heyting algebras as algebras are treated in [[algebraic logic|QYA4H5Y7VR]].

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Intuitionistic logic|Q11N8X99FK]]: the logic, its Hilbert system and its basic laws.
2. [[Minimal logic|~2f9e5781]]: intuitionistic logic without ex falso.
3. [[Kripke semantics|~11b532e9]]: models on partial orders, with persistence.
4. [[Beth semantics|~b3c503cf]]: models on trees with bars.
5. [[Topological semantics|~088e3e8d]]: open sets, and Tarski's completeness for the real line.
6. [[Heyting algebra semantics|~32a20f35]]: algebraic completeness and varieties.
7. [[Disjunction and existence properties|~59f11dc8]]: by Kripke models and by the slash.
8. [[Glivenko's theorem|~f74f3067]]: double negation and classical validity.
9. [[Gödel's theorem on finite matrices|~98b455d2]]: no finite truth table for intuitionistic logic.
10. [[Intuitionistic predicate logic|~d18966da]]: growing domains, and undecidability of the monadic fragment.
11. [[Kleene slash|~0d19f928]]: a syntactic proof of the disjunction property.
12. [[Admissible rules|~41ee4c18]]: Visser's rules, Iemhoff's basis and Rybakov's decidability.
13. [[Intermediate logic|~57af80cc]]: the lattice between intuitionistic and classical logic.
14. [[Gödel-Dummett logic|~694d0fb1]]: prelinearity and linear frames.
15. [[Law of the weak excluded middle|CX032ACTMZ]]: the logic KC and directed frames.
16. [[Kreisel-Putnam logic|~676a33e5]]: a logic with the disjunction property above intuitionistic logic.
17. [[Medvedev's logic|~6aab2c7f]]: finite problems, structural completeness and an open decision problem.
18. [[Jankov formula|~be90dd8c]]: characteristic formulas, splitting and Jankov's theorem on KC.
19. [[Rieger-Nishimura lattice|~fb59f18e]]: all formulas in one variable.
20. [[Maksimova's interpolation theorem|~a89c6574]]: the seven intermediate logics with interpolation.
21. [[Intuitionistic modal logic|~e9be76d0]]: modal operators over an intuitionistic base.
22. [[Dialogical logic|~991ba25f]]: dialogue games for intuitionistic validity.

### Prerequisites

1. [[The propositional calculus|JDK63589SA]]: classical propositional logic, its proof systems and completeness.
2. [[Proof systems for first-order logic|TTCRX3NFNY]]: natural deduction and sequent calculus.
3. [[Syntax, Kripke semantics and completeness of modal logic|SKY7AQFG1V]]: Kripke frames, canonical models and filtration.
4. [[Modal correspondence, bisimulation and extended modal logics|SPQVNPZDTB]]: helpful for the Gödel-McKinsey-Tarski translation and p-morphisms.
