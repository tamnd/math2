## Definition

Let $(M, g)$ be a time-oriented four-dimensional [[Lorentzian manifold|BY4QYYS788]] with signature $(-, +, +, +)$.
Let $T$ be a symmetric $(0, 2)$-tensor field on $M$, the [[stress–energy tensor|REDZ92MKXX]], with trace $\tr T = g^{ab} T_{ab}$.
A tangent vector $X$ is timelike if $g(X, X) < 0$ and null if $g(X, X) = 0$ and $X \ne 0$.
The *energy conditions* on $T$ are the following, each required at every point of $M$.

1. Null energy condition: $T(k, k) \ge 0$ for every null vector $k$.
2. Weak energy condition: $T(X, X) \ge 0$ for every timelike vector $X$.
3. Strong energy condition: $T(X, X) - \frac12 (\tr T)\, g(X, X) \ge 0$ for every timelike vector $X$.
4. Dominant energy condition: the weak energy condition holds, and for every future-directed timelike $X$, the vector $Y^a = -T^a{}_b X^b$ is future-directed causal or zero.

## Remarks

The weak and the strong conditions each imply the null condition, by continuity, since null vectors are limits of timelike vectors; the dominant condition implies the weak one.
The strong condition does not imply the weak one.
If $g$ solves the [[Einstein field equations|3MCZ97SW6V]] with zero cosmological constant, the strong condition is equivalent to $R_{ab} X^a X^b \ge 0$ for all timelike $X$, where $R_{ab}$ is the [[Ricci tensor|4GTNM0WHDH]].

For a [[perfect fluid|H4FNPG6ZY1]] with energy density $\rho$ and pressure $p$, the conditions read:
null, $\rho + p \ge 0$; weak, $\rho \ge 0$ and $\rho + p \ge 0$; strong, $\rho + 3p \ge 0$ and $\rho + p \ge 0$; dominant, $\rho \ge \abs{p}$.

Example: dust, a perfect fluid with $\rho \ge 0$ and $p = 0$, satisfies all four conditions.

Non-example: a positive cosmological constant acts like a perfect fluid with $p = -\rho$ and $\rho > 0$.
It satisfies the null, weak and dominant conditions, but $\rho + 3p = -2\rho < 0$, so it fails the strong energy condition.
