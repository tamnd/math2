## Summary

This topic is a part of [[global analysis and geometric analysis|S5M5GNQ7P1]].
It covers the evolution of hypersurfaces and submanifolds by functions of their curvatures, with mean curvature flow, its singularities and weak solutions, and the inverse mean curvature, Gauss curvature, Willmore and Lagrangian flows.
