## Summary

The Jacobi symbol $\left(\frac{a}{n}\right)$ extends the Legendre symbol to each odd positive integer $n$, as a product over the prime factors of $n$.
The [[definition of the Jacobi symbol|0XDB5AW7M1]] gives the precise form.
