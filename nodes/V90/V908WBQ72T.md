## Summary

A [[planar graph|029JQEK15B]] is a graph that has an embedding onto the Euclidean plane. A plane graph is a planar graph for which a particular embedding has already been fixed. A $k$-planar graph is one that can be drawn in the plane with at most $k$ crossings per edge.
