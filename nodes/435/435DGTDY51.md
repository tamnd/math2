## Statement

Let $m$, $n$ and $p$ be [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], where $0$ counts as a natural number.
Suppose that $p \neq 0$ and that the [[products|06J5DE801P]] $mp$ and $np$ are equal.
Then $m = n$.

## Remarks

The condition $p \neq 0$ is needed: $1 \cdot 0 = 2 \cdot 0$, but $1 \neq 2$.
