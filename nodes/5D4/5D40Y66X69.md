## Definition

Let $p$ be a prime and let $n \ge 1$ be an integer.
Let $BP$ be the Brown–Peterson spectrum at $p$, a summand of $p$-local [[complex cobordism|5CZHFNY9F3]], with coefficient ring $BP_* = \Z_{(p)}[v_1, v_2, \dots]$ and $\abs{v_i} = 2(p^i - 1)$.
The *Morava K-theory* $K(n)$ at $p$ is the [[spectrum|1GHF4EKBXS]] obtained from $BP$ in two steps:

1. Kill the elements $p, v_1, \dots, v_{n-1}, v_{n+1}, v_{n+2}, \dots$ by iterated cofibre sequences (the Baas–Sullivan construction).
2. Invert $v_n$ by a homotopy colimit along multiplication by $v_n$.

Its coefficient ring is
$$K(n)_* = \pi_* K(n) = \F_p[v_n, v_n^{-1}], \qquad \abs{v_n} = 2(p^n - 1),$$
where $\F_p$ is the [[finite field|0ZXZ82FTX2]] with $p$ elements.
The *Morava K-theory* of a space or spectrum $X$ is the [[generalized cohomology theory|H28PSZR7XG]] $K(n)^*(X)$ represented by $K(n)$, together with the homology theory $K(n)_*(X)$.

## Remarks

$K(n)$ is a [[ring spectrum|BAW0T68GWK]] and is complex oriented.
Its [[formal group law|TNHP7XXYZT]] can be chosen with $p$-series $[p](x) = v_n x^{p^n}$, so it has height $n$.
Every graded module over $K(n)_*$ is free, since $K(n)_*$ is a graded field.
This gives, for spaces $X$ and $Y$, a Künneth isomorphism $K(n)_*(X \times Y) \cong K(n)_*(X) \otimes_{K(n)_*} K(n)_*(Y)$.
The theory $K(n)$ is $2(p^n - 1)$-periodic.
By convention $K(0)$ is rational cohomology and $K(\infty)$ is mod $p$ cohomology.

Example: for $p = 2$ and $n = 1$, mod $2$ complex K-theory $KU/2$ has coefficients $\F_2[u, u^{-1}]$ with $\abs{u} = 2 = 2(2^1 - 1)$.
Its formal group law is the multiplicative one, whose $2$-series is $u x^2$ modulo $2$.
So $KU/2$ is a model of $K(1)$ at $2$.

Non-example: for an odd prime $p$, the spectrum $KU/p$ is not $K(1)$.
Its coefficients $\F_p[u, u^{-1}]$ have a unit in degree $2$, while $v_1$ has degree $2(p - 1) > 2$.
In fact $KU/p$ splits as a sum of $p - 1$ suspensions of $K(1)$.
