## Summary

The exponential function $x \mapsto e^x$ is the function that equals its own derivative and takes the value $1$ at $0$. It turns sums into products, and it is the sum of the power series $\sum x^n / n!$. The node [[exponential function|0RMVS7RXZX]] gives the precise definition.
