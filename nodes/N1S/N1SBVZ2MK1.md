## Summary

In mathematics, a **Hilbert modular form** is a generalization of modular forms to functions of two or more variables. It is a (complex) [[analytic function|081WKEDK6C]] on the *m*-fold product of upper half-planes $\mathcal{H}$ satisfying a certain kind of functional equation.
