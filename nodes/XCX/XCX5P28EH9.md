## Summary

A scheme *X* covered by Spec *A<sub>i</sub>*, where the *A<sub>i</sub>* are [[Noetherian|0670T59HGE]] rings. If in addition a finite number of such affine spectra covers *X*, the scheme is called *noetherian*. While it is true that the spectrum of a noetherian ring is a noetherian topological space, the converse is false. For example, most schemes in finite-dimensional algebraic geometry are locally Noetherian, but $GL_\infty = \cup GL_n$ is not.
