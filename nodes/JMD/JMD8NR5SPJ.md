## Summary

In geometry, the **lemniscate of Bernoulli** is a plane curve whose shape resembles the numeral 8 or the ∞ symbol. It can be defined from two given points $F_1$ and $F_2$, called the *foci*, as the locus of points $P$ satisfying the relation $|PF_1|\cdot|PF_2| = c^2,$ where the notation $|AB|$ means the distance between two points $A$ and $B$, and $c = \tfrac12|F_1F_2|$ is half the distance between foci. The name *lemniscate* derives from the Latin word la, meaning "decorated with hanging ribbons". The lemniscate of Bernoulli is a special case of the Cassini oval and is a rational algebraic curve of degree four.
