## Summary

A quantum vortex is a line, or a point in two dimensions, around which a [[superfluid|4PZBRJ78X4]] circulates.
Its [[circulation|BTHH4Y71QR]] is quantised: it is an integer multiple of $h/m$, where $h$ is Planck's constant and $m$ the mass of the particles.
The superfluid density vanishes on the vortex core, and the phase of the wave function winds by a multiple of $2\pi$ around it.
Quantum vortices carry the rotation of superfluids and form lattices in rotating condensates and tangles in quantum turbulence.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A superfluid flows as a single quantum wave, so it cannot spin smoothly like water in a bucket.
Instead it makes thin whirlpools, each with a hollow core and a fixed amount of swirl.
The swirl comes in whole units: one, two, three, never one and a half.
When a superfluid container is rotated, more and more of these whirlpools appear, and they arrange themselves in a regular triangular lattice.
