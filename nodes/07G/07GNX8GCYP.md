## Summary

The ring $R[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ is the ring of the polynomials in the variables $x_1, \dots, x_n$ with coefficients in a commutative ring $R$.
The [[definition of the multivariate polynomial ring|0CES91M79A]] gives the construction.
