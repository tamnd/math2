## Summary

The complex exponential is the function $z \mapsto e^z = \sum_{n \ge 0} z^n / n!$ on $\mathbb{C}$, see the [[definition|054VXHMCA3]].
It extends the real exponential function, it is entire, and it has period $2\pi i$.
