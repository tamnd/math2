## Summary

Divide and choose is the classical procedure for dividing a resource between two agents.
One agent cuts it into two pieces, and the other agent picks one piece.
If the cutter makes two pieces she values equally, each agent gets at least half by her own valuation.
The resulting allocation is proportional and [[envy-free|P3GPD0S0J7]], so the procedure solves [[fair cake-cutting|KCVV28DH6R]] for two agents.

## Explanations

### L1 human

This is the familiar rule "I cut, you choose" used by two children sharing a cake.
The cutter has every reason to cut as evenly as she can, because she will get whichever piece is left.
The chooser simply takes the piece she likes better.
Neither child then wishes she had the other's piece.
