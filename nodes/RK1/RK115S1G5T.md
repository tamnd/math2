## Summary

Vector optimization minimizes a function whose values lie in a vector space ordered by a [[convex cone|6VE4KA12VD]].
Since such an order is only partial, optimal points are defined as minimal elements: no other attainable value is smaller in the cone order.
With the cone of vectors with nonnegative coordinates in $\R^k$, it is [[multi-objective optimization|E560RGEKPP]] with Pareto optimality.
Other cones model other preferences, and the theory extends duality and optimality conditions to this setting.

## Explanations

### L1 human

With one objective, any two outcomes can be compared: one is smaller or they are equal.
With several objectives, two outcomes may be incomparable, as when one is cheaper but the other is safer.
A cone fixes which differences count as an improvement.
The best outcomes are those that no other outcome improves on in this sense.
