## Summary

This boundary condition says that the heat conducted to the surface of a body equals the heat carried away by the surrounding fluid.
The conducted flux comes from [[Fourier's law|9YJZ1PP2Y8]], and the convected flux is $h (T - T_\infty)$ with a [[heat transfer coefficient|J821AD6YTE]] $h$.
It is the most common [[Robin boundary condition|581AGFCCRX]] in practice.
It lies between a fixed surface temperature and a fixed surface flux.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A hot pipe in a cold room does not have a fixed surface temperature, and it does not lose a fixed amount of heat either.
Instead, the hotter its surface is compared with the air, the faster it loses heat.
The condition says that the heat arriving at the surface from inside must leave into the air at exactly this rate.
For a rod end at $x = L$, it reads $-k T'(L) = h \left(T(L) - T_\infty\right)$.
