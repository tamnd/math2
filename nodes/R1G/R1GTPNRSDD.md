## Summary

This topic is a part of [[commutative algebra|KKBKTW8YB8]].
It covers polynomial rings in one and several variables over a commutative ring as rings, with degree, evaluation, the universal property, division by monic polynomials, and the units, zero divisors and automorphisms of these rings.
