## Summary

This topic is a part of [[model theory|F3AXVABC35]].
It covers the tests for quantifier elimination and model completeness, model companions, existentially closed models and Robinson's forcing, with the standard algebraic examples.
A theory has quantifier elimination if every formula is equivalent to a quantifier-free one, and it is model complete if every embedding between its models is elementary.
The main examples are [[algebraically closed fields|~561b0711]], [[real closed fields|FSJ9NGRRPA]] and [[Presburger arithmetic|~0727c76f]].

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Quantifier elimination|7FDDXV3JK7]]: every formula is equivalent to a quantifier-free one.
2. [[Tests for quantifier elimination|~e415d689]]: it suffices to eliminate one existential quantifier, or to extend embeddings.
3. [[Model complete theory|YR6HCMABTE]]: every embedding between models is elementary.
4. [[Robinson's test|~3847820a]]: a theory is model complete if and only if existential formulas pass down from each model to its submodels that are models.
5. [[Existentially closed model|~524465b6]]: a model in which every existential sentence true in an extension already holds.
6. [[Model companion|~7269c0e3]]: the model complete theory of the existentially closed models.
7. [[Inductive theory|~719ccde0]]: theories preserved under unions of chains.
8. [[The theory of groups has no model companion|~826703ac]]: an inductive theory whose existentially closed models do not form an elementary class.
9. [[Robinson forcing|~357e6442]]: generic models when the existentially closed models are not elementary.
10. [[Quantifier elimination for algebraically closed fields|~561b0711]]: the theory $\mathrm{ACF}$.
11. [[Quantifier elimination for real closed fields|FSJ9NGRRPA]]: the theory $\mathrm{RCF}$ in the ordered signature.
12. [[Cylindrical algebraic decomposition|~00975ede]]: an effective form of elimination for real closed fields.
13. [[Quantifier elimination for Z-groups|~0727c76f]]: Presburger arithmetic with divisibility predicates.
14. [[Divisible torsion-free abelian groups|~e6977377]]: vector spaces over the rationals.
15. [[Relative quantifier elimination|~a7454f65]]: elimination down to a chosen set of formulas.

- [[Elementary extensions and the Löwenheim-Skolem theorems|2RE7GWWXM5]]: elementary substructures, chains and the Tarski-Vaught test.
