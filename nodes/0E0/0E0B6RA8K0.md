## Summary

The Laplacian of a twice differentiable function on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is the [[divergence|0MWV6ZTK6S]] of its [[gradient|0ABK9V9Y64]].
It measures how the value of the function at a point differs from its average value on small spheres around the point.
