## Summary

The inhomogeneous electromagnetic wave equations are [[wave equations|5EHD2XBQDG]] with source terms.
The fields and the potentials satisfy them in the presence of charges and currents.
They follow from [[Maxwell's equations|9BWWD4QK8Y]] by taking curls, or by choosing the Lorenz gauge for the potentials.
In the Lorenz gauge each potential solves a scalar wave equation whose source is the charge density or the current density.
Their solution by the retarded Green's function gives the fields radiated by moving charges.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Charges and currents that move create electric and magnetic waves.
The inhomogeneous wave equation says how: it is the ordinary wave equation with an extra term that describes the sources.
Far from the sources the extra term is zero and the waves travel freely at the speed of light.
For a charge at rest the time derivatives vanish, and the equation reduces to Poisson's equation of electrostatics.
