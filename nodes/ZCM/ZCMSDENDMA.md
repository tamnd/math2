## Summary

Top trading cycle is an algorithm for reallocating indivisible goods, such as houses, among agents who each own one good.
Each agent has preferences over all the goods.
Agents repeatedly point to the owners of their favourite remaining goods and trade along the cycles that form.
Lloyd Shapley and Herbert Scarf described it in 1974 for housing markets and credited the idea to David Gale.
Its outcome is in the core of the housing market, and no agent can gain by misreporting her preferences.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Everyone owns a house and points at the owner of the house they like best.
Following the arrows, one always comes back to a starting point, so there is a circle of people.
Everyone in the circle moves into the house they point at, and they leave the market.
For example, if Ann wants Bob's house and Bob wants Ann's, they swap at once.
The rest repeat the process with the remaining houses.
