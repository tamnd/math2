## Summary

A direct mechanism is incentive compatible if every agent does best by reporting her private information truthfully.
In the strong form, dominant-strategy incentive compatibility or strategy-proofness, truth-telling is best whatever the others report.
In the weaker Bayesian form, it is best in expectation when the others report truthfully.
By the revelation principle, any goal that some mechanism implements is also implemented by an incentive compatible direct mechanism.
This makes the notion central to [[mechanism design|DTXJQKEHDP]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

A rule is incentive compatible when nobody can gain by lying about what they want.
For example, if a seller asks two buyers for their values, gives the item to the higher one, and charges the lower value, then lying never helps a buyer.
If instead the winner pays her own reported value, buyers have a reason to report less than their true value.
