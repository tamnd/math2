## Summary

NL is the [[complexity class|ESPYW94XGR]] of decision problems that a [[nondeterministic Turing machine|M7SDX137WE]] can solve with logarithmic space.
It contains [[L|VRZ9H4EZ6V]] and lies inside [[P|GVT2NKN37K]].
Its typical problem is directed reachability: given a directed graph and two vertices $s$ and $t$, is there a path from $s$ to $t$?
It is open whether L equals $\mathrm{NL}$.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Picture a walker in a maze of one-way corridors who can only remember the room she is in and a step count.
At each room she guesses which corridor to take.
If some sequence of guesses reaches the exit, the answer is yes.
NL is the class of questions that such a guessing walker with a tiny memory can answer.
