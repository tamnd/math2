## Summary

A topological transformation group is a [[topological group|92700QAB5Y]] acting on a topological space so that the action map is continuous.
It is the topological version of a [[group action|0WCY42EV9V]], and it is the basic object of topological dynamics.
Each group element then acts by a homeomorphism of the space.
Homogeneous spaces, flows and actions of Lie groups on manifolds are all examples.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A group can move the points of a space around: rotations move the points of a sphere, for example.
In a topological transformation group, the motion depends continuously both on the group element and on the point.
Rotating by a slightly different angle, or starting at a slightly different point, changes the result only slightly.
The rotation group acting on the sphere is the standard picture.
