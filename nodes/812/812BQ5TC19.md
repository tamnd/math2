## Statement

In the mathematics of graph drawing, **Turán's brick factory problem** asks for the minimum number of crossings in a drawing of a [[complete bipartite graph|0V0GC7BQAN]]. The problem is named after Pál Turán, who formulated it while being forced to work in a brick factory during World War II.
