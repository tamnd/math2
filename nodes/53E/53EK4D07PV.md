## Summary

The fundamental ideal $I(F)$ of the [[Witt ring|KFVY0RPGF2]] of a field $F$ is the ideal of classes of even-dimensional quadratic forms.
Its powers $I^n(F)$ give a filtration of the Witt ring.
The quotients $I^n(F)/I^{n+1}(F)$ are described by the Milnor conjecture on quadratic forms, now a theorem, in terms of Milnor K-theory and Galois cohomology.
The powers $I^n(F)$ are generated by [[Pfister forms|7HMTK6P4W7]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Every quadratic form has a dimension, and the dimension modulo $2$ is well defined on Witt classes.
The fundamental ideal collects the forms of even dimension.
Its square consists of forms that are even in a finer sense, which also involves the discriminant.
For the real numbers, Witt classes are given by the signature, the fundamental ideal is $2\Z$, and its $n$-th power is $2^n \Z$.
So the filtration by powers measures how divisible a form is by $2$.
