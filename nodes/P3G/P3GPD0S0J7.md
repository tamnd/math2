## Summary

An allocation of resources among agents is envy-free if no agent prefers the share of another agent to her own share.
It is one of the main fairness criteria of [[fair division|X1X9TZ8GAR]].
For a divisible cake with valuations given by nonatomic measures, envy-free allocations always exist.
For indivisible goods they may not, and weaker forms such as envy-freeness up to one good are used.

## Explanations

### L1 human

After the sharing is done, each person looks at every other person's share and asks: "would I rather have that?"
If the answer is always no, the division is envy-free.
Two children who split a cake by "I cut, you choose" get an envy-free division.
The cutter made two pieces she likes equally, and the chooser took the one she likes more.
With a single indivisible toy and two children, no division is envy-free, since the child without the toy envies the other.
