## Statement

Let $m$, $n$ and $p$ be nonnegative integers. Then adding $m$ and $n$ first and multiplying the sum by $p$ gives the same number as multiplying each of $m$ and $n$ by $p$ and then adding: $(m+n)p = mp + np$.
Here $+$ is the [[addition|0W5DSDFQ4G]] and the product is the [[multiplication|0JRC32D6K2]] of nonnegative integers.

## Remarks

The sum is on the left factor, so this is the right law. The left law has the sum on the right factor, and it is the same result once multiplication is commutative.
