## Summary

A thermodynamic potential is a state function that contains all the equilibrium information about a system.
This holds when it is written in terms of its natural variables.
The [[internal energy|F6JVQ12X43]] as a function of entropy, volume and amounts of substance is the basic one.
The others, such as the enthalpy and the Helmholtz and Gibbs free energies, are its [[Legendre transforms|ZQYMWVT1TT]] in some of these variables.
Each potential is suited to a set of experimental conditions, and it is minimal in equilibrium under those conditions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A thermodynamic potential is like a master function for a system.
Its partial derivatives give temperature, pressure, entropy, volume and the other quantities.
Which potential one uses depends on what is held fixed: the energy suits fixed entropy and volume, the Gibbs free energy suits fixed temperature and pressure.
For a gas in a cylinder at room temperature and air pressure, the Gibbs free energy is the natural one.
