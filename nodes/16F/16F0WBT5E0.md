## Summary

Flory–Huggins theory is a lattice model of the free energy of mixing of a polymer with a solvent, or of two polymers.
It counts the ways to place chains on a lattice, which gives an entropy of mixing much smaller than for small molecules.
It adds a mean-field interaction energy measured by one parameter $\chi$.
From the resulting free energy one reads off when the mixture is stable and when it separates into two phases.
It is the starting point of the theory of polymer solutions and blends.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a grid where each cell holds either a solvent molecule or one bead of a polymer chain.
Because the beads of a chain are tied together, a polymer gains much less freedom by mixing than the same number of free molecules would.
So a small unfavourable interaction between polymer and solvent is enough to make them separate, like oil and water.
The parameter $\chi$ measures this unfavourable contact energy in units of $k_B T$.
For very long chains, the solvent dissolves the polymer at all concentrations only when $\chi$ is below about $1/2$.
