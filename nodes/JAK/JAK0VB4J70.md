## Summary

In graph theory, a vertex [[subset|0BJR9QR08E]] $S \subset V$ is a **vertex separator** (or **vertex cut**, or **separating set**) for nonadjacent vertices $a$ and $b$ if the removal of $S$ from the [[graph|00C0BR7D7H]] separates $a$ and $b$ into distinct connected components.
