## Statement

Let $m$ and $n$ be [[natural numbers|0MWKJ44V5A]], where $0$ is a natural number.
Then the product of $m$ and $n$ does not depend on their order: $m n = n m$.
Here the product is the [[multiplication of natural numbers|06J5DE801P]].

## Remarks

This is the commutative law for multiplication, a case of [[commutativity|099E72KTF8]].
