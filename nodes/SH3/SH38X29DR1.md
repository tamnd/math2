## Statement

In mathematics, **Carmichael's totient function conjecture** concerns the multiplicity of values of [[Euler's totient function|0DAMHCT17R]] $\varphi(n)$, which counts the number of integers less than and coprime to $n$. It states that, for every $n$ there is at least one other integer $m\ne n$ such that $\varphi(m)=\varphi(n)$. Robert Carmichael first stated this conjecture in 1907, but as a theorem rather than as a conjecture. However, his proof was faulty, and in 1922, he retracted his claim and stated the conjecture as an open problem.
