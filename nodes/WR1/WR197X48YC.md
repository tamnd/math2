## Summary

A Bogoliubov transformation is a linear change of [[creation and annihilation operators|7N0J4DF8N5]] that mixes creation with annihilation operators and keeps their commutation or anticommutation relations.
It gives a new set of modes, and so a new notion of particle and of vacuum.
It is the standard tool to diagonalize quadratic Hamiltonians, as in the theory of superfluidity and superconductivity.
In [[quantum field theory in curved spacetime|J2PSEYJVT4]] it relates the particle notions of different observers and gives particle creation and the [[Unruh effect|HM260RP02P]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Annihilation operators remove a quantum and creation operators add one.
A Bogoliubov transformation builds new annihilation operators out of a mixture of the old annihilation and creation operators.
The mixture is chosen so that the new operators obey the same algebraic rules as the old ones.
For one bosonic mode, $b = \cosh(r)\, a + \sinh(r)\, a^*$ is an example, because $\cosh^2 r - \sinh^2 r = 1$.
The old vacuum is in general not empty for the new operators: it contains pairs of the new quanta.
