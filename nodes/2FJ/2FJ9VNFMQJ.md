## Summary

The Blandford–Znajek process extracts rotational energy from a spinning black hole through magnetic field lines that thread its event horizon.
Surrounding matter keeps the field lines in place, and the black hole drags them into rotation.
So they carry a Poynting flux of electromagnetic energy outward.
The process is a leading explanation for the powerful relativistic jets of active galactic nuclei and gamma-ray bursts.
Its power grows with the square of the magnetic flux through the horizon and, for moderate spins, with the square of the spin.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A rotating black hole stores a huge amount of energy in its spin.
If magnetic field lines from a surrounding disk pass through the hole, the spinning space near the hole twists them.
Twisted magnetic field lines carry energy away, like a wire carrying current from a generator.
The black hole acts as a giant dynamo and slowly spins down while it powers jets that move at nearly the speed of light.
