## Summary

A contour line of a function $f$ of two real variables is a curve in the plane along which $f$ has a constant value $c$.
It is a [[level set|0B2RZ0778N]] $\{(x, y) : f(x, y) = c\}$ of $f$, drawn in the $(x, y)$-plane.
A contour map draws the contour lines for several equally spaced values of $c$. It shows the shape of the [[graph|0JBJYT69ZV]] of $f$ in the plane.

## Remarks

On a topographic map, $f$ is the height above sea level, and the contour lines join points of equal height.
Where the contour lines of equally spaced values are close together, the function changes fast.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine that you cut a hill with horizontal planes at heights $10$, $20$, $30$ and so on, and you look at the cuts from above.
Each cut gives a closed curve on the map, and these curves are the contour lines.
