## Problem

The **graph realization problem** is a decision problem in graph theory. Given a finite sequence $(d_1,\dots,d_n)$ of natural numbers, the problem asks whether there is a labeled [[simple graph|00C0BR7D7H]] such that $(d_1,\dots,d_n)$ is the degree sequence of this graph.
