## Example

The *Goryachev–Chaplygin top* is a heavy rigid body turning about a fixed point $O$ in a uniform gravitational field.
Its principal moments of inertia at $O$ are $I_1 = I_2 = 4I_3$, and its centre of mass lies in the plane of the first two principal axes.
Let $L$ be its angular momentum about $O$ and $\gamma$ the upward unit vertical, both in the body frame.
The vertical component $L \cdot \gamma$ of the angular momentum is always conserved, and so is the energy.
On the set of motions with $L \cdot \gamma = 0$, the top has one more first integral, of degree $3$ in the angular velocity.
On this set the motion can be integrated by quadratures.

## Remarks

It is a case of partial integrability: the condition for an extra integral restricts the initial conditions, not only the body.
Compare the [[Kovalevskaya top|EM6CSAK7GC]], with $I_1 = I_2 = 2I_3$ and the centre of mass in the same plane, which is integrable for all initial conditions.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Goryachev–Chaplygin top is a spinning top that can be solved exactly, but only when it is started in a special way.
The start must be such that the angular momentum has no vertical part.
It shows that a body can be solvable for some motions without being solvable for all of them.
