## Summary

The Borel regulator is a homomorphism from the odd [[K-groups of the ring of integers|4HXFKW55WJ]] of a number field to a real vector space.
For $K_1$ it is the logarithmic embedding of units used for the classical [[regulator of a number field|RWK0HNF1NA]].
Borel showed that for $K_{2m-1}$ with $m \ge 2$ its image is a lattice of full rank.
The covolume of this lattice is a rational multiple of a special value of the [[Dedekind zeta function|KXS5DQDFYD]].
It is the number field case of the [[Beilinson regulator|SXJWFF0WAJ]].

## Explanations

### L1 human

Dirichlet measured the size of the unit group of a number field by taking logarithms of absolute values of units, which turns units into vectors in a real space.
The volume of the lattice they form is the classical regulator, and it appears in the class number formula for the zeta function.
Borel found a similar construction for every odd K-group, using a special cohomology class of the general linear group over the complex numbers in place of the logarithm.
The volume of the resulting lattice controls the value of the zeta function at the integer $m$.
