## Summary

In mathematics, the **binary cyclic group** of the *n*-gon is the cyclic group of order 2*n*, $C_{2n}$, thought of as an extension of the cyclic group $C_n$ by a [[cyclic group|0B85Q41RP6]] of order 2. Coxeter writes the *binary cyclic group* with angle-brackets, ⟨*n*⟩, and the index 2 subgroup as (*n*) or \[*n*\]<sup>\+</sup>.
