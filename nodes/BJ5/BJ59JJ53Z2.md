## Statement

Let $G$ be a group whose [[classifying space|WA796ZCNRX]] $BG$ has a model that is a finite CW complex.
Let $\Z[G]$ be its integral [[group ring|4NMTSX0XDS]] and let $K_n(\Z[G])$ be its [[algebraic K-groups|5GVFZWMWC8]].
Let $\mathbf{K}(\Z)$ be the [[K-theory spectrum|2C1M0NWPB0]] of $\Z$, and let $H_n(BG; \mathbf{K}(\Z))$ be the [[homology|XT7VWNTKGB]] of $BG$ with coefficients in it.
The conjecture says that for every $n \in \Z$ the rationalized classical [[assembly map|2KT6XR8MCZ]]
$$H_n(BG; \mathbf{K}(\Z)) \otimes_\Z \Q \to K_n(\Z[G]) \otimes_\Z \Q$$
is an isomorphism.

## Remarks

A group with a finite model of $BG$ is [[torsion-free|PEMNN32A43]], since a nontrivial finite cyclic subgroup has infinite cohomological dimension.
Hsiang proposed the conjecture in his address to the International Congress of Mathematicians in Warsaw in 1983.
Rationally, the left side splits as $\bigoplus_{p + q = n} H_p(BG; \Q) \otimes_\Z K_q(\Z)$.
For $n = 1$ the left side is $G^{ab} \otimes \Q$, so the conjecture implies that the [[Whitehead group|8SY3AC4KW3]] $Wh(G)$ is a torsion group.
For torsion-free $G$ the K-theory part of the [[Farrell–Jones conjecture|B2X59B7J8V]] implies the integral version for all $n$, hence this conjecture.
Bökstedt, Hsiang and Madsen proved rational injectivity of the assembly map under homological finiteness conditions on $G$, using the cyclotomic trace.
Lück, Reich, Rognes and Varisco extended this injectivity result.
Surjectivity is open in general.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The K-groups of a group ring hold information used in the topology of manifolds, but they are hard to compute.
For a nice torsion-free group there is an obvious source of elements, made from the homology of the group and the K-groups of the integers.
Hsiang conjectured that, after ignoring torsion, this source gives everything and nothing is lost.
It is a K-theory analogue of the Novikov and Borel conjectures of manifold topology.
