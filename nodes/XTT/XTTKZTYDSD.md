## Summary

This topic is a part of [[mathematical logic|DYJ0KGTPMR]].
It covers the measurement of the strength of theories by ordinals: Gentzen's consistency proof, systems of ordinal notations, infinitary proof theory and the proof-theoretic ordinals of the standard theories.

## Remarks

### Reading order

1. [[Ordinal analysis|C5E5TG8PGP]]: the aim of the subject, what one analysis gives, and the [[precise definition|57PTMS2ADY]].
2. [[Ordinal notation system|~f8083877]]: computable well-orders with a decidable comparison, and the [[precise definition|~a8ed0faf]].
3. [[Epsilon nought|KT50SMH3EC]]: the least fixed point of $\alpha \mapsto \omega^\alpha$ and its Cantor normal form notation.
4. [[Gentzen's consistency proof|QPV0HNP22S]]: the consistency of PA from transfinite induction up to $\varepsilon_0$.
5. [[Transfinite induction in arithmetic|~e065bc19]]: PA proves induction up to each ordinal below $\varepsilon_0$, but not up to $\varepsilon_0$.
6. [[Proof-theoretic ordinal|C72RBH0CDR]]: the ordinal of a theory, with its [[precise definition|C19Z3B6V4P]] and the table of standard theories.
7. [[ω-rule and infinitary derivations|~e0034cfd]]: infinitary proofs, cut elimination and the upper bound $\varepsilon_0$.
8. [[Veblen function|JQFAE9S2F4]]: normal functions that list fixed points, with the [[definition|6QFEVWKE25]].
9. [[Feferman-Schütte ordinal|~5f65c1fc]]: $\Gamma_0$, the limit of predicative analysis.
10. [[Small and large Veblen ordinals|~fafc7335]]: the Veblen function with more arguments.
11. [[Bachmann-Howard ordinal|~fcf34cb0]]: the first ordinal of an impredicative theory.
12. [[Ordinal collapsing function|~766739ea]]: names of large countable ordinals from an uncountable one.
13. [[Buchholz's psi functions|E7WEFRMQTB]]: collapsing functions $\psi_\nu$ for $\nu \le \omega$.
14. [[Rathjen's psi function|~0d062a69]]: collapsing from large cardinals.
15. [[Takeuti-Feferman-Buchholz ordinal|~1df2a94e]]: the ordinal $\psi_0(\varepsilon_{\Omega_\omega + 1})$.
16. [[Theories of iterated inductive definitions|5VNT564Y2W]]: the theories $\mathrm{ID}_\nu$, with the [[definition|~cd19a8f1]].
17. [[Ordinal analysis of Π¹₁ comprehension|~9776e851]]: the first analysis of a strong impredicative subsystem of analysis.
18. [[Takeuti's conjecture|~fb73bfd1]]: cut elimination for simple type theory.
19. [[Local predicativity and Buchholz's Ω-rule|~af6dfa84]]: two methods for impredicative theories.
