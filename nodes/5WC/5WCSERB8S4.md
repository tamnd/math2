## Summary

Statics is the branch of mechanics that studies bodies and structures at rest under the action of forces.
Its basic equations are the conditions of [[mechanical equilibrium|KHT3FBB7NZ]]: the forces and the [[torques|9636MFFSGC]] on each body sum to zero.
It is used to find the loads in beams, trusses, cables and machine parts that do not move.
Variational tools such as the [[principle of virtual work|ZC3YKMKRGP]] give the same equilibrium conditions for constrained systems.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Statics asks how forces balance in things that stay still, such as bridges, shelves, cranes and ladders.
If you know the weights and how the parts are supported, statics tells you how hard each support must push.
For a $60$ kg person standing in the middle of a plank resting on two trestles, each trestle carries half the weight, about $300$ newtons.
