## Summary

The ideal MHD equations describe a perfectly conducting, inviscid, compressible fluid in a magnetic field.
They are the limit of [[magnetohydrodynamics|T8JWCBK9M6]] with no viscosity, no resistivity and no heat conduction.
They form a nonlinear hyperbolic system of conservation laws.
They are the basic model for the large-scale dynamics of plasmas in astrophysics and fusion devices.
In ideal MHD the magnetic field is frozen into the fluid.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Imagine a gas that conducts electricity perfectly and has no internal friction.
Its magnetic field lines then move with the gas as if they were drawn on it.
Squeezing the gas squeezes the field lines together, and the field pushes back with a magnetic pressure.
Bending a field line costs energy too, so the field lines act like taut strings that can carry waves.
