## Summary

- Ringel's conjecture that the [[complete graph|03WKB2H474]] $K_{2n+1}$ can be decomposed into $2n+1$ copies of any tree with $n$ edges (Richard Montgomery, Benny Sudakov, Alexey Pokrovskiy, 2020).
