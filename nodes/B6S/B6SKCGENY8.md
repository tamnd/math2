## Summary

The Liénard–Wiechert potentials are the [[retarded potentials|9Q1NE5EP37]] of a single point charge that moves along a given path.
They give the electric and vector potential in closed form, in terms of the position and velocity of the charge at the retarded time.
From them one gets the fields of a moving charge, the radiation of an accelerated charge, and the Larmor formula.

## Explanations

### L1 human

A charge that moves sends out its influence at the speed of light.
At a given point and time, what one feels comes from the charge at one earlier moment, the one whose light signal arrives right now.
The Liénard–Wiechert potential is the Coulomb potential of the charge at that earlier position, with a correction factor.
The factor depends on whether the charge was moving toward the point or away from it.
For a charge at rest it is simply the Coulomb potential.
