## Summary

The $n$-th cyclotomic polynomial is the monic polynomial whose roots are the primitive $n$-th roots of unity, each with multiplicity one.
It has integer coefficients.
The [[definition of the cyclotomic polynomials|0W0KVBM54S]] gives the formula.
