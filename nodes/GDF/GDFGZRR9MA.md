## Statement

**Dedekind's theorem on the linear independence of characters.** Let $M$ be a [[monoid|0N6R902FG1]] and $k$ an [[integral domain|09S3PFBEPM]], viewed as a monoid by considering the multiplication on $k$. Then any finite family $( f_{i} )_{i\in I}$ of distinct monoid homomorphisms $f_{i} : M \to k$ is linearly independent. In other words, every family $(\alpha_{i})_{i\in I}$ of elements $\alpha_{i} \in k$ satisfying

$$\sum_{i \in I}\alpha_i f_i = 0$$

must be equal to the family $(0)_{i\in I}$.
