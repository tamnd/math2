## Summary

Operations research is the study of mathematical models for the planning and running of operations, logistics, service systems and decisions.
An operations research model states a real problem as a mathematical program, a stochastic system or a decision problem, and the theory then solves it or bounds its value.
These models are used in transport, manufacturing, inventory control, telecommunications, health care and many other fields.
The subject took its name and its first methods from the work of British and American scientists on military operations in the Second World War.
This topic collects the operations research pages of math2: modelling, transportation, network and routing models, project planning and scheduling, location and packing, inventory and supply chains, queueing theory, stochastic dynamic programming, reliability, decision analysis, simulation, heuristics and revenue management.
Optimization, game theory, stochastic processes and mathematical economics have their own topics.
