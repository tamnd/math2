## Summary

A Killing Lie algebra is a finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra|5JJYBJ6A8K]] whose [[Killing form|TWVQT18JXE]] is nondegenerate.
The name follows the Mathlib library, where this condition is used as a hypothesis for the structure theory of root systems.
Every Killing Lie algebra is semisimple, and in characteristic $0$ the converse holds by Cartan's criterion.
In positive characteristic it still implies semisimplicity.

## Explanations

### L1 human

The Killing form compares two elements of a Lie algebra through their actions on the algebra.
A Lie algebra is called Killing when this comparison never ignores a nonzero element: for each nonzero $x$, some $y$ pairs nontrivially with it.
Over the complex numbers this is the same as being semisimple.
The Lie algebra of $2 \times 2$ trace zero matrices is an example.
