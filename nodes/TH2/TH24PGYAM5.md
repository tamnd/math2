## Summary

In mathematics, a **Weil group**, introduced by André Weil, is a modification of the absolute Galois group of a local or global field, used in class field theory. For such a field $F$, its Weil group is generally denoted $W_F$. There also exists "finite level" modifications of the Galois groups: if $E/F$ is a finite extension, then the **relative Weil group** of $E/F$ is $W_{E/F}=W_F/W_E^c$ (where the superscript $c$ denotes the [[commutator subgroup|08SJ5K2CBG]]).
