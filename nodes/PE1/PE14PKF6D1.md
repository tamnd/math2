## Summary

Let $M$ be a smooth real hypersurface in $\C^n$. A smooth function $f \colon M \to \C$ is a smooth CR function when every tangent vector of type $(0,1)$ to $M$ kills $f$.

In symbols, $X_p f = 0$ for each point $p \in M$ and each $X_p$ in the space $T^{(0,1)}_p M$ of anti-holomorphic tangent vectors at $p$.

This is the case of a [[CR function|JFKYYK2CEB]] where the ambient structure is a hypersurface in complex space and the function is required to be smooth. The condition is the system of the [[tangential Cauchy–Riemann equations|X7GP9BECD8]].
