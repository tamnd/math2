## Summary

The ADM energy is the total energy of an asymptotically flat initial data set in general relativity.
It is defined by a flux integral of the metric over a large sphere at spatial infinity.
Together with the ADM momentum it forms the ADM energy-momentum vector, whose Lorentzian length is the ADM mass.
It arises as the boundary term that the Hamiltonian of the [[ADM formalism|CFAFAPV7HB]] needs on an asymptotically flat slice.
It is conserved in time, and by the [[positive mass theorem|PYRPXHNQTB]] it is non-negative when the matter satisfies the dominant energy condition.

## Explanations

### L1 human

Far from an isolated star or black hole, space is almost flat, and the small deviation from flatness falls off like $1/r$.
The coefficient of this $1/r$ term measures the total energy inside, including the energy of the gravitational field.
The ADM energy extracts this coefficient by an integral over a very large sphere.
For the Schwarzschild black hole of mass $M$, the ADM energy equals $M$.
