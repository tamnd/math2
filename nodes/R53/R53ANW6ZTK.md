## Summary

In mathematics, a **uniform tree** is a locally finite [[tree|062D9KXJB1]] which is the universal cover of a finite graph. Equivalently, the full [[automorphism group|0CWXCWP0Y0]] $G=Aut(X)$ of the tree, which is a locally compact topological group, is unimodular and $G/X$ is finite. Also equivalent is the existence of a uniform X-lattice in $G$.
